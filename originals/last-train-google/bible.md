# The Last Train

An original, everyday comic scene at a small Russian railway station at night.
Anna is trying to catch the last train to Moscow. Boris, a kindly fellow traveller,
knows the correct platform but cannot find the ticket already in his hand.
The humour comes from their mutual help, not from mocking either character.

Keep Anna curious and alert, Boris warm and dry. Their names and plot are original.
This single-scene Google voice trial uses the standard lesson format: introduction,
Russian story, translated walkthrough with explanations, unchanged Russian replay,
and a brief outro with farewell. Narration is warm, female British English.
