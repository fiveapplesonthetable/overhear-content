# Sunshine House (햇살 하우스) — Visual Character Sheet

## Recurring Cast
- **`mina` (미나 / Mina):** Newcomer front-desk receptionist (mid-20s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `long` hair `#231F24`, signature top `#4EA8DE`.
- **`junho` (준호 / Junho):** Veteran handyman & night clerk (late 40s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `cap` hair `#383338`, signature top `#4A6FA5`.
- **`sora` (소라 / Sora):** Morning café baker (early 30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `bun` hair `#3B2926`, signature top `#F28482`.
- **`sungmin` (성민 / Sungmin):** Anxious owner-manager (late 50s, glasses often on forehead). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#928E8A`, signature top `#6D597A`, round glasses.
- **`youngmi` (영미 / Youngmi):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 (60s, glasses). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `bun` hair `#DDD8D2`, signature top `#9B5DE5`, round glasses.
- **`jihoon` (지훈 / Jihoon):** Quiet acoustic architect in the attic room (30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#242026`, signature top `#3A86FF`.
- **`minsu` (민수 / Minsu):** Older brother from City Hall (late 30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#1E1A20`, signature top `#2B59C3`.

## Recurring Places
- **Front Desk:** Warm wood counter `#7A543E`, brass bell `#F2C14E`, paper reservation ledger `#FFF6EA`, slate-blue backdrop.
- **Café Corner:** Caramel morning light `#C98B5E`, woven bread basket, blue teacups.
- **Courtyard (Seoul):** Leafy green/sage stone courtyard, old shade tree, golden hanging lanterns `#F6BD60`.
- **Third-Floor Attic:** Sloped timber beams `#5A3E2E`, warm brass reading lamp.
