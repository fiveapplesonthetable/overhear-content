# 햇살 하우스 (Sunshine House) — Show Bible

## Premise & Setting
Set in **Seoul**, **햇살 하우스** (*Sunshine House*) is an eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning bakery-café. `mina` (미나) begins her five-day trial week at the front desk alongside sceptical veteran handyman `junho` (준호), warm morning baker `sora` (소라), and anxious owner `sungmin` (성민), who constantly loses his glasses on his own forehead.

## Cast & Register Relationships
- **`mina` (미나 / Mina, f, mid-20s):** Resourceful newcomer at the front desk. Uses polite register with guests (`youngmi`, `jihoon`, `minsu`) and owner `sungmin`, and casual register with `sora`.
- **`junho` (준호 / Junho, m, late 40s):** Veteran handyman who trusts his paper ledger and brass keyring over tablets; gruff exterior, warm heart.
- **`sora` (소라 / Sora, f, early 30s):** Morning café baker; immediately warm and informal with colleagues, famous for cinnamon rolls and Friday lemon cake.
- **`sungmin` (성민 / Sungmin, m, late 50s):** Formal, polite owner obsessed with five-star reviews and punctuality.
- **`youngmi` (영미 / Youngmi, f, 60s):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 whose dark-blue suitcase is swapped on Monday's train.
- **`jihoon` (지훈 / Jihoon, m, 30s):** Soft-spoken acoustic architect in the third-floor attic room, secretly hoping to reconcile with his brother.
- **`minsu` (민수 / Minsu, m, late 30s):** `jihoon`'s estranged older brother who works in municipal contracts at City Hall.

## Running Jokes & Recurring Places
- The front door only opens smoothly if you lift the handle first.
- Room 4's old buzzer wire always rings twice on its own.
- `sungmin` searches for his reading glasses while wearing them on his forehead.
- Key places: Front desk, four-table café corner, Seoul courtyard under the shade tree, third-floor attic room, brick boiler cellar, and historic Eunhaeng Lane.

## Season 1 Episode Arc (6 × 8 Scenes)
1. **`s01e01` — 파란 여행 가방 (*The Blue Suitcase*):** Mina arrives for her first morning shift and Junho shows her the trick to the front door and his paper ledger. ... Cliffhanger: Late at night the front bell rings, and Mina and Junho find Youngmi's real suitcase on the step with an anonymous note.
2. **`s01e02` — 여덟 명분 아침 식사 (*Breakfast for Eight*):** On Tuesday morning Youngmi happily recovers her own suitcase while the unclaimed bag stays locked away. ... Cliffhanger: Checking the evening ledger, Sungmin turns pale: he booked two separate guests into Room 7 on a sold-out night.
3. **`s01e03` — 칠호실이 두 번 (*Room Seven Twice*):** Sungmin panics over the double-booked room while Junho unlocks the third-floor staircase. ... Cliffhanger: Coming up from the cellar, Junho delivers bad news: the hot-water boiler valve just cracked for the night.
4. **`s01e04` — 온수가 안 나와요 (*No Hot Water*):** At dawn Junho draws Mina a street map to the old hardware shop across the bridge before guests wake up. ... Cliffhanger: In the courtyard Youngmi describes the quiet younger brother listening from the stairs just as Jihoon walks out and hears her.
5. **`s01e05` — 금요일 정오 (*Friday at Noon*):** Jihoon explains how he accidentally swapped suitcases with Youngmi on Monday's train and panicked at the gate. ... Cliffhanger: As Minsu steps into the courtyard outside, Sungmin opens the landlord's letter inside and turns pale at a sale notice.
6. **`s01e06` — 안마당 등불 (*The Courtyard Lanterns*):** Mina brings tea to the courtyard table as Jihoon uses her three simple sentences to break the ice with Minsu. ... Cliffhanger: Fetching spare café chairs from the cellar, Junho uncovers a locked wooden crate stamped 'Room 9' and a ninth brass key.

## Localization & Cultural Notes
- Seoul Standard Korean (은행길 / Eunhaeng Lane). Polite 해요체/하십시오체 with guests and Sungmin; casual 해체 with Sora. Korean shoe size 280 mm (이백팔십).
