# Bridge Courtyard Inn (Дворик у моста) — Visual Character Sheet

## Recurring Cast
- **`kira` (Кира / Kira):** Newcomer front-desk receptionist (mid-20s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `ponytail` hair `#B85D3B`, signature top `#3B9B96`.
- **`matvey` (Матвей / Matvey):** Veteran handyman & night clerk (late 40s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#5C4A42`, signature top `#4B6584`, moustache.
- **`zoya` (Зоя / Zoya):** Morning café baker (early 30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `bun` hair `#3D2B24`, signature top `#D96B52`.
- **`yaroslav` (Ярослав / Yaroslav):** Anxious owner-manager (late 50s, glasses often on forehead). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#8C8580`, signature top `#574B90`, round glasses.
- **`larisa` (Лариса / Larisa):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 (60s, glasses). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `bun` hair `#D8D3CD`, signature top `#8854D0`, round glasses.
- **`roman` (Роман / Roman):** Quiet acoustic architect in the attic room (30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#2F292B`, signature top `#2D98DA`.
- **`gleb` (Глеб / Gleb):** Older brother from City Hall (late 30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#3A3032`, signature top `#3867D6`, short beard.

## Recurring Places
- **Front Desk:** Warm wood counter `#7A543E`, brass bell `#F2C14E`, paper reservation ledger `#FFF6EA`, slate-blue backdrop.
- **Café Corner:** Caramel morning light `#C98B5E`, woven bread basket, blue teacups.
- **Courtyard (St Petersburg):** Leafy green/sage stone courtyard, old shade tree, golden hanging lanterns `#F6BD60`.
- **Third-Floor Attic:** Sloped timber beams `#5A3E2E`, warm brass reading lamp.
