# Дворик у моста (Bridge Courtyard Inn) — Show Bible

## Premise & Setting
Set in **St Petersburg**, **Дворик у моста** (*Bridge Courtyard Inn*) is an eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning bakery-café. `kira` (Кира) begins her five-day trial week at the front desk alongside sceptical veteran handyman `matvey` (Матвей), warm morning baker `zoya` (Зоя), and anxious owner `yaroslav` (Ярослав), who constantly loses his glasses on his own forehead.

## Cast & Register Relationships
- **`kira` (Кира / Kira, f, mid-20s):** Resourceful newcomer at the front desk. Uses polite register with guests (`larisa`, `roman`, `gleb`) and owner `yaroslav`, and casual register with `zoya`.
- **`matvey` (Матвей / Matvey, m, late 40s):** Veteran handyman who trusts his paper ledger and brass keyring over tablets; gruff exterior, warm heart.
- **`zoya` (Зоя / Zoya, f, early 30s):** Morning café baker; immediately warm and informal with colleagues, famous for cinnamon rolls and Friday lemon cake.
- **`yaroslav` (Ярослав / Yaroslav, m, late 50s):** Formal, polite owner obsessed with five-star reviews and punctuality.
- **`larisa` (Лариса / Larisa, f, 60s):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 whose dark-blue suitcase is swapped on Monday's train.
- **`roman` (Роман / Roman, m, 30s):** Soft-spoken acoustic architect in the third-floor attic room, secretly hoping to reconcile with his brother.
- **`gleb` (Глеб / Gleb, m, late 30s):** `roman`'s estranged older brother who works in municipal contracts at City Hall.

## Running Jokes & Recurring Places
- The front door only opens smoothly if you lift the handle first.
- Room 4's old buzzer wire always rings twice on its own.
- `yaroslav` searches for his reading glasses while wearing them on his forehead.
- Key places: Front desk, four-table café corner, St Petersburg courtyard under the shade tree, third-floor attic room, brick boiler cellar, and historic Lipovy Lane.

## Season 1 Episode Arc (6 × 8 Scenes)
1. **`s01e01` — Синий чемодан (*The Blue Suitcase*):** Kira arrives for her first morning shift and Matvey shows her the trick to the front door and his paper ledger. ... Cliffhanger: Late at night the front bell rings, and Kira and Matvey find Larisa's real suitcase on the step with an anonymous note.
2. **`s01e02` — Завтрак на восьмерых (*Breakfast for Eight*):** On Tuesday morning Larisa happily recovers her own suitcase while the unclaimed bag stays locked away. ... Cliffhanger: Checking the evening ledger, Yaroslav turns pale: he booked two separate guests into Room 7 on a sold-out night.
3. **`s01e03` — Седьмой номер дважды (*Room Seven Twice*):** Yaroslav panics over the double-booked room while Matvey unlocks the third-floor staircase. ... Cliffhanger: Coming up from the cellar, Matvey delivers bad news: the hot-water boiler valve just cracked for the night.
4. **`s01e04` — Нет горячей воды (*No Hot Water*):** At dawn Matvey draws Kira a street map to the old hardware shop across the bridge before guests wake up. ... Cliffhanger: In the courtyard Larisa describes the quiet younger brother listening from the stairs just as Roman walks out and hears her.
5. **`s01e05` — В пятницу в полдень (*Friday at Noon*):** Roman explains how he accidentally swapped suitcases with Larisa on Monday's train and panicked at the gate. ... Cliffhanger: As Gleb steps into the courtyard outside, Yaroslav opens the landlord's letter inside and turns pale at a sale notice.
6. **`s01e06` — Фонарики во дворике (*The Courtyard Lanterns*):** Kira brings tea to the courtyard table as Roman uses her three simple sentences to break the ice with Gleb. ... Cliffhanger: Fetching spare café chairs from the cellar, Matvey uncovers a locked wooden crate stamped 'Room 9' and a ninth brass key.

## Localization & Cultural Notes
- Russian street: Липовый переулок (Lipovy Lane). Shoe size 44. Polite Вы with guests and Yaroslav; casual ты with Zoya.
