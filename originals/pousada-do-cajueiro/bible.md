# Pousada do Cajueiro (Cashew Tree Guesthouse) — Show Bible

## Premise & Setting
Set in **Recife**, **Pousada do Cajueiro** (*Cashew Tree Guesthouse*) is an eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning bakery-café. `camila` (Camila) begins her five-day trial week at the front desk alongside sceptical veteran handyman `jorge` (Jorge), warm morning baker `bianca` (Bianca), and anxious owner `otavio` (Otávio), who constantly loses his glasses on his own forehead.

## Cast & Register Relationships
- **`camila` (Camila / Camila, f, mid-20s):** Resourceful newcomer at the front desk. Uses polite register with guests (`helena`, `rafael`, `tiago`) and owner `otavio`, and casual register with `bianca`.
- **`jorge` (Jorge / Jorge, m, late 40s):** Veteran handyman who trusts his paper ledger and brass keyring over tablets; gruff exterior, warm heart.
- **`bianca` (Bianca / Bianca, f, early 30s):** Morning café baker; immediately warm and informal with colleagues, famous for cinnamon rolls and Friday lemon cake.
- **`otavio` (Otávio / Otavio, m, late 50s):** Formal, polite owner obsessed with five-star reviews and punctuality.
- **`helena` (Helena / Helena, f, 60s):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 whose dark-blue suitcase is swapped on Monday's train.
- **`rafael` (Rafael / Rafael, m, 30s):** Soft-spoken acoustic architect in the third-floor attic room, secretly hoping to reconcile with his brother.
- **`tiago` (Tiago / Tiago, m, late 30s):** `rafael`'s estranged older brother who works in municipal contracts at City Hall.

## Running Jokes & Recurring Places
- The front door only opens smoothly if you lift the handle first.
- Room 4's old buzzer wire always rings twice on its own.
- `otavio` searches for his reading glasses while wearing them on his forehead.
- Key places: Front desk, four-table café corner, Recife courtyard under the shade tree, third-floor attic room, brick boiler cellar, and historic Rua das Acacias.

## Season 1 Episode Arc (6 × 8 Scenes)
1. **`s01e01` — A mala azul (*The Blue Suitcase*):** Camila arrives for her first morning shift and Jorge shows her the trick to the front door and his paper ledger. ... Cliffhanger: Late at night the front bell rings, and Camila and Jorge find Helena's real suitcase on the step with an anonymous note.
2. **`s01e02` — Café da manhã para oito (*Breakfast for Eight*):** On Tuesday morning Helena happily recovers her own suitcase while the unclaimed bag stays locked away. ... Cliffhanger: Checking the evening ledger, Otavio turns pale: he booked two separate guests into Room 7 on a sold-out night.
3. **`s01e03` — Quarto sete duas vezes (*Room Seven Twice*):** Otavio panics over the double-booked room while Jorge unlocks the third-floor staircase. ... Cliffhanger: Coming up from the cellar, Jorge delivers bad news: the hot-water boiler valve just cracked for the night.
4. **`s01e04` — Sem água quente (*No Hot Water*):** At dawn Jorge draws Camila a street map to the old hardware shop across the bridge before guests wake up. ... Cliffhanger: In the courtyard Helena describes the quiet younger brother listening from the stairs just as Rafael walks out and hears her.
5. **`s01e05` — Sexta-feira ao meio-dia (*Friday at Noon*):** Rafael explains how he accidentally swapped suitcases with Helena on Monday's train and panicked at the gate. ... Cliffhanger: As Tiago steps into the courtyard outside, Otavio opens the landlord's letter inside and turns pale at a sale notice.
6. **`s01e06` — As lanternas do pátio (*The Courtyard Lanterns*):** Camila brings tea to the courtyard table as Rafael uses her three simple sentences to break the ice with Tiago. ... Cliffhanger: Fetching spare café chairs from the cellar, Jorge uncovers a locked wooden crate stamped 'Room 9' and a ninth brass key.

## Localization & Cultural Notes
- Brazilian Portuguese (Recife): Rua das Acácias. Brazilian shoe size 42. Tropical climate: in s01e03 Scenes 7-8, adapt the room radiator clue to the hot-water bathroom pipe/shower tap clanking and turning cool (Recife has hot showers via central gas/water boiler, not room heating radiators). Intercity bus/train arrival adapted naturally to intercity coach/station.
