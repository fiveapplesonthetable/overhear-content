# 小院客栈 (Courtyard Inn) — Show Bible

## Premise & Setting
Set in **Chengdu**, **小院客栈** (*Courtyard Inn*) is an eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning bakery-café. `qing` (小晴) begins her five-day trial week at the front desk alongside sceptical veteran handyman `chen` (老陈), warm morning baker `mei` (小美), and anxious owner `zhou` (周经理), who constantly loses his glasses on his own forehead.

## Cast & Register Relationships
- **`qing` (小晴 / Qing, f, mid-20s):** Resourceful newcomer at the front desk. Uses polite register with guests (`song`, `hao`, `peng`) and owner `zhou`, and casual register with `mei`.
- **`chen` (老陈 / Chen, m, late 40s):** Veteran handyman who trusts his paper ledger and brass keyring over tablets; gruff exterior, warm heart.
- **`mei` (小美 / Mei, f, early 30s):** Morning café baker; immediately warm and informal with colleagues, famous for cinnamon rolls and Friday lemon cake.
- **`zhou` (周经理 / Zhou, m, late 50s):** Formal, polite owner obsessed with five-star reviews and punctuality.
- **`song` (宋老师 / Song, f, 60s):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 whose dark-blue suitcase is swapped on Monday's train.
- **`hao` (志豪 / Hao, m, 30s):** Soft-spoken acoustic architect in the third-floor attic room, secretly hoping to reconcile with his brother.
- **`peng` (志鹏 / Peng, m, late 30s):** `hao`'s estranged older brother who works in municipal contracts at City Hall.

## Running Jokes & Recurring Places
- The front door only opens smoothly if you lift the handle first.
- Room 4's old buzzer wire always rings twice on its own.
- `zhou` searches for his reading glasses while wearing them on his forehead.
- Key places: Front desk, four-table café corner, Chengdu courtyard under the shade tree, third-floor attic room, brick boiler cellar, and historic Wutong Lane.

## Season 1 Episode Arc (6 × 8 Scenes)
1. **`s01e01` — 蓝色行李箱 (*The Blue Suitcase*):** Qing arrives for her first morning shift and Chen shows her the trick to the front door and his paper ledger. ... Cliffhanger: Late at night the front bell rings, and Qing and Chen find Song's real suitcase on the step with an anonymous note.
2. **`s01e02` — 八个人的早餐 (*Breakfast for Eight*):** On Tuesday morning Song happily recovers her own suitcase while the unclaimed bag stays locked away. ... Cliffhanger: Checking the evening ledger, Zhou turns pale: he booked two separate guests into Room 7 on a sold-out night.
3. **`s01e03` — 七号房订了两次 (*Room Seven Twice*):** Zhou panics over the double-booked room while Chen unlocks the third-floor staircase. ... Cliffhanger: Coming up from the cellar, Chen delivers bad news: the hot-water boiler valve just cracked for the night.
4. **`s01e04` — 没有热水 (*No Hot Water*):** At dawn Chen draws Qing a street map to the old hardware shop across the bridge before guests wake up. ... Cliffhanger: In the courtyard Song describes the quiet younger brother listening from the stairs just as Hao walks out and hears her.
5. **`s01e05` — 星期五中午 (*Friday at Noon*):** Hao explains how he accidentally swapped suitcases with Song on Monday's train and panicked at the gate. ... Cliffhanger: As Peng steps into the courtyard outside, Zhou opens the landlord's letter inside and turns pale at a sale notice.
6. **`s01e06` — 小院的灯笼 (*The Courtyard Lanterns*):** Qing brings tea to the courtyard table as Hao uses her three simple sentences to break the ice with Peng. ... Cliffhanger: Fetching spare café chairs from the cellar, Chen uncovers a locked wooden crate stamped 'Room 9' and a ninth brass key.

## Localization & Cultural Notes
- Standard Mandarin set in Chengdu (梧桐巷 / Wutong Lane). Polite 您 with Teacher Song and guests; everyday 你 among colleagues. Shoe size 44 (四十四码). In s01e03 Scenes 7-8, adapt the radiator pipe to the hot-water bathroom pipe/tap clanking and cooling.
