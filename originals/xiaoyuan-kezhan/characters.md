# Courtyard Inn (小院客栈) — Visual Character Sheet

## Recurring Cast
- **`qing` (小晴 / Qing):** Newcomer front-desk receptionist (mid-20s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `ponytail` hair `#242024`, signature top `#38A3A5`.
- **`chen` (老陈 / Chen):** Veteran handyman & night clerk (late 40s). Skin `#D9A074`, `short` hair `#464042`, signature top `#4D6A8A`.
- **`mei` (小美 / Mei):** Morning café baker (early 30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `bob` hair `#2E2426`, signature top `#E56B6F`.
- **`zhou` (周经理 / Zhou):** Anxious owner-manager (late 50s, glasses often on forehead). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#94908C`, signature top `#5E60CE`, round glasses.
- **`song` (宋老师 / Song):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 (60s, glasses). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `bun` hair `#DBD7D0`, signature top `#8367C7`, round glasses.
- **`hao` (志豪 / Hao):** Quiet acoustic architect in the attic room (30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#201D22`, signature top `#277DA1`.
- **`peng` (志鹏 / Peng):** Older brother from City Hall (late 30s). Skin `#D9A074`, `short` hair `#1C191E`, signature top `#1D4E89`.

## Recurring Places
- **Front Desk:** Warm wood counter `#7A543E`, brass bell `#F2C14E`, paper reservation ledger `#FFF6EA`, slate-blue backdrop.
- **Café Corner:** Caramel morning light `#C98B5E`, woven bread basket, blue teacups.
- **Courtyard (Chengdu):** Leafy green/sage stone courtyard, old shade tree, golden hanging lanterns `#F6BD60`.
- **Third-Floor Attic:** Sloped timber beams `#5A3E2E`, warm brass reading lamp.
