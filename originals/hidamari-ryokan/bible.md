# ひだまり旅館 (Hidamari Inn) — Show Bible

## Premise & Setting
Set in **Osaka**, **ひだまり旅館** (*Hidamari Inn*) is an eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning bakery-café. `mio` (美央) begins her five-day trial week at the front desk alongside sceptical veteran handyman `kenji` (健二), warm morning baker `yuka` (由香), and anxious owner `takashi` (隆), who constantly loses his glasses on his own forehead.

## Cast & Register Relationships
- **`mio` (美央 / Mio, f, mid-20s):** Resourceful newcomer at the front desk. Uses polite register with guests (`kazuko`, `haruto`, `daiki`) and owner `takashi`, and casual register with `yuka`.
- **`kenji` (健二 / Kenji, m, late 40s):** Veteran handyman who trusts his paper ledger and brass keyring over tablets; gruff exterior, warm heart.
- **`yuka` (由香 / Yuka, f, early 30s):** Morning café baker; immediately warm and informal with colleagues, famous for cinnamon rolls and Friday lemon cake.
- **`takashi` (隆 / Takashi, m, late 50s):** Formal, polite owner obsessed with five-star reviews and punctuality.
- **`kazuko` (和子 / Kazuko, f, 60s):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 whose dark-blue suitcase is swapped on Monday's train.
- **`haruto` (陽翔 / Haruto, m, 30s):** Soft-spoken acoustic architect in the third-floor attic room, secretly hoping to reconcile with his brother.
- **`daiki` (大輝 / Daiki, m, late 30s):** `haruto`'s estranged older brother who works in municipal contracts at City Hall.

## Running Jokes & Recurring Places
- The front door only opens smoothly if you lift the handle first.
- Room 4's old buzzer wire always rings twice on its own.
- `takashi` searches for his reading glasses while wearing them on his forehead.
- Key places: Front desk, four-table café corner, Osaka courtyard under the shade tree, third-floor attic room, brick boiler cellar, and historic Keyaki Lane.

## Season 1 Episode Arc (6 × 8 Scenes)
1. **`s01e01` — 青いスーツケース (*The Blue Suitcase*):** Mio arrives for her first morning shift and Kenji shows her the trick to the front door and his paper ledger. ... Cliffhanger: Late at night the front bell rings, and Mio and Kenji find Kazuko's real suitcase on the step with an anonymous note.
2. **`s01e02` — 八人分の朝ごはん (*Breakfast for Eight*):** On Tuesday morning Kazuko happily recovers her own suitcase while the unclaimed bag stays locked away. ... Cliffhanger: Checking the evening ledger, Takashi turns pale: he booked two separate guests into Room 7 on a sold-out night.
3. **`s01e03` — 七号室がふたつ (*Room Seven Twice*):** Takashi panics over the double-booked room while Kenji unlocks the third-floor staircase. ... Cliffhanger: Coming up from the cellar, Kenji delivers bad news: the hot-water boiler valve just cracked for the night.
4. **`s01e04` — お湯が出ない朝 (*No Hot Water*):** At dawn Kenji draws Mio a street map to the old hardware shop across the bridge before guests wake up. ... Cliffhanger: In the courtyard Kazuko describes the quiet younger brother listening from the stairs just as Haruto walks out and hears her.
5. **`s01e05` — 金曜日の正午 (*Friday at Noon*):** Haruto explains how he accidentally swapped suitcases with Kazuko on Monday's train and panicked at the gate. ... Cliffhanger: As Daiki steps into the courtyard outside, Takashi opens the landlord's letter inside and turns pale at a sale notice.
6. **`s01e06` — 中庭のちょうちん (*The Courtyard Lanterns*):** Mio brings tea to the courtyard table as Haruto uses her three simple sentences to break the ice with Daiki. ... Cliffhanger: Fetching spare café chairs from the cellar, Kenji uncovers a locked wooden crate stamped 'Room 9' and a ninth brass key.

## Localization & Cultural Notes
- Standard polite Japanese (です・ます with guests, warm casual with Yuka) set in Osaka (けやき通り / Keyaki Lane). Shoe size 28 cm (二十八センチ). In s01e03 Scenes 7-8, adapt the heating radiator pipe to the hot-water washbasin/bath pipe clanking and turning cool.
