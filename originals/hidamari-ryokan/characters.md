# Hidamari Inn (ひだまり旅館) — Visual Character Sheet

## Recurring Cast
- **`mio` (美央 / Mio):** Newcomer front-desk receptionist (mid-20s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `bob` hair `#262228`, signature top `#3B9B96`.
- **`kenji` (健二 / Kenji):** Veteran handyman & night clerk (late 40s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#3A363C`, signature top `#486581`.
- **`yuka` (由香 / Yuka):** Morning café baker (early 30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `ponytail` hair `#34282C`, signature top `#E26D5A`.
- **`takashi` (隆 / Takashi):** Anxious owner-manager (late 50s, glasses often on forehead). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#8F8B88`, signature top `#5C5470`, round glasses.
- **`kazuko` (和子 / Kazuko):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 (60s, glasses). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `bun` hair `#DAD6D2`, signature top `#845EC2`, round glasses.
- **`haruto` (陽翔 / Haruto):** Quiet acoustic architect in the attic room (30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#221E24`, signature top `#2C73D2`.
- **`daiki` (大輝 / Daiki):** Older brother from City Hall (late 30s). Skin `#F6D2B5`, `short` hair `#1E1B20`, signature top `#1F4E78`.

## Recurring Places
- **Front Desk:** Warm wood counter `#7A543E`, brass bell `#F2C14E`, paper reservation ledger `#FFF6EA`, slate-blue backdrop.
- **Café Corner:** Caramel morning light `#C98B5E`, woven bread basket, blue teacups.
- **Courtyard (Osaka):** Leafy green/sage stone courtyard, old shade tree, golden hanging lanterns `#F6BD60`.
- **Third-Floor Attic:** Sloped timber beams `#5A3E2E`, warm brass reading lamp.
