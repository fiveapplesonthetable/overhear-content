## Episode 1: The Tin Box Under the Tree

### Scene 1: Three Stones North (courtyard) — F1, M1, M2
- **Summary:** At dawn on Saturday before the upstairs guests wake, F1, M1, and M2 count three flat flagstones north from the courtyard shade tree.
- **Intro:** Saturday dawn breaks softly over {SHOW} in {CITY}. While the upstairs guests are still asleep, {F1}, {M1}, and {M2} tiptoe out to the quiet courtyard carrying a brass lantern and a flat iron bar. Listen first.
  - **M2:** Whisper, both of you—it is only half past six on Saturday morning!
  - **F1:** Help me lift table number one aside gently so the legs do not scrape.
  - **M1:** Read the postcard clue one more time before I touch the stones, {F1}.
  - **F1:** The retired mapmaker wrote: Count three flat stones north from the old shade tree.
  - **M2:** Which way is north in my own courtyard? Where did my glasses go?
  - **F1:** Your glasses are sitting above your eyebrows, {M2}, and north faces the front gate.
  - **M1:** Stone one, stone two, and here is stone three with the loose mortar edge.
  - **M2:** Tap the middle with your wooden handle, {M1}—does it really sound hollow?
  - **M1:** Listen to that clear echo underneath: there is a small space below this stone.
  - **F1:** Nobody has lifted this flagstone since that rainy May thirty summers ago!
  - **M2:** Please be careful, {M1}, because replacement courtyard stones cost a small fortune!
  - **M1:** Stand back half a step while I slide the flat bar under the corner.
- **Outro:** Three flat stones north of the old tree, the hollow flagstone is ready to rise after thirty quiet years. See you in the next scene.

- **Outro:** Brick walls, dry sand, and thick waxed cloth kept the thirty-year-old parcel safe below the courtyard stones. See you next time.
