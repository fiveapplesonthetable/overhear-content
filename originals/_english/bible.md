# Courtyard Inn — Master English Bible (Seasons 1–2)

## Premise
An eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning café on the ground floor. A resourceful newcomer in her mid-twenties starts her trial week at the front desk alongside a dry-humoured veteran handyman who distrusts computers, a cheerful morning baker who knows every regular on the street, and an anxious owner who constantly misplaces his reading glasses on his own head.

## Tone & Level
Warm, observant, gently comic everyday life at **CEFR A1**. Stakes are human and domestic: sticking doors, swapped suitcases, empty milk jugs before breakfast, a double-booked room on a rainy night, a silent boiler, and two estranged brothers reuniting under courtyard lanterns. Every episode ends on a small, genuine cliffhanger that pulls the listener into the next episode.

## Cast Archetypes (Localized Idiomatically per Language Pair)
- **`F1` (Newcomer Receptionist, female, mid-20s):** Polite with guests and older strangers, quick on her feet, observant, learning the quirks of the old house on her trial week.
- **`M1` (Veteran Handyman & Night Clerk, male, late 40s):** Fifteen years in the building; carries a heavy brass keyring and a paper notebook, grumbles about tablets, fixes everything, secretly kind.
- **`F2` (Morning Café Baker, female, early 30s):** Runs the four-table breakfast corner; speaks casually to friends right away, generous with warm bread and neighbourhood news.
- **`M2` (Owner-Manager, male, late 50s):** Formal, polite, perpetually worried about budgets and five-star online reviews; loses his glasses three times a day.
- **`F3` (Room 4 Guest — Retired Piano Teacher, female, 60s):** Calm, witty traveller arriving by train with a blue suitcase.
- **`M3` (Room 7 / Attic Guest — Quiet Acoustic Architect, male, 30s):** Soft-spoken guest with a leather notebook and tape measure, harbouring a personal reason for visiting the street.
- **`M4` (Older Brother, male, late 30s, Episode 6):** Arrives at the courtyard gate after three years of silence.

*Scene-speaker rule:* Every scene uses **2 or 3 speaking characters** (never more than 2 female or 2 male speakers in a single scene), guaranteeing distinct voices across every language pool.

## Recurring Places & Running Jokes
- **The Front Desk:** Brass bell, paper reservation ledger, and a wooden key board for Rooms 1 to 8. The front door handle only opens smoothly if you lift it slightly first.
- **The Four-Table Café Corner:** Smells of cinnamon rolls, fresh bread, and strong morning coffee or tea.
- **The Courtyard:** Paved stones, one old shade tree, a pot of fresh kitchen herbs, and strings of warm evening lanterns.
- **Running Jokes:** `M2`'s reading glasses sitting on his forehead while he searches for them; Room 4's desk buzzer always ringing twice; `M1` trusting his pencil more than any screen.

## Season 1 Arc (6 Episodes × 8 Scenes)
1. **Episode 1 — *The Blue Suitcase*:** `F1` starts her first morning shift, checks `F3` into Room 4, discovers `F3`'s blue suitcase was swapped at the train station for an identical bag holding a wooden toy train, and ends the night finding the *second* blue suitcase left anonymously on the courtyard step with a note: *"Please keep this until Friday."*
2. **Episode 2 — *Breakfast for Eight*:** `F3` gets her own bag back while the mystery suitcase goes into the closet; when the morning milk delivery fails, `F1` races to the corner market with cash, mixes up salted butter, and answers a mysterious phone call from a man promising to collect the blue suitcase on Friday at noon. That evening `M2` realises he double-booked Room 7 in a full house.
3. **Episode 3 — *Room Seven Twice*:** Before the second Room 7 guest arrives in the rain, `F1`, `M1`, and `F2` turn the quiet third-floor attic study into a cosy guest room; `M3` happily accepts the attic, but leaves behind a leather notebook filled with room measurements and ratings, convincing the staff he is a secret city travel-guide inspector—just as the upstairs radiator goes ice-cold.
4. **Episode 4 — *No Hot Water*:** At dawn the courtyard boiler stops; `M1` sends `F1` with street-by-street directions to buy a brass valve while `F2` stalls the guests with hot pastries. Once the water is hot again, `M2` confesses they think `M3` is an inspector—and `M3` laughs, explaining he is an acoustic architect studying quiet courtyards. Then `F3` spots the wooden toy train tag and recognises the address from twenty-five years ago.
5. **Episode 5 — *Friday at Noon*:** At noon on Friday, `M3` admits *he* left the blue suitcase on Monday: it belongs to his estranged older brother `M4`, and he feared `M4` wouldn't accept their childhood train set directly. `F3` realises she once taught piano on their street, the staff prepare a small courtyard welcome with lemon cake and lanterns, and `F1` coaches `M3` through a simple greeting—just as `M4`'s taxi arrives and `M2` opens a letter putting the building's lease up for sale.
6. **Episode 6 — *The Courtyard Lanterns*:** `M3` and `M4` reconcile warmly at the gate while `M2` hides the scary lease letter; when `F1` brings everyone together, `M3` reads page two and spots a city heritage clause protecting public neighbourhood cafés and guesthouses. `M2` offers `F1` the permanent desk job, `F2` plans a true courtyard café, and in the cellar `M1` uncovers an old wooden crate stencilled **"Room 9"**—in a house with only eight rooms.

## Vocabulary Progression Across Season 1
- **E1:** Greetings, names, polite vs. casual "you", jobs, numbers 1–8, door, key, room, floor, suitcase, tea, thank you.
- **E2:** Food & breakfast (bread, milk, butter, eggs, cheese, apple, sugar, coffee), money/prices, wanting & needing.
- **E3:** Upstairs/downstairs, furniture (bed, lamp, table, chair, pillow, towel, window), rain, tired/warm/dry, apologies.
- **E4:** Street directions (left, right, straight ahead, bridge, square, pharmacy, shop), hot/cold, broken/working, waiting.
- **E5:** Family (brother, mother, family), days of the week, clock times, feelings (nervous, happy, ready), cake & music.
- **E6:** Celebrations, staying/working, letters/pages, old/new, gratitude, and cumulative active review of E1–E5 vocabulary.


## Season 2 Arc — *Room Nine* (6 Episodes × 8 Scenes)
1. **Episode 1 — *Behind the Bookcase*:** Opening the cellar's `"Room 9"` cedar chest at dawn on Saturday, `F1`, `M1`, and `M2` find a heavy key, old postcards, and a floor plan pointing behind the hallway bookcase. Moving the books reveals a forgotten glass garden sunroom containing a walnut upright piano with one silent middle key (`D`)—until `M2` flips the old brass switch and pops the ground-floor fuse.
2. **Episode 2 — *The Saturday Market*:** While `M1` rewires Room 9 with new copper wire, `F1` and `F2` shop at the Saturday street market with `M4` and `M3` for fragrant lemons, honey, and twelve stoneware teacups. Back home, `M2` misreads his order sheet without his glasses and gets twenty-four folding chairs delivered early—just as the newly lit sunroom lamp reveals an unfinished handwritten `"Courtyard Waltz"` from May thirty years ago tucked inside the piano lid, and the city clerk moves the heritage inspection up to Wednesday at three.
3. **Episode 3 — *The Silent Key*:** Reading `M2`'s aunt's green guest book from thirty years ago explains why a rainy night locked Room 9 mid-song; `F3` offers to compose the missing sixteen bars, `M1` inspects the slipped felt hammer pin on middle `D`, `F1`, `M3`, and `M4` wash the arched glass, and `F1` and `M1` rescue the sanded wooden shutters seconds before a midnight downpour.
4. **Episode 4 — *Green Shutters and Lemons*:** With rain falling on Monday, `M3` sets up dry indoor airflow so `F1` and `M1` can paint the shutters green (`M2` promptly streaks green paint across the glasses on his forehead). On Tuesday `M1` restores middle `D` on the piano and hangs the shutters, `F1` makes welcome signs, `F2` twists her ankle stepping off a low stool and assigns Wednesday baking stations from two pillows, and `M2` refills two unlabeled white ceramic jars with salt and sugar.
5. **Episode 5 — *Wednesday at Three*:** After labelling the salt and sugar jars in red, the crew bakes crusty loaves, golden lentil soup, and twenty almond lemon tarts under `F2`'s seated command. At three sharp the city heritage review approves and stamps the public café protection, and as `F3` plays the completed `"Courtyard Waltz"` through the open green shutters, an older neighbour pauses at the gate humming along—the violinist from number ten who played Friday duets with `M2`'s aunt thirty years ago.
6. **Episode 6 — *Room Nine Is Open*:** On Friday evening `F2` is back on her feet squeezing forty lemons, `M1` hangs brass hook `#9` on the front-desk board, and twenty-four neighbours fill every single one of `M2`'s bargain chairs as piano and violin play at six o'clock. `M3` rents a ground-floor acoustic design studio down the street where `M4` joins him after City Hall hours with their grandfather's woodworking tools, `F3` extends her stay in Room 4, and a 30th-anniversary May postcard from a former Room 8 guest reveals a tin time capsule hidden under the third courtyard stone from the old tree.

## Vocabulary Progression Across Season 2 (CEFR A1 → A1+)
- **E1:** Furniture & rooms (bookcase, shelf, lid, glass, cloth, piano, switch, wire, dark/light), counting & simple budgets.
- **E2:** Street market shopping (basket, kilo, lemon, honey, cup, saucer, dozen, half-price, fold/unfold), paper & handwriting.
- **E3:** Days & weather (rain, thunder, dry/wet, bucket, soap, sponge, oil), music & sound, soup & tasting adjectives.
- **E4:** Colours & repairs (green, white, brush, paint, dry, sign, price), body & care (foot, ankle, shoe, ice, pillow, rest).
- **E5:** Kitchen actions (dough, stir, boil, sweet/salty, oven), hosting & welcoming guests, official approval, street neighbours.
- **E6:** Celebrations, selling out, instruments (piano, violin), neighbours & workshops, postcards/stamps, cumulative review of S1–S2.
