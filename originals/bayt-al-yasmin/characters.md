# Jasmine Courtyard House (بيت الياسمين) — Visual Character Sheet

## Recurring Cast
- **`nour` (نور / Nour):** Newcomer front-desk receptionist (mid-20s). Skin `#D9A074`, `long` hair `#261E22`, signature top `#2A9D8F`.
- **`tariq` (طارق / Tariq):** Veteran handyman & night clerk (late 40s). Skin `#D9A074`, `short` hair `#3D3538`, signature top `#457B9D`, moustache.
- **`salma` (سلمى / Salma):** Morning café baker (early 30s). Skin `#D9A074`, `bun` hair `#2E2327`, signature top `#E76F51`.
- **`mazen` (مازن / Mazen):** Anxious owner-manager (late 50s, glasses often on forehead). Skin `#D9A074`, `short` hair `#96918C`, signature top `#5E548E`, round glasses.
- **`suad` (سعاد / Suad):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 (60s, glasses). Skin `#D9A074`, `bun` hair `#D8D2CB`, signature top `#8A5A9E`, round glasses.
- **`rami` (رامي / Rami):** Quiet acoustic architect in the attic room (30s). Skin `#D9A074`, `short` hair `#241D20`, signature top `#3A86FF`.
- **`ziad` (زياد / Ziad):** Older brother from City Hall (late 30s). Skin `#D9A074`, `short` hair `#20191C`, signature top `#264653`, short beard.

## Recurring Places
- **Front Desk:** Warm wood counter `#7A543E`, brass bell `#F2C14E`, paper reservation ledger `#FFF6EA`, slate-blue backdrop.
- **Café Corner:** Caramel morning light `#C98B5E`, woven bread basket, blue teacups.
- **Courtyard (Amman):** Leafy green/sage stone courtyard, old shade tree, golden hanging lanterns `#F6BD60`.
- **Third-Floor Attic:** Sloped timber beams `#5A3E2E`, warm brass reading lamp.
