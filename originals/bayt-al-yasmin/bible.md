# بيت الياسمين (Jasmine Courtyard House) — Show Bible

## Premise & Setting
Set in **Amman**, **بيت الياسمين** (*Jasmine Courtyard House*) is an eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning bakery-café. `nour` (نور) begins her five-day trial week at the front desk alongside sceptical veteran handyman `tariq` (طارق), warm morning baker `salma` (سلمى), and anxious owner `mazen` (مازن), who constantly loses his glasses on his own forehead.

## Cast & Register Relationships
- **`nour` (نور / Nour, f, mid-20s):** Resourceful newcomer at the front desk. Uses polite register with guests (`suad`, `rami`, `ziad`) and owner `mazen`, and casual register with `salma`.
- **`tariq` (طارق / Tariq, m, late 40s):** Veteran handyman who trusts his paper ledger and brass keyring over tablets; gruff exterior, warm heart.
- **`salma` (سلمى / Salma, f, early 30s):** Morning café baker; immediately warm and informal with colleagues, famous for cinnamon rolls and Friday lemon cake.
- **`mazen` (مازن / Mazen, m, late 50s):** Formal, polite owner obsessed with five-star reviews and punctuality.
- **`suad` (سعاد / Suad, f, 60s):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 whose dark-blue suitcase is swapped on Monday's intercity bus.
- **`rami` (رامي / Rami, m, 30s):** Soft-spoken acoustic architect in the third-floor attic room, secretly hoping to reconcile with his brother.
- **`ziad` (زياد / Ziad, m, late 30s):** `rami`'s estranged older brother who works in municipal contracts at City Hall.

## Running Jokes & Recurring Places
- The front door only opens smoothly if you lift the handle first.
- Room 4's old buzzer wire always rings twice on its own.
- `mazen` searches for his reading glasses while wearing them on his forehead.
- Key places: Front desk, four-table café corner, Amman courtyard under the shade tree, third-floor attic room, brick boiler cellar, and historic Jasmine Street.

## Season 1 Episode Arc (6 × 8 Scenes)
1. **`s01e01` — الحقيبة الزرقاء (*The Blue Suitcase*):** Nour arrives for her first morning shift and Tariq shows her the trick to the front door and his paper ledger. ... Cliffhanger: Late at night the front bell rings, and Nour and Tariq find Suad's real suitcase on the step with an anonymous note.
2. **`s01e02` — فطور لثمانية (*Breakfast for Eight*):** On Tuesday morning Suad happily recovers her own suitcase while the unclaimed bag stays locked away. ... Cliffhanger: Checking the evening ledger, Mazen turns pale: he booked two separate guests into Room 7 on a sold-out night.
3. **`s01e03` — الغرفة السابعة مرتين (*Room Seven Twice*):** Mazen panics over the double-booked room while Tariq unlocks the third-floor staircase. ... Cliffhanger: Coming up from the cellar, Tariq delivers bad news: the hot-water boiler valve just cracked for the night.
4. **`s01e04` — ما في مي سخنة (*No Hot Water*):** At dawn Tariq draws Nour a street map to the old hardware shop past the downtown overpass before guests wake up. ... Cliffhanger: In the courtyard Suad describes the quiet younger brother listening from the stairs just as Rami walks out and hears her.
5. **`s01e05` — يوم الجمعة الظهر (*Friday at Noon*):** Rami explains how he accidentally swapped suitcases with Suad on Monday's intercity bus and panicked at the gate. ... Cliffhanger: As Ziad steps into the courtyard outside, Mazen opens the landlord's letter inside and turns pale at a sale notice.
6. **`s01e06` — فوانيس الساحة (*The Courtyard Lanterns*):** Nour brings tea to the courtyard table as Rami uses her three simple sentences to break the ice with Ziad. ... Cliffhanger: Fetching spare café chairs from the cellar, Tariq uncovers a locked wooden crate stamped 'Room 9' and a ninth brass key.

## Localization & Cultural Notes
- Spoken Jordanian/Levantine Arabic in Amman (شارع الياسمين / Jasmine Street). Cultural adaptations: intercity coach/bus station (محطة الباصات) instead of train platform; stone stairs/overpass across the downtown valley (الدرج الحجري / الجسر) instead of an open river in s01e04; municipal clock tower chiming twelve (برج الساعة) instead of church clock in s01e05.
