# Courtyard Haveli (आँगन हवेली) — Visual Character Sheet

## Recurring Cast
- **`kavya` (काव्या / Kavya):** Newcomer front-desk receptionist (mid-20s). Skin `#C6885E`, `long` hair `#221B1F`, signature kurti `#2A9D8F`.
- **`pratap` (प्रताप / Pratap):** Veteran handyman & night clerk (late 40s). Skin `#B87A52`, `short` hair `#362F33`, signature shirt `#457B9D`, neat moustache, brass keyring at belt.
- **`diya` (दीया / Diya):** Morning café baker (early 30s). Skin `#C98C63`, `bun` hair `#281F23`, signature top `#E76F51`, linen apron.
- **`mohan` (मोहन / Mohan):** Anxious owner-manager (late 50s, reading glasses often resting on forehead). Skin `#C2855B`, `short` hair `#8E8883`, signature waistcoat `#5E548E` over cream shirt, round wire glasses.
- **`sudha` (सुधा / Sudha):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 (60s, glasses). Skin `#C88D66`, `bun` hair `#D4CEC7`, signature shawl & kurta `#8A5A9E`, round glasses.
- **`kabir` (कबीर / Kabir):** Quiet acoustic architect in the third-floor attic study (30s). Skin `#BF8259`, `short` hair `#201A1D`, signature linen shirt `#3A86FF`, brown leather notebook.
- **`dev` (देव / Dev):** Older brother from Jaipur City Hall (late 30s). Skin `#BA7D55`, `short` hair `#1E181B`, signature jacket `#264653`, neatly trimmed beard.

## Recurring Places
- **Front Desk:** Warm sheesham wood counter `#7A543E`, brass bell `#F2C14E`, paper reservation register `#FFF6EA`, wooden board with eight brass keys.
- **Café Corner:** Warm morning sunlight `#C98B5E`, four wooden tables, woven bread basket, blue ceramic teacups.
- **Courtyard (Jaipur):** Sandstone-paved haveli courtyard, old neem shade tree, clay pot of fresh coriander and mint, warm evening lanterns `#F6BD60`.
- **Third-Floor Attic Study (`बरसाती कमरा`):** Sloped timber ceiling beams `#5A3E2E`, wide wooden desk under the courtyard window, warm brass reading lamp.
