# Locanda del Tiglio (Linden Courtyard Inn) — Show Bible

## Premise & Setting
Set in **Bologna**, **Locanda del Tiglio** (*Linden Courtyard Inn*) is an eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning bakery-café. `chiara` (Chiara) begins her five-day trial week at the front desk alongside sceptical veteran handyman `bruno` (Bruno), warm morning baker `giulia` (Giulia), and anxious owner `cesare` (Cesare), who constantly loses his glasses on his own forehead.

## Cast & Register Relationships
- **`chiara` (Chiara / Chiara, f, mid-20s):** Resourceful newcomer at the front desk. Uses polite register with guests (`lucia`, `davide`, `paolo`) and owner `cesare`, and casual register with `giulia`.
- **`bruno` (Bruno / Bruno, m, late 40s):** Veteran handyman who trusts his paper ledger and brass keyring over tablets; gruff exterior, warm heart.
- **`giulia` (Giulia / Giulia, f, early 30s):** Morning café baker; immediately warm and informal with colleagues, famous for cinnamon rolls and Friday lemon cake.
- **`cesare` (Cesare / Cesare, m, late 50s):** Formal, polite owner obsessed with five-star reviews and punctuality.
- **`lucia` (Lucia / Lucia, f, 60s):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 whose dark-blue suitcase is swapped on Monday's train.
- **`davide` (Davide / Davide, m, 30s):** Soft-spoken acoustic architect in the third-floor attic room, secretly hoping to reconcile with his brother.
- **`paolo` (Paolo / Paolo, m, late 30s):** `davide`'s estranged older brother who works in municipal contracts at City Hall.

## Running Jokes & Recurring Places
- The front door only opens smoothly if you lift the handle first.
- Room 4's old buzzer wire always rings twice on its own.
- `cesare` searches for his reading glasses while wearing them on his forehead.
- Key places: Front desk, four-table café corner, Bologna courtyard under the shade tree, third-floor attic room, brick boiler cellar, and historic Via dei Tigli.

## Season 1 Episode Arc (6 × 8 Scenes)
1. **`s01e01` — La valigia blu (*The Blue Suitcase*):** Chiara arrives for her first morning shift and Bruno shows her the trick to the front door and his paper ledger. ... Cliffhanger: Late at night the front bell rings, and Chiara and Bruno find Lucia's real suitcase on the step with an anonymous note.
2. **`s01e02` — Colazione per otto (*Breakfast for Eight*):** On Tuesday morning Lucia happily recovers her own suitcase while the unclaimed bag stays locked away. ... Cliffhanger: Checking the evening ledger, Cesare turns pale: he booked two separate guests into Room 7 on a sold-out night.
3. **`s01e03` — Camera sette due volte (*Room Seven Twice*):** Cesare panics over the double-booked room while Bruno unlocks the third-floor staircase. ... Cliffhanger: Coming up from the cellar, Bruno delivers bad news: the hot-water boiler valve just cracked for the night.
4. **`s01e04` — Senza acqua calda (*No Hot Water*):** At dawn Bruno draws Chiara a street map to the old hardware shop across the bridge before guests wake up. ... Cliffhanger: In the courtyard Lucia describes the quiet younger brother listening from the stairs just as Davide walks out and hears her.
5. **`s01e05` — Venerdì a mezzogiorno (*Friday at Noon*):** Davide explains how he accidentally swapped suitcases with Lucia on Monday's train and panicked at the gate. ... Cliffhanger: As Paolo steps into the courtyard outside, Cesare opens the landlord's letter inside and turns pale at a sale notice.
6. **`s01e06` — Le lanterne del cortile (*The Courtyard Lanterns*):** Chiara brings tea to the courtyard table as Davide uses her three simple sentences to break the ice with Paolo. ... Cliffhanger: Fetching spare café chairs from the cellar, Bruno uncovers a locked wooden crate stamped 'Room 9' and a ninth brass key.

## Localization & Cultural Notes
- Italian street: Via dei Tigli. Shoe size 44. Polite Lei with guests; casual tu with Giulia and Bruno.
