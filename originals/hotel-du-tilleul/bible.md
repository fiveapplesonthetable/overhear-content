# Hôtel du Tilleul (Linden Courtyard Hotel) — Show Bible

## Premise & Setting
Set in **Lyon**, **Hôtel du Tilleul** (*Linden Courtyard Hotel*) is an eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning bakery-café. `claire` (Claire) begins her five-day trial week at the front desk alongside sceptical veteran handyman `henri` (Henri), warm morning baker `nadia` (Nadia), and anxious owner `etienne` (Étienne), who constantly loses his glasses on his own forehead.

## Cast & Register Relationships
- **`claire` (Claire / Claire, f, mid-20s):** Resourceful newcomer at the front desk. Uses polite register with guests (`odette`, `antoine`, `thibault`) and owner `etienne`, and casual register with `nadia`.
- **`henri` (Henri / Henri, m, late 40s):** Veteran handyman who trusts his paper ledger and brass keyring over tablets; gruff exterior, warm heart.
- **`nadia` (Nadia / Nadia, f, early 30s):** Morning café baker; immediately warm and informal with colleagues, famous for cinnamon rolls and Friday lemon cake.
- **`etienne` (Étienne / Etienne, m, late 50s):** Formal, polite owner obsessed with five-star reviews and punctuality.
- **`odette` (Odette / Odette, f, 60s):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 whose dark-blue suitcase is swapped on Monday's train.
- **`antoine` (Antoine / Antoine, m, 30s):** Soft-spoken acoustic architect in the third-floor attic room, secretly hoping to reconcile with his brother.
- **`thibault` (Thibault / Thibault, m, late 30s):** `antoine`'s estranged older brother who works in municipal contracts at City Hall.

## Running Jokes & Recurring Places
- The front door only opens smoothly if you lift the handle first.
- Room 4's old buzzer wire always rings twice on its own.
- `etienne` searches for his reading glasses while wearing them on his forehead.
- Key places: Front desk, four-table café corner, Lyon courtyard under the shade tree, third-floor attic room, brick boiler cellar, and historic Rue des Tilleuls.

## Season 1 Episode Arc (6 × 8 Scenes)
1. **`s01e01` — La valise bleue (*The Blue Suitcase*):** Claire arrives for her first morning shift and Henri shows her the trick to the front door and his paper ledger. ... Cliffhanger: Late at night the front bell rings, and Claire and Henri find Odette's real suitcase on the step with an anonymous note.
2. **`s01e02` — Petit-déjeuner pour huit (*Breakfast for Eight*):** On Tuesday morning Odette happily recovers her own suitcase while the unclaimed bag stays locked away. ... Cliffhanger: Checking the evening ledger, Etienne turns pale: he booked two separate guests into Room 7 on a sold-out night.
3. **`s01e03` — Deux fois la chambre sept (*Room Seven Twice*):** Etienne panics over the double-booked room while Henri unlocks the third-floor staircase. ... Cliffhanger: Coming up from the cellar, Henri delivers bad news: the hot-water boiler valve just cracked for the night.
4. **`s01e04` — Plus d'eau chaude (*No Hot Water*):** At dawn Henri draws Claire a street map to the old hardware shop across the bridge before guests wake up. ... Cliffhanger: In the courtyard Odette describes the quiet younger brother listening from the stairs just as Antoine walks out and hears her.
5. **`s01e05` — Vendredi à midi (*Friday at Noon*):** Antoine explains how he accidentally swapped suitcases with Odette on Monday's train and panicked at the gate. ... Cliffhanger: As Thibault steps into the courtyard outside, Etienne opens the landlord's letter inside and turns pale at a sale notice.
6. **`s01e06` — Les lanternes de la cour (*The Courtyard Lanterns*):** Claire brings tea to the courtyard table as Antoine uses her three simple sentences to break the ice with Thibault. ... Cliffhanger: Fetching spare café chairs from the cellar, Henri uncovers a locked wooden crate stamped 'Room 9' and a ninth brass key.

## Localization & Cultural Notes
- French street: Rue des Tilleuls. Shoe size 44. Polite vous with guests and Étienne; casual tu with Nadia.
