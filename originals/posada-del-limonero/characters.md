# Lemon Tree Inn (Posada del Limonero) — Visual Character Sheet

- **Front Desk:** Warm wood counter `#7A543E`, brass bell `#F2C14E`, paper reservation ledger `#FFF6EA`, slate-blue backdrop.

- **Courtyard (Valencia):** Leafy green/sage stone courtyard, old shade tree, golden hanging lanterns `#F6BD60`.
