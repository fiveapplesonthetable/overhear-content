# Posada del Limonero (Lemon Tree Inn) — Show Bible

## Premise & Setting
Set in **Valencia**, **Posada del Limonero** (*Lemon Tree Inn*) is an eight-room neighbourhood guesthouse built around a quiet paved courtyard with a four-table morning bakery-café. `clara` (Clara) begins her five-day trial week at the front desk alongside sceptical veteran handyman `mateo` (Mateo), warm morning baker `rocio` (Rocío), and anxious owner `ernesto` (Ernesto), who constantly loses his glasses on his own forehead.

## Cast & Register Relationships
- **`clara` (Clara / Clara, f, mid-20s):** Resourceful newcomer at the front desk. Uses polite register with guests (`carmen`, `javier`, `tomas`) and owner `ernesto`, and casual register with `rocio`.
- **`mateo` (Mateo / Mateo, m, late 40s):** Veteran handyman who trusts his paper ledger and brass keyring over tablets; gruff exterior, warm heart.
- **`rocio` (Rocío / Rocio, f, early 30s):** Morning café baker; immediately warm and informal with colleagues, famous for cinnamon rolls and Friday lemon cake.
- **`ernesto` (Ernesto / Ernesto, m, late 50s):** Formal, polite owner obsessed with five-star reviews and punctuality.
- **`carmen` (Carmen / Carmen, f, 60s):** Retired piano teacher in Room 4 whose dark-blue suitcase is swapped on Monday's train.
- **`javier` (Javier / Javier, m, 30s):** Soft-spoken acoustic architect in the third-floor attic room, secretly hoping to reconcile with his brother.
- **`tomas` (Tomás / Tomas, m, late 30s):** `javier`'s estranged older brother who works in municipal contracts at City Hall.

## Running Jokes & Recurring Places
- The front door only opens smoothly if you lift the handle first.
- Room 4's old buzzer wire always rings twice on its own.
- `ernesto` searches for his reading glasses while wearing them on his forehead.
- Key places: Front desk, four-table café corner, Valencia courtyard under the shade tree, third-floor attic room, brick boiler cellar, and historic Calle de los Tilos.

## Season 1 Episode Arc (6 × 8 Scenes)
1. **`s01e01` — La maleta azul (*The Blue Suitcase*):** Clara arrives for her first morning shift and Mateo shows her the trick to the front door and his paper ledger. ... Cliffhanger: Late at night the front bell rings, and Clara and Mateo find Carmen's real suitcase on the step with an anonymous note.
2. **`s01e02` — Desayuno para ocho (*Breakfast for Eight*):** On Tuesday morning Carmen happily recovers her own suitcase while the unclaimed bag stays locked away. ... Cliffhanger: Checking the evening ledger, Ernesto turns pale: he booked two separate guests into Room 7 on a sold-out night.
3. **`s01e03` — Dos veces la siete (*Room Seven Twice*):** Ernesto panics over the double-booked room while Mateo unlocks the third-floor staircase. ... Cliffhanger: Coming up from the cellar, Mateo delivers bad news: the hot-water boiler valve just cracked for the night.
4. **`s01e04` — Sin agua caliente (*No Hot Water*):** At dawn Mateo draws Clara a street map to the old hardware shop across the bridge before guests wake up. ... Cliffhanger: In the courtyard Carmen describes the quiet younger brother listening from the stairs just as Javier walks out and hears her.
5. **`s01e05` — El viernes al mediodía (*Friday at Noon*):** Javier explains how he accidentally swapped suitcases with Carmen on Monday's train and panicked at the gate. ... Cliffhanger: As Tomas steps into the courtyard outside, Ernesto opens the landlord's letter inside and turns pale at a sale notice.
6. **`s01e06` — Los farolillos del patio (*The Courtyard Lanterns*):** Clara brings tea to the courtyard table as Javier uses her three simple sentences to break the ice with Tomas. ... Cliffhanger: Fetching spare café chairs from the cellar, Mateo uncovers a locked wooden crate stamped 'Room 9' and a ninth brass key.

## Localization & Cultural Notes
- Spanish street: Calle de los Tilos. Shoe size 44. Polite usted with Carmen and new guests; casual tú among staff.
