# paquita-salas: characters and places

Show: *Paquita Salas*, Flooxer / Netflix. PS Management is a small, old-school talent agency in Madrid run by veteran agent Paquita Salas with her loyal assistant Magüi Moreno. Language: Castilian Spanish (`es-en`).

Role keys: one character = one role key = the character's own name, in every scene of the show. The machine-readable cast is `cast.json` next to this file (key, display name in Spanish, English narration names, gender); it is the only source for keys and labels. There are no viewpoint slot keys and scripts carry no `voices`: the renderer picks voices from `gender`. Grammar always follows the character's gender. Rules: `spec/quality.md` "Cast".

## Paquita Salas (founder and agent at PS Management; first seen s01e01)

Her character's gender is female; grammar and voice casting are feminine throughout.
- Role key: `paquita` (see `cast.json`). Label: Paquita.
- Speech: warm, maternal, blunt, confident Castilian *tú* (`hija`, `cariño`, `venga`); old-fashioned about technology and fiercely devoted to her actors.
