# alrawabi-school: characters and places

Show: *مدرسة الروابي للبنات* (*AlRawabi School for Girls*). Set at an elite private girls' high school in Amman, Jordan, where quiet model student Mariam, loyal Dina, and independent newcomer Noaf push back against popular bully Layan and her clique. Language: Jordanian Arabic (`ar-en`).

Role keys: one character = one role key = the character's own name, lowercase Latin. `cast.json` next to this file is the only source for keys, labels, English names and gender (spec/quality.md "Cast").

## Mariam (quiet model student pushed past her breaking point; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: age 16, slim build, light skin `#F6D2B5`, rosy blush `#F0808A`, thick dark-brown wavy hair `#3A2620` pulled half-back with neat bangs, tidy AlRawabi school uniform (pastel-pink collared shirt `#F4C2D7`, navy-blue blazer `#2C3E6B`, navy pleated skirt), holding a cream notebook `#FFF6EA` or backpack strap.
- Role key: `mariam` (see `cast.json`). Label: مريم.
- Speech: polite and earnest with teachers (`مس فاتن`), natural everyday Jordanian Arabic with Dina and Noaf, turning quiet and determined once wronged ("مش بعد اليوم.", "مش كل إشي بتشوفه العين هو الحقيقة.").

## Layan (charismatic, feared leader of the popular clique; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: age 16, tall and confident, medium olive skin `#D9A074`, rosy blush `#F0808A`, long sleek honey-streaked chestnut hair `#6B442A` worn loose over her shoulders, open navy school blazer `#2C3E6B` over an untucked pastel-pink uniform shirt `#F4C2D7` with sleeves rolled up, small gold hoop earrings `#E0B456`, chin raised.
- Role key: `layan` (see `cast.json`). Label: ليان.
- Speech: bold, sharp, casual Amman dialect with peers, smoothly innocent in front of Ms. Faten ("ممكن أعرف أنا ليش هون؟", "رح أدمرك.").

## Noaf (cool, independent transfer student; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: age 16, lean build, light-olive skin `#F6D2B5`, rosy blush `#F0808A`, shoulder-length jet-black straight hair `#221F26` with an edgy side part, dark charcoal cardigan or open navy blazer `#2C3E6B` over her pastel-pink uniform shirt `#F4C2D7`, wired earphones or hands tucked in pockets.
- Role key: `noaf` (see `cast.json`). Label: نوف.
- Speech: dry, direct, unbothered Jordanian Arabic; refuses to bow to Layan's clique or let Mariam boss her around ("خليكم بحالكم وخلوني بحالي.", "خطتك؟").

## Dina (Mariam's warm, loyal, slightly anxious best friend; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: age 16, soft rounded build, warm medium skin `#D9A074`, rosy blush `#F0808A`, bouncy dark curly hair `#2E221E` in a high puff/ponytail, round dark-ink glasses `#2E2A3A`, neat navy school jumper `#2C3E6B` over a pastel-pink collar `#F4C2D7`, carrying a pastel backpack.
- Role key: `dina` (see `cast.json`). Label: دينا.
- Speech: warm, expressive, chatty Jordanian Arabic, quick to worry about getting caught ("كنت بعمل وظيفة.", "ما حكيت إشي!").

## Rania (Layan's fierce best friend and right hand; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: age 16, athletic build, medium skin `#D9A074`, rosy blush `#F0808A`, long straight caramel-brown hair `#7D5233` in a high sleek ponytail, navy school vest `#2C3E6B` over a pastel-pink shirt `#F4C2D7`, arms often crossed.
- Role key: `rania` (see `cast.json`). Label: رانيا.
- Speech: loyal to Layan, brisk and confrontational toward outsiders ("بس سؤال، كيف صرتي معنا بالغرفة؟").

## Roqayya (devout, image-conscious member of Layan's clique; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: age 16, slim build, light skin `#F6D2B5`, rosy blush `#F0808A`, neatly wrapped soft navy-and-cream hijab `#2C3E6B` / `#FFF6EA`, long-sleeved pastel-pink school blouse `#F4C2D7` under a buttoned navy blazer `#2C3E6B` and long navy skirt.
- Role key: `roqayya` (see `cast.json`). Label: رقية.
- Speech: prim, defensive of Layan's group, quick to back up her friends.

## Ms. Faten (principal of AlRawabi School for Girls; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: late 40s, poised upright build, light skin `#F6D2B5`, rosy blush `#F0808A`, structured dark-auburn bob `#5A2D28`, tailored plum-burgundy blazer `#6E3550` over a cream blouse `#FFF6EA`, pearl studs, seated at or standing beside a polished wood desk.
- Role key: `faten` (see `cast.json`). Label: مس فاتن.
- Speech: measured, formal-leaning educated Jordanian Arabic, always emphasizing the school's reputation and values ("أهم القيم عنا هون في مدرسة الروابي تحمل المسؤولية والصدق.").

## Ms. Abeer (strict school supervisor and floor monitor; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: early 40s, sturdy build, medium skin `#D9A074`, rosy blush `#F0808A`, dark hair `#2A201D` pulled into a tight low bun, rectangular glasses `#2E2A3A`, olive-slate tailored jacket `#526862` over a cream shirt `#FFF6EA`, holding a cream clipboard `#FFF6EA`.
- Role key: `abeer` (see `cast.json`). Label: مس عبير.
- Speech: clipped, authoritative, zero-tolerance ("هاي أول وآخر مرة.", "ارجعوا ع صفكم!").
