# Boris: characters and places

Role keys: one character = one role key = the character's own name, lowercase Latin. `cast.json` next to this file is the only source for keys, labels, English names and gender (`spec/quality.md` "Cast"). All designs are role-inspired stylised cartoons in the flat house style (`art/style/STYLE.md`), never portraits of actors.

## Alessandro (new directing intern; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: young man, light skin `#F6D2B5`, very short dark hair, small neat dark goatee, olive-green hooded field jacket `#6B7A4F` over a charcoal top `#3A3D45`, dark trousers. Often holding a small cream notepad `#FFF6EA` or a tray of espresso cups; slightly surprised, eager posture.
- Role key: `alessandro` (see `cast.json`). Label: Alessandro.
- Speech: polite `lei` when arriving (`Scusi, lei è il regista?`), shifts to `tu` with peers and crew; earnest, hesitant (`Veramente, sono lo stagista`).

## Arianna (first assistant director; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: woman in her thirties, light skin `#F6D2B5`, brown hair in a neat low ponytail, coral-red zip jacket `#E05A4F` over an olive high-neck sweater `#5E6B48`, dark trousers, carrying a cream clipboard `#FFF6EA`. Direct, brisk posture; raised open palm when giving instructions.
- Role key: `arianna` (see `cast.json`). Label: Arianna.
- Speech: crisp, direct `tu` on set (`Tu stai fermo qui, in silenzio`), diplomatic when managing divas.

## René (director of the soap opera; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: tired middle-aged man, medium skin `#D9A074`, dark curly hair with grey at the temples and a thick scruffy dark beard, navy quilted gilet `#2D4059` over a burgundy sweater `#8C3B4A`, dark jeans. Signature prop: a round glass bowl holding his lucky orange goldfish Boris `#F28C28`, or a studio megaphone/chair.
- Role key: `rene` (see `cast.json`). Label: René.
- Speech: informal `tu` and plural `voi` (`signori`, `ragazzi`), pragmatic, alternates pep talks (`forza, giriamo!`) with weary exasperation.

## Lopez (network delegate; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: smooth-talking man in his forties, light skin `#F6D2B5`, neatly combed dark-brown hair, slate-grey suit `#4A5568` over a pale-blue shirt `#A8D0E6` and warm gold tie `#E2B04A`, holding a small mobile phone. Polished corporate smile and open-armed speaker gesture.
- Role key: `lopez` (see `cast.json`). Label: Lopez.
- Speech: smooth corporate Italian, addresses the crew as `voi`, repeats `bisogna cambiare mentalità`.

## Biascica (chief electrician; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: stocky middle-aged man, medium skin `#D9A074`, short salt-and-pepper hair and stubble, tan multi-pocket utility vest `#C29B6B` over a maroon T-shirt `#8B2E3B`, dark work trousers. Often standing beside tall metal light stands or a foosball table.
- Role key: `biascica` (see `cast.json`). Label: Biascica.
- Speech: blunt, colloquial Roman-flavoured Italian (normalised to clear standard spelling for learners), `tu` with interns, polite title `Dottore` when asking bosses about unpaid overtime.

## Sergio (production manager; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: pragmatic man in his late forties, light skin `#F6D2B5`, receding grey-brown hair, camel blazer `#B88B58` over an open-collar cream shirt `#FFF6EA`, dark trousers, holding a phone to his ear and a stack of cream folders.
- Role key: `sergio` (see `cast.json`). Label: Sergio.
- Speech: dry, matter-of-fact `tu`/`voi` with the crew (`Quei soldi non li vedrete mai`), polite `lei` on the phone with network executives (`Non si preoccupi`).

## Stanis (lead actor; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: tall, vain actor in his thirties, light skin `#F6D2B5`, swept-back wavy dark hair, confident grin. Wears either a crisp cream tennis polo `#FFF6EA` with a navy sweater `#2D4059` draped over his shoulders, or a white doctor's coat `#FFF6EA` with a stethoscope around his neck.
- Role key: `stanis` (see `cast.json`). Label: Stanis.
- Speech: theatrical, self-important, informal `tu`, invents nicknames (`Seppia`).

## Corinna (lead actress; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: glamorous actress in her thirties, light skin `#F6D2B5`, voluminous glossy chestnut-brown curls, bright rosy blush `#F0808A`, rose-pink silk blouse `#E87A96` (or soft lavender dressing gown in makeup), expressive hands.
- Role key: `corinna` (see `cast.json`). Label: Corinna.
- Speech: dramatic diva cadence, insists on `diamoci del tu` with the press and refuses to state her scripted age.

## Duccio (director of photography; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: slouched man in his forties, medium skin `#D9A074`, messy grey-brown hair and scruffy beard, tinted amber sunglasses `#8C5A2B` worn indoors, faded denim shirt `#5B7C99` under a brown corduroy jacket `#7A563E`, relaxed in a folding canvas chair.
- Role key: `duccio` (see `cast.json`). Label: Duccio.
- Speech: slow, deadpan `tu`, cynical wisdom about television lighting and commercials.

## Places
- **Studio corridor and René's corner**: cool slate blue `#6E86A0`, plain studio doorway, studio lamp on a stand, small table with Boris's round glass fishbowl.
- **Studio 2 hospital set**: muted hospital mint `#86CDB2` and teal `#2E9A96`, wooden flat walls, camera on dolly, boom pole, tall metal light stands (`stativi`).
- **Dressing rooms (Corinna / Duccio)**: warm terracotta `#C98B5E` or dusky plum `#A58BD3` for Corinna's mirror-lit makeup room; dim warm ochre `#B88B58` with a folding chair for Duccio's hideout.
- **Break corner with foosball table**: lively tomato coral `#E2604F` / warm amber, classic red-and-blue wooden foosball table (`biliardino`) beside coiled studio cables.
