# Italian taster scope and decisions

Only Boris season1episode1 was used. The single lesson is a bounded taster of the original Italian exchange at approximately137.22–144.24seconds, not a complete episode or season.

The source MKV has no Italian subtitle track. `sources/boris/s01/e01.it.srt` is a new seven-cue transcript of this selected excerpt, located in original episode time. It is not a full-episode SRT or an official subtitle. All retained transcript bytes, including the two preceding questions excluded from the learner lesson, are bound by the explicit source hash in `scenes.json`. The complete private original video and the two unprompted ASR outputs remain outside the content repository. No source video, audio or still is to be published.

The preceding uncertain identity question, repeated encouragement, goldfish monologue, missing closing assent and later department line are excluded. The hesitation before the intern's introduction is omitted. Dictionary spelling `stagista` is supported by Treccani; the small decoder's doubled-g spelling is not silently described as a native-listener certification.

Five story lines are intentional for this short taster. The normal validator's short-story warning remains visible. No filler, invented reply or duplicated dialogue was added to meet a count.

Every target segment, including replays and newly authored `Ciao!`, has an English translation, exact whitespace tokens and contextual word notes. All 20 lemmas have dictionary entries, forms where useful, and checked primary-source word histories; the uncertain origin of `niente` remains explicitly uncertain. The closing farewell and lexicon practice examples are new teaching text, not claimed source dialogue.

Voice and publication gates remain owned by production: source speaker confirmation, genuine Italian references, English narrator, complete generated-speech review, word timing, loudness, cache fingerprints, final package validation and serving verification. The content draft itself does not establish those audio gates.
