## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- At the kitchen table over a bubbling fondue pot, Danya chats cheerfully: 'There is this English chef.'
- 'I will learn to cook from his shows.' Excited about her new kitchen television, Nina says she will learn to cook from the chef's shows.
- When Slavik asks if he can dip cucumbers in the melted cheese, Danya beams: 'Cucumbers are fine too, anything you want!'
- 'You get unique flavours.' Notice the self-action verb form on the verb for 'turn out', which directs the action back onto the subject.
- Danya turns to Samsonov: 'Well, is it tasty?'
- 'Well, honestly speaking, it is not for me,' Samsonov replies dryly.
- Old neighbour Klavdia Viktorovna cackles: 'Now if you could dip vodka in it, that would be another matter!'
- 'You won't fall asleep in the bathroom from cheese!' She teases Samsonov about his earlier nap.
- Samsonov glares at her: 'Klavdia Viktorovna, if you were my mother-in-law!' Notice the conditional particle with the past tense.
- 'Then I would put up with you.' He warns her, while Nina scolds the old lady for stirring up trouble.
- Unbothered, Klavdia Viktorovna adds: 'And Danya will marry Nina!'
- 'And we will live together in harmony and eat fondue.' Klavdia jokes that they will all live in harmony and eat fondue together.
- Samsonov forces a smile to hide his jealousy: 'All right, everything is fine.'
- 'We are having a nice time sitting together!' Samson uses the adverb for 'nicely' right alongside the verb meaning 'we are sitting'.
- 'Only it lacks our broad Russian scale.' He complains while looking at the tiny candle under the pot.
- 'The little flame is burning, but there is hardly any warmth.' Samson uses the adverb for 'little' right alongside the verb meaning 'is burning'.
- 'Now if we were out in nature, right?' Samsonov suggests.
- 'We have not been out in nature for a long time!' Suddenly inspired, Samson remarks that the family hasn't been out in nature for ages!
- 'Let's drive out to the forest for shashlik!' He proposes a real cookout instead of fondue.
- 'We will take Danya here along with us.' Clapping his rival on the shoulder, Samson insists on taking Danya along with them into the woods.
- Danya hesitates: 'Well, a picnic in winter is strange.'
- 'Let's just eat fondue instead.' Notice how 'let's' sets up the clause and leads naturally into 'fondue'.
- 'Look, Uncle Danya has gone pale, see!' Klavdia Viktorovna says to tease him.
- 'He is afraid of the winter forest.' Klavdia needles Danya, asking if a grown man is really afraid of the winter forest.
- Stung on his pride in front of Nina, Danya straightens up: 'No, why? I am ready!'
- 'Great, then we are going!' Samsonov says triumphantly.
- 'In an hour in the courtyard, okay?' The trap is set: Samson tells everyone to meet in the courtyard in an hour.
