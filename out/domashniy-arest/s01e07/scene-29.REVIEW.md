## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Maybe we should call a doctor? I'm very scared!' Because 'doctor' is an animate masculine noun, its object-form singular ends in '-a'.
- 'No, the doctors will take three hours to get here!' Literally, 'will be driving for three hours'.
- 'Do you want me to get an infection?' After 'want that', Russian uses the conjunction 'chtoby' plus a past-tense verb.
- 'Alright, fine, fine! I'll put the ointment on myself. Here?' Marina grabs a bottle of green antiseptic and tries to dab it on with her eyes averted.
- 'No, no, lower! You're rubbing my leg, Marina!' Arkady looks down and protests that she is missing the scratch completely.
- 'Then tell me where! I'm not looking, after all!' Keeping her face turned away, Marina demands directions.
- 'Marina, please look at what you are doing!' Arkady begs her to look for one second.
- 'There, yes, rub it! Ow, it burns! Blow on it, blow on it quickly!' The moment the stinging antiseptic touches the scratch, Arkady shrieks at the top of his lungs.
- 'Sorry to disturb you. I dropped by to apologize.' At that exact second, the door opens and Eduard Vladimirovich stands frozen on the threshold.
- 'But, apparently, I came in vain.' Seeing Marina leaning over Arkady while Arkady yells for her to blow on it, Eduard draws the worst possible conclusion.
- 'Edik, stop! I just accidentally pinched him!' Marina jumps up in panic and tries to explain the bathroom zipper accident.
- 'See, I'm looking for the glove!' Her explanation only sounds worse to Eduard.
- 'I beg you, don't! I don't want to hear these details.' Horrified, Eduard raises a hand to stop her.
- 'I used to have a wife and the woman I loved.' With 'by me there was', the past tense agrees with the first noun 'wife'.
- 'Only the wife is left.' Eduard bitterly sums up the end of his double life.
- 'Thank you, Marina. I won't need this key anymore.' He places his key to Marina's room on the table and walks out forever.
