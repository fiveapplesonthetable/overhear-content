## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'It's hot for me! I feel like I'm in childhood.' Arkady thoroughly enjoys making Marina wait on him.
- 'A little spoonful for daddy, one for mommy.' This is the classic Russian phrase parents say when coaxing a toddler to eat.
- 'I beg your pardon. Could you make me some tea?' Literally, 'of hot tea' in the partitive 'of'-form form.
- 'Right, eat! Eat and don't hold us up.' Marina snaps at him and keeps shoveling porridge into his mouth.
- 'Alright, that's it, enough of this tenderness here.' Eduard walks back into the kitchen and cannot stand watching Marina feed him.
- 'Marinochka, please put the spoon down. I'll feed him myself.' Eduard takes the spoon out of her hand.
- 'And you go to the room. Breakfast hasn't gone cold yet.' Once Marina leaves the kitchen, Eduard drops his polite tone.
- 'Well, not so fast! — I say, eat!' Right then, Eduard shoves huge spoonfuls of porridge at Arkady's face.
- 'Do you understand that you've made us sick of you already?' The verb literally means 'you have reached us'—meaning you have driven us crazy.
- 'Eduard Valentinovich! Why are you so nervous?' Arkady switches to the informal 'you' to mock him.
- 'Are you afraid that the little pill will stop working?' Arkady reveals he heard Eduard talking about his potency pill last night.
- 'How? And how did you find out about the little pill?' Eduard freezes in shock.
- 'So you were eavesdropping in there, yes? — Yes, I was eavesdropping.' In this moment, Arkady grins smugly in his chair.
- 'Do you know what I'm going to do to you now? — What will you do to me?' Right then, Arkady knows Eduard won't dare hit an injured man—so Eduard overturns the bowl of porridge onto Arkady's head!
- 'Marina! What have you done? He poured porridge on my head!' Arkady immediately screams for Marina to come see.
- 'What have you done? — Marina, he's lying, he's a faker!' When Marina rushes in, Eduard notices that Arkady is flexing his fingers inside the casts.
- 'He poured this porridge on his head himself! His arms are healthy!' Nobody could pour a bowl on his own head with two casts, so Eduard sounds completely unhinged.
- 'Get out of here! — Alright, calm down, he's gone.' Disgusted with Eduard's outburst, Marina throws him out of the apartment and comforts Arkady.
