## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Two cylinders are not working.' After the numeral 'two', Russian nouns take the 'of'-form singular form.
- 'They slipped me bad spark plugs!' Samsonov fumes after Danya's tiny car had to tow his broken SUV into the woods.
- 'Well, why are we sitting here?' Samsonov tries to take charge again.
- 'Let's get out!' Literally 'we are going out'.
- 'Yes, alright, let's get out.' Danya unbuckles his seatbelt and steps out of the car.
- 'Here it is, our little spot!' Samsonov gestures proudly at a snow-covered clearing.
- 'Oh, there is snow all around here!' Nina looks around at the deep drifts.
- 'And where are we going to sit?' There is not a single dry bench in sight.
- 'Son, come help me!' Samsonov calls Slavik over to a frozen fallen tree.
- 'Right now we'll roll this log over and clear the snow.' He wants everyone to sit on a cold log.
- 'And why do we need a log?' Danya opens his boot with a smile.
- 'I brought chairs right here!' He unfolds neat camping chairs for everyone.
- 'Why are you dragging these Chinese chairs around with you, Danya?' Samsonov scoffs at the folding chairs.
- 'This is not China, it's Norway!' Danya replies politely.
- 'They hold one hundred and fifty kilograms.' Even Samsonov cannot break them.
- 'Please, go ahead!' Literally 'I ask', a polite way to invite someone to sit down.
- 'Some kind of kindergarten.' Samsonov mutters sourly as the family sits down in comfort.
- 'You know, Danya is simply a wizard!' Nina beams with gratitude.
- 'Thank you!' That praise makes Samsonov even more determined to prove his toughness.
- 'Right, fine, men, off for firewood!' Notice the 'with'-form plural after the preposition meaning 'for'.
- 'Up you get, let's go!' He barks the order like an army sergeant.
- 'And why go for firewood?' Danya holds up a neat paper bag.
- 'I brought charcoal right here.' Once again Danya has thought of everything.
- 'Throw it out, Danya, that's chemicals!' Samsonov refuses to be outdone again.
- 'In the forest, only with firewood!' Even when Slavik asks what is wrong with charcoal, Samsonov will not budge.
- 'Well, forward!' He marches Danya and Slavik into the trees with an axe.
