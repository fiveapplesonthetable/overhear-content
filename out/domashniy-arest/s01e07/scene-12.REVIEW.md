## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I think I can guess why we are here.' Eduard starts connecting the dots while they wait.
- 'Do you feel something for him?' Literally, toward him do you feel something.
- 'What kind of nonsense is that?' Marina dismisses his suspicion right away.
- 'Edik, I just feel sorry for him.' Impersonal 'to'-form with the object-form of the person pitied.
- 'He charmed you!' A single-action form verb meaning to enchant or charm someone.
- 'Why do you need an old man when there's such a lively young neighbor nearby?' Russian omits the present-tense verb 'to be', connecting 'why' directly with 'neighbor'.
- 'Stop it! Nobody charmed me.' Remember that Russian uses double negation with nobody.
- 'Nobody? Not even me?' Eduard teases her with a wounded smile.
- 'I am the one who should be offended!' Marina turns the tables on him.
- 'Forgive me, I am at fault.' Eduard immediately backs down and apologizes.
- 'Forgive me, I will make up for my fault.' He promises to make things right.
- 'Are we going home already?' Marina asks just as Arkady walks out with both arms in casts.
