## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'It's ridiculous! I'm the head of the department, and my computer doesn't work.' At this point, Denis sits down at the keyboard.
- 'We'll fix it right now, Mikhail Mikhailovich. And what is your password?' The stern Colonel mutters the name of a purple cartoon moon-creature for toddlers.
- 'Luntik twenty eighteen. What are you looking at? My grandson came over and played!' Here, Lavrov and Ovechkin exchange a grin, and the Colonel barks at them.
- 'And what did you want again?' At this point, Lavrov steps forward.
- 'We need your help. We need to send requests to all the archives.' Notice how 'your help is needed' uses the short feminine adjective to agree with 'help'.
- 'Maybe we'll still find some dirt on Samsonov.' The Colonel scoffs.
- 'There is nothing on him! You already checked everything.' Parents, brothers, sisters — all clean. Lavrov wants to go further back.
- 'Then we have to dig deeper! Distant relatives, grandfathers and grandmothers.' And he explains why.
- 'What if there is something there? We want to come at it from another angle.' The Colonel agrees to give them his nephew, who knows the archive inside out.
- 'How delicate of you! Fine, here is Lieutenant Sokolov, he will help you.' Still stinging from yesterday's bridge fiasco, he adds a strict warning.
- 'Just don't drag him into anything!' Meanwhile, Lavrov nods to him in agreement.
- 'Yes, we got it already!' As Lavrov leaves, technician Denis finishes checking the Colonel's computer.
- 'Mikhail Mikhailovich, I checked your computer. Your graphics card is weak.' Denis notices what game is installed and what team the Colonel plays on.
- 'Wait, do you play computer games? As the bad guys?' Caught red-handed, the Colonel comes up with a professional excuse.
- 'It's relaxing! And I must understand their psychology! That's it, go!' And he shoos Denis out of the office.
