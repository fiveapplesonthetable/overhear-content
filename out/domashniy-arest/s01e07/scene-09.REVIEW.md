## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hi, Vanya!' Danya greets him cheerfully over the board-game cards. Samsonov frowns.
- 'Hi. And what is going on here?' In response, Nina smiles and holds up a deck of picture cards.
- 'It's a great game! Do you want to play with us? Take some cards.' In this moment, Samsonov sulks in the doorway.
- 'No, I don't want to play. I thought I'd spend the weekend with my family.' He sets down the bags he brought.
- 'I bought sweets for the kids here. Yet the family is having a good time without me!' Then he glares directly at Danya.
- 'Danya, what are you doing here?' In this moment, Danya explains politely.
- 'I came to see Arkady Borisovich.' Notice the preposition with the 'to/for' form for visiting a person. Samsonov looks around the room with heavy sarcasm.
- 'I see my wife, I see my kids. But I don't see Arkady Borisovich here!' In response, Danya points out why Arkady isn't here.
- 'They took him away in an ambulance.' In this moment, Samsonov steps closer to Danya.
- 'I know! Do you want them to take you there right now too?' Right then, Nina immediately intervenes.
- 'Samsonov, calm down! Arkasha was busy. And we invited Danya for tea.' Sensing the tension, Danya stands up.
- 'Alright, I think I'll get going. Bye, Nina!' Once Danya leaves, young Slavik mentions that Danya promised to bring a fondue pot tomorrow so they can dip bread into melted cheese.
- 'Go on, go! And what is he bringing tomorrow? Cheese fondue?' Samsonov is furious.
- 'He'll be stammering in a tiny voice when I'm done with him! I've told you everything!' In response, Nina stands her ground.
- 'Just lay a finger on him! We are always glad to see Danya here.' Notice the singular command 'just touch him' used as a warning: 'don't you dare touch him!'
- 'I've warned you!' Masculine past tense from Samsonov.
- 'I've warned you too!' And feminine past tense with the '-a' ending from Nina.
