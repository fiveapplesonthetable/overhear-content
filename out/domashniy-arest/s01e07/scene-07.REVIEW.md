## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Marina! Arkady Borisovich fell down the stairs!' Notice the preposition with the 'of' form for 'down from the stairs'. Ilona speaks into her phone to the prison service.
- 'Hello! We urgently need an ambulance!' Literally 'rapid help is urgently needed to us'. Marina hurries out in her robe.
- 'What happened?' Right then, Ilona smoothly delivers the romantic story she and Arkady just invented.
- 'He climbed up to clear cobwebs. And fell!' And why was Arkady cleaning the stairwell?
- 'He wanted to invite you for tea. But his place isn't tidy.' From the floor, Arkady pretends to be embarrassed.
- 'Why are you telling her? That was a secret!' Neighbour Nina comes out and offers to examine him since she is a nurse, which terrifies Arkady.
- 'Go away! You nearly cut my ear off last time!' In response, Ilona quickly blocks Nina from examining Arkady and discovering he isn't hurt at all.
- 'Don't! They said not to touch him and not to move him at all!' Sympathetic Marina kneels beside Arkady.
- 'Are you in pain?' Literally 'is it painful to you?' Arkady squints dramatically.
- 'I can't see anything! Where are my glasses?' In response, Marina spots them on the step.
- 'There lie your glasses.' In this moment, Arkady reaches for Marina's hand.
- 'You will ride with me to the hospital, won't you? Please!' Right then, Marina hesitates, glancing back at Eduard.
- 'Well, I don't know.' In response, Arkady generously waves a hand toward fuming Eduard.
- 'You can even take him along with you. That one!' In this moment, Eduard bristlingly asks what he means by 'that one', and Arkady gives Marina his most pitiful look.
- 'The main thing is that you are nearby, as a friend. Please, I'm in a lot of pain!' And tender-hearted Marina agrees to go with him to the hospital, ruining Eduard's evening.
