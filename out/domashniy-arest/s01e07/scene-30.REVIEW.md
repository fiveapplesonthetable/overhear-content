## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The second batch is ready! Come on, let's take a photo all together!' The self-action verb form verb means 'let us be photographed together'.
- 'Samson, come on! Smile! Cheese, cheese!' Nina waves Samsonov into the frame for the family photo.
- 'Ivan, stand further back, over there behind the pines.' After 'behind', the noun 'pines' takes the 'with'-form plural.
- 'Danya, is this your sharpener? And your axe?' Feeling sidelined, Samsonov spots Danya's blade sharpener and camping axe on the table.
- 'Yes, my axe. Wow! How did you do that?' Without warning, Samsonov spins and hurls the axe straight into a pine trunk, leaving Danya amazed.
- 'Just like that, Danya! A meter fifty off the ground!' Beaming with pride for the first time all day, Samsonov points to the height of the stuck blade.
- 'You can buy anything at all!' The pronoun phrase means 'whatever you please, anything at all'.
- 'But you can't buy skill, got it?' Russian uses the generalized second-person singular for 'one cannot buy'.
- 'We're strictly forbidden to show this. But you're family!' When Slavik asks why his dad never showed them this before, Samsonov claims it is a military secret.
- 'And there's also close combat! Watch what I can do!' Carried away by his children's applause, Samsonov launches into a demonstration of high kicks.
- 'Easy, Vanya, be careful! Why are you kicking the tree with your foot?' Nina watches nervously as Samsonov kicks the pine trunk where the axe is embedded.
- 'It's fine, don't be afraid! Can you do that, Danya, or not?' Samsonov challenges Danya to match his high kick.
- 'No, I can't do that. You kicked higher than me.' Danya tries a modest kick, rests his hand on the tree trunk to brace himself, and admits Samsonov kicked higher.
- 'And right now I'll kick even higher! Watch, everyone!' Samsonov winds up and slams his boot into the trunk — shaking the stuck axe loose right onto Danya's hand.
- 'Ow, my hand! The axe fell onto my hand!' Danya cries out as the falling axe slices his finger against the bark.
- 'Samsonov, have you lost your mind?! Quickly to the hospital!' Nina rushes to Danya's side in horror as they scramble for the car.
