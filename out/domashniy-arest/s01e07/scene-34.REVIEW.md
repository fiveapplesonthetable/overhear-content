## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'So what, are we going straight home, or what?' Outside the hospital, Samsonov tries to salvage the ruined day off.
- 'Let's go, maybe we'll have coffee with a pastry? It's a day off, after all.' He invites Nina and the children to a cafe.
- 'Drink your own coffee yourself! I don't want to see you.' Still disgusted by his behaviour in the forest, Nina refuses to look at him.
- 'Come on, sweetheart! In a week everything will be fine.' Samsonov waves off Danya's stitched finger as a minor mishap.
- 'Don't you understand that you ruin everything?' Nina stares at him in disbelief.
- 'Kids, why are you standing there? We're going home right now!' She marches Yulya and Slavik down the street without him.
- 'Good evening! We have a request for you.' Left alone and boiling with frustration, Samsonov is approached by Vitalik and Pasha's second bodyguard.
- 'Oh, you two always walk as a pair! What do you want?' Notice the instrumental singular noun meaning 'as a pair'.
- 'Withdraw your candidacy from the election.' Vitalik politely delivers Pasha Osipenko's ultimatum.
- 'Oh, and if I don't, then what?' Samsonov raises an eyebrow, almost hoping for a fight.
- 'And if you don't, then you'll end up in the hospital.' The two bodyguards step closer on the empty street.
- 'For long? You need to convince me somehow!' Delighted to have a way to vent his anger, Samsonov cracks his knuckles.
- 'We don't hit on the head!' Remembering that the two guards spared his head during their earlier fight, Samsonov sets the same sporting rule before thrashing them both.
- 'Good night, boys!' Having let off steam, he wishes the groaning bodyguards a polite good night and walks off.
- 'Yes, Pavel Yuryevich. We understood everything.' Bruised on the pavement, Vitalik answers Pasha's phone call and hands the second guard a knocked-out tooth.
