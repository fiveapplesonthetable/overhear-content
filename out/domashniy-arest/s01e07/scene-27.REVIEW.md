## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, is everything okay now? Is everything fine?' Both descriptive word adverbs describe an impersonal state.
- 'No, it is not fine, Marina! Now I've gotten all worked up!' This phrase means 'to get nervous or worked up', built from the noun for 'nerves'.
- 'Please, help me! I can't hold it!' Pointing at both arms in plaster casts, Arkady insists he cannot manage his trouser zipper on his own.
- 'Come on, hold it, you're not a child!' The prefixed command means 'endure for a little while, hold on'.
- 'I am not going to do that with my bare hands!' Notice the 'with'-form plural for 'with bare hands', expressing the instrument or means.
- 'Are you kidding me? Take that disgusting thing away!' Right then, Marina pulls on a hideous old rubber dishwashing glove from the sink, and Arkady recoils in horror.
- 'What's wrong with it? A good old glove!' Elderly neighbour Klavdia Viktorovna pokes her head in and proudly defends her household rubber glove.
- 'Klavdia Viktorovna, maybe you could? Close the door, please!' Mortified, Marina tries to hand the job over to Klavdia Viktorovna and then shoos her out.
- 'Wait, I'm nervous too! I don't want to see anything.' Inside the bathroom, Marina stands with her back turned and her eyes squeezed shut.
- 'Zipper down, down! Just like that, very good.' The Russian noun for 'lightning' also means a 'zipper'.
- 'Listen, Marina, the two of us have grown closer!' While Marina stands waiting with her back turned, Arkady tries to turn the awkward moment into a romantic bond.
- 'That doesn't mean anything! Listen, are you done?' Marina shuts down his sentimentality immediately.
- 'Yes, I'm done, thank you! Marina, zip up the zipper, please.' Arkady cheerfully asks her to zip his trousers back up.
- 'Ow, it hurts! You pinched my skin!' Still refusing to look, Marina yanks the zipper up too fast and catches a pinch of skin.
- 'I didn't pinch anything! Kindly don't bother me anymore!' Flustered and furious, Marina pulls off the rubber glove and marches out.
