## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello again! I wanted to apologize for today.' Arkady steps into Marina's room looking contrite after Eduard stormed out.
- 'Everything is my fault.' Notice the prepositional phrase literally meaning 'guilty in everything'.
- 'Oh, stop it, you didn't do it on purpose!' Marina sighs and tells him not to blame himself.
- 'Maybe it's for the best. Everything was falling apart for a long time.' She admits her romance with Eduard was already crumbling.
- 'You deserve the very best!' The short-form adjective takes the genitive case for what someone is worthy of.
- 'I have something for you.' Quietly slipping his right hand out of his cast behind his back, Arkady pulls out an antique brooch.
- 'Please take it! My grandfather gave this brooch to my grandmother.' Notice how the direct object comes first for emphasis.
- 'Very beautiful! What if you meet someone else?' Marina admires the family heirloom, hesitating to accept such a personal gift.
- 'I already have met her! But she rejects my advances.' Arkady looks straight into her eyes.
- 'A blue flame flickered! For the first time I sang of love!' Dropping both casts onto the table, Arkady recites Sergei Yesenin's famous love poem and leans in to kiss a moved Marina.
- 'Marina, that's the phone. Please turn it off!' Just as their lips are about to touch, Marina's mobile phone rings.
- 'Oh, it's Ilona! I'll put it on speaker.' Thinking Arkady's arms are in plaster casts, Marina taps speakerphone.
- 'Arkady Borisovich, why do you need those fake casts on your arms?' Ilona's voice rings out across the room.
- 'Ilona, what nonsense are you saying? Marina, I'll explain everything!' Arkady panics as Marina stares at his two healthy, unbandaged hands.
- 'Marina, listen, it's all because of love!' He pleads with her, swearing he only faked the broken arms to win her heart.
- 'So, you're a faker? Does it hurt right now?' Furious at how he tricked her all day, Marina grabs one of his heavy plaster casts and whacks him with it.
- 'No, I'm not a faker! Ow, it hurts me so much, Marina!' Even when he drops to his knees to kiss her shoes, she chases him out into the corridor.
- 'What, the shoe-kissing trick didn't work the third time?' Ilona asks dryly in the hallway, noting that two out of three is still a good score.
