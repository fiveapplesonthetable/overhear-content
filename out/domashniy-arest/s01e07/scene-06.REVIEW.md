## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I am not going to help you!' Ilona refuses to get dragged into Arkady's romantic schemes.
- 'Do you understand that this is kindergarten?' She calls his romantic jealousy childish nonsense.
- 'You found a fine time and place!' Ilona reminds him they are in the middle of a serious campaign.
- 'What difference does it make to you?' She asks why he cares so much about Marina's dating life.
- 'I cannot sleep in peace!' Arkady tries to sound like a noble protector of innocent youth.
- 'This old man is stealing the girl's best years.' Arkady indignantly protests against Eduard.
- 'What, did you fall in love with her?' Ilona cuts straight through his noble excuses.
- 'No! Or yes.' Arkady stammers and wavers in confusion.
- 'What difference does it make? Help me, I beg you.' He abandons pride and asks for her help.
- 'No, I said no.' Ilona turns away and refuses to assist him.
- 'Look, I got down on my knees!' Arkady drops dramatically to the corridor floor.
- 'I am kissing your feet.' He grabs her boots and presses his face against them.
- 'Don't kiss anything of mine!' Ilona pulls back in disgust.
- 'Arkady, stop it, I just came in from the street!' She warns him that her winter boots are filthy.
- 'Please, help me!' Arkady begs desperately from the corridor floor.
- 'I will keep kissing them anyway.' Arkady clings to her ankles and refuses to let go.
- 'Ugh, give me my feet back, I say!' Ilona tries to shake him off her boots.
- 'That's it, enough, stop! I agree.' Exhausted by his shameless antics, Ilona finally surrenders.
- 'Hooray, the little trick works!' Arkady jumps up with a victorious grin.
- 'The governor taught me this.' Notice that in Russian you teach someone in the accusative case.
- 'He begs the president like that every time.' Arkady explains that the governor uses this trick to keep his post.
- 'So that they don't fire him from his job.' The conjunction introduces a clause of purpose.
- 'Marina, come to me!' Inside the room, Eduard tries to entice Marina into bed.
- 'I took a pill today.' Eduard boasts that he took medication to enhance his romantic performance.
- 'So today I will be like a young hero!' He promises to feel youthful and energetic all evening.
- 'Blood pressure?' Eduard waves off the warning and says that it is nothing.
- 'It's nothing, you just make my head spin.' Literally, from you my head spins.
- 'Oh, what is that noise out there?' A sudden loud crash echoes from the stairwell outside.
- 'I don't know, let's go look.' Eduard and Marina rush to the corridor to see what happened to Arkady.
