## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- In Samsonov's dream, a wise Chinese general in silk robes greets him warmly: 'Well, hello, Samsonov!'
- 'Hello! And who are you?' Samsonov asks in surprise.
- 'What do you mean, who? I am Sun Tzu,' the general replies.
- Sun Tzu gets straight to business: 'Samsonov, you have big problems.' Notice the construction for 'you have' followed by the plural noun.
- 'This Danya has stepped onto your territory,' he warns.
- 'And love, Samsonov, is like war,' the strategist declares.
- 'The lawyer is your enemy.' Listen for the word meaning 'lawyer', which leads straight into 'enemy'.
- 'And how can I defeat him?' Samsonov asks.
- 'You read my book badly!' Sun Tzu scolds him.
- 'You must strike where the enemy is weak,' he quotes from his treatise.
- 'You need to lure him onto your own territory,' Sun Tzu explains.
- 'Right where you are strong.' Sun Tzu puts 'there' up front and finishes with 'strong' to make the point unmistakable.
- 'And where am I strong?' Samsonov wonders.
- 'Well, where did you serve in the army?' Sun Tzu asks.
- 'In the airborne troops, of course!' Samsonov answers proudly.
- 'That means you have survival skills,' Sun Tzu points out.
- 'And he does not have them!' Notice the negative existential form.
- 'Got it, Sun Tzu, got it! What should I do?' Samsonov asks eagerly.
- Suddenly Nina bursts into the dream, and Sun Tzu shouts: 'Run, Samsonov!'
- 'You must fight for your love!' he calls out as Nina throws him out the window.
- In reality, Nina is banging on the bathroom door: 'Don't block the way!'
- 'You fell asleep in the toilet with your book again!' she yells, waking him up.
