## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, family, five more minutes!' After the numeral 'five', the noun 'minutes' stands in the plural 'of' form.
- 'And Dad's shashlik will soon be ready!' The short masculine adjective 'ready' serves as the descriptive phrase.
- 'And mine is ready too! Where do you get your meat?' Lawyer Danya is grilling his own skewers right next to Samsonov.
- 'I always get it at the market. They marinate it themselves.' Samsonov brags about buying pre-marinated meat in a plastic bucket at the town market.
- 'Got it. They probably put bad meat in there for you.' Danya gently warns him that pre-marinated market buckets often hide tough scraps.
- 'Still, it smells delicious! I always buy from them.' The conjunction at the start means 'on the other hand, to make up for it'.
- 'Of course it's delicious! That's my shashlik smelling, the wind is from here.' Danya smiles and points out which way the breeze is blowing.
- 'Well, let's check right now whose shashlik is tastier! Right, Nina?' Notice the comparative adjective 'tastier'.
- 'Danya, give me the last little piece! Very tasty meat!' Minutes later, Nina, Yulya, and Slavik are fighting over the very last skewer from Danya's platter.
- 'Calm down, I'll make more! Where should I put the plate?' While Yulya and Slavik argue over who was first in line, Danya promises a second batch.
- 'And why is nobody eating my shashlik? There's plenty of meat!' The descriptive word 'plenty' takes the 'of'-form singular of mass nouns like 'meat'.
- 'No, Vanya, it's very tough.' Nina waves Samsonov's platter away with brutal honesty.
- 'What do you mean tough? You always loved it!' Samsonov is hurt and reminds them that they ate his barbecue every year before.
- 'That's because there was nothing else!' With negative 'was not', 'other' stands in the 'of' form singular.
- 'Eat it yourself.' Nina leaves the entire platter of tough skewers to Samsonov.
- 'I'll eat it all myself! Danya, how do you prepare the meat?' Chewing stubbornly on his own tough skewer, Samsonov finally caves and asks Danya for his secret.
- 'Try it! Onion, salt, pepper — and that's all.' Danya offers him a tender piece and shares his simple recipe.
