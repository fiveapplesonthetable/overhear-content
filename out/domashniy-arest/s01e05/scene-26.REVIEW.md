## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why is your face so sour?' Literally, 'what at you is the face so sour?', with neuter endings on 'such' and 'sour' to match the neuter word 'face'.
- 'Nothing. I don't like your work, Uncle Grisha.' In Russian, 'I don't like' literally says 'does not please to me', with 'your work' as the sentence subject.
- 'They just caught us off guard! It was a surprise.' Uncle Grisha tries to save face after being easily taken down by Zavarzin.
- 'What surprise? He grabbed me like a specialist!' Kostik can still feel the professional security-service hold.
- 'Why did you go at them empty-handed?' Literally, 'with empty hands', using the little word 'with' and the 'with'-form plural.
- 'Where is your baton?' Uncle Grisha asks about the metal baton Kostik was supposed to carry.
- 'It's in the box. It's lying there!' He left it inside the car's glove compartment.
- 'It must be in your hand always! There, that's more like it.' Right then, Kostik takes the baton out of the glovebox and snaps it open.
- 'What are we going to do about that woman's debt?' After the little word 'with', 'debt' stands in the 'with/as' form, followed by 'of that woman' in the 'of' form.
- 'Now she owes us money. And the gold watch too!' Because Lavrov and Zavarzin took both Marina's loan contract and Arkady's Swiss watch, Uncle Grisha blames Marina for both losses.
- 'We will scare her badly! We'll throw a bottle through her window.' Both action words are one-time action words first-person plural futures: 'we will scare' and 'we will throw'.
- 'Because this is our turf! We call the shots here!' The word for 'turf' literally means a 'clearing in the woods', used in slang for one's territory, and 'we decide here' means 'we run things here'.
- 'Our turf! We call the shots! Yes!' Kostik waves the baton and shouts along, feeling brave again. Just then, a giant fist taps politely on the car window.
- 'Excuse me, please. Are you debt collectors?' Standing outside the car are Arkady's giant former bodyguards, led by fair-haired Vitalik, who felt guilty about ignoring Arkady's phone call earlier and came to help after all.
- 'We are from Arkady Borisovich. Nice piece!' The little word 'from' takes the 'of' form, and Vitalik calmly takes Kostik's baton out of his trembling hand before giving the collectors their second beating of the day.
