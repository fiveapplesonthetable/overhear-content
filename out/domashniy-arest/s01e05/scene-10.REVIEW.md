## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'My poor thing! It's okay, we'll sort it all out.' At first the dean sounds warm and reassuring. Marina says she wasn't only worried about herself.
- 'Never mind me, it's Lyoshka who got scared.' Literally 'what am I — here Lyoshka got scared'. The dean asks for the details.
- 'Now let's go through everything in order.' Literally 'now let's everything by order'.
- 'Tell me, how much do you owe them?' Marina gives him the number.
- 'A hundred and fifty thousand.' In spoken Russian, word order is flexible: 'a hundred' comes first to set the focus, followed by 'thousand'.
- 'I borrowed twenty.' She used the ongoing-action form of 'to take': 'I took twenty'. She explains how twenty turned into a hundred and fifty.
- 'And they piled it on with their gangster interest rates.' The verb literally means 'wound up, cranked up'. And the collectors blamed her for not reading the contract carefully.
- 'They said that you have to read the fine print.' Literally 'small font'. Instead of taking her side, the dean nods sagely.
- 'Marinochka, of course they're right.' In spoken Russian, word order is flexible: 'of course' comes first to set the focus, followed by 'right'.
- 'You have to read the fine print too.' Marina cuts straight to the point.
- 'Will you help me?' Notice the future tense of 'help' takes the 'to/for' form: 'to me'. The dean immediately starts making excuses.
- 'Well, of course! I'm thinking how.' Listen for the word meaning 'of course', which leads straight into 'how'.
- 'Alla Yevgenyevna has taken over all my income.' His domineering wife controls every rouble he earns.
- 'I withdraw money from my card.' Literally 'I take off money from the card'.
- 'And Alla Yevgenyevna immediately gets a text message.' Marina isn't buying it.
- 'But you're the rector!' That particle adds 'after all'.
- 'You have some possibilities.' He spreads his hands helplessly.
- 'What possibilities? I run a university!' Literally 'I have a university!'. Seeing he won't give her a single rouble, Marina asks what his brilliant plan is.
- 'Fine. What should I do?' Literally 'what to me to do?'. The dean brightens up with a solution that costs him nothing.
- 'You need to run!' He says it with complete seriousness.
- 'Run to the village to your mum's.' Notice 'into the village' uses the object-form, and 'to mum' uses the preposition 'toward' plus 'to'-form.
- 'Go away with Alyosha for six months.' The line opens with 'go away' and lands on 'months' at the end for emphasis.
- 'And everyone will forget everything!' Notice those two almost identical words side by side: 'everyone' with an 'e' and 'everything' with 'yo'. Marina thinks of Major Lavrov's business card.
- 'I think that I have a man.' Marina puts 'think' up front and finishes with 'man' to make the point unmistakable.
- 'He will be able to protect me.' As she walks out, the dean calls after her indignantly.
- 'Marina, I'm doing everything possible!' The line opens with 'Marina' and lands on 'possible' at the end for emphasis.
- 'Listen, you're being unfair!' Eduard uses the command form command form of the verb meaning 'listen'.
