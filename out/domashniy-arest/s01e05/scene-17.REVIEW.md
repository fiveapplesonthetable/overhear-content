## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Right now, you see, I'm making copies.' Ilona peeks out the door and sees Pasha coming up to the archive.
- 'Lydia Alexeyevna, he's coming up already!' The reflexive verb means 'is climbing' or 'is coming upstairs'.
- 'Well stop him!' That is the plural imperative for 'hold him back!' or 'delay him!'.
- 'How am I supposed to stop him?' Lydia has no idea what to do in the corridor.
- 'I don't know, any way you like!' Literally 'how pleasing'. Lydia steps out into the corridor and blocks Pasha's way.
- 'Pavel Yuryevich, this is urgent!' She calls him by his full name and patronymic.
- 'I must tell you.' Because Lydia is a woman, the short adjective for 'must' ends in '-a'. Pasha tries to brush past her.
- 'I have no time.' Literally 'to me there is no when'.
- 'Say what you want to say.' Lydia clasps her hands to her heart and takes a deep breath.
- 'I love you! Deeply!' Pasha freezes in his tracks in the middle of the corridor.
- 'Really? How so?' Lydia pours out her heart while Ilona quietly slips out of the archive behind Pasha's back.
- 'I understand I'm no match for you.' Literally 'I am not a pair to you'.
- 'You're a handsome man, and I'm old.' Then she throws her arms around Pasha's neck. Horrified, Pasha tries to peel her hands off.
- 'Lydia Alexeyevna, calm down, please!' He uses the polite imperative to ask her to calm down.
- 'You're behaving like a little girl.' Literally 'like a small one you conduct yourself'.
- 'I'm asking you, calm down!' He begs her to let go of his neck.
- 'Everything will be fine.' He pats her shoulder awkwardly.
- 'Maybe some medicine?' He wonders if she is feeling unwell.
- 'Do you need medicine?' Literally 'are medicines needed to you?'. Seeing that Ilona has safely escaped down the corridor with the folder, Lydia suddenly lets go and steps back.
- 'Forgive me!' She steps back and lowers her eyes modestly.
- 'Forgive and forget!' Both verbs are polite plural commands.
- 'Do you promise me?' She looks up at Pasha for his word.
- 'This is my womanly honour, after all!' She reminds him that her reputation is at stake.
- 'Do you promise?' Desperate to escape this awkward moment, Pasha nods eagerly.
- 'I promise! I promise!' He repeats his promise twice just to get away.
- 'Thank you!' Lydia smiles to herself as Pasha hurries into the archive too late.
