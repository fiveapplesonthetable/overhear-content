## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Oh, the collectors' Victim! Did you want something?' The word 'collectors' is in the plural 'of' form: 'victim of collectors'. Marina looks at him earnestly.
- 'Yes. I came to say thank you and ask forgiveness.' Earlier today at the university, she saw Arkady's gold watch on Captain Zavarzin's wrist and realised it was really Arkady who paid her debt.
- 'What for?' Arkady plays innocent.
- 'I offended you. I was wrong.' Notice the feminine endings on both 'offended' and 'wrong'. Earlier she accused him of staging the whole thing as a cheap trick. Arkady smiles.
- 'That's how naughty you are.' Literally, 'such a not-good one you are', said in a gentle, teasing tone. Marina's eyes fill with tears.
- 'Really, thank you.' And she adds what is weighing on her heart.
- 'But I just don't know how to thank you.' In the show she says 'how to repay you', and bursts into tears on Arkady's shoulder. Arkady awkwardly pats her back.
- 'Alright, all sorts of things happen. Oh, come on.' The neuter word 'every kind of thing' with the action word 'happens' is the everyday Russian way to say 'these things happen'.
- 'Calm down. Everything is behind us now.' The word at the end literally means 'behind'.
- 'Have a seat. Everything is fine now, it's all over.' He sits her down on his narrow iron bed and dries her tears.
- 'There, that's wonderful. That's normal.' For a moment, Arkady actually looks like a decent human being.
- 'A man must help a woman, right?' After 'help', the word 'woman' takes the 'to/for' ending. Marina nods gratefully — and then Arkady ruins everything.
- 'And a woman must help a man.' Notice how 'must' changes to the feminine form to match 'woman', and 'man' takes the 'to/for' ending. And with that, Arkady leans in and tries to kiss her! Marina shoves him back and slaps his face.
- 'What do you think you're doing?!' Literally, 'you what?!'. Arkady holds his cheek in disbelief.
- 'But you said yourself that you want to help!' He completely misread her tears of gratitude.
- 'But you don't know how! And I know!' At this point, Marina glares at him in fury.
- 'You have lost your mind!' Literally, 'you went down from your mind'. She storms out and slams the door, leaving Arkady calling after her.
- 'Wait, you just said so yourself!' The two-word phrase before the action word means 'just now'.
