## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Excuse me, do you think they are serious?' Even when asking Arkady for advice, Marina stays on the polite 'you'.
- 'Serious about what?' Arkady is calmly eating his breakfast.
- 'Well, were those men seriously threatening us?' Here, Arkady just shrugs at her.
- 'How would I know?' Literally 'from where do I know?' — a very common everyday reply.
- 'Well, what should I do?' Meanwhile, Arkady offers the most obvious answer.
- 'Give them the money.' Here, Marina flares up.
- 'How interesting! I don't print money!' At this point, Arkady takes another sip of tea.
- 'Well, sell something.' Meanwhile, Marina glances at the expensive gold watch gleaming on Arkady's wrist.
- 'Sell what? I don't have a gold watch either!' Notice that 'watch' in Russian is always plural — like 'glasses' or 'scissors' — and takes the plural 'of' form after 'there is no'. Arkady covers his wrist indignantly.
- 'Why are you shouting at me?' Here 'what' is used colloquially to mean 'why'.
- 'I have no money either.' Then he thinks of Marina's former lover, the university dean.
- 'Let your friend pay!' The particle at the start makes a third-person command: 'let him pay'. Marina defends the dean's honesty.
- 'Eduard Vladimirovich is a scholar, and he lives on a salary.' As mayor, Arkady knows the inside story of everyone in Sineozersk, and he laughs.
- 'Really? Then ask him where he put the computer lab.' And he adds two more details.
- 'Which the city gave him! And where Alla Yevgenyevna got her new fur coat!' Alla Yevgenyevna is the university rector — and the dean's wife. Marina refuses to believe it, though she decides to go and ask the dean for help.
- 'Listen, don't judge everyone by yourself!' Literally 'do not measure everyone by yourself'.
