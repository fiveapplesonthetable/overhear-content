## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What took you so long? Come in. Forty-seven thousand?' Literally, 'why are you for so long?' When Golovkin gasps the price of the television, Arkady repeats the number in disbelief, then marches into Klavdia Viktorovna's room.
- 'Klavdia Viktorovna, you have visitors! Ta-da!' Literally, 'to you guests'. But the surprise is on Arkady: Ilona is already standing there beside an even bigger television.
- 'You're too late, Arkasha. Other people came along.' The reflexive verb at the end literally means 'other people were found'.
- 'See what a beauty they've brought!' Russians affectionately call a handsome thing or a fine piece of equipment a 'handsome man', so it takes the animate accusative ending.
- 'How can this be? We had an agreement!' The verb for agreeing has the reciprocal ending, showing that the two of them made a deal with each other. Klavdia shrugs.
- 'First come, first served.' Literally, 'whoever stood up first, the slippers are his': a classic communal-flat proverb!
- 'All right, sweetheart. I keep my word. Here.' She calls Ilona 'daughter' and holds out her phone with the video of Samson. Arkady panics.
- 'Stop! One second, Klavdia Viktorovna!' Then he remembers what she complained about in the kitchen that morning.
- 'Didn't you want a plot at the cemetery? Next to your Grisha!' After 'next to', the preposition 'with' takes the instrumental case: 'with your Grisha'.
- 'I'll get it for you! And I'll leave you this television set too!' He offers both the burial plot and the second television. Ilona tries to stop her.
- 'Wait, Klavdia Viktorovna. We fulfilled our promises.' But for the old pensioner, a place beside her late husband beats any screen.
- 'Give me back my little phone. Forgive me, sweetheart.' She uses a soft diminutive for the phone as she takes it straight out of Ilona's hands.
- 'I won't be watching TV for much longer. But I'll be lying next to Grishka for eternity.' Literally, 'to watch television to me is not for long, and with Grishka to lie is eternity'. Ilona smiles coldly.
- 'It was a great pleasure doing business with you. All the best.' Her polite farewell is pure ice. Once Ilona leaves, Klavdia fixes Arkady with a stern look.
- 'Well? Just don't cheat me!' She still holds the phone until Arkady delivers the cemetery papers.
