## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Samson, you're driving very fast!' Russian has a special word for going by vehicle rather than on foot, and this is its 'you' form. Nina has had a few glasses of sparkling wine at dinner.
- 'My head is spinning already.' Literally, 'at me already the head is turning round'. Samson has his eye on the clock.
- 'We have to return the car before ten o'clock.' The dealership manager lent him the luxury car only until ten in the evening. After the little word 'before', both 'ten' and 'hours' take the 'of' form. And Samson is proud of his new title.
- 'I am, for your information, the people's candidate!' The two words in the middle mean 'by the way' or 'for your information'. He adds a rule.
- 'The people's candidate isn't late, got it?' In Russian, 'to be late' is a single action word, not an describing word. And then comes Samson's logic.
- 'And I mustn't drive slowly right now.' Literally, 'to me right now it is forbidden to drive slowly'. Why not?
- 'Otherwise everyone will realise the driver is drunk!' Literally, 'that behind the steering wheel is a drunk man'. Tipsy Nina finds this argument completely convincing.
- 'True. Go faster, Samson!' The word for 'faster' is the 'more' form of 'fast'. Suddenly Samson spots flashing lights ahead.
- 'Quiet, quiet! I see the police!' The word for 'police' changes its '-ya' ending to '-yu' as the direct object. Samson isn't worried.
- 'Now we'll get away from them.' Adding the prefix 'u-' to the word for driving means 'to drive away'. He turns into the dark courtyards behind the Society of the Blind.
- 'I know this town really well!' In the show he says he knows the town like his own five fingers. Nina is thrilled by the ride.
- 'Samson, I love you so much!' After all their quarrels, this evening out has made her happy again. Samson grins.
- 'And I love you too! Look!' Right ahead of them is a loading ramp in front of the glass wall of the car dealership.
- 'We're flying! Hop!' The car launches off the ramp and crashes straight through the showroom window, landing inside right on time at ten o'clock.
