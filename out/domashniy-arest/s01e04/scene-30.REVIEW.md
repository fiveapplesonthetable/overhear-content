## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'And what about Osipenko?' Osipenko is Pasha's surname; Ukrainian surnames ending in '-ko' never change their ending after a preposition. Stepan reminds his partner of the deadline.
- 'We only gave him three days.' Remember from Episode 2 that these two men aren't real government officials at all, but conmen after Arkady's millions. Fyodor Grigoryevich gets ready to call Pasha.
- 'Put on some background noise.' They play fake sound effects on a tablet so Pasha thinks they are busy men travelling the world. Stepan scrolls through his sound files.
- 'Will the stock exchange in Tokyo do?' The verb literally means 'will it come up and fit?'. Fyodor rolls his eyes.
- 'Are you joking? Put on the airport!' With airport announcements playing in the background, he dials Pasha's number, and Pasha answers right away.
- 'Yes, I'm listening.' This is a standard Russian way to answer the phone. Fyodor uses his smooth, official voice.
- 'Hello, Pavel Yuryevich, this is Fyodor Grigoryevich.' Both men address each other by first name and patronymic.
- 'I have good news for you.' In Russian, 'news' is plural here, 'good news-items'. And he explains.
- 'In Moscow they liked Sineozersk more than other places.' Literally, 'Sineozersk pleased in Moscow more than other places'. After 'more than', 'other places' takes the plural 'of' ending. So he offers Pasha a favour.
- 'We are giving you a little more time.' After 'a little', the word for 'time' takes its special 'of' form. And he hooks Pasha deeper.
- 'And we're starting to prepare the documents.' After 'start', Russian uses the imperfective infinitive.
- 'Gather the money!' A polite plural command. Pasha is thrilled.
- 'Thank you very much, I understood everything!' Literally, 'a big thank-you'. Fyodor wraps up the call.
- 'All right then, we'll be in touch.' He hangs up and turns off the airport noise. Stepan is thinking about Arkady's wife Vika, who actually has the money in her name.
- 'It won't be that easy with Anikeev's wife.' Literally, 'with the wife of Anikeev so simply it will not turn out'. Fyodor isn't worried.
- 'All right, I'll call her in the morning. Let's go to sleep.' And with that, the two conmen drive off into the night.
