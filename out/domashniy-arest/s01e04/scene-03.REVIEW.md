## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why does everyone beat me in this house?' Literally 'why do all beat me in this house?'. Nobody feels sorry for him. Instead, Nina looks at the little television on the counter.
- 'And who is that singing?' Old Klavdia Viktorovna is shocked that Nina doesn't recognise her idol, the famous Soviet singer Lev Leshchenko.
- 'Young people these days, they don't know anything! That's Leshchenko! A star!' And then Klavdia shares a proud memory from her youth.
- 'He courted me after a concert once. He asked me to marry him and come to Moscow!' In Russian, a woman says 'he called me into marriage', using a special adverb that applies only to women marrying a man. Arkady can't resist teasing her.
- 'And nobody asked you to marry him and move to Paris?' In this moment, Klavdia ignores him and sighs over the choice she made instead.
- 'I married my Grisha.' Literally 'I went out behind my Grisha', the standard phrase for a woman marrying a man.
- 'Now they won't even bury me with him!' Keep that wish in mind—being buried beside her late husband Grisha is going to matter later in this episode. Nina knows why the cemetery refuses.
- 'That's because everything there takes a bribe.' In this moment, Arkady scoffs, as an expert on bribes.
- 'What sort of bribes could there be? A hundred and fifty thousand roubles.' That is how much the cemetery director wants for a plot next to Grisha. Klavdia is furious.
- 'And is that a small sum to you? My pension is twelve thousand!' Literally 'and to you of this is little?'. Then she reminds Arkady what he did in episode two, when he broke into her padlocked fridge.
- 'You ate up my caviar! And you drank a poor old woman's cognac!' Both verbs are single-action verbs past forms: he ate it all up and drank it all down.
- 'I told you I'll pay it all back! What a greedy woman you are.' And he adds a dark joke.
- 'You can't put caviar in your coffin to take with you!' Literally 'into the coffin with yourself you won't put caviar'.
- 'Better into my coffin than to you! You're a bad man, Arkady.' Just then Samson comes in for breakfast, and Nina tries to calm the old lady down—and switches the channel.
- 'Klavdia Viktorovna, there's a child here, we're having breakfast.' Nina reminds Klavdia Viktorovna that there is a child right there at the breakfast table.
- 'And the television, by the way, is ours!' Remember that Klavdia wants a television of her very own. And the moment Nina changes the channel, a breaking news report comes on.
- 'Oh, look, they're showing Samson on television! Several million views already!' It's the video from episode one, where Samson threw the corrupt mayor out the window and said ordinary people can't live decent lives because of crooks like him—and the whole country is watching it.
