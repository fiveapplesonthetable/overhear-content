## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well now, consider we won the election!' Back in the communal kitchen, Arkady is already celebrating victory. "Schitay" means "consider it done" or "you can count on it".
- 'It is all a matter of proper PR organization.' Naturally, Arkady takes full credit for engineering the miracle.
- 'I still have not understood anything.' While Arkady gloats, poor Samson is still trying to process how he ended up on the president's schedule.
- 'I am very afraid to meet with the president.' Unlike seasoned politicians, Ivan is honest enough to admit he is terrified.
- 'You are a simple working man of the people.' Arkady turns Ivan's lack of polish into his best selling point.
- 'He really likes such simple people!' In Russian political theater, an ordinary guy from the provinces is gold in front of the cameras.
- 'From fear sweat has already broken out on me.' Literally the Russian says "sweat has stepped out", capturing his stage fright.
- 'And remember, this information is strictly classified.' Arkady lowers his voice: a leak now could ruin the whole setup.
- 'Do not tell anyone, even your wife.' Knowing how fast gossip travels in their building, Arkady singles Nina out by role.
- 'Right here our entire program is written.' He hands over a thick folder of talking points for Ivan to cram.
- 'The president really likes well-informed people.' Five seconds ago Ivan was supposed to be a simple working man; now he also has to memorize every statistic.
- 'You must answer the questions clearly.' No mumbling and no improvising when the big moment comes.
- 'Therefore come on, quickly pull yourself together!' Literally "gather yourself with your strength"—the classic Russian pep talk before a test.
- 'Yes, I have already pulled myself together completely!' Samson repeats the word back like a soldier trying to convince himself he is ready.
