## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'On the day of his arrest, Anikeev met with Samsonov.' Over the phone from the surveillance van, Lyova lays out the breakthrough from the visitor log.
- 'We received copies of all important records.' Those overlooked police files finally tie the whole conspiracy together.
- 'In the mayor's office there was a hidden camera.' Even better, Arkady's own office camera recorded the meeting.
- 'That is all the information obtained.' Having delivered the facts, Lyova waits for the general's reaction.
- 'Understood, I got your point.' "Yasno"—"clear" or "got it"—shows Kruglov grasps what this means for the case.
- 'And where is Major Lavrov right now?' Now that the investigation is paying off, the general asks after the man he just told to resign.
- 'Major Lavrov is writing a report right now.' Sitting right beside him, Lavrov is dutifully drafting the resignation letter Kruglov demanded.
- 'Tell him not to be difficult.' Literally "tell him not to climb into a bottle"—a classic Russian idiom for losing your temper or being stubborn over nothing.
- 'I will not accept any report from him!' That is Kruglov's gruff way of apologizing and keeping his best investigator on the job.
- 'In short, we continue our operation.' "Koroche"—"in short"—cuts off the drama and puts the team back to work.
- 'Is everything clear to you, Captain?' A brisk military check before hanging up.
- 'Yes sir, Comrade General, everything is clear!' "Yest"—literally "there is"—is how Russian soldiers and officers say "yes, sir!" to an order.
- 'That is it, he disconnected the call.' Lowering the receiver, Lyova turns to his partner with a grin.
- 'Now we can calmly continue our work.' Nobody is going into private security after all.
