## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'This is an elementary setup, dear Arkashenka!' Pasha turns the tables with a gloat. In Russian slang, "razvod" means both a divorce and a con job or setup.
- 'Did you set all this up in advance?' Only now does Arkady realize the court release and the getaway were bait to make him lead Pasha to the stash.
- 'How did you not figure this out?' "Prosyok" is a punchy colloquial word for "caught on" or "saw through the trick".
- 'You are not the same man, not at all!' Months in the communal flat have dulled the old shark's instincts.
- 'We made an honest agreement after all.' Hearing a career embezzler appeal to "an honest agreement" is rich.
- 'Your millions will now stay with me.' Pasha takes the heavy bags that Arkady and Lidia just finished loading for him.
- 'Give me at least my share.' Reduced to bargaining, the former mayor begs for a cut of his own loot.
- 'You will not get another single ruble.' "Ni rublya"—"not a single ruble"—leaves Arkady completely empty-handed.
- 'I can call the police right now.' With Lyolya out of reach and his leverage gone, Arkady tries an empty bluff.
- 'You have no proof whatsoever against me.' Pasha already wiped the phone evidence clean, so Arkady has nothing left to threaten him with.
- 'You will answer for this vile betrayal.' All Arkady can do now is shake his fist as his fortune drives away.
- 'Time to return to your cramped communal flat.' Instead of a jet to Monaco, Arkady's next stop is the shared hallway he just left.
- 'I will never forgive you for this deception.' Between thieves, stealing the stash is the one unforgivable sin.
- 'Get used to your new modest life.' Pasha's parting advice lands like a slap in the face.
