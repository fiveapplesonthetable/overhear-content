## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Marina, listen to me very carefully.' Just minutes after staging a loud breakup, Arkady slips back into Marina's room without Lyolya.
- 'Arkady, what do you want to say again?' Marina is exhausted by his back-and-forth drama and just wants him gone.
- 'I am leaving the city forever today.' Drop the act: this time he is telling her the real plan.
- 'And where will you go now?' Even though she is done with him, she cannot help asking where he is running off to.
- 'I am flying abroad, let's go together!' Despite all his bluster earlier, Marina is the one he actually wants to take with him.
- 'Please, immediately leave our family alone!' "Otstan" means "back off" or "leave me alone", and she says it without hesitation.
- 'I am not joking and am seriously getting out of here.' The slang word "svalivayu" means "I'm bolting" or "getting out of here for good".
- 'I am completely not interested in your plans.' Private jets and foreign villas mean nothing to Marina.
- 'Do you really not care at all?' For a narcissist like Arkady, indifference hurts much worse than anger. "Vsyo ravno" means "all the same".
- 'Yes, I completely do not care about you.' She repeats his "vsyo ravno" straight back to him so there is no mistaking it.
- 'Huge money and freedom await us there.' He makes one last pitch with the only two things he knows how to offer.
- 'Go away from here, I do not need your money!' Stolen millions will never buy Marina over.
- 'Then farewell, I must urgently leave.' Rejected for good, Arkady says "proshchay"—the heavy word for "farewell forever"—and walks out.
