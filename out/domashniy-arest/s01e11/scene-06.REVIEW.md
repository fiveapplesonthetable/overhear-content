## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'So, repeat that clearly once more.' Pavel demands a clear repetition.
- 'The president himself will arrive here soon.' The future verb indicates an imminent official arrival.
- 'He will visit an airfield in our region.' The location phrase specifies the geographic location.
- 'He wants to meet Ivan Samsonov in person.' The modal construction takes an 'with'-form object.
- 'Wait, is this information accurate at all?' Pavel questions the credibility of the sudden report.
- 'We contacted the presidential administration.' The self-action verb form verb governs an 'with'-form complement.
- 'There they officially confirmed this information.' The past plural verb indicates confirmation from officials.
- 'And will there be a meeting with me?' Pavel asks nervously.
- 'No further visits are planned.' The short passive form denotes completed scheduling.
- 'And what about the current governor?' Pavel brings up regional leadership with an emphatic particle.
- 'I can drive over there too!' The modal verb expresses Pavel's desperate eagerness to attend.
- 'No, they are not meeting with the governor either.' The reciprocal self-action verb form shows strict protocol.
- 'The president will meet only with Samsonov.' The restrictive particle emphasizes Samsonov's exclusive status.
- 'This is simply a catastrophe for our campaign!' Pavel laments the devastating blow to his chances.
