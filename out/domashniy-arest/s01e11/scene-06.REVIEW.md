## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'So, repeat that clearly once more.' Pavel is so rattled by what he just heard that he makes his assistant say it all over again.
- 'The president himself will arrive here soon.' Across town at city hall, the same news lands like a bomb rather than a gift.
- 'He will visit an airfield in our region.' It is a quick stopover at the local airfield, not a grand tour of the city.
- 'He wants to meet Ivan Samsonov in person.' Out of everyone in the province, the head of state asked for the ordinary guy from the communal flat.
- 'Wait, is this information accurate at all?' Pavel is grasping at straws, hoping the whole report is just a rumor.
- 'We contacted the presidential administration.' His assistant has already checked straight with Moscow.
- 'There they officially confirmed this information.' No rumor, no mistake: the visit is locked in.
- 'And will there be a meeting with me?' Notice how small Pavel sounds here, asking if there is a slot for him too.
- 'No further visits are planned.' That cold bureaucratic reply leaves Pavel completely out in the cold.
- 'And what about the current governor?' Surely the regional boss isn't being skipped too! "A kak zhe" is how Russians ask "and what about...?"
- 'I can drive over there too!' Now Pavel is ready to crash the airfield uninvited just to get in the frame.
- 'No, they are not meeting with the governor either.' Even the governor is shut out, which shows how tightly Moscow is stage-managing this stop.
- 'The president will meet only with Samsonov.' "Only with Samsonov" is the nightmare line for Pavel's entire campaign.
- 'This is simply a catastrophe for our campaign!' One handshake at the airfield is enough to wipe out Pavel's lead overnight.
