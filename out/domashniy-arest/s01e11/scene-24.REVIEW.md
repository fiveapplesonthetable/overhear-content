## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Stand calmly and do not make sharp movements.' Instead of a pre-flight checkup for the president, Samson has landed in the psychiatric admitting ward.
- 'Where did you bring me, release me!' Only now does Ivan realize the "strict doctors" from earlier were psychiatric orderlies.
- 'You are in the city hospital, calm down.' The orderly speaks in the bored, matter-of-fact tone of someone who hears this every shift.
- 'I am completely healthy, do not touch me with your hands!' Of course, shouting that you are completely sane in a psychiatric ward never convinces anyone.
- 'Are you going to swear or behave quietly?' In the hospital, the orderly gives him a simple choice: cut out the cursing or get strapped down.
- 'I will shout to this whole town!' Literally "shout onto the whole town"—the worst possible answer to give an orderly.
- 'There is a strict regime here and one cannot make loud noise.' Hospital rules are stated with "nelzya"—"it is not allowed".
- 'I need to urgently go to an important meeting.' Poor Ivan really does have a meeting at the airfield, which only makes him sound more delusional.
- 'The doctor will come soon and decide everything himself.' The orderly brushes off the protests and leaves the decision to the psychiatrist.
- 'What doctor, I am a candidate for mayor!' "Kakoy yeshchyo vrach"—"what doctor are you talking about!"—expresses total indignation.
- 'All our new patients say exactly that.' That classic psychiatric-ward punchline seals Ivan's fate: every patient claims to be somebody important.
- 'You will regret this mistake very much!' Threatening the staff only makes him look even more agitated.
- 'Sit on the bed and wait for the doctor here.' With a firm order, the orderly parks the mayoral candidate on a hospital cot.
