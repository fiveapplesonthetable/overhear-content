## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Who are you hinting at right now, Major?' General Georgiy Borisovich challenges his subordinate's insubordination.
- 'And I am not hinting at anyone.' The negative particle construction rejects the accusation.
- 'I am telling you straight to your face.' Lavrov uses an idiom of direct honesty.
- 'Will the president really punish you for the truth?' The question challenges bureaucratic cowardice.
- 'We simply want to return the money to the state.' The modal verb with basic verb form states their investigative purpose.
- 'You are constantly afraid of any risk.' The 'of'-form object follows the verb of fearing.
- 'That is why nothing ever happens with us!' The negative pronoun construction denotes stagnation.
- 'You have really overstepped now, Major Lavrov!' The past verb indicates insubordination.
- 'Write your resignation report immediately!' The commanding officer orders immediate resignation.
- 'For goodness' sake, I will write it right now!' Lavrov readily accepts dismissal.
- 'Your behavior is very brave, but foolish.' The short adjectives contrast courage with recklessness.
- 'You have just destroyed your own career.' The past tense verb marks irreparable consequences.
- 'At least my conscience will remain completely clean.' Lavrov stands by his moral integrity.
- 'Brave, but very foolish, Major.' The general gives his final grim assessment of the sacrifice.
