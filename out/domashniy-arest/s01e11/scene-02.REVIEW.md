## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Samson, you are late for football!' Nina reminds Ivan of his favorite hobby.
- 'Nothing terrible, it will do without football.' Samson dismisses the soccer game.
- 'I will not leave here anywhere.' The negative pronoun with a future-tense form expresses stubborn refusal.
- 'And how long do you plan to sit here?' Nina asks about his persistence.
- 'I will wait as long as is necessary.' The compound future verb with a correlative adverb affirms his patience.
- 'It is getting very cold outside.' This phrase describes the deteriorating weather.
- 'I am not cold at all in my jacket.' Samson uses a 'to'-form experiencer construction with a qualifying adverb.
- 'You are behaving like a stubborn child!' Nina compares him to an unruly boy using the self-action verb form construction for conduct.
- 'I simply love you very much.' Samson responds with an earnest declaration of affection.
- 'We have known each other only one month.' Nina repeats her rational objection with an adverb of limitation.
- 'For true love this is quite enough.' The preposition governing 'of'-form introduces a romantic justification.
- 'Please stop standing under the windows.' Nina urges him to leave.
- 'I will wait for your answer anyway!' Samson vows persistence using the adverbial idiom of determination.
