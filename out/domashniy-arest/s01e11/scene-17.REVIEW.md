## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Ilona, hi.' Direct informal greeting addressed to a female colleague.
- 'Is there an important meeting on Samsonov's schedule?' The verb for standing describes scheduled events on a calendar.
- 'What? No, of course not, there is no meeting!' Colloquial question followed by an emphatic negative construction with 'of'-form case.
- 'What a talkative man he is!' Exclamatory adjective construction with the intensifying particle.
- 'Great, now he is behaving insanely.' The nautical idiom expresses hitting a dead end or an unexpected absurd turn.
- 'Yes, I will go consult with a good doctor.' Future tense verb with a location phrase in the 'with/as' form.
