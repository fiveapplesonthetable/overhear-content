## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Ilona, hi.' Nina gets straight to the point as soon as Samsonov's campaign manager picks up.
- 'Is there an important meeting on Samsonov's schedule?' In Russian, an appointment on a schedule literally "stands" on the calendar.
- 'What? No, of course not, there is no meeting!' Because the visit is classified, Ilona lies through her teeth and denies the whole thing.
- 'What a talkative man he is!' Taking Ilona's denial at face value, Nina concludes Ivan is just rambling out of heartbreak.
- 'Great, now he is behaving insanely.' The Russian word "priplyli" literally means "we have sailed in" or "we've arrived", used sarcastically when things hit rock bottom.
- 'Yes, I will go consult with a good doctor.' Convinced her husband has lost his mind, Nina decides to call in psychiatric help—setting off a disaster of her own.
