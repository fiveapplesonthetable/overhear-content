## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You stole everything from me!' Fresh out of the clinic and still wired, Samson corners Nina and Danya in the kitchen.
- 'You stole even my memories!' Remember the nineties cafe flashback that opened the episode—those memories are what hurt him most.
- 'Vanya, you are absolutely wrong right now.' Nina stays calm and uses his warm home name "Vanya" to try to ground him.
- 'You destroyed everything yourself with your own hands!' Years of neglect and stubbornness broke their marriage long before Danya showed up.
- 'It was you who wormed into our family!' Turning on Danya, Ivan uses the blunt word "vlez"—literally "climbed in" where you don't belong.
- 'You ruined everything completely, understand?' Adding "ponyal?"—"got it?"—at the end of a sentence is a classic street challenge.
- 'There was nothing left to ruin there!' Instead of backing down, the young lawyer fires right back.
- 'Only ruins remained of your family.' Calling their marriage "only ruins" is accurate, cruel, and guaranteed to start a fight.
- 'Who did you just call ruins?' Ivan takes the metaphor literally and steps in with his fists clenched.
- 'Is that how you want to talk with me?' That quiet question is the last warning before punches fly.
- 'Back off from him immediately, Vanya!' Nina throws herself between the two men before the kitchen turns into a boxing ring.
- 'If you touch him, I will hit you!' Nobody in the apartment doubts for a second that Nina would make good on that threat.
- 'She is saving you from the hospital!' In the original Russian, Ivan says "intensive care" rather than just "the hospital".
- 'I am protecting a human being from your anger.' However she feels about either man, she refuses to let Ivan turn violent.
