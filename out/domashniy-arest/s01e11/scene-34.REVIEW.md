## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You stole everything from me!' Ivan angrily blames Nina for taking away their shared life.
- 'You stole even my memories!' Samsonov laments the loss of his past nostalgia.
- 'Vanya, you are absolutely wrong right now.' Nina calmly rejects his bitter accusations.
- 'You destroyed everything yourself with your own hands!' Nina points out his personal responsibility.
- 'It was you who wormed into our family!' Samson turns his fury toward lawyer Danya.
- 'You ruined everything completely, understand?' Ivan accuses Danya of wrecking his marriage.
- 'There was nothing left to ruin there!' Danya boldly defends his presence in the family.
- 'Only ruins remained of your family.' Danya describes the broken state of Ivan's household.
- 'Who did you just call ruins?' Samson steps closer, physically intimidating the young lawyer.
- 'Is that how you want to talk with me?' Ivan threatens violence against Danya.
- 'Back off from him immediately, Vanya!' Nina steps between the men to prevent a fight.
- 'If you touch him, I will hit you!' Nina issues a fierce warning to her estranged husband.
- 'She is saving you from the hospital!' Samson tells Danya that Nina is shielding him from a beating.
- 'I am protecting a human being from your anger.' Nina defines her moral boundary against Samson's wrath.
