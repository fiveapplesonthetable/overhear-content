## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Look at what they are doing over there!' Samson stares at the phone screen in outrage. The word "tvoryat" is what Russians use when someone is up to outrageous antics.
- 'They posted a photo straight from the shower.' Seeing Nina and her new boyfriend flaunt their vacation online is too much for him to bear.
- 'I will go and break his bones!' Ivan's first instinct is always to settle scores with his fists.
- 'Samson, calm down and don't wind yourself up.' Arkady steps in as the voice of reason. Literally he tells Ivan "don't wind yourself up" like a spring.
- 'You will soon win the election and become mayor.' For Arkady, winning city hall is the cure for every heartbreak.
- 'You will have a hundred such women!' That is a cynical politician's pep talk, and of course it misses how Ivan actually feels about Nina.
- 'Hello everyone! Why are you sad here?' Ilona breezes into the room, completely misreading the dark mood at the table.
- 'Ilona, why are you so joyful today?' When you are miserable, nothing is more annoying than someone walking in beaming.
- 'I have one important piece of news.' She pauses on purpose, savoring the moment before dropping her scoop.
- 'And what news do you have?' Arkady perks up right away, hoping for a campaign break.
- 'The president is coming to our town!' That single sentence changes the whole election in Sineozersk.
- 'The president? Are you speaking completely seriously?' Ivan forgets all about the shower photo for a second, stunned by what he just heard.
- 'Yes, the president himself is coming to us!' Adding "sam"—"the president himself"—drives home just how huge this visit is.
