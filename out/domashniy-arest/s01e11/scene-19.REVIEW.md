## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You must hide in a very reliable place.' While the apartment fills with people, Samson pulls Lyolya aside to protect their insurance policy.
- 'Why do I need to hide somewhere right now?' Lyolya does not yet realize how dangerous those photos on her phone really are.
- 'If they begin to interrogate me, I will tell everything.' At least Ivan knows his own weakness: under pressure, he cannot keep a secret.
- 'Therefore even I should not know the address.' You cannot spill a hiding place if you do not know where it is yourself.
- 'And how much time do I need to sit there?' She wants to know when it is safe to come out of hiding.
- 'Wait for my call strictly until eight in the evening.' Eight o'clock is the deadline: if things go smoothly, he will ring her by then.
- 'And what if you don't call me on time?' Like any good conspirator, she asks what the backup plan is.
- 'Then immediately give all the important photographs to the police.' That is the dead-man's switch keeping Pasha and Arkady in check.
- 'Fine, I can go to my friend Lena.' Her first idea is simply to crash at her best friend's apartment.
- 'You cannot go to Lena, they will find you there immediately.' Any obvious friend's address is the very first place Pasha's goons would look.
- 'Then I will choose another secret place myself.' Taking his warning to heart, she keeps the new hiding spot to herself.
- 'Well done, be very cautious on the street today.' "Molodets"—"good job" or "attaboy"—is how Samson praises her quick thinking.
- 'And right now strict doctors have come to see me.' Little does Ivan suspect that these "doctors" were actually called by Nina for a psychiatric commitment.
