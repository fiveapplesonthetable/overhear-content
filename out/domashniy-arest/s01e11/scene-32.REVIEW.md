## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'And I knew that you would return!' Klavdiya gloats triumphantly upon seeing him back in detention.
- 'Real evil always comes back.' A biting folk proverb applied directly to her former nemesis.
- 'Why are you standing and keeping silent?' Klavdiya notices his uncharacteristic lack of witty retorts.
- 'It is simply very hard for me right now.' Arkady admits his genuine psychological exhaustion.
- 'What is happening to you?' Klavdiya's mockery shifts into genuine surprise as his composure crumbles.
- 'Can these really be your crocodile tears?' An idiom questioning whether tears are genuine or feigned.
- 'Viktor, bring the Corvalol here quickly!' Klavdiya calls for common Russian heart calming drops.
- 'Don't you see: the man is unwell?' A compassionate observation acknowledging physical and emotional distress.
- 'Thank you, Klavdiya, I don't need anything.' Arkady politely declines medical intervention.
- 'Uncle Arkasha, please do not cry!' Young neighbor Slavik expresses childlike sympathy.
- 'Let's go to your room quickly.' Slavik offers to guide the weeping adult away from onlookers.
- 'Thank you, Slavik, you are a kind boy.' Arkady appreciates the child's tender support.
- 'Go rest, Arkady, calm your nerves.' Klavdiya gently urges him to recover his mental equilibrium.
- 'I will go and lie down in bed.' Arkady accepts the advice and retreats to his room.
