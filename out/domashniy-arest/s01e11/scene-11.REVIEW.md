## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'No one has ever made breakfast for me before.' Vika is genuinely touched. Russian piles up "never", "no one", and "not" in the same sentence to make the point emphatic.
- 'I was assigned to take care of you, Viktoriya.' Lev stays modest and polite, treating the pancake breakfast like part of his security detail.
- 'Thank you very much, this is very tasty!' Simple, warm praise that works at any Russian table.
- 'I actually like cooking food very much.' Behind the tough bodyguard exterior, Lev turns out to be a natural homemaker.
- 'I always made breakfast for my wife.' That quiet mention of his past marriage makes Vika curious right away.
- 'So are you married now or not?' She asks straight out, while still keeping the polite formal "vy".
- 'No, I have been divorced for a long time.' A plain, unhurried answer—no self-pity, just the facts.
- 'I was on duty all the time.' Being "na sluzhbe"—on state or security duty around the clock—cost him his family life.
- 'And I am actually a very loyal woman.' Coming from Vika, who has spent the season juggling powerful men, that line has a gentle irony.
- 'In our times that is a great rarity.' Lev takes her at her word with old-fashioned gallantry.
- 'Men often do not appreciate good women.' Thinking of Arkady and Pavel, she has plenty of reason to complain.
- 'A real man will always appreciate your kindness.' Lev's quiet reassurance is the kindest thing anyone has said to her all season.
