## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The general just hung up the phone.' Literally "put down the receiver"—Lyova sets the phone down inside the cramped surveillance van.
- 'And what did our boss say?' Pen in hand over his resignation sheet, Lavrov braces for the verdict.
- 'He did not accept your resignation report.' Those six words save Lavrov's twenty-year career.
- 'Really? Did he refuse to sign it?' After shouting in the general's face earlier, Tolya can hardly believe he is off the hook.
- 'He ordered our operation to continue.' Thanks to the visitor log they uncovered, the wiretap mission is back on.
- 'Does that mean I won't be fired from service?' His pension—and his mission—are both safe.
- 'You remain a major, Anatoliy.' Calling him by his full first name adds a touch of warm respect.
- 'What a huge relief this is for me!' For a guy who lives for his work, staying on the case means everything.
- 'We are colleagues again now!' After almost quitting together an hour ago, the two partners celebrate.
- 'I am very glad to work with you.' Moments like this show how much the veteran major values his younger partner.
- 'Then sit down at the surveillance equipment quickly.' Celebration over—there are still crooks in Sineozersk to catch.
- 'We must monitor every step of the candidate.' Literally "follow behind every step" as election day approaches.
- 'Our work is only just beginning.' With the final episode ahead, all the threads of the case are coming together.
- 'Then let us continue listening to this broadcast.' Putting their headphones back on, they tune back into the communal flat.
