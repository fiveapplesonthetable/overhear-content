## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Greetings to everyone! I am Ivan Samsonov.' Whatever the doctor injected worked a little too well: Ivan marches in wired like a human lightning bolt.
- 'Hello, Ivan. Come in here, please.' Lavrov greets him politely, though he can already see something is off.
- 'This is my political consultant and my psychiatrist.' Only in Sineozersk does a mayoral candidate show up to a briefing flanked by both a spin doctor and a shrink.
- 'We are listening to you carefully, Ivan.' Keeping his voice calm, Lavrov tries to gauge whether Samsonov can hold a normal conversation.
- 'What did the doctors actually do with you?' One look at Ivan's wide, unblinking eyes tells Lavrov the clinic did something drastic.
- 'They did not do anything bad with me.' Samson waves the question off, feeling invincible as the stimulants kick in.
- 'I simply have a powerful inner resilience.' "Vnutrenniy resurs"—"inner resource"—is the bureaucratic buzzword they use to explain away his chemical buzz.
- 'You seem too excited and abrupt.' Even trying to be polite, Lavrov has to point out that the candidate is practically vibrating.
- 'I am ready to speak with any person!' Ten minutes ago he was unconscious; now he is ready to debate the whole world.
- 'The main thing is do not say unnecessary foolishness.' Lavrov's only goal is keeping Ivan from saying something crazy in front of the cameras.
- 'I am a simple person from the people.' Even on hospital stimulants, Ivan remembers the #1 line Arkady drilled into him.
- 'We rely on your good sense.' That sounds less like confidence and more like a quiet prayer.
- 'I will definitely win a clean victory.' In Russian you literally "hold a victory" when you win an election or a battle.
- 'Then sit down and repeat your speech.' Lavrov gets him into a chair before he can march through a wall.
