## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Diversification, diversification... I am tired of repeating this!' Pacing the hallway, Samson trips over the six-syllable buzzword Arkady is forcing him to memorize.
- 'Vanya, you must clearly learn the terms.' Arkady plays the strict tutor: if Ivan stumbles over the big words, the whole illusion falls apart.
- 'Why do I need to know these difficult words?' Ivan still cannot see why an ordinary guy needs textbook economics to talk about his own town.
- 'You are a candidate and will soon meet with the president!' That is the whole stake of the afternoon in one reminder.
- 'My head already aches badly.' Repeating all this political jargon is giving Samson a literal headache.
- 'Pull yourself together, the city economy depends on you.' Arkady makes it sound like a civic duty, though of course his own freedom is what really hangs on it.
- 'What does this diversification actually mean at all?' After repeating the word twenty times, Ivan finally asks what he is actually saying.
- 'This is the development of different directions in business.' Arkady boils the fancy term down to a one-line definition.
- 'You should have said so in simple words right away!' "Tak by srazu" means "you should have said so right from the start!"—now it finally makes sense to him.
- 'Repeat one more time loudly and confidently.' Like a stage director before curtain call, Arkady drills his star one last time.
- 'Diversification of the municipal economy of Sineozersk!' Samson rolls the whole mouthful out without a single stumble.
- 'Now that sounds like a real mayor!' Arkady grins—his homemade candidate is ready for prime time.
