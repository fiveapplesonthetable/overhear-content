- 'My long-awaited pension is right around the corner.' A colorful Russian idiom for an imminent life event.

- 'But why does leadership not value us at all?' Negative question with an accusative personal pronoun.
- 'Calm down, the leadership simply does not know the whole truth.' Reflexive imperative followed by genitive of negation.
- 'Look what lies in this old box.' Imperative perception verb introducing a prepositional location clause.
- 'This is the mayor's visitor log from police evidence.' Nominal sentence with layered genitive noun modifiers.
- 'Why did the investigators take this strange document?' Past perfective question about police procedures.
- 'The names of all guests before the arrest are recorded here.' Short passive participle agreeing with a plural subject.
- 'This could help us solve the real crime!' Modal auxiliary verb followed by a dative pronoun and perfective infinitive.
