## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I will reach the sofa myself.' Limping into the room, Ivan waves off help and insists on making it to the couch on his own.
- 'Consider that you were born under a lucky star.' To be "born under a lucky star" is a classic Russian expression for walking away from trouble in one piece.
- 'I have had enough of these Jedis today.' After tangling with Danya's martial-arts moves on the street, Ivan has had his fill of kung-fu fighters.
- 'Yulia, bring the first aid kit quickly!' Every Russian home has an "aptechka"—a household medicine box stocked with bandages and iodine.
- 'Right away, Mom, I'm already bringing it!' Their daughter Yulia comes running with the kit.
- 'There is no need to bring any first aid kit.' Like a stubborn kid, he wants no bandages and no fuss.
- 'The doctors already gave me injections today.' Between the tranquilizer and the stimulants at the clinic, he has been poked with enough needles for one lifetime.
- 'Mom, look quickly at the TV!' Suddenly the evening news flashes a familiar face on the screen.
- 'They are showing people's candidate Samsonov there!' Despite all the chaos at the clinic, his campaign segment is airing across the region.
- 'Everyone talks, but Samsonov acts!' Nina reads his campaign slogan off the screen—which sounds hilarious right after his street fight.
- 'And now they moved on to other news.' Moments later, the anchor pivots to a breaking report from Moscow.
- 'Did the president cancel the important meeting?' A sudden weather or schedule change means the airfield stopover was called off after all.
- 'Well, now everything has definitely ended.' Neither candidate got the presidential handshake, leaving the race wide open again.
- 'Can you call me back a little later?' When his phone rings, exhausted Ivan chooses a quiet evening with his family over politics.
