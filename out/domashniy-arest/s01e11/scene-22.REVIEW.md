## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Wait one minute right outside this door.' Before leaving the communal apartment for good, Arkady tells his guards to wait in the hall.
- 'Fine, Arkady Borisovich, we are waiting for you here.' Hearing the full name and patronymic again reminds Arkady that he is back in command.
- 'I will quickly grab all necessary documents right now.' With his foreign passport back in his pocket, he just needs his last papers.
- 'The car is already parked downstairs in the courtyard.' In Russian, a parked car literally "stands" downstairs in the yard.
- 'Is the driver ready to head toward the airport?' Every thought in Arkady's head is already pointed toward the runway.
- 'Yes, all things have already been loaded into the trunk.' Bags packed, trunk shut—nothing left to hold him in Sineozersk.
- 'I need to think calmly before the long journey.' According to an old Russian custom, travelers pause quietly for a moment before setting off on a big trip.
- 'Do not worry, our flight departs strictly on time.' The guard reassures him there is plenty of time to make the plane.
- 'I am finally completely free from house arrest.' After months of ankle monitors and communal soup, he savors the word "free".
- 'I congratulate you on the long-awaited city court decision.' Even the bodyguard knows how hard-fought that court ruling was.
- 'Thank you, now a new life begins for us.' Arkady is already picturing himself abroad with his millions—a dream that will last about twenty minutes.
- 'We are ready to reliably protect you everywhere.' Famous last words from a security detail that is about to let Pasha rob him blind.
- 'Excellent, open the door, we are exiting immediately!' Arkady steps across the threshold like a man who owns the world again.
