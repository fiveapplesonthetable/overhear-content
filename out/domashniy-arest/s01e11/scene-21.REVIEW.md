## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well then, Pashenka, why have you puffed up so?' Fresh out of house arrest, Arkady pinches Pasha's pride by calling him "Pashenka" and asking why he is pouting like a hamster.
- 'Arkady, there is no need to mock me now.' Pasha is fuming, forced to chauffeur the boss he tried to lock away.
- 'You were jealous of me and wanted to take the money!' Arkady lays Pasha's betrayal out in plain sight: he wanted the mayor's chair, the wife, and the cash.
- 'If you turn me in, I will tell everything.' In the original line Pasha says he will "sing like a nightingale" to the investigators if Arkady crosses him.
- 'Don't be afraid, I will not turn you in.' Right now Arkady cares much more about his hidden millions than about settling scores.
- 'Our private plane departs in three hours.' That three-hour countdown sets the clock ticking for the rest of their escape.
- 'And where must we go quickly right now?' Pasha plays along dutifully, acting as if he is just following orders.
- 'The car is already waiting for us in the courtyard.' Everything is lined up for a fast getaway from the communal building.
- 'Wait, are you really going together with me?' Notice that flash of nerves from Pasha—he was counting on going to the stash alone.
- 'Of course, I am curious where the millions are hidden.' There is no way Arkady is letting Pasha near that pile of cash without him.
- 'I will show you the secret place with the money.' Pasha smoothly recovers his composure and agrees to lead the way.
- 'Then get ready quickly, time does not wait for us!' Literally "time does not wait for us"—Arkady is itching to get his hands on the bags.
- 'Fine, let us go out onto the street faster.' Pasha plays the obedient sidekick, all while leading Arkady straight into a trap.
