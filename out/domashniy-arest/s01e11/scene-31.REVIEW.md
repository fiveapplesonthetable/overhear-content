## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Arkady Borisovich, prepare for the procedure.' Even while strapping an ankle monitor back onto him, the officer uses the formal name and patronymic.
- 'I am ready, come into the hallway.' Arkady holds out his leg without a fuss—he knows the drill by heart now.
- 'This is a mandatory technical procedure.' Bureaucratic language makes locking a man in a communal flat sound like routine maintenance.
- 'I already wore this electronic bracelet.' Only a few hours ago he was celebrating taking that very cuff off.
- 'We must fasten the sensor on your leg.' Snapping the plastic strap shut officially ends Arkady's brief taste of freedom.
- 'Please do not tighten the strap too tightly.' Stripped of his millions, the one thing he can still negotiate is a little room around his ankle.
- 'Everything is ready, the electronic bracelet is working.' A blinking light confirms he is back on the federal map.
- 'You know everything very well yourself from here.' Since this is his second round under house arrest, the officer skips the standard lecture.
- 'I remember all the rules established by law.' Hearing a career crook recite his respect for the law has a dry irony to it.
- 'You are strictly forbidden from leaving this apartment.' Cross the front threshold again, and the next stop is a real prison cell.
- 'I will sit at home and wait for the trial.' Remember that double meaning of "sidet" in Russian: both "sit at home" and "do time".
- 'Any breach of signal is recorded immediately.' One bad signal from the ankle cuff brings a squad car straight to the courtyard.
- 'I do not intend to run away anywhere.' With no passport and no cash left, he has nowhere to run anyway.
- 'Then observe the regime and do not violate.' With that standard parting warning, the officer leaves Arkady to his neighbors.
