## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Arkady Borisovich, prepare for the procedure.' A formal administrative command using the patronymic.
- 'I am ready, come into the hallway.' Arkady courteously invites the officer into the residence.
- 'This is a mandatory technical procedure.' Official bureaucratic terminology explaining the fitting.
- 'I already wore this electronic bracelet.' Arkady notes his familiarity with the tracking device.
- 'We must fasten the sensor on your leg.' The officer describes the physical installation process.
- 'Please do not tighten the strap too tightly.' A polite negative command asking for physical comfort.
- 'Everything is ready, the electronic bracelet is working.' Confirmation that the monitoring signal is active.
- 'You know everything very well yourself from here.' An acknowledgment of the prisoner's prior experience.
- 'I remember all the rules established by law.' A formal confirmation of legal compliance.
- 'You are strictly forbidden from leaving this apartment.' A direct statement of the core house arrest restriction.
- 'I will sit at home and wait for the trial.' Arkady expresses his passive acceptance of detention.
- 'Any breach of signal is recorded immediately.' A warning highlighting automated electronic surveillance.
- 'I do not intend to run away anywhere.' Arkady disclaims any intention to attempt escape.
- 'Then observe the regime and do not violate.' A final standard police instruction ordering good behavior.
