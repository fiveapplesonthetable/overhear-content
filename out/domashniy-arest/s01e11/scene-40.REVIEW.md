## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'How much money does it take to be happy?' After watching Arkady lose every ruble he stole, the closing song brings back this central question with a wink.
- 'How much money do you need?' Now the singer points the question straight at the listener.
- 'A little bit there, a little bit here.' That is how every corrupt fortune in the show began: one quiet envelope at a time.
- 'And just like that, you've got six million!' Before long, the pocket money has snowballed into millions.
- 'A villa in Monaco, an account in Switzerland.' Exactly the kind of getaway Arkady was dreaming of before Pasha intercepted him.
- 'But you need, need, need even more!' Greed never knows when to stop, which is why none of these crooks ever retires on time.
- 'A supermodel wife and a mistress too.' Even the personal lives of the officials follow the same predictable checklist.
- 'Only she's just a little bit younger!' A sly little rhyme that pokes fun at middle-aged vanity.
- 'A white yacht sails across the sea.' Picture the cartoon mayor cruising across the waves without a care in the world.
- 'Happiness is getting closer, almost here!' No matter how much he steals, true happiness stays just out of reach.
- 'Paintings by Dalí and Van Gogh in the kitchen.' World-famous art hung next to the refrigerator is the ultimate parody of newfound wealth.
- 'A church, a mosque, and a synagogue are built.' Just in case heaven is keeping score, he donates to all three at once.
- 'And for insurance, a Buddhist temple too!' Throwing in a fourth religion "for insurance" is the punchline of the verse.
- 'If it doesn't pan out here, it will pan out there!' One way or another, the crooked official hopes a deal will pull him through.
