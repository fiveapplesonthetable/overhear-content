## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, Pasha, hi.' Informal telephone greeting using the diminutive name form.
- 'You are the one who will be sitting in prison soon, Arkady!' The verb for sitting down is used colloquially to mean going to jail.
- 'I cannot get through to my wife.' The prefixed verb of telephoning takes a preposition and a genitive object.
- 'Then I will give all this data to the police.' Future perfective verb followed by a dative noun.
- 'Alright, calm down! What do you want from me?' The imperative verb of calming down followed by a question phrase.
- 'Take a pen quickly, I will dictate.' The imperative imperfective verb of taking accompanied by an adverb and a compound future tense.
