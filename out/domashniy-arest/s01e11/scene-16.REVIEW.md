## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, Pasha, hi.' Arkady starts the call in a purring, friendly voice because he knows he holds the winning cards.
- 'You are the one who will be sitting in prison soon, Arkady!' In Russian, the word for "sit" also means "do time in prison", so Pasha turns Arkady's joke about the mayor's chair into a threat.
- 'I cannot get through to my wife.' Arkady casually brings up Vika, setting the trap for his former deputy.
- 'Then I will give all this data to the police.' With the incriminating photos from Pasha's phone in hand, Arkady names his price.
- 'Alright, calm down! What do you want from me?' Pasha's swagger vanishes in an instant once he realizes Arkady has real proof.
- 'Take a pen quickly, I will dictate.' Just like old times, the boss is giving orders and his deputy is taking notes.
