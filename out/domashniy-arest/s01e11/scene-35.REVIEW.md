## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vanya, are you completely alright?' Out on the pavement, the kitchen showdown has spilled into a full street brawl, and Nina rushes to check on Ivan.
- 'Yes, everything is fine, don't worry.' Even with a bruised face, Samson tries to act as if nothing happened.
- 'Stop this fight right now!' She shouts at the combatants to break it up before the police or reporters arrive.
- 'Let's go home quickly, please.' Notice how she still calls the communal flat "home" for the two of them.
- 'Fine, calm down, let's go quickly.' Hearing the panic in her voice, Ivan finally lets her lead him back inside.
- 'Mom, what happened out there on the street?' Inside the apartment, young Misha meets them at the door with wide eyes.
- 'Your dad had a big fight again.' Saying "again" tells you this is hardly the first time Samson has come home with bruised knuckles.
- 'Dad, did someone beat you up?' For a little boy who sees his father as a tough guy, that is the unthinkable question.
- 'Nobody beat me up, son.' Wounded pride makes Ivan deny it at once in front of his boy.
- 'I was simply defending our honor.' He dresses up a jealous street fight as a noble defense of family honor.
- 'Vanya, you must not fight on the street!' "Dratsya nelzya"—"fighting is not allowed"—is the rule she lays down for both father and son.
- 'You are the future mayor of the city after all!' Even after everything, Nina is still reminding him of who he can become.
- 'You are right, I am a candidate after all!' That title immediately puffs his chest back out.
- 'Then behave calmly, Vanya.' If he wants to run the city, he has to start by governing his own temper.
