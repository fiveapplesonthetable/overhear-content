## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Here you are, my dear money!' Arkady talks to the duffel bags of stolen cash more tenderly than he ever talked to his wife or mistress.
- 'Hello, dear Arkady Borisovich!' Waiting by the stash is his loyal former secretary Lidia Alexeyevna, beaming with pride.
- 'Take out all the large bags quickly.' With a plane to catch, Arkady wastes no time getting the haul out into the open.
- 'We are carefully loading all the money.' Every brick of bills goes straight into the getaway bags.
- 'Pavel never valued his own employees.' Why did Lidia betray Pasha and guard the money for Arkady? Because Pasha treated the office staff like dirt.
- 'He always spoke rudely with me.' A little basic politeness at work would have saved Pasha his entire fortune.
- 'And I regularly spat in his tea!' That is the secretary's secret weapon against an arrogant boss—and she says it with a proud smile.
- 'Thank you very much for your help, Lidia Alexeyevna.' Arkady may be a crook, but he always remembered his secretary's name and patronymic.
- 'One must know how to work with staff properly.' "Kadry" means "personnel" or "staff", echoing the famous Soviet slogan about managing people.
- 'You showed real courage and loyalty.' Flattering her costs Arkady nothing and buys him millions.
- 'I always respected only you, Arkady.' For Lidia, the old mayor was the only real boss in city hall.
- 'Now we will take the bags to a safe place.' Arkady thinks the hard part is over and the runway is clear.
- 'Be very careful with these millions.' She waves him off like a mother sending her son out with a packed lunch.
- 'Do not worry, now everything will be fine.' Famous last words, spoken seconds before Pasha's ambush.
