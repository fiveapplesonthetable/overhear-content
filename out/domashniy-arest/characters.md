# domashniy-arest: characters and places

Show: Домашний арест (Domashniy Arest, "House Arrest"), S1. Setting: the small provincial town of Синеозерск
(Sineozersk), lakes and meadows, an 800-year-old monastery. Names below are as they appear in the subtitles.

Story role keys (see NOTES.md): the protagonist key `anya` (voice alisa) is the viewpoint character of the scene.
Arkady is the viewpoint character whenever he is in a scene (child or adult): `anya`, with
`LABELS = {"anya": "Аркадий"}` in addition to the other roles' labels. Other characters use descriptive role keys
with voices `anja` / `angelina`. Grammar always follows the character, not the voice: Arkady and Samson are men
(я родился, я сказал), whatever voice reads them.

## Аркадий Борисович Аникеев (Arkady; the mayor of Sineozersk; first seen s01e01)
- Who: mayor for four years, running for a second term. Charming in public, cynical and vindictive in private.
  Grew up in a communal flat (коммуналка), where he is still officially registered; after he is caught taking a
  bribe the court puts him under house arrest there, with an electronic ankle tag. His wife Vika and his deputy Pasha
  betray him. Childhood nicknames: Аркаша, Аркашка; the bully's name for him is "очкарик" (four-eyes).
- Appearance for the art (adult): man about 40, slim, light skin `#F6D2B5`, neat dark-brown hair with a side parting,
  small round thin-rimmed glasses, clean-shaven, a small confident smile. Navy suit `#2F3E66`, white shirt, red tie
  `#C8373B`. Under house arrest: same suit trousers, white shirt with rolled sleeves, tie gone, and a black ankle tag
  with a small green light on his right ankle; socks or slippers (the generator tends to draw him barefoot). Later in the flat: a bandaged finger (from s01e01 scene 17 on).
- Appearance for the art (child, 1980s flashback): thin boy of about 10, big round glasses, dark-brown hair in a bowl
  cut, Soviet school uniform: blue jacket `#3D5A9E`, white shirt, red pioneer scarf `#D94A3D`.
- Story role key / voice: `anya` -> alisa. Label: Аркадий.
- Speech: polite *вы* in public and with strangers, *ты* with Samson, Pasha and Vika. Likes "Послушай", "Всё",
  "Спасибо", "Я серьёзно", "У нас мир".

## Иван Самсонов, "Самсон" (Samson, Ваня; Arkady's childhood neighbour and bully; first seen s01e01)
- Who: big, hot-tempered, simple. As a boy he dunked little Arkasha's head in the toilet. Now an excavator operator
  on a building site, still living in the same communal flat with wife Nina and kids Yulia and Slavik. Ex-army.
- Appearance for the art (adult): broad, heavy-set man about 40, light skin, very short dark-blond buzz cut, short stubbly dark-blond
  beard, thick neck. Blue-and-white striped army vest (тельняшка: white `#FFF6EA` with navy `#2F3E66` stripes) under an
  open khaki work jacket `#7A7A4A`. Big hands.
- Appearance for the art (child): stocky boy of about 11, dark-blond buzz cut, striped blue-and-white T-shirt,
  grey shorts.
- Story role key / voice: `samson` -> anja. Label: Самсон.
- Speech: informal *ты*, blunt, short sentences. "Всё нормально", "Да ладно!". (Source is full of swearing: never in
  story lines.)

## Нина Самсонова (Nina, Нин, Нинок; Samson's wife; first seen s01e01)
- Who: a nurse (she "brings bandages home from work"), tired of the communal flat, the leaking pipe and the chores;
  dreams of the sea, a proper flat and a bidet. More practical than Samson.
- Appearance for the art: woman about 38, light skin, curly bleached-blonde shoulder-length hair `#E8C56B`, pink
  quilted housecoat `#E58FA8`, hands often on hips; sometimes a roll of white bandage.
- Story role key / voice: `nina` -> angelina. Label: Нина.
- Speech: *ты* with family and with Arkady; sharp, practical. "Я хочу нормально жить!", "Я хочу на море!"

## Юля и Славик (Yulia / Yulka and Slavik; the Samsonovs' children; first seen s01e01)
- Yulia: teenage girl about 16, light skin, long dark-brown hair in a high ponytail, oversized mint hoodie `#8FD1B8`,
  phone in hand. Needs to get into university.
- Slavik: boy about 10, light skin, ginger hair, freckles, yellow T-shirt `#F2C14E`.
- Role keys if they ever speak: `yulia` / `slavik` (anja or angelina). Labels: Юля / Славик.

## Павел Юрьевич, Паша (Pasha; Arkady's deputy, later acting mayor; first seen s01e01)
- Who: smooth, ambitious. Pushes the Moscow industrial "cluster" (кластер); secretly sets up Arkady's arrest and is
  having an affair with Vika. Becomes acting mayor and runs for mayor himself.
- Appearance for the art: man about 35, slim, light skin, slicked-back light-brown hair with a shiny quiff, thin
  smug smile, light-grey suit `#9AA3AD`, lilac tie `#9B7BC8`, slim black folder or phone.
- Story role key / voice: `pasha` -> anja (or angelina if anja is taken in the scene). Label: Паша.
- Speech: *ты* with Arkady ("Аркаш"), oily politeness in public.

## Виктория, Вика (Vika, Викуля; Arkady's wife; first seen s01e01)
- Who: glamorous; all of Arkady's money is in her name; in love with Pasha for a month. Drives a white Porsche
  Cayenne.
- Appearance for the art: woman about 35, light skin, long straight platinum-blonde hair `#F1E3B0`, big sunglasses
  pushed up on her head, white coat `#FFF6EA`, red lips, a car key in her hand.
- Story role key / voice: `vika` -> angelina. Label: Вика.

## Марина Сергеевна Валинкина (Marina Sergeyevna; university lecturer and activist; first seen s01e01)
- Who: associate professor at Sineozersk University (surname garbled in the subtitles: Валинкина / Былинкина /
  Булинкина). Protests with her students against the demolition of the monastery wall; fired by her dean (and
  ex-lover) in s01e02. Lives in the same communal flat as Arkady with her son Lyosha. Approached by the security
  service to inform on Arkady: refuses, then (end of s01e02) agrees.
- Appearance for the art: woman about 33, light skin, dark-brown hair in a low bun with a pencil through it, no
  glasses, teal coat `#2E8F8A` over a mustard scarf `#E0B341`, a folder of papers held up like a placard.
- Story role key / voice: `marina` -> angelina (or `anya` when she is the viewpoint character, label Марина).
  Label: Марина.
- Speech: polite *вы* with the mayor; firm, idealistic. "Не надо врать."

## Лидия Алексеевна (Lidia Alekseyevna; the mayor's secretary; first seen s01e01)

## Places
- Коммуналка (the communal flat): long dim corridor with old olive-green walls `#7C8B4F`, brown wooden doors,
  bicycles and coats on hooks, a single bare lamp; a shared kitchen with several old stoves; the shared toilet with a
  leaking riser pipe wrapped in rags. Recurring flat colours: olive and mustard. In the 1980s flashback the same
  corridor, warmer and brighter (a sepia-orange tint `#D9955B`).
- Arkady's room in the flat: tiny, behind a wardrobe; narrow iron bed, faded wallpaper, the white box of the ankle-tag
  base station on a stool.
- Mayor's office (мэрия; Pasha's office once he is acting mayor, s01e02 sc. 9): big desk of dark wood, tall window, flag-like coloured drape (no symbols), dominant deep
  blue `#3E5C8A`.
- Square in front of the town hall: pale stone building with columns, a small stage with a microphone.
- University: dean's office with bookshelves, warm brown.
- Santa Fe café: dim, red booths `#A63D40`, a corner table half hidden by a plant.
- Arkady and Vika's house (flashback, s01e02): big modern house, cream walls, a New Year tree with gold balls, a
  dark leather sofa; warm gold `#E0B341` light.
- Security-service headquarters: grey-green office, long table, a big wall screen (shapes only), filing boxes for
  the archive; cold steel-grey `#6E7F8A`.
- Building site: mud, a yellow excavator, stacked white radiators, a small site cabin with a kettle; orange and
  grey.
- Governor's office / gym: wood panels, a judo mat; restaurant: white tablecloths, dim amber.
