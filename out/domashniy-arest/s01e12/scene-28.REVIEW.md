## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Is all of this only for the post of minister?' Watching the governor's men haul out bag after bag of cash, even a seasoned bribe-taker like Arkady is stunned by the price tag.
- 'And what did you think? Everything is serious there.' In the subtitle he says "everything there is grown-up" ("po-vzroslomu")—small-town bribes are kid stuff compared to Moscow.
- 'Maybe it is worth bargaining a little?' Ever the haggler, Arkady wonders if you can ask for a discount on a cabinet post.
- 'Arkasha, they never bargain in Moscow.' Stepanovich laughs off the provincial question: in the capital, the price is the price.
- 'I will quickly win all this money back.' He uses the slang word "otobyu" ("beat/win back"), treating a public ministry like an investment that will pay for itself in six months.
- 'The sum really seems enormous.' Remember that Arkady is secretly working with the FSB—he is marveling at how much bait the governor just brought out.
- 'I decided to make you governor of the region.' Why is Stepanovich suddenly promising Arkady the governorship? To keep him smiling and docile until they get to the lake.
- 'Are you really speaking completely seriously?' Even knowing this is part of a sting, Arkady's ego can't help lighting up at the word "governor".
- 'You are a young and very promising leader.' He lays the flattery on with a trowel, using standard Soviet-style praise for a rising official.
- 'I will not give the region to strangers.' "Chuzhim lyudyam" ("to alien/outside people") is how corrupt clans talk about anyone outside their circle.
- 'Load all the bags into the trunk quickly.' Once those heavy bags hit the trunk, the FSB's evidence for the bribery sting is locked in.
- 'Viktor Stepanovich, huge thanks for your trust.' Both men are lying through their teeth here, each thinking he is the one playing the other.
- 'That's it, relax, now you are under protection.' Chilling irony: "under protection" actually means "my thugs have you in the car and you aren't leaving".
- 'I will never let you down.' And with that polite bow, Arkady climbs into the SUV that is taking him out to the ice hole.
