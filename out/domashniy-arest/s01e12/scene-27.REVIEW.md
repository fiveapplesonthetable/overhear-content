## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Stepanych, I will soon become a federal minister!' Hanging up the phone, the governor bursts in on his aide Stepanych like a kid on his birthday.
- 'What ministry specifically will you head?' Stepanych knows his boss well enough to ask which slice of the federal pie they are getting.
- 'The ministry of fisheries of the whole country!' And there is the comic punchline: not finance or defense, but Minister of Fish for all of Russia.
- 'Well look at that, real good luck!' Stepanych doesn't laugh at "fisheries"—to an insider, any federal agency is a license to print money.
- 'Even I did not expect such a gift.' Coming right after a near-disaster with the Sineozersk scandal, a promotion to Moscow looks like a miracle.
- 'I congratulate you from all my heart.' "Ot vsey dushi" literally means "from the whole soul", the warmest way to congratulate someone in Russian.
- 'This is a real gold mine after all!' Russian calls a gold mine a "golden bottom" ("zolotoye dno")—and for fisheries, a "golden seabed" is unintentionally funny.
- 'Yes, this opens huge opportunities.' Neither man says a word about helping fishermen; "opportunities" means kickbacks on every trawler and caviar quota.
- 'Now we will solve absolutely all problems.' Moving to a federal ministry in Moscow means leaving every local scandal in Sineozersk behind.
- 'First one must finish an important matter.' Before he can celebrate, he has to haul a hundred and fifty million rubles out of his safe.
- 'Are we going right now?' When the boss has that manic look in his eye, Stepanych knows the car needs to be running.
- 'Yes, let's go to the residence immediately.' That is where the secret stash of cash—and poor, unsuspecting Arkady—are waiting.
- 'Where exactly are we heading right now?' Stepanych is still scrambling to keep up with the sudden change of plans.
- 'It is time to make our move before influential people.' Notice how Viktor repeats the exact slang word—"oboznachitsya"—that Pyotr used on the phone two minutes ago.
