## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What kind of person was he, Arkadiy?' Marina asks thoughtfully about the fallen official.
- 'He could have become a very good boss.' Arkady reflects on what might have been under better circumstances.
- 'If not for his greed?' Marina points to insatiable greed as his tragic fatal flaw.
- 'If only he had never taken bribes.' Arkady highlights the corrupting influence of illegal kickbacks.
- 'And if he had not betrayed his friends.' Marina emphasizes the sacred value of friendship and loyalty.
- 'And had not envied another's wealth.' Arkady notes how jealousy poisoned his character.
- 'In every person there are different sides.' Marina reflects on the complex nature of human beings.
- 'In everyone there is bad.' Arkady acknowledges that dark impulses exist within all people.
- 'But after all there is good too!' Marina reminds him of the positive potential in everyone.
- 'Yes, good is always there too.' Arkady agrees that goodness never completely disappears.
- 'The main thing is to remember this in time.' Marina stresses making moral choices before disaster strikes.
- 'Power changes people very strongly.' Arkady reflects on the dangerous temptations of high political office.
- 'But you were able to remain human.' "Ostatsya chelovekom" ("to remain a human being") is high praise in Russian for someone who kept their decency under pressure.
- 'Thank you, Marina, that is very important to me.' Arkady is touched by her heartfelt words.
