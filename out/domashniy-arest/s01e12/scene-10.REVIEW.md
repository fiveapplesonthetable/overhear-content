- 'Samson, it is time for us to leave here for home.' Predicative word with dative experiencer and imperfective infinitive.
- 'No, I will place a bet one more time!' Negative particle with first-person future perfective verb and adverbial phrase.
- 'Place your bets, gentlemen players.' Formal imperative verb governing an accusative plural possessive noun phrase in the predicate.
- 'I bet all the money on red!' Present transitive verb governing quantified accusative noun with prepositional phrase.
- 'You risk losing the family's last money.' Present tense verb governing perfective infinitive and genitive modifier.
- 'I definitely must get lucky here today.' Impersonal necessity with dative experiencer and impersonal reflexive infinitive.
- 'The bets are placed, no more bets.' Short passive participle followed by genitive of negation with comparative adverb.
- 'I worry about you very strongly.' Present reflexive verb governing prepositional phrase of concern with intensifier.
- 'Watch carefully, the wheel is already spinning fast!' Imperative verb with manner adverb and reflexive present verb.
- 'Please, here is the new winnings at the table.' Courtesy particle with demonstrative particle and spatial prepositional phrase.
