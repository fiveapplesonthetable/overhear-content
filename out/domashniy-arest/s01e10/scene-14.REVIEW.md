- 'Only you knew about this hiding place.' Limiting particle with subject pronoun and past action verb.
- 'Where did you put them, answer me!' Directional question with plural pronoun and command of reply.
- 'Vikusya, calm down, let us talk calmly.' Self-action verb form command of composure followed by 'let's' expression verb.
- 'Do not touch me until you return the money!' Negative command with temporal clause of condition.
