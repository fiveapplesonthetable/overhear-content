## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vika, my dear, please forgive me!' Standing in her doorway, Pasha pours on the charm to soften her up.
- 'I made a terrible mistake, I am guilty.' Notice how he admits to a vague "mistake" without mentioning the stolen cash.
- 'I love you very much!' In Russian, "strongly" is the standard way to say you love someone deeply.
- 'Let us start everything from the very beginning.' Hoping to wipe the slate clean for free, he pitches a fresh start.
- 'Pasha, where is my money?' Vika cuts straight through the sweet talk to the only thing she cares about.
- 'What money? What are you talking about?' Cornered, he tries playing dumb.
- 'The money that you stole from the nightstand.' She spells it out plainly: the stash from her bedside table.
- 'I did not take anything from your nightstand!' Even caught red-handed, he keeps denying it to her face.
- 'Only you knew about this hiding place.' Nobody else knew where the cash was hidden, so the math is simple.
- 'Where did you put them, answer me!' In Russian, "money" is always plural, so she asks where he put "them".
- 'Vikusya, calm down, let us talk calmly.' Switching to an affectionate nickname, he tries to lower the temperature.
- 'Do not touch me until you return the money!' When he reaches out, she pushes him back and makes her condition clear.
