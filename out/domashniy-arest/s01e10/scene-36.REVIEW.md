## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vanya, we are going to have a third child.' A profound revelation announcing an unexpected pregnancy.
- 'When did you find out about this?' Samsonov immediately seeks to verify the timeline of her news.
- 'I found out two months ago.' Nina reveals she has concealed the information for several weeks.
- 'Why didn't you tell me right away?' An accusatory question challenging her long silence.
- 'I thought and doubted for a very long time.' Nina explains the internal emotional turmoil she faced.
- 'Where would we put a third with such a hard life?' A realistic concern about crushing poverty.
- 'And now I see that everything is improving.' Nina expresses cautious hope about their family future.
- 'So everything clicked in your head?' Samsonov bitterly quotes her rationalization back at her.
- 'Earlier you walked with our young neighbor!' Samsonov brings up his lingering jealousy over Danya.
- 'And now a good prospect appeared for me.' Samsonov notes his newfound political influence.
- 'And you immediately changed your decision!' An accusation that her loyalty depends entirely on money.
- 'Vanya, I am telling the pure truth!' An earnest plea maintaining the complete honesty of her words.
- 'Soon we need to walk out onto this stage.' Practical reality intrudes as public duties call.
- 'But I will never forgive your deceit.' Samsonov makes a harsh vow of permanent resentment.
