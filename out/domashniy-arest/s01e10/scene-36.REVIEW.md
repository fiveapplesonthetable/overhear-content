## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vanya, we are going to have a third child.' Calling him by the gentle name "Vanya", Nina drops huge news right behind the stage curtain.
- 'When did you find out about this?' Instead of celebrating, Ivan immediately starts doing the math in his head.
- 'I found out two months ago.' She has been carrying this secret since the very start of the campaign.
- 'Why didn't you tell me right away?' Suspicion creeps into his voice as he wonders why she kept quiet.
- 'I thought and doubted for a very long time.' With their marriage falling apart and money tight, she wasn't sure what to do.
- 'Where would we put a third with such a hard life?' Literally "where to us a third", a raw question for a family crammed into a communal flat.
- 'And now I see that everything is improving.' "Nalazhivayetsya" means things are falling into place and getting back on track.
- 'So everything clicked in your head?' Bitterly, Ivan twists her hopeful words into something ugly.
- 'Earlier you walked with our young neighbor!' In Russian, "walked with" is a classic euphemism for having a fling with someone.
- 'And now a good prospect appeared for me.' Now that he is the frontrunner for mayor, he thinks she is only staying for his success.
- 'And you immediately changed your decision!' In the original line he uses slang for "changing your shoes" — switching sides mid-air.
- 'Vanya, I am telling the pure truth!' Hurt by his cynicism, Nina pleads with him to believe her.
- 'Soon we need to walk out onto this stage.' Outside the curtain, the announcer is already introducing their "united family".
- 'But I will never forgive your deceit.' Seconds before stepping into the spotlight to sing a cheerful cartoon song, Ivan shuts his heart.
