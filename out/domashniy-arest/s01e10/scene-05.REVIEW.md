## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'They don't let you play with the fire truck?' Literally "to you they don't give to play", Arkady crouches down to hear what is bothering the boy.
- 'No, and they don't let me play with the tank either.' Poor Lyosha is locked out of all the best toys in the playroom.
- 'The teacher yells at everyone except Petukhov!' One spoiled kid gets a free pass while the rest of the class gets shouted at.
- 'And why does she yell at everyone?' Always thinking like a schemer, Arkady wants to know the angle behind the teacher's behavior.
- 'Because his dad comes by often.' Even a kindergartener can see that the boy's father is buying the staff's favor.
- 'And this teacher is not Petukhov's mom?' First he checks the simplest explanation: nepotism in the family.
- 'No, his mom is a completely different woman.' So it isn't a relative; the teacher is just flattering a wealthy parent.
- 'Today they will let you play with all toys.' Already planning a quiet phone call behind the scenes, Arkady makes a bold promise.
- 'No, they definitely will not let me.' Used to being pushed aside at school, the little boy doesn't believe a word of it.
- 'If they don't let you, you won't go there anymore!' Raising the stakes, he offers the ultimate kid-friendly guarantee.
- 'Really? Is that a boy's word of honor?' "Slovo patsana" is street-code for an unbreakable promise between guys, and hearing it from a little kid is adorable.
- 'Yes, word of honor, we have a deal.' Treating Lyosha like an equal, Arkady shakes on it seriously.
- 'I didn't understand how you managed that.' Having struggled all morning to get her son dressed, Marina watches in disbelief.
- 'These are our little men's secrets!' With a wink, he keeps the bond between the two of them.
