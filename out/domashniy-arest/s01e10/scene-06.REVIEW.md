## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You do not know how to work at all!' Fed up with empty-handed reports, the FSB boss tears into his agents.
- 'Soon both of you will simply be fired.' In Russian, saying "they will fire you" without naming who makes the threat sound like fate.
- 'I can quickly find that money.' Ever the hothead, Sokolov brags that he can crack the case in no time.
- 'And how exactly will you find it?' Knowing Sokolov's track record, his superior asks the question with heavy skepticism.
- 'With the help of force and threats!' Being subtle is not in Lyova's playbook; he just wants to rough people up.
- 'Sokolov, wasn't the kindergarten enough for you?' Earlier in the season, Lyova's undercover stint at a daycare ended in complete farce.
- 'You are under my command.' Pulling rank, the boss reminds his reckless operative who gives the orders here.
- 'Take your feet off the table quickly!' Even while being chewed out, Sokolov was lounging like he owned the office.
- 'You are going on an assignment now.' Sending him out the door right this minute leaves zero room for backtalk.
- 'Set up surveillance on Anikeyev's house.' Instead of action, he gets stuck watching the mayor's empty mansion.
- 'Are you just throwing me under the bus?' Literally "draining me away", a vivid Russian slang word for dumping someone off a case.
- 'Yes, but orders are not discussed!' Notice the brutal honesty: the boss doesn't even deny sidelining him.
- 'Fine, I understood everything.' Grumbling like a teenager, Lyova slouches toward the door.
- 'Not fine, but yes sir!' Casual "ladno" won't fly in uniform; the boss demands a crisp military "tak tochno".
