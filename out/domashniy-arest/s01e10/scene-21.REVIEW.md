## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Uncle Grisha, let us drive away from here quickly.' Sitting in the parked car outside the mansion, Kostik already wants to bail out.
- 'I have a strong anxiety in my soul.' Russians locate worry and heavy feelings "on the soul", and Kostik's nerves are frayed.
- 'You are always afraid of everything, Kostik.' Grisha has zero sympathy for his jittery young accomplice.
- 'And have you yourself ever killed people?' Swallowing hard, the younger man asks the question that has been haunting him.
- 'I haven't killed people, but slaughtered pigs.' Not exactly a reassuring answer from the guy in the passenger seat.
- 'A person is like a mosquito: squashed and gone!' Casual "netu" — "and there's none" — makes his comparison sound even colder.
- 'I am tired of living in eternal poverty.' Literally "it has bored me to live in poverty", explaining why he is willing to risk a major crime.
- 'Look at how richly these officials live!' Nodding toward the luxury house, he uses crooked politicians to justify their own heist.
- 'And do you want fifteen years in prison?' Kostik reminds him what the criminal code hands out for murder.
- 'If you are afraid, sit at home in poverty.' Knowing how to push Kostik's buttons, Grisha dares him to walk away broke.
- 'I can do everything completely alone.' Threatening to cut him out of the share works like a charm.
- 'No, Uncle Grisha, I am with you!' Afraid of being left behind, Kostik caves in at once.
- 'Then sit quietly and watch carefully.' Having put his sidekick back in line, Grisha returns to business.
- 'Now we will inspect this big house.' Little do they know that an undercover FSB agent is also watching the very same property.
