## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What happened here? Answer immediately!' Storming onto the scene after the botched stakeout, the senior boss demands answers.
- 'Everything is fine, dear Uncle Misha.' Calling his superior "Uncle Misha", Lavrov tries to smooth things over using family ties.
- 'Our agent was wearing body armor.' Pointing out the bulletproof vest is a weak excuse for letting Sokolov get kidnapped.
- 'Why did you let him go alone?' The boss goes straight to the real blunder: leaving one man out on the street with zero backup.
- 'You were just sitting in your van!' While their partner was being hauled away by crooks, Tolya was relaxing inside the truck.
- 'I will report about this to the general now!' Bringing the general into it means Lavrov's career is on the line.
- 'Uncle Misha, I am to blame for everything myself.' Remembering their heartfelt talk in the van, Lyova steps up to take the fall for his partner.
- 'I should have called them right away.' He claims he broke protocol by rushing in without calling the van first.
- 'But I didn't call.' Saying "ne stal" means "I decided not to" or "I didn't bother to", taking the blame squarely on himself.
- 'I severely underestimated the real danger.' Sounding like an official report, Sokolov recites his own fault to shield Tolya.
- 'I committed a very foolish act.' For all his clumsiness in the field, Lyova proves he is a loyal friend.
- 'You acted extremely recklessly, Sokolov.' Buying the confession, the boss turns his anger onto the junior operative instead.
- 'I promise this will not happen again.' With a crisp promise to do better, he saves his mentor's badge.
