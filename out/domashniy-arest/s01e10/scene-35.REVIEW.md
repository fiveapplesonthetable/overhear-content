## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Where are you driving the car so fast?' An irritated question asking why he is speeding recklessly.
- 'I simply want to arrive faster.' Kostik defends his high speed with a naive desire for haste.
- 'You always create big problems for us.' Grisha blames his younger partner for causing trouble.
- 'Why do you start cursing right away?' Kostik objects to his uncle's immediate verbal attacks.
- 'I am sick of your constant reproaches!' A personal complaint expressing deep frustration with nagging.
- 'Every day you repeat the same thing.' An idiom highlighting monotonous and endless repetition.
- 'You say that Kostik is a foolish person.' A polite restatement of his partner's rude insults.
- 'And isn't that the pure truth?' A rhetorical question confirming Grisha's low opinion of him.
- 'You better slow down before the turn!' A practical driving command telling him to decelerate.
- 'I had an excellent clever thought.' Kostik attempts to prove his intellectual capability.
- 'What thought could you possibly have?' A sarcastic dismissal doubting his ability to think.
- 'We must frighten our captive.' The modal construction expresses their criminal intention.
- 'Stop chattering, look at the road!' An urgent command telling the driver to focus on driving.
- 'Alright, I am watching the road carefully.' Kostik reluctantly agrees to watch the highway.
