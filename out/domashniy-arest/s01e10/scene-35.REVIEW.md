## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Where are you driving the car so fast?' After accidentally grabbing agent Sokolov in the dark, they speed off and Grisha yells at the driver.
- 'I simply want to arrive faster.' Rattled by the kidnapping, Kostik has his foot floored on the gas pedal.
- 'You always create big problems for us.' Everything that goes wrong on this job gets blamed on Kostik.
- 'Why do you start cursing right away?' Behind the wheel, the younger crook bristles at being scolded non-stop.
- 'I am sick of your constant reproaches!' Literally "your constant reproaches have bored me", as Kostik finally snaps back.
- 'Every day you repeat the same thing.' "Odno i to zhe" is the everyday Russian phrase for "the exact same thing over and over".
- 'You say that Kostik is a foolish person.' Referring to himself in the third person shows how wounded his pride is.
- 'And isn't that the pure truth?' Instead of apologizing, Grisha calmly doubles down on the insult.
- 'You better slow down before the turn!' Seeing a sharp bend ahead, Grisha grabs the dashboard.
- 'I had an excellent clever thought.' Still trying to prove he has brains, Kostik pitches his own idea.
- 'What thought could you possibly have?' Grisha's dry reply leaves zero doubt about what he thinks of Kostik's intellect.
- 'We must frighten our captive.' With their prisoner tied up in the car, Kostik wants to play the tough interrogator.
- 'Stop chattering, look at the road!' Right now, not crashing the getaway car comes first.
- 'Alright, I am watching the road carefully.' Muttering to himself, Kostik turns his eyes back to the headlights.
