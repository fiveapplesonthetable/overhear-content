## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, dear wife!' Fresh from a night out, Ivan strolls into the kitchen way too cheerfully.
- 'Well then, is our issue closed?' Having taken her advice to go cheat and make the score one-all, he considers their fight settled.
- 'And why are you so pleased?' In casual speech, "chego" ("what") often steps in for "why".
- 'You walk around here and sing songs!' Watching him beam and whistle after visiting another woman drives Nina up the wall.
- 'I am glad that everything is in order with us.' Blind to her simmering anger, he acts as if their marriage is fixed.
- 'You think everything is fine with us?' Repeating his own words back as a question is her warning shot.
- 'And why are you so gloomy?' Only now does Ivan notice the thundercloud on her face.
- 'I got very tired yesterday.' Her own attempt at getting even with Danya turned into an exhausting disaster.
- 'Believe me, I got tired too.' Trying to sound sympathetic, he claims his evening was hard work too.
- 'From your face, this is not visible!' That ear-to-ear grin of his gives the game away.
- 'Let us not quarrel again.' Sensing danger, Ivan hurries to change the subject before another fight erupts.
- 'Children, wash your hands and come eat!' Instead of answering him, she calls the kids to the table.
