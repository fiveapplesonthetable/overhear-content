## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'They will soon find and punish you all!' Still blustering, Sokolov warns his captors that the whole service is coming for them.
- 'Uncle Grisha, what if he's a real agent?' Holding the badge in his hand, Kostik is getting more nervous by the minute.
- 'Kostik, who would ever hire him there?' One look at their pint-sized hostage convinces Grisha he must be a fake.
- 'Where did he get this ID badge?' In the original line he calls it a "korka" — literally a "crust", slang for a hard-cover ID booklet.
- 'You can buy such a paper at the market.' Nothing Kostik shows him will shake Grisha's stubborn skepticism.
- 'Look, he even has a holster!' Searching the prisoner's jacket, Kostik discovers a leather shoulder holster.
- 'Is there a gun inside there?' Excitedly, he checks whether the holster is actually loaded.
- 'I had a gun, I dropped it.' Embarrassed, the tough secret agent admits he lost his service weapon in the scuffle.
- 'First we need to understand who he is.' An empty holster doesn't clear things up, so Grisha pauses to figure out their prisoner's angle.
- 'I can pay you for protection.' Switching tactics fast, Sokolov offers to put his own kidnappers on the payroll.
- 'Uncle Grisha, this is very suspicious.' Ironically, when the hostage offers cash, Kostik suddenly becomes the suspicious one.
- 'Wait, Kostik, let me think calmly.' The mention of money immediately catches the older crook's attention.
- 'This is actually not a bad option.' "Neplokhoy variant" — "not a bad option" — is understated Russian praise for a deal worth taking.
- 'Fine, let's hear his offer.' Both kidnappers pull up a chair to hear their prisoner's business pitch.
