## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Sokolov, report to me calmly and to the point.' Back in business mode, Lavrov tells his excitable partner to stick to the facts.
- 'Near Anikeev's house two suspicious people were walking.' While patrolling outside the house, Lyova spotted Uncle Grisha and Kostik casing the place.
- 'They said that they were installing fast internet.' Notice that Russian keeps the present "are installing" inside "they said that".
- 'And what is so strange about that?' Half asleep in the van, Tolya doesn't see why cable guys are worth a panic.
- 'They look nothing like real technicians.' Two shady thugs in leather jackets hardly pass for internet repairmen.
- 'And what signs should technicians have?' Still skeptical, Lavrov quizzes him on how a real installer is supposed to look.
- 'They did not install any cable or equipment.' For starters, real technicians usually bring tools and wire.
- 'They just stood near the entrance and quickly left.' Hanging around the doorway and driving off is textbook reconnaissance.
- 'Understood. And what do you suggest doing now?' Tolya humors his junior partner by asking for his tactical plan.
- 'I think we urgently need reliable backup.' Hoping for a squad of officers, Sokolov calls for "usileniye" — reinforcement.
- 'Yes, physical reinforcement certainly wouldn't hurt you.' Twisting the word for "backup" into "muscle", Lavrov pokes fun at Lyova's build.
- 'When will you finally start regularly going to the gym?' Instead of calling headquarters, he tells Sokolov to hit the weights.
- 'Again you are joking in such a serious moment!' Nobody takes poor Lyova seriously, even when he actually spots real criminals.
