## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Tolya, maybe we should go to headquarters?' Stuck in the surveillance van, Sokolov is itching to call it a day.
- 'We have been sitting in the car for two days.' Literally "we sit in the car already the second day", since Russian uses the present for an ongoing wait.
- 'Wait a bit, Sokolov. Do not hurry.' Instead of starting the engine, Tolya gets unexpectedly solemn.
- 'I am to blame for all our troubles.' Exhaustion and guilt finally catch up with the senior partner.
- 'I am a very bad officer and a bad leader.' After their string of bungled missions, Lavrov beats himself up over his leadership.
- 'Tolya, you are a wonderful leader and an excellent officer!' Alarmed to see his partner so down, Lyova rushes to build him back up.
- 'I would go into reconnaissance with you!' Going "into reconnaissance" with someone is the ultimate Russian expression for trusting them with your life.
- 'You are my main teacher and true mentor.' For all his brashness, Sokolov genuinely looks up to his older partner.
- 'Sokolov, you have moved me very deeply right now.' Wiping away a tear, Tolya is overcome by the tribute.
- 'Let me hug you tightly, my friend!' Literally "give I will hug you tightly", reaching across the front seat for a bear hug.
- 'Tolya, just don't cry, everything is fine.' Gently patting his shoulder, Lyova tries to keep the moment from turning into a crying fit.
- 'What strange music is playing in the car now.' Right in the middle of their embrace, a romantic ballad comes on the car radio.
- 'Yes, it is a bit awkward hugging to such music.' Two tough operatives hugging to a love song in a parked van suddenly feels ridiculous.
- 'You ruined such a wonderful and sentimental moment!' And just like that, the tender mood snaps back into annoyance.
