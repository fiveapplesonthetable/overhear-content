## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Tolya, maybe we should go to headquarters?' A polite suggestion using the modal phrase and future motion verb.
- 'We have been sitting in the car for two days.' Present tense verb expressing an action continuing into the present.
- 'Wait a bit, Sokolov. Do not hurry.' Two short command form urging patience and calmness.
- 'I am to blame for all our troubles.' Short-form adjective taking a location case object.
- 'I am a very bad officer and a bad leader.' Parallel descriptive phrase nouns modified by negative qualitative adjectives.
- 'Tolya, you are a wonderful leader and an excellent officer!' Strong words of encouragement countering self-doubt.
- 'I would go into reconnaissance with you!' A classic Russian cultural idiom for ultimate professional trust.
- 'You are my main teacher and true mentor.' Respectful vocational nouns expressing deep loyalty.
- 'Sokolov, you have moved me very deeply right now.' Direct-action single-action form with an object-form direct object pronoun.
- 'Let me hug you tightly, my friend!' An affectionate command construction followed by an adverb of manner.
- 'Tolya, just don't cry, everything is fine.' Reassuring command with the negative particle.
- 'What strange music is playing in the car now.' Exclamatory adjective introducing a description of the background audio.
- 'Yes, it is a bit awkward hugging to such music.' Location construction.
- 'You ruined such a wonderful and sentimental moment!' Single-action form tense verb with an expressive direct object.
