## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'And so, we have a children's craft contest.' "Itak" is the standard Russian word for "and so" when kicking off an official announcement.
- 'In third place is Sveta Kopylova.' Literally "on the third place", starting the countdown from bronze to gold.
- 'In second place is Vova Petukhov.' Even the rich kid who used to run the classroom gets bumped down to runner-up today.
- 'And first place goes to Alexei Bylinkin!' Literally "first place is at Alexei", handing the trophy to Marina's astonished son.
- 'Lyosha, remind us, what is this?' Looking at the lumpy blob of clay in her hands, the teacher cannot even tell what animal it is supposed to be.
- 'This is a lamb. You have to flip the work.' Deadpan, the little boy points out that she is holding his masterpiece upside down.
- 'Wonderful! Applause for our winner!' Covering her slip-up with a bright smile, she calls for a round of clapping.
- 'Take your works and give them to parents.' With the ceremony over, the kids carry their clay creations back to their families.
- 'Mama, look! I took first place!' After dreading kindergarten all morning, Lyosha is over the moon.
- 'Lyoshka, sweetie, I congratulate you!' Using the affectionate form "Lyoshka", Marina hugs him tight.
- 'Thank you for the fair judging.' Having no clue the contest was rigged, Marina sincerely thanks the staff for being fair.
- 'Congratulations. Goodbye to you.' Guilty and nervous, the teacher mutters a quick goodbye and hurries off.
- 'She looked at us very strangely.' Even Marina notices the teacher's weird, furtive look as she walks away.
- 'Come home early today, Arkady.' Calling Arkady on the phone, she invites him home to celebrate.
- 'A very tasty dinner is waiting for you.' Grateful for how he cheered up her son, she promises a special meal tonight.
