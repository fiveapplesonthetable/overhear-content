## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Children, we need to tell you an important thing.' Nina opens the serious family conversation with modal obligation.
- 'Your father and I decided to live separately.' The plural subject construction uses the preposition 'with' followed by the 'with/as' form.
- 'Speak plainly, Nina: we are getting divorced!' Samsonov cuts through euphemisms.
- 'Your father made a very big mistake.' Nina uses a formal single-action form to lay the blame on Ivan.
- 'And your mother made an even bigger one!' Samsonov retorts with a comparative degree adverb.
- 'Let's not fight in front of the children.' This phrase calls for parental restraint.
- 'Alright, we agreed not to argue after all.' The self-action verb form verb recalls their earlier agreement.
- 'And we already know everything anyway!' Yulia interrupts her parents using an adverbial phrase of obviousness.
- 'And what exactly do you already know?' Nina asks a nervous question with the emphatic particle 'zhe'.
- 'That you and Dad had a bad fight.' The second half of the sentence explains the children's awareness of parental conflict.
- 'The neighbor already told us everything long ago.' The past feminine verb shows how quickly neighborhood gossip spreads.
- 'Wonderful, simply magnificent!' Samsonov reacts with biting sarcasm using qualitative adverbs.
- 'Now the whole building knows about this.' The subject-verb inversion emphasizes the loss of family privacy.
