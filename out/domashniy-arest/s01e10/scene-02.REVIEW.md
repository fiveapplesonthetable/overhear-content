## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Children, we need to tell you an important thing.' Nina sits the kids down with the gentle, serious tone parents use before bad news.
- 'Your father and I decided to live separately.' Rather than saying "your father and I", Russian says "we with your dad", softening the blow at first.
- 'Speak plainly, Nina: we are getting divorced!' Samsonov has no patience for polite phrasing and blurts out the truth.
- 'Your father made a very big mistake.' So much for staying calm: she immediately points the finger at Ivan for ruining their marriage.
- 'And your mother made an even bigger one!' Ivan fires right back, turning their serious talk into a competition over who messed up worse.
- 'Let's not fight in front of the children.' Catching herself, Nina tries to put a lid on the argument while the kids are watching.
- 'Alright, we agreed not to argue after all.' The little word "zhe" adds that "after all" reminder that they had promised each other to behave.
- 'And we already know everything anyway!' Yulia pops their bubble: the big secret they were trying to break gently is old news.
- 'And what exactly do you already know?' Suddenly nervous, Nina wonders just how much of the adult drama has reached the kids' ears.
- 'That you and Dad had a bad fight.' In a cramped communal apartment, a loud fallout between parents is impossible to hide.
- 'The neighbor already told us everything long ago.' Thanks to chatty Klavdia next door, the hallway grapevine beat the parents to it.
- 'Wonderful, simply magnificent!' Dripping with sarcasm, Ivan is thrilled to learn their private mess is public entertainment.
- 'Now the whole building knows about this.' Putting "the whole building" at the very end of the Russian sentence makes the embarrassment land even harder.
