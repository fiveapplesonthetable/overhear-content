## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Right, Petukhov, go stand in the corner right now!' After the phone call from the security service, the former favorite is marched straight into the corner.
- 'Stop misbehaving and bothering the other children.' Suddenly the teacher has zero patience for the rich kid's antics.
- 'May I take this pretty toy car?' Still shy after days of being scolded, Lyosha cautiously asks if he is allowed to touch a toy.
- 'Of course, Lyosha, take any toy you like!' Overnight, the whole playroom belongs to him.
- 'Play calmly and do not be afraid of anyone.' She lays the sweetness on thick so he will go home with a glowing report.
- 'Thank you very much, I will build a big garage.' Thrilled by the sudden change of heart, the little boy dives right into his game.
- 'And afterwards we will go to have lunch.' Keeping her voice warm and gentle, she walks him through the rest of the morning.
- 'Today in the kitchen they cooked delicious soup.' Even ordinary daycare soup gets billed like a five-star meal today.
- 'I like hot soup very much!' Easy to please, Lyosha is just happy someone is being nice to him.
- 'Eat well so that you grow up strong.' "Kushay" is the softer, affectionate word for "eat" that Russians use with children.
- 'Thank you for your care, Anna Ivanovna!' Polite as ever, he addresses his teacher respectfully by her first name and patronymic.
- 'Well done, Lyosha, you are our best boy.' "Molodets" — "good job" or "attaboy" — tops off her nervous flattery.
