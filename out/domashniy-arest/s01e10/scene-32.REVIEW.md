## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Marina, are you pleased with your project?' The short adjective governs the instrumental case.
- 'Yes, I finished important work.' The feminine past tense verb describes completing an assignment.
- 'Fine, go work on your project further.' The particle sequence expresses resentful dismissal.
- 'Our salaries were cut because of you!' The preposition meaning 'because of' takes the genitive pronoun.
- 'They cut it exactly in half.' The plural past tense verb functions as an impersonal predicate.
- 'What? Why because of me?' Short interrogative questions express genuine shock and bewilderment.
- 'All the money went to your project.' The motion verb denotes expenditure toward a specific goal.
- 'Lena's husband is actually disabled.' The possessive construction uses the preposition with the genitive.
- 'They pay him very tiny pennies.' Russian uses the small coin word to denote pitifully low pay.
- 'I knew absolutely nothing!' The intensifier reinforces standard Russian double negation.
- 'Now all employees will suffer.' The compound future construction combines with an imperfective infinitive.
- 'I will go to the dean right now.' The directional preposition governs the dative title noun.
- 'I will sort out this situation.' The perfective verb expresses taking active charge of an issue.
- 'Try, if they believe you.' The singular imperative challenges the listener to act.
