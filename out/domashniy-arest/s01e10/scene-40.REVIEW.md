## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good evening! Welcome to dinner!' Wearing an apron in the communal kitchen, Arkady greets Marina like a head waiter.
- 'Tonight for dinner we have pasta with sauce!' Russian says pasta "under sauce", and he presents the dish with a flourish.
- 'Arkady, what is the matter again?' Walking in after her awful afternoon at the university, Marina is in no mood for a party.
- 'Why are you doing all this?' All the pieces have clicked together: the daycare threats, the beaten dean, the rigged contest.
- 'My god, what a fool I was!' Bitter at herself for believing things were magically working out, she realizes Arkady pulled every string.
- 'You deceived me and my son!' Even little Lyosha's clay lamb trophy feels tainted now.
- 'Marina, please listen to me calmly.' Dropping the chef act, Arkady begs for a chance to explain himself.
- 'I simply really wanted to help you.' In his crooked way, bullying the town was the only method he knew to make her life easier.
- 'I wanted you to be happy.' For once, the cynical mayor isn't lying about his motive, even if his methods ruined everything.
- 'And did anyone ever ask you for that?' That is the question he has no answer for: she never wanted favors bought by intimidation.
- 'You do everything only for yourself.' To Marina, his grand gestures are just another way of controlling people.
- 'Leave our family alone.' Literally "leave our family in peace", drawing a hard line between them.
- 'Marina, forgive my foolish mistakes.' Left standing over his fancy dinner, Arkady watches the one person he cares about walk away.
