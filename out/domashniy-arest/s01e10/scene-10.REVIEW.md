## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Maybe we will not perform at all?' Seconds after helping with the costumes, Ivan gets cold feet about pretending to be a happy couple on television.
- 'Samson, we agreed on everything!' Exasperated, Nina reminds him they already settled this.
- 'I am a living person too, after all.' Beneath his gruff exterior, he is still hurting over her affair and struggling to fake a smile.
- 'Then try for the sake of our children.' When all else fails, appealing to the kids is the one argument that reaches him.
- 'You know what? Cheat on me too!' Out of nowhere, Ivan blurts out a completely absurd idea for getting even.
- 'What? What are you saying?' Nina stops in her tracks, sure she must have misheard him.
- 'So that our score is one-one!' Treating marriage like a football match, he figures a tie score of one-all will wipe the slate clean.
- 'Then this will never end.' She sees right away where that kind of scoreboard logic leads.
- 'Go, I am telling you seriously.' Stubborn as ever, he doubles down on his ridiculous offer.
- 'And with whom should I cheat?' Deciding to call his bluff, she plays along with dry sarcasm.
- 'With the clerk from the grocery store!' Off the top of his head, Ivan picks the guy behind the counter at the corner shop.
- 'Have you completely lost your mind, Samson?' That suggestion is the final straw for her patience.
- 'Stop the foolishness and just pull yourself together!' Literally "gather yourself", the standard Russian way to tell someone to focus.
- 'Alright, I am ready for the performance.' Having vented his frustration, he finally drops the nonsense and gets back on board.
