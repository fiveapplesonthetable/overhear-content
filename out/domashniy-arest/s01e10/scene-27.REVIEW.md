## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'A bright streak has begun in my life.' Russians picture life as a zebra of black and white stripes, and a "white stripe" means a run of good luck.
- 'Everything changed when you appeared.' Eduard gives Marina all the credit for his sudden good fortune.
- 'Even Lyosha started going to daycare with pleasure.' He has no idea that an FSB phone call is the real reason her son loves kindergarten now.
- 'Is that really so?' Literally "what are you saying", a lively way to react with pleasant surprise.
- 'Yes, people around are changing for the better.' Naively, he thinks the whole town has suddenly grown kinder.
- 'It is a shame that our project cannot be launched.' Seizing the moment, Marina gently nudges him about her stalled monastery documentary.
- 'Why cannot the monastery project be launched?' Having secretly found a way to fund it to please her, he plays coy.
- 'Well, there is no money at all right now.' She repeats the exact budget excuse he gave her earlier.
- 'But everyone liked the idea very much!' Beaming, he hints that the obstacles have mysteriously vanished.
- 'And that is the most important thing.' To Eduard, keeping Marina happy is worth raiding the university payroll.
- 'Wait.' Pausing for a second, she realizes what he is implying.
- 'Maybe everything will still work out.' "Obrazuyetsya" is a comforting Russian word meaning things will take shape and sort themselves out.
- 'I am certain that everything will be great.' Famous last words before his faculty finds out where the money came from.
