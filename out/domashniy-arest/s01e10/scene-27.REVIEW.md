## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'A bright streak has begun in my life.' The Russian idiom describes a run of good luck.
- 'Everything changed when you appeared.' The second half of the sentence uses the past tense.
- 'Even Lyosha started going to daycare with pleasure.' The phrase denotes genuine enjoyment.
- 'Is that really so?' An expressive conversational phrase of mild, pleasant surprise.
- 'Yes, people around are changing for the better.' This phrase means to alter oneself.
- 'It is a shame that our project cannot be launched.' This phrase expresses sorrow.
- 'Why cannot the monastery project be launched?' Notice the preposition with object-form topic.
- 'Well, there is no money at all right now.' The negative descriptive word takes plural 'of' form.
- 'But everyone liked the idea very much!' The 'to'-form subject construction expresses liking.
- 'And that is the most important thing.' The superlative compound highlights key priority.
- 'Wait.' An informal command urging someone to pause before worrying.
- 'Maybe everything will still work out.' A self-action verb form meaning to fall into place.
- 'I am certain that everything will be great.' The short adjective expresses full confidence.
