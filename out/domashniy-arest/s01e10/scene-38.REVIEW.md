## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Did you hear a strange crunch in the bushes?' Out in the dark woods, every snapping twig makes Kostik jump.
- 'That was just a wild dog running past.' Calm as ever, Grisha waves off the noise in the trees.
- 'You are all immediately under arrest!' Even tied up and hauled out to the forest, Sokolov tries to arrest his kidnappers.
- 'The Federal Security Service is operating here!' Shouting the full name of the FSB is his trump card to scare them off.
- 'You work very poorly, young man.' Considering their federal agent is currently tied up on the ground, Grisha has a point.
- 'Our secret operatives will find you soon!' Little does Lyova know that his partner Tolya is still sitting in the van.
- 'Shut up and stop frightening us.' Threats from a hostage don't impress an old hand like Grisha.
- 'Uncle Grisha, what if he is a real officer?' Unlike his partner, Kostik is starting to sweat over who they just kidnapped.
- 'Kostik, you surprise me again with your foolishness.' Grisha refuses to believe they could have stumbled into grabbing a real spy.
- 'Who would take such a small man there?' Looking at Sokolov's short frame, he figures the agency only hires giants.
- 'But he has a real identification card!' Having searched the prisoner's pockets, Kostik holds up the official red booklet.
- 'You can buy such a paper in any underpass.' Calling the badge a "little piece of paper", he claims peddlers sell fake badges in pedestrian subways.
- 'You will pay in full for this crime!' "Spolna" means "in full measure", promising they will get every year they deserve.
- 'Sit quietly and do not open your mouth.' Tiring of the lecture, Grisha tells their loudmouthed captive to pipe down.
