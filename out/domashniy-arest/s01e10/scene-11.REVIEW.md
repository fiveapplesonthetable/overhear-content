## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well now, have all the journalists assembled already?' The conversational opener introduces an inquiry to the whole group.
- 'All the new stories are already in your email.' Location case phrase showing destination of incoming emails.
- 'Yes, I saw them. This is a total nightmare!' Feminine past tense verb followed by an expressive evaluative noun phrase.
- 'Why a nightmare? We tried to do it well.' Plural past self-action verb form expressing collaborative effort.
- 'All of you need to work more and better.' Impersonal modal construction with 'to me/you' pronoun and comparative adverbs.
- 'You need to walk around town with a camera!' 'to'-form location phrase of motion through the streets.
- 'I am pulling your story about bitcoins from air.' Professional television jargon meaning to cancel a planned broadcast.
- 'But we already advertised it in advance!' Emphatic particle reinforcing surprise with a past action verb.
- 'This material is far too old and boring.' Demonstrative adjective with paired descriptive word descriptive adjectives.
- 'Is it really so difficult to find a good story?' Rhetorical particle expressing doubt with descriptive word adverb.
- 'Almost nothing happens in our small town.' Negative pronoun with negative self-action verb form in the present tense.
- 'Search for interesting news, I expect them in an hour!' Plural command verb followed by a time location phrase.
