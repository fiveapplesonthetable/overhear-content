## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Eduard Valentinovich, what is going on here?' Storming into the dean's office, Marina demands an explanation.
- 'Why are you mistreating our staff?' She confronts him directly over the slashed faculty paychecks.
- 'Their salaries went toward your project.' Eduard admits it plainly, as if robbing the staff were a romantic gesture.
- 'Then there will be no project!' Without hesitation, she kills her own dream project on the spot.
- 'I completely drop it.' Marina wants nothing to do with money taken from her colleagues.
- 'Wait! What do you mean you drop it?' Panic hits Eduard's voice as she turns to leave.
- 'You must do this project!' Suddenly the polite suitor sounds desperate and frantic.
- 'I owe nothing to other people!' In the original line she refuses to do anything at other people's expense.
- 'Wait! Otherwise they will simply kill me!' Cornered, the dean finally blurts out why he is so terrified.
- 'Who will kill you? Are you joking?' Thinking it is just another pathetic excuse, she doesn't believe him.
- 'They beat me literally every day!' That explains the mysterious "nut allergy" bruises on his face.
- 'Two huge and scary men!' Those two thugs sent by Arkady have been shaking him down behind the scenes to keep Marina happy.
- 'These are your problems, not mine.' Coldly, she refuses to be blackmailed into taking stolen wages.
- 'I will not work for someone else's money.' Meanwhile, all the clues are starting to point back to Arkady's meddling.
