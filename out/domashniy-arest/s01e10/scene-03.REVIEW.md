## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Samsonov family, big greetings to everyone!' Bursting in with an armful of costumes, Arkady puts on his warmest politician's smile.
- 'I took costumes from the children's theater.' He tracked down real stage outfits from the local youth theater to make their act shine.
- 'The day after tomorrow you will sing a song at the festival.' Russian packs "the day after tomorrow" into one single word, setting a tight deadline for City Day.
- 'Television will show a story about your united family.' For his election campaign, he needs the cameras to see a picture-perfect household.
- 'Wait, what is happening here?' Looking around at their stone-cold faces, he finally senses that something is terribly wrong.
- 'Nina and I decided to get divorced.' Samsonov drops the bombshell without a trace of ceremony.
- 'Arkady, close the door, we need to talk.' Knowing the neighbors are already eavesdropping, Nina wants the door shut first.
- 'Have you completely gone out of your mind?' Literally "walked off your mind", the classic Russian way to ask if someone has gone crazy.
- 'We have a very difficult situation right now.' Notice how he says "we": to Arkady, their marriage crisis is first and foremost his campaign crisis.
- 'This performance is our only chance!' With the election hanging by a thread, everything rides on this one family number.
- 'You have lived together for twenty years.' Russian uses the present for something that started twenty years ago and is still going on today.
- 'Arkady, please don't pressure us.' Literally "don't press on us"; Nina pushes back against his heavy-handed guilt trip.
- 'This is our personal family matter!' Even if Arkady is running the campaign, Ivan draws a line around their marriage.
- 'I am leaving, and you speak calmly.' Backing out of the room, he leaves them alone in hopes they will patch things up.
- 'And definitely try on these costumes!' Even on his way out the door, he cannot resist one last nudge about the show.
