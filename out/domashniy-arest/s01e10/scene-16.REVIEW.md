## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'These flowers are for you.' Samsonov presents the bouquet as an awkward opening gesture.
- 'If you want, I will buy others?' The conditional sentence uses the present tense verb to offer alternatives.
- 'Men usually court women.' The verb governs the preposition and 'with/as' form for the person being courted.
- 'Then invite me to the movies.' The command form directs him to ask her out on a date.
- 'I love romantic movies.' The adjective is used in the object form plural to describe her preferred genre.
