## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'How much money does it take to be happy?' As the credits roll, Slepakov's guitar brings back the show's signature question.
- 'How much money do you need?' By asking "to you" directly, the lyric pokes at everyone's secret price tag.
- 'A little bit there, a little bit here.' Taking a bit from this budget and a bit from that one sounds almost harmless at first.
- 'And just like that, you've got six million!' Before you know it, those little favors add up to a small fortune.
- 'A villa in Monaco, an account in Switzerland.' Hiding the money in a Swiss bank and a Monaco house is the classic getaway dream.
- 'But you need, need, need even more!' Chanting "nado" three times in a row captures the itch that never goes away.
- 'A supermodel wife and a mistress too.' One trophy partner isn't enough when you are trying to show off.
- 'Only she's just a little bit younger!' That playful prefix "po-" on "younger" softens the comparison to "just a touch younger".
- 'A white yacht sails across the sea.' Seeing a gleaming boat on open water is the ultimate symbol of making it.
- 'Happiness is getting closer, almost here!' Every new luxury promises that true happiness is right around the corner.
- 'Paintings by Dalí and Van Gogh in the kitchen.' Even the room where you fry eggs gets museum canvases on the wall.
- 'A church, a mosque, and a synagogue are built.' Giving money to three faiths at once is how a nervous crook hedges his bets.
- 'And for insurance, a Buddhist temple too!' Adding a fourth temple "for insurance" is the punchline to the whole verse.
- 'If it doesn't pan out here, it will pan out there!' Whether in this life or the next, the schemer figures one of his bets will pay off.
