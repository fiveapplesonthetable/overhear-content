## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Our acquaintance started somehow wrong.' Eduard tries to smooth over their awkward first meeting at the university.
- 'I want to officially congratulate you.' Putting on his most courteous administrator voice, he welcomes her to the staff.
- 'Our collective is small, but very friendly.' In Russian workplaces, "kollektiv" is the classic word for the whole office team.
- 'If there are questions, you are welcome.' "Milosti prosim" is an old-fashioned, courtly Russian phrase meaning "you are most welcome".
- 'We are always in a creative search.' Flattering her academic pride, he pretends his sleepy department is hungry for bold ideas.
