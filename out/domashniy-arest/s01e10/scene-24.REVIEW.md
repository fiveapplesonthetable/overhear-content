## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Marinochka, hello! We definitely need to talk calmly.' Waiting for her on the street, Eduard switches to the sweet nickname "Marinochka".
- 'Eduard Valentinovich, what is there for us to talk about?' Keeping her distance, she answers with his full formal name and polite "you".
- 'I was a foolish scoundrel, but everything changed.' Calling himself a scoundrel is his dramatic opening move to win her back.
- 'I have finally left my wife Alla.' After years of empty promises during their affair, he announces he has actually walked out on his wife.
- 'You did that completely in vain. Everything is over.' Short and cold: "zrya" means "for nothing", because she has already moved on.
- 'Marinochka, I beg you, marry me!' Literally "go out behind me in marriage", the traditional Russian phrase a man uses to propose to a woman.
- 'Look, I bought a real gold wedding ring!' Pulling out a ring box right on the pavement only makes the scene more awkward.
- 'Nothing will definitely work out between us.' Even while turning down his proposal, she keeps the formal "vy" between them.
- 'Because I truly love another person.' Against all odds, Marina has fallen for Arkady.
- 'Marina, he is a dangerous criminal and bribe-taker!' Eduard isn't wrong about Arkady's past, even if his own hands aren't clean either.
- 'But with him I am happy for the first time in my life.' That honest confession stings Eduard worse than any insult could.
- 'Marina, help me, my foot hurts!' When romance fails, he immediately tries limping and begging for sympathy.
- 'Be a real man at last and go home.' Seeing right through the act, Marina tells him to grow a spine and walks away.
