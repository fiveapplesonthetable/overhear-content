## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dear parents and children!' A polite formal opening welcoming families in the hall.
- 'Our craft contest has come to an end.' The idiom means arrived at its conclusion.
- 'All our young creators made wonderful crafts!' Notice plural adjectives and nouns.
- 'Every work was very interesting.' The past tense feminine verb takes 'with'-form descriptive phrase.
- 'It was very difficult to choose!' An impersonal construction using basic verb form and adverb.
- 'All the children tried very hard.' The self-action verb form plural indicates diligent effort.
- 'Let's give a round of applause to all our participants!' The 'let's' expression command takes 'to'-form.
- 'They deserved the loudest applause.' The compound superlative emphasizes the highest volume.
- 'And now the long-awaited moment has arrived.' The single-action form marks a grand arrival.
- 'Before I announce the winner, your attention!' A temporal clause introduced by before.
- 'Who took first place after all?' The past tense single-action form pairs with ordinal object-form.
- 'Let's find out the name of our grand winner!' This phrase urges discovering the name.
