## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well then, how is it?' In this moment, Ilona is genuinely impressed.
- 'Incredible!' she exclaims.
- 'Is it Spanish?' In response, Ignatyev beams with local pride.
- 'We make it right here, in Lozhkino.' The key word here means 'right here', anchored at the close by 'make'.
- 'And better than over there!' Then his face darkens as he thinks of the town authorities.
- 'If only the people from City Hall weren't strangling me!' Notice the conditional particle 'would' with the past tense for an unreal condition.
- 'I would really expand here.' Charmed by Ilona's praise, he makes her a generous offer.
- 'Ilonochka, I like you.' Literally 'you were pleasing to me'.
- 'I'll give you a fifteen percent discount.' Seeing her opening, Ilona reveals why she really came.
- 'You see, I'm not quite a wholesaler.' In this moment, Ignatyev frowns.
- 'Then who are you?' Right then, Ilona hands him her card.
- 'I head the election campaign of Ivan Samsonov.' Furious at anything connected to politics, Ignatyev throws her out at once. Outside in the corridor, Yulya catches up with her.
- 'He didn't give any?' she asks.
- 'Wait, he does have one weak spot.' Right then, Ilona stops and turns around.
- 'So what kind of weak spot?' That phrase 'what for a...' means 'what kind of...'. First Yulya secures her deal.
- 'And you won't tell my parents about me?' In this moment, Ilona promises.
- 'I won't tell.' So Yulya shares the boss's family secret.
- 'He loves his daughter madly.' The line opens with 'daughter' and lands on 'loves' at the end for emphasis.
- 'And she doesn't want to see him.' Yulya explains why they fell out.
- 'He wanted her to continue his business.' Notice how 'wanted that she continue' uses the past tense after 'in order that'. Instead, his daughter chose the exact opposite path.
- 'And she's a ballerina, and a vegetarian on top of that!' Literally 'yes still and a vegetarian!'.
- 'They haven't spoken for a year already.' Yulia uses the adverb for 'already' right alongside the verb meaning 'speak to each other'.
- 'She left home.' And Yulya adds one last observation.
- 'And he never learned how to talk to girls.' In response, Ilona's eyes light up with a new plan.
