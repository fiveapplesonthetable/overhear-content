## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello! Can you help me very urgently?' Yulya uses the polite plural form 'you can' followed by the dative 'to me' and the infinitive 'to help'.
- 'And what happened?' Kind-hearted Ignatyev immediately stops his work to listen.
- 'The doctors said that Grandma needs an operation in Moscow.' Remember that earlier Yulya had told Ignatyev she was an orphan living only with her sick grandmother. Here 'to Grandma' is dative and 'operation' is the feminine subject of 'is needed'.
- 'I need one hundred and fifty-seven thousand six hundred roubles!' In fact, she wants the exact sum for her first year of paid university tuition in Moscow.
- 'I will return everything to you! Honestly, I will work.' Before Ignatyev can open his safe, the security phone on his desk rings.
- 'Hold on. Yes, who is downstairs? A man came?' The guard at the front gate reports that an angry man named Samsonov is demanding to come through. Hearing her father's name, Yulya panics.
- 'Don't let him in, please! That is my ex-boyfriend.' The adjective for 'former' is used as a noun meaning 'ex'.
- 'He drank a lot and hurt me! Please, help!' To stop Ignatyev from letting her father inside, Yulya invents another wild story on the spot.
- 'Why didn't you tell me right away?' Outraged on the poor girl's behalf, Ignatyev stands up from his desk.
- 'Sit here, I will go out to him right now!' He marches down to the plant courtyard to chase the 'ex-boyfriend' away. Outside, Samson is waiting, convinced by school gossip that the wealthy plant owner is his teenage daughter's older lover.
- 'You're the boss here, right? Well, take that, you old fool!' The adjective 'main, chief' means 'the boss', and 'hold this!' is what Russians say when throwing a punch. Samson knocks Ignatyev to the ground just as Yulya, Nina, and Ilona rush into the yard.
- 'Dad, stop! Don't!' Hearing Yulya call the man 'Dad', Ignatyev rubs his bruised jaw in utter disbelief.
- 'What?! Is this your father?'
- 'You said that he died!' Earlier at the orphanage, Yulya had told Ignatyev that both her parents had been crushed flat in a factory accident!
- 'And this woman is your mum? So she is alive too?!' As Nina shouts at Samson too, every single one of Yulya's stories falls apart at once.
- 'Forgive me, please! I will explain everything later.' Ashamed, Yulya clasps her hands and begs everyone's forgiveness.
