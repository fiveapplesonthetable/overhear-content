## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- At the library desk, the stern librarian hands Samsonov his book: 'Samsonov, here, please, is your book. This is Sun Tzu, as you asked.'
- 'The book is new, we just received it.' The librarian warns him that the book is brand new—they only just received it.
- 'Please treat it carefully.' She asks him politely to handle the volume with care.
- Samson grins reassuringly: 'Don't you worry, everything will be great!' That casual tone sets the librarian off.
- 'I don't have to give you anything at all!' Annoyed by his tone, the librarian snaps that she doesn't have to give him anything at all!
- 'Because you are a chronic debtor.' Literally a malicious or habitual debtor.
- 'When did you borrow Nosov's book?' At this point, Nikolai Nosov is the famous Soviet author of the children's stories about Neznaika, or Know-Nothing.
- 'In the year eighty-four!' Notice the location ending '-u' on the word for 'year'.
- 'Thirty-four years have passed already!' The librarian points to the date stamp in disbelief: thirty-four years have passed already!
- 'Aren't you ashamed?' Literally 'to you is it not shameful?'.
- Samson defends himself: 'But I did return Machiavelli!'
- 'And where that Neznaika is, I don't know.' He borrowed it when he was ten years old!
- 'Look for it at home! And treat the new book carefully.' She tells him to go look for the missing book at home and to treat the new one carefully.
- Samson takes the book politely: 'Thank you. Goodbye!' Outside the library, Major Lavrov and Captain Ovechkin inspect young Lieutenant Lyova in his oversized child's clothes.
- 'Well, does Lyova look like a child?' Russian says 'similar onto a child' when asking if grown-up Lyova looks anything like a kid.
- 'Stop pulling the hat down onto your forehead!' Lavrov snaps at Lyova to stop pulling his cap down over his forehead.
- Spotting Samsonov coming out of the library doors, Lavrov gives the signal: 'There goes Samsonov! Right, come on, lieutenant, go!' Suddenly Lavrov notices a bulge under Lyova's jacket.
- 'Stop, why did you take a pistol?' Spotting the weapon, Major Lavrov stops him and asks why on earth he brought a pistol.
- 'Are you out of your mind? Give it here!' Lavrov confiscates the service pistol before sending Lyova to pick a fight.
- 'When you come back, I'll give it back.' Lavrov confiscates the gun, promising to hand it back when Lyova returns. Meanwhile, Samson answers a call from Nina.
- 'Hello, Nina! I was at the library. What happened? Right, I'll be there soon.' Samson answers Nina's call, explains he was at the library, and promises to be home soon.
- 'I'm walking already!' Samson hurries out the door, calling back into the phone that he is already on his way.
- 'Right now through the park and the bridge, and I'm home.' That footbridge is where Lyova is waiting for him.
