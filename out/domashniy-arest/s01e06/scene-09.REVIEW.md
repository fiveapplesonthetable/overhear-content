## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'There is no compromising material on him at all. Just an ordinary guy.' Major Lavrov hopes there is a hidden weakness.
- 'Maybe some secret passions?' Anna reads out Samsonov's entire list of interests.
- 'Fishing, cars, football.' Captain Ovechkin mutters that Samsonov sounds just like him, so Lavrov decides they will have to invent a scandal.
- 'We'll have to make something up. Maybe we'll say he is a cheat and a drunkard?' Colonel Mikhail Mikhailovich shakes his head.
- 'It won't work, and we'll ruin the man's life!' Lavrov is surprised by the Colonel's sympathy.
- 'What's with the sentimentality?' The Colonel admits he actually likes Samsonov.
- 'I like him, he's a decent guy. Fine, then come up with something yourselves.' Anna suggests a milder rumour.
- 'I don't know, maybe he offended a disabled person?' Lavrov, however, is still fixated on involving a child.
- 'I can't stop thinking about a child. Now if he had hit a child!' Notice the conditional particle with the past tense: 'if he had hit'. Just as the Colonel asks how that could ever happen, his short, baby-faced nephew Lieutenant Lyova Sokolov walks in carrying archive folders.
- 'Well, how? Lyova, I didn't request any documents!' Lyova only brought the folders as an excuse to peek into the meeting.
- 'Come on, back to the archive at once!' But Major Lavrov is staring intently at tiny Lyova from head to toe.
- 'And what if he is not quite a child?' Realising Lavrov wants to dress him up as a schoolboy and have Samsonov hit him on camera, Lyova panics and shouts that he doesn't agree. Uncle Mikhail immediately shields his nephew.
- 'Lyova, go to the archive! And stay there!' He shoos Lyova out of the office.
- 'And don't bring any more documents in here!' Once the door closes, the Colonel turns back to Lavrov and Ovechkin.
- 'No! Forget even thinking about it! I'll kill you!' He refuses to let Moscow agents use his nephew as bait.
