## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, are you satisfied? Did you see the news?' Instead of catching Samson in a scandal, their park operation turned him into a local hero on television.
- 'You both raised Samsonov's rating!' After 'raised the rating', Samsonov is in the dative case: 'to Samsonov'.
- 'And you nearly killed my nephew in the river!' Because short little Lieutenant Lyova was wearing Yulia's pink jacket on the bridge, Samson mistook him for a drowning schoolboy and dragged him out of the icy water.
- 'Now his mother will pester me to death!' Literally, 'will muddle my whole head'. Lavrov protests that they rubbed Lyova down with medical alcohol so he wouldn't catch a cold.
- 'You rubbed him with alcohol? And on the inside too?!' The noun 'alcohol' is in the instrumental case: 'with alcohol'.
- 'He came home drunk to his mother!' Lavrov mutters that they only gave him a 'frontline hundred grams' to warm up.
- 'A hundred grams is a little for you!' Notice the spoken genitive form after 'one hundred'.
- 'And for him it's like three hundred!' Just then the colonel's phone rings: it is his sister Lyusya, Lyova's mother.
- 'Hello, Lyusya, hi! Is Lyova's head spinning?' Poor Lyova is lying in bed with the room spinning around him.
- 'Have him put one foot down on the floor.' That old Russian folk remedy is called 'grounding' yourself when you have had too much to drink. But Lyusya reports a problem.
- 'He can't reach the floor? Put a book under his foot!' Because Lyova's legs are too short to reach the floor from the bed! Meanwhile, back at the communal flat, teenage Yulia is pouting that she will run away because nobody cares about her. Nina sits beside her.
- 'Yulia, where will you go away from us?' Nina speaks gently to her daughter.
- 'How could we not care about you?' Literally, 'how could it be all the same to us about you?'.
- 'Everyone came running to save you — both me and Dad!' When they thought she was going to jump off the bridge, the whole family raced to the park.
- 'And even Uncle Arkady sacrificed his own time!' After the verb 'sacrificed', 'his own time' takes the instrumental case. Then Samson opens his arms wide.
- 'Yulia, come here, don't be upset! Come let me hug you!' Literally, 'come, I will hug'. And the Samsonov family hugs tightly on the sofa.
