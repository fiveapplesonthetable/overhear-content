## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'He won't find anyone! What should I do?' Russian uses double negation here: 'not he will find nobody', and 'what to me to do?' uses the dative with an infinitive.
- 'Don't worry. We can find out by phone where she is.' Ilona calmly explains that she can check Yulya's location through her phone.
- 'I don't understand anything about that!' Literally, 'in this I nothing not understand', with the prepositional case.
- 'I'll come right over.' Hanging up, Ilona turns back to Ignatyev's twenty-year-old daughter Galina, who is waiting nervously in her ballet costume before her big audition.
- 'I've said it ten times! I don't want to see him.' After 'ten', the word for 'times' has a zero ending in the genitive plural, equal to the nominative singular.
- 'Someday you will want to make up with your dad.' The prefix '-nibud' on 'when' means 'someday, at some point', and 'with your own dad' uses the instrumental case.
- 'But it will already be too late!' Ilona tries a gentle, sympathetic tone first, thinking of her own past.
- 'I wanted to make up with him a hundred times!' Because Galina is speaking, the past-tense verb 'wanted' ends in feminine '-a'.
- 'He immediately starts shouting! Leave me in peace.' Remembering what the ballet teacher said about Galina freezing up with stage fright unless she gets angry, Ilona suddenly changes tactics.
- 'Fine. And do you know why you are so angry?' The reflexive verb at the end means 'you get angry'.
- 'Because your dad is right!' The short masculine adjective 'right' has no ending.
- 'You have no talent.' With negative 'there is no', the noun 'talent' stands in the genitive singular.
- 'I saw your dance. You are no ballerina at all!' Literally, 'a ballerina out of you is none whatsoever', a classic colloquial way to say someone is no good at a profession.
- 'Nobody will even take you into a simple theatre!' Ilona smirks and walks away just as the announcer calls Galina's name for the audition.
- 'What?! Why, I will show everyone right now!' Forgetting all about her stage fright, furious Galina marches out onto the stage to prove Ilona wrong.
