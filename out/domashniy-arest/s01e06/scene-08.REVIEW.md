## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, Marinochka, did you have a laugh?' At this point, Marina is still amazed by the polite collectors with flowers.
- 'Yes, that was unusual.' Meanwhile, Edik embellishes his story.
- 'I wanted to make them memorise a poem by Blok!' Alexander Blok is the famous Silver Age poet. Marina smiles politely.
- 'I think that would have been too much.' Then Edik asks about tonight.
- 'Listen, will we see each other this evening?' Meanwhile, Marina takes a deep breath: she has decided to end their secret affair.
- 'Edik, I've been thinking about us here. It seems we've hit a dead end.' Literally 'walked into a blind alley'.
- 'You have Alla Yevgenyevna. There is simply no future.' Alla Yevgenyevna is the university rector and Edik's wife. Suddenly Edik adopts a grand, solemn tone.
- 'Dear Marina Sergeyevna! I told Alla Yevgenyevna about us.' Meanwhile, Marina can't believe her ears.
- 'What?' Here, Edik continues triumphantly.
- 'Now she knows everything! Of course, she is in shock.' And he adds a scholarly excuse for why the divorce will take time.
- 'It won't be fast, because we are co-authors. But the process has started!' Meanwhile, Marina is completely speechless.
- 'Well, why are you silent?' Here, Marina finds her voice.
- 'I am stunned.' Happy that she believes him, Edik immediately plans their romantic evening.
- 'Wonderful! I'll bring a bottle of champagne and candles.' And he gives Marina her task.
- 'And you make my favourite condensed milk cake! See you tonight, Marina.' Notice how the 'with/as' form is used after 'with': 'cake with condensed milk'. As soon as she hangs up, young Lyosha looks up from his crutches.
- 'Am I going to Grandma Lyuba's?' Poor Lyosha already knows that whenever his mother bakes that cake, he gets sent to his grandmother's for the night.
