## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hi, Arkasha! I came as soon as I could.' Days ago Arkady begged her to visit him, and she has picked the worst possible moment to show up. Passing Marina in the corridor, Lyolya smiles brightly.
- 'Hello! It's very nice here. Where do I go?' Marina watches in silence as Arkady hurries Lyolya into his room.
- 'Yes, in here, into my room.' Lyolya looks around the tiny room behind the wardrobe.
- 'Are there no cockroaches here? I've been afraid of them since childhood!' And she starts unbuttoning her coat. Arkady immediately stops her.
- 'Listen, Lyolya, I really appreciate that you came.' He holds her coat on her shoulders.
- 'But now isn't the time. Don't, don't take your coat off!' The reflexive verb means 'to undress' or 'to take off one's coat' when coming indoors. Lyolya stares at him.
- 'What do you mean? But I want to stay!' Literally, 'in what sense?'. Arkady gently steers her back toward the door.
- 'Lyolya, everything is fine. Let's do it another time!' All Arkady can think about is getting back to his evening with Marina. Lyolya narrows her eyes.
- 'I don't get it, are you upset with me right now?' She thinks he is sulking because she didn't visit sooner.
- 'No, I'm not upset. Everything is still the same!' The adverb at the end means 'as before' or 'as always'.
- 'Here is your perfume. Let me walk you out. Bye!' He hands her a box of French perfume he bought for her and practically pushes her out the front door. Then he hurries straight to Marina's room.
- 'Marina, listen, she has left! Let's drink the rakia!' He beams expectantly, holding the bottle. But Marina's face is cool and distant.
- 'Somehow I don't feel like it anymore. The moment has passed.' Literally, 'the moment is let slip'. Seeing his glamorous young girlfriend walk in broke the spell completely.
- 'Nothing happened between her and me, really!' Literally, 'by us with her nothing was'.
- 'Oh, I don't care anyway.' Literally, 'to me it is all the same'. Arkady tries one last plea.
- 'But surely a spark passed between us!' The verb means 'flashed by' or 'flickered for a moment'.
- 'That was your overconfidence flashing by. Good night!' And with that, Marina closes the door in his face.
