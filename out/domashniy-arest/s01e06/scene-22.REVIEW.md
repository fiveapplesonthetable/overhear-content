## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I'm telling you again, I didn't walk out! I fell out the window!' Notice the two prefixes on the verbs of motion: 'walked out' versus 'fell out'. The guard hands Arkady his broom and sends him back upstairs.
- 'In one piece? Thank you, Arkashenka! Now I can go near the window.' Back in the kitchen, Klavdia Viktorovna admires the clean view outside.
- 'The money! — What money? — Five thousand roubles!' Bruised and covered in snow, Arkady holds out his hand.
- 'Arkash, where would I get that kind of money? I'm a pensioner!' Klavdia Viktorovna widens her eyes in innocent surprise.
- 'I get twelve thousand! I can barely make ends meet.' Literally, 'I barely bring ends together with ends' — the Russian idiom for making ends meet.
- 'I don't care! Where are my five thousand roubles?' Arkady has washed her laundry, given her a pedicure, and fallen out a window for that money.
- 'Don't you yell at me! I told you in plain Russian.' Literally, 'with the Russian language I told you'.
- 'I'm a pensioner! And I have no money!' Notice how 'money' shifts into the genitive plural after the negative word 'there is no'.
- 'So you cheated me? I'll get my revenge on you!' The verb 'to take revenge' takes the dative case for the person you get back at.
- 'You already have! I keep telling you, my pension is twelve thousand.' She hints at all the years Arkady robbed the town as mayor. Arkady storms out of the kitchen.
- 'Actually, Klavdia Viktorovna, he did his job.' Even Marina, standing by the caramel-splattered stove, feels sorry for Arkady.
- 'And you cheated him!' Klavdia Viktorovna immediately turns on Marina.
- 'Look who's found a conscience! You stole a man from his family yourself!' Literally, 'an honest one was found! Yourself led a man away from his family'.
- 'And now she's lecturing an old grandmother about morals!' The verb 'to teach' takes the accusative for the person and the dative for the subject taught.
- 'He and his wife have had a rocky relationship for a long time! — Yeah, tell me another one!' Klavdia Viktorovna scoffs and walks out.
