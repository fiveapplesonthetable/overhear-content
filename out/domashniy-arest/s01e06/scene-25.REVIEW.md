## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I didn't think at all that you would do this.' Marina is touched, admitting she never thought Eduard would do something so brave for her.
- 'I did it! I told my wife everything.' Eduard puffs out his chest, claiming he finally told his wife Alla that he is leaving her.
- 'I made a cake. Do you like sweet things?' The describing word for 'sweet' stands on its own here to mean 'sweets, sweet things'.
- 'You, Marina, are my sweetest cake! Oh, the phone.' Just as he leans across the candlelit table, his mobile phone rings in his pocket.
- 'Yes, Allochka? What do you mean you didn't fly out? Why?' It is his wife Alla on the phone; he uses the sweet pet name Allochka and panics that her flight didn't leave.
- 'Oh, bad food? Does your stomach hurt?' Alla had only gone to the airport for a trip, and now she is coming back home after getting food poisoning at the airport!
- 'No, I'm at home! I just stepped out to the shop.' Eduard immediately panics and lies to his wife that he is right near home.
- 'I'll be right there! Maybe I should buy medicine at the pharmacy?' Literally, 'I now will be', the standard Russian way to say 'I'll be right there'.
- 'Okay, I'm running! I'll be there soon.' He puts his phone away and sees Marina staring at him in icy silence.
- 'Marina, why are you looking at me like that?' Caught red-handed on the phone with his wife, Eduard nervously asks Marina why she is looking at him like that.
- 'Forgive me, I exaggerated a little.' He hadn't left his wife at all—he had just taken advantage of her trip to spend a free evening with Marina!
- 'But I will tell her everything about us soon!' He scrambles to promise that he will tell his wife everything about the two of them very soon.
- 'Say quickly that you are not angry with me.' Cornered and desperate, Eduard begs Marina to say right now that she isn't angry with him.
- 'I'm not angry.' In the first-person singular, the stem consonant 'd' changes to 'zh'. Marina answers in a flat, heartbroken voice.
- 'Well, that's it, wonderful! I'm off, Marina!' Literally, 'I started running', and Eduard grabs his coat and bolts out the door back to his wife.
