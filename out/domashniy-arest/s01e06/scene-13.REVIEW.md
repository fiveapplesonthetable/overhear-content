## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- Across the desk, English teacher Inga Evgenyevna hands a printed picture to Nina and Samson: 'Do you understand what the matter is? Just look at this.'
- 'He brought this into the classroom. And he was showing it to everyone.' The teacher complains that Slavik brought the picture into class and kept showing it to everyone.
- Nina stares at the picture in shock and turns to her son: 'Oh, and where did you get this?'
- 'Were you spying on Inga Evgenyevna?' Saying 'spying on' in Russian literally works as 'peeking behind' someone.
- The teacher is indignant at Nina's naive question: 'Do you think this is a real photograph?'
- 'No, only the face here is mine.' A short, punchy nominal phrase built around the Russian word for 'face'.
- 'He made this on the computer, in Photoshop.' Inga points out that he made the whole picture on the computer in an editing program.
- 'And he disrupted the lesson!' Literally he tore off or ruined the class.
- Samson glares at his son: 'Slavik, do you hear what the teachers are saying?'
- 'What are we to do with him?' The exasperated teacher asks Samson what they are supposed to do with the boy now.
- 'He already has a failing grade for the term.' In Russian schools, a two out of five is a failing mark.
- Samson apologises to the teachers: 'Excuse us, we will fix everything.'
- Then the teacher turns to Slavik: 'You should follow your sister's example!' Literally 'you would take an example from your sister'.
- 'Your sister is smart, serious, and hardworking.' She praises Yulia as smart, serious, and hardworking—which leads to an unexpected question.
- 'By the way, how is her health?' Russian leaves out 'am', 'is', or 'are' in the present, linking 'by the way' straight to 'health'.
- Nina blinks in surprise: 'Who, Yulya? Fine, thank you.' After all, Yulya leaves the flat with her schoolbag every morning.
- 'So the operation went well?' Still believing Yulia's excuse, the teacher asks if her operation went well.
- Samson is completely baffled: 'What operation?'
- 'It's something serious for you,' the teacher says sympathetically.
- 'The girl has been lying in the regional clinic for two weeks!' To cover for her, the teacher repeats the story that Yulia has been lying in the regional clinic for two weeks!
- Realising Yulya has been skipping school for two weeks behind their backs, Samson covers for her in front of the teacher: 'Yes, you know, it is a very serious operation. We'll fix everything. Goodbye!'
- Nina pulls Slavik up from his chair: 'Get up, Slavik! You'll tell your father everything yourself at home.'
