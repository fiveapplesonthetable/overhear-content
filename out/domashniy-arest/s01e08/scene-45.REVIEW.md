## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Handle the money, Mayor!' Major Lavrov firmly instructs Pasha Osipenko to secure his campaign sponsors.
- 'Yes, sir, Comrade Major!' Pasha gives a crisp military affirmative and hurried nod.
- 'So what are we going to do now?' Captain Zavarzin turns to Lavrov once Pasha steps outside the van.
- 'I don't know for sure yet.' Lavrov studies the surveillance reports on his laptop.
- 'Anikeyev loves this woman deeply.' He knows Arkady's genuine affection for Marina is his biggest vulnerability.
- 'But we have no influence over her.' Zavarzin points out that Marina despises both City Hall and the security services.
- 'Some don't, but some do!' Lavrov smirks with a sly gleam in his eye.
- 'Who do you have in mind?' Zavarzin asks, leaning closer to the desk.
- 'Eduard Valentinovich Kargopolov.' Lavrov pulls up the file on Marina's former dean.
- 'Marina Belinkina's former lover.' Notice the masculine noun for a lover in the nominative case.
- 'This is a very interesting character.' Lavrov notes Kargopolov's shady academic bookkeeping.
- 'He likes to help himself to public funds.' The instrumental case is used with the reflexive verb meaning 'to make use of'.
- 'Excellent plan, Comrade Major!' Zavarzin grins, realizing they can use the dean's corruption to squeeze Marina and Arkady.
