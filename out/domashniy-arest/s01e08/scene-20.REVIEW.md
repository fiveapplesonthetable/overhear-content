- 'Then run, go grab a clothespin quickly!' The command form commands him to fetch the key part.

- 'Klavdia Viktorovna, don't scare the child!' Arkady stands up for the frightened boy.

- 'Or she will sell you to us!' Her grim dark humor shocks the hallway.
- 'There has been no trial yet, grandma!' Arkady reminds her that he is not convicted yet.
- 'Go away from here, cursed Arkashka!' The old woman snaps back before slamming her door.
