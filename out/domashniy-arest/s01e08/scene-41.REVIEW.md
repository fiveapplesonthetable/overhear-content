## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The Germans are advancing! The gates must be closed immediately!' In the wartime flashback, Grandfather Samson springs into action.
- 'Samson, have you gone mad? They will kill us!' Young partisan Mitya fears they will both be overwhelmed by enemy fire.
- 'Run into the woods, I will hold the defense!' Samson commands the boy to retreat into the dense forest for safety.
- 'One person cannot stop an entire regiment!' The odds seem impossible against an armed enemy battalion.
- 'I am a Soviet scout! Leave quickly!' Samson reveals that his apparent collaboration was an undercover military mission.
- 'He held the line for three whole hours.' The elderly veteran recalls how Samson single-handedly delayed the enemy advance.
- 'Our troops arrived and saved the town.' Soviet reinforcements reached the battlefield just in time.
- 'Mitya, are you alive? Is everything alright?' Wounded and soot-covered, Samson checks on his young comrade first.
- 'You are a real hero, Samson!' Uncle Mitya praises the courageous warrior who protected their homeland.
- 'Drop the cigarette! Smoking is very harmful!' Samson knocks the tobacco from the teenager's hand in the dugout.
- 'That is how he saved my life a second time.' Uncle Mitya humorously credits Samson with saving his health for decades.
- 'I lived to eighty-seven years of age.' Giving up cigarettes during the war enabled the veteran to reach a ripe old age.
- 'Your grandfather was a brave and honest person.' The veteran concludes his testimony, vindicating the Samsonov family name.
