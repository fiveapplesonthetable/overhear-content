## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I am asking you one last time!' The German officer is out of patience as he stands over the captured boy.
- 'Tell me where the partisans are!' He barks a blunt order demanding the location of the resistance unit.
- 'He is not saying anything. Samsonov, deal with him!' The captain calls in local collaborator Pavel Samsonov to take over.
- 'He will talk soon! Look me in the eyes!' In this moment, Samsonov steps up and demands that the boy look at him.
- 'Speak, where is your uncle? Where is the commander?' In wartime colloquial speech, the Russian word refers to the boy's elder relative or detachment leader.
- 'I won't tell you anything, traitor!' Russian stacks two negatives here — literally 'nothing to you I will not say' — to make the refusal hit harder.
- 'Your descendants will curse you!' Even with his hands tied, the young partisan looks his captor in the eye and warns him what history will think.
- 'What did he say?' The German captain does not understand Russian well and asks for a translation.
- 'My descendants will be driving German cars.' Pavel Samsonov laughs off the curse and brags that his grandchildren will live in German luxury.
- 'And they will drink German beer! Understood?' In this moment, Samsonov sneers at the boy with cynical contempt.
- 'That will never happen!' Literally 'there will not be such a thing ever'.
- 'We will win!' The brave young boy declares his faith in victory.
- 'What a brave boy! What are you doing, Samsonov?' The captain intervenes when Samsonov loses his temper.
- 'We need to find out information. Interrogate him calmly.' Literally, 'to us it is necessary to find out information' — the officer wants answers, not a beating.
- 'Where is your uncle? Answer!' In response, Samsonov repeats his demand one more time.
- 'I won't tell you anything, Pavel!' The boy stands firm and addresses the collaborator by his first name.
