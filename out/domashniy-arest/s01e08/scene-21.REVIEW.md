## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Cold.' Samsonov makes a drunken joke, comparing the chilled vodka to a woman.
- 'I mean, the vodka is good.' He clarifies his clumsy words with a sigh.
- 'What are your plans?' Nina demands to know if he has any intention of fighting back.
- 'I will be drinking. Sitting at home.' He admits his total defeat and surrender.
- 'Brilliant plans! Very useful.' Nina uses biting sarcasm to shake him awake.
- 'Calm your wife down! Get ready and go to the debate.' She lays out a clear path forward.
- 'The apple does not fall far from the tree!' Nina invokes the famous proverb about family flaws.
- 'Your grandfather betrayed his country in the war.' The historical rumor stings Samsonov deeply.
- 'And you are betraying your family right now!' She links the grandfather's failure to his drinking.
- 'Do not lecture me on morals!' The colloquial phrase means to preach or scold.
- 'And you are becoming an alcoholic. Goodbye!' Nina turns on her heel and leaves the room.
