## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Where is Samsonov? He is not answering his phone.' Ilona enters the office urgently asking where their candidate has gone.
- 'I do not know. He did not spend the night at home.' Nina looks pale and worried after an anxious sleepless night.
- 'We have the debate tomorrow!' Ilona reminds Nina about the impending live television broadcast.
- 'I know. I am very worried about him.' Nina expresses her deep distress over Ivan's sudden disappearance.
- 'He is completely unprepared.' The political strategist stresses that Ivan has not practiced his debating points.
- 'Vanya is very upset because of the rumors.' Nina explains the emotional toll of the smear campaign on her husband.
- 'The rumors about his grandfather are Osipenko's provocation.' Ilona identifies the dirty political scheme behind the scandal.
- 'But people believe this lie.' Nina laments that public opinion in town is turning against them.
- 'We must win this election!' The modal verb with the infinitive expresses strong obligation and determination.
- 'Without Vanya we will do nothing.' Notice the preposition with the genitive case and the double negative.
- 'We need to find him urgently!' The impersonal modal construction takes the accusative pronoun and the infinitive.
- 'Where are we going to look for him?' The compound future tense expresses a planned search action.
- 'Check all the bars and old friends.' The plural imperative verb directs the campaign workers to look everywhere.
- 'I will call acquaintances right now.' The particle emphasizes that the phone calls will happen immediately.
