## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Marina, I made it in time! He doesn't need surgery!' Arkady breathes a huge sigh of relief upon reaching Marina.
- 'I already know everything, Arkady.' Marina's gentle response surprises him after his frantic dash across town.
- 'How do you know about the diagnosis?' Arkady wonders how the local provincial clinic received the medical update.
- 'Dr. Tanenbaum called our chief physician personally.' In this moment, Arkady's esteemed specialist in Moscow made an urgent direct phone call.
- 'So the Moscow professor really helped?' Arkady's risky video consultation succeeded in saving the boy from surgery.
- 'The operation is cancelled, the boy has Lyme disease.' The surgeon explains that a tick-borne infection caused the joint swelling.
- 'He developed a high temperature.' A fever prevented any invasive surgical procedure from taking place that morning.
- 'We have prescribed a course of the correct antibiotics.' Targeted medication will cure the bacterial illness effectively.
- 'Thank god! Will Lyosha be walking soon?' Arkady's primary concern remains the boy's ability to run and play again.
- 'Yes, in a month he will be healthy.' The doctor provides an encouraging and positive medical prognosis for recovery.
- 'Thank you, Arkady, for your help.' Marina expresses sincere gratitude for Arkady's selfless efforts.
- 'Arkady Borisovich, the police are waiting for you downstairs.' An escort arrives because Arkady breached his house arrest terms.
- 'The main thing is that the child is safe.' Arkady accepts the impending arrest gladly knowing Lyosha is out of danger.
