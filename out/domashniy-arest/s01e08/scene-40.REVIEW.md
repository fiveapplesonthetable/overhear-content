## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Ivan Samsonov was scared and did not come.' Osipenko smugly announces to the television cameras that his rival has forfeited.
- 'He lost his nerve, like his grandfather during the war!' The incumbent mayor continues his vicious public character assassination.
- 'Let me in! I am a mayoral candidate!' Ivan bursts through studio security guards, demanding his rightful time on air.
- 'Look at him, citizens! This man is a fascist!' Osipenko points dramatically at Ivan to provoke the live audience.
- 'Don't touch my children! They are honest people!' Ivan is furious that his opponent slandered his family on live television.
- 'Security, remove this hooligan from here immediately!' Osipenko panics and orders the station guards to drag Ivan away.
- 'You are lying about me and my family!' Ivan resists the guards and shouts directly into the television microphones.
- 'Hey now, take your hands off the young man!' An elderly wartime veteran rises from the audience with authority.
- 'Esteemed veteran, before you is the grandson of a traitor!' Osipenko attempts to justify his hostility to the decorated elder.
- 'What difference does it make who his grandfather was?' The veteran sternly rejects holding children accountable for ancestors.
- 'Only fascists judged people by their blood!' The old partisan draws a sharp moral parallel to wartime Nazi persecution.
- 'I fought from age thirteen for the sake of justice!' Uncle Mitya reminds the audience of his genuine sacrifices in the war.
- 'And now listen to the truth about his grandfather.' The veteran prepares to share a crucial wartime secret with the public.
