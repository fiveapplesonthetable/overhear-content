## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Where is the operating room located here?' Arkady breathlessly asks the hospital medical staff for directions.
- 'It is located straight down the corridor.' The nurse points along the bright sterile hospital hallway.
- 'Stop the operation immediately!' Arkady bursts through the double swing doors of the surgery theater.
- 'What is the matter? Leave the operating room!' The chief surgeon demands that the unauthorized intruder exit at once.
- 'Where is the boy? He must not have surgery!' Arkady shouts frantically, trying to protect young Lyosha from harm.
- 'There is no boy here.' The bewildered surgeon points to an elderly adult resting on the operating table.
- 'Doctor, why aren't you operating on me?' The confused elderly patient speaks up from beneath the sterile surgical sheets.
- 'My son and I are enemies of the people.' The old patient quotes the political propaganda he heard broadcast earlier that morning.
- 'Calm down, Osipenko is simply speaking on television.' The hospital staff try to reassure the anxious elderly patient.
- 'My son said that it is time for us to go home.' The patient's family decides to leave after hearing the shocking TV speech.
- 'So Lyosha is not here?' Arkady realizes with great relief that the young boy has not been cut open.
- 'The boy is in another room on the second floor.' The nurse clarifies where Marina and her young son are currently waiting.
- 'Thank you! I must find Marina urgently!' Arkady turns around and dashes toward the second floor treatment area.
