## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'How many times do I have to tell you?' Samsonov expresses exasperation.
- 'I just went to a concert.' The past masculine verb combines with the adverb for 'simply' or 'just'.
- 'I was there with a classmate.' Notice the 'with/as' form following the preposition for 'with'.
- 'I didn't cheat on you!' The verb for 'to be unfaithful' takes the 'to/for' form in standard Russian.
- 'And people say otherwise.' Nina remains skeptical about his late-night whereabouts.
- 'I don't care what you think!' The idiom with the 'to me/you' pronoun literally means 'to me it is all the same'.
- 'Did you understand me?' A short past tense question checking whether she heard him.
- 'I will divorce you!' This phrase indicates an intention to dissolve the marriage.
- 'Is everything clear to you?' Samsonov demands acknowledgment.
- 'Samson, that's enough, stop it.' Nina dismisses his threat with a soothing particle combination.
- 'Don't make me laugh, please.' This phrase urges him not to act ridiculous.
- 'Let's just go to sleep.' Nina tries to defuse the midnight argument and turn off the light.
- 'We will see, Nina. We will see.' Samsonov repeats the future plural verb as a stubborn final warning.
