## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'How many times do I have to tell you?' Samsonov expresses exasperation.
- 'I just went to a concert.' Samsonov insists his night out was completely innocent, just a quick trip to the concert.
- 'I was there with a classmate.' He claims he was only hanging out there with an old friend from school.
- 'I didn't cheat on you!' Backed into a corner, he swears up and down that he stayed faithful to her.
- 'And people say otherwise.' Nina remains skeptical about his late-night whereabouts.
- 'I don't care what you think!' Literally, 'to me it is all the same' — he snaps that he couldn't care less what she believes.
- 'Did you understand me?' He barks a sharp check to make sure his threat is sinking in.
- 'I will divorce you!' Out of patience, Samsonov escalates the fight by threatening to end their marriage.
- 'Is everything clear to you?' Samsonov demands acknowledgment.
- 'Samson, that's enough, stop it.' Nina brushes off his outburst like a tired parent calming down a cranky child.
- 'Don't make me laugh, please.' She tells him to stop being ridiculous because she doesn't buy his tough act for a second.
- 'Let's just go to sleep.' Nina tries to defuse the midnight argument and turn off the light.
- 'We will see, Nina. We will see.' Stung by her laughter, he mutters a dark warning twice under his breath.
