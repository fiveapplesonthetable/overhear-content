## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why did you come here again?' A cold and unwelcoming question rejecting her presence.
- 'Let us please go home right away.' Nina's desperate plea asking her husband to return home.
- 'Vanya, I am very guilty before you.' A sincere admission of fault seeking reconciliation.
- 'Nothing happened between me and Danya.' Nina firmly denies any infidelity with their neighbor.
- 'I feel very bad without you.' An honest emotional confession expressing her loneliness.
- 'Earlier you only laughed at me.' Samsonov recalls past humiliation to justify his anger.
- 'Do you want me to get on my knees?' A dramatic offer to humble herself completely before him.
- 'Stand up straight and do not disgrace yourself here.' Samsonov curtly rejects theatrical groveling.
- 'We have children together!' An appeal to their shared family responsibilities and history.
- 'Thanks, I do not want any more children.' A harsh rejection shattering hopes for rebuilding their bond.
- 'Just like that! This is the bargaining stage!' Samsonov boasts of recognizing a classic psychological phase.
- 'Why did you humiliate her so much?' Ilona is shocked by how cruelly Samsonov treated his wife.
- 'Let her be in my shoes!' A bitter idiomatic desire to make someone experience the same suffering.
- 'Everything is going exactly according to our plan.' Samsonov naively believes he is in total control.
