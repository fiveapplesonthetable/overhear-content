## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'And everything piles on at once.' An idiom meaning multiple misfortunes are happening simultaneously.
- 'This foolish fish is like a bad sign.' Samsonov treats an everyday mishap as an omen of bad luck.
- 'You know, you are being difficult too.' Nina pushes back against his cold and accusing attitude.
- 'What did I do that was so bad?' A defensive question asking what terrible mistake she committed.
- 'I simply went to an ordinary concert.' Nina minimizes her outing as completely harmless fun.
- 'I do not recognize you at all lately.' Samsonov expresses how alienated he feels from his wife.
- 'Before you were calm and laughed.' He contrasts her past warm demeanor with her recent behavior.
- 'And now you got yourself a young suitor!' Samsonov reveals his bitter jealousy over the neighbor.
- 'Vanya, wait, there is nothing between us!' Nina urgently denies any romantic connection with Danya.
- 'Did you tell Samsonov about the baby?' Marina inquires about Nina's undisclosed pregnancy.
- 'No, I did not tell him anything.' Nina admits she has kept the major news completely hidden.
- 'Where would we put a third child?' Nina asks this pointed question to drive home how cramped their home already is.
- 'Am I supposed to put him on my head?' An exasperated Russian expression expressing complete lack of space and means.
- 'You are a fool! Go and tell him everything.' Marina bluntly urges Nina to be honest with Samsonov.
