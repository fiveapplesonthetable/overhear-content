## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Arkady, you don't understand anything!' Samsonov vents his frustration at Arkady's failed advice.
- 'She is simply humiliating me in front of the children!' Samsonov is mortified that Nina turned his big walkout into a joke in front of the kids.
- 'Well, bear it. Don't react.' Arkady tells him to grit his teeth and keep a poker face no matter what she says.
- 'Easy to say, don't react!' Sitting on the sidelines giving orders is easy when you aren't the one being laughed at.
- 'Am I a man or not, after all?' Literally 'at the end of ends,' the closing phrase shows Samsonov's wounded masculine pride.
- 'What should I answer her?' Samsonov asks for concrete words to restore his dignity.
- 'Don't say anything. Look at her.' Arkady recommends maintaining complete silence.
- 'Look how? Explain to me.' Not trusting his own instincts, Samsonov asks for step-by-step instructions on how to glare.
- 'Look heavily, intensely.' Arkady coaches him to fix her with a heavy, unblinking stare that shows he means business.
- 'Like a real man, for about ten seconds.' Putting the number after the word 'seconds' makes it sound like a rough ten-second count.
- 'You can even count in your head.' Literally 'in the mind,' he suggests silently counting the seconds inside his head.
- 'Alright. I understood everything!' Samsonov heads back to execute the stare.
