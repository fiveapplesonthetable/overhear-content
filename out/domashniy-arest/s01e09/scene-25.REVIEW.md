## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Is contact made?' Eager for news from the wiretap, Pasha asks whether she made headway with Arkady.
- 'What did he say?' Osipenko demands the exact words from the cafe.
- 'He named the place where the money lies.' Marina reports that Arkady actually gave up the exact spot where the cash is stashed.
- 'And where are these millions?' Greedy and impatient, Pasha demands to hear where the millions are hiding.
- 'In the Golden Key kindergarten.' She passes along the tip: a local kindergarten called the Golden Key.
- 'It seems to me, somehow everything is very simple.' Pasha's instincts tell him that Arkady gave up the secret way too easily.
- 'And could he have lied?' Starting to second-guess herself, Marina wonders aloud whether Arkady made the whole thing up.
- 'He lied, of course!' Osipenko assumes Arkady is always plotting deceit.
- 'So what is good about that then?' Confused by Pasha's grin, she asks what could possibly be good about being lied to.
- 'It was a test.' Pasha realizes the fake tip is Arkady's way of testing whether Marina is a spy.
- 'You think he is testing us?' It dawns on her that Arkady set a trap to see if the police show up.
- 'We will check that kindergarten all the same.' Even suspecting a setup, Pasha can't resist checking the kindergarten just in case.
- 'Be careful, he is very cunning.' Having seen Arkady up close, Marina warns Pasha not to underestimate how sly he is.
