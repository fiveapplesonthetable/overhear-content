## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Is contact made?' An informal expression meaning successful contact was established.
- 'What did he say?' Osipenko demands the exact words from the cafe.
- 'He named the place where the money lies.' The verb of location takes the plural subject.
- 'And where are these millions?' The emphatic particle adds impatience to the question.
- 'In the Golden Key kindergarten.' The location form indicates physical location.
- 'It seems to me, somehow everything is very simple.' The impersonal construction expresses doubt.
- 'And could he have lied?' The negative particle softens the speculative question.
- 'He lied, of course!' Osipenko assumes Arkady is always plotting deceit.
- 'So what is good about that then?' The 'of'-form singular of the adjective follows what.
- 'It was a test.' The past tense feminine noun refers to a probe or trial.
- 'You think he is testing us?' The ongoing-action form denotes an ongoing suspicion.
- 'We will check that kindergarten all the same.' The fixed idiom means regardless.
- 'Be careful, he is very cunning.' The command form plural expresses an urgent warning.
