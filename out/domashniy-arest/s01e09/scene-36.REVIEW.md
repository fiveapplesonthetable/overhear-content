## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Marina, thank you for visiting me.' Sitting up in his hospital bed, Arkady warmly thanks Marina for coming to see him.
- 'I wanted to know how you are feeling.' Marina says she wanted to check in on him and see how he is holding up.
- 'I really wanted to talk with you.' Dropping his usual banter, Arkady admits he really needed to talk to her.
- 'What do you want to talk about?' Curious, Marina asks what is on his mind.
- 'I want to ask your forgiveness.' For once sounding completely sincere, Arkady says he wants to ask her forgiveness.
- 'What do you want to apologize for?' Surprised by his solemn tone, she asks what he has to apologize for.
- 'Because I lied to you about the money.' He comes clean: the story he told her earlier about the money was a lie.
- 'I don't want to lie to you anymore.' Now that he believes she didn't betray him, he says he doesn't want to lie to her anymore.
- 'Where is that money then?' Trying to keep her voice steady, Marina asks where the money really is.
- 'I hid them in the kindergarten.' In a classic twist, he reveals that the cash really is hidden in a kindergarten after all.
- 'In the kindergarten? Why there?' Baffled, Marina asks why he just told her the exact same hiding spot.
- 'Because nobody looks there.' He explains the double bluff: once a place has been checked and cleared, nobody looks there twice.
- 'You are a very strange man, Arkady.' Half amazed and half exasperated by his mind games, Marina calls him a very strange man.
