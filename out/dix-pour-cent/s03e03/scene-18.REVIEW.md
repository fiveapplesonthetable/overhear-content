## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The film's numbers are catastrophic.' Julien shares the disappointing ticket sales. The descriptive adjective conveys commercial failure.
- 'Even my family did not come!' Julien laments that his own kin stayed away. The negative compound past verb highlights his personal sting.
- 'This film will become a cult classic!' Gabriel attempts to cheer up his director. The near future verb predicts long-term artistic veneration.
- 'We lacked money everywhere!' Sofia vents about extreme budgetary constraints. The compound past verb of lacking recalls the shoestring production.
- 'We had a stuffed badger toy.' Sofia cites an absurd on-set cost cutting measure. The imperfect verb recalls a ridiculous prop replacement.
- 'It is a true artistic masterpiece!' Julien refuses to measure success in ticket sales. The emphatic noun phrase affirms his uncompromising pride.
- 'Gabriel scared away the television network.' Sofia reveals the truth behind lost funds. The causative verbal phrase attributes the financing failure to Gabriel.
- 'Gabriel, what is she talking about exactly?' Julien turns to his agent in shock. The interrogative inversion demands an immediate accounting of the facts.
- 'I invented a crazy story.' Gabriel confesses his wild fabrication to the broadcaster. The compound past verb admits intentional interference.
- 'I wanted to keep Julien with me.' Gabriel reveals his possessive fear of losing his star. The imperfect modal verb explains his selfish motivation.
- 'That is totally unforgivable, Gabriel!' Julien initially reacts with righteous anger. The predicate adjective condemns the agent's duplicity.
- 'But that is also a stroke of genius!' Julien experiences a sudden creative epiphany. The approving colloquial idiom reframes the disaster into artistic blessing.
- 'Thanks to you, I am free!' Julien embraces total creative autonomy. The causal prepositional phrase expresses unexpected liberation.
- 'You are both completely crazy!' Sofia marvels at their sudden mutual affection. The plural adjective captures her sheer bewilderment.
