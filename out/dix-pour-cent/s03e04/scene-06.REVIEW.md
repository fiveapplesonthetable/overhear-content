## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You do not have to answer.' Colette gently tells Andrea to ignore her ringing mobile phone.
- 'I do not want to disappear overnight.' Andrea insists that she must remain available for top talent.
- 'Oh yes, you are in the maternity ward!' Colette reminds her that she just delivered their daughter.
- 'I cannot afford to lose Fabrice!' Andrea refuses to risk her relationship with star actor Fabrice Luchini.
- 'Hello Fabrice? Are you doing well?' Andrea answers her client with energetic professional cheer.
- 'Thank you, that is truly very kind.' Andrea warmly receives Fabrice's congratulations on the newborn baby.
- 'Listen, everything is going super well.' Andrea assures him that motherhood will not disrupt their work.
- 'The little girl is truly magnificent.' Andrea shares a tender description of their newborn infant.
- 'Do not worry at all.' Andrea comforts Fabrice about his upcoming movie schedule and costume concerns.
- 'Andrea, please hang up right now.' Colette tries to steer Andrea's attention back to family time.
- 'Fabrice, can I call you back?' Andrea finally concedes and prepares to pause the conversation.
- 'I have an unexpected situation right now.' Andrea invents an urgent excuse to end the work call.
- 'Thank you, let's finally think about our baby.' Colette welcomes Andrea back into their peaceful hospital room. Now let's listen to the maternity clinic conversation once more to solidify what you have learned.
