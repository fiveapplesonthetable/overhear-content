## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'It's still pretty funny.' Andrea grumbles humorously as she steps into the agency.
- 'Because of that idiot and his car, I'm two days behind in my work.' The preposition 'a cause de' expresses a negative cause.
- 'Are you okay, Mathias? You look pale.' Gabriel notices Mathias's sickly expression.
- 'Where were you?' The inversion 'etiez-vous' forms a formal past question.
- 'I come to marry you and you aren't here.' Andrea teasingly references her desperate plea from the night before.
- 'Hello, Andrea.' Hicham greets his former high school classmate with calm confidence.
- 'Perfect, everyone is here.' The expression 'tout le monde' functions as a singular subject.
- 'In any case thanks, Andrea, for sending us Mr. Janowski.' The compound infinitive 'avoir envoye' marks a completed past action.
- 'We could even call him ASK's knight in shining armor!' The conditional 'pourrait' makes a playful suggestion.
- 'Mathias Barneville, the fourth partner.' Gabriel introduces his stunned colleague.
- 'Hicham Janowski, delighted to meet you.' The participle 'enchante' is the standard polite greeting.
- 'Mr. Janowski, your new partner.' Andrea proudly presents the agency's savior.
- 'Mathias, are you okay?' Gabriel calls out as Mathias sways on his feet.
- 'Mathias, hold on!' Gabriel cries in terror as Mathias collapses to the floor.
- 'Try mouth-to-mouth!' The imperative 'essayez' commands urgent action.
- 'It's just his tie!' Andrea kneels down to loosen his tight collar.
