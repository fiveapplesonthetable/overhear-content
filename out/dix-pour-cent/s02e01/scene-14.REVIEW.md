## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Mr. Mayor.' Andrea addresses the village official politely. Monsieur le maire is the formal title.
- 'Andrea, I am delighted to see you here.' Ravi is an adjective meaning thrilled or delighted.
- 'Delighted to be here.' Andrea responds with the feminine agreement ravie.
- 'We have a slight change of schedule.' Leger means slight, and le programme means schedule.
- 'We have a surprise guest: Hicham Janowski!' Un invite surprise means a surprise guest.
- 'The guy from the dating site?' Un site de rencontres is the French phrase for a dating website.
- 'You do not mind sharing the spotlight?' Partager la vedette is an idiom meaning to share the spotlight.
- 'Of course not.' Bien sur que non is an emphatic expression meaning of course not.
- 'Here he is! Hicham, welcome to Couchey.' Le voila means here he is, introducing his arrival.
- 'I know you, right?' Hicham recognizes Andrea from their childhood in the village.
- 'We were at school together.' Ensemble is an adverb meaning together.
- 'That is it! Andrea Marteau.' C'est ca is a conversational expression meaning that's right.
- 'Martel. My stage name.' Un nom de scene is a professional or stage pseudonym.
- 'So you changed your name.' Donc means so, noting her professional identity change.
- 'Hicham sold his site for two hundred million euros!' Vendre means to sell, followed by a huge sum.
- 'Very well. Couchey humor!' L'humour is a masculine noun meaning humor or joking.
