## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'It is funny to meet again like this.' Andréa breaks the ice as they drive.
- 'Maybe it is a sign.' Hicham replies with an amused tone.
- 'Nobody expected much of us in high school.' She looks back on their awkward adolescence.
- 'We still did pretty well.' Hicham proudly reflects on their unexpected success.
- 'Why did you get into the car?' He cuts through the small talk with a direct question.
- 'When you are rich, you learn to recognize self-interest.' Hicham shows he is no naive fool.
- 'It's true, I had something in mind.' Andréa admits that she has an ulterior motive.
- 'But I must make sure that I can trust you.' She sets high terms before unveiling her plan.
- 'We were in high school together. At that age, one doesn't lie.' Hicham invokes their shared roots.
- 'Do you remember it?' Andréa tests his memory of their wild high-school parties.
- 'Yes, first heartbreak!' Hicham laughs about the memory of his first heartbreak.
- 'I wanted to show that I would never be like them.' He explains his rebellious outsider spirit.
- 'So we are kind of the same.' Andréa establishes their deep emotional affinity.
- 'The truth is that our agency is magnificent.'s a great company. A jewel, envied by all.' She begins her pitch.
- 'The owner died last winter, since then, the jewel has been for sale.' She explains the agency's crisis.
- 'We don't want someone to buy us, but to marry us.' She delivers her brilliant closing metaphor.
- 'We're here.' Hicham pulls the car to a halt right as she finishes her pitch.
