## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Andrea? Why me and not you for Virginie?' Gabriel asks why Mathias picked him. Pourquoi means why.
- 'Because I would have said no.' Andrea uses the past conditional j'aurais dit to explain her reaction.
- 'Of course, he knew I would be less suspicious.' Mefiant is an adjective meaning wary or suspicious.
- 'He is passing me a hot potato!' Refiler is an informal French verb meaning to pass along or dump onto someone.
- 'That way, if Virginie falls out with the agency, it is my fault.' Se brouiller means to quarrel or fall out with.
- 'Thanks, Mathias!' Gabriel exclaims with sarcasm. Merci is used ironically here.
- 'I had not seen that.' Andrea admits she had not realized Mathias's ulterior motive.
- 'It is exactly the kind of thing he would do!' Le genre de chose means the type or kind of thing.
- 'Poor me. Wait...' Attends is the familiar imperative of attendre, meaning wait.
- 'I have to go, I have to speak to the mayor.' Andrea cuts the call short to attend her ceremony in Couchey.
- 'Keep me posted. Take care of yourself.' Tenir au courant is a common idiom meaning to keep someone informed.
- 'All right, see you soon Gabriel.' A bientot is a friendly parting phrase meaning see you soon.
- 'Good luck in Couchey.' Bonne chance is the standard French wish for good luck.
