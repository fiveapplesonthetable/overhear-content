## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Come on, Jean Gabin. Take your walk.' Arlette encourages her dog on the sidewalk. Notice the imperative form of the verb to go.
- 'What are you doing here?' Bréhier steps out aggressively. The question uses the standard question phrase for what.
- 'I am walking my dog.' Arlette answers calmly. The verb to walk something uses a direct object.
- 'You are even crazier than expected.' Bréhier insults her mental state. The comparative structure uses more followed by than.
- 'You will soon end up at Pont-aux-Dames.' He threatens her with an elderly nursing home. The near future combines aller with the infinitive to end up.
- 'You know, the retirement home.' He spells out his threat clearly. The expression uses the formal second-person pronoun.
- 'Everyone knows that I worked there.' Arlette refuses to hide her past. The pronoun y replaces the place name.
- 'And I met a lot of people there.' She stands her ground firmly. The past tense combines to have with the past participle.
- 'They don't know how you managed their money.' Bréhier hints at past financial secrets. The question word how introduces the embedded clause.
- 'That is slander!' Arlette protests against the accusation. The partitive article indicates an abstract noun.
- 'No, it is a threat. I have proof.' Bréhier makes his blackmail explicit. The word for threat is a feminine noun.
- 'What do you want?' Arlette demands to know his real demand. Notice the verb to want in the second-person plural.
- 'It is war between ASK and StarMedia.' The rival agency head sets the stakes. The preposition between links the two rival agencies.
- 'Nobody buys anyone out.' He demands an end to the buyout talks. The double negative personne ne wraps the verb to buy out.
- 'Otherwise the old files come out.' He issues a final ultimatum. The conjunction otherwise warns of severe consequences.
- 'Come on, Jean Gabin, let's go.' Arlette walks away without yielding. The fixed expression on y va means let's go.
