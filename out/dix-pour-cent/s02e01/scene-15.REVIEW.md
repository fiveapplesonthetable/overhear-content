## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Mathias, you seem to have misunderstood.' Avoir l'air de means to seem or look like.
- 'Ramzy no longer answers my calls and I am the producer of the film!' Repondre a means to answer.
- 'He has disappeared a week before the release.' Disparaitre means to disappear, and la sortie means release.
- 'Virginie is doing the promotion, right?' La promotion refers to publicity for a film or book.
- 'Are you kidding? She is like a zombie!' Tu plaisantes is a frequent question meaning are you joking.
- 'Not a single usable photo!' Utilisable is an adjective meaning usable or suitable for print.
- 'We are releasing a comedy next week!' Sortir can mean to release a movie in theatres.
- 'Act in a responsible manner!' Agir means to act, and de maniere responsable means responsibly.
- 'Michel, you are completely right.' Avoir raison means to be right, intensified by tout a fait.
- 'I hang up and I find Ramzy.' Raccrocher means to hang up the telephone.
- 'If he does not do Drucker's show this week, I will sue him!' Attaquer means to sue or take legal action.
- 'Do not worry, he will be there.' Ne t'inquiete pas is a standard phrase meaning do not worry.
- 'It is a disaster, Mathias!' Camille reacts with alarm to the legal threat.
- 'I must settle this problem immediately.' Regler un probleme means to solve or settle a problem.
