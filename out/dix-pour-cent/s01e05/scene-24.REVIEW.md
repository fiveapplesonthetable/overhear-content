## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Julie, you come in this way and sit down next to him.' The phrase at the end means 'next to him', literally 'at the side of him'.
- 'And then you play the scene, okay?' The word after 'and' means 'then' or 'next'.
- 'Are there insects in the hay?' Tough guy Didier looks nervously at the straw on the floor.
- 'I'm allergic!' The crew tries not to laugh at his excuse.
- 'Can we start? Silence, please!' Samir sends everyone else out of the barn so the two actors can concentrate.
- 'Why are you looking at me like that?' Lying in the hay, Didier feels awkward under Julie's gaze.
- 'If you're already angry, it's impossible!' In French, 'to be angry' is literally 'to be in anger'.
- 'Rolling! Action!' Samir calls for the camera to roll.
- 'Cut! No, that's not working.' Their first kiss is stiff and awkward.
- 'They were waiting for this moment for months!' Samir reminds them that their characters are madly in love.
- 'We're too cold, is that it?' Julie admits they look wooden on camera.
- 'Yes, a bit. Right, let's start again. Action!' They try a second take.
- 'An insect! There was an insect!' Suddenly Didier jumps up in panic and knocks over a lit candle into the dry hay.
- 'Fire! Fire!' The French cry for 'fire!' is literally 'to the fire!'.
- 'It's okay, Julie. Come here!' As the crew puts out the flames and smoke fills the barn, Didier pulls Julie into his arms to protect her.
- 'There! That's perfect!' Looking into each other's eyes after the scare, Julie and Didier kiss for real, and Samir keeps the camera rolling. Let's hear the whole scene again from start to finish.
