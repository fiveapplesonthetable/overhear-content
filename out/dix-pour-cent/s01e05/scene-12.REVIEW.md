## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Arlette, I want to talk to you about Jonathan Joubert.' Mathias and Arlette use the formal 'you' here.
- 'That's his armchair.' She means her little dog, Jean Gabin, who has priority over every partner at ASK.
- 'Marie-Sophie doesn't want your flowers.' The screenwriter saw straight through the fake apology.
- 'You can't do anything against Ryan's illness.' The negative wraps around the verb 'can', with 'nothing' after it.
- 'We're going to lose a lot of money!' After the word for 'a lot', French adds the short word for 'of' with no article.
- 'Not everything revolves around money.' Literally 'all does not turn around the money'.
- 'Arlette, your share in this agency is very small.' Now Mathias shows his cold side.
- 'And Samuel is no longer here to protect you.' The two-part negative here means 'no longer' or 'not anymore'.
- 'Your threats don't impress me.' Arlette has been in the cinema business for fifty years, and Mathias does not scare her for a second.
- 'I'm not talking about money, I'm talking about dignity and loyalty.' She launched Marie-Sophie's career decades ago, and she stands by her writer.
- 'Marie-Sophie is worth a thousand of that idiot Jonathan Joubert!' Literally 'is worth a thousand times that idiot of Jonathan Joubert'.
- 'So as for your Ryan, cut!' Arlette mimes a pair of scissors with two fingers. At that moment Camille knocks and steps in.
- 'Excuse me, we have to think of the real fans of the series.' Camille has been listening at the door and can't help speaking up.
- 'Yes, Camille?' Both older agents turn to stare at the young assistant.
- 'Andréa wanted to show you something, but not now.' Camille loses her nerve in front of Arlette.
- 'I'll come back later.' Literally 'I am going to come back more late'. Yet as soon as Mathias leaves Arlette's office, Camille will pitch him her plan.
