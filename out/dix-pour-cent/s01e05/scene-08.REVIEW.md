## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'One is black and the other is yellow. What does it taste like?' Literally 'That has what taste?' — French uses the verb 'to have' with the noun 'taste'.
- 'It's horrible! Gabriel!' Sofia spits out the snack and lights up when she sees Gabriel walking across the courtyard.
- 'Gabriel Sarda! It's Gabriel Sarda, my agent!' Sofia proudly announces Gabriel's full name to the other extras at the buffet.
- 'He came to encourage me!' Sofia assumes Gabriel drove all the way from Paris just to watch her two-line scene.
- 'How's it going?' Gabriel greets Sofia warmly, happy to see her enjoying her first day on a movie set.
- 'Yes! I saw JoeyStarr. I rehearsed my scene!' Both past-tense verbs use the helper verb 'to have': 'I have seen' and 'I have rehearsed'.
- 'He said I was great! Good, right?' Sofia beams with pride after her rehearsal with JoeyStarr.
- 'I'm really happy for you. One second. Hello?' Gabriel's phone rings: it is Anna, the blonde woman he was just telling JoeyStarr about.
- 'It's Anna. Yes, Anna, is everything okay?' Gabriel steps aside to talk to Anna on the phone.
- 'I'll ask my agent. When does it come out?' While Gabriel is on the phone with Anna, Sofia interrupts him to answer another extra's question about the movie's release date.
- 'No idea.' Gabriel whispers a quick answer so he can get back to his call.
- 'Can I have invitations? Yes, he says yes!' Sofia doesn't even wait for Gabriel's answer before promising premiere tickets to her new friend.
- 'I'll leave you to concentrate.' Gabriel politely excuses himself to escape Sofia's questions.
- 'There are horses and everything!' Sofia keeps bragging about the grand period production.
- 'Here, darling. This will do you good. Come here.' Nearby, Andrea hands Julie Gayet a warm cup of tea and leads her toward the greenhouse for a surprise meeting.
