# dix-pour-cent: characters and places

Show: *Dix pour cent* (Call My Agent!), France 2. ASK (Agence Samuel Kerr) is a talent agency in Paris; each episode
has a real French star playing themselves as a client. Language: French (`fr-en`).

Voice rule for this show: the protagonist key `lea` (voice `lucie`) is the **viewpoint character of each scene**, so
it changes from scene to scene (often Camille, sometimes Andréa, Cécile, Sofia, Miranda or Arlette); its `LABELS`
entry gives the real name. Other speakers use `emilie` (women), `theo` (men, usually the main male agent in the
scene) and `marcel` (a second or older man). Recurring default voices when not the viewpoint are listed below.
The generic reference "Léa" (blonde bob) in `art/style/` is the app's learner and is **not** a character of this show.

## Camille Valentini (new assistant, Mathias's secret daughter, arrives from the Côte d'Azur; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: early 20s, slim, light skin #F6D2B5 with a few freckles, long wavy chestnut-brown hair worn
  loose, mustard-yellow cardigan over a white T-shirt, coral skirt with small cream flowers, brown canvas tote bag.
- Story role key / voice: `lea` -> lucie when she is the viewpoint (most of her scenes); otherwise `emilie`. Label: Camille.
- Speech: warm, direct, a bit naive; *tu* with Mathias (her father) and Hervé, *vous* with Noémie at first, with Andréa,
  Arlette and stars. Lines to reuse: "Je veux juste voir Mathias.", "Il me connaît.", "Je suis bien ici."
