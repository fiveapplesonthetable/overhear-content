# dix-pour-cent: characters and places

Show: *Dix pour cent* (Call My Agent!), France 2. ASK (Agence Samuel Kerr) is a talent agency in Paris; each episode
has a real French star playing themselves as a client. Language: French (`fr-en`).

Voice rule for this show: the protagonist key `lea` (voice `lucie`) is the **viewpoint character of each scene**, so
it changes from scene to scene (often Camille, sometimes Andréa, Cécile, Sofia, Miranda or Arlette); its `LABELS`
entry gives the real name. Other speakers use `emilie` (women), `theo` (men, usually the main male agent in the
scene) and `marcel` (a second or older man). Recurring default voices when not the viewpoint are listed below.
The generic reference "Léa" (blonde bob) in `art/style/` is the app's learner and is **not** a character of this show.

## Camille Valentini (new assistant, Mathias's secret daughter, arrives from the Côte d'Azur; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: early 20s, slim, light skin #F6D2B5 with a few freckles, long wavy chestnut-brown hair worn
  loose, mustard-yellow cardigan over a white T-shirt, coral skirt with small cream flowers, brown canvas tote bag.
- Story role key / voice: `lea` -> lucie when she is the viewpoint (most of her scenes); otherwise `emilie`. Label: Camille.
- Speech: warm, direct, a bit naive; *tu* with Mathias (her father) and Hervé, *vous* with Noémie at first, with Andréa,
  Arlette and stars. Lines to reuse: "Je veux juste voir Mathias.", "Il me connaît.", "Je suis bien ici."

## Andréa Martel (agent; tough, funny, always on the phone; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: around 35, light skin #F6D2B5, short tousled dark-brown hair (side-swept, ears visible),
  black leather jacket over a grey T-shirt, dark jeans, smartphone in hand.
- Story role key / voice: `lea` -> lucie when she is the viewpoint; otherwise `emilie`. Label: Andréa.
- Speech: fast, bossy, informal; *tu* with Gabriel, Arlette and Hervé, *vous* with Camille; with Mathias the cues use
  *vous* (s01e02 meeting). "Improvise!"

## Gabriel Sarda (agent; kind, anxious, avoids conflict; Cécile de France's agent; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: mid 40s, stocky, round face, medium skin #D9A074, messy dark curly hair, short dark stubble,
  rumpled navy suit with open white collar and no tie, phone often pressed to his ear.
- Story role key / voice: `theo`. Label: Gabriel.
- Speech: soft, hesitant, apologetic. With Cécile the s01e01 subtitles use *vous* in the opening phone call (cues 13-30)
  and *tu* from the restaurant scene on; scripts follow the cues. "Je vous rappelle.", "Et comment!"

## Mathias Barneville (agent; ambitious, cold, smart; Camille's father, married; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: early 50s, tall and slim, light skin #F6D2B5, swept-back grey hair, clean-shaven, sharp
  charcoal three-piece suit, white shirt, burgundy pocket square, no tie.
- Story role key / voice: `theo` (or `marcel` when Gabriel also speaks as `theo`). Label: Mathias.
- Speech: smooth, cutting, *vous* with clients at first, *tu* with Camille and colleagues.

## Arlette Azémar (the oldest agent in Paris; calls herself an *imprésario*; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art (house look, see `art/style/fr-01-agence.webp`): about 70, medium skin #D9A074, silver-grey
  hair in a round bun, round dark glasses, raspberry-pink jumper, string of cream pearls. Usually with her dog.
- Story role key / voice: `emilie`, or `lea` -> lucie when she is the viewpoint. Label: Arlette.
- Speech: grand, witty, sharp tongue, *vous* to most people.

## Jean Gabin (Arlette's dog; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: small white Jack Russell terrier with tan patches on the ears and one eye, red collar.
- No voice.

## Hervé (Gabriel's assistant; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art (house look, see `art/style/fr-03-rendez-vous.webp`): late 20s, deep skin #9A6341, short black

## Noémie (Mathias's assistant, secretly in love with him; first seen s01e01)

## Line Renaud (star client, plays herself; singer and actress, about 85; Samuel's old friend; first seen s01e02)
