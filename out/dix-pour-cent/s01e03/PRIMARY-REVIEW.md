# S01E03 editorial and primary-source review

All 22 playable lessons were reviewed as shortened French retellings, with English teaching and the supplied full episode source. Draft speaker lists and the French half of the dual subtitles were not treated as authoritative. The original episode picture was checked privately across every lesson interval. No original video, source clips, signed URLs or credentials are included here.

The source audio was checked with unprompted, local CPU Whisper-small for the family argument, the final agency exit, Laura's audition interruption and Gabriel's phone appeal. This exposed real register errors in the supplied French subtitles: Nathalie and Laura address each other with **tu**, and Gabriel also uses **tu** with Andréa outside. The retellings and explanations now follow that relationship, rather than inventing a family habit of formal vous. CPU ASR is supporting evidence, not a substitute for editorial judgement; some words in a musical/English phone sequence were not reliable enough to use. The English part of that staged call is omitted from the French lesson.

Important factual repairs:

- 01: Gabriel forgot the tax letter; Camille was not responsible. Gabriel also makes the initial call to Cécile.
- 02: Sofia rehearses the Chekhov speech and studied it at drama school. Nathalie recognises it and advises Sofia; Laura receives the later posture comment.
- 06: Camille discovers the invitation; the assistant reactions and questions have been checked against the picture.
- 16 and 22: Hervé proposes the Bardot act, Gabriel objects, and later Gabriel pretends to fire Hervé. The subtitle's feminine *virée* was corrected to masculine *viré*.
- 19: Camille's mother is on a laptop video call, not visiting her room in Paris.
- 24: Noémie brings the director's later callback news.
- 26: familiar family questions, direct address to “my mother”, and a final hope of working together replace backtranslated wording.
- 28: Colette's document exchange ends indoors. The following conversation is Gabriel and Andréa outside; Gabriel invites her to dinner, Andréa declines and Gabriel accepts the answer. It is not Colette flirting with Andréa.
- 30: the English outro preserves the final François/Mathias offer and the threat to ASK.

The house recipe permits unvoiced characters to be narrated or folded into the nearest role. Short folds in 03, 07, 14, 15, 17, 18 and 24 are identified in authoring/REVIEW notes and disclosed in narration; they preserve events and keep the three-voice limit. In 17, the audition's dramatic text is read in Nathalie's voice while the director retains her own instructions. The lessons are not claimed to reproduce the original dialogue word for word.

Full and partial English translations, contractions, repeated story passes, word lemmas, contextual glosses, IPA, useful forms and examples were reviewed. Unfounded generated etymology and regularity claims were removed. Individual articles/pronouns are no longer attached to unrelated phrases. Reflexive forms for the actual uses of *se comporter* and *se disputer* are included, as are direct object uses of *le/la/les*. Original scene ranges and explicit merged neighbours are preserved. Scene 20 and 26 cite their genuine merged-neighbour source cues, rather than unrelated cues inside the main range.

Existing art was viewed. Source-verified cast/setting repairs for 01, 02, 19, 22 and 24 use the built-in image-generation tool with existing house illustrations as references, then were visually inspected and saved as 1024-square WebPs. The prompt set preserves the canonical cartoon identities and changes only the false subject/setting: Gabriel holding the forgotten letter; Sofia rehearsing with Nathalie; mother on a laptop call; Hervé with Gabriel after the firing joke; Hervé beside Gabriel's phone call. No source-video frames were used as generation references.

Validation: the corrected adjacent-explicit-merge source gate and `tools/validate.py <episode> --require-notes --strict-teaching` pass with zero errors. Generated lesson audio, timing, native-device playback and remote publication are separate production gates and are not certified by this editorial review.
