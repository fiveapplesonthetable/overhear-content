## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We're going on the attack with Luchini.' Gabriel shares agency strategy. The idiom passer à l'attaque means to launch an offensive or make a move.
- 'That would be amazing! Is it good?' Sofia is enthusiastic about the project. The conditional form ce serait expresses wonder.
- 'Really funny, with very fine roles.' Gabriel praises the new comedy screenplay. The adverb vraiment intensifies the adjective drôle.
- 'It was Camille who thought it was good for me.' Sofia credits the young assistant. Notice the cleft construction c'est qui highlighting the subject.
- 'It's so kind of her. What an adorable girl.' Sofia shows genuine appreciation. The phrase de sa part means on her part.
- 'She would make an excellent agent.' Sofia sees natural talent in Camille. The conditional ferait expresses potential.
- 'So... would you let her represent you?' Gabriel tests the waters carefully. The verb laisser followed by an infinitive means to let someone do something.
- 'Are you crazy? You are my agent.' Sofia reacts with shocked loyalty. The question is direct and affectionate.
- 'Hypothetically speaking, I mean.' Gabriel tries to soften his awkward suggestion. The phrase je veux dire clarifies intent.
- 'You don't want to be my agent anymore?' Sofia worries that Gabriel is backing away. The negative structure ne ... plus means no longer.
- 'Of course I do! It's just that working together is sometimes difficult.' Gabriel reassures her quickly. The emphatic French response bien sûr que si contradicts a negative question.
- 'They say it's hard to mix everything up.' Gabriel repeats the partners' warnings. The impersonal phrase c'est dur describes difficulty.
- 'It can hurt you.' He worries about her professional career. The expression faire du mal means to cause harm or pain.
- 'No, I would never change agents.' Sofia refuses to let him step aside. The conditional ne changerais jamais emphasizes total loyalty.
- 'You found me, you know me, defend me!' She demands his unwavering support. The imperative défends-moi carries strong emotional urgency.
- 'I don't want anyone else but you.' Sofia closes the debate with devotion. The structure ne ... personne d'autre que means no one else but.
