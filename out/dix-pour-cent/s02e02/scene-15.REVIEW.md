## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Fabrice! You are finally here.' Linda welcomes Luchini with warmth and apparent relief.
- 'Sorry, I didn't keep you waiting?' Fabrice politely apologizes for being late to the cafe.
- 'Your agent Bréhier never answers me.' Linda delivers the initial blow by claiming his agent is unresponsive.
- 'Really? That is very strange.' Fabrice expresses genuine confusion and surprise.
- 'I wanted to offer you the next Bond.' Linda reveals the high-profile international project she allegedly wanted him for.
- 'You would make an excellent villain.' Linda compliments his acting ability to play a memorable 007 antagonist.
- 'Is that true? That is my childhood dream!' Fabrice is visibly moved and confesses his lifelong ambition.
- 'It would be absolutely wonderful for me.' Fabrice reflects on how deeply meaningful the opportunity would have been.
- 'But it is too late, alas.' Linda dramatically dashes his hopes with counterfeit regret.
- 'Without an answer, we chose Antonio Banderas.' Linda claims the production was forced to sign another international star.
- 'Antonio Banderas? I do not understand!' Fabrice is outraged and bewildered by the perceived loss.
- 'I have a small film in Ireland.' Linda offers a meager consolation project in Northern Ireland.
- 'A role of teacher in the rain.' Linda describes an unglamorous low-budget production under harsh weather.
- 'In the rain? Bréhier will hear from me!' Furious at the indignity, Fabrice vows to confront his negligent agent.
