## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hi Julien. Mathias could not make it.' Gabriel announces Mathias's absence casually. The compound tense 'n'a pas pu venir' uses the past participle of pouvoir followed by an infinitive.
- 'Okay, no problem.' Julien accepts the situation smoothly. The negative phrase 'pas de probleme' is the standard conversational way to dismiss an inconvenience.
- 'Welcome, Sofia. How are you?' Julien greets Sofia warmly. The interjection 'bienvenue' welcomes a guest, paired with the familiar question 'ca va'.
- 'Hi, I am doing very well.' Sofia returns the friendly greeting. The adverbial intensifier 'tres bien' indicates that she is feeling enthusiastic and ready.
- 'I made a few different arrangements for the song.' Julien reveals his creative work. The indefinite plural determiner 'quelques' modifies the plural noun 'arrangements'.
- 'Your voice inspired me so much...' Julien compliments Sofia's musical talent. The adverb 'tellement' underscores how deeply her singing influenced his composition.
- 'Oh really? Thank you very much.' Sofia responds with modest gratitude. The conversational reaction 'ah oui' conveys pleasant surprise followed by thanks.
- 'This way, come along.' Julien directs Sofia toward the recording area. The spatial locution 'par ici' guides a visitor politely through the studio.
- 'Sorry, Gabriel, I prefer that the booth is not full of people.' Julien gently excludes Gabriel. The verb 'preferer que' governs the subjunctive form 'ne soit pas'.
- 'All right, see you in a bit.' Gabriel steps back politely. The temporal idiom 'a tout a l'heure' promises a reunion later in the day.
- 'Here we are, this is the most important room in the studio.' Julien highlights the sacred space. The superlative 'la plus importante' emphasizes the central value of the vocal booth.
- 'The most intimate. I will let you go in.' Julien ushers Sofia into the vocal chamber. The factitive verb 'laisser entrer' means to permit someone to step inside.
- 'Thank you, it is very impressive.' Sofia admires the professional setting. The adjective 'impressionnant' describes the awe-inspiring acoustic equipment.
- 'Here are your headphones.' Julien hands her the audio gear. The presentative 'voici' presents an object directly to the listener.
- 'Relax, everything is fine. Whenever you are ready.' Julien offers warm reassurance. The reflexive imperative 'detendez-vous' encourages calm concentration before singing.
- 'Okay, I am ready.' Sofia collects herself to perform. The feminine adjective 'prete' agrees in gender with Sofia as the speaker.
