## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Wait, come over here, Camille.' Herve calls Camille over to discuss her interview. The imperative 'viens la' invites someone closer to have a private conversation.
- 'Imitate Hicham at your interview? Shouldn't you be yourself?' Herve questions the psychological tactic. The conditional 'tu ne devrais pas' offers gentle, friendly advice.
- 'My head is spinning.' Camille confesses her mounting panic. The idiom 'avoir la tete qui tourne' indicates dizziness or anxiety.
- 'The candidates' resumes are Harvard level!' Camille marvels at the fierce competition. The acronym 'CV' stands for curriculum vitae, modified by the prepositional phrase 'du niveau'.
- 'You have the advantage of working here.' Noemie reminds Camille of her insider status. The noun 'avantage' is followed by the preposition 'de' plus infinitive 'travailler'.
- 'Look, that is Eugenie Leon-Brun.' Noemie identifies the most formidable rival. The imperative 'regarde' directs attention toward the newly arrived applicant.
- 'Law school, specialized in intellectual property.' Noemie recites Eugenie's stellar academic qualifications. The past participle 'specialisee' functions as an adjective agreeing with the feminine candidate.
- 'Three years in production, then two at VMA.' Noemie details Eugenie's extensive industry experience. The preposition 'chez' indicates employment at a specific company or agency.
- 'And plus she is pretty, Hicham is going to love her.' Noemie points out Eugenie's obvious appeal. The informal connective 'et en plus' introduces an added advantage.
- 'I can destabilize her if you want.' Noemie offers to take matters into her own hands. The conditional clause 'si tu veux' makes a polite or conspiratorial suggestion.
- 'You cannot do that, it is not done!' Camille refuses to win through dirty tricks. The impersonal expression 'ca ne se fait pas' denotes socially unacceptable behavior.
- 'Yes, that would be disgraceful. Still...' Noemie wavers between professional ethics and agency loyalty. The conditional 'ce serait' pairs with the strong evaluative adjective 'ignoble'.
- 'Go on, Noemie, crush the rival!' Herve enthusiastically encourages the sabotage. The imperative interjection 'allez' encourages direct action.
- 'I will talk to her about NLP and body language.' Noemie devises a psychological scheme. The near future 'je vais lui parler' indicates imminent action.
- 'Did you really take a training course?' Camille asks skeptically about Noemie's qualifications. The passe compose 'tu as fait' combines with the adverb 'vraiment'.
- 'That is how I learned to control my emotions.' Noemie claims newfound emotional mastery. The verb 'apprendre a' takes an infinitive complement 'controler'.
