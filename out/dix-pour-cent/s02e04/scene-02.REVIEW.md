## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello Isabelle, are you doing well today?' Andréa inquires about well-being.
- 'Very well, Andréa, thank you very much.' Isabelle replies graciously and thanks Andréa warmly.
- 'Do you handle the director Sacha Hartman?' Isabelle inquires about client representation.
- 'Yes, I take care of him.' Andréa confirms that she represents the young director.
- 'I love his work, he is very talented.' High praise from an esteemed cinema star.
- 'Winning a Palme d'Or, that is wonderful.' Isabelle expresses admiration for his prestigious festival win.
- 'He has a very fine role for you.' Andréa links client to new project.
- 'Really? But I never solicit roles.' Isabelle asserts her artistic dignity as a star who never chases roles.
- 'Isabelle prefers that a director asks her.' Mathias defends his star client's traditional protocol.
- 'Directors also like to feel wanted.' Andrea counters with modern auteur psychology.
- 'We had better read the script.' Isabelle cautiously suggests that they read the script first.
- 'Sacha is in Paris right now.' Andréa creates opportune urgency.
- 'All right, but please, be discreet.' Isabelle demands absolute discretion.
- 'Yes, of course, I call him now.' Andréa promises to call the director right away.
