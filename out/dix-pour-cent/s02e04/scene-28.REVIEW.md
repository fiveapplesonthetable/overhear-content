## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Congratulate Mathias on his absence.' Isabelle delivers a bitterly sarcastic remark before leaving.
- 'For what, Isabelle?' Camille asks with genuine confusion and concern.
- 'Tell him to wait awhile.' Isabelle uses a polite imperative directed at the missing agent.
- 'Say, the rest of my life.' Isabelle emphasizes that she never wants to hear from him again.
- 'Mathias, it is a disaster!' Camille bursts into the office in complete panic.
- 'Adjani and Hartman stormed out.' The past tense describes their sudden departure.
- 'We must go right away!' Camille urges Mathias to take immediate action.
- 'Go on, I am staying here.' Mathias refuses to move, paralyzed by guilt and embarrassment.
- 'Tell them whatever you want.' Mathias surrenders all control to his assistant.
- 'Say that I have disappeared.' Mathias seeks any possible excuse to hide.
- 'What? Are you crazy?' Camille is shocked by his complete defeatism.
- 'Say that I am dead.' Mathias exaggerates his professional ruin with grim despair.
- 'This is not you.' Camille firmly challenges his uncharacteristic weakness.
- 'You are a fighter, a winner!' Camille motivates him with powerful words of encouragement.
- 'Come on, get up now!' Camille commands him to snap out of his stupor.
- 'All right, I am coming with you.' Mathias finally gathers the resolve to stand up. Now listen to the whole scene again at normal speed, and see how much more you catch.
