## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Is everything going well, Mathias?' Noemie checks on her secret lover at his private apartment. The greeting tout va bien inquires about someone's general well-being.
- 'Can I come in for a moment?' She politely asks permission to cross the threshold. The modal verb peux expresses a courteous request.
- 'Of course, do come in.' Mathias warmly welcomes his assistant inside. The adverb donc adds gentle emphasis to the imperative entre.
- 'I would have tidied up if I had known.' He apologizes for the disorganized state of his flat. The past conditional aurais range combined with si j'avais su expresses regret about past circumstances.
- 'I have nothing to offer you.' Mathias laments his bare cupboards after moving out. The structure n'ai rien a followed by an infinitive indicates a lack of provisions.
- 'That is great news for Adjani!' Noemie rejoices over solving the major agency contract crisis. The feminine noun nouvelle means a piece of news.
- 'Yes, everything is well sorted out.' The senior agent confirms that the dispute with the star is resolved. The past participle regle means settled or resolved.
- 'I do not feel well.' Mathias admits that intense professional stress is taking a toll on his health. The reflexive expression se sentir bien describes physical well-being.
- 'Sit down in this armchair.' She gently directs him toward a comfortable seat. The reflexive imperative assieds-toi invites someone to sit down.
- 'I am not a fan of massages.' Mathias expresses initial reluctance about physical relaxation. The borrowed noun fan indicates enthusiasm for an activity.
- 'Let me eliminate all this tension.' Noemie begins massaging his stiff shoulders to relieve his knots. The imperative laisse-moi asks for permission to act.
- 'Breathe calmly and relax.' She instructs him to slow his breathing and let go of his worries. The reflexive imperative detends-toi tells someone to unwind.
- 'Oh yes, that is truly excellent.' Mathias sighs with deep relief as the soothing massage takes effect. The adverb vraiment emphasizes genuine appreciation.
- 'You just needed some rest.' Noemie lovingly observes the immediate benefits of stepping away from work. The imperfect phrase avais besoin de denotes an ongoing need.
