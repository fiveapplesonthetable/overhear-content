## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I am here to see Sacha Hartman.' Isabelle politely introduces herself at the reception desk.
- 'Thank you for coming to our meeting.' Sacha welcomes the acclaimed actress with an edgy, formal tone.
- 'Take all the time that you need.' Isabelle demonstrates professional generosity and patience.
- 'Are you ready? Introduce yourself now.' Sacha immediately subjects Isabelle to a beginner audition routine.
- 'When one auditions, really?' Isabelle reacts with mild disbelief at being asked to audition like an amateur.
- 'My name is Isabelle Adjani and I am an actress.' Isabelle calmly complies despite the awkwardness.
- 'We are going to do a short improvisation.' Sacha announces an acting exercise to test her humility.
- 'You are Circe and you ask for forgiveness.' Sacha assigns her the mythological role of Circe facing Ulysses.
- 'Do you really want to humiliate me like that?' Isabelle confronts the director about his gratuitous spite.
- 'You broke my artist heart.' Sacha reveals that his resentment stems from a past rejection.
- 'You rejected my screenplay in the past.' Sacha accuses her of dismissing his earlier work out of hand.
- 'I have never seen your text!' Isabelle states truthfully that she never received his submission.
- 'It is my fault, forgive me both of you.' Camille steps forward bravely to explain what actually occurred.
- 'I was an intern and I threw away this script.' Camille confesses that she inadvertently discarded the manuscript.
- 'Isabelle never read it!' Camille completely exonerates Isabelle from any wrongdoing or neglect.
- 'At least, we cleared the air together.' Isabelle smiles with relief that at least they cleared the air together.
