## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'My mother always used to say...' Outside the agency, Camille shares a hopeful quote from her mother with Hervé.
- 'It is at night that you must believe in the light.' She suggests that they must remain optimistic during this dark time.
- 'Be realistic, Camille. It is over.' Hervé dismisses her optimism, telling her to accept that the agency is doomed.
- 'Focus on what you are going to do next.' He advises her to start thinking about her future career plans.
- 'I would like to start an agency for young talent.' Camille reveals her ambitious dream to open her own business.
- 'That is perfect for you! It is obvious.' Hervé is highly encouraging, agreeing that it suits her perfectly.
- 'Would someone approaching thirty interest you?' He hints at his own secret aspiration to become an actor.
- 'Of course I would sign you at my agency.' She eagerly agrees to represent him if he pursues an acting career.
- 'Are you going to become an actor?' She asks if he has finally decided to pursue his dream.
- 'I do not know, it is too risky.' He hesitates, afraid of the financial insecurity of the profession.
- 'Either way, there will always be a desk for you.' Camille promises him a job as an agent if his acting career fails.
- 'Are you really going to let me produce the film?' Elsewhere, Noémie asks Mathias if he is truly giving her the project.
- 'I am letting you produce the film because you are capable of it.' He assures her that he trusts her skills and abilities completely.
- 'My real gift is that you take care of yourself.' He tells her that seeing her succeed and thrive is his true reward. Now let's listen to Camille and Mathias's career plans once more from start to finish.
