## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'He died in his sleep.' The vet assures her it was peaceful.
- 'It is the best death.' He tries to make her feel better.
- 'And for us too.' He adds that it is ideal for anyone.
- 'It is true, thank you.' Arlette agrees sadly.
- 'I reserved a place at the cemetery.' He took care of the arrangements.
- 'They will come pick him up.' He explains the next step.
- 'Can I do anything else?' He offers further assistance.
- 'It is fine, thank you.' She declines politely.
- 'Can you leave us?' She asks for some privacy.
- 'Of course.' The vet respects her wish.
- 'He is really gone.' She realizes the finality of it.
- 'Goodbye, my dog.' She says a heartfelt farewell.
