## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We are not in court yet.' At the agency, Mathias tells the panicked staff that bankruptcy is just a procedure.
- 'Ask the mother of Camille!' The staff argue over whose fault the impending disaster is, bringing up past mistakes.
- 'Panic is not the solution! Enough!' Mathias tries to calm everyone down and restore order in the chaotic office.
- 'That does not mean that we are closing.' He explains that filing for bankruptcy does not necessarily mean the agency will close.
- 'Hicham, we lost our talents.' Gabriel points out the harsh reality of their massive talent loss.
- 'I know! Our cash flow is underwater!' The agency owner admits that their financial situation is completely disastrous.
- 'So we are on a sinking ship.' Gabriel compares the failing agency to a famous doomed ocean liner.
- 'The Titanic! We are not going to jump!' He refuses to go down with the agency without putting up a fight.
- 'I am just waiting for Andrea.' The owner says he is delaying any decisions until the senior agent arrives.
- 'Does anyone know where she is?' He asks the rest of the team if they have heard from her.
- 'We headed straight into the iceberg.' As Andréa finally arrives, Gabriel dramatically announces their imminent doom.
- 'It feels like the morgue in here.' Andréa notices the gloomy atmosphere and comments on the silence in the office.
- 'What is your plan to save the agency?' She immediately asks the owner how he intends to fix the situation.
- 'Can we stop saying that?' Mathias gets annoyed by the continuous morbid comparisons to death and disasters. Now let's listen to the sinking ship comparison once more from start to finish.
