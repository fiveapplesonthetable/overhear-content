## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Now we can finally talk.' Jean settles in to share what has been troubling him.
- 'I am very worried about you.' Andrea voices her deep concern for the veteran actor.
- 'I am a very simple man.' Jean explains that his personal tastes are genuine and unpretentious.
- 'I love family and friends.' Jean describes what truly matters to him in life.
- 'Cinema has become too complicated.' Jean expresses his frustration with modern industry commercialism.
- 'One must know when to leave now.' Jean reflects on the necessity of knowing when to bow out gracefully.
- 'To leave the front of the stage.' Jean describes the act of stepping away from the spotlight.
- 'I had a very beautiful role.' Andrea mentions an exceptional part she had lined up for him.
- 'A movie about the French Revolution.' Andrea outlines the ambitious historical project.
- 'It sounds good, but I decline.' Jean politely rejects the prestigious screenplay.
- 'Do you prefer ending as Santa Claus?' Andrea questions whether he truly wants a comedy costume to be his finale.
- 'I leave you to ponder that.' Jean prepares to exit while offering some parting wisdom.
- 'I will not give up the fight!' Andrea firmly declares that she will not give up on his career.
- 'Good, then continue the great fight.' Jean offers a warm and supportive farewell.
