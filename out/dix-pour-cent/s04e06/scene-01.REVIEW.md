## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I know, you are swamped.' Jean plays Santa Claus complains to his co-star about her busy schedule.
- 'But I organized this party!' He reminds her that he had arranged the celebration a long time ago.
- 'You are not the only one.' Andréa plays his wife retorts that other fairytale characters are also busy.
- 'Snow White works hard, you know?' She mentions Snow White as an example of someone who works diligently.
- 'It is not easy to bring them together!' Santa complains about the logistical nightmare of gathering fairy tale icons.
- 'After the presents, I go straight home.' She promises to return immediately after delivering the presents.
- 'That is not your line, Lolita.' The director interrupts the scene to correct the actress's dialogue.
- 'It is: Go home and take care of my little cat.' He instructs her to use a heavily sanitized line about a cat.
- 'But what is this line?' The actress questions the absurdity of her new line.
- 'It is a movie for children.' The director reminds them that the film is intended for children and must be appropriate.
- 'But it is forty degrees in here!' The actor complains about the unbearable heat under the studio lights.
- 'We are picking up from the bar crawl.' The director instructs the crew to resume filming from the bar-hopping scene.
- 'I cannot act like this.' The actor loses his patience and declares he cannot work under these ridiculous conditions.
- 'I am fed up! Enough!' He throws a tantrum and storms off the set in frustration. Now let's listen to Jean Reno's movie set outburst once more from start to finish.
