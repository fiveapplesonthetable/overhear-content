## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What do you want to celebrate today?' Gabriel asks what victory she thinks she is marking.
- 'Do you want to bleed our agency dry?' Gabriel challenges her predatory tactics against ASK.
- 'It was not difficult at all.' Elise casually brushes off the effort required to steal their talent.
- 'Tell me quickly what you want.' Gabriel demands that she get straight to the point of the meeting.
- 'You and Andrea formed a duo.' Elise brings up his historic collaboration with Andrea.
- 'Come to StarMedia with your actors.' Elise formally invites him to defect with his roster.
- 'Let us form a new team together.' Elise suggests combining forces at her new agency.
- 'You stop at nothing, ever.' Gabriel condemns her shameless lack of professional ethics.
- 'Your secret dream is to be Andrea.' Gabriel accuses her of wanting to emulate Andrea in every way.
- 'But you will never be her.' Gabriel tells her firmly that she will always fall short of Andrea's stature.
- 'It is just a friendly proposal.' Elise plays down the tension with an innocent posture.
- 'You know where to shove it, right?' Gabriel uses a colloquial rebuff to reject her overture completely.
- 'I am planting a useful seed.' Elise uses a metaphor for planting a persuasive idea in his mind.
- 'See calmly if it sprouts.' Elise suggests that he will reconsider in due time. Now listen to the whole scene again at normal speed, and see how much more you catch.
