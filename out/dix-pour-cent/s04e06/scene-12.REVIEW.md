## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, Madam Producer...' Mathias teases Andrea with her new title.
- 'You scared me.' She was startled by his sudden appearance.
- 'Croissants.' He brought some pastries for breakfast.
- 'Oh, marvelous.' She is delighted by the food.
- 'Long night?' He notices she looks tired.
- 'I studied the project.' She explains what she was doing.
- 'I reviewed the whole budget...' She spent the night looking at the finances.
- 'The numbers do not budge.' She found no room for savings.
- 'We are missing 6 millions.' They have a massive deficit.
- 'Did you call Xavier?' He asks if she spoke to the director.
- 'Yes.' She confirms she made the call.
- 'But he does not want to give in.' The director refuses to compromise.
