## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We have an offer to make you.' Mathias tells Gabriel they have a mutually beneficial proposal for the agency.
- 'That is called a mutual favor.' Gabriel remains skeptical of his former rival's seemingly generous intentions.
- 'And we created a production company.' Noémie reveals that they have started their own film production business.
- 'We secured American financing.' She explains they have secured international backing for a major historical film.
- 'But we would need a very famous actor.' Mathias adds that they need an internationally recognized star for the lead role.
- 'Who do you have in mind?' Gabriel asks which specific actor they are hoping to cast.
- 'Jean Reno.' Noémie names the famous French actor, who happens to be represented by Gabriel's agency.
- 'He would not be working for me.' Mathias clarifies that he won't be managing the actor directly due to his health.
- 'Producing and heart problems do not mix.' Gabriel notes that producing films is too stressful for someone with a weak heart.
- 'Noemie is the one producing the film.' Mathias states that his partner will be the sole producer of the movie.
- 'Okay, that way you avoid asking us.' Gabriel realizes Mathias orchestrated this to avoid asking for a direct favor.
- 'And the film by Jean-Jacques Annaud?' Gabriel wonders about the actor's current commitment to another director's project.
- 'Annaud is over.' An assistant interrupts to inform them that the director has abandoned the other film.
- 'That was Jean... Is he leaving us?' After taking a phone call, Gabriel announces shocking news about his star client. Now let's listen to the casting negotiations once more from start to finish.
