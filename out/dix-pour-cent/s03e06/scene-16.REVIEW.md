## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good evening Mathias, here I am at last!' Isabelle greets her longtime agent. The presentation word 'voila' marks her joyful arrival.
- 'So, is the shoot finished?' Mathias asks about the secret film production. The adjective 'termine' indicates completion.

- 'You achieved an incredible feat.' Mathias marvels at her talent. The noun 'exploit' highlights an extraordinary achievement.
- 'The team sends everything to editing.' Isabelle details the postproduction steps. The noun 'montage' denotes the film editing stage.
- 'What pride to see you here.' Mathias welcomes the beloved star warmly. The noun 'fierte' expresses deep admiration.
- 'I wanted to be here tonight.' Isabelle affirms her commitment to the party. The imperfect 'voulais' conveys her persistent wish.
- 'We celebrate thirty years of ASK.' Mathias marks the major milestone. The verb 'feter' means to celebrate an anniversary.
- 'This agency matters a lot to me.' Isabelle expresses her emotional loyalty. The expression 'compter pour' means to matter dearly to someone.
- 'You are always our favorite star.' Mathias showers her with compliments. The adjective 'preferee' agrees in gender with star.
- 'Look at all these friends gathered.' Isabelle observes the crowd of colleagues. The past participle 'reunis' describes people brought together.
- 'We form a large united family.' Mathias reflects on their solidarity. The adjective 'unie' describes a closely knit bond.
- 'Let's have a drink all together.' Isabelle proposes a shared toast. The imperative 'buvons' invites the group to drink together.
- 'To our agency and to the future!' Mathias raises his glass in celebration. The preposition 'a' is commonly used when toasting.
