## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I just had the actress on the phone.' Andrea explains that star Isabelle Huppert herself is calling.
- 'It is really very important to her.' Andrea underlines the urgency of the production problem.
- 'Patients at the hospital are important too!' Isabelle counters that healthcare duties matter more than movies.
- 'I can offer you free tickets.' Andrea tries to sweeten the deal with exclusive premiere passes.
- 'We mostly need staff here.' Isabelle emphasizes the critical shortage facing public hospitals.
- 'Come to our big party tonight!' Andrea invites the nurse to ASK's glamorous anniversary celebration.
- 'I would be much too embarrassed there.' Isabelle feels completely out of place at a celebrity gala.
- 'My agency is going through a terrible crisis.' Andrea's professional composure begins to fracture.
- 'I am so exhausted these days.' Andrea admits her deep physical and emotional depletion.
- 'My baby does not sleep at night!' Andrea reveals the sleepless nights behind her frantic pace.
- 'Did you just have a newborn?' Isabelle immediately shifts from annoyance to maternal empathy.
- 'It is normal to feel exhausted.' The experienced nurse validates Andrea's postpartum struggle.
