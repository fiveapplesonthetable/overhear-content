- 'Gabriel, I need to talk to you.' Camille tries to deliver her exciting career news.
- 'Not now, Camille!' Gabriel brushes her off, consumed by anxiety over Sofia.
- 'Guy wants me to be his agent.' Camille proudly announces her first major client.
- 'Great, but have you seen Sofia?' Gabriel barely registers the news in his panic.
- 'She was talking with France 2 earlier.' Camille points Gabriel toward the television executives.
- 'Are you looking for someone, Gabriel?' Hicham casually checks on his agitated agent.
- 'Yes, I am desperately looking for Sofia.' Gabriel rushes away to continue his search.
- 'Camille, you should come back to ASK.' Hicham makes an unexpected pitch to hire her back.
- 'That is very kind, Hicham.' Camille responds with polite diplomatic gratitude.
- 'I really need an assistant.' Hicham admits that he needs her organizational talents.
- 'Mathias is the one who pleaded your case.' Hicham reveals that her father advocated for her.
- 'I prefer going to New York with Andréa.' Camille declines in favor of an international adventure.
- 'Working for that American agency?' Hicham inquires about the famous talent representation firm.
- 'Yes, the agency is called UBA.' Camille names the prestigious international agency.
