## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Did Hicham discover your secret about the baby?' Gabriel asks with deep concern. The compound past a découvert comes from découvrir.
- 'Yes, he knows everything and claims to be the father.' Andréa shares her distress. The verb prétend indicates an assertion without proof.
- 'You should maybe clarify things with him.' Gabriel suggests open communication. The conditional devrais offers gentle advice.
- 'He is my boss, I cannot avoid him.' Andréa points out their daily workplace reality. The infinitive éviter means to avoid.
- 'And how is the rehearsal going with Juliette?' Gabriel shifts the focus to work. The feminine noun répétition refers to rehearsal.
- 'Very well, but we have a big problem.' Andréa introduces an urgent dilemma. The masculine adjective gros precedes the noun.
- 'What is happening with Jean-Denis Gillières?' Gabriel recognizes the billionaire sponsor's name. The question qu'est-ce qui arrive inquires about events.
- 'He is inviting Juliette onto his private yacht.' Andréa explains the inappropriate invitation. The noun yacht is pronounced in French with silent final letters.
- 'Did Juliette accept this invitation?' Gabriel asks about her response. The feminine noun invitation takes the demonstrative cette.
- 'No, she categorically refuses to go there.' Andréa affirms her client's stance. The adverb catégoriquement means firmly and without exception.
- 'Careful, this man is a powerful patron.' Gabriel urges political caution. The masculine noun mécène refers to a financial sponsor or patron of arts.
- 'Actresses are not courtesans!' Andréa stands up for female dignity. The feminine noun courtisanes evokes historical courtiers.
- 'You must use your diplomatic skills.' Gabriel counsels tact over confrontation. The plural adjective diplomatiques agrees with talents.
- 'I refuse to give in to his ridiculous whims.' Andréa remains determined. The verbal phrase céder à means to give in to.
- 'Think about the financing of Juliette's next movie.' Gabriel highlights the high financial stakes. The imperative pense à advises strategic foresight.
- 'Alright, I will speak to him with firmness.' Andréa accepts the diplomatic mission. The noun phrase avec fermeté means resolutely.
