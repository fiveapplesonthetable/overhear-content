## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Do you want to eat an ice cream with me?' Annick suggests a relaxing treat to distract Camille from her worries.
- 'Yes, if you like, Mom.' Camille agrees in a gentle, affectionate tone.
- 'Stop torturing yourself over this agency.' Annick advises her daughter not to dwell on past professional pain.
- 'They are real people with lives.' Camille acknowledges the glamour of festival agents while keeping perspective.
- 'Look, it is Mathias calling you!' Annick alerts Camille as the phone rings on the boulevard.
- 'Camille, I have an invitation for you tonight.' Mathias invites Camille to a prominent film party in a private villa.
- 'That is kind, but I no longer work at ASK.' Camille feels awkward about attending without agency credentials.
- 'I really want to introduce you to important people.' Mathias shows genuine paternal care by offering networking opportunities.
- 'I have nothing suitable to wear.' Camille points out that she has no formal evening dress in Cannes.
- 'Look, I found a pretty dress for you!' Mathias arrives with an elegant dress tailored for the event.
- 'It is magnificent, but I am in flip-flops!' Camille points comically to her casual beach footwear.
- 'I completely forgot to get shoes.' Mathias realizes he overlooked formal footwear during his shopping trip.
- 'Take mine, we wear the same shoe size.' Annick generously offers her own stylish shoes to save the day.
- 'Thank you Mom, you are truly wonderful!' Camille thanks her mother with heartfelt enthusiasm.
- 'Go on, darling, enjoy your evening.' Annick encourages Camille to seize the exciting opportunity with confidence.
