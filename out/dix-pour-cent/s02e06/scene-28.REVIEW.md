## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Gabriel, come dance with me tonight!' Sofia tries to pull Gabriel into the joyous party mood.
- 'Wait a moment, my phone is ringing again.' Gabriel stops because an urgent incoming call interrupts them.
- 'Hello Julien, have you finally arrived in Cannes?' Gabriel checks whether Julien made the journey down south.
- 'No, I did not feel like coming.' Julien admits he stayed back in Paris due to grief over his project.
- 'You were right, the atmosphere is heavy.' Gabriel validates his choice, knowing Cannes can be cruel when morale is low.
- 'I do not understand the failure of my film.' Julien struggles to process how years of effort vanished overnight.
- 'This story with France 2 is incomprehensible.' Julien remains deeply baffled by the sudden withdrawal of television funding.
- 'There is perhaps nothing to understand.' Gabriel gently cautions him against searching for logic in arbitrary industry decisions.
- 'Yes there is, there is necessarily a reason!' Julien cannot accept that a life-defining project could end without cause.
- 'I no longer sleep and I have nightmares.' Julien confesses the physical and emotional distress haunting his nights.
- 'My film went from possible to impossible.' Julien captures the fragility of cinema in a poignant contrast.
- 'Everything flipped in a fraction of a second!' Julien highlights how instantly fortunes change in the film industry.
- 'I am coping very badly with this terrible injustice.' Julien opens up honestly about his helplessness and despair.
- 'Keep courage, we will find a solution tomorrow.' Gabriel reassures him that the fight is far from over.
- 'Courage Julien, we are thinking very warmly of you.' Sofia steps in to offer her genuine friendship and empathy.
