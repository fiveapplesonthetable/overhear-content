## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'There are actresses you see a lot.' Jean-Denis Gillieres reflects on transient fame in cinema.
- 'Then they work less, and the charm has vanished.' Jean-Denis laments how careers can decline.
- 'You no longer feel like watching them.' Jean-Denis describes audience fatigue.
- 'But not Juliette. She is still blooming.' Jean-Denis sets Juliette Binoche apart from fading stars.
- 'She opens up, like a wonderful flower.' Jean-Denis offers poetic praise for Juliette's charisma.
- 'Hello, I am Andréa Martel, who represents Juliette Binoche.' Andrea introduces herself to the festival guest.
- 'Jean-Denis Gillières, a great friend of the festival.' Jean-Denis presents himself with pride.
- 'Of course. Delighted.' Andrea responds with professional courtesy.
- 'I am a huge fan of Juliette.' Jean-Denis shares his passionate admiration for the actress.
- 'I would really like to greet her, if possible.' Jean-Denis requests a personal meeting.
- 'Do you want me to tell her now?' Andrea clarifies the timing of the introduction.
- 'Yes, I am calling her right away.' Andrea takes out her phone to arrange the meeting.
- 'Agent... It is a strange profession, isn't it?' Jean-Denis ponders the nature of talent representation.
- 'Working in the shadows so that others can shine.' Jean-Denis describes an agent's self-effacing role.
- 'Shoen... Did you say Schoenaerts?' Jean-Denis stumbles clumsily over Belgian actor names.
- 'Andréa Martel! That is much simpler.' Andrea corrects him briskly with dry wit.
