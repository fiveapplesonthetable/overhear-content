## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Jose? Do you hear me?' Andrea snaps her fingers to regain Jose's attention.
- 'I cannot believe it.' Jose remains dumbfounded by Laurence's unexpected presence.
- 'What is happening to you all of a sudden?' Andrea inquiries about his sudden pallor and paralysis.
- 'Laurence is my great youth romance.' Jose confesses that Laurence was his deepest early love.
- 'We parted on very angry terms.' Jose recalls their stormy and painful separation years ago.
- 'That goes back twenty years already!' Andrea emphasizes that decades have healed old wounds.
- 'I cannot act with her.' Jose expresses intense anxiety about shooting intimate scenes together.
- 'My heart is beating much too fast.' Jose describes the physical symptoms of his panic attack.
- 'Breathe deeply and calm down.' Andrea coaches him through relaxation techniques.
- 'I have terrible hiccups beginning.' Jose reveals that his nervous spasm is triggering sudden hiccups.
- 'The director will call us soon.' Jose fears ruining the shooting schedule.
- 'Drink a large glass of cool water.' Andrea recommends a quick remedy to stop the spasms.
- 'I will never be ready on time.' Jose doubts his ability to pull himself together before filming.
- 'You are a wonderful actor, Jose.' Andrea boosts his professional confidence with sincere praise.
