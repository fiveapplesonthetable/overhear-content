- 'Precisely, this new project pleases me a lot.' Sandrine welcomes the fresh artistic challenge. The verb meaning 'pleases' pairs naturally with the noun for 'project' in everyday conversation.
- 'And the shoot of the historical film?' Gabriel worries about letting down the production team. The descriptive adjective for 'historical?' agrees in gender and number with its noun.
