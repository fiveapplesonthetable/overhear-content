## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Sandrine, did you read this great script?' Gabriel asks if she reviewed the prestigious period biopic.
- 'I do not want sad dramas anymore.' Sandrine rejects repetitive tragic film roles.
- 'Daniele Thompson offers a magnificent role!' Gabriel emphasizes the prestige of the director.
- 'I prefer to make the audience laugh.' Sandrine reveals her newfound desire for comedy.
- 'You want to go on stage alone?' Gabriel is shocked by her sudden comedy ambition.
- 'Comedy gives me crazy energy.' Sandrine describes the rush of live humor.
- 'But it is a very difficult trade.' Gabriel warns her of the harsh stage world.
- 'Precisely, this new project pleases me a lot.' Sandrine welcomes the fresh artistic challenge.
- 'And the shoot of the historical film?' Gabriel worries about letting down the production team.
- 'Tell them that I am unavailable.' Sandrine firmly turns down the period biopic.
- 'The producers expect your prompt reply though.' Gabriel stresses mounting commercial pressure.
- 'I already landed an opening act!' Sandrine proudly announces her upcoming stage debut.
- 'Are you really going to perform five minutes?' Gabriel realizes that her mind is completely made up.
- 'Yes, get ready for my show!' Sandrine invites him to see her live performance.
