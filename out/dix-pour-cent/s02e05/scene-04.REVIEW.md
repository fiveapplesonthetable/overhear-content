## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Mathias, can you sign my employment contract?' Camille asks for managerial contract renewal.
- 'Sorry Camille, I really do not have time.' Mathias avoids immediate workplace commitment.
- 'It is to extend my contract at the agency.' Camille clarifies the administrative purpose.
- 'Is it not for Andréa to sign?' Mathias deflects responsibility back to her direct supervisor.
- 'Andréa is furious and refuses to speak to me.' Camille explains the office tension paralyzing her status.
- 'Do not worry, it is going to calm down.' Mathias urges patience.
- 'But I have no official contract now.' Anxious insistence highlighting legal vulnerability.
- 'Nobody at the agency wants your departure.' Reassurance that her employment is valued.
- 'Then I am going to ask Hicham directly.' Camille boldly threatens to take her request straight to Hicham.
- 'No, especially not, Hicham is very impulsive.' Mathias warns her against provoking the impulsive boss.

- 'How can I live without a salary here?' Camille asks a blunt practical question about living without pay.
- 'Look, how much money do you need?' Mathias instinctively offers financial assistance.
