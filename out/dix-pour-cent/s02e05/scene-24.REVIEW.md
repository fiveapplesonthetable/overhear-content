## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Silence for a rehearsal please!' Vera calls for quiet on the bustling soundstage.
- 'I wanted to say... this girl is called Marianne.' Guy begins his lines with hesitant pacing.
- 'Guy, you are saying my lines!' Hippolyte immediately realizes Guy is reciting the wrong part.
- 'It was to relax you before starting.' Guy playfully covers up his mistake with gentle humor.
- 'Do you not know that, swapping lines?' Guy proposes an acting exercise to get a feel for the scene.
- 'I would like to see what that produces.' Vera finds the unconventional technique intriguing.
- 'Hippolyte, can you say Guy's lines?' Vera asks Hippolyte to play Guy's role directly.
- 'Okay, I start with the first sentence.' Hippolyte accepts the challenge with good grace.
- 'She looked at me and I looked at her.' Hippolyte demonstrates romantic longing with poetic poise.
- 'She was even more beautiful than before.' Hippolyte delivers the emotional description smoothly.
- 'And what could I say to her now?' Guy responds in character with genuine curiosity.
- 'To seduce, one must be very active!' Hippolyte coaches Guy to take romantic initiative.
- 'Hello, I remember you very well.' Guy adjusts his tone into an assertive, seductive greeting.
- 'It is much better, we grasp this moment!' Hippolyte commends the newfound energy of their exchange.
- 'Perfect, let us continue this fine rehearsal together.' Vera approves of their creative breakthrough. Now let's listen to the scene once more from start to finish.
