## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Did it go well with Julien?' Gabriel inquires about Sofia's rehearsal day with the pop star.
- 'I am completely exhausted this evening.' Sofia reveals how physically draining the creative work has been.
- 'Was the session very difficult?' Gabriel asks for specifics about the workshop exercises.
- 'Yes, the rehearsal was truly intense.' Sofia stresses the demanding nature of the artistic preparation.
- 'Did you do dance?' Gabriel probes into the physical movements involved in the musical.
- 'Julien does not want us to talk about it.' Sofia explains that Julien requested artistic secrecy.
- 'I am not jealous, Sofia.' Gabriel insists his concerns are purely professional.
- 'You are acting like a jealous fool!' Sofia cuts through his pretense and calls out his jealousy.
- 'You are the one who asked me for this role!' Gabriel reminds her that he helped secure the audition in the first place.
- 'The boundary between work and private life vanishes.' Gabriel expresses concern over blurred professional lines.
- 'You are confusing fiction and reality.' Sofia explains that stage performance must not be mistaken for real romance.
- 'And the late rehearsals until two in the morning?' Gabriel highlights the disruptive late-night hours.
- 'Julien sleeps very little at night, Gabriel.' Sofia justifies the nocturnal work habits of the artist.
