- 'Look at the tests, it is not Alzheimer's.' The doctor rules out a feared degenerative illness.

- 'So that is good news, doctor?' Gabriel seeks confirmation with a relief-seeking question.
- 'You simply had a minor stroke.' A clinical diagnosis identifying the medical episode.
- 'A small blood vessel got blocked.' A clear anatomical explanation of the transient ischemic attack.
- 'That explains your recent confusion at work.' The physician attributes the on-set disorientation.
- 'Will that pass quickly?' Guy inquires about the expected duration of recovery.
- 'Yes, that will improve with time.' A hopeful prognosis promising natural gradual healing.
- 'Wonderful, I am going to sleep very well tonight.' An optimistic reaction from the spirited veteran actor.
- 'And tomorrow morning, I will go shoot by bike!' Defiant athletic determination to return to work immediately.
- 'Watch out, you will still have moments of confusion.' A strict medical caution warning of temporary lapses.
- 'Moments of confusion during filming?' Gabriel spots the massive professional and financial risk.
- 'Yes, over the coming weeks.' A realistic timeline establishing the danger window for the film.
- 'Thank you doctor, we are going to be very careful.' Gabriel politely commits to vigilant monitoring.
