# E04 editorial and source review

All 28 playable lessons were read and checked against the full original episode: source-video frames for setting and speakers, plus unprompted original-French audio ASR. The dual French subtitles are an English backtranslation and were not accepted as the authority for register or unusual dialogue.

The adapted learner dialogue is intentionally shorter and simpler than the episode. Crude insults may be softened, group exchanges may be condensed, and an ordinary paraphrase may replace a harder original expression. Source situations, important relationships and polite/casual address have been checked against the original audio. Individual annotations keep one entry per whitespace token, with contextual meanings and full English clauses. Regular dictionary forms are retained; reflexive alternatives were added where the ordinary table would not explain the use.
