## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, come in. Are you Pauline Adam?' Both verbs are in the polite form for a stranger. The young woman corrects her.
- 'No. Armelle Borzek.' In this moment, Audrey doesn't recognise the name.
- 'Did you change your name?' Literally 'you have changed of name', in the past tense. Armelle bursts out.
- 'I trained for months.' A reflexive verb in the past, built with 'to be', so the ending takes a feminine letter for Armelle.
- 'Wait, I haven't started the interview.' Audrey still thinks this is about looking after her children. Then Armelle holds out her hands.
- 'Look at my blisters! Touch my hands.' Two polite commands. Her palms are raw from months on the metal pole. Mathias steps in.
- 'Excuse me, what's the problem?' A direct, everyday question with the word for 'what' in the middle.
- 'I learned Canadian French! Why did you do that to me?' Both sentences are in the past with 'have'. Armelle falls to her knees in tears.
- 'You have to leave now.' The verb 'must' followed by 'to leave'. Mathias wants her out of the flat, but Audrey is moved.
- 'Don't cry. But I didn't know.' First a negative command, then the past tense for how things were: Mathias never told Audrey that another actress already had the role.
- 'You can't take my part from me! It was my big chance!' Notice the little word for 'from me' placed right before 'to take'.
- 'It's a cruel profession.' Mathias shrugs it off as normal in the cinema world. Audrey has had enough of his coldness.
- 'No, Mathias! Calm down. We're going to go into the kitchen.' She tells both of them to calm down, and leads Armelle away using the near future.
- 'We're going to have some tea, okay?' Literally 'take some tea'. Over tea in the kitchen, Audrey decides to give the role back to Armelle. A little later, at the door, they are like old friends.
- 'You know what? Thank you so much, Audrey!' Listen to the shift: Armelle now uses the casual 'you' with Audrey.
- 'Come on, enjoy the shoot. Good luck!' And Audrey answers with the casual command too, wishing her luck on the film. Now let's hear the whole scene again from start to finish.
