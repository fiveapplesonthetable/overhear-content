## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I didn't have time to buy an outfit.' Literally 'I have not had the time to buy'. With two small children, she barely has a minute to herself.
- 'I have one month.' Before filming begins.
- 'I've done it before, but a long time ago.' Notice how 'ago' is expressed with the little phrase that usually means 'there is'.
- 'Do we start with the carousel? Oh, that's fast!' The coach shows her a spinning move on the pole.
- 'Careful, it's slippery!' Audrey spins round the pole, slips off, and accidentally bumps her little boy, who starts crying.
- 'Sweetheart! Mummy didn't do it on purpose!' Literally 'my heart!'. The last two words mean 'on purpose'.
- 'Let me see, darling. Did I hurt you?' Literally 'did I do bad to you?'. Just then, Mathias walks into the studio.
- 'Hello, Mathias. Thank you for coming.' Literally 'thank you for having come'.
- 'Crossing Paris with the children is hard.' That is why she asked Mathias to bring the contracts to the studio.
- 'No problem, Audrey.' Mathias is in a good mood now that she is taking the role.
- 'Say hello to Mathias.' Audrey speaks to her little girl, who answers with a cheeky toddler word instead.
- 'Hey, what is this nonsense?' The last word means 'silly things' or 'nonsense'.
- 'She senses that I'm leaving. Children are like sponges.' Audrey is already feeling guilty about leaving them to shoot the film.
- 'I've brought five copies of the contracts.' Mathias gets down to business.
- 'Wait a second. This is a questionnaire for the nanny.' Before signing anything, Audrey wants Mathias's opinion on her list of questions for nanny candidates.
- 'Do you have a problem with alcohol, Coca-Cola, sweets?' Mathias reads the question aloud in disbelief.
- 'What? Sweets?' He cannot believe she puts sweets on the same list as alcohol.
- 'Sugar is very bad! I saw a documentary.' Audrey is dead serious.
- 'Tell me what you think of it.' Literally 'tell me that which you think of it'. Let's listen to the whole scene again without stopping.
