## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Are you sure it's the only solution?' Even though Audrey and Mathias are old friends, in this opening exchange the subtitles use the polite 'vous'.
- 'Trust me, Audrey. Beyoncé did that after her concert.' Literally 'make confidence to me'. Mathias reassures her that giving the fee to charity will turn a scandal into good publicity.
- 'I am delighted to be with you tonight.' Now Audrey is on stage at the microphone.
- 'The media can be cruel.' The verb 'can' is in the plural form to match 'the media'.
- 'I came solely to help the children.' On stage, Audrey almost slips and says 'for my children', then quickly turns it into 'the children of the whole world'.
- 'I am giving all of Mr. Kadropov's money to UNICEF.' Audrey delivers a grand, formal sentence for the cameras.
- 'Ten thousand euros. Twenty thousand euros, sorry. Twenty thousand euros.' She tries to keep half the money, catches Mathias's eye, and reluctantly says the full twenty thousand.
- 'You forgot to sign.' Even when she hands over the giant cheque, she tries one last trick—leaving off her signature!
- 'Sorry. Excuse me. Thank you.' She signs at last, to big applause.
- 'Still no news from the nanny.' Backstage with Mathias, she checks her phone and sighs.
- 'Well, I may have a film for you.' Notice how Mathias switches back to the friendly 'tu'.
- 'What?' One short question word.
- 'Michel Hazanavicius. He saw you with Kadropov.' The famous director of The Artist saw the tabloid photos too—and thought Audrey looked glamorous.
- 'He wants you for the next OSS one-one-seven.' That's the hit French spy-comedy series.
- 'Mathias, that's great!' After everything she went through today, a real starring role has landed in her lap.
- 'You'll be able to pay your taxes.' Literally 'you are going to be able to pay your taxes'.
- 'Can you tell them? I'm scared of the tax office.' Literally 'I have fear of the administration', a playful nod to a real French political scandal.
- 'I'll go and get some champagne.' Literally 'I am going to fetch some champagne'.
