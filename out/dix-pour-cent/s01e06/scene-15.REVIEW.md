## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Don't worry, be natural.' In the studio hallway, Gabriel reassures his nervous client.
- 'Sofia, shall we go?' The casting director opens the door and calls her in.
- 'Yes, I am ready.' Sofia steps forward with a polite smile.
- 'My name is Sofia Leprince, I am twenty-five years old.' Facing the camera, she introduces herself.
- 'And my agent is Gabriel Sarda at ASK.' She proudly adds her agency representation.
- 'Perfect! We start right away.' Constance nods briskly and sets the pace.
- 'First the choreography, then your scene, okay?' She lays out the audition schedule.
- 'Okay, whenever you want.' Sofia agrees politely.
- 'They want to fight with me?' Launching straight into her dialogue, Sofia shouts with intense energy.
- 'I run faster than them!' She boasts about her speed.
- 'Now I am here, now I am no longer here!' She jumps around the room.
- 'Do you see me landing like a heroine?' She strikes a dramatic pose.
- 'This is not at all what I had asked for.' Staring in deadpan silence, Constance stops her. Let's listen to Sofia's audition one more time from beginning to end.
