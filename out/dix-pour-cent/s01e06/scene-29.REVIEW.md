## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I can also give you all my heart.' At the party, Colette told Andréa that all she knew how to give was her body.
- 'I know that I'm not very good at it.' Andréa admits her emotional vulnerabilities and fears.
- 'It's because I am afraid.' In French, one literally says that one has fear.
- 'But I want to learn.' Andréa drops her proud armor for the first time.
- 'I am not strong enough to teach you, Andréa.' Colette tells Andréa gently that she is not strong enough to teach her how to love.
- 'No, I want to learn from you. From you and from no one else.' This phrase 'person other' means 'anyone else' or 'no one else'.
- 'My God! They're together! No, no...' Up on the balcony, the whole agency watches in shock as Colette pulls Andréa into a passionate kiss.
- 'Take care of yourself, Andréa.' This expression 'to take care of' uses the noun for 'care'.
- 'Why do you say that? You kiss me and you leave?' This reflexive expression 'to go oneself away from it' is the everyday way to say 'to leave'.
- 'I am not going to marry her. She prefers women.' Up on the balcony, Arlette teases Gabriel about Andréa's drunken marriage proposal from the night before.
- 'Second, because she is my colleague and my friend.' Even though 'friend' is feminine here, French uses the masculine form 'my' before a vowel sound so the words flow smoothly.
- 'It is difficult to mix work and love.' Gabriel glances toward Sofia at reception as he realizes how true his own words are.
