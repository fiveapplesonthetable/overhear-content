## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'François, it is me. I am here, as planned.' Andrea announces her arrival at the country house.
- 'That is enough! Fox, quiet!' François calms his barking terrier dog Fox.
- 'I am having a swimming pool dug.' François explains why there is an excavator digging up his garden.
- 'Seriously?' Andréa is astonished to hear that François is putting in a swimming pool.
- 'I cannot take the shame of not knowing how to swim anymore.' François confesses the secret shame that made him walk out of the play.
- 'I have turned down so many films.' He admits he has turned down countless roles over the years to hide it.
- 'Which ones? Titanic?' Andréa teases him with humor.
- 'I am proud of you, François.' Andréa tells François warmly how proud she is of him for facing his fear.
- 'I am really sorry about Dom Juan.' Andréa expresses genuine sympathy for how badly rehearsals went.
- 'I know that Christophe Bel has vision.' François admits that Christophe Bel is a genuinely visionary director.
- 'Are you going to do it, then?' Andrea encourages him to reconsider the role.
- 'No, it is too late now.' François hesitates out of wounded pride.
- 'Christophe regrets it too. He admires you, you know that!' Andréa assures him that Christophe regrets the fight and deeply admires him.
- 'Then why does he not call me directly?' François wonders why Christophe has not called him in person to apologize.
- 'Christophe? It is Andréa Martel.' Andrea takes initiative right away and dials his number.
- 'I hope you are free tonight, I am inviting you to dinner.' Andréa calls Christophe on the spot and invites him to dinner that evening.
- 'All right, with pleasure.' Christophe gladly accepts Andréa's dinner invitation. Now listen to Andrea and Francois's conversation once more without interruption.
