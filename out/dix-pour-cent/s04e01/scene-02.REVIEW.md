## Part 1 — Intro
> At the airport arrival terminal, Andrea Martel greets internationally acclaimed actress Charlotte Gainsbourg after an eleven-hour flight. When Charlotte inquires about the artistic merit of her lifelong friend Oscar Rondo's screenplay, Andrea bluffs and lavishes it with praise. Just as Camille sends a text warning that the script is unwatchable, Oscar himself walks up to seal the deal. Let's listen to the scene in French first.
