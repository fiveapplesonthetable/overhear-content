## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Mathias's departure weakens our agency.' Gabriel recognizes the immediate vulnerability caused by a senior partner's exit.
- 'It is the opportunity to rethink our work.' Andréa frames executive turmoil as a chance for positive reinvention.
- 'I said yes to Charlotte too quickly.' Andréa acknowledges a reckless editorial commitment.
- 'She cannot shoot this dud!' Gabriel protests the actress starring in poor-quality material.
- 'Simply tell her the truth without waiting.' Gabriel urges transparency before the project advances.
- 'We must first save face.' Andréa demands an impression of stability before industry competitors.
- 'Everyone expects our downfall.' Andréa notes widespread rival expectations of agency decline.
- 'We are going to prove our great strength.' Andréa affirms ASK's resilience and vitality.
- 'Let's immediately recruit great new talent.' Andréa calls for the aggressive scouting of fresh artists.
- 'We must also bet on television series.' Andréa seeks profitable multi-season broadcast opportunities.
- 'Let's not sacrifice arthouse cinema!' Arlette safeguards cinematic tradition against commercialization.
- 'Series bring regular income.' Andréa highlights steady recurring revenues.
- 'These new projects are going to save us.' Andréa rallies the team with optimism around their new projects.
- 'It remains to resolve Charlotte's problem.' Gabriel reminds everyone that the immediate crisis with Charlotte is still unresolved.
