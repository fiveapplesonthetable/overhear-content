## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello Noemie, I am looking for my dear Camille.' Valentin stops by the reception desk. The verb 'chercher' means to look for or seek.
- 'Camille is finishing a meeting in her office.' Noemie explains that Camille is still occupied. The verb 'terminer' means to end or finish.
- 'Alright, but I must quickly leave now.' Valentin is pressed for time and cannot wait. The verb 'partir' means to leave or depart.
- 'Tell her that I send her big kisses!' Valentin leaves an affectionate message. The verb 'embrasser' means to kiss or hug.
- 'Yes, good luck for your day, Valentin!' Noemie sends warm encouragement. The noun 'courage' idiomatically wishes someone strength.
- 'ASK agency, hello, I am listening to your request.' Noemie picks up an incoming phone call. The noun 'demande' refers to a request or inquiry.
- 'Is this for the thirty-year anniversary party?' Noemie handles event logistics. The cardinal numeral 'trente' means thirty.
- 'We are organizing a magnificent party tonight.' Noemie describes the major milestone celebration. The feminine noun 'fete' denotes a formal party or celebration.
- 'There is no private parking.' Noemie warns about Parisian parking constraints. The masculine noun 'parking' denotes a car park.
- 'But delivery spots are free.' Noemie points out designated loading zones. The noun 'livraison' means delivery.
- 'They are located right downstairs.' Noemie directs the driver to the street below. The reflexive verb 'se trouver' indicates location.
- 'You can drop off the equipment in the street.' Noemie guides the delivery team. The verb 'deposer' means to drop off or unload.
- 'You are welcome, see you shortly!' Noemie concludes the logistical call politely. The temporal phrase 'a tout a l'heure' means see you soon.
