## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Here is Sofia, she is at reception.' Hicham introduces the receptionist to his son. The presentative 'voila' introduces the subject, followed by the spatial phrase 'a l'accueil'.
- 'Hello! Everything is going very well today.' Sofia greets them warmly with polite professionalism. The fixed idiom 'tout va tres bien' is standard for answering how things are.
- 'Here is my son Elvis, for the week.' Hicham proudly introduces his boy. The presentative 'voici' presents a person standing close by.
- 'A week between boys in Paris!' Hicham looks forward to male bonding in the capital. The preposition 'entre' denotes mutual companionship between peers.
- 'He goes to the French school in London.' Hicham shares his son's schooling location. The preposition 'a' indicates the city location 'a Londres'.
- 'It is really one of the best schools.' Hicham boasts about academic excellence. The superlative structure 'une des meilleures' uses the feminine plural adjective.
- 'We will be in my office, see you later.' Hicham announces their destination inside ASK. The parting formula 'a tout a l'heure' is customary when meeting again soon.
- 'I have tickets for the PSG match!' Hicham shares his exciting football plans. The plural noun phrase 'des billets pour' specifies admission tickets.
- 'Camille, do you want to come with us?' Hicham extends a spontaneous invitation. The verb 'vouloir' followed by 'venir' frames an informal invite.
- 'Thank you, Mr. Janowski, that is very kind.' Camille responds with gratitude. The courteous expression 'c'est tres gentil' acknowledges thoughtful generosity.
- 'Do you want to become an agent later on?' Sofia inquires about Camille's career dreams. The idiom 'avoir envie de' governs the infinitive 'devenir'.
- 'Yes, I would very much like to be an agent one day.' Camille confesses her ambition. The conditional 'j'aimerais' conveys a polite future aspiration.
- 'The future of French cinema is in your hands!' Sofia encourages her colleague playfully. The noun phrase 'l'avenir du cinema' evokes the cultural industry.
- 'Do not forget that many actors are actresses.' Sofia reminds her of gender representation. The negative imperative 'n'oublie pas' urges thoughtful awareness.
- 'You do not want me to apply?' Camille seeks clarification on agency training. The verb 'postuler' means to submit a job or training application.
- 'Of course I do, I will think about it with you.' Sofia pledges friendly career advice. The adverbial pronoun 'y' replaces the topic of career consideration.
