## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I am having lunch with my mother at the Grand Colbert.' Camille shares her special lunch plans. The prepositional contraction au precedes the restaurant name.
- 'We need time alone together.' Mother and daughter cherish their rare reunion. The verbal idiom avoir besoin de indicates genuine emotional necessity.
- 'It has been ages since I saw her.' The idiom signifies a very long absence. The fixed temporal expression emphasizes years of separation.
- 'What time is he coming?' Annick checks the exact hour of Mathias's expected arrival. The interrogative phrase asks for specific clock time.
- 'Maybe he forgot.' Annick voices self-doubt about whether Mathias will show up. The modal adverb introduces hesitant uncertainty.
- 'Or he does not want to see me.' She worries about old wounds and unresolved tension. The conjunction ou coordinates alternative fears.
- 'He said he would come for coffee.' Camille reassures her mother about the agreed plan. The conditional viendrait expresses future action relative to the past statement.
- 'Okay, Mom?' Camille uses a gentle prompt to calm her mother's rising anxiety. The tag d'accord seeks mutual emotional reassurance.
- 'I have not done my roots.' Annick fusses over her hair color before seeing him. The plural noun racines refers to untreated hair roots.
- 'You do not have a single white hair.' Camille inspects her mother with reassuring eyes. The adjective seul reinforces total absence of gray.
- 'This is not a romantic date.' Annick pretends indifference despite her visible nervousness. The adjective galant modifies rendez-vous to describe a date.
- 'It has been eight years!' She emphasizes the long passage of time since they last met. The temporal construction specifies elapsed years.
- 'This is a great product.' Camille fixes her mother's hair with a cosmetic spray. The prefix super intensifies product quality in everyday speech.
- 'There, you look very nice.' Camille offers heartfelt encouragement before Mathias arrives. The enthusiastic interjection marks the successful completion of hair styling.
