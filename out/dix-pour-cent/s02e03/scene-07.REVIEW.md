## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hi Gabriel, I have an idea for the ending.' Julien arrives in Gabriel's office bursting with creative ideas.
- 'I want to finish the film in the ocean.' Julien explains why a powerful ocean setting is better than a narrow river.
- 'This is Sofia, she is an actress and singer.' Gabriel introduces Sofia Leprince to his client.
- 'She would be a great fit for your musical.' Gabriel pitches Sofia for the lead singing role.
- 'It is an erotic musical.' Julien emphatically corrects Gabriel by specifying the erotic theme.
- 'Sofia truly has a sublime voice.' Gabriel praises her natural musical talent.
- 'I have never recorded professionally.' Sofia humbly notes her lack of studio recording experience.
- 'I am not looking for anyone famous.' Julien insists that he deliberately avoids established singers.
- 'I am looking for a sensual and vegetal voice.' Julien elaborates on his poetic vision of plant life and sensory feelings.
- 'Lows that evoke the Earth.' Julien describes the rich lower frequencies he wants to hear.
- 'And highs like foliage.' Julien compares the higher notes to vibrant tree foliage.
- 'Nature is important in his project.' Gabriel helpfully synthesizes Julien's artistic concept for Sofia.
- 'It is a very erotic musical!' Julien repeats his favorite phrase with infectious enthusiasm. Now let's listen to the scene once more from start to finish.
