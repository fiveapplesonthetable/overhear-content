# die-discounter: characters and places

Role keys: one character = one role key = the character's own name, lowercase Latin. `cast.json` next to this file is the only source for keys, labels, English names and gender (`spec/quality.md` "Cast"). All art is stylized flat cartoon inspired by the roles, with blank geometric badges and zero text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere.

## Titus (new trainee; first seen s01e01)
- Appearance for the art: young adult male supermarket employee, fair skin `#F6D2B5`, short tousled pale blond hair `#E8D08B`, light gray-blue short-sleeved button-up work shirt `#9BB3C9` over a white crewneck undershirt `#FFF6EA`, dark trousers `#2E2A3A`, small blank red-and-yellow rectangular badge with no lettering. Earnest, slightly awkward expression.
- Role key: `titus`. Label: Titus.

## Places
- **Branch office (`Thorsten's office`)**: warm amber/caramel `#C98B5E` or slate `#6E86A0`, simple wooden desk, plain computer monitor, cream paper stacks (blank, no text), office chair, glass partition overlooking aisles.
- **Supermarket aisles & entrance**: market green `#6BB26D` or sky `#78B9E8`, neat geometric shelves stocked with colourful plain-shaped jars, fruit crates (strawberries, bananas, watermelons) and shopping trolleys — strictly no price tags, numbers or signs.
- **Loading bay & outdoor break corner**: warm brick/terracotta `#D47A5A` or market green `#6BB26D`, dark brickwork, roll-up loading entrance, stacked plain wooden pallets and green plastic crates.
- **Checkout area (`Kasse`)**: teal `#2E9A96` or mint `#86CDB2`, checkout conveyor belt, simple register screen (blank shapes, no digits), small coin dish.
- **Stockroom & walk-in cold room (`Lager` / `Kühlraum`)**: cool slate blue `#5C7FA3`, metal storage racks with plain brown boxes, heavy silver insulated cold-room door with a handle, plain square cardboard pizza box wedged in the doorway.
