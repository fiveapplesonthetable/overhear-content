## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You're a new KBS employee, right?' The camera operator checks Seung-chan's new-employee status. The word for new employee combines newly joining with company staff. Its final sounds politely invite confirmation.
- 'Ah, yes.' His short yes confirms the question. The little word at the start is a natural reaction while he catches his breath. The same acknowledgement works when someone checks an appointment.
- 'Why are you running?' The first word means why and asks for the reason. The other word addresses the running person with respect. Keep those final sounds when you ask a stranger a question.
- 'Ah, I have an induction ceremony.' The short word for I is used when speaking respectfully. The ceremony word joins the ideas of entering a company and a formal event. He gives this as background to his hurry.
- 'Because I think I'm late.' The word for being late carries the main news. The phrase around it presents his own impression, then links that impression back to the question about running. The small spoken word in the middle means a thing or fact.
