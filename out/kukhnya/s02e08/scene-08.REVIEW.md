## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'May I have a word with you for a minute?' Max directs the comment for Barinov: the single Russian word "mozhno" ("it is possible, one may") sets up the whole statement without needing a separate word for "it is".
- 'I did not quite catch that.' Barinov weighs in decisively as they talk, shifting the whole mood of the room in a single beat.
- 'Go on a date with you?' Elena focuses on one pointed detail for Barinov: "svidaniye" means "date", and it carries the main weight of what Elena is saying.
- 'With an old, fat alcoholic?' Max frames this point toward Elena: Russian keeps the exact word order of a normal statement here, turning it into a question for Elena purely through rising pitch at the end.
- 'I just cannot do it.' Barinov opens up sharply without holding back, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Vitya, pull yourself together.' Elena gives this line without pause: spoken Russian trims this down to just 2 words, dropping the extra helper words that English has to spell out.
- 'Look at you, you are shaking.' Max makes the reply before Barinov: the "-sya" ending on "tryaseshsya" ("to tremble") turns the action inward or makes it happen between people naturally.
- 'Be quiet and make a bet with me.' Barinov steps in dryly across the room, shifting the whole mood of the room in a single beat.
- 'Bet me that I will not ask Elena out on a date.' Elena stresses one clear element for Barinov: "svidaniye" means "date", and it carries the main weight of what Elena is saying.
- 'How much are we betting?' Max puts that remark with ease: putting the question word "chto" right at the front of the line lets Elena hear the point of the question on the very first beat.
- 'Five thousand rubles.' Barinov follows up wryly without a pause, saying out loud what both of them already know.
- 'Here, take it.' Elena leans on one memorable element with Max: "vozmi" means "take, pick up" (from "vzyat"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'I bet you do not have the guts to ask Elena out on a date?' Max shapes the comeback right away: putting the question word "chto" right at the front of the line lets Barinov hear the point of the question on the very first beat.
