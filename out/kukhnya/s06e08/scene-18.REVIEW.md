## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Look, we are on fire, everyone outside immediately!' Fedya insists that they are on fire everyone outside immediately, leaving Denis no room to argue. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Does anyone know where our fire extinguisher is?' Asking whether anyone know where their fire extinguisher is cuts straight through the tension between Fedya and Denis.
- 'Well that is it, I missed out on my sixty thousand.' Adding that that is it he missed out on his sixty thousand fills in the missing piece for Denis.
- 'After all, my happiness was so close today!' Because after all his happiness was so close today, Senya does not bother softening the blow for Denis. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Yes, literally within reach of an outstretched finger.' With the conversation and room in play, Fedya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Alright, bye, Denis, see you tomorrow morning.' Around the work and restaurant, Senya sets a calm, everyday tone with Denis.
- 'Denis, and why are you so sad today?' Asking why Denis so sad today is is how Fedya puts Denis on the spot. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Did Katya really not like your gift?' Here asking whether Katya really not like Denis's gift shows Fedya looking for the real truth.
- 'I did not give her anything at all today.' Saying that he did not give her anything at all today is how Denis sets the tone with Fedya. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'You see, everything is very complicated between her and me.' Realizing that everything is very complicated between her and him changes how Denis handles the situation. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Actually I wrote a song for her.' Noting that he wrote a song keeps Denis's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'I simply do not want to pressure her with my feelings.' Even though he simply does not want to pressure her with his feelings, Denis keeps a steady face in front of Fedya.
