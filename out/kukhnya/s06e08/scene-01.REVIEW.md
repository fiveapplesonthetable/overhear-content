## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, why did you freeze up over there?' Barinov opens up sharply during the talk and reaches for the Russian word "vdrug" ("suddenly, what if") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Chef, would you like to break my finger?' Mentioning his finger beside Barinov, Senya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Barinov purely through rising pitch.
- 'If you do not work, I will break your back!' Barinov speaks up lightly on the spot, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Four years ago I cut my finger.' Bringing up his finger in this beat, Senya centers the remark on "palets" ("finger") without any extra fuss.
- 'And the very next day he took out an insurance policy.' Bringing up an insurance during the talk, Fedya brings in "sleduyushchiy" ("next") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'And in four years he has not had a single scratch.' Fedya chimes in calmly without a pause, testing Senya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'And today is the very last day of this insurance of mine.' Touching on the insurance during the talk, Senya centers the thought on "strakhovki" ("insurance", from "strakhovka") without wasting a word.
- 'If my finger is broken, I will get money.' Mentioning his finger at once, the "-sya" ending on "palets" ("finger") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'In my opinion, it is simply great and profitable business.' Senya speaks frankly toward Barinov, so Barinov knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'You might as well break your own head right away!' Mentioning the head around Senya, Barinov reaches for the Russian word "golovu" ("head", built from "golova") right at the center of the line.
- 'They will clearly pay you more money for your head.' Barinov replies candidly with a nod, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'They will give very little for his empty head.' Fedya jumps in at once on the spot, so Barinov knows he is not just making small talk.
