## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Wait, did you rent a room here in the hotel?' Cutting in calmly with Barinov, Nagiyev puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Barinov hears the focus immediately.
- 'Tell me, who were you just talking to?' Barinov inquires briskly why things suddenly took this turn between them.
- 'I was talking to whoever I needed to.' Nagiyev steps in evenly beside Barinov and leans on "razgovarival" ("spoke, talked", a form of "razgovarivat") to give the remark its edge.
- 'And you do not need to know about it at all!' Reacting earnestly to Barinov, Nagiyev adds "sovsem" ("completely, at all") so the statement leaves zero room for doubt.
- 'Guys, and where is our Katya right now?' Barinov presses curiously how Katya expects that to work in practice.
- 'She just went upstairs to a hotel room.' Emphasizing the number with Barinov, Katya centers the remark on "nomer" ("number") without any extra fuss.
- 'Well, sweetheart, did you already deliver the order?' Holding firm boldly right here, Barinov puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Katya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Yes, can you imagine, an acquaintance of Nagiyev's is in that room!' Katya jumps in openly with a sigh, testing Nagiyev's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Wow, what interesting news today!' Emphasizing the news to Katya, Barinov leans on "interesnaya" ("interesting", a form of "interesnyy") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Listen, Elya, do you not want to open this door?' Replying smoothly in the exchange, Barinov keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Katya purely through rising pitch.
- 'What if there is something interesting behind this door too.' Barinov speaks up dryly around Katya, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'I do not understand what you are talking about right now.' Katya opens up boldly right here and leans on "govorish" ("you say", a form of "govorit") to give the remark its edge.
