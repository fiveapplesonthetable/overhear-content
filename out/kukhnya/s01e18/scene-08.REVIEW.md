## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vika, our owner is acting like a lunatic.' -- Viktor vents his disgust with Nagiyev's absurd frugality.
- 'I never thought that I would agree with you.' -- Victoria admits her unexpected alignment with the head chef.
- 'He wants to destroy all the work of our restaurant.' -- Viktor frames the boss's actions as catastrophic sabotage.
- 'He forced the light to be turned off even in the dining hall.' -- The manager laments the compromised guest ambiance.
- 'And he demands to buy the cheapest meat.' -- The culinary leader rails against sacrificing ingredient quality.
- 'Guests are already starting to loudly criticize our restaurant.' -- Victoria reports direct customer dissatisfaction.
- 'We must urgently stop this terrible nightmare.' -- Viktor insists on taking immediate action to end the chaos.
- 'However, what exactly can we do right now?' -- Victoria presses for practical ideas on how to intervene.
- 'We must act together against a common enemy.' -- Viktor suggests forming a united front against their erratic employer.
- 'Are you proposing to fight against our greedy owner?' -- Victoria confirms the high-stakes goal of the partnership.
- 'We will protect the restaurant and restore normal order.' -- Viktor outlines their noble mission to save Claude Monet.
- 'Fine, let us calmly devise a good plan together.' -- Victoria agrees to cooperate and brainstorm a clever countermeasure.
- 'Great, together we will definitely defeat this greed!' -- The head chef concludes with renewed optimism for their joint effort.
