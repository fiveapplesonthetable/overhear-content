## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'In my restaurant I will control everything!' -- Dmitry announces his new strict policy to the staff.
- 'Immediately turn off all the extra lights!' -- He orders the cooks to save electricity in the kitchen.
- 'Where can we get so much money in a week?' -- Kostya worries about paying the higher rent for their flat.
- 'Listen, how about we sell your watch?' -- Max suggests selling a personal item for quick cash.
- 'No way, this is my grandfather's old watch.' -- Kostya refuses to part with a precious family gift.
- 'You are a bartender, you always have money.' -- Max assumes that working at the bar brings easy tips.
- 'I have never cheated our guests!' -- Kostya proudly states that he always pours honest drinks.
- 'You are simply the first honest bartender in the world.' -- Max teases his friend for being so noble.
- 'But we really need to get money quickly.' -- He reminds Kostya that their landlady is waiting for payment.
- 'Then listen, there is a clever way.' -- Max offers to teach him a secret bar trick.
- 'You need to put more ice into the glass.' -- He explains how ice takes up space in a cocktail.
- 'That way in a day you can save a lot!' -- Max shows how small portions add up to big savings.
