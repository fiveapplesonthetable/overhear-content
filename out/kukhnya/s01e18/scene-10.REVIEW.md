## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, are you all right?' Fedya checks on his friend in the locker room.
- 'No, I feel really bad.' Russians say 'to me is very bad' where we say 'I feel really bad'.
- 'I told you not to eat that fish.' Fedya reminds Senya that he warned him about the spoiled fish.
- 'Did you take activated charcoal?' In Russian, you 'drink' charcoal tablets rather than 'take' them.
- 'Yes, I already took two packs.' Senya explains that he swallowed two whole packs of charcoal.
- 'But it did not help me at all.' The medicine has not eased his stomachache.
- 'When I served in the Navy, I accidentally ate a jellyfish.' Fedya shares one of his classic tall tales from his naval service.
- 'Back then the boatswain immediately poured me a strong drink.' He claims the ship's boatswain saved him with strong spirits.
- 'Do you hear what I am saying?' Fedya makes sure the miserable Senya is paying attention.
- 'You need to drink something strong.' He advises Senya to find a strong drink at the bar.
- 'Alright, I will go look for some at the bar now.' Senya agrees to go search for a drink.
- 'If I survive, I will stop stealing food!' Miserable and regretful, Senya vows to give up pilfering kitchen ingredients.
