## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dima, why have the bright lights been turned off?' Barinov is an old friend, so it's the short name. "Vyklyuchili" ends in "-li" with no 'they' in front: someone did it.
- 'We shouldn't spend money on lights.' "Ne dolzhny", we must not. "Tratit na", to spend on; "svet", light, also means the electricity bill.
- 'But it's hard for the cooks to cook in the dark!' "Povaram trudno", literally 'to the cooks it's hard'. "V temnote", in the dark, from "temno", dark.
- 'But at least we'll save a lot of money.' "Zato" again, 'but at least'. "Sekonomim", we'll save, is Nagiyev's favourite word this episode: "ekonomiya".
- 'Timur has just brought us fresh meat.' "Tolko chto", literally 'only what', means just now. "Privyoz" is brought by van or car; on foot it would be "prinyos".
- 'This meat costs far too much!' "Stoit" stressed on the first syllable means costs; stressed on the second it means stands. "Slishkom dorogo", too expensive.
- 'But he always has the very best produce.' "U nego", at him: he has. "Produkty" are food, groceries, not products in general.
- 'Buy only cheap meat with my money.' "Na moi dengi", literally 'on my money'. "Pokupay" is a command to a friend: buy.
- 'Our guests will notice the poor quality of these dishes.' "Kachestvo" is quality. "Etikh blyud", of these dishes: "blyudo", a dish, loses its ending here.
- 'Let the waiters light pretty candles on the tables.' "Pust" plus the 'they' form "zazhgut" means 'let them light'. "Na stolakh", on the tables, ends in "-akh".
- 'Candles won't save a bad dinner.' "Spasti" is to save as in rescue; saving money is "ekonomit". "Ne pomogut", won't help.
- 'It's a great marketing move!' A "khod" is a move in a game, from "khodit", to walk. "Marketingovyy" is the English word with a Russian ending.
- 'You're turning into far too greedy an owner, Dima!' After "stanovishsya", becoming, both words change: greedy owner is "zhadnym khozyainom". "Khozyain" is the owner, the boss of the place.
