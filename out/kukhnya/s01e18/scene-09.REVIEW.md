## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Our rent?' Kostya repeats the word for apartment rent in surprise.
- 'What do you mean?' This short two-word phrase is the everyday Russian way to ask 'what do you mean?'.
- 'In the literal sense.' Max answers with the matching phrase meaning 'literally'.
- 'Now I will explain everything to you.' Max promises to lay out his plan step by step.
- 'I do not understand anything.' Kostya is still puzzled by how the bar can pay their rent.
- 'Let us suppose one simple situation.' Max sets up a hypothetical example.
- 'Timur brings us cognac.' He names the supplier who offered them cheap alcohol.
- 'Five liters of cognac every week.' Max specifies how much Timur can deliver.
- 'Does he bring it for free?' Rising intonation turns this statement into a question.
- 'Yes, absolutely free.' Max confirms that the first supply costs nothing.
- 'And what do we do with it?' Kostya asks how that turns into rent money.
- 'We swap the bottles and pay for the apartment.' Max reveals the core of his risky scheme. Now let's listen to the conversation again without interruption.
