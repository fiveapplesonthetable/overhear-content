## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, have we arrived?' Nagiyev steps out of his car outside the restaurant.
- 'Let us go see how things are going.' He heads inside to address the team.
- 'Guys, these past few days I went too far.' The Russian idiom 'bent the stick' means to go overboard.
- 'Dima, well, we understand everything.' Right then, Chef Viktor warmly accepts his boss's apology.
- 'It is your restaurant, your heart aches for it.' Literally 'the soul hurts', a warm everyday expression of care.
- 'At home I sat down and thought it over.' Nagiyev explains that he reflected on the problem at home.
- 'Kristina and I talked it over.' Right then, Viktor's secret phone call to Kristina clearly worked.
- 'The restaurant should be run by a knowledgeable person.' Nagiyev agrees that a professional is needed.
- 'Well yes, that is completely right.' In this moment, Chef Viktor nods eagerly, expecting to be put back in charge.
- 'That person is right here among us.' Nagiyev builds suspense in front of the staff.
- 'Dima, I am always ready to help.' In response, Viktor steps forward to accept the role.
- 'This is my wife Kristina.' Instead, Nagiyev surprises everyone by naming Kristina as the new boss. Now let's listen to the scene again without interruption.
