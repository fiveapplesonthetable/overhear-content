## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What do you mean?' Chef Viktor can hardly believe what he just heard.
- 'Well, in the literal sense.' Here, Nagiyev confirms he is completely serious.
- 'She is smart and beautiful.' He begins listing his wife's qualifications.
- 'But this is difficult work.' Viktor points out how demanding the restaurant business is.
- 'She studied restaurant management.' Here, Nagiyev notes that Kristina studied hospitality management.
- 'And then she worked here.' He reminds the team of her past experience at Claude Monet.
- 'Did she work in our restaurant?' Rising intonation turns the statement into a surprised question.
- 'Yes, she worked here for a whole year.' Here, Nagiyev emphasizes that she spent a full year working there.
- 'Yes, I remember that time well.' Viktor recalls the days before Kristina married the owner.
- 'In short, please welcome and respect her.' This classic Russian idiom means 'please give her a warm welcome'.
- 'Here is your new boss.' Here, Nagiyev officially hands authority over to Kristina.
- 'Welcome, Kristina Semyonovna.' Viktor forces a polite smile for his new boss. Now let's listen to the conversation again without interruption.
