## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Here, look, I will show you a clever method.' Max grabs his friend's attention, eager to demonstrate a highly questionable moneymaking scheme.
- 'What other method do you want to show?' Kostya is already defensive, wondering what kind of terrible idea is coming next.
- 'Guests often do not understand alcohol at all.' Max rationalizes his scam by claiming that the wealthy customers actually have no taste.
- 'And what did you come up with doing now?' Kostya demands to know exactly what kind of trouble they are about to get into.
- 'We sold cheap cognac for a thousand rubles!' Max excitedly reveals that they successfully passed off terrible liquor for a massive profit.
- 'Have you lost your mind, this is real deception!' Kostya is absolutely horrified, calling the trick exactly what it is.
- 'On the plus side, we quickly earned easy money.' Max brushes off the moral concerns, focusing entirely on how fast they made cash.
- 'If they catch us, they will immediately jail us!' Kostya panics about the legal consequences, terrified of ending up in prison.
- 'Do not be afraid, no one will ever know the truth.' Max tells him to relax, completely confident that their victims are too clueless to notice.
- 'The law is strictly against such dangerous business.' Kostya reminds him that stealing from patrons is a serious crime.
- 'You are a smart guy, you will protect us.' Max tries to flatter his friend into going along with the cover up.
- 'I will not defend such dangerous schemes.' Kostya refuses to be an accomplice, firmly drawing a line against participating.
- 'Calm down, let us simply hide this money!' Max ignores the lecture and urges him to just stash the stolen cash before someone sees.
