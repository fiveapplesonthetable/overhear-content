## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'For me this is too energetic a dance.' Denis weighs in decisively with Elena, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Denis, tell me, what happened to you right now?' Elena weighs in promptly to Denis and uses "sluchilos" ("happened", from "sluchitsya") to make the point stick.
- 'My sore arm is aching badly again.' Bringing up the hand in their talk, Denis lets Elena see exactly where things stand.
- 'No, let us better go back to our hotel.' Denis pushes back plainly with Elena, watching Elena's face to see how the news lands.
- 'I still need to pack my things there.' Denis replies frankly during the talk and chooses "yeshchyo" ("more, still") so Elena gets the point right away.
- 'Yes, of course, then let us go back quickly.' Elena reacts candidly before Denis and uses "konechno" ("of course") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Lena, forgive me, we were just joking around foolishly.' Barinov jumps in lightly without a pause, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'It happened by accident and sent itself.' Barinov speaks plainly during the talk and puts the weight on "poluchilos" ("worked out", from "poluchitsya") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'You know I barely understand computers.' Barinov answers briskly around Elena, so Elena knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'But nothing terrible happened today after all.' Barinov speaks wryly with Elena, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Big deal, some funny article came out.' Discussing the article in the exchange, Barinov brings in "smeshnaya" ("funny, ridiculous", from "smeshnoy") as the key Russian word here.
- 'That is your whole real attitude, Vitya!' Noting how real things are in front of Barinov, Elena links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Lena, wait, please listen to me calmly!' Barinov steps in dryly beside Elena, before Elena can change the subject.
