## Part 1 — Intro

> With Chef Barinov away, guest chef Elena Pavlovna Sokolova takes command of the Claude Monet kitchen. Accustomed to Barinov's relaxed habits, the cooks struggle under Elena's uncompromising discipline. When she refuses smoke breaks and orders sous-chef Lyova to fetch herbs from the cellar, tension flares. Let's listen to their confrontation in Russian first.
