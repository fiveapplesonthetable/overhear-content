## Part 1 — Intro

> Pushed to her limit by Barinov's blatant condescension on live air, Elena Sokolova seizes the moment to turn the tables. She proposes a public wager before millions of viewers: she will work a full dinner shift in Barinov's kitchen at Claude Monet, and if she succeeds without quitting, he must take her out to dinner. Let's listen to their wager in Russian first.
