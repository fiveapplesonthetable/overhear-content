## Part 1 — Intro

> Continuing his provocative commentary on the morning cooking broadcast, Chef Viktor Barinov declares that he would never allow a female chef to work in the Claude Monet kitchen. He argues that women talk too much and cannot withstand the grueling pressure of haute cuisine, but Elena Sokolova fiercely defends the competence and discipline of her staff.
