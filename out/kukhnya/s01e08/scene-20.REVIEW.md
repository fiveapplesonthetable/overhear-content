## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You see, we are an ideal and harmonious couple.' 'You see' is a polite 'there you are'. 'Harmonious' stems from 'friend', so it means 'friendly together'.
- 'We love animals very much and take care of them.' 'Take care' comes from 'worry' or 'care', and it ends in 'sya'. 'About them' is two short words.
- 'In our good apartment it is always warm and peaceful.' The words for 'warm' and 'calm' stand on their own with no subject, the way English says 'it is warm'. 'In our apartment' takes an 'e' ending.
- 'Hector will be able to walk in the beautiful park every day.' The Russian word for 'will be able' is one word that carries the future. 'Walk' is the casual one, for a stroll.
- 'And sleep on the soft couch by the bed.' 'Bed' is a she-word.
- 'The owner is very glad about our kind words.' The Russian for 'glad' takes the 'to' form of what you're glad of, so 'words' ends in 'am'. 'Kind' agrees with it.
- 'She sees how much we suit each other.' 'Suit' is 'fit' or 'come up to'. 'Each other' is literally 'friend to friend'.
- 'Careful, Max, this is just our clever game.' 'Careful' is a one-word warning. 'Clever' here is closer to 'sly'.
- 'The main thing is that the dog finally found its home.' 'The main thing' is a handy phrase to begin a point. 'Found' ends in 'a', since 'dog' is one of the she-words.
- 'I am sincerely happy to take this good dog.' The term for 'happy' carries 'a' since Vika is a woman. The Russian for 'dog' here is the he-form, 'pyos', since the dog is a boy.
- 'Let's go quickly, Hector is waiting for us by the door.' The word for 'waits' takes 'us' directly, with no word for 'for'. 'By the door' uses the short 'u' word for 'at'.
- 'Now our life will become much better together.' 'Will become' is a one-word future. 'Much better' is two words.
