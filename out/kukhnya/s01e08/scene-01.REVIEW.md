## Part 1 — Intro

> Backstage at the television studio of a morning culinary program, restaurant owner Dmitry Nagiev corners Chef Viktor Barinov. Barinov grumbles that he is a professional cook rather than a television star, but Nagiev insists that as the face of Claude Monet, he must represent the establishment on air. Let's listen to their backstage argument in Russian first.
