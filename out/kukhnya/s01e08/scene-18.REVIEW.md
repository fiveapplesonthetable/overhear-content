## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'All these people around are ungrateful scumbags.' Barinov continues his dark rant, painting his entire staff as heartless villains.
- 'Chef, one should not think so badly about everyone.' Lyova gently pushes back against this sweeping, unfair judgment of their friends.
- 'I taught them to cook the city' The chef lists his grievances, reminding Lyova of everything he built from scratch.
- 'Every cook remembers your great lesson.' Lyova insists that the crew deeply respects the foundation he gave them.
- 'Then why are they following someone else' Barinov demands to know why they are following Elena if they are truly loyal to him.
- 'They are simply afraid of losing their job.' The sous-chef points out that basic survival and fear for their jobs drove their obedience.
- 'A true master is never afraid of difficulties.' Barinov counters that a real professional stands up to threats no matter what.
- 'You are the main culinary master for us.' Lyova flatters him, calling him the only true authority they recognize.
- 'Let them handle the complex banquet themselves now.' He spitefully decides to let them drown in their own complicated dinner service.
- 'Without your participation, chaos will break out in the kitchen.' Lyova predicts total disaster if the head chef refuses to intervene.
- 'That is great, let them see my strength!' Barinov relishes the idea of them failing, wanting them to realize how much they need him.
- 'I will stay by your side anyway.' Lyova pledges his own loyalty, promising to stand by his boss regardless of the outcome.
