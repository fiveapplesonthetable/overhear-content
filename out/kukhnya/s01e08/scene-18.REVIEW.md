## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'All these people around are ungrateful scumbags.' 'Ungrateful' grows out of 'not' plus 'grateful'. The last word is rude slang, and Barinov says it with a flourish.
- 'Chef, one should not think so badly about everyone.' 'one mustn't' covers a whole range of 'should not'. 'About everyone' turns the word for 'about' into a longer form.
- 'I taught them to cook the city's best dishes.' The term for 'taught' is formed from a prefix and the Russian for 'to teach'. 'Of the city' ends in 'a', which is how Russian says 'of'.
- 'Every cook remembers your great lesson.' 'remembers' is put together from the term for 'memory'. 'Your' is the courteous form, with an ending that matches 'lesson'.
- 'Then why are they obeying someone else's orders now?' 'Listen' also means 'obey', which suits a chef. 'Someone else's' is one word, and it agrees with 'orders'.
- 'They are simply afraid of losing their job.' After 'afraid', the next word is a bare 'lose'. 'Their own' is a word that points back at the cooks.
- 'A true master is never afraid of difficulties.' 'is afraid' is followed by the 'of' form, so 'difficulties' changes its shape. 'Never' keeps its 'not'.
- 'You are the main culinary master for us.' 'The very main' is made by stacking the expression for 'most' on the Russian for 'main'. 'For us' is two short words.
- 'Let them handle the complex banquet themselves now.' 'Let them' is the word 'pust'. 'With a complex banquet' uses 'so' rather than 'with', which is easier to say.
- 'Without your participation, chaos will break out in the kitchen.' 'Participation' rests on 'part'. 'Will set in' is a word that is used for a season or a mood arriving.
- 'That is great, let them see my strength!' The first two words are a way to say 'that's just fine'. 'Let them see' is the same 'pust' again.
- 'I will stay by your side anyway.' 'All the same' is the Russian for 'anyway'. 'Next to you' is a phrase with 'ryadom', and it's one of the warmest in the language.
