## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, why are you walking without a uniform?' Vika confronts Max for wandering in casual clothes.
- 'Today is my legitimate day off!' Defending his right to relax away from the cooking line.
- 'My only jeans are in the wash right now.' Explaining why he is not properly dressed.
- 'This is a restaurant, not a home laundromat.' Reminding him of basic workplace standards.
- 'And whose little angry dog is this?' Max eyes the growling dog suspiciously.
- 'This is Tyson, Nagiyev's favorite dog.' Revealing the celebrity pedigree of the VIP pet.
- 'Careful, the dog grabbed my hot dog!' Shouting as the terrier steals his lunch.
- 'Tyson, drop it, drop the sausage immediately!' Vika commands the dog to drop the stolen food.
- 'Oh, it seems the dog was hit by electric current!' Max notices the sparks from the faulty wall socket.
- 'My God, he is lying and not breathing!' Panicking as the little dog collapses motionless on the floor.
- 'I told you, this socket is broken!' Reminding Vika of his earlier safety warning.
- 'What will I say to Dmitry Vladimirovich now?' Imagining the owner's terrifying wrath.
- 'Calm down, let's put the dog in a bag.' Max scrambles for a way to transport the unconscious pet.
- 'Quick, let's run to the vet to save the dog!' Rushing out the door to find emergency medical help.
