## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well then, let's start cooking, stop chattering, chop the onions!' Bringing up the onion in this beat, Barinov leans on "nachnyom" ("we start", a form of "nachat") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Yes, we are getting started on the work right now!' Underscoring the work to Barinov, Nagiyev chooses "pristupayem" ("are getting started", from the root "pristupat") so Barinov hears the exact meaning.
- 'Sir, could you be more careful, step back, please!' Barinov pushes back evenly in their talk, so Nagiyev knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Fedya, toss me the fish quickly!' Bringing up the fish to Nagiyev, Barinov brings in "rybu" ("fish", from "ryba") as the key Russian word here.
- 'What kind of mess is this in your kitchen?' Dwelling on the mess before Barinov, spoken Russian drops "is" and "are" in the present, letting Nagiyev place the two halves side by side.
- 'Excuse me, usually nobody stands in this spot in our kitchen.' Barinov speaks up quietly without any doubt, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Hey, step aside, cannot you see I am mopping the floor!' Mentioning the floors during the talk, Barinov reaches for the Russian word "otsyuda" ("from here") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Stop, now all you have left to do is put this into the oven!' Barinov reacts sharply hearing Nagiyev, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Well done, just like in the movies, Nagiyev's eleven friends!' Nagiyev follows up evenly in their talk, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Everyone did great and everyone is simply brilliant today!' Underscoring the handsome man right here, Nagiyev uses "krasavtsy" ("handsome man", from "krasavets") to make the point stick.
- 'Vika, forgive me, I did not mean to scare you like that.' Max reacts sharply in their talk, watching Nagiyev's face to see how the news lands.
- 'Tell me, are you definitely alright right now?' Max inquires calmly whether Nagiyev really thought this through.
