## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'There was nothing really serious there.' The guest tells Barinov that there was nothing really serious there without dressing it up. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'I simply loved playing cards.' Since he simply loved playing cards, the guest does not waste words on excuses.
- 'God gave me such a talent.' Mentioning that someone gave him such a talent moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'It is nothing terrible, you will make up soon.' Saying that it is nothing terrible the guest will make up soon is how Barinov sets the tone with the guest. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.

- 'His important poker game fell through today.' Explaining that his important poker game fell through today lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
