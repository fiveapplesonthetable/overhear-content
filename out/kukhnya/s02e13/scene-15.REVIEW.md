## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Maksim. I do not know.' Even though he does not know, Max keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'He did not like the vegetarian rolls.' Admitting that he did not like the vegetarian rolls shows Barinov being honest for a change.
- 'Just the way he asked.' Saying that just the way he asked is how Lyova sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'Maksim, he asked you to make them just like this morning.' Once he asked Barinov to make them just like this morning, Max can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'Anyway, please try to remember how you made them.' Adding that anyway please try to remember how Lyova made them fills in the missing piece for Lyova.
- 'What kind of vegetarian is he?' Asking whether what kind of vegetarian is he puts Barinov right on the defensive. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Very good.' With the police and conversation in play, Max keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Good thing you brought him straight here.' Mentioning that good thing Lyova brought him straight here moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'I never would have guessed.' Lyova reminds Barinov that he never would have guessed before the moment passes. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'I told you so.' Because he told Barinov, Max treats the matter as settled. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'You need to carry your documents with you.' Explaining that Lyova needs to carry Lyova's documents with Lyova lays the cards on the table for Lyova.
- 'Goodbye.' With the restaurant and shift in play, Lyova keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'That helps us out.' Claiming that that helps them out sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts.
