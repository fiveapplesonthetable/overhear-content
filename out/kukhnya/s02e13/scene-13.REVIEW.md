## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'No, that is not me.' Even though that is not him, Nagiyev keeps a steady face in front of Senya.
- 'You have the wrong person.' Admitting that Nagiyev has the wrong person shows Senya being honest for a change. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'That is all. Goodbye.' Noting that that is all keeps Nagiyev's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'The police! Apparently.' With the shift and conversation at stake, Senya reacts without holding back.
- 'Why is he taking so long?' By asking why he taking so long is, Nagiyev presses Senya for a straight answer.
- 'What are we supposed to do now?' Wondering what they supposed to do now are cuts straight through the tension between Senya and Nagiyev. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Hold on, hold on, just a second.' By telling Senya to hold on hold on just a second, Nagiyev keeps his side of the story grounded.
- 'Hello. Hello.' Over the hospital and the kitchen, Senya answers Nagiyev without a second thought.
- 'Dima, where are you?' By wondering where Senya is, Nagiyev presses Senya for a straight answer. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I am in that place...' Wondering if he is in that place shows Senya caught off guard.
- 'At the restaurant.' Around the hospital and work, Nagiyev sets a calm, everyday tone with Senya.
- 'Drinking coffee.' Over the hospital and the truth, Senya answers Nagiyev without a second thought.
- 'I will call you back.' Claiming that he will call Senya back sounds confident, even if Senya has doubts.
