## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'And what are you doing in here?' Here asking what Kristina doing in here is shows Nastya looking for the real truth. Even in the middle of the drama, she keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'That was not part of our deal.' Admitting that that was not part of their deal shows Nagiyev being honest for a change.
- 'I thought so.' Between the moment and the situation, Kristina gives Nagiyev a quick, grounded response.
- 'I do not understand.' Once she does not understand, Nastya can finally tell Kristina what she means.
- 'Wait, do you know this weirdo?' Here checking whether Kristina knows this weirdo shows Nagiyev looking for the real truth.
- 'Ah, so that is what this is about.' Saying that that is what this is is how Kristina sets the tone with Nagiyev.
- 'You hired a private detective to spy on me?' With the truth and situation hanging in the air, Nastya puts Kristina on the spot. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'I thought he was a journalist.' Mentioning that he thought he was a journalist moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Not me personally, of course.' With the conversation and situation in play, Kristina keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'These handsome guys right here did it.' Because these handsome guys right here did it, Nastya treats the matter as settled.
- 'I will sort everything out right now.' Explaining that he will sort everything out right now lays the cards on the table for Kristina.
- 'I certainly hope so.' Around the work and truth, Kristina sets a calm, everyday tone with Nagiyev.
- 'I already told you.' Claiming that she already told Kristina sounds confident, even if Kristina has doubts.
