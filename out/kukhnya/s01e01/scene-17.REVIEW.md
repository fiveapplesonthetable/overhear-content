## Part 1 — Intro

> Turning a corner in the restaurant corridor, Vika is stunned to run into Max, the young man from the nightclub. Acting completely relaxed, Max claims the famous head chef is practically begging him to join the kitchen staff. Indignant at his casual attitude and cocky remarks about the restaurant, Vika demands an explanation. Let's listen to their conversation in Russian first.
