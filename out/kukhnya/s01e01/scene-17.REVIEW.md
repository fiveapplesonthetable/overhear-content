## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Wait a minute... What are you doing here at all?' Vika stops in her tracks, utterly bewildered by the unexpected appearance of her recent date.
- 'Hello, I am simply strolling through your restaurant.' Playing it incredibly cool, the young man pretends he is merely taking a casual wander inside.
- 'How did you dare to come into my establishment?' Furious at his audacity, the manager views his presence as a direct invasion of her territory.
- 'I was just chatting for a long time with your head chef.' Dropping a casual brag, the intruder implies he just had a lengthy sit-down with the boss.
- 'And what did you talk with him about?' Highly skeptical of this claim, the art director demands to know the nature of their chat.
- 'He is persistently inviting me to work with him.' Exaggerating wildly, the cocky cook claims the culinary genius is begging for his services.
- 'You are joking, the chef never takes novices!' Knowing the strict hiring practices, the manager immediately calls out the sheer improbability of this.
- 'Here I stand and think, whether to agree or not.' Maintaining the facade, the young chef pretends to be weighing his options about the job offer.
- 'Do you dare to still doubt our level?' Insulted by his hesitation, the proud manager defends the high prestige of her workplace.
- 'Frankly speaking, your kitchen seems a bit basic to me.' Throwing shade, the cheeky applicant acts unimpressed by the culinary complexity of their menu.
- 'Our restaurant is one of the best in Moscow!' Defending her establishment's honor, the fiery woman boasts about their elite status in the city.
- 'Then I, perhaps, will agree to show my talent.' Smirking in victory, the newcomer graciously agrees to grace them with his supposed culinary brilliance.
- 'I will personally check these ridiculous words right now!' Marching off to investigate, the manager refuses to believe a single word of his ridiculous story.
