## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Thank you, guys, let us work now.' Even though guys let them work now, Senya keeps a steady face in front of Eleonora. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Alright, I congratulate you on the victory.' Admitting that Alright he congratulates Eleonora on the victory shows Fedya being honest for a change.
- 'Oh, I suddenly felt sick.' Saying that he suddenly felt sick is how Senya sets the tone with Eleonora.
- 'Is that from your nerves?' By checking whether that from Eleonora's nerves, Fedya presses Eleonora for a straight answer. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'This is such a fair fight, right?' When Fedya asks if this is such a fair fight right, Eleonora has to answer fast.
- 'Timur, where were you even looking?' Wondering where Eleonora even looking was is how Fedya puts Eleonora on the spot. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'All around there is nothing but corruption!' Because all around there is nothing but corruption, Fedya does not bother softening the blow for Eleonora. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Who brought a horse into the hotel?' Wondering if who brought a horse into the hotel shows Eleonora caught off guard.
- 'Forgive me, I tied her up tightly.' Around the kitchen and fish, Senya sets a calm, everyday tone with Eleonora.
- 'But she came untied and ran away.' Because she came untied and ran away, Senya treats the matter as settled.
- 'She rushes around and bites the guests!' Over the shift and the team, Eleonora lets her real feelings show.
- 'There will be no more such incidents.' Senya mentions that there will be no more such incidents so Eleonora sees what is at stake.
