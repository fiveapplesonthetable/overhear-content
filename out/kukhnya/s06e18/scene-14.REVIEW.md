## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'But this is a secret vote, after all.' Fedya frames that reply with warmth: notice how Russian needs no word for "am", "is", or "are" in the present — placing the words side by side says it all.
- 'Go on out of here, call the next person in.' Timur answers wryly toward Senya, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Good afternoon, I have a question.' Senya picks one key choice for Fedya: "dobryy" means "good", and it carries the main weight of what Senya is saying.
- 'Can the independent observer not watch?' Senya phrases the remark frankly here: Russian keeps the exact word order of a normal statement here, turning it into a question for Timur purely through rising pitch at the end.
- 'No, one cannot do that at all.' Timur speaks up evenly with a sigh, testing Fedya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Understood, and what if like this?' Senya leans on this vivid piece in reply: "ponyatno" means "clear", and it carries the main weight of what Senya is saying.
- 'Great, now you have voted fairly.' Timur gives that answer plainly here: the single Russian word "otlichno" ("great, excellent") sets up the whole statement without needing a separate word for "it is".
- 'Let us sum up the results of our fair contest.' Timur steps in decisively without holding back, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Today Arseny won the election!' Timur emphasizes one colorful element at once: "vyborakh" means "election" (from "vybory"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'How did he suddenly win like that?' Fedya gives the comeback frankly here: putting the question word "kak" right at the front of the line lets Timur hear the point of the question on the very first beat.
- 'Does anyone have any objections right now?' Timur questions promptly where this conversation is heading next.
- 'You can take the ballots for salad.' Timur focuses on this sharp piece in reply: "byulleteni" means "ballots" (from "byulleten"), giving the line its exact edge.
