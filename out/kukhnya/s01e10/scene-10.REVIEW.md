## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, hurry up the order already!' Kostya rushes the kitchen line during service.
- 'The guest at table five is waiting.' Kostya highlights the demanding customer.
- 'Everything is ready, you can take the dish.' Viktor hands over the freshly prepared meal.
- 'Great, taking it to the dining room.' Kostya prepares to deliver the hot plate.
- 'Wait, one second, wait for me here!' Viktor halts the waiter before he can leave.
- 'Chef, I need to work right now.' Kostya points to his busy dining room duties.
- 'Kostya, tell me, how are your affairs?' Viktor tries small talk to break the ice.
- 'Fine, tips are good today.' Kostya mentions his successful shift at the tables.
- 'Really good? You are a wealthy man!' Viktor flatters him with exaggerated compliments.
- 'I just need to borrow a little.' Viktor makes his direct financial request.
- 'Chef, you want my tips?' Kostya reacts with protective disbelief.
- 'Only a couple of thousand until morning!' Viktor begs for a temporary emergency loan.
- 'Chef, let us talk later!' Kostya makes a swift excuse and hurries away.
- 'All right, go, but I will return!' Viktor calls after him with stubborn persistence.
