## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Fine, it is ordinary pike roe, Dim.' Russians shorten a name when they call someone: "Dima" becomes "Dim". "Ordinary" is the plain word. Barinov gives up. His tone is almost relieved.
- 'Scientists say it is even healthier than black!' "Healthier" is "useful" with the comparative ending. There is no "than": black caviar takes the "of" ending. Barinov tries a last defence.
- 'Sorry, we could not get decent one in time.' "Get hold of" means obtaining something. "Decent" is the word "normal". "In time" is one word. He sounds almost apologetic.
- 'Therefore I decided to dye it a little.' "Poetomu" is "therefore", the cousin of "because". "Dye" is the verb from the kitchen. Barinov confesses without shame.
- 'If your scam surfaces, they will simply kill you!' "Surfaces" means floats up. "They will kill" has no one named. Nagiyev exaggerates, and the exaggeration is part of the joke.
- 'They will kill you and bury you in the woods.' "In the woods" is "v lesu", with a special ending for forest. "Bury" is "dig in". Nagiyev adds detail, and the detail is cheerful.
- 'I will not even weep at your grave.' "Will not even" is "dazhe ne budu". "Grave" is "mogila". Nagiyev speaks lightly of a heavy matter. The effect is comic.
- 'And if it does not surface, you are a real lucky guy.' "Lucky one" grows from the word that meant "you got lucky" in the first scene. "Real" is the word you know. Nagiyev's logic is generous.
- 'You will work an entire month without salary!' "Without" is the small word from "without lumps". "Salary" takes the "of" ending. Nagiyev's sentence is a harsh one, and he delivers it calmly.
- 'I am fining you two hundred thousand rubles.' "Fine" is the verb from the noun for a fine. Two hundred thousand is exactly what Timur wanted for the caviar. It's a tidy joke.
- 'Seriously? On what will I live for a month?!' "On what" is the cook's question about the stove, and now it concerns life. "Live" is the root of "life". Barinov is dismayed.
- 'That is all, go to the kitchen and do not anger me!' "That's all" is "vsyo". "Do not anger" is "don't make me angry", from the word for "evil". Nagiyev ends the talk, and it is time for the chef to go.
- 'Yes sir, chef, I mean Dmitry Vladimirovich.' "Slushayus" is "I listen to myself", with the -sya, and means "yes sir". Max slips and calls Nagiyev "chef". "That is" is the word he uses to correct himself.
