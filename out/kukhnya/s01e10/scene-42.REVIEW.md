## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Fine, it is ordinary pike roe, Dim.' Barinov finally drops the charade and admits the truth.
- 'Scientists say it is even healthier than black!' After confessing that it is dyed pike roe, Viktor jokes weakly that scientists say pike roe is even healthier.
- 'Sorry, we could not get decent one in time.' Barinov cites delivery failures for the scam.
- 'Therefore I decided to dye it a little.' He explains that since they could not get sturgeon caviar in time, he decided to tint the pike roe.
- 'If your scam surfaces, they will simply kill you!' Nagiyev whispers in horror that if the tough VIP guests find out, they will kill Viktor.
- 'They will kill you and bury you in the woods.' The owner paints a terrifying picture of revenge.
- 'I will not even weep at your grave.' He jokes darkly that if they bury Viktor in the woods, he will not even cry at his grave.
- 'And if it does not surface, you are a real lucky guy.' However, if the guests never notice the dye, Nagiyev says Viktor is the luckiest man alive.
- 'You will work an entire month without salary!' Even so, for pocketing the caviar money, Nagiyev sentences Viktor to work a full month without pay.
- 'I am fining you two hundred thousand rubles.' The restaurant owner names the stiff financial penalty.
- 'Seriously? On what will I live for a month?!' Viktor protests in dismay, asking how he is supposed to live for a month after a two-hundred-thousand-ruble fine.
- 'That is all, go to the kitchen and do not anger me!' Nagiyev tells him to march back to the kitchen before he makes the fine even worse.
- 'Yes sir, chef, I mean Dmitry Vladimirovich.' Defeated, Barinov mutters 'yes, chef' — before correcting himself to address the owner.
