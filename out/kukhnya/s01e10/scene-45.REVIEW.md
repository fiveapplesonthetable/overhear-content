## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, you definitely should not play poker.' "To you not worth it" is the Russian for "you shouldn't". The russian for "worth" also means "costs". Vika teases, and the teasing is affectionate.
- 'You do not know how to bluff calmly at all.' "Know how to" is the skill word. "Calmly" is the same as the caviar scene's "calm". Vika has watched him, and she knows his tells.
- 'What was all this silly deception for anyway?' "To what" is Russian for "what for". "Deception" is the noun for the word "deceive". Max is half annoyed, half relieved.
- 'I just wanted to test your initial reaction.' "Test" covers checking. "Reaction" is borrowed. "First" in front of it gives "initial". Vika says it lightly.
- 'Why were you immediately frightened of adult responsibility?' "Adult responsibility" is the phrase from the lecture in the office. "Frightened" is the -sya word. Vika asks the real question.
- 'I was not frightened, I was just not ready.' "I was not ready" uses the short form "gotov" with "was". Max defends himself, and it's a weak defence. He admits the rest by saying it.
- 'By the way, people do not joke about such things at all.' "Among other things" is Russian for "by the way". "They do not joke" is a "they" without a subject: it means "people don't". Max is sulking.
- 'I already came up with a real beautiful name.' "Came up with" means inventing. "Name" is "imya". Max is proud of his idea, and he wants credit.
- 'Zhenya is the most beautiful name in the world.' "In the world" is "v mire". "The most beautiful" has the "most" first. Max is loyal to his name, even though the baby is imaginary.
- 'It will suit a boy and a girl perfectly.' "Suits" is the "approach" word again. "A boy and a girl" are in the "for" form. Max argues like a lawyer.
- 'My God, Zhenya is a very strange name!' "My God" is the exclamation from Barinov's chocolate panic. Vika is not impressed. She teases, and he has given her the chance.
- 'Life gives us thousands of different opportunities.' "Thousands" is a plural noun. "Opportunities" grows out of "possible". Max begins a speech. The tone changes.
- 'An opportunity to change and start over.' "Start all from the beginning" is the Russian for "start over". "Change" has the -sya. Max sounds philosophical, like the end of a lesson.
- 'Or a chance to discover new talents within oneself.' "In oneself" is "v sebe". "Discover" is the "open" word. Max finishes the thought, and the episode is nearly over.
