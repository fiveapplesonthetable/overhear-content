## Part 1 — Intro

> In the dining room of Claude Monet, a representative from a television studio meets with Chef Viktor Barinov. Impressed by his past guest appearance, producers want him to become the permanent host of a weekly morning cooking program, but their proposed fee leads to immediate outrage. Let's listen to their conversation in Russian first.
