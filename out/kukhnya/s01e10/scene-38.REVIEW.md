## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Maxim, did you think that I would get scared?' She calls him "Maksim", in full. "Get scared" is a future after "that", as in English. Vika is calm, and her calm is worse than anger.
- 'I simply made you a beautiful proposal.' Russians "make a proposal" to propose marriage. In Russian, "proposal" is also a sentence in grammar. Max says it with an innocent face.
- 'You wanted to test my courage now?' "Test" covers checking, as at the banquet. "Courage" grows out of "brave". Vika sees what Max was doing, and she says it back to him.
- 'I am ready to create a strong family with you.' "Create" is a grand word. "Strong" matches in "strong tea". Max talks as if he were reading a vow.
- 'Are you confident in your loud words?' "Your own" is the word that marks the speaker's own words. "Loud" is the cousin of "loudly". Vika puts a doubt into a question.
- 'I am absolutely confident in every word of mine!' "In every own word" is the literal. "Every" and "own" both show the "in" ending. Max doubles down.
- 'Then listen to my honest answer in front of everyone.' "At everyone" is Russian for "in front of everyone". "Answer" shares a root with "answer for", from the chef's speech. Vika prepares her audience.
- 'I agree to become your lawful wife!' "Agree" has the woman's -a. After "become", "wife" takes the "as" ending. "Lawful" grows out of "law". Vika says yes, in a loud voice.
- 'Are you really speaking the pure truth now?' "Really" is built on "action". "Pure truth" is the "clean truth". Max can't believe it, and he asks again.
- 'Yes, I accept your unexpected proposal.' "Accept" is the root of "accepted" in the bet scene. "Unexpected" is "not awaited". Vika is formal, even in the middle of the joke.
- 'Now we will become real groom and bride!' "Groom" and "bride" are the two words, with the "as" ending after "become". The pair sounds like a title. Max shouts it with false cheer.
- 'We will see how you handle this, Maxim!' "We will see" is a short future with "po-". "Handle" is the "cope" word. Vika puts a challenge to him in a single sentence.
- 'I am ready for all the difficulties of our wedding!' "Difficulties" is the plural of a word that comes from "work". "To all" has the "to" ending. Max sounds like a general on parade.
