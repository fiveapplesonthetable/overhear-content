## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, why are you shouting so loudly?' Max steps cautiously into the office hallway.
- 'Quickly get out of here and close the door!' Storming into his office after losing both the bet and his monthly salary, Barinov yells at Max to get out and shut the door.
- 'I just brought clean chef aprons.' The young cook tries to justify his presence.
- 'I do not need any aprons right now, runt!' When Max holds up clean aprons, Viktor snaps that he could not care less about aprons right now.
- 'Did Spartak really lose this match again?' Max correctly deduces the source of the anger.
- 'They conceded a goal in the last minute!' Barinov recounts the tragic finish in horror.
- 'Chef, it is only an ordinary sports game.' After learning about the match, Max tries to console the chef by saying football is only a game.
- 'For me football is all my life!' Viktor roars back that for him, football is his entire life.
- 'But guests in the hall are waiting for hot dishes.' Max gently points toward restaurant priorities.
- 'Let Lyova command the hot station himself!' When Max mentions the dinner orders, Viktor tells him to let Lyova run the hot line himself.
- 'Lyova is very anxious without your strict instructions.' Max points out that Lyova gets anxious without the chef's command.
- 'Fine, go peel potatoes and do not bother me!' Viktor waves Max away, telling him to go peel potatoes and leave him in peace.
- 'Right away, chef, I am already running back to the kitchen.' Max quickly salutes the furious chef and hurries back to the kitchen.
