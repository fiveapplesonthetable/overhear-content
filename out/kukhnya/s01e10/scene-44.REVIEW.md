## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, why are you shouting so loudly?' "Why" and "so loudly" come with "shout". Max uses "vy", the polite you, even when asking about noise. He steps into the room cautiously.
- 'Quickly get out of here and close the door!' "Go away" is the command with "u-". "From here" is the single word you know. Barinov wants to be alone, and he wants the door shut.
- 'I just brought clean chef aprons.' "Cook's" is built from "cook", so it's "cook-ish aprons". "Clean" is the same as "pure" in "pure truth". Max explains his errand. It's honest and useless.
- 'I do not need any aprons right now, runt!' "Not any" is "nikakie" with the double negative. "Oguzok" is the nickname, and Barinov uses it when he is in a bad mood. Max has heard it often.
- 'Did Spartak really lose this match again?' "Really" is the "neuzheli" of the guests. Max asks a question to which he knows the answer. It's a risky thing to do.
- 'They conceded a goal in the last minute!' Football slang: "let a goal pass". "In the last minute" has the "in" ending. Barinov reports it as a disaster.
- 'Chef, it is only an ordinary sports game.' "Only just" is two small words. "Sports" is a describing word. Max says the wrong thing. It's a blasphemy to the chef.
- 'For me football is all my life!' "All my life" is "vsya moya zhizn". "Life" is the root of "alive". Barinov means it. His entire mood depends on a ball.
- 'But guests in the hall are waiting for hot dishes.' "Wait dishes" has no "for". "Hot" is the cooks' word. Max tries to bring him back to work.
- 'Let Lyova command the hot station himself!' "Let" is back from before. "Himself" is "sam". Barinov hands over the work to Lyova, and he does it grumpily.
- 'Lyova is very anxious without your strict instructions.' "Worries" is the live-through word. "Instructions" comes from "to point out". Max tells the chef the truth, and he gets away with it.
- 'Fine, go peel potatoes and do not bother me!' "Peel" is the same as "clean", the word from the first scene's "peel and cut". "Do not bother" is the other meaning of "meshat". Max is demoted.
- 'Right away, chef, I am already running back to the kitchen.' "Slushayus" is "I obey". "Run" is the on-foot word, in the moment. "Back" is the word "obratno". Max flees, as he does.
