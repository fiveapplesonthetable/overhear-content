## Part 1 — Intro

> Having tasted the black caviar and praised its flavor, Chef Barinov is hit with a hefty price tag of two hundred thousand rubles. When Barinov admits he lost all his cash on a bad football bet, the supplier flatly refuses credit and demands payment for the sample. Let's listen to their conversation in Russian.
