## Part 1 — Intro

> In his private office at Claude Monet, Chef Viktor Barinov desperately needs a quick financial windfall to pay off debts. Calling bookmaker Tamara, he shocks her by placing a massive hundred-thousand-ruble wager on CSKA to beat his lifelong beloved club Spartak. Let's listen to their phone call in Russian first.
