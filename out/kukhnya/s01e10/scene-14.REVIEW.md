## Part 1 — Intro

> In the Claude Monet kitchen, line cook Senya is astonished to discover that their large commercial stove has disappeared without explanation. To conceal the fact that he secretly pawned the kitchen equipment to place a football bet, Chef Barinov insists he sent it for routine maintenance.
