## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good morning to all early viewers of our show!' "Early" and "viewers" are two words with endings for "to". The host greets his audience in a warm voice, as hosts do on morning television.
- 'With you again is your favorite show Delicious Morning.' Russian TV hosts say "with you again", where English says "welcome back". "Broadcast" is "peredacha", from "pass on". The name is "Vkusnoe utro".
- 'Today we will prepare simple appetizing sunny-side-up eggs.' "Glazunya" comes from "glaz", the term for "eye": a fried egg looks like an eye. Russians have a lovely name for it.
- 'Every morning opens a new day before us.' "Opens" is the "open" word. "Before us" has the "by" ending. Max talks over the cooking, with a sense of calm.
- 'We take fresh eggs and heat the frying pan.' The host says "we take", as presenters do. "Heat" is what the pastry chef used for the chocolate. "Fresh" is the croissants' word.
- 'Sometimes simple things turn out to be the most important.' "Turn out" is the -sya word. "Simple things" are in the plural. Max says it softly, as a wise voice-over.
- 'We add a little butter and salt.' Russian butter is "creamy oil". "Salt" and "butter" take the "some of" ending. The host adds ingredients, as in a recipe.
- 'We often look for complex answers to questions.' "Often" is a short word. "Look for" is what the chef used for Alisa. Max says it to the audience.
- 'A few minutes and a wonderful breakfast is ready!' "A few" is one word. "Ready" is the neuter "gotov" form again. The host is cheerful, as always.
- 'The main thing is never to miss your opportunities.' "Never miss" is "never let slip", the word the guest used for Spartak's victory. Max's advice has the same sound as a proverb.
- 'Fate might not give us a second chance.' "Fate" is "sudba". "Second" stands for the second one. Max says it solemnly, with the voice of a narrator.
- 'Bon appetit and have a good day everyone!' Russian wishes take the "of" form: "of pleasant appetite". It is the host's sign-off. He says it, and the show ends.
- 'Value every moment of your real life.' "Value" is a command to many. "Moment" is a short, poetic word. "Real" is the word the chef loved. Max closes the episode with a line of advice.
