## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Tell me, has everyone gone home already?' Questioning whether tell him has everyone gone home already is how Max tests Barinov's story.
- 'Yes, and the Chef is finally sleeping.' Saying that the Chef is finally sleeping is how Katya sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'I barely managed to put him to bed today.' Since she barely managed to put him to bed today, Katya does not waste words on excuses.
- 'He kept trying to burst out to find you.' Mentioning that he kept trying to burst out to find Barinov moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'So, what have you cooked up over there?' Wondering if what have Barinov cooked up over there shows Katya caught off guard. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'You will see right now, come on through here, please.' Because Barinov will see right now come on through here please, Max treats the matter as settled.
- 'And just what is our plan?' Katya asks whether just what is their plan, refusing to let the point slide.
- 'Look, does this not remind you of anyone?' By checking whether this not remind Barinov of anyone, Max presses Barinov for a straight answer. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'What on earth happened in here yesterday?' With the conversation and restaurant hanging in the air, Barinov puts Katya on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Do you really remember nothing at all?' Checking whether Barinov really remember nothing at all is how Katya puts Barinov on the spot.
- 'Of course, you can just get drunk and forget.' Katya explains that Barinov can just get drunk and forget, keeping her voice calm and direct. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Here is the video from the surveillance camera.' Realizing that here is the video from the surveillance camera changes how Max handles the situation.
