## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Nastenka, hello, my beloved daughter.' Around the kitchen and point, the guest sets a calm, everyday tone with Nastya.
- 'Hello, I wanted to talk with you.' Nastya tells the guest that she wanted to talk with the guest without dressing it up.
- 'Speak, daughter, I am all ears.' Here speaking daughter he is all ears sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'I have never asked you for anything.' Having never asked the guest for anything is how Nastya lays out the situation for the guest. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'But right now things are a bit tight with money for us.' Nastya reminds the guest that right now things are a bit tight with money for them before the moment passes.
- 'Kostik even got a second job.' Because someone even got a second job, Nastya treats the matter as settled.
- 'He works without days off, my poor darling.' Explaining that he works without days off her poor darling lays the cards on the table for the guest.
- 'He tries so hard and is saving up for the wedding.' Nastya mentions that he tries so hard and is saving up for the wedding so the guest sees what is at stake.
- 'Could you possibly lend us some money?' Here asking whether the guest possibly lend them some money shows Nastya looking for the real truth. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Daughter, I would have given it to you anyway.' Pointing out that daughter he would have given it to Nastya anyway gives Nastya a sobering reality check.
- 'No, this is a loan, I will pay it back.' Nastya reminds the guest that this is a loan she will pay it back before the moment passes.
- 'Just do not tell Kostik, or he will get angry.' Realizing that just do not tell or he will get angry changes how Nastya handles the situation.
