## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The universe is wrapped in thin invisible threads.' Touching on the universe in front of Barinov, Max brings in "vselennaya" ("universe") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'They connect us with other people.' Max counters openly in their talk, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Every action of other people changes us.' Talking about the act before Barinov, Max chooses "postupok" ("act, deed") so Barinov gets the point right away.
- 'Think about those whom you will change.' Max jumps in dryly during the talk, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Maxim, come over to me for a minute.' Barinov follows up boldly toward Max, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Yes, Chef, I am listening to you.' Max proceeds plainly in front of Barinov and reaches for the Russian word "slushayu" ("I'm listening", built from "slushat") right at the center of the line.
- 'Forgive me for yesterday.' Barinov openly offers this personal apology to Max using "prosti" ("forgive me") — the close, informal way to say "forgive me" or "I'm sorry" to someone dear.
- 'Thank you for not filing a police report.' Barinov smoothly chooses this word of thanks in front of Max using "spasibo" ("thank you") — the everyday word for "thank you", which grew out of the old blessing "spasi Bog" ("God save you").
- 'Alright, Chef, it is all behind us now.' Max speaks openly beside Barinov and leans on "khorosho" ("good, fine") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'I got a sobriety implant because of you!' Pressing on evenly to Max, the "-sya" ending on "zashilsya" ("got an anti-alcohol implant") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'Easy, Chef, just take it easy, please!' Max counters pointedly during the talk, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'I will kill you, get over here!' Barinov counters quietly around Max and chooses "ubyu" ("I'll kill", from the root "ubit") so Max hears the exact meaning.
