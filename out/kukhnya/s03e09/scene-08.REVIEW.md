## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Maximka, you are simply my lucky talisman!' Returning to the talisman in front of Nastya, Barinov uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'You and I got really lucky.' Barinov opens up at once across the room, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Kostik, are we going to the rally today?' Bringing up the action beside Kostya, Nastya uses "aktsiyu" ("action, protest", from "aktsiya") to make the point stick.
- 'For animal rights, I told you about it.' Talking about the animal beside Kostya, Nastya brings in "zhivotnykh" ("animal", from "zhivotnoye") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Listen, Nastya, I cannot make it today.' Kostya weighs in lightly with a smile, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'I am heading to work right now.' Focusing on the job beside Nastya, Kostya chooses "rabotu" ("job", from the root "rabota") so Nastya hears the exact meaning.
- 'What do you mean to work? It is our day off!' Discussing her day in the exchange, Nastya reaches for "u nas" ("by us, at our place") to talk about what they share.
- 'I got a second job.' Kostya counters earnestly on the spot, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'And just where did you get a job?' Nastya fires back naturally to Kostya and uses "ustroilsya" ("to settle, to get a job", from "ustroitsya") to make the point stick.
- 'At a sports bar, money for wedding expenses will not hurt.' Emphasizing a sports bar beside Nastya, Kostya brings in "svadebnyye" ("wedding", from "svadebnyy") as the key Russian word here.
- 'And why are you going there in a suit?' Nastya presses quietly how Kostya expects that to work in practice.
- 'It is just my first day today.' Emphasizing how first things are in front of Nastya, Kostya lands on "pervyy" ("first") as the key everyday word here.
