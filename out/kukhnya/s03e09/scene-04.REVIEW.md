## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'No, I cannot work here.' Refusing to work here moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'Nastya will definitely be against this.' Kostya tells the guest that Nastya will definitely be against this without dressing it up.
- 'And we will not tell her anything.' Since they will not tell her anything, the guest does not waste words on excuses. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'They do not offer a job like this every day.' Mentioning that they do not offer a job like this every day moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Well, do you like it here?' Asking whether Kostya likes it here cuts straight through the tension between the guest and Kostya. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Yes, and is this real leather?' Checking whether this real leather is how Kostya puts the guest on the spot. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Of course, this is genuine pigskin leather.' Explaining that this is genuine pigskin leather lays the cards on the table for Kostya.
- 'And now press the little button.' Over the fear and the point, the guest answers Kostya without a second thought.
- 'Bring me some coffee, please.' By telling the guest to bring him some coffee please, Kostya keeps his side of the story grounded.
- 'So, are you going to work for me?' Asking whether Kostya going to work for him is how the guest puts Kostya on the spot.
- 'Let me try working here for now.' Here letting him try working here for now sets up the next turn in their exchange. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'I have a convenient schedule at the restaurant.' Kostya explains that he has a convenient schedule at the restaurant, keeping his voice calm and direct. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
