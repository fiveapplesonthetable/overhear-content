## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Elya, tell us, how is your health overall?' Wondering if someone tell them how is Eleonora's health overall shows Barinov caught off guard. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Just a regular flu, thank goodness, it already passed.' Around the room and plan, Eleonora sets a calm, everyday tone with Barinov.
- 'Wait, what do you mean just a regular flu?' By wondering what Eleonora means just a regular flu, Barinov presses Eleonora for a straight answer.
- 'In two months we are having a wedding banquet here.' Mentioning that in two months they are having a wedding banquet here moves the talk in a new direction. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Dimochka and I are officially getting married.' Eleonora reminds Barinov that someone and she are officially getting married before the moment passes.
- 'Is Dimochka really going to be our boss again?' Here checking whether really going to be their boss again shows Barinov looking for the real truth.
- 'The main thing is that our story has a happy ending.' Mentioning that the main thing is that their story has a happy ending moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Vika agreed to stay with me in Moscow.' Between the situation and the conversation, Max gives Barinov a quick, grounded response.
- 'And I finally became a real head chef!' Over the truth and the situation, Max lets his real feelings show.
- 'Vika and I will be working together again.' Explaining that Vika and he will be working together again lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
- 'It turns out the dearest dreams really do come true!' Because it turns out the dearest dreams really do come true, Max does not bother softening the blow for Barinov. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'When one story ends, another one always begins.' Realizing that when one story ends another one always begins changes how Max handles the situation.
