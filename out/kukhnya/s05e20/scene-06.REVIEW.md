## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Have you heard, our owner is dying.' Adding that have Lyova heard their owner is dying fills in the missing piece for Lyova. Even in the middle of the drama, she keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'If I hear one more time that you are spreading gossip...' Saying that if he hears one more time that Gulya is spreading gossip is how Mikhail sets the tone with Gulya.
- 'I will fire you that very same day!' Mikhail declares that he will fire Gulya that very same day as the tension in the room peaks. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Honestly, I am not spreading gossip.' Gulya explains that she is not spreading gossip, keeping her voice calm and direct.
- 'That is all, go work calmly and do not cry.' Saying that that is all go work calmly and do not cry is how Mikhail sets the tone with Gulya.
- 'Why are you gloomier than a storm cloud today?' Asking why Gulya gloomier than a storm cloud today is cuts straight through the tension between Lyova and Gulya. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'They say our Eleonora Andreyevna is dying soon.' Mentioning that they say their Eleonora Andreyevna is dying soon moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'And Mikhail Dzhekovich almost fired me for it.' With the conversation and shock in play, Gulya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Wait, what do you mean Eleonora is dying?' Here asking what Gulya means Eleonora is dying shows Mikhail looking for the real truth. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'She is very seriously ill, everyone already knows.' Explaining that she is very seriously ill everyone already knows lays the cards on the table for Gulya.
- 'Where on earth did you get all of this?' When Mikhail asks if where on earth did Gulya gets all of this, Gulya has to answer fast.
- 'Viktor Petrovich himself told us this morning.' Realizing that Viktor Petrovich himself told them this morning changes how Lyova handles the situation.
