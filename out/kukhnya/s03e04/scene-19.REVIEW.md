## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I swear, I don't know where your elephant is.' Max leans on one familiar word toward Vika: "klyanus" means "I swear" (from "klyastsya"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'I had an epiphany today.' Max states this answer toward Vika: Russian phrases this around "u menya" (literally "by me") rather than "I have", a pattern you will hear in almost every scene.
- 'I suddenly realized that you are everything I need.' Max holds firm calmly in their talk, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Let's start everything from a clean slate. Just you and me.' Max spotlights one direct expression for Vika: "chistogo" means "clean" (from "chistyy"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'And what next?' Vika gives the reply with warmth: putting the question word "chto" right at the front of the line lets Max hear the point of the question on the very first beat.
- 'Next I will get on my feet, we will get married in about two years.' Max pushes back frankly during the talk, so Vika knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Then we will have kids in five or six years.' Max favors this sharp piece at once: "zavedyom" means "will have, start" (from "zavesti"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'In five or six?' Vika aims that remark briskly now: Russian keeps the exact word order of a normal statement here, turning it into a question for Max purely through rising pitch at the end.
- 'We will live for ourselves, grow old together, and die on the same day.' Max answers calmly without a pause, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'And if you want, I can die first! Well, do you like my plan?' Max underscores one concrete piece with Vika: "nravitsya" means "is pleasing", and it carries the main weight of what Max is saying.
- 'It is a good plan, Max.' Vika shares this statement briskly now: notice how Russian needs no word for "am", "is", or "are" in the present — placing the words side by side says it all.
- 'Only that is your plan, not ours.' Vika reacts briskly hearing Max, testing Max's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
