## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I swear, I don't know where your elephant is.' Addressing the elephant on the spot, Max uses "klyanus" ("I swear", from "klyastsya") to make the point stick.
- 'I had an epiphany today.' Max answers pointedly beside Vika, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'I suddenly realized that you are everything I need.' Max continues calmly with Vika, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Let's start everything from a clean slate. Just you and me.' Addressing a clean slate on the spot, Max lands on "chistogo" ("clean", built on "chistyy") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'And what next?' Leaning in promptly with Max, Vika puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Max hears the focus immediately.
- 'Next I will get on my feet, we will get married in about two years.' Max continues frankly with a sigh, making his position completely clear.
- 'Then we will have kids in five or six years.' Addressing the children in this beat, Max puts the weight on "detey" ("children", from "rebyonok") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'In five or six?' Weighing in briskly right here, Vika keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Max purely through rising pitch.
- 'We will live for ourselves, grow old together, and die on the same day.' Max reacts calmly with a nod, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'And if you want, I can die first! Well, do you like my plan?' Emphasizing his plan right here, Max centers the remark on "pervyy" ("first") without any extra fuss.
- 'It is a good plan, Max.' Underscoring how good things are in the exchange, Vika links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Only that is your plan, not ours.' Vika opens up briskly toward Max, steering the talk right where she wants it.
