## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Here, friend, this is the cheapest bottle in the bar.' Confirming how cheap things are right here, Kostya centers the thought on "deshyovaya" ("cheap, inexpensive", from "deshyovyy") without wasting a word.
- 'Tell me, Denis, what happened to you?' Holding firm promptly with Denis, Kostya uses "u tebya" (literally "by you") where English says "you have" or "your".
- 'I have big problems with a certain girl.' Denis chimes in earnestly with a smile, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'What, did the two of you quarrel badly and break up today?' Kostya proceeds firmly in the exchange and reaches for the Russian word "possorilis" ("had a fight", built from "possoritsya") right at the center of the line.
- 'It turned out that we had not even started dating.' Reacting plainly before Kostya, the "-sya" ending on "vstrechatsya" ("to meet") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'All right, let me drink a sip with you!' Kostya proceeds plainly with a shrug, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Kostya, are you drinking right at work again?!' Pointing to the work at once, Nastya puts the weight on "rabote" ("work", from "rabota") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'Nastya, you are offending me, I did not drink!' Kostya speaks lightly with a nod, watching Nastya's face to see how the news lands.
- 'You are brazenly lying to my face again!' Nastya cuts in briskly in this moment, making her position completely clear.
- 'Then let our whole family be heavy drinkers!' Emphasizing the drinkers right here, Nastya centers the thought on "pyushchey" ("drinkers", from "pyushchya") without wasting a word.
- 'At the same time you will look at yourself from the outside, dear!' Bringing up the side around Kostya, Nastya lands on "posmotrish" ("you will see", built on "posmotret") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Denis, after all, she does not know how to drink at all!' Kostya steps in promptly hearing Denis, so Denis knows he is not just making small talk.
