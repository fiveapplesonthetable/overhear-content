## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Here, friend, this is the cheapest bottle in the bar.' Kostya sets a bottle on the counter for the heartbroken young cook.
- 'Tell me, Denis, what happened to you?' Russians say 'by me' or 'with us' where English says 'I have' or 'we have', without needing a separate word for 'have'.
- 'I have big problems with a certain girl.' Denis sighs without naming Katya.
- 'What, did the two of you quarrel badly and break up today?' Here Kostya anchors the thought on 'today' and finishes on 'to today', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'It turned out that we had not even started dating.' He admits that the whole courtship was a one-sided illusion.
- 'All right, let me drink a sip with you!' Feeling sorry for Denis, Kostya pours himself a small shot.
- 'Kostya, are you drinking right at work again?!' Nastya walks up behind the bar and catches him immediately.
- 'Nastya, you are offending me, I did not drink!' Kostya tries to deny it, but the glass gives him away.
- 'You are brazenly lying to my face again!' In everyday Russian, 'again' flows straight into 'eyes' without extra linking words.
- 'Then let our whole family be heavy drinkers!' Nastya grabs a glass to mirror Kostya's behavior.
- 'At the same time you will look at yourself from the outside, dear!' Notice how Russian puts 'at the same time, while I'm at it' and 'expensive' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'Denis, after all, she does not know how to drink at all!' Alarmed, Kostya rushes after Nastya to stop her.
