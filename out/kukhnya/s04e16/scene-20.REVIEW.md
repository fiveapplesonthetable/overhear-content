## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Ladies and gentlemen, welcome the lord of our evening!' In her devil costume, Vika announces Count Dracula to the bank executives.
- 'Today in the coffin I nearly died twice!' After the coffin falls flat on stage, a bruised Nagiyev storms into the back room.
- 'Vika, you completely ruined our important corporate party!' He blames Vika for the disastrous entrance.
- 'Forgive me, I do not know who locked the padlock.' Vika apologizes, still unaware that Denis clicked the lock shut.
- 'That is it, Vika, I am firing you from here immediately!' Furious, Nagiyev threatens to fire her on the spot.
- 'Dmitry Vladimirovich, then fire me today too!' German steps forward using a perfective plural imperative.
- 'You cannot dump all the blame on one person!' A Russian verbal idiom means to pin all the blame on someone.
- 'After all, I was the one who suggested the Halloween party to you!' German reminds Nagiyev that the vampire theme was his idea.
- 'All right, then everyone works for half their salary!' Rather than lose both his manager and his chef, Nagiyev fines the entire staff.
- 'German, thank you, you acted very nobly!' Deeply moved, Vika kisses German on the lips.
- 'The puppeteer and the puppet are always tied by a single thread.' The narrator reflects using a short passive participle and an instrumental feminine phrase.
- 'Sometimes the puppet can pull on the thread itself!' Meanwhile, Eva pulls the ultimate string by telling Chef Barinov that she is pregnant.
