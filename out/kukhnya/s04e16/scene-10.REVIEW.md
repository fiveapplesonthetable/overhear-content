- 'Come in quickly, Denis, what do you need right now?' In spoken Russian, the words stay in their normal statement order and Chef Barinov's rising voice at the end turns the line into a question.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, I have a very important conversation.' Denis stands straight and addresses Katya's father respectfully.
- 'Do you mind if Katya and I go out together?' He asks for Barinov's blessing using a polite question and a conditional clause.
- 'Of course I am categorically against such a relationship!' As promised to Katya, Barinov initially rejects him flatly.
- 'Go work, I have no time for you at all right now.' Here Chef Barinov anchors the thought on 'go, walk' and finishes on 'at all', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'But why are you against me, Viktor Petrovich?' Denis refuses to leave and asks for a reason.
- 'Because my fatherly trust must be earned!' Spotting the burned matchstick house, Barinov suddenly changes tactics.
- 'Good, tell me, I am ready for any test!' Notice how Russian puts 'good, well, all right' and 'test' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'How exactly can I prove my love?' Here Denis anchors the thought on 'how / as' and finishes on 'love', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'Can you quickly assemble a mansion like this out of matches?' Barinov hands him the photo of Lyova's ancestral estate and boxes of matches.
- 'Easily! I will do everything in the best possible way!' Denis accepts the challenge without suspecting the trick.
- 'Excellent, then start gluing right this evening!' Barinov grins as his matchstick problem solves itself.
