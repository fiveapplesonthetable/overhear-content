## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Viktor Petrovich, look, your palace is completely ready!' Denis proudly sets the rebuilt matchstick estate on the desk.
- 'You have already been running errands for two weeks.' A colloquial Russian idiom means to run errands at someone's beck and call.
- 'I will rest when you allow me to go out with Katya!' Denis reminds Barinov of his promise using two perfective future verbs.
- 'Dad, do not stay silent, give him an impossible task!' Katya pulls her father aside and urges him to invent an impossible test.
- 'What other task? My imagination has run out!' Barinov whispers back that he has no more ideas.
- 'After all, you were just reading Gogol's fairy tale!' Katya reminds him of the story about Vakula the blacksmith.
- 'Right! Did Nagiyev put on his expensive moccasins for nothing?' Barinov remembers Nagiyev bragging about his Italian loafers.
- 'Listen to me closely, Denis, there is one last test.' He turns back to Denis with a solemn expression.
- 'Bring me Dmitry Nagiyev's famous slippers today!' He uses Gogol's folkloric plural noun for fancy royal slippers.
- 'Excuse me, what slippers do you want to get?' Denis blinks in confusion at the old-fashioned word.
- 'Bring me Nagiyev's red moccasins, then we will talk!' Barinov clarifies the demand in plain modern Russian.
- 'Are you speaking completely seriously right now, Viktor Petrovich?' Stunned but determined, Denis prepares to hunt down the owner's shoes.
