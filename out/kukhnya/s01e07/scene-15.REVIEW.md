## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, please taste this hot dish.' "Poprobuyte" is the polite command of poprobovat', the right form for a boss. "Goryacheye" ends in -eye to match blyudo, a neuter word.
- 'Give the plate here, I will taste it now.' "Davay" here means 'give', a casual 'hand it over'. "Syuda" is 'to here', with movement, since the plate has to travel.
- 'Very tasty, we can serve it to the guests.' "Mozhno", 'it's allowed', covers 'we can' and 'one may'. "Gostyam", 'to the guests', is the 'to' plural of gost'.
- 'Are you really telling me the pure truth?' "Neuzheli", 'really?', shows surprise with some doubt. "Chistuyu pravdu" is the honest truth the cooks promised to tell.
- 'Yes, the dish turned out truly magnificent.' "Poluchilos'" has -o because blyudo is neuter. As with the sauce, the word that follows ends in -ym: velikolepnym.
- 'Chef, you are smiling so joyfully now!' "Ulybaetes'" is the vy form Max owes his boss, from ulybat'sya, with -sya shortened to -s'. "Radostno", 'joyfully', grows out of radost', joy.
- 'Why, can a cook no longer smile?' A chto at the start is a challenging 'so what?'. "Ulybnut'sya" is the one-time partner of ulybat'sya, so it means 'give a smile'.
- 'Do not smile like that, you look like a clown.' "Pokhozh na", 'similar to', gives you 'look like'. After na a clown, being alive, takes -a: klouna.
- 'Be quiet right now, insolent rump!' "Zamolchi" adds za-, 'start', to molchat', to be silent. "Seychas zhe", 'right now', adds zhe for insistence.
- 'From this moment on, all truth is canceled!' "Otmenyaetsya", 'is canceled', uses -sya to make the verb passive. S etogo momenta, 'from this moment', works for any announcement.
- 'I understood you, we return to work.' Ya vas ponyal, 'I understood you', is a common 'got it' to a boss. K rabote, 'to work', changes rabota to rabote.
- 'And one more important word, Maxim.' Barinov switches to Max's full name to sound serious. "Eshchyo odno", 'one more', is literally 'still one'.
- 'Thank you for the excellent work in the kitchen.' "Spasibo za", 'thanks for', puts the thing into its object form: otlichnuyu rabotu. "Otlichno" is also the top mark at school.
- 'Thank you very much, chef!' "Spasibo vam bol'shoye" means 'big thanks to you', and the vam makes it warmer. "Bol'shoye" agrees with spasibo, which is neuter.
