## Part 1 — Intro

> In the dining room between shifts, Max asks waitress Nastya if she knows of any cheap rooms for rent in Moscow. To his horror, she reveals that Kostya just invited her to move into their shared flat. Desperate to protect his living space, Max cleverly persuades Nastya that living with Kostya would be a noisy nightmare. Let's listen to their conversation in Russian first.
