## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, how are things going with our edible menu?' Here wondering how things going with their edible menu are shows Vika looking for the real truth. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Do not worry, I was just about to make it.' Realizing that do not worry he was just about to make it changes how Max handles the situation.
- 'Tell me, have I ever deceived you before?' Max asks whether tell him have he ever deceived Timur, refusing to let the point slide.
- 'Max, hi, here is everything exactly as you asked!' Insisting that here is everything exactly as Max asked shows Timur is not backing down. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Here is rice paper, edible paints, and a food printer.' Timur mentions that here is rice paper edible paints and a food printer so Max sees what is at stake.
- 'Thank you, Timur, you are a true friend, you helped me out a lot!' Declaring that Timur is a true friend Timur helped him out a lot brings Max's temper right to the surface.
- 'Listen, this really is a very cool thing!' Timur insists that this really is a very cool thing, leaving Max no room to argue. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'I should make myself an important document out of such paper.' Timur explains that he should make himself an important document out of such paper, keeping his voice calm and direct. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'And if there is a sudden inspection, eat it right on the spot!' Timur declares that if there is a sudden inspection eat it right on the spot as the tension in the room peaks.
- 'Great idea, come on, carry the printer to the kitchen quickly.' Over the shift and the menu, Max answers Timur without a second thought. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Make sure that by evening everything is nicely printed.' Here telling Max to make sure that by evening everything is nicely printed sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'It will be done in the best possible way, Victoria Sergeyevna.' Admitting that it will be done in the best possible way Victoria Sergeyevna shows Max being honest for a change.
