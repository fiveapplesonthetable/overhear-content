## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Alright, I will go, I need to pack for my trip already.' Mentioning that Alright he will go he needs to pack for his trip already moves the talk in a new direction. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'My plane for the north takes off very soon.' The guest reminds Barinov that his plane for the north takes off very soon before the moment passes. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Do not be sad, old friend, everything will be fine!' Once do not be sad old friend everything will be fine, Barinov makes sure Elena hears every word.
- 'She probably still loves me a little bit after all.' Explaining that she probably still loves him a little bit after all lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
- 'Forgive me, old friend, and take good care of her.' Around the moment and office, the guest sets a calm, everyday tone with Barinov.
- 'Everyone knows that marriages are made in heaven.' Claiming that everyone knows that marriages are made in heaven sounds confident, even if Elena has doubts.
- 'Even if it is already your fourth happy marriage!' Barinov declares that even if it is already Elena's fourth happy marriage as the tension in the room peaks. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Well then, Vitya, did you manage to submit the documents?' Asking whether Barinov manages to submit the documents is how Elena puts Barinov on the spot. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Yes, today I finally submitted our application to the registry office!' Between the wedding and the work, Barinov delivers a sharp reaction.
- 'True, our turn will come only in three months.' Noting that their turn will come only in three months keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'That is alright, three months will fly by very quickly.' Even though that is alright three months will fly by very quickly, Elena keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'The main thing is to move together in the same direction!' Barinov insists that the main thing is to move together in the same direction, leaving Elena no room to argue.
