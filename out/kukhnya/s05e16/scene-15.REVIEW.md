## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dasha, I urgently need to go back right this second!' Max follows up sharply in their talk, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Why? What suddenly happened with you in Moscow?' Eleonora chimes in smoothly in front of Max and lands on "sluchilos" ("happened", built on "sluchitsya") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'My ex-wife is giving birth to my baby right now!' Making clear how former things are on the spot, Max uses "byvshaya" ("former", from "byvshiy") to make the point stick.
- 'Alright, once we land in Sochi you will fly back on the very first flight.' Eleonora cuts in dryly watching Max, testing Max's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'No, tell me, can we turn the plane around right now?' Emphasizing the plane in the exchange, Max brings in "samolyot" ("plane") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Max, what are you talking about, that costs a lot of money!' Eleonora chimes in steadily without a pause, steering the talk right where she wants it.
- 'Dasha, is there a parachute anywhere on this plane?' Max questions quietly what Eleonora is actually getting at here.
- 'Max, have you lost your mind? Call them one more time!' Talking about the mind with Max, Eleonora leans on "yeshchyo" ("more, still") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'We just took off, I will jump and catch a car!' Discussing a car around Eleonora, Max chooses "mashinu" ("car", from the root "mashina") so Eleonora hears the exact meaning.
- 'Gulya, well then, has Eleonora Andreyevna arrived in the hall yet?' Mikhail challenges evenly before Eleonora can change the subject.
- 'She asked to have dinner brought to her room for two people.' Emphasizing the persons in the exchange, Eleonora centers the thought on "persony" ("persons", from "persona") without wasting a word.
- 'That means one person is waiting in the room for the second person!' Bringing up the second person with Eleonora, Mikhail uses "persona" ("person") to carry the main weight of the thought.
