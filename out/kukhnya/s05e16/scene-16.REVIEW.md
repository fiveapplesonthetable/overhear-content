## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Guys, we have a disaster, our Max just crashed!' Addressing the boys during the talk, Kostya chooses "rebyata" ("the boys, the lads") so Max gets the point right away.
- 'What do you mean crashed? He was flying on a plane!' Talking about the airplane with Kostya, Senya puts the question word "kak" right at the start so Kostya hears the focus immediately.
- 'He got a message that Vika is giving birth right now.' Mentioning the news watching Senya, Kostya cuts straight through the tension with Senya.
- 'And he immediately jumped down with a parachute!' Focusing on a parachute to Senya, Kostya puts the weight on "parashyutom" ("parachute", from "parashyut") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'He got badly tangled in the lines and landed poorly.' Addressing the rope before Senya, Kostya leans on "veryovkakh" ("rope, cord", a form of "veryovka") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Doctors just called me from his phone.' Kostya continues at once around Senya, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'The doctors said that his condition is very serious.' Bringing up the state with Senya, Kostya reaches for the Russian word "sostoyaniye" ("state") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Fedya, what have you and I done today?' Following up firmly beside Kostya, Senya puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Kostya hears the focus immediately.
- 'We just wanted to play a harmless joke on him!' Senya pushes back earnestly beside Kostya, watching Kostya's face to see how the news lands.
- 'Hello, Max, you should have seen their pale faces just now!' Focusing on the face at once, Kostya centers the thought on "blednyye" ("pale, pallid", from "blednyy") without wasting a word.
- 'Listen, do you think we did not joke too harshly?' Jumping in briskly beside Max, Kostya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Max purely through rising pitch.
- 'It is fine, because of them I almost jumped with a parachute myself!' Max continues frankly toward Kostya, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
