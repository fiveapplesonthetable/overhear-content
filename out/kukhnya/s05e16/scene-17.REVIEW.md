## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Have you lost your mind? He is still alive!' By asking whether Senya lost Senya's mind, Barinov presses Senya for a straight answer. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Guys, did I leave my car keys here with you?' Checking whether she leaves her car keys here with Barinov is how Vika puts Barinov on the spot. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Vika, sit down, the main thing is do not worry right now.' Adding that Senya should sit down the main thing is do not worry right now fills in the missing piece for Senya.
- 'Vitya, you are scaring me, what happened here?' Vika asks whether Barinov is scaring her what happened here, refusing to let the point slide. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Here is the thing with our Maxim Lavrov...' Mentioning that here is the thing with their Maxim Lavrov moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Today he jumped from a plane with a parachute a bit unsuccessfully.' Over the room and the car, Barinov answers Senya without a second thought.
- 'But Victoria Sergeyevna, do not worry, he is still alive!' Once do not worry he is still alive, Senya makes sure Barinov hears every word. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Victoria Sergeyevna, please forgive Fedya and me!' Here forgiving Fedya and him lets Senya's real feelings break through. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'We were the ones who played such a foolish prank on him today.' Senya mentions that they were the ones who played such a foolish prank on him today so Barinov sees what is at stake.
- 'We sent him a message from your forgotten phone.' Claiming that they sent him a message from Barinov's forgotten phone sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts.
- 'We wrote to him on the plane that you were already giving birth!' Senya insists that they wrote to him on the plane that Barinov was already giving birth, leaving Barinov no room to argue. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Oh, it seems I suddenly feel really unwell right now!' Saying that it seems she suddenly feel really unwell right now is how Vika takes charge of the moment.
