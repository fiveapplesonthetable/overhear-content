- 'Ladies, I want to tell you that you have disappointed me.' Max features one clear touch at once: "razocharovali" means "disappointed" (from "razocharovat"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Vika, I told you that you can trust me.' Max highlights one sharp touch at once: "doveryat" means "to trust", and it carries the main weight of what Max is saying.
- 'Eva, and would you have stayed with me for the sake of a test?' Max prompts promptly what Vika is actually getting at here.
- 'What test? Max, what on earth are you talking about?' Vika sharpens one familiar element with Eva: "proverki" means "check, inspection" (from "proverka"), giving the line its exact edge.

- 'You talked, and Eva immediately started flirting with me.' Max spotlights one telling term on cue: "pogovorili" means "had a talk" (from "pogovorit"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'But I am not a fool at all, I figured it all out!' Max makes that reply to Eva: adding "sovsem" ("completely, at all") pushes the statement all the way, leaving zero room for doubt.
- 'No, you are not a fool, you are an idiot! I did not ask Eva!' Vika counters firmly across the room, so Max knows she is not just making small talk.
