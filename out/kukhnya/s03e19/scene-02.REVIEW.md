## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'And how many Moscow homeless people eat at my expense?' Talking about the homeless people beside Vika, Max puts the question word "skolko" right at the start so Vika hears the focus immediately.
- 'Only this one, but please do not worry.' Vika opens up firmly on the spot, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Rodion Sergeyevich is a very cleanly and decent man.' Insisting on how cleanly things are beside Max, Vika reaches for the Russian word "chistoplotnyy" ("cleanly, neat") right at the heart of the line.
- 'You will talk about that with the police tomorrow.' Max continues lightly with a nod, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'I would not like to end up at the police station at all.' Max answers quietly watching Vika, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'They both feed you poorly there and the people are unfriendly.' Explaining how unfriendly things are in this beat, Max centers the thought on "neprivetlivyye" ("unfriendly", from "neprivetlivyy") without wasting a word.
- 'Sasha, wait, what do you mean the baby is not Ilya's?' Mentioning the child around Sasha, Max puts the question word "kak" right at the start so Sasha hears the focus immediately.
- 'I mean, and what are you going to do now?' Max checks quietly what Sasha is actually getting at here.
- 'I am calmly planning to have this baby.' Addressing the child to Max, Sasha leans on "rebyonka" ("child", a form of "rebyonok") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Yes, hi, Vika! No, I am not at work yet.' Turning to the work on the spot, Max links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'I think that I will be at our restaurant soon.' Max speaks honestly facing Sasha, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'Why did you just tell her a lie on the phone?' Addressing the untruth before Max, Sasha brings in "nepravdu" ("untruth, lie", from "nepravda") as the key Russian word here.
