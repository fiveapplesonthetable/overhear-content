- 'It is just that I have let you down so many times before.' Once it is just that he has let Eva down so many times, Max can finally tell Eva what he means.
- 'I understand very well myself how hard it is to believe me.' Noting that he understands very well himself how hard it is to believe him keeps Max's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'But we must do this, otherwise we will have no future.' Max tells Eva that they must do this otherwise they will have no future without dressing it up. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'I beg you, Vika, believe me, please!' Once he begs Eva Vika believe him please, Max makes sure Eva hears every word.
- 'Excuse me, I should probably quietly go home now.' Mentioning that she should probably quietly go home now moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'And what about all this lovely evening romance?' Over the shift and the trust, Eva presses Max for a straight answer. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'That is alright, I will clear everything from the table myself later.' Because that is alright he will clear everything from the table himself later, Max treats the matter as settled.
