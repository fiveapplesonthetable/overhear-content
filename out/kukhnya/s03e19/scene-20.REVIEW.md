- 'Why did you drive my own girl to tears?' Wondering why Max drives his own girl to tears cuts straight through the tension between Barinov and Max.
- 'You played with her while Vika was away, and dumped her?' When Barinov asks if Max played with her while Vika was away and dumped, Max has to answer fast. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
