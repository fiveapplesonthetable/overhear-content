- 'Hello, Sasha! May I congratulate you already?' When Vika asks if may she congratulates Max already, Max has to answer fast. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Yes, I am getting married soon, I am having a baby.' Explaining that she is getting married soon she is having a baby lays the cards on the table for Max. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.

- 'What do you have to do with it? Learn to count, daddy!' Wondering what Max has to do with it cuts straight through the tension between Sasha and Max. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.

- 'Vika, I lied because I got scared because of Sasha.' Max tells Sasha that he lied because he got scared because of Sasha without dressing it up.
- 'I thought everything had become fine between us lately.' Since he thought everything had become fine between them lately, Max does not waste words on excuses. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
