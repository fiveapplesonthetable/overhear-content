## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Viktor Petrovich, can you imagine, this is my wife!' Highlighting his wife right here, Rodion brings in "predstavit" ("to imagine") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'I have not remembered her yet, but that makes it even nicer.' Rodion reacts pointedly around Katya and uses "priyatneye" ("more pleasant", from "priyatnyy") to make the point stick.
- 'Thank you, Viktor Petrovich, for not letting my husband starve.' Katya fires back lightly watching Barinov, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'I want to forget everything except your onion soup.' Mentioning the soup beside Katya, Rodion reaches for the Russian word "lukovogo" ("onion", built from "lukovyy") right at the center of the line.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, we will all miss you very much.' Katya chimes in plainly without any doubt, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Believe me, you have nothing at all to worry about.' Rodion cuts in right away without holding back, before Katya can change the subject.
- 'Pass on my warmest regards to our Dmitry Vladimirovich.' Rodion reacts sharply before Katya and leans on "plamennyy" ("fiery, warm") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Tell him I sincerely appreciate his part in the recent conflict.' Mentioning the participation in front of Katya, Rodion chooses "uchastiye" ("participation") so Katya gets the point right away.
- 'Ekaterina Viktorovna, I am leaving these glorious places.' Rodion cuts in firmly facing Barinov, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'And me too! Dad, here is my letter, I am quitting.' Talking about the statement to Rodion, Katya uses "zayavleniye" ("statement") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Katya, is this because of Max? Why are you leaving?' Replying briskly on the spot, Barinov puts the question word "pochemu" right at the start so Katya hears the focus immediately.
- 'It does not matter, I no longer want to and will not work here!' Katya replies dryly without holding back, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
