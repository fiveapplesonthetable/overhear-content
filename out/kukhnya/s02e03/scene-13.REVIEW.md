## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, did you already bring that belt?' Fedya reminds Senya of the bet he lost.
- 'Just do not eat it all at once by yourself!' Fedya jokes as if the belt were a delicacy.
- 'Leave a small piece for me too.' The diminutive noun means 'a small piece'.
- 'You are joking very amusingly today.' Senya replies with heavy sarcasm.
- 'I will slice it finely with a knife first.' Senya plans to cut the leather into tiny strips.
- 'And then I will calmly eat it all.' The perfective future verb means 'I will eat it all up'.
- 'Yes, you thought of everything rightly.' Fedya pretends to praise Senya's strategy.
- 'Just tenderize it well first!' The imperative refers to pounding meat with a kitchen mallet.
- 'Otherwise it will be very tough.' Fedya warns that the leather will be hard to chew.
- 'Just like your steak from yesterday!' Fedya takes a jab at Senya's cooking.
- 'My meat dishes are always delicious!' As the meat chef, Senya proudly defends his steaks.
- 'Leave me alone, did you understand?' Senya is tired of Fedya's jokes.
- 'Got it, I wish you a good appetite!' Fedya leaves him with a parting joke.
