## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Do not worry so much.' Max tries to put Sasha at ease after work.
- 'I will quickly catch a car right now.' Max offers to hail a taxi on the street.
- 'I will drive you home myself.' Sasha insists on giving him a ride in her car.
- 'Sasha, is everything alright with you?' Max notices the tension in her voice.
- 'Yes, everything is fine with me.' Sasha brushes off his concern.
- 'Why are you so sad all evening?' Max wonders why she has been quiet all shift.
- 'Well here you go starting again!' Sasha is frustrated that he keeps bringing up their relationship.
- 'After all, we already talked this morning.' They agreed earlier not to complicate things between them.
- 'I just wanted to ask you.' Max says he was only checking on her.
- 'You and I are just good friends.' Sasha reminds him of the boundary they set.
- 'Fine, I understood you very well.' Max accepts her answer.
- 'Then get into the car quickly.' Sasha motions toward the car door.
- 'Thank you for driving me home.' Max appreciates the ride home.
