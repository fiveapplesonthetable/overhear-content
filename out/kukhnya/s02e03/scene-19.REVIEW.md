## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You saved me. That was quick thinking.' Max uses the perfective verb meaning 'to help out' to focus on the single completed result, pairing it with 'quickly' and 'understand'.
- 'By the way, did that girl leave a message for me?' There is no question word here — in Russian, rising intonation on 'pass on' is enough to make it a question. Sasha answers straight away.
- 'No idea. I did not even see her leave.' The negative particle sits directly before the verb for 'have' to negate the action.
- 'Excuse me, would you like the check?' Vika leads with the direct command form meaning 'excuse me', pairing it with 'can' and 'be'.
- 'I would like to thank the chef personally.' Max chooses the imperfective verb meaning 'want' for the ongoing action, followed by 'personally' and 'to thank'.
