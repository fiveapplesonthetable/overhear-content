## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You saved me. That was quick thinking.' Max uses the action verb meaning 'to help out' to focus on the single completed result, pairing it with 'quickly' and 'understand'.
- 'By the way, did that girl leave a message for me?' There is no question word here — in Russian, rising intonation on 'pass on' is enough to make it a question. Sasha answers straight away.
- 'No idea. I did not even see her leave.' Notice how naturally this everyday Russian phrase expresses the speaker's point in the conversation.
- 'Excuse me, would you like the check?' Vika leads with the direct command form meaning 'excuse me', pairing it with 'can' and 'be'.
- 'I would like to thank the chef personally.' Max chooses the action verb meaning 'want' for the ongoing action, followed by 'personally' and 'to thank'. Now let's listen to the whole conversation again from start to finish.
