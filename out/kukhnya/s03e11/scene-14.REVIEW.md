## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good evening! How do you like our new cuisine?' Kostya phrases the comeback in earnest: putting the question word "kak" right at the front of the line lets Katya hear the point of the question on the very first beat.
- 'May we invite the cook Katya here right now?' Kostya presses point-blank where this conversation is heading next.
- 'Good evening! Did you not like something today?' Katya colors this sharp expression at once: "ponravilos" means "pleased, was liked" (from "ponravitsya"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'No, not at all, everything is simply great!' Kostya lays out this statement before Max: the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") works like a shrug in the sentence, making a big claim sound effortless.
- 'How does your dish hold its shape like that?' Kostya questions skeptically before Katya can change the subject.
- 'What is the secret? I can show you everything!' Katya colors this clear touch with Max: "sekret" means "secret", and it carries the main weight of what Katya is saying.
- 'He said that there is old furniture here.' Katya replies bluntly with a nod, before Kostya can change the subject.
- 'The guest will now go with me to the kitchen.' Katya continues evenly as things stand, without trying to dress up how she really feels.
- 'Nagiyev told Vika that the furniture needs to be changed.' Kostya reaches for one direct expression toward Katya: "govoril" means "said, were saying" (from "govorit"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Kostya, I am sure that Katya is lying.' Max responds earnestly in this moment, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'You should have seen her face this afternoon!' Max continues warmly facing Katya, testing Katya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'She thinks that only she knows how to bluff.' Max picks one telling term toward Kostya: "dumayet" means "thinks" (from "dumat"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Never mind, we have already been through this before!' Max chimes in promptly facing Katya, making his position completely clear.
