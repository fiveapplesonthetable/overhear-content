## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Tell me honestly, is this true about Max?' Mentioning the honestly to Max, Vika brings in "pravda" ("honestly") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'No, I simply decided to teach him a lesson.' Cutting in frankly before Max, Vika uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'That is very right, it serves him right!' Vika fires back frankly with a nod, so Max knows she is not just making small talk.
- 'Kostya, Katya set me up badly today.' Max follows up naturally around Kostya and brings in "segodnya" ("today") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Maybe you simply do not remember anything?' Pushing back frankly around Max, Kostya uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'After all, that sometimes happens to you.' Kostya proceeds wryly with a smile, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'I know for sure that I did not sleep with her.' Max proceeds warmly in the exchange and brings in "tochno" ("definitely") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'And it seems to me she is lying to all of us.' Answering firmly to Kostya, the "-sya" ending on "kazhetsya" ("it seems, I think") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'Then she must have exact proof.' Kostya chimes in boldly facing Max, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'We just need to do one quick test.' Talking about the test in the exchange, Kostya centers the remark on "bystryy" ("fast") without any extra fuss.
- 'Boys, come inside to us for lunch quickly!' Underscoring the boys with Kostya, Vika reaches for the Russian word "malchiki" ("boys", built from "malchik") right at the center of the line.
- 'Thank you, we do not want to eat on the street today.' Max speaks up candidly around Vika, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Right, it is always better to eat calmly at home.' Kostya proceeds calmly during the talk and puts the emphasis on "pravilno" ("right") in the middle of the talk.
