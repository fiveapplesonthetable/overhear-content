## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Add more salt and stir quickly!' By telling Katya to add more salt and stir quickly, Barinov raises the stakes in the room at once. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Your fish is still completely raw!' Barinov declares that Katya's fish is still completely raw as the tension in the room peaks.
- 'Chef, I know how to prove that Katya is lying.' Admitting that he knows how to prove that Katya is lying shows Max being honest for a change.
- 'Get away from me, I feel sick enough already.' Once Katya should get away from him he feels sick enough already, Barinov can finally tell Katya what he means.
- 'Let her take this test right now.' Telling Barinov to let her take this test right now moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'You will see, she is lying to all of you.' Admitting that Barinov will see she is lying to all of Barinov shows Max being honest for a change. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'What are you saying? And what if Katya is not lying?' Asking what Katya saying is cuts straight through the tension between Barinov and Katya. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Then I am taking your entire paycheck!' By being taking Katya's entire paycheck, Barinov raises the stakes in the room at once.
- 'Take even two of my paychecks, Chef!' Telling Barinov to take even two of his paychecks shows Max is done holding back. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'No, let us make it three paychecks right away! Deal.' Proposing that they make it three paychecks right away shows Barinov is done holding back. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'But our restroom is not working today.' Since their restroom is not working today, Katya does not waste words on excuses.
- 'The repairmen will come soon and fix everything.' Mentioning that the repairmen will come soon and fix everything moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Katya, go to the guest restroom then!' Here going to the guest restroom then lets Max's real feelings break through. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
