- 'I am not a professional chef like you.' Realizing that she is not a professional like Max changes how Vika handles the situation. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'So in your opinion, am I a bad wife?' Over the table and the exchange, Vika presses Max for a straight answer.
- 'I am not the one who said that.' Claiming that he is not the one who said sounds confident, even if Nastya has doubts. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Vika, come on, I was only joking!' Once Nastya should come on he was only joking, Max makes sure Nastya hears every word. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Hey, easy, take it easy!' With the doctor and argument at stake, Max reacts without holding back.
- 'Wait, did you take your medical check-up or not?' Asking whether Nastya takes Nastya's medical check up or not is how Max puts Nastya on the spot. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Back this morning. What does that change?' Over the doctor and the exchange, Vika presses Max for a straight answer. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Oh, Mom, it is such a pity you are leaving.' Realizing that Mom it is such a pity Max is leaving changes how Nastya handles the situation.

- 'No, turning back is bad luck.' Even though turning back is bad luck, Nastya keeps a steady face in front of Max.
