## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vitya, I express to you my extreme outrage!' "Vyrazhayu", "I express", is official language, and she uses it to scold. "Vozmushchenie" is "outrage". She calls him Vitya, the short form of Viktor.
- 'I am deeply disappointed by the bad service in the restaurant.' "Razocharovana", "disappointed", has the woman's -a. "Servis" is borrowed, and "plokhim servisom" has the "by means of" ending. She keeps up the formal act.
- 'Mom, do not shout here, we are busy with work.' He says "mama", the everyday word for mother, which grown men use too. "Zanyaty", "busy", is the short plural form. "Ne krichi" is "don't shout".
- 'Be home at twelve o'clock sharp!' After "chtoby", "so that", the verb takes its past form, "byl", though the order is for the future. "Rovno" is "exactly", "sharp". "Doma" is "at home", while "domoy" is "homeward".
- 'Chef, turn on the TV quickly, the food critic is on!' "Vklyuchay", "turn on", is the opposite of "vyklyuchili", Lyova's "turned off" for the noisy equipment. Nagiyev says "shef" but uses "ty", as an old friend.
- 'Has the critic already released his devastating review?' "Vypustil", "released", is related to "vypusk", the TV "episode" Barinov was offered money for. "Razgromny", "devastating", grows from "razgrom", a rout. Barinov fears the worst.
- 'He says that our main virtue is integrity.' "Dostoinstvo", "virtue", is also the word for "dignity". "Printsipialnost", "integrity", is built on "printsip", as in Barinov's "glupykh printsipov".
- 'Really? And what did he say about the kitchen?' "Pravda?", "truth?", is how Russians ask "really?". "Pro kukhnyu" is "about the kitchen". Barinov wants to hear about his own domain.
- 'He called all our food simply delicious!' After "nazval", "called", the praise takes the "as" ending: "vostkhititelnoy". The word means "delightful", a grand word, not one only for food. Nagiyev reads the praise with relish.
- 'And he called bartender Kostya a real professional.' The "as" ending again, in "nastoyashchim professionalom". The critic noticed Kostya, the man who was about to be fired.
- 'The critic gave the restaurant nine points out of ten!' Russians "put" a mark: "postavil devyat ballov". "Iz desyati", "out of ten", uses "iz", "from". Nagiyev announces the score like a sports result.
- 'Nine out of ten? That is an incredible success!' "Neveroyatny", "incredible", is built on "verit", "to believe": "unbelievable". Barinov repeats the score, delighted.
- 'Vitya, urgently bring Kostya back to work!' "Verni", "give back", covers returning things and people. "Obratno" is "back", and "srochno" is "urgently". Nagiyev gives the order that ends the episode.
