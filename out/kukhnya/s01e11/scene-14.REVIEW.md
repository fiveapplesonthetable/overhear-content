## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vika, do not panic, I am already carrying the hot dish.' Russian borrowed "panic" and gave it a verb ending, so "don't panic" is nearly English. Max is proud and a little smug, and the plate is still hot.
- 'Are you sure you are carrying it to the right guest?' Vika uses "ty" with Max, and "sure" ends bare because he is a man. A woman would say "uverena". The question is a rising voice, nothing more.
- 'I am delivering this masterpiece to your chief critic.' "Masterpiece" is the French word you've met, and "to your chief critic" has an ending on every word to show "to". Max is showing off. He loves the sound of the phrase.
- 'Well done, only I am no longer sure about her.' Russians say "molodets", literally a fine young fellow, to praise anyone, men and women alike. "About her" gets an "n" in front of "her", as it does after small words.
- 'What changed during these five short minutes?' "Within five minutes" is shown by the small word "za". "Changed" has a neuter ending because "what" is neuter in Russian. Max asks, perplexed, as the critic has moved.
- 'It seems the real critic is sitting by the other window.' "It seems" is a single word. "By the other window" uses the "at" word. Vika has a new theory, and it's the third one this hour.
- 'And that is already your personal problem!' The "personal" here grows from the "personally" the chef used. Max shrugs, which is a very Max thing to do. The new theory is Vika's to deal with.
- 'Wait a second, bring the expensive plate back.' "Give back" stands for returning. The plate is called "dear", which is also "expensive". Vika changes her mind in the middle of the corridor, and Max is nearly out of patience.
- 'The guest is already waiting and looking right here.' "Here" with the sense of "to here" is a word you know from the cooks' scene. Max sees the lady staring at him. He is stuck between two bosses.
- 'Tell her that this is a special gift from the Chef.' "Of the chef" is shown by an ending, not a word. "Gift" is a warm word. Vika invents a cover story on the spot. Restaurants do this all the time.
- 'I will smile and say the most polite words.' This "smile" is the once-only kind, a single smile, unlike the habitual "we smile". "Most polite" puts the "most" word before "polite". Max is ready to charm.
- 'Go quickly, do not make an important person wait.' Russian says "important person" as the thing being made to wait, so the word for "person" takes a special ending, the same as for "of a person". Vika is brisk. The critic is waiting.
