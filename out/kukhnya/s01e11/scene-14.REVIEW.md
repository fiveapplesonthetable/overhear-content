## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vika, do not panic, I am already carrying the hot dish.' "Ne panikuy" is "panika", "panic", turned into a verb. "Nesu" is "I am carrying", from "nesti". "Goryachee blyudo" is "hot dish".
- 'Are you sure you are carrying it to the right guest?' "Uveren", "sure", ends bare because Max is a man, and a woman would say "uverena". "Pravilnomu gostyu" is "to the right guest".
- 'I am delivering this masterpiece to your chief critic.' "Shedevr" is French "chef-d'oeuvre", the word Max used about artists signing their work. "Tvoemu glavnomu kritiku" ends in -u on every word to show "to".
- 'Well done, only I am no longer sure about her.' "Molodets" is literally "fine young fellow", praise for anyone, men or women. "Uverena v ney" is "sure in her".
- 'What changed during these five short minutes?' "Za pyat minut" is "within five minutes". "Izmenilos", "changed", ends in -lo because "chto", "what", is neuter.
- 'It seems the real critic is sitting by the other window.' "Kazhetsya", "it seems", is one word. "U drugogo okna" is "by the other window", with "u", "at". Vika has a new theory.
- 'And that is already your personal problem!' "Lichnye", "personal", is the family of "lichno", "personally", from the chef's dessert line. "Tvoi problemy" is "your problems".
- 'Wait a second, bring the expensive plate back.' "Verni", "give back", is the command of "vernut". "Dorogaya" means both "dear" and "expensive". "Podozhdi" is "wait!".
- 'The guest is already waiting and looking right here.' "Syuda" is "to here", the word in Kostya's "syuda poka nikto ne zvonil". "Gostya" is the woman guest again.
- 'Tell her that this is a special gift from the Chef.' "Podarok shefa" is "gift of the chef": the -a on "shefa" does the "of". "Osoby" is "special".
- 'I will smile and say the most polite words.' "Ulybnus" is a single smile, unlike the habit in "ulybaemsya", "we smile". "Samye vezhlivye slova" is "the most polite words".
- 'Go quickly, do not make an important person wait.' "Ne zastavlyay" is "don't make", "don't force". "Vazhnogo cheloveka", "an important person", takes the -ogo of the one being made to wait.
