## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What do you want to order with us today?' Nastya approaches the table politely. She asks the man what he would like to eat.
- 'Bring me any dish to your taste.' The guest acts a bit too familiar. He tells her to just pick something she likes for him.
- 'Good, I will choose the best for you.' Nastya keeps things professional. She agrees to select a great meal for him.
- 'Why do you not take a seat nearby?' He crosses a line with his waitress. The man invites her to sit down next to him.
- 'Excuse me, but I am at work right now.' She politely declines the offer. The waitress reminds him that she is currently busy working.
- 'I am your customer and I pay for everything.' The man tries to use his money as power. He thinks paying for food means he can make the rules.
- 'I am obliged only to bring your dishes.' Nastya firmly states her job duties. She explains that her only task is to serve the food.
- 'You were the first to make pretty eyes at me.' He accuses her of flirting first. The customer claims she was making eyes at him earlier.
- 'I was simply smiling politely at you, guest.' She corrects his misunderstanding. The waitress clarifies that her smile was just basic customer service.
- 'Do not leave, sit with me a little.' The guy refuses to take no for an answer. He begs her to stay and keep him company.
- 'I do not have the right to sit here.' Nastya explains the restaurant rules. Waitresses are simply not allowed to sit with customers.
- 'You must fulfill any requests of mine.' He acts incredibly entitled. The man actually believes she has to do whatever he asks.
- 'Excuse me, I am going to the kitchen right now.' She ends the uncomfortable conversation. The waitress excuses herself and quickly escapes to the kitchen.
