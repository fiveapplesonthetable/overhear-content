## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, look carefully at the dining room!' Kostya urges his friend to scan the tables.
- 'Which one of them could be the critic?' Max wonders as he observes the guests.
- 'Those two work at the bank next door.' Kostya identifies familiar neighborhood patrons of the bar.
- 'Yes, they are our regular guests.' Max rules them out based on past visits.
- 'And that man by the window?' Kostya points toward a solitary diner.
- 'He ordered fish and red wine.' Max evaluates the strange culinary combination.
- 'No, a real critic will not order that.' Kostya dismisses him for poor gastronomic pairing.
- 'What about the guy at table number three?' Max directs his attention to another table.
- 'He is too young for a restaurant critic.' Kostya doubts that a young person holds such authority.
- 'Critics usually sit in solitude.' Max shares his knowledge of food review behavior.
- 'And write down everything in a notepad.' Kostya adds another telltale sign of an undercover reviewer.
- 'Look, the woman at table seven!' Max spots a promising new candidate.
- 'She is looking very strictly at the menu.' Kostya observes the severe facial expression on her face.
- 'It seems we found our critic!' Max concludes excitedly after noticing her behavior.
