## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, attention! An emergency situation in the kitchen.' Kostya bursts into the workspace with urgent news.
- 'Kostya, what happened in the dining room?' Viktor asks as he stops cooking.
- 'A suspected restaurant critic has been spotted!' Kostya delivers the alarming announcement.
- 'She is sitting at table seven.' Kostya identifies the exact location of the threat.
- 'And I have a salad rejection!' Kostya reports the disastrous development.
- 'How, a rejection? My perfect salad?' Viktor gasps in personal and professional shock.
- 'She sent the plate back, chef.' Kostya confirms that the dish was refused.
- 'Which table? Definitely the seventh?' Viktor demands precise confirmation of the customer.
- 'Yes, a strict lady in glasses.' Kostya describes the appearance of the difficult diner.
- 'What exactly did she not like?' Viktor asks for the culinary criticism.
- 'She said the sauce was too sour.' Kostya relays her sharp feedback.
- 'This is an author recipe of haute cuisine!' Viktor defends his refined flavor profile.
- 'Chef, she has already opened a notepad.' Kostya warns that a review is being written.
- 'I will personally prepare her a dessert!' Viktor vows to win over the critic with sweet culinary mastery.
