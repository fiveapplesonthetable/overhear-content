## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'This soup is really very tasty and hot.' Rodion enjoys the meal and praises the hot food. He is very happy with his dish.
- 'Kostya, tell honestly, what happened with you?' Rodion notices something is wrong and asks the bartender to be honest. He wants to know what happened.
- 'Everything is fine, I am simply wiping this glass.' Kostya tries to act normal and says he is just wiping a glass. He is clearly avoiding the question.
- 'You have been washing it for a whole half hour already.' Rodion points out that the guy has been cleaning the same glass for half an hour. It is a dead giveaway.
- 'An important critic is sitting at Nastya' Kostya finally admits that a VIP is sitting at his girlfriend's table. He is very stressed about it.
- 'This insolent man is rudely hitting on her.' Kostya explains that this arrogant man is hitting on her rudely. It makes him furious.
- 'I am afraid of doing something bad for the restaurant.' Kostya confesses his fear of doing something that will harm the business. He feels trapped between love and work.
- 'You see, the problem is not at all in this critic.' Rodion wisely tells him that the problem is not actually the VIP at all. There is a deeper issue here.
- 'The main question here is in who you are.' Rodion explains that the real question is about his own identity. It is about what kind of man he wants to be.
- 'Pass me the salt, please, young friend.' Rodion casually asks his young friend to pass the salt. He pauses his deep advice for a moment.
- 'Whether he is a janitor or a critic—that is completely unimportant.' Rodion says the man's job title does not matter at all. A janitor or a VIP, a jerk is a jerk.
- 'Do you allow someone to insult your girlfriend or not?' Rodion asks the tough question of whether he will allow someone to insult his girl. This is the real test.
- 'The life is yours, and only you make the decision!' Rodion reminds him that it is his life and his choice to make. No one else can decide for him.
