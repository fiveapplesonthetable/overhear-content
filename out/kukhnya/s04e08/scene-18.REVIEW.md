## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I am the one who wants to ask what is going on here!' Emphatic 'this I' fronts the subject in righteous anger.
- 'How can you fire Elena Pavlovna from here?' In everyday Russian, 'how / as' flows straight into 'pavlovna' without extra linking words.
- 'After all, she put her whole soul into this restaurant!' Idiom 'invested her whole soul' praises Elena's dedication.
- 'I will not allow you to replace her with this guy!' Putting the short word for 'not' right in front of the action word is all Russian needs to make a sentence negative, with no extra helper words like 'do' or 'did'.
- 'Stop shouting, nobody is firing her at all!' The owner cuts off Barinov's tirade in astonishment.
- 'First of all, Elena Pavlovna is leaving us on her own.' Pronoun 'herself' means 'of her own accord'.
- 'And second of all, I was planning to replace her with you!' That was the phone call Barinov had overheard the day before!
- 'By the way, she herself vouched strongly for you.' Notice how Russian puts 'other' and 'to strongly' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'And this young man is simply our new designer!' Barinov realizes he smashed the car window of an innocent interior designer.
- 'Lena, why did you not tell me that you are leaving?' Barinov turns to Elena in hurt surprise.
- 'I was planning to tell you everything tonight.' That was the real reason Elena had invited him to dinner this evening.
- 'By the way, now you owe me a new designer!' As Roman bolts from the room, Max returns home with a new shawl.
