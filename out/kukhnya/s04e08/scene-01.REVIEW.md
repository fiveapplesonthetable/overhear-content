## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'It seems to me that women have some kind of weapon.' Pointing out that it seems to him that women have some kind of weapon gives Denis a sobering reality check.
- 'With its help they easily control men.' Around the work and surprise, Max sets a calm, everyday tone with Denis.
- 'Forgotten feelings suddenly appear in you again.' Between the work and the surprise, Max gives Denis a quick, grounded response. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'And a short message changes your peaceful life.' Noting that a short message changes Denis's peaceful life keeps Max's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Vika, Mom is coming to visit us tomorrow already!' Because Mom is coming to visit them tomorrow already, Max does not bother softening the blow for Denis. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'She simply decided to give us a big surprise.' Admitting that she simply decided to give them a big surprise shows Max being honest for a change.
- 'We have such a terrible mess at home right now!' Once they have such a terrible mess at home right now, Vika makes sure Denis hears every word.
- 'Today we are doing a deep cleaning of the apartment!' Declaring that today they are doing a deep cleaning of the apartment brings Vika's temper right to the surface. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'We also need to decide where we will put Mom.' Adding that they also need to decide where they will put Mom fills in the missing piece for Denis. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'Old pal, you need to move out of our place urgently.' Max tells Denis that old pal Denis needs to move out of their place urgently without dressing it up.
- 'All right, let's discuss this another time.' Since all right let them discuss this another time, Denis does not waste words on excuses. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Forgive me, but I will not be able to keep you here.' Mentioning that he will not be able to keep Denis here moves the talk in a new direction. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
