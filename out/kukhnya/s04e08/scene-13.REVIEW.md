## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, it does not look at all like he is grieving.' Pointing to the grief at once, Marina uses "pokhozhe" ("like, similar") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Look at him, he is far too cheerful today!' Explaining how cheerful he is in the exchange, Marina chooses "vesyolyy" ("cheerful, merry") so Senya gets the point right away.
- 'Marina, why are you picking on the man for nothing?' Senya challenges firmly how Marina expects that to work in practice.
- 'He is under heavy stress, and laughter is a defense.' Highlighting the defence before Denis, Senya reaches for the Russian word "zashchita" ("defence, protection") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Senya, maybe we could watch an interesting movie tonight?' Checking on an interesting movie in front of Senya, Denis keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Senya purely through rising pitch.
- 'A big passenger plane crashes in the mountains in it!' Denis follows up steadily in their talk, so Senya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Denis, let's rather watch a movie another time.' Talking about the cinema with Denis, Senya lands on "posmotrim" ("have a look", built on "posmotret") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Marina, it seems everything is even worse than I thought!' Replying dryly around Marina, the "-sya" ending on "kazhetsya" ("it seems, I think") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'From such grief he has completely lost his mind!' Senya responds right away across the room, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'I beg you, keep a close eye on him.' Talking about a close eye beside Marina, Senya chooses "priglyadyvay" ("eye") so Marina gets the point right away.
- 'Denis, do you want to go for a walk right now?' Replying naturally on the spot, Marina keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Denis purely through rising pitch.
- 'Yes, I want to go visit my brother right now!' Denis speaks up candidly on the spot, refusing to let the moment slip past.
