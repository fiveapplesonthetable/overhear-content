## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, it does not look at all like he is grieving.' Marina hears Denis chatting and laughing in the hallway.
- 'Look at him, he is far too cheerful today!' Adverb 'too/excessively' modifies the masculine adjective 'cheerful'.
- 'Marina, why are you picking on the man for nothing?' Reflexive verb 'to pick on / find fault with' governs 'to' plus dative.
- 'He is under heavy stress, and laughter is a defense.' Senya invents a psychological explanation on the spot.
- 'Senya, maybe we could watch an interesting movie tonight?' Denis walks in and inadvertently tests Senya's plane-crash lie.
- 'A big passenger plane crashes in the mountains in it!' Reflexive present verb 'crashes' describes the plot of the movie.
- 'Denis, let's rather watch a movie another time.' Senya hurries Denis out of the kitchen before he says more.
- 'Marina, it seems everything is even worse than I thought!' Comparative adverb 'worse' turns the blunder into deeper tragedy.
- 'From such grief he has completely lost his mind!' Senya claims the trauma has unhinged poor Denis.
- 'I beg you, keep a close eye on him.' Imperfective imperative 'keep an eye on' takes the preposition 'behind' plus instrumental.
- 'Denis, do you want to go for a walk right now?' Seeing Denis put on his jacket, Marina speaks to him gently.
- 'Yes, I want to go visit my brother right now!' Since Marina believes Denis's whole family died, she thinks he is going to the cemetery!
