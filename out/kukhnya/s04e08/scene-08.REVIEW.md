## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Here is our kitchen, and further over there is the office.' Demonstrative particle 'here is' points out each area of the restaurant.
- 'Over there are the locker rooms and shower for our staff.' Preposition 'for' governs the genitive collective noun 'staff'.
- 'I do not even know what else to show you here.' Infinitive question 'what else to show' takes the dative pronoun 'to you'.
- 'Everything is clear to me, a lot needs to be changed here.' Unaware the man is an interior designer, Barinov bristles at his comment.
- 'Lyova, tell me quickly, where is Lena right now?' Barinov checks when Elena Pavlovna is expected back.
- 'The owner sent her somewhere, she will be back in an hour.' Indefinite adverb 'to somewhere' marks an unspecified errand.
- 'Do you see, Lyova, that guy right there is your new chef!' Barinov jumps to the conclusion that the young man is the new head chef.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, maybe we should not get involved?' Gentle Lyova senses trouble as Barinov rolls up his sleeves.
- 'Lyova, do not start, we will simply have a talk with him.' Perfective future 'we will talk' sounds much calmer than Barinov's real plan.
- 'We will explain to him that he does not need to work here.' Dative pronoun 'to him' combines with 'does not need to work'.
- 'We will speak with him very politely and civilly!' Adverb 'culturally/civilly' is pure irony coming from Chef Barinov.
- 'Come on, Lyova, let's go after him outside quickly!' The two chefs follow the young man out to the parking lot.
