## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I heard that strange conversation with my own ears!' Mentioning the ear on the spot, Barinov uses "strannyy" ("strange, odd") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Boys, I am going to step out right now for a couple of hours.' Dwelling on the boys before Barinov, Elena links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Vitya, please keep an eye on the kitchen here.' Elena cuts in calmly watching Lyova, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'It does not look like they are going to fire Elena Pavlovna.' Lyova weighs in boldly in the exchange and lands on "sobirayutsya" ("gather, are gathering", built on "sobiratsya") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Be quiet, Lyova, do not shout so loudly!' Barinov cuts in quietly on the spot, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Lyova, you did not understand, she does not know this.' Barinov speaks up dryly toward Lyova, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'They simply want to quietly replace her with another chef.' Barinov fires back dryly on the spot and brings in "zamenit" ("to replace") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Then we definitely need to warn her!' Holding firm briskly in this beat, Lyova leans on "nado" ("it is necessary, one needs to"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'What is the use, after all you know her well.' Barinov counters bluntly in their talk, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'She will then simply walk out of here proudly on her own.' Barinov speaks smoothly on the spot and leans on "otsyuda" ("from here") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'And I really do not want her to leave.' Barinov fires back firmly to Lyova and lands on "ukhodila" ("to leave, to go away", built on "ukhodit") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'She is not going anywhere, did you understand me, Lyova?' Barinov inquires straight out what Lyova is actually getting at here.
