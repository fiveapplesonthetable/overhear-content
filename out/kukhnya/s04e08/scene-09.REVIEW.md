## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Young man, hold on, this is for psychological effect!' Notice how Russian puts 'young' and 'effect, impact' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'What are you doing, take your hands off me immediately!' Here the guest anchors the thought on 'are doing' and finishes on 'hands', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'Listen to me carefully, you will not work here!' Compound future with negation delivers a categorical prohibition.
- 'Did you understand me well right now, young man?' Masculine past verb 'understood' checks that the warning sank in.
- 'If we see you here one more time...' Notice how Russian puts 'still, yet, more' and 'to see' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'This cook right here will immediately break your nose!' Barinov volunteers gentle Lyova as his enforcer.
- 'Lyova, tell him, you will definitely break it, right?' Poor Lyova nods reluctantly to back up his boss.
- 'And do not tell anyone about our conversation today!' Chef Barinov pairs 'matter' with 'to speak, to say, to talk' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
- 'Yes, I understood everything, just let me go!' Terrified, the young man agrees to stay away from the restaurant.
- 'Quiet, sit in your seat, Elena is coming this way!' Spotting Elena Pavlovna entering the courtyard, Barinov panics.
- 'Lyova, get in the car quickly and do not make noise!' Both chefs duck inside the car so Elena does not see the confrontation.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, it seems she is going to spot us right now!' Instead, Barinov steps out smoothly to greet Elena.
