## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Young man, hold on, this is for psychological effect!' Barinov cuts in openly with a nod, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'What are you doing, take your hands off me immediately!' Underscoring the hands in the exchange, the guest puts the emphasis on "nemedlenno" ("immediately") in the middle of the talk.
- 'Listen to me carefully, you will not work here!' Barinov weighs in dryly beside the guest, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'Did you understand me well right now, young man?' Barinov checks promptly to see how the guest will answer on the spot.
- 'If we see you here one more time...' Barinov answers decisively right here and brings in "yeshchyo" ("more, still") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'This cook right here will immediately break your nose!' Barinov jumps in calmly with a shrug, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'Lyova, tell him, you will definitely break it, right?' Barinov tests sharply about what Lyova just brought up.
- 'And do not tell anyone about our conversation today!' Mentioning his conversation at once, Barinov leans on "razgovor" ("conversation") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Yes, I understood everything, just let me go!' The guest replies frankly at once and reaches for the Russian word "otpustite" ("let go, release", built from "otpustit") right at the center of the line.
- 'Quiet, sit in your seat, Elena is coming this way!' Barinov steps in honestly toward the guest, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Lyova, get in the car quickly and do not make noise!' Touching on the car with Lyova, Barinov uses "mashinu" ("car", from "mashina") to make the point stick.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, it seems she is going to spot us right now!' Turning briskly during the talk, the "-sya" ending on "kazhetsya" ("it seems, I think") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
