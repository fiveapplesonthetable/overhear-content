## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'All right, a new order, pasta and two steaks!' Genitive singular 'of steak' follows the numeral 'two'.
- 'And for table five we are serving hot onion soups!' Accusative phrase 'onto the fifth table' designates the destination.
- 'Lyova, come over here, can I have you for a minute?' Elliptical request 'is it possible (to have) you for a minute' is natural spoken Russian.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, I am working right now, there are orders in the kitchen!' Lyova tries to keep the kitchen running smoothly.
- 'Come here quickly, Lyova, this is very important!' Neuter short adjective 'important' makes it sound like a kitchen emergency.
- 'All right, Viktor Petrovich, but only for one minute.' Lyova hurries into the office expecting a culinary crisis.
- 'Tell me, Lyova, which shirt is better to put on?' Instead, Barinov is holding up two hangers in front of a mirror!
- 'It seems to me that this shirt right here is too bright.' Feminine adjective 'bright' agrees with 'shirt'.
- 'And this second shirt right here is kind of boring.' Indefinite particle 'somehow / kind of' softens 'boring'.
- 'Quickly choose a shirt for me and go work!' Perfective imperative 'choose' demands an instant fashion verdict.
- 'It seems to me that this shirt right here is much better.' Lyova points to one hanger so he can get back to his stoves.
- 'Thank you, Lyova, I have always trusted your taste!' Meanwhile, back at Claude Monet, Max tries to save his mother's shawl.
