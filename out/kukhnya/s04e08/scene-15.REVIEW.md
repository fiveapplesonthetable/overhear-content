## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Ainura, tell me, do you know how to knit well?' Present-tense verb 'know how' takes the imperfective infinitive 'to knit'.
- 'And what exactly do you need knitted right now?' Modal adverb 'needed' pairs with the perfective infinitive.
- 'This warm shawl of Mom's needs to be fixed urgently!' Possessive adjective 'Mom's' modifies the masculine noun 'shawl'.
- 'Oh my, and do you have knitting needles with you?' Seeing the tangled mess of yarn, Ainura backs away.
- 'No, Maksim, I do not know how to knit something like that.' Ainura wisely declines the impossible repair job.
- 'I told you that everything will be fine!' Even after the old shawl falls apart completely, Max stays upbeat.
- 'Max, and just what are we going to do now?' Compound future 'what will we do' expresses Vika's exasperation.
- 'Do not worry, right now we will definitely think of something.' Indefinite pronoun 'something or other' pairs with 'will invent'.
- 'What, are you going to lie to your mother again?' Imperfective infinitive 'to lie' follows the auxiliary 'will you'.
- 'Vika, I promise you, this is for the last time!' Max swears that one last fib will solve everything.
- 'Your lying never leads to anything good!' Neuter verbal noun 'lying' is the subject of the negative idiom.
- 'I will simply buy her the exact same shawl!' Max decides to buy a new shawl and tell his mother Vika knitted a replacement.
