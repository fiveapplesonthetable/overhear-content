## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Marina, what happened, and where is Senya right now?' Waking up in the dark, Denis is startled to see Senya's wife beside his bed.
- 'He is in the bathroom right now, and I stopped in to see you.' Feminine past verb 'dropped in' takes 'to you'.
- 'You must know that you are not alone now!' Marina thinks she is comforting a lonely orphan.
- 'Thank you very much, but you do not need to help me!' Denis has no idea about the plane-crash story Senya told her.
- 'Do not be so afraid, you will feel better right away!' the speaker pairs 'fear' with 'easier, better' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
- 'Senya and I will help you start a new life.' Idiomatic 'we with Senya' means 'Senya and I'.
- 'Listen, when we made our deal, Senya kept quiet about this!' Denis thinks the married couple is proposing something scandalous!
- 'On such strange terms I refuse to rent the room!' In everyday Russian, 'strange' flows straight into 'refuse' without extra linking words.
- 'Senya, come here, is he renting a room from us?' Marina's sympathy instantly turns to fury as she calls her husband in.
- 'No, Marina, he is simply living with us for free!' Senya frantically signals Denis to stay quiet.
- 'Some 'simply', for twenty-five thousand rubles!' Exclamation 'nothing to itself' expresses indignant disbelief.
- 'Marina, calm down, I will explain everything honestly to you right now!' Evicted on the spot, Denis heads back to Max's apartment.
