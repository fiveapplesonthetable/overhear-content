## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You can start working right today.' Pointing out that Kostya can start working right today gives Kostya a sobering reality check.
- 'Get your uniform and go change.' By telling Barinov to get Barinov's uniform and go change, Vika keeps her side of the story grounded.
- 'Thank you, Viktoriya Sergeyevna.' With the call and kitchen in play, Kostya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Awesome. Thank you.' Over the call and the room, Barinov answers Kostya without a second thought.
- 'How are things going?' Wondering how things going are is how Vika puts Barinov on the spot. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Here we are at the airport already.' Admitting that here they are at the airport already shows Kostya being honest for a change.
- 'You got there awfully fast somehow.' Saying that Kostya got there awfully fast somehow is how Barinov sets the tone with Kostya. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'We drove right behind an ambulance.' Once they drove right behind an ambulance, Vika can finally tell Barinov what she means.
- 'After all, I am supposedly a patient too.' Adding that after all he is supposedly a patient fills in the missing piece for Barinov.
- 'Chef, please, be quiet.' Between the kitchen and the call, Barinov gives Kostya a quick, grounded response.
- 'I will call you back later.' Since she will call Barinov back later, Vika does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Anyway, that is it.' Mentioning that anyway that is it moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'There is a security check at the entrance here.' Saying that there is a security check at the entrance here is how Barinov sets the tone with Kostya.
