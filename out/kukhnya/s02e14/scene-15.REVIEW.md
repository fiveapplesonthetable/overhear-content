## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dmitry Vladimirovich, you have a visitor.' Barinov follows up honestly beside Elena, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'It is me again.' Holding firm frankly on the spot, spoken Russian drops "is" and "are" in the present, letting Elena place the two halves side by side.
- 'You were not expecting me, hello again.' Nagiyev counters briskly to Barinov and uses "zdravstvuyte" ("hello") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Hello, Elena Pavlovna.' Barinov readily drops this formal greeting in the exchange using "zdravstvuyte" ("hello") — the polite Russian greeting that literally wishes the other person good health.
- 'You see, I feel guilty about this.' Elena fires back calmly in the exchange and uses "vinovatoy" ("guilty, at fault", from "vinovatyy") to make the point stick.
- 'For sending Viktor Petrovich away for treatment.' Touching on the treatment around Barinov, Nagiyev centers the remark on "lecheniye" ("treatment") without any extra fuss.
- 'I wish someone would send me away like that.' Barinov responds firmly with a nod, watching Nagiyev's face to see how the news lands.
- 'Well, his kitchen has been left unsupervised.' Underscoring the supervision at once, Elena brings in "prismotra" ("supervision", from "prismotr") as the key Russian word here.
- 'I could fill in for him while he is gone.' Elena follows up calmly to Barinov and chooses "zamenit" ("to replace") so Barinov gets the point right away.
- 'Oh, no, there is really no need.' Barinov opens up candidly without holding back, making his position completely clear.
- 'We could not leave your boss short-staffed like that.' Touching on the comrade to Barinov, Elena uses "tovarishcha" ("comrade", from "tovarishch") to make the point stick.
- 'Oh, that is not a problem at all.' Addressing a problem in this beat, Nagiyev drops in "voobshche" ("in general, altogether") to sum up the whole situation at once.
- 'My sous-chef can easily cover for me.' Barinov cuts in evenly toward Nagiyev, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
