- 'What are we going to do?' Here wondering what they going to do are shows Sasha looking for the real truth. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Everyone pay attention, while our head chef...' Around the conversation and situation, Elena sets a calm, everyday tone with Barinov.

- 'Yes, while our head chef is resting, so to speak.' Even though while their head is resting so to speak, Sasha keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'Getting medical treatment. Yes.' Between the conversation and the situation, Elena gives Barinov a quick, grounded response.
- 'Elena Pavlovna right here will be working with you today.' Saying that Elena Pavlovna right here will be working with Elena today is how Barinov sets the tone with Elena. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'There is nothing to worry about.' Once there is nothing to worry, Sasha can finally tell Barinov what she means.
- 'Alright, best of luck to you all.' Between the work and the truth, Elena gives Barinov a quick, grounded response.
- 'What now?' Around the room and kitchen, Barinov wants to know where Elena really stands. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.

- 'No, everything is fine between us.' Mentioning that everything is fine between them moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Did Sasha dump you?' Here checking whether Sasha dump Elena shows Barinov looking for the real truth. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
