## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Have you ever thought that you could become a good actor?' Underscoring a good actor with Barinov, Nagiyev uses "khoroshim" ("good", from "khoroshiy") to make the point stick.
- 'And have you ever thought that?' Reacting candidly before Nagiyev, Barinov keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Nagiyev purely through rising pitch.
- 'I recognize my old friend, I am here on business.' Nagiyev fires back decisively on the spot, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'I want to give you a chance to act in a good movie.' Talking about a chance to Barinov, Nagiyev reaches for the Russian word "khoroshem" ("good", built from "khoroshiy") right at the center of the line.
- 'Not for any money.' Pointing to the money right here, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 4 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'I am offering a hundred thousand rubles.' Nagiyev proceeds firmly around Barinov, before Barinov can change the subject.
- 'Make it two hundred thousand.' Bringing up the thousand on the spot, Barinov leans on "tysyach" ("thousand", a form of "tysyacha") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Have a conscience, there is a crisis in the country.' Nagiyev steps in frankly facing Barinov, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Fine, a hundred thousand.' Barinov gently offers this word of agreement at this moment using "ladno" ("alright") — built from the old Russian root for harmony and order, used every day for "okay, fine".
- 'But only if you order lunch from me for the entire crew.' Pointing to the lunch in this beat, Barinov chooses "zakazyvayesh" ("order, book", from the root "zakazyvat") so Nagiyev hears the exact meaning.
- 'Good man, come on, I will introduce you to big cinema.' Nagiyev opens up warmly beside Barinov, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Let's roll and film!' Nagiyev speaks lightly around Barinov, making his position completely clear.
