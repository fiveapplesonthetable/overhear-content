## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Let me pay you thirty thousand a month.' Telling Barinov to let him pay Barinov thirty thousand a month is how Kostya lays out the situation for Barinov. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'It is normal when parents help their children.' Explaining that it is normal when parents help their children lays the cards on the table for Eleonora. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'I did not have parents.' Saying that he did not have parents is how Kostya sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'I know, but after all we are one family.' Knowing but after all they are one family is how Barinov lays out the situation for Eleonora.
- 'We are now your parents too, do you understand?' Barinov asks whether they are now Eleonora's parents too do Eleonora understands, refusing to let the point slide. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Then let's drink your cognac.' Noting that they should drink Barinov's cognac keeps Kostya's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'As I understand it, the kitchen did not work today.' Realizing that as she understands it the kitchen did not work today changes how Eleonora handles the situation.
- 'And this day can be deducted from your salary.' Noting that this day can be deducted from Barinov's salary keeps Eleonora's side of the story simple and clear. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'Why say that, the auteur cuisine brought in a few pennies.' Even though why say that the auteur cuisine brought in a few pennies, Barinov keeps a steady face in front of Eleonora.
- 'Vitya, you can keep this money for yourself.' Admitting that Barinov can keep this money for themselves shows Eleonora being honest for a change.
- 'There are plenty of banknotes like those lying around out there.' Saying that there are plenty of banknotes like those lying around out there is how Eleonora sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'This is prop money.' Once this is prop money, Eleonora can finally tell Barinov what she means.
