## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Timur, help me out, I have a problem.' Helping him out he has a problem is how Max lays out the situation for Barinov.
- 'I warned you in advance, after all!' Over the moment and the exchange, Timur lets his real feelings show. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Today the shrimp are not of the first freshness.' Once today the shrimp are not of the first freshness, Timur can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'They are waiting for me in the back courtyard right now.' Adding that they are waiting for him in the back courtyard right now fills in the missing piece for Barinov.
- 'Maybe you can talk to them calmly?' Asking whether maybe Barinov can talk to them calmly puts Barinov right on the defensive. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Well then everything is fine, we will talk to them right now.' Since everything is fine they will talk to them right now, Timur does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Really, listen, I am in your debt now!' Because really listen he is in Barinov's debt now, Max does not bother softening the blow for Barinov.
- 'You do not owe me anything, you heard nothing about the shrimp.' Saying that Barinov does not owe him anything Barinov heard nothing about the shrimp is how Timur sets the tone with Barinov. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'Hello, Katya, just do not hang up, listen to me.' Because just do not hang up listen to him, Barinov treats the matter as settled.
- 'In short, I was wrong and I want to apologize.' Explaining that in short he was wrong and he wants to apologize lays the cards on the table for Max.
- 'Come to our restaurant tonight together with Stas.' Explaining that Max should come to their restaurant tonight together with Stas lays the cards on the table for Max.
- 'Alright, we will sit together tonight, my treat!' Saying that Alright they will sit together tonight his treat is how Barinov takes charge of the moment. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
