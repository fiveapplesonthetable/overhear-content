## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, ambulance, someone is sick here, probably appendicitis!' Saying that ambulance someone is sick here probably appendicitis is how Max takes charge of the moment.
- 'Please come to us as quickly as possible!' Here telling Barinov to come to them as quickly as possible lets Max's real feelings break through. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'And I thought you were a real man.' Once he thought Barinov was a real man, Timur can finally tell Barinov what he means. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Sorry, bro, but I am forced to drag you out by force.' Adding that bro but he is forced to drag Barinov out by force fills in the missing piece for Barinov.
- 'Stop resisting, I have no time!' Saying that Barinov should stop resisting he has no time is how Timur takes charge of the moment. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Our Katya came into the dining room with bikers.' Since their Katya came into the dining room with bikers, Barinov does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Timur, just one phone call and I will come right out!' Max insists that just one phone call and he will come right out, leaving Barinov no room to argue.
- 'Mind you do not let me down, I will come back and check.' Here minding Barinov does not let him down he will come back and check sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'Hello, Katya, hi, who are those bikers with you out there?' Here asking who those bikers with Max out there are shows Barinov looking for the real truth. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'We are not expecting anyone else, and here you are with a surprise.' Explaining that they are not expecting anyone else and here Max is with a surprise lays the cards on the table for Max.
- 'Stas thinks that Dad will set an ambush for him.' Max mentions that Stas thinks that dad will set an ambush for him so Barinov sees what is at stake. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'And what happened, so he is expecting an ambush?' Questioning whether what happened so he is expecting an ambush is how Barinov tests Max's story. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
