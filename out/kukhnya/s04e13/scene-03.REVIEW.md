## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Here are my new apartments.' Explaining that here are his new apartments lays the cards on the table for Katya. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'It is very cozy here in your place.' Saying that it is very cozy here in Barinov's place is how Katya sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'Previously this office belonged to a woman.' Over the office and the shift, Barinov answers Katya without a second thought.
- 'I see you have already settled in.' Since she sees Barinov has already settled, Katya does not waste words on excuses. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Of course, tell me how your life is.' Telling Katya to tell him how Katya's life is is how Barinov lays out the situation for Katya.
- 'I am currently looking for a new job.' Once she is currently looking for a new job, Katya can finally tell Barinov what she means.
- 'Dad, will you hire me to work with you?' Wondering if dad will Barinov hires her to work with Barinov shows Katya caught off guard. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'You did not call for a whole year!' Saying that Katya did not call for a whole year is how Barinov takes charge of the moment.
- 'I have gone even longer without seeing you.' Pointing out that she has gone even longer without seeing Barinov gives Barinov a sobering reality check. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I always had my own reasons.' Saying that he always had his own reasons is how Barinov sets the tone with Katya. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Chef, they delivered potatoes and tomatoes there.' With the argument and family in play, Lyova keeps this short reply brisk and direct.

- 'All right, Dad, I am leaving already.' Saying that all right dad she is leaving already is how Katya sets the tone with Barinov.
