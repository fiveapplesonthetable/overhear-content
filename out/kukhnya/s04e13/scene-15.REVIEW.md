## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Denis, get this cat out of here quickly!' Katya replies honestly with a nod, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Katya, where did a cat even come from here?' Mentioning a cat at once, German brings in "voobshche" ("at all") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'I myself know absolutely nothing!' Holding firm candidly with German, Katya pairs the negative word with "ne" ("not"), since Russian naturally doubles its negatives for emphasis.
- 'What is going on over there with you right now?' German tests frankly where this conversation is heading next.
- 'Hurray, I finally caught this cat!' Emphasizing the cat in the exchange, Denis brings in "nakonets" ("at last, finally") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Wait, there is a phone number on the collar here.' Pointing to the collar before German, Denis chooses "osheynike" ("collar") so German gets the point right away.
- 'We can call and find out whose cat it is.' Denis counters evenly without holding back, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'Thank you, Denis, let me call right now.' Pointing to the thank at once, German leans on "spasibo" ("thank you") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Wait, why is my cell phone ringing?' Underscoring how ringing things are at once, Katya puts the question word "pochemu" right at the start so German hears the focus immediately.
- 'German Mikhailovich, this is some kind of cruel setup!' Katya reacts plainly in this moment, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Honestly, this is not my cat!' Pointing out how honest things are beside German, Katya leans on "chestnoye" ("honest", a form of "chestnyy") to give the remark its edge.
- 'I do not want to listen, you are fired!' German follows up openly on the spot, before Katya can change the subject.
