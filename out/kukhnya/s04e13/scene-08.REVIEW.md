- 'She is now under his protection.' Senya clarifies his phrasing using the instrumental singular noun for patronage.
- 'Your Katya got a job working at Monet.' A reflexive past verb pairs with an infinitive to express getting hired.

- 'Chef, where are you going right now?' Senya panics as Barinov marches toward the door.
- 'Just do not say that I told you!' Senya pleads for secrecy with a negated plural imperative and a subordinate clause.
