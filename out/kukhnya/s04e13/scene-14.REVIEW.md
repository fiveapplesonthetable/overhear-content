## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Viktor Petrovich, here I brought everything.' Explaining that here he brought everything lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
- 'Why did you even need this cat?' By asking why Barinov even need this cat, Timur presses Barinov for a straight answer. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'It is a small present for one person.' Claiming that it is a small present for one person sounds confident, even if Senya has doubts.
- 'By the way, take these miracle drops too.' Telling Barinov to take these miracle drops moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'With them it is easy to win a woman's heart.' Timur explains that with them it is easy to win a woman heart, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'All right, hand over the cat here, as I asked.' Realizing that all right hand over the cat here as he asked changes how Barinov handles the situation.
- 'There is a good collar on it, everything as needed.' Noting that there is a good collar on it everything as needed keeps Timur's side of the story simple and clear. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Chef, the coast is clear in the kitchen.' Even though the coast is clear in the kitchen, Senya keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'Then it is time to let the tiger out of the cage!' Once it is time to let the tiger out of the cage, Barinov makes sure Senya hears every word.
- 'Senya, I really believe in you.' Saying that he really believes in Senya is how Barinov sets the tone with Senya. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Chef, we actually did not agree on this!' Senya insists that they actually did not agree on this, leaving Barinov no room to argue. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Will five thousand rubles be enough for you right now?' Asking whether five thousand rubles be enough for Senya right now is how Barinov puts Senya on the spot.
- 'For your sake I am ready for absolutely anything!' Senya declares that for Barinov's sake he is ready for absolutely anything as the tension in the room peaks. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
