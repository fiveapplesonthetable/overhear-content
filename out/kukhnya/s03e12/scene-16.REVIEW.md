## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Who is here? Who came in?' Wondering who here is is how Katya puts the hostess on the spot.
- 'It is me again, your landlady.' Explaining that it is her again Katya's landlady lays the cards on the table for Katya. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I need to take the readings from the water meters.' Since she needs to take the readings from the water meters, the hostess does not waste words on excuses. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Take the readings and leave right away.' Here taking the readings and leave right away sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'But they are in the bathroom.' Pointing out that they are in the bathroom gives Katya a sobering reality check.
- 'I will write everything down myself and bring it to you.' Katya explains that she will write everything down herself and bring it to the hostess, keeping her voice calm and direct. Notice how she sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Alright, then I will wait.' Realizing that Alright then she will wait changes how the hostess handles the situation.
- 'What is this? For the second day there is some knocking.' Over the point and the restaurant, Nagiyev presses the hostess for a straight answer. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'He does not seem to be in a hurry to apologize on his knees.' Even though he does not seem to be in a hurry to apologize on his knees, the hostess keeps a steady face in front of Katya.
- 'Well it is fine, I will wait a little longer.' Admitting that it is fine she will wait a little longer shows the hostess being honest for a change.
- 'What happened here with you again?' With the plan and restaurant hanging in the air, Nagiyev puts the hostess on the spot. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'What on earth are you doing here?' With the plan and kitchen hanging in the air, Nagiyev puts the hostess on the spot. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
