## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I feel so sorry for that old man from the second floor.' Noting that he feels so sorry for that old man from the second floor keeps Lyova's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'He was a grumpy man, of course.' Explaining that he was a grumpy man of course lays the cards on the table for Lyova.
- 'Dima, why did you kill the old man?' Wondering why Lyova kills the old man is how Barinov puts Lyova on the spot. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'I did not kill any old man!' Because he did not kill any old man, Nagiyev does not bother softening the blow for Barinov. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'I saw myself how they were just loading him into the ambulance.' Pointing out that he saw himself how they were just loading him into the ambulance gives Lyova a sobering reality check.
- 'I did not touch him with a finger, I swear to you!' Once he did not touch him with a finger he swears to Barinov, Nagiyev makes sure Barinov hears every word. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Kirill Ilyich, you are alive! I beg your forgiveness!' Declaring that Kirill Ilyich Barinov is alive brings Nagiyev's temper right to the surface. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'I accidentally disgraced the honor of a Soviet officer.' With the situation and moment in play, Nagiyev keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'I beg your forgiveness, this will not happen again!' By begging Barinov's forgiveness this will not happen again, Nagiyev raises the stakes in the room at once. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Well then, peace? — A truce!' With the plan and shift hanging in the air, Barinov puts Lyova on the spot. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Chef, we just questioned the paramedic outside.' With the situation and plan in play, Lyova keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Nobody killed the old man, it was just an accident.' Once nobody killed the old man it was just an accident, Lyova can finally tell Barinov what he means. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
