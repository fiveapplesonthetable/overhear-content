## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'He poured soup all over me.' Max follows up wryly without any doubt, so Barinov knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Senya, did you make the soup without meat?' Emphasizing the soup in the exchange, Barinov brings in "prigotovil" ("cooked", from "prigotovit") as the key Russian word here.
- 'You know the old man only needs an excuse.' Barinov proceeds right away in their talk, testing Senya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Chef, this is an excellent chicken broth.' Senya opens up quietly with a sigh, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Grandpa Kirill is having a flare-up again.' Touching on a flare in front of Senya, Barinov uses "obostreniye" ("flare-up, relapse") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Will someone explain to me who Grandpa Kirill is?' Turning to the grandfather in front of Barinov, Max puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Barinov hears the focus immediately.
- 'In our building all the apartments are bought up.' Max responds at once with a sigh, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Only the apartment of Kirill Ilyich remained.' Highlighting the flat with Barinov, Max reaches for the Russian word "kvartira" ("flat") right at the heart of the line.
- 'They tried to buy it out for big money.' Addressing the money in this beat, Max puts the weight on "bolshiye" ("big, serious", from "bolshoy") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'It turned out Grandpa Kirill does not need money.' Max pushes back pointedly across the room, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'And nobody knows what he needs.' Max speaks up promptly in this beat and uses "nuzhno" ("necessary") to carry the main weight of the thought.
