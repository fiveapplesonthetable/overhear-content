## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Calm, you say?' When Max asks if calm German says, German has to answer fast.
- 'What did you break?' Wondering what German breaks is how Vika puts German on the spot. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'That was not me, that was Styopa.' Max mentions that that was not him that was Styopa so German sees what is at stake. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Kostya asked us to look after him.' Claiming that Kostya asked them to look after him sounds confident, even if German has doubts.
- 'He is a cool kid, right?' Asking whether he is a cool kid right puts German right on the defensive. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'He is a cute boy.' Vika explains that he is a cute boy, keeping her voice calm and direct.
- 'I still need to unpack my things.' Realizing that she still needs to unpack her things changes how Vika handles the situation.
- 'Viktoriya, come to the restaurant urgently.' Around the conversation and dish, German sets a calm, everyday tone with Max.
- 'We have been robbed!' German declares that they have been robbed as the tension in the room peaks. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Yes, I will be right there.' Admitting that she will be right there shows Vika being honest for a change.
- 'Styopa is staying with you.' Saying that Styopa is staying with German is how Vika sets the tone with German. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'And what am I supposed to do with him?' Wondering if what am he supposed to do with him shows Max caught off guard. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Practice, imagine that he is our son.' Adding that practice imagine that he is their son fills in the missing piece for German.
- 'He is climbing into the oven right now!' Max declares that he is climbing into the oven right now as the tension in the room peaks. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
