## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'These keys do not fit.' When a key fits a lock, Russian uses the motion verb for 'to approach' or 'suit'.
- 'Oh, I forgot to check the second one.' The ordinal number 'second' acts as a noun here for the second pocket.
- 'And what are we supposed to do now?' Fedya pairs 'now, nowadays' with 'to do' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
- 'Guys, we need to get out of here.' Russian expresses feelings and needs by saying 'to me it is needed' or 'to me it seems', putting the person in the 'to me' form.
- 'Here is the plan, we have to steal the safe.' The two-word opener 'so like this' is how a boss announces a new plan of action.
- 'Chef, they will put us in prison!' In Russian, the third-person plural of 'to seat' is the standard colloquial way to say 'they will jail us'.
- 'Who even needs it?' In spoken Russian, the words stay in their normal statement order and Chef Barinov's rising voice at the end turns the line into a question.
- 'It has stood here empty for a hundred years.' Russian uses the present tense for an action that started in the past and continues right up to now.
- 'Carrying it out unnoticed will not work.' Here Fedya anchors the thought on 'issue, hand down, carry out' and finishes on 'will work out', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'Besides, it is very heavy.' The three-word connector at the front is a useful phrase meaning 'moreover' or 'besides'.
- 'Let's drop it out the window!' Starting with the everyday word for 'let us' is how Russian speakers warmly invite someone to do something together.
- 'If it breaks open, even better.' Notice how Russian puts 'to break down, get broken' and 'better' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'Go to the kitchen and keep watch.' Two singular imperatives send Senya to act as the lookout.
- 'We can drop it now, go!' The modal word for 'it is clear/possible' gives the green light to push the safe.
