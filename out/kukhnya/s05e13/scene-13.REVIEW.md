## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'So, the defendant was kissing Sasha in the storage room.' Notice how Russian puts 'defendant' and 'storeroom, warehouse' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'Precisely, he was only kissing!' The meaning hinges on 'here' working together with 'kissed' in spoken Russian.
- 'An ordinary kiss does not constitute infidelity!' Putting the short word for 'not' right in front of the action word is all Russian needs to make a sentence negative, with no extra helper words like 'do' or 'did'.
- 'Is that so, darling? Well, if that is how it is!' Nastya brings out the contrast between 'yes' and 'really' in a few short words.
- 'Nastya, that is enough already, stop immediately!' The grammar packs 'Nastya' and 'immediately' into a tight, idiomatic kitchen exchange.
- 'I take all my words back!' Notice how Russian expresses 'I' and 'back' with crisp inflection rather than helper words.
- 'I agree, a kiss is real infidelity.' The meaning hinges on 'agree' working together with 'infidelity' in spoken Russian.
- 'Your Honor, I have an important question.' Kostya brings out the contrast between 'your' and 'honor' in a few short words.
- 'Victoria, are you sure that they were kissing?' Spoken Russian keeps this brisk, leaning on 'proper name' and finishing on 'were kissing'.
- 'Maybe they were simply sitting next to each other?' Kostya brings out the contrast between 'maybe' and 'be' in a few short words.
- 'What if she was taking a speck out of his eye?' Kostya raises the pitch on 'eyes' to turn the phrase into a direct spoken question.
- 'In the storage room in the dark and without pants?' Spoken Russian keeps this brisk, leaning on 'on' and finishing on 'pants'.
