- 'And I am very pleased too, join us.' Eleonora counters candidly with a shrug, making her position completely clear.
- 'By the way, thank you for moving me to the luxury suite.' The guest focuses on one colorful term on cue: "pereselili" means "moved, upgraded" (from "pereselit"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'The suite is simply magnificent.' The guest gives that remark at once: the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") works like a shrug in the sentence, making a big claim sound effortless.

- 'Good afternoon, I am the head chef of this restaurant.' Barinov hands over the thought around the guest: notice how Russian needs no word for "am", "is", or "are" in the present — placing the words side by side says it all.
