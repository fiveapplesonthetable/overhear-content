## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good evening, respected ladies and gentlemen!' German uses a polite everyday Russian courtesy expression that sets the tone of the conversation right away.
- 'Today you will see and taste unusual dishes.' In everyday Russian, 'today' flows straight into 'of dish' without extra linking words.
- 'This evening will change your perception of reality!' German's dramatic marketing line sounds terribly suspicious to the police.
- 'And so, friends, we are beginning our culinary show!' He steps aside and invites Katya to demonstrate the first dish.
- 'Tell me, miss, what kind of powders are those?' From the audience, the undercover detective asks a pointed question.
- 'That is our main culinary secret of the evening.' Katya smiles mysteriously, unintentionally confirming the cop's suspicions.
- 'I can tell you only one thing right now.' Katya pairs 'can, am able to' with 'to say' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
- 'It is precisely these powders that create all the magic of the kitchen!' Notice how Russian puts 'powder' and 'kitchen' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'Please, taste our first hot dish.' Waiters serve the foaming molecular plates to the guests.
- 'Listen, this really is very tasty after all!' The young lieutenant takes a bite and marvels at the flavor.
- 'Eva, and your homemade cutlets are simply a miracle!' Meanwhile at Lyova's flat, Lyova praises the dinner Eva cooked.
- 'Thank you very much, I am very pleased right now!' Both women beam as their cooking wins over the room.
