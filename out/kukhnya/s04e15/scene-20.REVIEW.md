## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The main problem of men has always been very simple.' Denis reflects using a feminine past verb and instrumental adjective.
- 'We do not know at all what women want.' The verb to want governs the genitive case of the interrogative pronoun.
- 'Perhaps they simply like tormenting us for a long time.' A dative plural pronoun pairs with a reflexive verb and imperfective infinitive.
- 'Oh, Denis, how heartless I really am!' Seeing his bruised face, Katya softens immediately.
- 'Or they want to see us weak sometimes.' The verb to see governs an accusative pronoun and instrumental plural adjective.
- 'Then they can pity us and take care of us.' Two imperfective infinitives express tenderness and care.
- 'Daughter, it looks like your molecular show succeeded splendidly!' Nagiyev and Barinov congratulate Katya after the VIP guests rave about the thrill.
- 'Denis, well what do you need from me again?' At the bar after work, Katya tries to sound stern when Denis approaches.
- 'How many times must you repeat that there is nothing between us?' Later at a café, Denis spots his marked fifty-ruble note in his change.
- 'Take twenty thousand and hand her this fifty-ruble bill!' Denis bribes the bartender to give the marked bill to Katya in her change.
- 'Just make sure you do not tell anyone about our deal!' A negated imperative keeps his romantic trick a secret.
- 'I think women simply want real love.' Katya smiles in wonder when the marked fifty-ruble note returns to her hands.
