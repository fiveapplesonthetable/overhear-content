## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Leave some room for the cutlets and compote too.' In everyday Russian, 'leave' flows straight into 'compote' without extra linking words.
- 'Eva, and when did you find time to make all this?' Vera Ivanovna marvels at Eva's speed in the kitchen.
- 'Our Eva is very quick and skillful in general.' Barinov praises her nervously while signaling her to stay quiet.
- 'You have probably settled into Moscow completely by now?' In spoken Russian, the words stay in their normal statement order and Lyova's rising voice at the end turns the line into a question.
- 'Over two years in Moscow I have already gotten used to it!' Eva forgets Barinov's lie that she flew in from Vladivostok today.
- 'Wait, what do you mean over two years?' Vera Ivanovna catches the contradiction immediately.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, help me move the seedlings.' She calls Barinov onto the balcony for a private word.
- 'Of course, you know how much I adore seedlings!' Barinov follows her out and prepares to confess.
- 'Vera Ivanovna, I will explain everything to you right now.' In everyday Russian, 'Ivanovna' flows straight into 'will explain' without extra linking words.
- 'No need, I already figured everything out myself!' Vera Ivanovna interrupts him with a knowing smile.
- 'So, my Lyova has finally found himself a girlfriend!' She assumes Barinov was covering for her shy son Lyova.
- 'Yes, only Lyova asked to keep quiet for now!' Relieved, Barinov eagerly endorses her theory.
