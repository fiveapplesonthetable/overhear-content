## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Comrade Captain, why did you let him go just now?' Highlighting the comrade at once, Denis chooses "tovarishch" ("comrade") so the inspector gets the point right away.
- 'Turn on your head at last and think!' Emphasizing the head beside Denis, the inspector lands on "golovu" ("head", built on "golova") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'This young guy is just a small-time courier.' The inspector continues promptly during the talk, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Through him we will get to the big fish!' Focusing on the big fish with Denis, the inspector uses "krupnuyu" ("large, prominent", from "krupnyy") to make the point stick.
- 'Look, he went into this expensive restaurant.' Highlighting the restaurant on the spot, Denis lands on "restoran" ("restaurant") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Wait, and who else is that in the courtyard?' Denis checks openly if there is more going on behind the scenes.
- 'And here the serious suppliers have arrived here!' Bringing up the supplier right here, the inspector leans on "postavshchiki" ("supplier, purveyor", a form of "postavshchik") to give the remark its edge.
- 'We have hit the trail of a major syndicate.' Turning to a major syndicate in front of Denis, the inspector leans on "gruppirovki" ("syndicate") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'But we do not have direct evidence yet.' Denis speaks up openly in this moment, watching the inspector's face to see how the news lands.
- 'That means we need to quietly infiltrate inside.' The inspector follows up sharply to Denis and reaches for the Russian word "vnedritsya" ("to inside") right at the heart of the line.
- 'I propose naming our secret operation Hurricane!' Touching on the operation with the inspector, Denis reaches for the Russian word "sekretnuyu" ("secret", built from "sekretnyy") right at the center of the line.
- 'Let's go into the dining room, we will find out everything there now!' The inspector pushes back evenly watching Denis, grounding the moment in plain reality.
