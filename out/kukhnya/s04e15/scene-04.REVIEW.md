## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I must not haul such heavy loads at all!' Senya uses this key expression in earnest: "voobshche" means "at all", and it carries the main weight of what Senya is saying.
- 'My fingers will become rough from the crates.' Senya drops that point on cue: Russian phrases this around "u menya" (literally "by me") rather than "I have", a pattern you will hear in almost every scene.
- 'Miss, did we not happen to wake up together this morning?' Denis asks openly as Katya tries to explain the situation.
- 'Denis, listen to me carefully, calm down!' Katya centers this direct detail at once: "vnimatelno" means "carefully, attentively", and it carries the main weight of what Katya is saying.
- 'You have no chances with me at all.' Katya brings this answer frankly here: Russian says "u tebya" (literally "at you" or "by you") where English uses "you have" or "your", keeping the phrasing close and informal.
- 'What happened yesterday was just a fluke!' Katya jumps in candidly with a nod, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'A fluke, or maybe it is our destiny?' Denis chooses one key detail with Katya: "sluchaynost" means "accident", and it carries the main weight of what Denis is saying.
- 'So, you believe in fate then?' Katya hands over that line with warmth: the phrase "u nas" literally means "by us" or "at our place", the standard Russian way to talk about what a group or couple shares.
- 'Look, this is a ticket for a date with me.' Denis responds earnestly in their talk, shifting the whole mood of the room in a single beat.
- 'If fate wants it, this bill will return to you!' Denis features one practical detail in earnest: "vernyotsya" means "will return" (from "vernutsya"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Nastya, take this, please, and change this money.' Denis aims this line for Katya: dropping in "pozhaluysta" ("please") keeps the request polite on the surface even in a brisk exchange.
- 'What an interesting game you two have today!' Senya follows up evenly across the room, grounding the moment in plain reality.
