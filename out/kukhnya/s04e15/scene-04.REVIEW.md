- 'Miss, did we not happen to wake up together this morning?' Denis asks openly whether Katya really thought this through.
- 'Denis, listen to me carefully, calm down!' Katya speaks smoothly during the talk and brings in "vnimatelno" ("carefully, attentively") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'You have no chances with me at all.' Talking about the chances in this beat, Katya uses "u tebya" (literally "by you") where English says "you have" or "your".
- 'What happened yesterday was just a fluke!' Katya counters candidly with a sigh, watching Denis's face to see how the news lands.
- 'A fluke, or maybe it is our destiny?' Addressing the accident during the talk, Denis centers the remark on "sluchaynost" ("accident") without any extra fuss.
- 'So, you believe in fate then?' Mentioning the fate with Denis, Katya reaches for "u nas" ("by us, at our place") to talk about what they share.
- 'Look, this is a ticket for a date with me.' Denis cuts in earnestly in their talk, watching Katya's face to see how the news lands.
- 'If fate wants it, this bill will return to you!' Underscoring the return during the talk, Denis chooses "kupyura" ("return") so Katya gets the point right away.
- 'Nastya, take this, please, and change this money.' Focusing on the change on the spot, Denis centers the remark on "razmenyay" ("change") without any extra fuss.
- 'What an interesting game you two have today!' Senya responds evenly with a sigh, grounding the moment in plain reality.
