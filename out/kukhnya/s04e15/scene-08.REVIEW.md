## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'German Mikhailovich, may I come in for a minute?' Ainura knocks politely using the modal predicate and a directional dative phrase.
- 'Ainura, excuse me, but I am very busy right now.' German tries to avoid the conversation using a short masculine adjective.
- 'I want to talk seriously about us today.' The preposition about governs the prepositional plural pronoun.
- 'Ainura, I respect you as a valuable employee.' German uses a formal corporate phrase with an animate accusative noun.
- 'And as a person I find you very likable.' A dative experiencer pronoun pairs with a plural short adjective.
- 'Hush, do not say anything, just listen to me!' Ainura switches to the informal singular imperative and silences him.
- 'What happened at night was a big mistake.' She takes charge of the narrative with a feminine nominative predicate.
- 'Yesterday you gave me beautiful flowers on purpose.' Ainura reminds him of the rejected bouquet he handed her.
- 'You smiled tenderly at me the whole day!' The imperfective masculine past verb takes a dative recipient.
- 'But we cannot be together any longer.' She delivers the classic breakup line with an infinitive and adverb.
- 'Understand, you and I are overly different people!' The comitative subject phrase pairs with a plural adjective.
- 'Let me go, please, and do not hold on anymore!' When her sleeve catches on his button, she tells him not to hold her back.
