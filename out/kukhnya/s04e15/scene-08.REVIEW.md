## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'German Mikhailovich, may I come in for a minute?' Questioning whether may she comes in for a minute is how Ainura tests German's story. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Ainura, excuse me, but I am very busy right now.' Claiming that he is very busy right now sounds confident, even if Ainura has doubts.
- 'I want to talk seriously about us today.' By wanting to talk seriously about them today, Ainura keeps her side of the story grounded.
- 'Ainura, I respect you as a valuable employee.' Because he respects Ainura as a valuable employee, German treats the matter as settled. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'And as a person I find you very likable.' Since as a person he finds Ainura very likable, German does not waste words on excuses. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Hush, do not say anything, just listen to me!' Insisting that hush do not say anything just listen shows Ainura is not backing down.
- 'What happened at night was a big mistake.' Ainura reminds German that what happened at night was a big mistake before the moment passes. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Yesterday you gave me beautiful flowers on purpose.' Because yesterday German gave her beautiful flowers on purpose, Ainura treats the matter as settled. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'You smiled tenderly at me the whole day!' With the truth and office at stake, Ainura reacts without holding back.
- 'But we cannot be together any longer.' Even though they cannot be together any longer, Ainura keeps a steady face in front of German.
- 'Understand, you and I are overly different people!' Once German should understand German and she are overly different people, Ainura makes sure German hears every word. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Let me go, please, and do not hold on anymore!' Insisting that German should let her go please and do not hold on anymore shows Ainura is not backing down.
