## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Listen, my heart is beating like a drum!' After eating five portions, the lieutenant mistakes indigestion for an overdose.
- 'My fault, Comrade Colonel, permit me to report to you!' Spotting the colonel at a VIP table, the officers snap to attention.
- 'Now report in order, what are you doing here?' The colonel demands an explanation using an adverbial phrase meaning in order.
- 'Comrade Colonel, there is a dangerous den in this restaurant!' Lieutenant Sudnik launches into his absurd report with pride.
- 'During the day we tracked a small-time courier on the street.' He points toward Denis using an animate masculine accusative phrase.
- 'They bring strange powder here under the guise of a novelty.' The prepositional idiom means under the guise of.
- 'And this girl mixes it right into the food!' He accuses Katya, who stares at him in disbelief.
- 'Good Lord, what a foolish person you really are!' Katya cannot believe the police mistook agar-agar for narcotics.
- 'Strange, I myself visit this restaurant often.' The colonel frowns, noting that he dines at Claude Monet regularly.
- 'Who are you people at all and by what right?' German steps forward to defend his restaurant and staff.
- 'Comrade Colonel, we need to interrogate all the employees!' The eager lieutenant asks for permission to arrest the kitchen.
- 'And we will urgently send the white powders for testing!' Just then, Chef Barinov walks in and spots his old friend.
