- 'Quiet, they spread it out so thinly on purpose.' The inspector follows up steadily in this moment, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Dear guests, taste our new hot dish!' Katya holds firm right away with a sigh, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Well, tell me honestly, what do you feel right now?' The inspector grounds one practical expression at once: "chuvstvuyesh" means "feel" (from "chuvstvovat"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'It seems my heart is beating much faster!' The guest gives this statement to the inspector: the "-sya" ending on "kazhetsya" ("it seems, I think") turns the action inward or makes it happen between people naturally.
- 'And I really want more of this food!' The guest continues warmly during the talk, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'I literally cannot stop, it is so tasty!' The guest emphasizes this vivid term at once: "ostanovitsya" means "to stop", and it carries the main weight of what the guest is saying.
- 'Everything is clear, this is definitely a strong stimulant!' The inspector phrases this update beside Katya: notice how Russian needs no word for "am", "is", or "are" in the present — placing the words side by side says it all.

- 'Comrade Captain, what are we going to do now?' The guest emphasizes this telling expression in earnest: "tovarishch" means "comrade", and it carries the main weight of what the guest is saying.
- 'We are immediately calling our arrest team here!' The inspector fires back evenly across the room, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'And now we will show you our dessert!' Katya continues sharply to settle things, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
