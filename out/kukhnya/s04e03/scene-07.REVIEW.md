## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Can you imagine making me her sous-chef?' By checking whether Elena imagines making him, Barinov presses Elena for a straight answer.
- 'You broke her heart.' Over the work and the revenge, Elena answers Barinov without a second thought. Even in the middle of the drama, she keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'And this is her little feminine revenge.' Admitting that this is her little feminine revenge shows Elena being honest for a change. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Is she humiliating me because things didn't work out between us?' Here checking whether she humiliating him because things did not work out between them shows Barinov looking for the real truth.
- 'Then I will work in such a way that she kicks me out herself.' Once he will work in such a way that she kicks him out herself, Barinov can finally tell Elena what he means.
- 'Does that mean going back to prison?' Checking whether that mean going back to prison cuts straight through the tension between Elena and Barinov. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'You need her not to fire you, but to let you go.' Elena tells Barinov that Barinov needs her not to fire Barinov but to let Barinov goes without dressing it up.
- 'Of her own free will.' Since of her own free will, Elena does not waste words on excuses.
- 'You are not ironing, you are crumpling the jacket with the iron.' Mentioning that Barinov is not ironing Barinov is crumpling the jacket with the iron moves the talk in a new direction. Notice how she sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'What is wrong with you? Maybe a pill?' Wondering what wrong with Barinov is is how Elena puts Barinov on the spot.
- 'No, there are no pills for old age.' Because there are no pills for old age, Barinov treats the matter as settled.
- 'I have grown completely old.' Claiming that he has grown completely old sounds confident, even if Elena has doubts.
