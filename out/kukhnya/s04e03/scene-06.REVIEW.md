## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'How is the new head chef?' Wondering how the new head is cuts straight through the tension between Senya and German. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'He is sitting in the office sorting through papers.' Realizing that he is sitting in the office sorting through papers changes how Lyova handles the situation.
- 'He is not touching anything.' Being not touching anything moves the conversation forward without extra drama. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'Colleagues! A moment of your attention.' Around the surprise and truth, German makes his point loud and clear. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'You and I have known each other for one day.' Once Lyova and he has known each other for one day, German can finally tell Lyova what he means.
- 'And it would be worthwhile for us to get to know each other closer.' Adding that it would be worthwhile for them to get to know each other closer fills in the missing piece for Lyova.
- 'We could go to a sauna together.' Senya tells German that they could go to a sauna together without dressing it up. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'That is good too. But I have a better idea.' Since that is good, German does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Let's hold a skills evaluation today.' Admitting that they should hold a skills evaluation today shows German being honest for a change. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Believe me, nothing brings people closer like that.' Believing him nothing brings people closer like is how German lays out the situation for Lyova.
- 'Are there any objections? Well, that is wonderful.' Here asking whether there any objections shows German looking for the real truth.
- 'I jinxed it.' With the work and surprise in play, Lyova keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
