## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You are doing physical exercises without being used to them.' Talking about the exercise right here, Senya brings in "uprazhneniyami" ("exercise", from "uprazhneniye") as the key Russian word here.
- 'You have to prepare and warm up your body first.' Touching on the organism in front of Senya, Fedya leans on "nuzhno" ("it is needed, one has to"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'All right, hold on just a minute.' Senya steps in promptly around Fedya, so Fedya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Is there anything you would like to tell me?' Fedya continues briskly around Senya and centers the remark on "rasskazat" ("to tell") without any extra fuss.
- 'You never would have guessed on your own.' Holding firm calmly with Fedya, Senya drops in "voobshche" ("in general, altogether") to sum up the whole situation at once.
- 'Oh, it all started with that ridiculous gym bag.' Fedya replies boldly with a shrug, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'I had guests over at my place.' Highlighting the visitors at once, Senya reaches for the Russian word "gosti" ("visitors, guests", built from "gost") right at the center of the line.
- 'In the morning I was going to return it, and then I ran into you.' Turning quietly at once, the "-sya" ending on "sobralas" ("to get ready") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'It felt awkward to explain all of that to you on the spot.' Senya steps in candidly with a smile, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'You are not going to tell me that you play sports, are you?' Underscoring the sport to Senya, Fedya centers the thought on "zanimayetes" ("are busy with", from "zanimatsya") without wasting a word.
- 'Maybe the two of us should go jogging together?' Reacting dryly to Fedya, Senya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Fedya purely through rising pitch.
- 'I should have told you the whole truth right away.' Fedya speaks frankly without any doubt, making his position completely clear.
- 'Let us do it first thing tomorrow.' Senya opens up firmly in the exchange and reaches for the Russian word "davayte" ("let's", built from "davat") right at the center of the line.
