## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Lyova, step in for a minute.' Claiming that Kostya should step in for a minute sounds confident, even if Kostya has doubts.
- 'Just do not ask any questions.' Kostya reminds Barinov that just do not ask any questions before the moment passes.
- 'Wait, what do you need that for?' Wondering what Barinov needs cuts straight through the tension between Lyova and Barinov. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'That is an interesting thought, but they do not have enough money.' Explaining that that is an interesting thought but they do not have enough money lays the cards on the table for Kostya.
- 'Get out of here.' Pointing out that Barinov should get out of here gives Barinov a sobering reality check.
- 'Lyova, I have decided to take up sports.' Once he has decided to take up sports, Lyova can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'For my health.' Over the shift and the conversation, Barinov answers Kostya without a second thought.
- 'Oh, that is great.' Kostya explains that that is great, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'Wait, where did my money go?' Here wondering where his money go shows Lyova looking for the real truth. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'What money, Lyova?' Around the conversation and morning, Barinov wants to know where Kostya really stands.
- 'You are not in church, nobody is going to cheat you.' Even though Barinov is not in church nobody is going to cheat Barinov, Kostya keeps a steady face in front of Barinov. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Lyova? You are not in church, right?' Questioning whether Barinov is not in church right is how Lyova tests Barinov's story. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I will pay you back later.' Saying that he will pay Kostya back later is how Barinov sets the tone with Kostya.
