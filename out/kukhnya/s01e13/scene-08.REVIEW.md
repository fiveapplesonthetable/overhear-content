## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Kostya, I need to tell you something.' Nastya looks worried.
- 'I acted wrongly.' She admits her mistake.
- 'Nastya, did you change your mind about tonight?' Kostya is worried about their date.
- 'That's not what I'm talking about.' Nastya shakes her head.
- 'It was me who broke Viktor Petrovich's mirror.' She finally confesses.
- 'And now they are fighting because of me.' She feels terrible about the consequences.
- 'Wow!' Kostya is surprised.
- 'And what do we do now?' he asks.
- 'So, is everything still on for tonight?' He immediately checks if their date is safe.
- 'Kostya!' Nastya is offended.
- 'How could you?' She can't believe his priorities.
- 'You only think about one thing!' She scolds him for being insensitive.
