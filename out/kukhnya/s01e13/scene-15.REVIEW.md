## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, why are you hiding here again?' Victoria discovers him lounging in the stockroom. She demands to know why he is slacking off.
- 'I was simply waiting for you here.' Max gives a smooth reply. He claims he was simply anticipating her arrival.
- 'What an unserious child you are!' Vika scolds his immature behavior. She compares him to a frivolous kid.
- 'Then punish me strictly for that!' Max turns her reprimand into a flirtatious dare. He playfully invites discipline.
- 'Max, stop joking, I am serious.' Victoria attempts to maintain professionalism. She asks him to drop the jokes.
- 'You are young, you want fun.' She reminds him of their generational divide. Vika implies he only cares about entertainment.
- 'At work one must be an adult.' Victoria insists on responsible workplace conduct. She lectures him on acting maturely on the clock.
- 'Vika, why are you so worried?' Max asks why she lets small worries trouble her. He thinks she is overthinking things.
- 'The age difference is not important.' He dismisses her concerns about age barriers. Max believes the years between them mean nothing.
- 'It seems we are making a mistake.' Vika voices her doubts about their relationship. She worries this is all a bad idea.
- 'Beside me you will forget all worries.' Max offers a romantic promise. He assures her that being with him will chase away her fears.
- 'Max, you are an incorrigible romantic.' Victoria melts with a smile despite her hesitation. She calls out his relentless charm.
- 'Because I love you very much, Vika.' Max delivers an earnest confession. He plainly states his deep affection for her.
