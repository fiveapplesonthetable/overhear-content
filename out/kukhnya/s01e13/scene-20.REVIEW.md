## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good morning, Dmitry Vladimirovich.' Vika offers a polite, standard greeting. She addresses Nagiyev formally.
- 'I would say it is afternoon, Vika.' Nagiyev corrects her with a hint of sarcasm. He points out it is already later in the day.
- 'Why didn't you fly out to the exhibition?' He interrogates her about her missed trip. Nagiyev asks why she did not depart as planned.
- 'It was cancelled.' Vika sticks to her fabricated story. She casually claims the event was called off.
- 'I called my contacts.' She invents a source for her information. Vika says she checked with her own contacts.
- 'Some businessman called you, right?' Nagiyev reveals he knows more than she thinks. He mentions the mysterious caller.
- 'Who?' Vika plays dumb. she asks for clarification on who he means.
- 'Yeah, he called.' She quickly agrees to maintain the lie. Vika confirms she received a call.
- 'Let's see.' Nagiyev prepares to reveal his trump card. He playfully promises to check the details.
- 'Alexander Del Piero.' He reads out the ridiculous fake name Max used. Nagiyev exposes the obvious lie.
- 'He said the exhibition was cancelled.' Nagiyev repeats the lie one last time. She insists the man delivered the cancellation news.
- 'Was it a call from a Moscow number?' Nagiyev traps her with logic. He points out the absurdity of a local call for an international event.
- 'Yeah, here is the number.' He presents the undeniable proof. Nagiyev shows her the local caller ID on the phone.
