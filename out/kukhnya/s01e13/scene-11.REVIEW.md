## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I handled one of your trash bins.' -- The stubborn Chef refuses to surrender the territory.
- 'Here is a document that proves it.' -- Elena presents official papers to win the argument.
- 'This is the plan from the district office.' -- She relies on local government regulations.
- 'The bins must stand exactly in this place.' -- The rival chef points at the pavement.
- 'Are there any more questions for me?' -- Elena smiles with smug satisfaction at him.
- 'Timur? You decided to supply her with food?' -- Viktor Petrovich notices his supplier nearby.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, my respects.' -- The supplier greets the angry chef nervously.
- 'Keep in mind, I will not return the debt.' -- Barinov immediately threatens his business partner.
- 'Excuse me, our cooperation has come to an end.' -- Timur politely declines Elena's business offer.
- 'I cannot contradict an older man.' -- He explains his respect for the old chef.
- 'Especially since you owe me fifteen thousand.' -- He reminds Barinov about his massive debt.
- 'Ten.' -- The Chef immediately corrects the financial numbers.
