## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Let's have you walk from here.' Vika suggests they separate before reaching the restaurant. She wants him to walk the rest of the way.
- 'We do not need to appear at work together.' She makes her priority clear. Arriving together is simply out of the question.
- 'You are absolutely right, Vika.' Max pretends to agree completely with her concern. He nods along with her logic.
- 'It could cause damage to my reputation.' He jokingly twists her words about professionalism. Max claims his own image is on the line.
- 'Max, are you sure that this is right?' Vika double-checks his attitude. She wants to make sure he is taking this seriously.
- 'We will see.' Max gives a non-committal answer. He just says they will see how it goes.
- 'I do not want extra questions from the boss.' Vika wants to avoid any unnecessary office drama. The boss is already hard enough to deal with.
- 'No one will find out about our secret romance.' Max promises to keep their special relationship hidden. He playfully calls it their secret romance.
- 'In this restaurant people really love gossip.' Vika knows how quickly rumors spread among the staff. She knows the kitchen loves to talk.
- 'I will keep quiet and just cook food.' Max assures her he will focus entirely on his duties. He promises to just put his head down and cook.
- 'Then you go first, and I will go later.' She decides they must enter the building at different times. Vika plans out their staggered arrival.
- 'Good, see you in the kitchen in ten minutes.' Max accepts the clever plan and begins his short walk. He gives her a precise time for their reunion.
