## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The boss is arguing with her again.' -- Nastya looks out the window in absolute terror.
- 'Come on, Nastya, everything will work out.' -- The bartender tries to calm his panicked girlfriend.
- 'Things won't work out for us today either?' -- She assumes their romantic evening is totally ruined.
- 'No, Nastya, you simply promised.' -- Kostya refuses to accept another cancelled dinner date.
- 'Do you want me to go to the boss?' -- He suggests a very dangerous rescue plan.
- 'I will say that I broke the mirror.' -- He offers to take the terrible blame himself.
- 'Do not do it, he will fire you.' -- Nastya begs him to protect his own job.
- 'I said it, which means I will go.' -- The determined bartender ignores her desperate warnings.
- 'Kostik, wait, do not go.' -- She desperately tries to stop him from confessing.
- 'Boss, it was me who broke your mirror.' -- He boldly delivers the fake confession to Barinov.
- 'No, do not believe him.' -- Nastya suddenly runs up to interrupt his lie.
- 'I broke your mirror.' -- She finally admits her terrible accident to the Chef.
- 'He just wants to cover for me.' -- She explains why her boyfriend was lying previously.
