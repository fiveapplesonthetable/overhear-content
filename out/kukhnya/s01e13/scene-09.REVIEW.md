## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why didn't you tell me you were leaving?' Max stops Vika in the hallway.
- 'Let's talk later.' Vika is not in the mood.
- 'No, we're talking now!' Max insists.
- 'Let me explain what's going on.' He wants to apologize.
- 'You've been avoiding me all day!' Vika points out his hypocrisy.
- 'I need to think.' She doesn't want to listen right now.
- 'We'll talk in three days when I get back.' She says she is flying to Milan.
- The next morning, Max confidently walks into her office. 'Vika, good morning!'
- 'I brought you coffee.' He hopes this will fix things.
- 'I hate you!' Vika glares at him.
- 'You don't like coffee?' Max is confused.
- 'What the hell coffee!' She is furious.
- 'I missed the Milan trip because of you!' She reveals that their argument made her miss her flight.
