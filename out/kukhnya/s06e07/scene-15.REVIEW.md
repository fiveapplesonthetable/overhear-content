## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Yes, I remember, you are leaving for the dacha.' Noting that he remembers the doctor is leaving for the dacha keeps Lyova's side of the story simple and clear. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'In exactly an hour I will already be home.' Pointing out that in exactly an hour he will already be home gives the doctor a sobering reality check.
