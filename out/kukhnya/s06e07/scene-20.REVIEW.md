- 'Well alright, let me tell her everything.' Claiming that alright let him tell her everything sounds confident, even if Gulya has doubts.
- 'Hello, respected Elena Pavlovna.' With the conversation and point in play, Lyova keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'I really did go take the medical tests in the chef's place.' Lyova mentions that he really did go take the medical tests in the place so Gulya sees what is at stake.
- 'Forgive us, please, for this deception.' Forgiving them please for this deception is how Lyova lays out the situation for Gulya.
- 'Yes, all the best, goodbye.' With the work and moment in play, Lyova keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'So you did not go to the hospital because of an illness?' Asking whether Lyova did not go to the hospital because of an illness puts Lyova right on the defensive. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'No, on the contrary, my test results are excellent!' Saying that on the contrary his test results are excellent is how Lyova takes charge of the moment.
- 'And why did you suddenly ask about that?' By asking why Gulya suddenly ask, Lyova presses Gulya for a straight answer. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Oh no, nothing, everything is very good.' Once nothing everything is very good, Gulya can finally tell Lyova what she means.
- 'What a strange evening it has turned out to be today!' Insisting that what a strange evening it has turned out to be today shows Lyova is not backing down.
