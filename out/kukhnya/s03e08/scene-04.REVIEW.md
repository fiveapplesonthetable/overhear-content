## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dima, you are the owner, you decide here.' Bringing up the owner before Nagiyev, Barinov leans on "khozyain" ("owner") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'It is very good that you remember that.' Nagiyev counters pointedly with a smile, before Barinov can change the subject.
- 'Dima, I have one small request.' Barinov follows up lightly around Nagiyev, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'If you are going to ask for money, I will not give any.' Discussing the money during the talk, Nagiyev lands on "dengi" ("money") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Today our favorite club Spartak is playing.' Stressing how favourite things are in the exchange, Barinov leans on "lyubimyy" ("favourite") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'And Elena is taking me to a meeting with her friends.' Barinov speaks openly on the spot, before Nagiyev can change the subject.
- 'And what, should I call them so they do not start without you?' Nagiyev counters evenly at once and lands on "nachinali" ("started", built on "nachinat") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'No, I will tell her that you are not letting me go.' Barinov continues right away with a shrug, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Because we have an important banquet today.' Barinov follows up plainly across the room, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'And you will simply confirm that in front of her.' Barinov counters quietly right here and leans on "podtverdish" ("will confirm", a form of "podtverdit") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Tell me honestly, who are you lying to right now?' Speaking warmly to Barinov, Nagiyev keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Barinov purely through rising pitch.
- 'That depends on how our game ends.' Barinov jumps in openly without a pause, letting the remark speak for itself.
