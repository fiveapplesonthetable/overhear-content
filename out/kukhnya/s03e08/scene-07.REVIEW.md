## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Some great actor he turns out to be!' Once some great actor he turns out to be, Barinov makes sure Elena hears every word.
- 'Real actors act in good cinema.' With the shift and revenge in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'And he only looks funny on television.' Explaining that he only looks funny on television lays the cards on the table for Elena.
- 'And besides, that clown acts very badly.' Barinov mentions that besides that clown acts very badly so Elena sees what is at stake.
- 'He is simply ashamed to look people in the eye.' Because he is simply ashamed to look people in the eye, Barinov treats the matter as settled.
- 'Vitya, I have thought it over well here.' Here having thought it over well here sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'After all, we have many banquets, and we have only one of you.' Nagiyev explains that after all they have many banquets and they have only one of Barinov, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'So rest calmly today.' Telling Barinov to rest calmly today moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'Thank you very much, Dmitry Vladimirovich!' With the plan and truth at stake, Elena reacts without holding back. Notice how she sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Not at all, dear Elena Pavlovna.' Over the conversation and the revenge, Nagiyev answers Barinov without a second thought.
- 'Dima, what, did you get offended at me?' Wondering what Elena gets offended at him cuts straight through the tension between Barinov and Elena. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'What, is it hard to tell, I acted poorly, after all I am just a clown.' Saying that what is it hard to tell he acted poorly after all he is just a clown is how Nagiyev sets the tone with Barinov.
