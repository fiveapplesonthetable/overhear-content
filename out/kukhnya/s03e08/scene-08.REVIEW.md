## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, why did you put that on right now?' Opening up boldly to Max, Ainura puts the question word "zachem" right at the start so Max hears the focus immediately.
- 'Well our chef took me back into the kitchen.' Max speaks sharply across the room, before Barinov can change the subject.
- 'Now I am a cook here again.' Emphasizing a cook in the exchange, Max brings in "povar" ("chef, cook") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'What cook, the chef said that you are my assistant.' Ainura speaks evenly across the room, leaving no doubt about where she stands.
- 'Wait, so I am a cleaner now?' Max prompts firmly while looking straight at Ainura here.
- 'I am the cleaner, and you are the cleaner's assistant.' Bringing up the assistant at once, Ainura leans on "pomoshchnik" ("assistant") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'And what do you want, they told me to take you, I took you.' Barinov replies boldly during the talk, before Ainura can change the subject.
- 'And what you will work as in my kitchen is up to me to decide.' Barinov speaks up right away around Ainura, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Or do you not want to be a cleaner?' Talking about a cleaner with Max, Barinov brings in "uborshchikom" ("cleaner, janitor", from "uborshchik") as the key Russian word here.
- 'No, I do not want to at all.' Holding firm plainly to Barinov, Max adds "sovsem" ("completely, at all") so the statement leaves zero room for doubt.
- 'Well why did you not say so right away, I am promoting you!' Barinov speaks warmly hearing Max, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'You will be a food delivery courier, that is all, the conversation is over.' Discussing the courier in this beat, Barinov chooses "kuryerom" ("courier", from the root "kuryer") so Max hears the exact meaning.
