## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Our team won, come on, pour quickly!' Nagiyev insists that their team won come on pour quickly, leaving Barinov no room to argue. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Dima, our team lost today, alas.' Because their team lost today alas, Nagiyev treats the matter as settled.
- 'Alright, well what are you starting!' Barinov declares that Alright well what are Nagiyev starting as the tension in the room peaks.
- 'Well if we lost today, we lost.' Barinov mentions that if they lost today they lost so Nagiyev sees what is at stake.
- 'And what if you have a draw today?' Nagiyev asks whether what if Barinov has a draw today, refusing to let the point slide. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'I do not want to hear anything, pour again!' Once he does not want to hear anything pour again, Nagiyev makes sure Barinov hears every word.
- 'So you had better go out in public with your beloved woman.' Nagiyev explains that Barinov had better go out in public with Barinov's beloved woman, keeping his voice calm and direct. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'At least your liver will be healthy.' Realizing that at least Barinov's liver will be healthy changes how Nagiyev handles the situation. That little word "zato" means "on the bright side", flipping a complaint into an advantage.
- 'After all, I am bringing Max back only for your sake, Dima.' Noting that after all he is bringing Max back only for Nagiyev's sake Dima keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'I am going against all my principles.' Because he is going against all his principles, Barinov treats the matter as settled.
- 'That is it, we agreed, your condition is accepted.' Admitting that that is it they agreed Barinov's condition is accepted shows Nagiyev being honest for a change. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Max, today you are returning to the kitchen.' Saying that today Nagiyev is returning to the kitchen is how Barinov sets the tone with Nagiyev.
