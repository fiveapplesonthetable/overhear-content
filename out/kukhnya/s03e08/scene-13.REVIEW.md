## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Wait, did you move to a new apartment?' Checking whether Nagiyev moves to a new apartment cuts straight through the tension between Max and Nagiyev.
- 'No, I just came to visit a friend.' Because she just came to visit a friend, Vika treats the matter as settled.
- 'Darling, for some reason I already started missing you.' Explaining that for some reason he already started missing Max lays the cards on the table for Max.
- 'Oh, hi, good job dropping by, we really missed you!' Over the apartment and the job, Nagiyev lets his real feelings show.
- 'Everything is clear with you, so you came for a visit.' Claiming that everything is clear with Nagiyev so Nagiyev came for a visit sounds confident, even if Nagiyev has doubts. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Dmitry Vladimirovich, may I speak with Victoria Sergeyevna about work?' Asking whether may he speaks with Victoria Sergeyevna about work puts Nagiyev right on the defensive. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Dima, tell me, is everything alright with you out there?' Wondering if tell him is everything alright with Max out there shows Nagiyev caught off guard.
- 'Max, maybe you really should quit?' Questioning whether maybe Max really should quit is how Vika tests Max's story. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Yes, it seems that way will be better for everyone.' Noting that it seems that way will be better for everyone keeps Max's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'And what are you going to do now?' Here asking what Max going to do now is shows Vika looking for the real truth.
- 'They already offered me a position at a new restaurant.' Over the moment and the job, Max answers Nagiyev without a second thought.
- 'Then from the bottom of my heart I wish you luck.' Saying that from the bottom of her heart she wishes Max luck is how Vika sets the tone with Max. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'And I do not wish you luck, after all you have achieved everything anyway.' Once he does not wish Nagiyev luck after all Nagiyev has achieved everything anyway, Max can finally tell Nagiyev what he means.
