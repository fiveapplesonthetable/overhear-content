## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, since we kissed, that means we are dating.' Nagiyev reminds Vika that since they kissed that means they are dating before the moment passes.
- 'Hooray, I propose we celebrate this!' With the work and restaurant at stake, Nagiyev reacts without holding back. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Let us drink some good champagne.' Realizing that they should drink some good champagne changes how Nagiyev handles the situation.
- 'I bought out the second half of the restaurant from Kristina.' Nagiyev mentions that he bought out the second half of the restaurant from Kristina so Vika sees what is at stake.
- 'Bring us some champagne, please.' Mentioning that Vika should bring them some champagne please moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Dmitry Vladimirovich, actually I am at work right now.' By being at work right now, Vika keeps her side of the story grounded.
- 'Not anymore, you have a day off today starting this minute.' Nagiyev explains that not anymore Vika has a day off today starting this minute, keeping his voice calm and direct. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Right now we are driving calmly to your home.' Realizing that right now they are driving calmly to Vika's home changes how Nagiyev handles the situation. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Go get yourself ready at home.' By telling Vika to go get themselves ready at home, Nagiyev keeps his side of the story grounded.
- 'And I will drop by your place in about two hours.' Even though he will drop by Vika's place in about two hours, Nagiyev keeps a steady face in front of Vika. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'After all, you and I are grown-up people.' Admitting that after all Vika and he are grown up people shows Nagiyev being honest for a change. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Wait, do I really have a day off today?' Here asking whether she really has a day off today shows Vika looking for the real truth.
