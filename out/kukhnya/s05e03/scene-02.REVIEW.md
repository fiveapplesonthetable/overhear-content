## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Did you remember that you have a daughter?' Pointing to a daughter in the exchange, Barinov puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Eleonora hears the focus immediately.
- 'I always remembered her.' Eleonora speaks up frankly around Barinov, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'And did you ever think of me?' Eleonora proceeds candidly with Barinov and reaches for the Russian word "vspominal" ("remembered", built from "vspominat") right at the center of the line.
- 'I thought of you constantly.' Stepping in warmly on the spot, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 4 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'There were difficult moments in life.' Barinov chimes in at once facing Eleonora, so Eleonora knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'One remembers you, and things do not seem so bad.' Barinov weighs in openly in the exchange and lands on "vspomnish" ("will remember", built on "vspomnit") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'I will go, bite each other without me.' Leaning in promptly on the spot, the "-sya" ending on "kusaytes" ("bite each other") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'Wait, you did not really tell me anything.' Eleonora chimes in lightly without any doubt, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Everything is fine with her at the new place.' Mentioning the order with Eleonora, Barinov lands on "poryadke" ("order", built on "poryadok") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'You can go stroll around for another five years.' Barinov speaks up firmly right here and chooses "yeshchyo" ("more, still") so Katya gets the point right away.
- 'Vitya, meet her right now.' Katya counters quietly facing Barinov, leaving no doubt about where she stands.
