- 'I will tell you one secret right now.' Realizing that she will tell Denis one secret right now changes how Ainura handles the situation.
- 'Back home we sometimes steal the bride.' Around the plan and money, Ainura sets a calm, everyday tone with Denis. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.

- 'And later you save money for the wedding together.' Adding that later Denis saves money for the wedding together fills in the missing piece for Denis.
- 'My husband also stole me when I was young.' Ainura tells Denis that her husband also stole her when she was young without dressing it up.
- 'Kostya, tell me, why did you call us?' With the truth and money hanging in the air, Denis puts Ainura on the spot. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'What is this strange sheet of paper?' Wondering what this strange sheet of paper is cuts straight through the tension between Denis and Ainura.
- 'Katya left this at the bar today.' Lyova reminds Ainura that Katya left this at the bar today before the moment passes. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
