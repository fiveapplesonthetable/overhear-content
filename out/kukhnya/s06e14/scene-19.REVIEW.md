## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Now I pronounce you husband and wife.' With the order and kitchen in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Do not be afraid, darling, come quickly with me.' Even though do not be afraid darling come quickly with him, Barinov keeps a steady face in front of the guest.
- 'Katya, Nastya, what are you doing here?' With the order and kitchen hanging in the air, Senya puts Barinov on the spot. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Show us the path of how to live on.' Showing them the path of how to live is how the guest lays out the situation for Barinov.
- 'My children, go calmly to your homeland.' With the order and work in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Wait for me there and cultivate the land.' Around the order and hotel, Barinov sets a calm, everyday tone with the guest.
- 'We thank you for your wisdom, great prophet!' Here thanking Barinov for Barinov's wisdom great prophet lets the guest's real feelings break through.
- 'Chef, and what should we do now?' By asking what they do now should, Senya presses Barinov for a straight answer. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'And you need to quickly go work!' Barinov declares that the guest needs to quickly go work as the tension in the room peaks. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'Everyone work immediately in our kitchen!' With the kitchen and hotel at stake, Barinov reacts without holding back.
- 'And this month everyone is without a bonus!' Insisting that this month everyone is without a bonus shows Barinov is not backing down. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Understood, chef, we are already going to the stoves.' Explaining that understood they are already going to the stoves lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
