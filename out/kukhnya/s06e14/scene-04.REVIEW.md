## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Did you see, she did not even touch it!' Fedya counters plainly in front of Senya and puts the weight on "tronula" ("to touch", from "tronut") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'After all, a dog never eats dog.' Talking about a dog in the exchange, Fedya pairs the negative word with "ne" ("not"), since Russian naturally doubles its negatives for emphasis.
- 'Chef, imagine what strange meat they brought.' Talking about the meat with Barinov, Senya makes it clear to Barinov that he means every word.
- 'Here, look at this dog yourself.' Discussing the dog in front of Barinov, Senya reaches for the Russian word "sobaku" ("dog", built from "sobaka") right at the center of the line.
- 'Immediately remove the dog from my kitchen!' Turning to his kitchen on the spot, Barinov leans on "nemedlenno" ("immediately") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Quickly take her out to the courtyard, fools!' Barinov replies right away facing Senya, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'What kind of foolish stereotypes do you have?' Focusing on the stereotypes on the spot, Barinov chooses "stereotipy" ("stereotypes", from the root "stereotip") so Senya hears the exact meaning.
- 'I see for sure that this is beef.' Pointing to the beef around Senya, Barinov centers the remark on "govyadina" ("beef") without any extra fuss.
- 'Chef, maybe we should bury this?' Senya challenges earnestly about what Barinov just brought up.
- 'Or maybe I should bury all of you?' Barinov opens up decisively on the spot and leans on "pokhoronit" ("to bury") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'No, chef, we will not do it anymore.' Fedya reacts evenly without holding back, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Go away, I will cook everything myself today.' Barinov replies frankly with a sigh, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
