## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I do not know, this is not mine.' Katya follows up briskly beside Senya, testing Senya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Who else needs to choose between you two?' Katya chimes in quietly at once and uses "yeshchyo" ("more, still") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Maybe Kostya is choosing a best friend for himself.' Highlighting how best things are during the talk, Katya leans on "luchshego" ("best", a form of "luchshiy") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Chef, you were completely right today.' Senya answers lightly without a pause, watching Barinov's face to see how the news lands.
- 'That meat was definitely not a dog.' Turning to a dog beside Barinov, Senya reaches for the Russian word "sobaka" ("dog") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Well finally you turned on your brains!' Barinov speaks up lightly with a smile, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'Wait, are you making fun of me?' Barinov presses eagerly how Senya expects that to work in practice.
- 'No, chef, we read it on the internet.' Addressing the internet in the exchange, Senya reaches for the Russian word "internete" ("internet", built from "internet") right at the center of the line.
- 'If my wife finds out, I am done for.' Bringing up the end in this beat, the "-sya" ending on "konets" ("end") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'And it also says here about the prophet.' Senya chimes in promptly around Barinov, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'His spirit is reborn from the sacred flame.' Making clear how sacred things are before Barinov, Senya centers the thought on "svyashchennogo" ("sacred, holy", from "svyashchennyy") without wasting a word.
- 'Get out of here while I am still in a good mood!' Underscoring how good he is on the spot, Barinov puts the emphasis on "dobryy" ("good") in the middle of the talk.
