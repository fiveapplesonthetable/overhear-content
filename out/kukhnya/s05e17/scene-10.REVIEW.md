## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Here, quietly drive his car away.' Focusing on the car during the talk, Eleonora brings in "mashinu" ("car", from "mashina") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Leave it a couple of blocks away over there.' Pointing to the city blocks in this beat, Eleonora chooses "kvartalov" ("city blocks", from the root "kvartal") so Nikita hears the exact meaning.
- 'Later they will calmly find it.' Eleonora chimes in steadily hearing Nikita, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'And what if Dmitry Vladimirovich notices?' Nikita speaks up decisively right here and reaches for the Russian word "zametit" ("notices") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Do not worry, he will not need the car anytime soon.' Focusing on the car at once, the "-sya" ending on "ponadobitsya" ("to be needed") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'Thank you very much, Eleonora Andreyevna.' Meeting Eleonora simply without hesitation, Nikita says "spasibo" ("thank you"), the everyday word for "thank you", which grew out of the old blessing "spasi Bog" ("God save you").
- 'From now on court Katya without crime.' Touching on the crime with Nikita, Eleonora leans on "kriminala" ("crime", a form of "kriminal") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Senechka, open the door for me immediately!' Underscoring the door in the exchange, Nikita centers the remark on "nemedlenno" ("immediately") without any extra fuss.
- 'Chef said that you will sober up first.' Senya chimes in evenly without any doubt, testing Nikita's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Senechka, maybe you and I can make a deal?' Nikita counters vividly before Senya and brings in "dogovorimsya" ("we'll arrange", from "dogovoritsya") as the key Russian word here.
- 'I will not let you out even for money.' Senya jumps in lightly in their talk, making his position completely clear.
- 'Sit quietly in the office.' Senya speaks up wryly beside Nikita, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
