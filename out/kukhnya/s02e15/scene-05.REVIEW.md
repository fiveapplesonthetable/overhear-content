## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I do not understand, why do you need this?' Wondering if he does not understand why do Sasha needs this shows Max caught off guard. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'I just knew for a long time what she wanted.' Explaining that she just knew for a long time what she wanted lays the cards on the table for Max.
- 'Since she chose you instead of me.' Over the promise and the plan, Max answers Sasha without a second thought.
- 'Hold on a second.' Once Max should hold on a second, Sasha can finally tell Max what she means.
- 'I do not know.' Explaining that he does not know lays the cards on the table for Sasha.
- 'What you are scheming.' Sasha explains that what Max is scheming, keeping her voice calm and direct.
- 'It is not going to work.' Realizing that it is not going to work changes how Max handles the situation. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'I will tell Sasha to give them back to you.' Noting that she will tell Sasha to give them back to Max keeps Sasha's side of the story simple and clear. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Max, you are amazing.' Even though Sasha is amazing, Max keeps a steady face in front of Sasha.
- 'I do not have a boyfriend like that.' Admitting that she does not have a boyfriend like shows Sasha being honest for a change. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Well, I do.' Around the kitchen and promise, Max sets a calm, everyday tone with Sasha. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Did you want to say something to me?' Asking whether Max wants to say something cuts straight through the tension between Sasha and Max.
- 'I will pay you back for them tomorrow.' Adding that he will pay Sasha back for them tomorrow fills in the missing piece for Sasha.
