## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What is this apple core doing here?' Here asking what this apple core doing here is shows Max looking for the real truth.
- 'Lavrov was going around asking everyone for money earlier.' Explaining that Lavrov was going around asking everyone for money earlier lays the cards on the table for Max.
- 'Who here is Lavrov?' Asking whether who here is Lavrov puts Max right on the defensive. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'I am. Right here.' Over the shift and the police, Max answers Nagiyev without a second thought.
- 'So, kid, you started stealing from the rich?' Questioning whether kid Max started stealing from the rich is how Vika tests Max's story.
- 'Which is all the proof we need.' Nagiyev explains that which is all the proof they need, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'I did not take your money.' Realizing that he did not take Nagiyev's money changes how Max handles the situation.
- 'Vika, call the police.' Calling the police is how Vika lays out the situation for Max.
- 'I did not take the money!' Nagiyev declares that he did not take the money as the tension in the room peaks.
- 'Maybe we can handle this without the police?' Questioning whether maybe they can handle this without the police is how Max tests Nagiyev's story.
- 'And why is that?' With the truth and call hanging in the air, Vika puts Max on the spot. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Let us just fire him on the spot.' Saying that they should just fire him on the spot is how Nagiyev sets the tone with Max. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Go on, pack your things.' Telling Nagiyev to go on pack Nagiyev's things moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
