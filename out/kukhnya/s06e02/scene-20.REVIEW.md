## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What happened, do we have problems?' Touching on the problems at once, Barinov reaches for "u nas" ("by us, at our place") to talk about what they share.
- 'Vitya, you do not have to resign at all.' Eleonora answers honestly with Barinov, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Did you feel sorry for me?' Barinov follows up plainly at once and puts the weight on "pozhalela" ("felt sorry for", from "pozhalet") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'I felt sorry for myself, if I fire you Katya will nag me to death.' Eleonora continues honestly with a smile, without trying to dress up how she really feels.
- 'Stay at the restaurant.' Eleonora replies plainly on the spot, leaving no doubt about where she stands.
- 'Stay with my menu?' Talking about his menu to Eleonora, Barinov reaches for the Russian word "ostatsya" ("to remain, to be left") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Only let's do this without conditions, alright?' Dwelling on the conditions at once, Eleonora starts with "davay" ("come on, let's") to invite Barinov right into the action.
- 'Elya, I am in my fifties.' Barinov continues frankly hearing Eleonora, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'This menu is my last chance to get a star.' Pointing out how last things are in the exchange, Barinov brings in "posledniy" ("last") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Either I stay with my menu, or both my menu and I leave.' Emphasizing his menu beside Eleonora, Barinov lands on "menyu" ("menu") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Alright, stay.' Eleonora replies evenly in their talk, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'On Saturday we are going to meet my mom.' Emphasizing the mum in this beat, Barinov centers the remark on "znakomitsya" ("get acquainted") without any extra fuss.
