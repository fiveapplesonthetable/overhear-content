## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, did you kiss Gulya?' Kostya quizzes wryly while looking straight at Lyova here.
- 'Even you already know.' Lyova reacts vividly to Kostya and brings in "uzhe" ("already, now") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Do you know how Nastya and I kissed for the first time?' Pressing on warmly during the talk, the "-sya" ending on "potselovalis" ("kissed") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'We were skating on rollerblades.' Addressing the rollerblades watching Lyova, Kostya lets Lyova see exactly where things stand.
- 'I caught her and dipped her.' Kostya replies decisively at once and puts the weight on "naklonil" ("dipped, leaned", from "naklonit") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'I understood everything, I am ready.' Describing how ready he is around Kostya, Lyova uses "gotov" ("ready", from "gotovyy") to make the point stick.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, hello.' Kostya simply sends this formal greeting in one quick breath using "zdravstvuyte" ("hello") — the polite Russian greeting that literally wishes the other person good health.
- 'This is the show's producer, we are changing the concept of our program.' Focusing on the concept in this beat, Kostya centers the thought on "kontseptsiyu" ("concept", from "kontseptsiya") without wasting a word.
- 'And now we will film in real restaurants.' Touching on the restaurant with Barinov, Kostya lands on "restoranakh" ("restaurant", built on "restoran") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Why such an honor all of a sudden?' Barinov challenges promptly how Kostya expects that to work in practice.
- 'Rumors reached us that you introduced a new auteur menu.' Bringing up the rumors in front of Barinov, Kostya puts the weight on "avtorskoye" ("auteur, signature", from "avtorskiy") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'Who, if not you, is the best chef in the country?' Confirming how best things are at once, Kostya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
