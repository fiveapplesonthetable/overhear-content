## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good job, Vitka.' "Ty molodets" is "you're a fine fellow", the everyday praise. "Vit'ka" is an overfamiliar short form of "Viktor", and Max says "ty" to his boss in his sleep.
- 'Vityushenka, my Oguzok.' "Vityushen'ka" is a doting pet form of "Viktor", with extra syllables for tenderness. Max then flips the chef's nickname for him back onto the chef: "moy oguzok".
- 'Have you completely lost your mind?' "S uma soshyol" is "came down off the mind". "Ty chto" at the start means "what's wrong with you?".
- 'Why are you sleeping here?' Here "ty chto" does the job of "why", as in speech. "Spish'" is "you sleep".
- 'Chef, please forgive me.' Awake, Max switches to "vy": "prostite", forgive, with the polite -te.
- 'I lay down for five minutes.' "Pri-" turns "lyog", lay down, into "prilyog", lay down for a bit. "Na pyat' minut" is "for five minutes".
- 'You cannot sleep in the kitchen!' "Nel'zya" is "it's not allowed", and it needs no subject. "Spat'" is the plain "to sleep".
- 'I could not sleep all night.' "Vsyu noch'" needs no "during". "Ne mog usnut'" is "could not fall asleep", with "mog" the man's past of "can".
- 'Why didn't you sleep at night?' "Noch'yu", at night, is "noch'" with -yu. Other times of day work the same way: "utrom", in the morning, and "vecherom", in the evening.
- 'Probably it is acclimatization.' Max gives the same long borrowed word as before, "akklimatizatsiya". "Navernoye" is "probably".
- 'What acclimatization?' "Kakaya yeshchyo" repeats the word back with an edge, like "what acclimatization, exactly?".
- 'I am not used to Moscow yet.' "Privyk k" is "got used to", and "k" puts an -e on the city: "k Moskve". "Yeshchyo ne" is "not yet".
- 'Quickly get up and wash dishes!' "Moy" here is the command "wash", from "myt'", and it is spelled the same as "moy", my. "Posuda", dishes, is one singular word in Russian.
- 'Already getting up, Chef.' "Uzhe vstayu" is "already I get up". The plain present stands in for "I'm getting up".
