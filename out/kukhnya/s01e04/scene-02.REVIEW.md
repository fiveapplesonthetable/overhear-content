## Part 1 — Intro

> Outside the restaurant, Chef Viktor Barinov bumps into his culinary rival, Elena Sokolova. Barinov mocks Arcobaleno's newspaper advertisements for new kitchen staff, asserting that real cooks never answer ads. Elena smoothly retorts that top talent from respectable kitchens has already applied, hinting that Claude Monet staff might be among them. Let's listen to their confrontation in Russian first.
