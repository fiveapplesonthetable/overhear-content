## Part 1 — Intro

> Chef Viktor Barinov storms into the rival restaurant Arcobaleno to confront chef Elena Pavlovna. Furious that one of his Claude Monet cooks applied for a job there, he demands to know the applicant's identity. Elena playfully refuses to give up the cook's name and politely shows Barinov the door. Let's listen to their fiery confrontation in Russian.
