## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Katya, is that made from banana?' Talking about the banana to Katya, Kostya leans on "banana" ("from banana", a form of "banan") to give the remark its edge.
- 'No, Kostya, this is made from egg yolk.' Addressing the egg yolk around Kostya, Katya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Listen, I just wanted to ask you.' Kostya cuts in at once hearing Katya, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Kostya, is everything alright with you?' Katya continues directly with Kostya and lands on "khorosho" ("good, fine") as the key everyday word here.
- 'And what if you make jelly from buckwheat?' Turning to the buckwheat to Katya, Kostya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Katya purely through rising pitch.
- 'What will that look like?' Katya checks promptly what Kostya is actually getting at here.
- 'Kostya, go away and do not distract me.' Emphasizing the losing at once, Katya reaches for the Russian word "otvlekay" ("losing") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Has anyone seen my phone here?' Addressing her phone beside Denis, Katya pairs the negative word with "ne" ("not"), since Russian naturally doubles its negatives for emphasis.
- 'I left it right here on the table.' Katya responds pointedly around Denis, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'No, here it is, this one is mine.' Reacting promptly in front of Denis, spoken Russian drops "is" and "are" in the present, letting Katya place the two halves side by side.
- 'Let me make a call, I need to urgently.' Speaking quietly in the exchange, Denis leans on "nado" ("it is necessary, one needs to"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'Well, of course, take it. Thank you.' Katya calmly speaks this word of thanks right on cue using "spasibo" ("thank you") — the everyday word for "thank you", which grew out of the old blessing "spasi Bog" ("God save you").
