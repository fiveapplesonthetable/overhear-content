## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Victoria Sergeevna, here is what I just thought.' Saying that here is what he just thought is how Kostya sets the tone with Denis.
- 'What if the real secret guest suddenly shows up?' Kostya asks whether what if the real secret guest suddenly shows up, refusing to let the point slide.
- 'Those two will not believe you anymore.' Explaining that those two will not believe Denis anymore lays the cards on the table for Denis. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Please do not tell them anything.' Kostya mentions that do not tell them anything so Denis sees what is at stake. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'And if that is not the secret guest, then who is he?' Questioning whether if that is not the secret guest then who is he is how Vika tests Denis's story.
- 'Just an ordinary person, nobody.' Over the inspector and the moment, Vika answers Denis without a second thought.
- 'It seems to me she still has not watched it.' Denis mentions that it seems to him she still has not watched it so Kostya sees what is at stake.
- 'I already regret that I confessed.' Realizing that he already regrets that he confessed changes how Denis handles the situation.
- 'You were completely right.' Noting that Kostya was completely right keeps Denis's side of the story simple and clear. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Kostya, can you distract her a little?' Checking whether Kostya distract her a little is how Denis puts Kostya on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'I want to delete the video quickly.' Because he wants to delete the video quickly, Denis treats the matter as settled.
- 'Listen, Denis, I will try.' Saying that he will try is how Kostya sets the tone with Denis.
