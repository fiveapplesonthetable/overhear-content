## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hey, guys, if you finished everything, go on home!' Emphasizing the boys to Max, Barinov leans on "rebyata" ("the boys, the lads") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Chef, Katya and I refined Sauce Lavrov!' Focusing on the sauce with Barinov, Max brings in "dorabotali" ("refined, improved", from "dorabotat") as the key Russian word here.
- 'And what, now it does not make people nauseous anymore?' Barinov checks point-blank where this conversation is heading next.
- 'Wait, whom did you just serve this sauce to?' Touching on the sauce at once, Barinov chooses "podozhdi" ("wait", from the root "podozhdat") so Max hears the exact meaning.
- 'I served it to Dmitry Vladimirovich Nagiyev!' Max speaks readily around Nagiyev and centers the thought on "podal" ("submitted, filed", from "podat") without wasting a word.
- 'What have you done, have you lost your minds?' Barinov wonders promptly before Max can change the subject.
- 'Chef, do not worry, Katya separated everything out in there.' Max chimes in candidly before Barinov and chooses "perezhivayte" ("worry", from the root "perezhivat") so Barinov hears the exact meaning.
- 'In short, now our sauce is safe for people who are drinking!' Pointing out how short things are during the talk, Max links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Alright, confess, who cooked this fish?' Nagiyev prompts promptly to see how Max will answer on the spot.
- 'I made it, Dmitry Vladimirovich.' Max weighs in promptly with a smile, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Well done, very delicious, and why did you only make it for me?' Reacting candidly on the spot, the "-sya" ending on "molodets" ("well done, good boy") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'Make it quickly for everyone, my friends want some too!' Nagiyev steps in firmly in this moment, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
