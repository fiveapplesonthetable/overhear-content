## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'All of you hated him so much!' Vika reacts plainly with a nod, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'So now he is taking revenge on all of us!' Vika cuts in candidly across the room, before Nagiyev can change the subject.
- 'And why are you standing there looking so funny?' Stressing how ridiculous things are around Vika, Nagiyev puts the weight on "smeshnyye" ("ridiculous", from "smeshnoy") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'You are standing there as if you saw a ghost!' Nagiyev replies boldly with a sigh, making his position completely clear.
- 'Friends, please welcome and favor her!' Nagiyev proceeds at once around Vika, testing Vika's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Who used to be our director before?' Underscoring his director right here, Nagiyev reaches for the Russian word "direktorom" ("director, head teacher", built from "direktor") right at the center of the line.
- 'Before it was Viktoria Sergeyevna, and now it is Kristina Semyonovna!' Nagiyev follows up plainly in front of Kristina and chooses "ranshe" ("earlier, previously") so Kristina gets the point right away.
- 'Yes, of course I understand everything very well.' Kristina jumps in evenly watching Nagiyev, leaving no doubt about where she stands.
- 'Earlier you and I had our disagreements.' Underscoring her disagreements around Nagiyev, Kristina centers the thought on "raznoglasiya" ("disagreements", from "raznoglasiye") without wasting a word.
- 'But I hope that now you will upset me less.' Kristina answers earnestly in this moment, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Alright, and where is our head chef right now?' Nagiyev probes evenly to see how Vika will answer on the spot.
- 'The chef is picking Katya up from the hospital today.' Turning to the hospital around Nagiyev, Vika chooses "bolnitsy" ("hospital", from the root "bolnitsa") so Nagiyev hears the exact meaning.
