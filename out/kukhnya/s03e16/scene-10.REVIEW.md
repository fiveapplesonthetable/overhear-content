- 'But not all guests in the restaurant drink alcohol!' Max colors one sharp piece in reply: "restorane" means "restaurant" (from "restoran"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'You cannot be let near food at all, you will mop the floors instead of Ainura!' Barinov puts this point at once: the conversational word "voobshche" ("in general, altogether") zooms out to sum up the bigger picture.
- 'Thank you for the kind words, Chef!' Max reacts evenly hearing Katya, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
