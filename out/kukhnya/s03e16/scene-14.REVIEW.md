## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, in thyme there is one nasty substance.' Katya steps in pointedly in their talk, watching Max's face to see how the news lands.
- 'That is what causes nausea, do you understand now?' Katya probes straight out about what Nagiyev just brought up.
- 'With the help of chemistry we can remove this substance from the thyme.' Katya underscores this telling choice in earnest: "veshchestvo" means "substance", and it carries the main weight of what Katya is saying.
- 'And the taste of the sauce will remain the same, do you get it?' Katya sends that remark with candor: the "-sya" ending on "ostanetsya" ("will remain") turns the action inward or makes it happen between people naturally.
- 'Well, I almost got it, I will bring fresh thyme right now!' Max opens up sharply without holding back, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Look at the screen, are you not scared yet?' Max stresses this telling detail right here: "yeshchyo" means "more, still", and it carries the main weight of what Max is saying.
- 'I do not believe in those ghosts!' Max steps in promptly with a sigh, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Somehow I am having no luck at all in cards tonight.' Nagiyev proceeds steadily before Max, so Max knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'On the other hand, I got very lucky in love, right, sweetheart?' Nagiyev uses one telling detail on cue: "lyubvi" means "love" (from "lyubov"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Dima, at this rate you will soon be left without a restaurant!' Katya focuses on this direct detail on cue: "restorana" means "restaurant" (from "restoran"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Right now I will drink a little more and relax.' Nagiyev follows up right away as they talk, shifting the whole mood of the room in a single beat.
- 'You will see, soon my luck will definitely turn!' Nagiyev colors one direct word toward Max: "obyazatelno" means "surely, without fail", and it carries the main weight of what Nagiyev is saying.
