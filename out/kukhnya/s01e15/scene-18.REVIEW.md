## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Therefore I will not pressure you.' Vika promises to give him space, placing a small negative word immediately before "will be" to show she won't push.
- 'Let everything remain as it was.' Suggesting they keep the status quo, Vika puts the question word for "how" right before the past action "was."
- 'Yes, but I...' Max trails off uncertainly, stringing together "yes," "but," and "I" without any extra words.
- 'We will definitely live together.' Giving him hope for the future, Vika mixes "surely" with "will" and "to live."
- 'And now good night.' Bidding him farewell for the evening, Vika smoothly links "now" directly to "good night."
- 'Until tomorrow, Max.' Saying his farewell, Max delivers a quick phrase built around "tomorrow."
- 'Until tomorrow.' Vika echoes the sentiment with her own punchy, one-word-based goodbye.
- 'Moved to live in the hallway.' Grumbling about his new living situation, Max uses a completed action word to describe his move to the stairwell.
- 'Hi, Max.' Kostya chimes in over the phone with a snappy one-word greeting.
- 'What do you need?' Answering irritably, Max uses an impersonal structure meaning "what to you is necessary."
- 'Max, come back.' Kostya gets straight to the point, pairing his roommate's name directly with a command to return.
- 'We miss you.' Kostya misses his buddy. The phrase literally says that Max is lacking to them.
- 'We will divide the apartment by three.' He offers a solution to the money problem by offering to split the rent three ways.
- 'I will try to come.' Max is touched by the offer and promises to make an effort to return.
