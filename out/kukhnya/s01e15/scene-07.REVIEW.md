## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Lyova, do not get carried away with pancakes.' -- Viktor warns him about his poor diet.
- 'And what is bad about these tasty pancakes?' -- The cook asks him an innocent question.
- 'Remember your last test.' -- The chef reminds him of his health.
- 'My cholesterol level was almost in range.' -- Lyova defends his medical test to him.
- 'Go to the dry cleaners and take my suit.' -- The boss gives him a new chore.
- 'I do not have time. That is the other way.' -- Lyova tells him it is too far.
- 'Lyova, come on, favor for a favor.' -- Viktor proposes a simple deal to him.
- 'What useful favor can there be from you?' -- Lyova doubts his boss can help him.
- 'You get me the suit, and I allow you to be late.' -- He offers him a pass for tardiness.
- 'Alright, I agree to these strange conditions.' -- Lyova accepts the deal from him.
- 'That is it, you are no longer sous-chef.' -- Later, the chef fires him from his post.
- 'How can you act like this with me?' -- Lyova asks him with deep shock.
- 'March to the utility room.' -- Viktor banishes him to the small closet.
