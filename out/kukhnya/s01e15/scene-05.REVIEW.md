## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Where are you going?' -- She asks him where he is heading.
- 'I have to go.' -- He tells her he needs to leave.
- 'Stay with me for one more minute.' -- She begs him to stay with her.
- 'I cannot, I am rushing very much.' -- He apologizes to her for his hurry.
- 'Do you again have many important things?' -- She complains about his busy daily schedule.
- 'I need to stop by home, take a shower, change.' -- He lists his tasks for her.
- 'Listen, are you not tired of this?' -- She asks him about his exhausting routine.
- 'What exactly should have strongly tired me?' -- He asks her to clarify her question.
- 'Sleeping here, but taking a shower far from here.' -- She points out his silly daily logic.
- 'Yes, it takes a lot of my time.' -- He agrees with her logical sad observation.
- 'Then why do you do this every day?' -- She pushes him for a real answer.
- 'Because all my things lie there.' -- He reminds her about his personal belongings.
- 'Maybe you should move in with me?' -- She finally makes a big romantic proposal.
