## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well? Did you talk to Kostya?' -- Vika asks him about his important chat.
- 'Yes, he begs me not to move.' -- Max tells her about his friend's plea.
- 'Why does he strongly not want this?' -- She asks him for the real reason.
- 'He has a problem with money now.' -- He explains the financial issue to her.
- 'But you still decided and moved out.' -- She notes that he left his friend.
- 'Yes so fast that I forgot the wallet.' -- He admits his silly mistake to her.
- 'And where do you live?' -- She asks him for his current address.
- 'Nowhere for now.' -- Max confesses his homelessness to her.
- 'So better nowhere than with me?' -- She feels insulted by his strange choice.
- 'Well alright, let me come to you.' -- He casually agrees to live with her.
- 'No, you do not need to do me a favor.' -- She rejects his reluctant offer to her.
- 'So you will not come?' -- He asks her to clarify her words.
- 'You understand, it is important that we want.' -- She tells him it must be mutual.
