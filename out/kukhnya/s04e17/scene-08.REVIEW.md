## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vera Ivanovna, may I speak with you?' Barinov asks whether Vera Ivanovna may he speaks with the guest, refusing to let the point slide. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'A certain friend of mine has big problems.' Claiming that a certain friend of his has big problems sounds confident, even if the guest has doubts.
- 'Tell me, which friend exactly?' Around the truth and plan, the guest wants to know where Barinov really stands.
- 'Oh, you do not know him at all.' Barinov explains that the guest does not know him at all, keeping his voice calm and direct. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'In short, there is a certain young woman.' Realizing that in short there is a certain young woman changes how Barinov handles the situation.
- 'And moreover, the girl is almost a stranger.' Noting that moreover the girl is almost a stranger keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'She is pregnant, and he is the father.' Even though she is pregnant and he is the father, Barinov keeps a steady face in front of the guest.
- 'So Lyova found himself a girlfriend!' The guest declares that Lyova found himself a girlfriend as the tension in the room peaks. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'How happy I am for your friend!' Because how happy he is for Barinov's friend, the guest does not bother softening the blow for Barinov.
- 'Children are simply wonderful!' Insisting that children are simply wonderful shows the guest is not backing down. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Yes, but what should he do now?' Here asking what he do now should shows Barinov looking for the real truth. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Your friend grew up to be a real man.' Saying that Barinov's friend grew up to be a real man is how the guest sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'He will manage to take responsibility for his actions.' Since he will manage to take responsibility for his actions, the guest does not waste words on excuses.
