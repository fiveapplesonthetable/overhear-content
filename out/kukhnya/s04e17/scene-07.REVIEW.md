## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Meet my future husband Arseny.' Pointing to the husband with Denis, Katya puts the emphasis on "budushchiy" ("future") in the middle of the talk.
- 'And this is my future husband Louis.' Underscoring how future things are during the talk, Katya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Oh, it is very nice for me too.' Denis follows up briskly watching Katya, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'No, these options do not suit at all.' Emphasizing the option around Denis, Katya brings in "varianty" ("option, variant", from "variant") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Denis, will you be my fiancé?' Pointing to the bridegroom in this beat, Katya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Denis purely through rising pitch.
- 'What did you just say to me?' Denis inquires calmly before Katya can change the subject.
- 'I take it that means yes?' Katya proceeds dryly in the exchange and brings in "ponimayu" ("understand", from "ponimat") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Well, and what do you want to play?' Opening up briskly around Senya, Denis puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Senya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Well, I do not know, think of something.' Senya follows up warmly hearing Denis, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'We need very beautiful and passionate music.' Emphasizing the music before Denis, Senya puts the weight on "strastnaya" ("music", from "strastnyy") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'So that she wants me right away.' Senya continues warmly in the exchange and reaches for the Russian word "srazu" ("at once") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Got it, then let's start with a simple melody.' Denis speaks up wryly on the spot, testing Senya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
