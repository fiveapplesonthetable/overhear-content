## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Viktor Petrovich, you should know too.' Eva mentions that Barinov should know so Barinov sees what is at stake.
- 'I am expecting a child right now.' Eva explains that she is expecting a child right now, keeping her voice calm and direct.
- 'I am pregnant by you.' Explaining that she is pregnant by Barinov lays the cards on the table for Barinov. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'What on earth are you saying, Eva?' Over the conversation and the news, Barinov presses Eva for a straight answer.
- 'It is still very early.' Realizing that it is still very early changes how Eva handles the situation.
- 'But soon everything will be noticeable.' Noting that soon everything will be noticeable keeps Eva's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Vitya, I really am pregnant.' With the moment and news in play, Eva keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, is everything all right with you?' Here asking whether everything all right with Barinov shows Lyova looking for the real truth. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Yes, Lyova, everything is fine.' Saying that everything is fine is how Barinov sets the tone with Eva.
- 'Well, I have a big problem.' Having a big problem is how Lyova lays out the situation for Barinov.
- 'Lyova, I am pregnant too.' Realizing that she is pregnant changes how Eva handles the situation.
- 'We need to check everything urgently.' Here needing to check everything urgently sets up the next turn in their exchange. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
