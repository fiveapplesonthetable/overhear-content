## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Katya, forgive me for letting you down.' Denis replies quietly on the spot, before Katya can change the subject.
- 'I really wanted to help you very much.' Emphasizing the honestly around Katya, Denis reaches for the Russian word "pravda" ("honestly") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Denis, it is not your fault at all.' Replying firmly before Denis, Katya adds "sovsem" ("completely, at all") so the statement leaves zero room for doubt.
- 'Apparently I am just that problematic myself.' Katya pushes back at once beside Denis, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'There are always only strange people around me.' Stressing how strange things are beside Denis, Katya leans on "strannyye" ("strange", a form of "strannyy") to give the remark its edge.
- 'It is as if I attract them on purpose.' Bringing up the purpose in this beat, Katya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Our owner is simply a terrible person.' Katya chimes in firmly beside Denis, so Denis knows she is not just making small talk.
- 'And my dad is a whole other story.' Stressing how separate things are before Denis, Katya centers the remark on "otdelnyy" ("separate") without any extra fuss.
- 'I have never come across normal guys.' Mentioning the guys on the spot, Katya pairs the negative word with "ne" ("not"), since Russian naturally doubles its negatives for emphasis.
- 'Tell me, what is wrong with me?' Katya wonders promptly how Denis expects that to work in practice.
- 'Calm down, everything is fine with you.' Denis replies evenly in this beat and uses "uspokoysya" ("calm down", from "uspokoitsya") to make the point stick.
- 'You perceive everything in a minor key.' Denis continues plainly without holding back, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'Look, you see life like this.' Denis replies promptly with a sigh, testing Katya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
