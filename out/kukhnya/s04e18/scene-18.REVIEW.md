## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'How long do we have to wait? I want to eat.' Asking whether how long do they have to wait puts Denis right on the defensive. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Denis, forgive me, I am a little late.' Realizing that she is a little late changes how Katya handles the situation.
- 'I am not surprised at all, let's go upstairs.' Being not surprised at all let them go upstairs moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'Only I came for just a short while tonight.' Explaining that only she came for just a short while tonight lays the cards on the table for Denis.
- 'Yeah? Why did you even bother coming then?' Wondering why Katya even bother coming then is how Denis puts Katya on the spot.
- 'Well yes, you are right, I will head back.' Claiming that Denis is right she will head back sounds confident, even if Denis has doubts. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Wait, you are not going anywhere right now.' Mentioning that Katya is not going anywhere right now moves the talk in a new direction. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Denis, why are you pretending to be a tough guy?' Wondering why Denis pretending to be a tough guy is cuts straight through the tension between Katya and Denis.
- 'Katya, I do not know who I should be.' Because he does not know who he should be, Denis treats the matter as settled.
- 'Always stay the way you are.' Noting that always stay the way Denis is keeps Katya's side of the story simple and clear. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'That is the exact way I liked you.' Even though that is the exact way she liked Denis, Katya keeps a steady face in front of Denis.
- 'All right, I will definitely try to be myself.' Admitting that all right he will definitely try to be himself shows Denis being honest for a change.
