## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'How long do we have to wait? I want to eat.' Senya probes eagerly where this conversation is heading next.
- 'Denis, forgive me, I am a little late.' Katya follows up quietly in the exchange and chooses "opozdala" ("to be late", from the root "opozdat") so Denis hears the exact meaning.
- 'I am not surprised at all, let's go upstairs.' Explaining how surprised he is at once, Denis adds "sovsem" ("completely, at all") so the statement leaves zero room for doubt.
- 'Only I came for just a short while tonight.' Katya follows up briskly toward Denis, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Yeah? Why did you even bother coming then?' Denis continues sharply on the spot and centers the thought on "priyezzhala" ("to come, to arrive", from "priyezzhat") without wasting a word.
- 'Well yes, you are right, I will head back.' Katya continues honestly watching Denis, watching Denis's face to see how the news lands.
- 'Wait, you are not going anywhere right now.' Denis steps in openly across the room, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Denis, why are you pretending to be a tough guy?' Pointing to the man before Denis, Katya reaches for the Russian word "izobrazhayesh" ("to portray", built from "izobrazhat") right at the center of the line.
- 'Katya, I do not know who I should be.' Denis opens up plainly hearing Katya, so Katya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Always stay the way you are.' Katya counters openly with a nod, so Denis knows she is not just making small talk.
- 'That is the exact way I liked you.' Katya proceeds decisively at once and centers the thought on "ponravilsya" ("was liked, pleased", from "ponravitsya") without wasting a word.
- 'All right, I will definitely try to be myself.' Denis steps in plainly on the spot and reaches for the Russian word "obyazatelno" ("surely, without fail") right at the heart of the line.
