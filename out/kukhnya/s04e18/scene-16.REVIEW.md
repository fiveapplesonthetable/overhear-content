- 'And we will interrupt at the most interesting part.' A superlative prepositional phrase describes interrupting German right at the climax of his date.
- 'But first we need to warm him up well.' A culinary infinitive meaning to heat or warm up is used figuratively for stirring up passion.
- 'Vika, maybe we could have dinner together tonight?' In the hallway, German invites Vika out using a perfective first-person plural future verb.

- 'We will have dinner tonight at my place.' A genitive personal pronoun followed by the adverb at home means at my apartment.
- 'Here is Viktoriya Sergeyevna's favorite wine.' Meanwhile, the chefs prepare a spiked bottle of Vika's favorite wine for the date.
- 'May I take this bottle?' German picks up the exact bottle they prepared, sealing his fate for the evening.
