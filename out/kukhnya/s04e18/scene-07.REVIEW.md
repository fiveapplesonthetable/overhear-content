## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We need to introduce a new menu.' Discussing a new menu in front of Barinov, Nagiyev uses "novoye" ("new", from "novyy") to make the point stick.
- 'I have seen it, it works wonderfully.' Nagiyev chimes in candidly in the exchange and chooses "prekrasno" ("perfectly") so Barinov gets the point right away.
- 'Dima, let's step aside for a minute.' Barinov speaks up boldly in their talk, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'What are you holding there in your hands?' Emphasizing the hands right here, Nagiyev brings in "rukakh" ("hands", from "ruka") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Vitya, are you playing games?' Answering promptly beside Barinov, Nagiyev keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Barinov purely through rising pitch.
- 'Speak, I have no secrets from colleagues.' Nagiyev counters promptly in their talk, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Dima, we have a big problem.' Talking about a big problem with Nagiyev, Barinov centers the thought on "bolshaya" ("large", from "bolshoy") without wasting a word.
- 'You have warmed a snake on your chest.' Barinov jumps in evenly with a shrug, so Nagiyev knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'My ex-wife is far away right now.' Nagiyev jumps in decisively across the room, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'I am talking about your German.' Barinov chimes in decisively beside Nagiyev and brings in "govoryu" ("am telling", from "govorit") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Take a look at this yourself.' Barinov follows up evenly around Nagiyev, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'Well go on, show me the video.' Nagiyev speaks pointedly without a pause, making his position completely clear.
