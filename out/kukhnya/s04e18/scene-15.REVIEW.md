## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Just make sure you do not lose that key.' In Vika's flashback, German warns his helper with a negated perfective imperative.
- 'Do not worry, only I have the key.' The helper reassures him politely using a negated plural reflexive imperative.
- 'So German himself set all of this up!' Back in the present, Vika realizes the truth with an emphatic pronoun and past perfective verb.
- 'What a cunning scoundrel he is!' She vents her anger using an exclamatory modifier and a masculine nominative noun meaning scoundrel.
- 'That is the kind of handsome man he is.' Viktor Petrovich remarks ironically on German's true character using a demonstrative phrase.
- 'Vika, he will pay for everything.' Literally meaning will answer for everything, the perfective future verb promises retribution.
- 'I firmly promise you that.' Barinov pledges his word with a dative personal pronoun and a first-person singular present verb.
- 'And I want to help too.' Vika volunteers to serve as the bait in Barinov's trap using a verb of wishing and an infinitive.
- 'Tonight we are having dinner at Senya's place.' Meanwhile, Denis tries out Senya's macho advice by announcing their evening plans as a fact.
- 'But I have plans for the evening.' The accusative time phrase expresses plans scheduled for the evening.
- 'That's right, you are going with me to Senya's.' Denis overrides her objection with a present-tense verb of motion used for a decided future action.
- 'We will meet in the evening after work.' He walks off without waiting for an answer, leaving a stunned pause behind him.
