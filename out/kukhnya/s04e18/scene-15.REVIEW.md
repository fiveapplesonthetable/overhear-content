## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Just make sure you do not lose that key.' Adding that just make sure Denis does not lose that key fills in the missing piece for Denis.
- 'Do not worry, only I have the key.' Saying that do not worry only he has the key is how Denis sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'So German himself set all of this up!' Vika declares that German himself set all of this up as the tension in the room peaks. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'What a cunning scoundrel he is!' Because what a cunning scoundrel he is, Vika does not bother softening the blow for Barinov. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'That is the kind of handsome man he is.' Barinov mentions that that is the kind of handsome man he is so Denis sees what is at stake.
- 'Vika, he will pay for everything.' Claiming that he will pay for everything sounds confident, even if Denis has doubts.
- 'I firmly promise you that.' With the promise and revenge in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'And I want to help too.' Noting that she wants to help keeps Vika's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Tonight we are having dinner at Senya's place.' Realizing that tonight they are having dinner at Senya place changes how Denis handles the situation.
- 'But I have plans for the evening.' Pointing out that she has plans for the evening gives Barinov a sobering reality check. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'That's right, you are going with me to Senya's.' Denis explains that that is right Barinov is going with him to Senya, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'We will meet in the evening after work.' Admitting that they will meet in the evening after work shows Denis being honest for a change.
