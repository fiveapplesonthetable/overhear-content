## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Just make sure you do not lose that key.' Putting the short word for 'not' right in front of the action word is all Russian needs to make a sentence negative, with no extra helper words like 'do' or 'did'.
- 'Do not worry, only I have the key.' Russians say 'by me' or 'with us' where English says 'I have' or 'we have', without needing a separate word for 'have'.
- 'So German himself set all of this up!' Spoken Russian keeps this brief and expressive, built right around 'engineered, set up' without extra helper words.
- 'What a cunning scoundrel he is!' Here Vika anchors the thought on 'clever, cunning' and finishes on 'scoundrel, villain', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
