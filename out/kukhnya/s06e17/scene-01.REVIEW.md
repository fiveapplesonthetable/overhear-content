## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Do not look at me like that.' Barinov speaks bluntly facing the inspector, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'You must not be overfed right now.' Stepping in calmly before the inspector, Barinov leans on "nelzya" ("it is not allowed, one must not"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'Hello, Viktor Petrovich.' The inspector evenly chooses this formal greeting toward Barinov using "zdravstvuyte" ("hello") — the polite Russian greeting that literally wishes the other person good health.
- 'I am inspecting your restaurant.' Touching on the restaurant in front of Barinov, the inspector brings in "inspektiruyu" ("I inspect", from "inspektirovat") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Is everything alright with you?' Touching on the order with the inspector, Barinov links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Yes, it is just so unexpected.' The inspector pushes back firmly with a nod, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Hello, I am very pleased to meet you.' Barinov promptly gives this formal greeting in the exchange using "zdravstvuyte" ("hello") — the polite Russian greeting that literally wishes the other person good health.
- 'Your signature set is simply excellent.' Noting how excellent things are beside Barinov, the inspector uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'It overshadowed that unpleasant incident.' The inspector answers dryly watching Barinov, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Thank you for the kind words.' Barinov evenly sends this word of thanks in front of the inspector using "spasibo" ("thank you") — the everyday word for "thank you", which grew out of the old blessing "spasi Bog" ("God save you").
- 'Just do not worry.' Speaking firmly before the inspector, the "-sya" ending on "volnuytes" ("worry") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'We will wash everything out quickly.' Barinov proceeds openly without holding back, refusing to let the moment slip past.
