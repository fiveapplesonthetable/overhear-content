- 'I am inspecting your restaurant.' The inspector stresses one loaded piece with Barinov: "inspektiruyu" means "I inspect" (from "inspektirovat"), giving the line its exact edge.

- 'We will wash everything out quickly.' Barinov fires back openly to settle things, making his position completely clear.
