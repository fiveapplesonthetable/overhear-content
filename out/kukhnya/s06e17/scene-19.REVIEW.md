## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, do not look at me like that.' Admitting that do not look at him like shows Barinov being honest for a change.
- 'You must not be overfed so much.' Saying that Fedya must not be overfed so much is how Barinov sets the tone with Fedya. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'A Michelin inspector has come to see you.' Since a Michelin inspector has come to see Barinov, Fedya does not waste words on excuses. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Hello, respected Viktor Petrovich.' With the inspector and kitchen in play, the inspector keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'My name is Alexey Smolin.' The inspector reminds Barinov that his name is Alexey Smolin before the moment passes.
- 'I am inspecting your restaurant today.' Claiming that he is inspecting Barinov's restaurant today sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Is everything alright with you right now?' Asking whether everything alright with Barinov right now is how the inspector puts Barinov on the spot.
- 'Yes, it is just so unexpected.' Barinov mentions that it is just so unexpected so Fedya sees what is at stake. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Hello, it is very nice to meet you.' Claiming that it is very nice to meet Barinov sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts.
- 'Call an ambulance here!' By telling Barinov to call an ambulance here, the inspector raises the stakes in the room at once.
- 'Chef, I will bring you to your senses!' Once he will bring Barinov to Barinov's senses, Fedya makes sure Barinov hears every word. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Stop, do not touch him right now!' Once Barinov should stop do not touch him right now, the inspector makes sure Barinov hears every word. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
