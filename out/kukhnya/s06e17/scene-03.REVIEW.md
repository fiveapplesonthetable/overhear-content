## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, those were just coconuts.' Adding that those were just coconuts fills in the missing piece for Barinov. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Be quiet right now, Fedya!' With the work and inspector at stake, Barinov reacts without holding back.
- 'I will bring him to his senses now.' Since he will bring him to his senses now, Fedya does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Stand right there!' Once Fedya should stand right there, Barinov makes sure Fedya hears every word. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Chef, excuse me, please.' With the point and calm in play, Fedya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Please do not kill me.' Because do not kill him, the inspector treats the matter as settled.
- 'We do not wish you any harm.' Explaining that they do not wish Fedya any harm lays the cards on the table for Fedya. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Then why did you hit me?' Wondering why Barinov hits him cuts straight through the tension between the inspector and Barinov.
- 'This is just a foolish misunderstanding.' Claiming that this is just a foolish misunderstanding sounds confident, even if Fedya has doubts. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Let me go from here, please.' Since Barinov should let him go from here please, the inspector does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Of course, you are completely free.' Barinov explains that Fedya is completely free, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'Do you want to go to your room?' Checking whether Fedya wants to go to Fedya's room is how Barinov puts Fedya on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Fedya, escort the man, please.' With the truth and calm in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
