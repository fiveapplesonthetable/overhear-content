- 'Chef, this is somehow completely insecure.' Kostya chimes in sharply on the spot and chooses "nenadyozhno" ("unreliably") so Barinov gets the point right away.
- 'That is so she does not run away.' Barinov counters frankly on the spot and chooses "sbezhala" ("to escape, flee", from the root "sbezhat") so Kostya hears the exact meaning.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, please let us go.' Kostya responds quietly hearing Barinov, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'We will definitely not tell anyone.' Kostya counters readily in the exchange and lands on "rasskazhem" ("to tell", built on "rasskazat") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'I asked you last time.' Barinov speaks steadily with a shrug, testing Kostya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Yes, that was a funny incident.' Talking about the case facing Barinov, Kostya speaks plainly so Barinov gets the message at once.
- 'So they tied us up rightly.' Kostya opens up evenly around the inspector and leans on "pravilno" ("right") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'I need to go to the restroom right now.' Turning to the toilet right here, the inspector leans on "nuzhno" ("it is needed, one has to"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'Aha, look how sly he is.' Kostya responds pointedly on the spot, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'People do that when they want to escape.' Kostya continues calmly in this beat and centers the thought on "delayut" ("they do", from "delat") without wasting a word.
- 'Well done, Senya, good thought.' Reacting warmly beside Kostya, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 4 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Go with him and keep watch.' Barinov weighs in briskly across the room, to clear the air between them once and for all.
