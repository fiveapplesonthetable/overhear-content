# Kukhnya (Кухня) — Characters & Voice Mapping

## Protagonist Voice (`alisa` — role key `anya`)
- **Макс Лавров (Max Lavrov, "Огузок")** — Young aspiring chef from Voronezh who comes to Moscow to cook at the upscale French restaurant *Claude Monet*. Whenever Max is in a scene, he takes the protagonist voice (`alisa`).
- **Вика Гончарова (Vika Goncharova)** — Art director of *Claude Monet*, sharp, ambitious, and romantically entangled with Max. Takes `alisa` in scenes where Max is absent, or `anja`/`angelina` opposite Max.

## Supporting Cast (`anja`, `angelina`)
- **Виктор Петрович Баринов (Viktor Petrovich Barinov, "Шеф")** — Brilliant, hot-tempered head chef of *Claude Monet*, passionate about cuisine, Spartak Moscow, and high-stakes bets.
- **Дмитрий Владимирович Нагиев (Dmitry Nagiyev)** — Celebrity owner of *Claude Monet*, flamboyant showman who demands culinary perfection to impress Moscow elites and rivals.
- **Костя Анисимов (Kostya Anisimov)** — Friendly bartender and Max's best friend and roommate in Moscow.
- **Настя Фомина (Nastya Fomina)** — Kind-hearted vegetarian waitress at *Claude Monet*.
- **Сеня (Senya) & Федя (Fedya)** — Veteran line cooks and kitchen pranksters (meat and fish specialists).
- **Лёва Соловьёв (Lyova Solovyov)** — Quiet, dutiful sous-chef of *Claude Monet*.
- **Луи Бенуа (Louis Benoit)** — French pastry chef at *Claude Monet*.
- **Саша (Sasha) & Илья (Ilya)** — Dining room waitstaff.
- **Айнура (Ainura)** — Kitchen cleaner and dishwasher from Bishkek.
- **Элеонора Андреевна (Eleonora Andreyevna)** — Pampered regular guest (a miniature dog in s01e01; also the name of Barinov's ex-wife in later seasons).
