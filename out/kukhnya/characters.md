# kukhnya: characters and places

Show: Кухня (*Kukhnya*, "The Kitchen"), S1–S2. Setting: Moscow's upscale French restaurant *Claude Monet* («Клод Моне»), owned by celebrity showman Dmitry Nagiyev and run by brilliant, hot-tempered head chef Viktor Petrovich Barinov. Language: Russian (`ru-en`).

Role keys: one character = one role key = the character's own name (`max`, `barinov`, `kostya`, `nastya`, ...), in every scene of the show. The machine-readable cast is `cast.json` next to this file (key, Russian display name, English narration names, gender); it is the only source for keys and labels. There are no viewpoint slot keys and scripts carry no `voices`: the renderer picks voices from `gender`. Grammar always follows the character's gender. Rules: FEEDBACK.md "P-1".

The role key / display name / gender table lives in `cast.json` (no voice column any more).


## Макс Лавров, "Огузок" (Max Lavrov; young chef from Voronezh; first seen s01e01)
- Who: enthusiastic, charming, slightly reckless young cook from Voronezh who bluffs his way into the kitchen of *Claude Monet*. Constantly gets into scrapes with Chef Barinov (who nicknames him "Огузок"), shares a Moscow flat with his best friend Kostya, and falls for art director Vika Goncharova.
- Appearance for the art: young man about 23, slim, light skin `#F6D2B5`, neat side-parted chestnut-brown hair `#6B4423`, clean-shaven, bright expressive eyes and a wry grin. In the kitchen: crisp double-breasted white chef jacket `#FFF6EA` with two rows of dark buttons `#2E2A3A`, slate-grey collar piping `#6E86A0`, and a navy-striped waist apron `#3D5A80`. Outside the kitchen: casual teal shirt `#2E8B78` under a brown leather or denim jacket `#5A4638`, often holding a chef's knife or tasting spoon.
- Role key: `max` (see `cast.json`). Label: Макс.
- Speech: informal *ты* with Kostya, Nastya, Vika (when alone), Senya, and Fedya; respectful *вы* ("Шеф", "Виктор Петрович", "Дмитрий Владимирович") with Barinov and Nagiyev.

## Виктор Петрович Баринов, "Шеф" (Chef Viktor Barinov; head chef of *Claude Monet*; first seen s01e01)
- Who: culinary genius and tyrant of the *Claude Monet* kitchen. Passionate about haute cuisine, Spartak Moscow football, fine whiskey, and high-stakes bets; divorced three times (ex-wife Eleonora Andreyevna, younger second ex-wife Tatyana, daughter Alisa), secretly tender-hearted beneath his roaring temper, and fiercely competitive with rival chef Elena Pavlovna of *Arcobaleno*.
- Appearance for the art: stocky, broad-shouldered man about 50, light skin `#F6D2B5`, rosy cheeks, thick expressive dark-brown moustache and neat goatee beard `#4A3A3C`, stern furrowed brows. Wears a tall, puffy cream-white chef's toque `#FFF6EA`, a double-breasted white executive chef jacket `#FFF6EA` with black piping and four black buttons, and a signature crimson-red neckerchief `#C8373B` tucked into the collar. Often holding a tasting spoon, cleaver, or crystal tumbler.
- Role key: `barinov` (see `cast.json`). Label: Шеф.
- Speech: booming, theatrical, sarcastic; calls novices "огузки" and his brigade "инвалиды"; *ты* to all his kitchen staff, *вы* to Nagiyev and VIP guests.

## Виктория Сергеевна Гончарова, Вика (Vika Goncharova; art director of *Claude Monet*; first seen s01e01)
- Who: poised, ambitious, career-driven art director from Kaliningrad who manages the dining room, VIP bookings, and restaurant events. Has a complicated on-and-off romance with Max after their impulsive night together before he starts work.
- Appearance for the art: slender, elegant woman about 27, light skin `#F6D2B5`, long sleek dark-espresso hair `#2A1810` worn straight over her shoulders, arched eyebrows, ruby lipstick. Wears a tailored crimson-burgundy blazer `#A62B4C` over a cream silk blouse `#FFF6EA` and dark pencil skirt `#2E2A3A`, small gold pendant necklace, often holding a leather menu folder or smartphone.
- Role key: `vika` (see `cast.json`). Label: Вика.
- Speech: crisp, composed, professional *вы* with staff and guests in the dining room; sharp, emotional *ты* with Max and her friend Nastya.

## Костя Анисимов (Kostya Anisimov; bartender and Max's best friend; first seen s01e01)
- Who: good-natured, handsome bartender at *Claude Monet* who lets Max move into his Moscow flat; bad at lying, loyal to Max, and falling in love with waitress Nastya Fomina.
- Appearance for the art: tall, athletic young man about 24, light skin `#F6D2B5`, tousled sandy-blond hair `#D4A459`, warm friendly smile. Wears a crisp white dress shirt `#FFF6EA` with rolled sleeves under a tailored charcoal-plum bartender's waistcoat `#3B2E4A`, a neat dark bow tie, and a dark bar apron; often polishing a wine glass or holding a cocktail shaker.
- Role key: `kostya` (see `cast.json`). Label: Костя.
- Speech: relaxed, friendly *ты* with Max, Nastya, and the staff.

## Настя Фомина (Nastya Fomina; waitress at *Claude Monet*; first seen s01e01)
- Who: sweet, idealistic, compassionate waitress and committed vegetarian/animal-rights advocate who champions homeless people and stray animals, while sharing a tender romance with Kostya.
- Appearance for the art: petite young woman about 22, light skin `#F6D2B5`, bright copper-auburn hair `#C4623A` in a high bouncy ponytail with a soft side fringe, big round expressive eyes. Wears a white short-sleeved blouse `#FFF6EA` under a fitted dark waistcoat `#2E2A3A` and a teal or burgundy bistro apron `#3FA7A0`, carrying a round silver serving tray.
- Role key: `nastya` (see `cast.json`). Label: Настя.
- Speech: earnest, gentle, emotional; *ты* with Kostya, Max, and Vika.

## Дмитрий Владимирович Нагиев (Dmitry Nagiyev; celebrity owner of *Claude Monet*; first seen s01e01)
- Who: famous TV host and actor playing a flamboyant, vain, mercurial version of himself as the owner of *Claude Monet*; married to his jealous wife Kristina in Season 1 before their turbulent split.
- Appearance for the art: charismatic man about 45, tanned light skin `#D9A074`, closely cropped/bald head with a stylishly combed sandy-brown top lock `#6E5239`, neat dark stubble, tinted rectangular sunglasses `#2E2A3A`, and a dazzling showman grin. Wears a tailored deep-plum velvet blazer `#5E2B58` over an open-collar black silk shirt `#24242C` with a gold pocket square `#E0B456`.
- Role key: `nagiyev` (see `cast.json`). Label: Нагиев.
- Speech: theatrical, self-important, dramatic pauses; calls Barinov "Витя" and Max "Максимка".

## Лёва Соловьёв (Lyova Solovyov; sous-chef of *Claude Monet*; first seen s01e01)
- Who: Viktor Barinov's loyal, hardworking right-hand sous-chef who lives with his doting mother Vera and struggles with a mild stutter when stressed or excited.
- Appearance for the art: man about 32, light skin `#F6D2B5`, neat short medium-brown hair `#5A3E2B`, earnest worried eyebrows. Wears a double-breasted white sous-chef jacket `#FFF6EA` with slate-blue collar trim `#4B6B8A` and a slate-blue neckerchief, holding a clipboard or whisk.
- Role key: `lyova` (see `cast.json`). Label: Лёва.
- Speech: polite, conscientious, occasionally hesitates on the first syllable when nervous; loyal to "Шеф".

## Сеня Чуганин (Senya Chuganin; meat-station chef and kitchen prankster; first seen s01e01)
- Who: veteran meat specialist at *Claude Monet*, inseparable prankster partner of Fedya, and habitual pilferer of gourmet ingredients from the restaurant kitchen (which drives his wife Marina crazy and amuses Barinov).
- Appearance for the art: stocky, round-faced man about 34, light skin `#F6D2B5`, short sandy-brown hair `#8C6239` under a white chef's toque `#FFF6EA`, light cheek stubble, mischievous grin. Wears a white double-breasted chef jacket `#FFF6EA` with a green neckerchief `#4E8A6E`, often holding kitchen tongs or a steak pan.
- Role key: `senya` (see `cast.json`). Label: Сеня.
- Speech: jovial, conspiratorial *ты*; loves hazing new recruits with Fedya.

## Федя Юрченко (Fedya Yurchenko; fish-station chef and kitchen prankster; first seen s01e01)
- Who: tall, swaggering fish specialist who claims to be a former naval cook from Moldova; best friend and prank co-conspirator of Senya.
- Appearance for the art: tall, broad-shouldered man about 35, medium-warm skin `#D9A074`, dark wavy hair `#2E221F` under a tilted white chef's cap/toque, cheeky grin. Wears a white chef jacket `#FFF6EA` open at the throat to reveal a blue-and-white striped naval undershirt (тельняшка `#2F3E66` / `#FFF6EA`) and a navy neckerchief `#2F3E66`, holding a fish or filleting knife.
- Role key: `fedya` (see `cast.json`). Label: Федя.

## Places
- **Кухня «Клод Моне» (Claude Monet Kitchen)**: gleaming stainless-steel pass counters and prep islands `#6E86A0`, overhead pot rack with hanging copper and steel saucepans `#C98B5E`, glowing gas range with steaming stockpots, order-ticket rail with fluttering cream slips `#FFF6EA`, and tiled walls. Dominant colours: kitchen slate `#6E86A0`, knife teal `#2E9A96`, or warm pass amber `#F39A3C`.
- **Кабинет Шефа (Chef Barinov's Office)**: warm wood-panelled office `#8A5A3C` separated from the kitchen by a large interior glass window; wooden desk with a green banker's lamp, laptop, and crystal decanter, leather armchair, and a red-and-white Spartak football scarf/pennant on the wall (shapes only, no text). Dominant colour: warm cognac brown `#B86B5A` or amber `#C98B5E`.
- **Зал ресторана «Клод Моне» (Claude Monet Dining Room)**: elegant French dining room with rich burgundy-rose walls `#B85A6A`, warm golden wall sconces, framed abstract art, and round tables dressed in crisp cream tablecloths `#FFF6EA` with wine glasses, candles, and silver cloches. Dominant colours: burgundy rose `#C75B7A`, warm gold `#E0B456`, or soft plum `#8C6BB1`.
- **Барная стойка (Bar Counter)**: polished dark-wood bar counter `#5A3B2E` in the dining room, backlit glass shelves lined with colourful bottles (`#2E9A96`, `#E2604F`, `#F2C14E`), hanging wine glasses, and an espresso machine. Dominant colour: cafe caramel `#C98B5E` or plum `#8C6BB1`.
- **Холодильная камера и склад (Walk-in Fridge & Storeroom)**: cool steel-blue insulated walls `#5B7FA6`, metal shelving stacked with wooden crates of vegetables, golden cheese wheels `#F2C14E`, and hanging cured sausages `#B85A4A`. Dominant colour: icy steel blue `#5B7FA6`.
- **Внутренний двор / Служебный вход (Back Courtyard & Alley)**: brick exterior wall `#B86B5A` behind the restaurant, metal service door with steps, green waste bins `#5E9A78`, and stacked wooden delivery crates. Dominant colour: brick terracotta `#C97B63` or evening slate `#4B6B8A`.
- **Раздевалка и коридор (Staff Locker Room & Corridor)**: row of tall grey-blue metal staff lockers `#6E86A0`, wooden changing bench, and mirror. Dominant colour: sage teal `#5E9A78` or slate `#6E86A0`.
- **Квартира Макса и Кости / Квартира Вики (Max & Kostya's Flat / Vika's Flat)**: cosy Moscow apartment with warm patterned wallpaper, a plush sofa, floor lamp, window with curtains, and a small kitchen table with tea mugs. Dominant colour: warm peach `#D97B66` or soft mustard `#F2C14E`.
