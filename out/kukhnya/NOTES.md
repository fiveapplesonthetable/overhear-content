# Kukhnya (Кухня) — Authoring, Translation & Validation Notes

## Timing Gap Warnings (`validate.py`)
- `tools/validate.py` emits `WARN ... gap to prev is ...s (> 10s)` when music montages, kitchen prep sequences, or exterior transitions occur between subtitle cues in `.dual.srt` or `.ru.srt`. All spoken subtitle cues from cue 1 to the final cue of each episode are covered contiguously in `scenes.json` with `merge: null` on active scenes.

## Voice Assignment & Character Conventions
- Per `spec/recipe.md` and `out/kukhnya/characters.md`, every scripted scene maps its viewpoint speaker (Max Lavrov whenever present, or Chef Viktor Barinov, Vika Goncharova, Nastya Fomina, Kostya Anisimov, etc.) to role key `anya` (`alisa`), and supporting speakers to role keys mapped to `anja` and `angelina`, with canonical Russian display names in `labels` (`Макс`, `Шеф`, `Вика`, `Костя`, `Настя`, `Сеня`, `Федя`, `Лёва`, `Луи`, `Нагиев`, `Елена`, `Айнура`, `Кристина`, `Саша`, `Илья`, `Родион`, `Тимур`, `Алиса`, `Таня`, `Элеонора`, `Инспектор`, `Гость`, `Ангелина`).
- Kitchen slang and mild insults (e.g., *огузок*, *инвалиды*, *бабки*, *халява*) are explained naturally in the narrator walk-through and adapted to clean A1–A2 Russian suitable for language learners.

## Machine-Transcription Recognition Quirks (S2 `e04.ru.srt` – S6 `e20.ru.srt`)
Per `README.md` ("If you do work from a Russian-only file, translate it yourself for your own understanding, and note in `out/kukhnya/NOTES.md` that the transcript may have recognition errors"):
- **Source Files (`sources/kukhnya/s02/e04.ru.srt` .. `s06/e20.ru.srt`)**: Starting at Season 2 Episode 4, the subtitle sidecars are Russian-only machine-transcribed `.ru.srt` files without pre-existing English translations. All dialogue lines have been translated directly from Russian into idiomatic English for `scenes.json`, `authoring/scene-NN.py`, `scene-NN.json`, `scene-NN.REVIEW.md`, and `scene-NN.notes.json`.
- **Proper-Noun & Restaurant-Name ASR Quirks**:
  - `«Клуб Моне»` / `«Кот Моне»` (e.g., `s02/e04.ru.srt` cue 41) is a phonetic ASR misrecognition of `«Клод Моне»` (*Claude Monet*). In authored story lines we either quote the clean greeting fragment (`Добрый день.`) or adapt the restaurant name to `«Клод Моне»` while tying `source.lines` to the exact invariant cue substring.
  - `Баранова` / `Барановой` (e.g., `s02/e04.ru.srt`) appears in the ASR transcript for `Баринова` (Chef Viktor Barinov's surname).
  - `Аркабалена` (e.g., `s02/e08.ru.srt`) is the phonetic Russian ASR spelling of the rival Italian restaurant *Arcobaleno* («Аркобалено»); normalized to lemma `аркобалено` (`pos: "name"`).
  - In Season 3 (`s03/e01.ru.srt`..`e20.ru.srt`), ASR phonetic misrecognitions such as `Ежливость` (for `Вежливость` in `s03/e02.ru.srt`), `Нагеев` / `Дагиев` (for `Нагиев`), `буабес, не суаться` / `несуаса` (for `буйабес, нисуаз` in `s03/e02.ru.srt`–`e03.ru.srt`), `увольна` (for `уволена` in `s03/e02.ru.srt`), `Гаша` (for `Катя` in `s03/e03.ru.srt` cue 332), `хомоватая` (for `хамоватая`), and `жраби` (for `жребий`) are normalized to clean Russian in `segments` while anchoring `source.lines` to exact invariant cue words.
  - In Season 4 (`s04/e01.ru.srt`..`e20.ru.srt`), ASR compound/phonetic mergers such as `шахповара` (for `шеф-повара`), `Клод Мане` (for `Клод Моне`), `Первый блинком` (for `Первый блин комом`), `Скрылупа` (for `Скорлупа`), `Рик` (for `Вик`), `париказ` (for `приказ`), `трампунг`/`тропут` (for `травмпункт`), `с Ушефом`/`отцу шефа` (for `су-шефом`/`от су-шефа`), `эволюция` (for `овуляция`), and `тутку` (for `дудку`) are normalized to clean Russian in `segments` while anchoring `source.lines` to exact invariant cue words.
  - In Season 5 (`s05/e01.ru.srt`..`e20.ru.srt`), ASR phonetic misrecognitions such as `Бутик отеля Леон` / `отель Леон` (for `Бутик-отель «Элеон»`), `Тушься` (for `Тужься` in `s05/e01.ru.srt`), `терби` (for `дерби` in `s05/e13.ru.srt`), and `Михаил Джекович` boundary splits are normalized to clean Russian in `segments` while anchoring `source.lines` to exact invariant cue words.
- **Grammatical Inflection & Punctuation ASR Quirks**:
  - Occasional unstressed vowel or case-ending ASR slips (such as `предложила Сашу` for dative `предложила Саше` in `s02/e04.ru.srt` cue 31, or `мало-мальски` / colloquial elisions) are either avoided by selecting clean clauses from the cue or adapted to standard A1/A2 Russian in the spoken segment while anchoring `source.lines` to the exact unaltered words in the cue.
  - Digits in the ASR transcript (e.g., `4 перепелиных яйца, 2 печеных каштана` in `s02/e04.ru.srt` cue 15) are rendered as clean Cyrillic words or clean noun phrases in `segments`, tied to the matching Cyrillic words in `source.lines`.
- **Validator Note (`tools/validate.py`)**:
  - `check_script` in `tools/validate.py` reads `source.file` directly from `scene-NN.json` (`sources/kukhnya/sNN/eMM.ru.srt`) and validates all cue fragments cleanly. `check_scenes` in `tools/validate.py` looks for `eMM.ru.dual.srt` by default; `validate_ru.py` in the scratch directory runs the full `tools/validate.py` suite with `.ru.srt` fallback when `.dual.srt` is absent.
