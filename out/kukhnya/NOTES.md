# Kukhnya (Кухня) — Authoring, Translation & Validation Notes

## Timing Gap Warnings (`validate.py`)
- `tools/validate.py` emits `WARN ... gap to prev is ...s (> 10s)` when music montages, kitchen prep sequences, or exterior transitions occur between subtitle cues in `.dual.srt` or `.ru.srt`. All spoken subtitle cues from cue 1 to the final cue of each episode are covered contiguously in `scenes.json` with `merge: null` on active scenes, and 100% of `source.lines` cues sit within their scene's `[start, end]` window.

## Show-Wide Canonical Voice & Role-Key Assignment (`P1.7` & `out/kukhnya/characters.md`)
- Per `spec/recipe.md` and `out/kukhnya/characters.md`, every recurring character across all 120 episodes (`s01e01`–`s06e20`) has one fixed canonical role key and Russian display label, and scenes avoid assigning the same TTS voice (`alisa`, `anja`, `angelina`) to two characters in the same scene:
  - `anya` -> `alisa` (`Макс`, plus non-overlapping viewpoint/guest speakers `Костя`, `Федя`, `Луи`, `Илья`, `Тимур`, `Алиса`, `Инспектор`, `Гость`, `Денис`, `Катя`, `Герман`)
  - `chef` (`Шеф`), `nastya` (`Настя`), `lyova` (`Лёва`), `nagiev` (`Нагиев`), `ainura` (`Айнура`), `kristina` (`Кристина`), `sasha` (`Саша`), `rodion` (`Родион`), `eleonora` (`Элеонора`), `mikhail` (`Михаил`) -> `anja`
  - `vika` (`Вика`), `senya` (`Сеня`), `elena` (`Елена`), `tanya` (`Таня`), `angelina` (`Ангелина`), `eva` (`Ева`), `gulnara` (`Гульнара`), `nikita` (`Никита`), `sveta` (`Света`) -> `angelina`
- Every scene ends with a short Russian farewell (`Пока!`, `До свидания!`, `До встречи!`, `До завтра!`, `Увидимся!`, `Хорошего дня!`, `Всего хорошего!`) spoken by one of the scene's dialogue characters and annotated in `scene-NN.notes.json`.
- Coarse/vulgar expressions in the raw transcript are softened or omitted in the authored A1–A2 lines while preserving natural kitchen banter.

## Whisper Machine-Recognition Transcript Notes (`eNN.ru.srt`, S2 `e04` – S6 `e20`)
Per `README.md` and `FEEDBACK.md`:
- **Whisper Machine-Recognised Transcripts (`sources/kukhnya/s02/e04.ru.srt` through `sources/kukhnya/s06/e20.ru.srt`, 97 episodes)**:
  - Unlike `s01e01`–`s02e03` (which have human-aligned `.ru.dual.srt` files), all 97 episodes from `s02e04` through `s06e20` are Russian-only **Whisper ASR machine-recognised transcripts** (`eNN.ru.srt`).
  - We translated all 97 episodes directly from Russian into idiomatic English and audited every cue used in `scene-NN.json` / `scene-NN.notes.json` for Whisper recognition errors:
    1. **Proper names & restaurant/hotel names**: Whisper frequently mishears *Claude Monet* (`«Клод Моне»`) as `«Клуб Моне»`, `«Кот Моне»`, or `«Клод Мане»`; *Arcobaleno* (`«Аркобалено»`) as `Аркабалена`; *Boutique Hotel Eleon* (`«Элеон»`) as `отель Леон` or `Леонория`; `Баринов` as `Баранов`; `Нагиев` as `Нагеев`/`Дагиев`; `Елизавета Генриховна` as `Елизавета Геодриховна` / `Или за это Генрихом на все`.
    2. **`Шеф` / `су-шеф` / `Лёва` forms**: Whisper strips `ё` across all cues (`Лева`, `Леве`, `Леву`, `Левой` for `Лёва`, `Лёве`, `Лёву`, `Лёвой`; `еще` for `ещё`; `все` for `всё`) and occasionally splits or merges `шеф-повар` / `су-шеф` (`шахповара`, `с Ушефом`, `отцу шефа`). In authored story lines and `notes.json`, `Лёва` and `Шеф`/`су-шеф` are restored to standard Russian spelling and stress while `source.lines` quotes the exact invariant substring from the `.ru.srt` cue.
    3. **Numbers & digits**: Whisper outputs Arabic numerals (`2`, `3`, `4`, `5`, `10`, `50`, `100`, `500`, etc.) in `.ru.srt` cues; in spoken `segments` and `notes.json` token lists, numerals are written out as full Russian words (`два`, `три`, `четыре`, `пять`, `сто`, etc.) or adapted to clean A1/A2 phrases.
    4. **French & Italian culinary terms**: Whisper phonetically approximates culinary vocabulary (`буабес, не суаться` / `несуаса` for `буйабес, нисуаз`; `Особука из телятины с гримолатой и палертой` for `Оссобуко из телятины с гремолатой и полентой`; `в единый шлен` / `звезду мечтан` for `в гиде Мишлен` / `звезду Мишлен`; `Амар` for `омар`). Authored segments use standard Russian spelling and anchor `source.lines` to exact invariant words in the cue.
