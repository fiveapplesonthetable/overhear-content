## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Take a good look.' The Chef points at the mess.
- 'This is your fault.' Blaming someone is easy.
- 'You nearly killed a man.' He exaggerates the poisoning.
- 'Chef, enough.' She tries to calm him down.
- 'He doesn't understand Russian.' The Japanese chef is confused.
- 'These are products from Timur.' The Chef shifts the blame to the supplier.
- 'Timur, come here.' They call the supplier over.
- 'A person got poisoned.' Stating the facts.
- 'We do not need your services.' A formal way to fire someone.
- 'That's it, you're dismissed.' The Chef is harsh.
- 'This is Tigran.' A new supplier appears instantly.
- 'Nice to meet you, Tigran.' Business moves fast.
- 'Well, then it's settled.' A new deal is struck.
