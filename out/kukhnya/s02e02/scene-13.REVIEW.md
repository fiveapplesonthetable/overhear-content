## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Samurai, why are you shouting in the kitchen?' Chef Viktor nicknames the sushi chef 'samurai'.
- 'Because no one is allowed to take his knife.' At this point, Max explains what triggered the outburst.
- 'He said that it is only his knife.' Every sushi master guards his personal blade closely.
- 'Good heavens, he will scare everyone right now!' Literally 'Japanese god', a playful Russian exclamation fitting the sushi theme.
- 'I just wanted to slice the fish quickly.' At this point, Max defends his innocent mistake.
- 'It is better not to joke with his sharp knife.' Senya warns Max to stay away from the sushi station.
- 'This new cook really annoys me.' Chef Viktor has had enough of the sushi chef in his French kitchen.
- 'He stops us from working normally.' Viktor complains about the disruption to the brigade.
- 'Make sure he is not here by tonight.' Viktor demands that the sushi chef be gone by evening.
- 'Gone for good?' The word at the start means 'really' or 'completely' in casual speech.
- 'Yes, gone for good.' Chef Viktor repeats the exact phrase to leave no doubt.
- 'Finally there will be order in the kitchen again.' Meanwhile, Max looks forward to peace returning to the kitchen.
