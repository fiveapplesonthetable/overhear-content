## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello.' A polite start to a hostile conversation.
- 'Konstantin Anisimov?' Checking the name.
- 'Yes, that is me.' Max makes a huge mistake by lying.
- 'Are you here to fix the internet?' Max was expecting a technician.
- 'I am Nastya's father.' The worst possible answer for Kostya's roommate.
- 'If you hurt Nastya, I will turn you into sausage.' A very creative threat.
- 'Wait.' Max tries to backpedal quickly.
- 'You understood incorrectly.' Trying to clear up the confusion.
- 'I am not Kostya.' He confesses the lie.
- 'I am Max.' Offering his real name for safety.
- 'And I am here to fix the internet.' The real technician finally shows up.
- 'Which one of you is Konstantin?' Now the technician is confused.
- 'Me.' The real Kostya speaks up at last.
