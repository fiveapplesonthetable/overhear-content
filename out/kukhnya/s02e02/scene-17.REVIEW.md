## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Vika, Kristina wants to tell you something.' Leading Vika up to his wife, Nagiyev prompts Kristina to say what they agreed on.
- 'You are not fired, I was simply joking.' Forced to swallow her pride, Kristina claims the firing was just a joke.
- 'You have a simply terrible sense of humor.' Vika dryly remarks that Kristina has a terrible sense of humor.
- 'But I am not planning to return here.' Still hurt, Vika declares that she has no intention of coming back to work here.
- 'Well, no means no.' Delighted for an excuse, Kristina shrugs and says no means no.
- 'Vika, what are you doing?' Panicking that the restaurant will fall apart without Vika, Nagiyev asks what she is doing.
- 'Well, you showed off and that is enough.' He tells Vika gently that she has made her point and does not need to stay stubborn.
- 'I will not return.' Vika stands firm and repeats that she will not return.
- 'What does it mean, you do not want to?' Nagiyev cannot believe she really wants to walk away.
- 'Can you really abandon all this?' He asks how she could possibly abandon everything they built together.
- 'This is your life.' He reminds her that Claude Monet is her whole life.
- 'Everything here is made by your efforts.' Every detail of the dining room runs on her hard work.
- 'You bought these plush sofas yourself!' Trying to be sentimental, Nagiyev even points out that she picked out the plush sofas herself.
