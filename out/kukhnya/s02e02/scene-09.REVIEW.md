## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'All right, I am ready.' Gathering the supplies.
- 'Let's go.' They are heading out.
- 'All right, see you.' Their colleague bids them farewell.
- 'Say hi to the bums.' A rather rude joke.
- 'Not bums, but homeless.' Kostya corrects the rude term.
- 'Look.' A warning word.
- 'Do not bring infection home.' She is worried about hygiene.
- 'From whom?' He asks playfully.
- 'From the homeless.' She clarifies her worry.
- 'We will be careful.' A promise to stay clean.
- 'I hope.' She remains skeptical.
- 'Until evening.' They will be back later.
- 'Bye.' They finally leave.
