- 'We will hide the ring inside her pastry.' Highlighting the pastries beside Nagiyev, Vika reaches for the Russian word "pirozhnoye" ("pastries, cakes") right at the heart of the line.
- 'That is an excellent idea.' Emphasizing how excellent things are on the spot, Nagiyev links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Not Vika—Vitya.' Nagiyev steps in pointedly without holding back, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'The ring is under your personal responsibility.' Touching on the responsibility during the talk, Nagiyev puts the emphasis on "otvetstvennost" ("responsibility") in the middle of the talk.
- 'I beg you, be very careful with it.' Vika pushes back evenly with a nod, letting the remark speak for itself.

- 'Do not worry about it.' Vika responds calmly with Nagiyev and centers the thought on "volnuysya" ("worry", from "volnovatsya") without wasting a word.
- 'Everything will be fine.' Focusing on the order right here, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 4 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'How are things going with you?' Vika wonders briskly if there is more going on behind the scenes.
