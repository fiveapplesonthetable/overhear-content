## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I have an idea.' Having an idea moves the conversation forward without extra drama. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Get out of here.' Nagiyev reminds Vika that Vika should get out of here before the moment passes.
- 'We should put the ring inside a pastry.' Pointing out that they should put the ring inside a pastry gives Nagiyev a sobering reality check. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'You remember how much she loved that pastry, right?' Asking whether Vika remembers how much she loved that pastry right puts Vika right on the defensive. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'We will hide the ring inside her pastry.' Realizing that they will hide the ring inside her pastry changes how Vika handles the situation.
- 'That is an excellent idea.' Noting that that is an excellent idea keeps Nagiyev's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Not Vika—Vitya.' Over the conversation and the truth, Vika answers Nagiyev without a second thought.
- 'The ring is under your personal responsibility.' Admitting that the ring is under Vika's personal responsibility shows Nagiyev being honest for a change.
- 'I beg you, be very careful with it.' Noting that she begs Nagiyev is very careful with it keeps Vika's side of the story simple and clear. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'First of all, it is expensive, and second, I bought it on credit...' Once first of all it is expensive and second he bought it on credit, Nagiyev can finally tell Vika what he means.
- 'Do not worry about it.' Adding that do not worry about it fills in the missing piece for Nagiyev.
- 'Everything will be fine.' Nagiyev tells Vika that everything will be fine without dressing it up.
- 'How are things going with you?' Wondering how things going with Nagiyev are cuts straight through the tension between Vika and Nagiyev. Notice how she sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
