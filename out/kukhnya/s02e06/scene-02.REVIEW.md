## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Excuse me, but at the moment your parking spot is taken.' Barinov mentions that at the moment Max's parking spot is taken so Max sees what is at stake. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'You knew it was mine.' Claiming that Barinov knew it was sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'And for how many years now.' With the boss and moment in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'In short.' Over the boss and the conversation, Max answers Barinov without a second thought.
- 'I understand, everything is fine.' Because he understands everything is fine, Barinov treats the matter as settled.
- 'A man drove up and paid for it.' Noting that a man drove up and paid for it keeps Max's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'What an unexpected meeting.' Around the friend and guest, Barinov sets a calm, everyday tone with Max.
- 'And what are you doing here?' By asking what Barinov doing here is, Max presses Barinov for a straight answer. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'By the way, he is the one who took your spot.' Once he is the one who took Max's spot, Barinov can finally tell Max what he means.
- 'Chef, I have a counter-proposal.' Noting that he has a counter proposal keeps Max's side of the story simple and clear. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'I will handle it myself, Chef.' Adding that he will handle it himself fills in the missing piece for Max.
- 'Damn, I screwed up again.' Around the friend and point, Max sets a calm, everyday tone with Barinov.
- 'How was I supposed to know?' Asking how he supposed to know was is how Barinov puts Max on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
