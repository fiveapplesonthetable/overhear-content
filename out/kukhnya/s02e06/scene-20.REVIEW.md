- 'For the owner to marry that shrew.' Between the work and the kitchen, Barinov gives Kristina a quick, grounded response.

- 'Perhaps.' Around the work and conversation, Barinov sets a calm, everyday tone with Kristina.

- 'Here is your bill.' Barinov tells Kristina that here is Kristina's bill without dressing it up.
