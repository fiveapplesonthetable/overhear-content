## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, forgive us, I really did not mean to.' Max responds wryly toward Barinov, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'And I do not care at all anymore.' Barinov answers sharply right here and uses "sovershenno" ("quite, completely") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'We have nothing at all to feed the guests in the hall.' Discussing the guests in front of Max, Barinov adds "sovsem" ("completely, at all") so the statement leaves zero room for doubt.
- 'It is all over, our restaurant is finished.' Barinov counters steadily hearing Max, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'That means I will not have to tolerate you anymore.' Barinov reacts promptly during the talk and centers the thought on "pridyotsya" ("you'll have to", from "priytis") without wasting a word.
- 'And you too, I am going home right now.' Barinov responds boldly with a smile, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'There is nothing to feed the guests, we must do something fast.' Max weighs in sharply with a shrug, watching Barinov's face to see how the news lands.
- 'Do not think that we are skimping on electricity.' Bringing up the electricity on the spot, Nagiyev brings in "elektrichestve" ("on electricity", from "elektrichestvo") as the key Russian word here.
- 'We are the true elite of Moscow's restaurant business.' Underscoring how real things are at once, Nagiyev links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'We are trying to create a special warm atmosphere here.' Nagiyev reacts right away with a smile, testing Max's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Dima, and when will they finally bring us food?' Max reacts naturally beside Nagiyev and brings in "nakonets" ("at last, finally") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Do I need to invest money in the restaurant first?' Bringing up the restaurant at once, Max leans on "nuzhno" ("it is needed, one has to"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
