## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, what difference does it make who earns money?' Checking on the difference beside Barinov, Max puts the question word "kakaya" right at the start so Barinov hears the focus immediately.
- 'A real restaurant makes good food.' Barinov speaks up honestly during the talk, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'Only Louis's accent will remain in our restaurant.' Bringing up his restaurant during the talk, Barinov puts the weight on "restorane" ("restaurant", from "restoran") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'Because they will kick all of us out of here.' Barinov responds decisively in the exchange and lands on "vygonyat" ("will throw out", built on "vygnat") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Guys, attention, everyone listen carefully.' Nagiyev answers steadily with Barinov, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Today we have a banquet for an important man.' Touching on a banquet beside Barinov, Nagiyev leans on "vazhnogo" ("important", a form of "vazhnyy") to give the remark its edge.
- 'He is a very important man with big money.' Describing how important he is with Barinov, Nagiyev links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'So let us do everything at the highest level.' Nagiyev weighs in candidly facing Barinov, before Barinov can change the subject.
- 'Did everyone understand me? Come on, brothers, to work!' Touching on the brothers in this beat, Nagiyev centers the thought on "brattsy" ("brothers, fellows", from "bratets") without wasting a word.
- 'We are even supposed to help sell ourselves more expensively!' Barinov proceeds calmly with Nagiyev and uses "podorozhe" ("more expensively", from "dorogoy") to make the point stick.
- 'No way, they will not live to see that!' Barinov continues openly beside Nagiyev, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'We will throw such a banquet for this guest today!' Mentioning the guest at once, Barinov leans on "gostyu" ("to the guest", a form of "gost") to give the remark its edge.
