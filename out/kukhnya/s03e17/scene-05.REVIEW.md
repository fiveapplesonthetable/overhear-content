## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, what difference does it make who earns money?' Max shapes that line without pause: putting the question word "kakaya" right at the front of the line lets Barinov hear the point of the question on the very first beat.
- 'A real restaurant makes good food.' Barinov speaks up honestly on the spot, making his position completely clear.
- 'Only Louis's accent will remain in our restaurant.' Barinov uses one key word toward Max: "restorane" means "restaurant" (from "restoran"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Because they will kick all of us out of here.' Barinov stands firm sharply as they talk, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'Guys, attention, everyone listen carefully.' Nagiyev follows up steadily on the spot, shifting the whole mood of the room in a single beat.
- 'Today we have a banquet for an important man.' Nagiyev reaches for this sharp term on cue: "vazhnogo" means "important" (from "vazhnyy"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'He is a very important man with big money.' Nagiyev shapes that answer facing Barinov: notice how Russian needs no word for "am", "is", or "are" in the present — placing the words side by side says it all.
- 'So let us do everything at the highest level.' Nagiyev counters candidly to be clear, before Max can change the subject.
- 'Did everyone understand me? Come on, brothers, to work!' Nagiyev stresses this sharp piece at once: "brattsy" means "brothers, fellows" (from "bratets"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'We are even supposed to help sell ourselves more expensively!' Barinov speaks up pointedly facing Nagiyev, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'No way, they will not live to see that!' Barinov continues openly with a smile, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'We will throw such a banquet for this guest today!' Barinov reaches for one loaded element on cue: "gostyu" means "to the guest" (from "gost"), giving the line its exact edge.
