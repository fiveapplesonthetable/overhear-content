## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Imagine, they carried off the entire safe.' Nagiyev explains that Kristina should imagine they carried off the entire safe, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'With all the money and everything inside.' With the truth and deal in play, Nagiyev keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Oh, what a difficult situation you have.' Max reminds Kristina that what a difficult situation Kristina has before the moment passes. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'I sincerely sympathize with you, Dima.' Around the truth and deal, Max sets a calm, everyday tone with Kristina.
- 'You will like my new purchase offer.' Explaining that Kristina will like his new purchase offer lays the cards on the table for Kristina. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Why are you standing here listening?' Wondering why Kristina standing here listening is is how Nagiyev puts Kristina on the spot. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Go and do some work in the dining room.' Mentioning that Kristina should go and do some work in the dining room moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Stand calmly and smile nicely.' Standing calmly and smile nicely is how Nagiyev lays out the situation for Kristina.
- 'Come on, go back to work quickly.' Because Kristina should come on go back to work quickly, Nagiyev treats the matter as settled. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'That man offered Nagiyev to sell the restaurant.' With the kitchen and deal in play, Kristina keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'And what did our Nagiyev answer him?' Here asking what their Nagiyev answer him shows Max looking for the real truth. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'He just sent me back to work.' Even though he just sent her back to work, Kristina keeps a steady face in front of Max.
