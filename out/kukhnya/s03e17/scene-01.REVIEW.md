- 'A team of professional chefs works here.' Talking about a team right here, Max reaches for "u nas" ("by us, at our place") to talk about what they share.
- 'There are always products of the highest quality here.' Max weighs in honestly in their talk, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Our guest service is at the highest level.' Highlighting the service beside Max, the hostess reaches for the Russian word "obsluzhivaniye" ("service") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Excuse me, a waiter will come to you soon.' The hostess cuts in boldly in this moment, so Max knows she is not just making small talk.
- 'We are not in much of a hurry today.' Max counters briskly watching the hostess, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'There are very many orders in the dining room today.' Bringing up the orders beside Max, the hostess centers the thought on "zakazov" ("orders", from "zakaz") without wasting a word.
- 'We understand everything perfectly.' Holding firm naturally around the hostess, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 4 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Of course, our restaurant needs a good owner.' Max counters wryly with a sigh, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
