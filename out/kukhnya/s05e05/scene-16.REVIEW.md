## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Stop, hand over the bag!' The guest highlights this direct choice for Barinov: "otdayte" means "give, hand over" (from "otdat"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'What is going on, this is my bag!' Elena voices that reply beside the guest: notice how Russian needs no word for "am", "is", or "are" in the present — placing the words side by side says it all.
- 'Security, give back my bag!' The guest responds pointedly with a nod, making his position completely clear.
- 'Alright, what happened?' Barinov centers one loaded element on cue: "sluchilos" means "happened" (from "sluchitsya"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Take your hands off me!' Elena hands over the comment facing Barinov: spoken Russian trims this down to just 4 words, dropping the extra helper words that English has to spell out.
- 'Quiet, quiet, what on earth is happening?' Barinov challenges plainly what the guest is actually getting at here.
- 'You were completely right.' The guest grounds one memorable choice for Barinov: "sovershenno" means "quite, completely", and it carries the main weight of what the guest is saying.
- 'She is a thief!' The guest shares that answer toward Elena: notice how Russian needs no word for "am", "is", or "are" in the present — placing the words side by side says it all.
- 'What?' Elena prompts calmly to see how Barinov will answer on the spot.
- 'Let her go, this is a misunderstanding.' Barinov frames one key detail right here: "nedorazumeniye" means "misunderstanding", and it carries the main weight of what Barinov is saying.
- 'Thank you for your vigilance.' The guest shares this warning calmly now: the everyday word "spasibo" ("thank you") grew out of an old blessing meaning "save you", and it anchors the whole line here.
- 'Vitya, explain to me what this means!' Elena speaks up firmly across the room, grounding the moment in plain reality.
