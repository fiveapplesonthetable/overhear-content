- 'Forgive me, this is very important.' Here the Russian phrasing highlights 'forgive' before landing on 'important'.
- 'I need to know the truth.' Max brings out the contrast between 'to me' and 'needed' in a few short words.
- 'I cannot tell you anything.' Here the Russian phrasing highlights 'nothing' before landing on 'to say'.
- 'The thing is that this is my child.' The grammar packs 'business' and 'child' into a tight, idiomatic kitchen exchange.
