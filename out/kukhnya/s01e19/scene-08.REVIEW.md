## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, just do not worry.' Kostya tries to comfort his friend after the flower incident.
- 'And how can I not worry here?' Max cannot hide his frustration over Victoria's wealthy admirer.
- 'Did you see that huge bouquet?' He points out the giant roses that just arrived.
- 'The size of the bouquet is not important at all.' Kostya insists that grand gifts are not everything.
- 'The main thing is not the size of the boat.' Kostya recites the first half of a philosophical quote.
- 'The main thing is the swell of the ocean.' He finishes the metaphor about passion and feelings.
- 'Wait, where are such beautiful words from?' Max is suspicious of Kostya's sudden poetry.
- 'Did you read someone's status on the internet?' Max immediately guesses where the quote came from.
- 'Yes, it is Nastya's new status.' Kostya admits he read it on Nastya's social media page.
- 'She wrote it this morning.' He mentions when the post appeared.
- 'I wonder what she is hinting at?' Literally 'interesting, about what is she?', a natural way to say 'I wonder what she means'.
- 'Probably she is writing about your relationship.' Max leaves Kostya with a new worry of his own.
