## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, what are you doing here?' Vika asks what he is doing here in the parking lot.
- 'I am just standing and breathing air.' Max casually claims he is just standing and breathing the fresh air.
- 'Are you following me again?' Vika suspects he is following her again.
- 'You have an important business meeting right now.' Max points out that she has an important business meeting right now.
- 'Max, tell the truth, are you jealous?' Vika demands he tell her honestly if he is jealous of the other man.
- 'I am not jealous of you at all, Vika.' Max insists he is not jealous of her at all.
- 'This is a meeting regarding the sale of the restaurant.' Vika clarifies that the meeting is strictly about selling the restaurant.
- 'I am calm, go to your meeting.' Max tells her to go to her meeting and asserts he is completely calm.
- 'Please drive my car home.' Vika asks him for a favor to drive her car home.
- 'And you will go in his car?' Max asks if she will be traveling in the stranger's car.
- 'Yes, we will go together to negotiations.' Vika confirms they will travel together to the negotiations.
- 'It is time for me to go, bye, Max.' Vika says her goodbyes as it is time for her to depart.
- 'Yes, of course, safe travels, Vika.' Max wishes her a safe trip.
