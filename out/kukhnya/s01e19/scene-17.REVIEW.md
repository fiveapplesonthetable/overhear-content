## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What are you doing here?' Dima is caught by surprise. We use 'are' in English, but Russian skips it.
- 'You are supposed to be in Monaco!' The word for 'supposed' or 'must' has a feminine ending, because Dima is talking to a woman.
- 'But I came back!' Kristina is furious. The verb is in the past tense feminine.
- 'You sent me there on purpose.' The verb is in the past tense masculine, matching 'you'.
- 'Who is this woman, Dima?' She demands to know the truth. The word 'is' is not translated.
- 'I don't know anything.' Dima plays dumb. Notice the double negative in Russian: 'nothing not know'.
- 'Sasha told me everything.' Sasha is the waitress who gossips. The object 'me' comes first.
- 'She works here and sees everything.' Kristina trusts the waitress completely. Both verbs are in the present tense.
- 'Can I say something?' Max steps in to save Vika. Literally, 'Is it possible I will say?'.
- 'Victoria Sergeyevna is my girlfriend.' Max uses Vika's formal name to show respect. The word for 'is' is replaced by a dash in writing.
- 'We have a serious relationship.' Literally, 'By us with her are serious relations'.
- 'Let your wife apologize to my girlfriend!' Max demands respect. The word for 'let' is used to make a strong suggestion or command.
- 'Let's go home, my dear.' Dima quickly tries to defuse the situation. The word for 'dear' is a feminine adjective.
- 'Tomorrow we will fly to rest, okay?' He promises her a vacation. The verb for 'fly' is in the future tense.
