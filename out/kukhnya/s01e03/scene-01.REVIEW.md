## Part 1 — Intro

> Max arrives in the Claude Monet kitchen eager to start cooking after a full week of manual unloading. Instead of a saucepan, Chef Viktor Barinov hands him a live goose delivered for tonight's grand banquet. Barinov declares that every true cook must be able to slaughter and prepare their own meat. Let's listen to their confrontation in Russian first.
