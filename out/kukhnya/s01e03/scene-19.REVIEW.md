## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, excuse us for this noise.' The dining guest graciously apologizes for her companion's drunken outburst.
- 'Do not worry, this pistol was not real.' She reassures the alarmed chef that the weapon was harmless.
- 'Here is generous money for these inconveniences.' She hands over compensation for the staff's troubles.
- 'Thank you, we are glad to help our guests.' Barinov politely accepts the apology and generous payment.
- 'Runt, come here right now!' The chef turns his attention back to his troublesome new cook.
- 'Chef, do you want to fire me?' Max nervously asks if his employment has come to an end.
- 'Let us sum up your first work week.' Barinov begins reciting the astonishing list of workplace catastrophes.
- 'On the first day you almost poisoned a guest.' The chef recalls the near-fatal unauthorized food swap.
- 'On the second day you started a brawl in the bar.' The chef details the clash with bartender Kostya.
- 'You angered our chief art director.' Barinov highlights the ongoing tension between Max and Vika.
- 'And today you were almost shot!' The chef marvels at the latest bizarre incident with the gun.
- 'Chef, I promise to work much better.' Max earnestly pleads for another chance to prove his skill.
- 'Looks like you fit right into the team!' The chef delivers his surprising verdict with dry humor.
- 'Let us head quickly to work in our kitchen.' Barinov warmly welcomes the newcomer back to the stove.
