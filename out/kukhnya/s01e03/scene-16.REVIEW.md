## Part 1 — Intro

> Chef Viktor Barinov personally brings a complimentary birthday course to a table of influential guests. The guest of honor is delighted and hints at useful connections, but the chef's perfectionism takes over upon tasting. Listen to their polite conversation in Russian first, then we will break down each line in English.
