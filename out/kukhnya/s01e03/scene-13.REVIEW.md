## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, did you bring that special remedy?' "Brought" is a man's past, a carrying word. "That special" takes the matching endings, and "remedy" is a general word for a means.
- 'Yes, chef, this is a strong remedy.' "Strong" comes from "sila", strength. And "this is" is just "eto".
- 'This is brand new powerful medicine.' "Powerful" is from "power", and "medicine" is a pharmacy drug. Senya is selling it.
- 'How quickly does it start to act?' "Act" comes from "action". "It" is the neuter one, matching "remedy".
- 'Very quickly, chef, literally right away.' "Literally" starts from "letter", so it means "to the letter". "Right away" is the short word.
- 'One pill solves all your problems.' "One" is feminine, because "pill" is. "Solves" is the same word as "decides", and "all your" takes the plural.
- 'And what will be the exact effect?' "Exact" is a word you know. "Effect" is borrowed, so it's easy.
- 'I promise, the customer will not leave the toilet!' "Will not step away" needs "from" and the toilet in the "from" form. Senya is rude.
- 'Excellent, the judge will remember this anniversary!' "Judge" is a feminine word, even though it ends in "-ya". "Zapomnit'" takes "za-" to mean "commit to memory".
- 'Shall we add the pill into her soup?' "Her" never changes, like "his". Adding is "dobavit'", the same root as "seconds".
- 'No, we will put the powder directly on the goose.' Putting something flat takes "polozhit'", not the word for standing it up. "Powder" is a short word, and "goose" ends in "-ya".
- 'The main thing is that other guests do not get poisoned.' "The main thing, so that" is the pattern for what matters. "Get poisoned" has "self" on the end.
- 'Do not fear, I will do everything personally.' "Don't be afraid" is a command with "self" on the end. You've heard "personally" before.
