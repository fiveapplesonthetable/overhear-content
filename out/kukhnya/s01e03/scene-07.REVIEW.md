## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Here is dessert from our pastry chef.' Viktor Barinov politely presents the complimentary dish.
- 'Thank you for changing the menu for me.' The guest expresses gratitude for accommodating her request.
- 'I am the chef and I fulfill any orders.' Barinov proudly boasts of his culinary flexibility.
- 'And what do you do, if it is not a secret?' Barinov casually inquires about her profession.
- 'I work as a judge at the Tverskoy court.' The woman reveals her prestigious judicial position.
- 'Whoa! And they suspended my license yesterday!' Barinov shares his recent driving misfortune.
- 'What is your last name?' The judge asks for his identity with sudden legal curiosity.
- 'My last name is Barinov. Viktor Barinov.' The chef introduces himself without anticipating the trap.
- 'So it was I who issued that decision!' The judge recognizes the defendant from her morning docket.
- 'Maybe we can fix everything?' Barinov immediately tries to negotiate an informal compromise.
- 'No. You knocked down a traffic light on the road!' The judge sternly cites the egregious violation.
- 'But I had a valid excuse.' Barinov attempts a futile defense of his reckless behavior.
- 'Say thank you that the suspension is only a year.' The judge reminds him that the sentence could have been worse.
