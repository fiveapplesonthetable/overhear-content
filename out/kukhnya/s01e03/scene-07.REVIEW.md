## Part 1 — Intro

> In the dining room of Claude Monet, Viktor Barinov personally brings a traditional dessert to an important guest. Bragging about his culinary stature, the Chef is horrified to discover that the woman is the exact judge who recently suspended his driving license. Let's listen to their confrontation in Russian first.
