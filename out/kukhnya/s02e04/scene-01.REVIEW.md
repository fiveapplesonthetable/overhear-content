## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Everything in my life is great.' Saying that everything in his life is great is how Barinov sets the tone with Sasha.
- 'No, I am serious.' Explaining that she is serious lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
- 'I started dating a new girl, Sasha.' Adding that she started dating a new girl fills in the missing piece for Barinov.
- 'She is outgoing and beautiful.' Barinov tells Sasha that she is outgoing and beautiful without dressing it up.
- 'And overall everything is going well for me.' Since everything is going well, Vika does not waste words on excuses. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Good morning.' Between the trip and the visit, Sasha gives Barinov a quick, grounded response.
- 'I have a surprise for you.' Barinov tells Sasha that he has a surprise for Sasha without dressing it up.
- 'Kris gave me two vouchers to Cyprus.' Because someone gave her two vouchers to Cyprus, Vika treats the matter as settled.
- 'Something did not work out for her there.' Explaining that something did not work out for her there lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
- 'Let us fly together.' Claiming that they should fly together sounds confident, even if Sasha has doubts.
- 'Wait, I do not understand?' Questioning whether she does not understand is how Vika tests Barinov's story. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, this man is a genius.' Pointing out that this man is a genius gives Barinov a sobering reality check.
- 'And throw all of this into the trash.' Barinov tells Sasha that Sasha should throw all of this into the trash without dressing it up.
