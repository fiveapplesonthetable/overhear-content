## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Enjoy your meal, my dear Arkady.' Barinov warmly addresses his pet fish in the tank.
- 'You feed the fish better than our guests.' Vika sarcastically notes that the fish eats better than their customers.
- 'Arkady, be quiet and do not listen to her.' Barinov tells the fish to ignore the manager's complaints.
- 'Do you remember that tomorrow is the first?' Vika reminds him that tomorrow is the first of the month, meaning bills are due.
- 'I will return your money in a week.' Barinov promises to pay back his loan in a week.
- 'You promised to return the debt today.' Vika points out he originally promised to settle the debt today.
- 'Your money is with the footballer Magidi right now.' Barinov blames a football player, explaining his cash is tied up in a bet.
- 'He missed the goal, what has he to do with it?' Vika dismisses the excuse, bringing up the player's missed shot.
- 'Ask for your money directly from him.' Barinov deflects the blame, suggesting she collect her cash from the athlete.
- 'You promised a new seasonal dish, Viktor.' Vika pivots to demanding the new seasonal dish he promised.
- 'When money is needed, one always promises a lot.' Barinov deflects by claiming people promise anything when they need cash.
- 'Tomorrow I expect the money or the dish.' Vika gives him an ultimatum for tomorrow.
- 'Fine, Vika, I will come up with something.' Barinov finally caves and promises to invent a new dish.
