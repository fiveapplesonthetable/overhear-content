## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, well where are you?' Vika calls him impatiently.
- 'Are you coming soon?' She wants to know his ETA.
- 'I am already here.' He is waiting outside her door.
- 'Why are you standing there?' She is confused by his delay.
- 'I am waiting for my friends.' He reveals he is not alone.
- 'What friends?' She has no idea what he is talking about.
- 'Nastya and Kostya.' He names his colleagues from the restaurant.
- 'Did you invite them here?' She is shocked by his boldness.
- 'Yes, it is a surprise.' He thinks it is a great idea.
- 'I am not ready for guests.' She is unprepared for a party.
- 'Everything will be fine.' He tries to reassure her.
- 'Come in faster.' She tells him to enter the apartment.
