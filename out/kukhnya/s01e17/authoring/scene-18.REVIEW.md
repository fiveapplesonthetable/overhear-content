- 'Well, Senya, you are a corpse.' Viktor threatens him with dire consequences.
- 'Chef, it is not me!' The cook pleads with his boss.
- 'Who then?' Viktor demands a name.
- 'Maybe Fedya.' He tries to pass the blame to his friend.
- 'I do not believe you.' The chef refuses to buy his story.
- 'Honest word!' He swears he is telling the truth.
