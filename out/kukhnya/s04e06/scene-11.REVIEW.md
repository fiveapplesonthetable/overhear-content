## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Lena, well how did you like today's relaxation?' The idiom 'how is it to you' asks for someone's impression.
- 'Excellent, I had already forgotten how relaxing it is!' Feminine past-tense verb marks Elena as the speaker.
- 'I am very glad that you liked everything.' Masculine short adjective 'glad' pairs with an impersonal dative verb.
- 'But that is far from the whole surprise.' Negated determiner 'all/the whole' builds suspense for the second part of his plan.
- 'I booked a table for us for seven o'clock.' Preposition 'for' with the accusative hour specifies the reservation time.
- 'Vitya, tell me honestly, what is going on today?' Elena is intrigued by Barinov's sudden romantic gestures.
- 'Lena, believe me, I really need to talk with you.' Singular perfective imperative 'believe' adds emotional sincerity.
- 'And it is more convenient to talk in a calm evening setting.' Comparative predicate adverb 'more convenient' takes an infinitive.
- 'Well all right, but after all we have work right now.' Elena hesitates to leave her restaurant during the dinner rush.
- 'Do not worry, Lyova will supervise everything here himself.' Perfective future verb promises complete oversight.
- 'Vitya, are you definitely sure about that?' Masculine short adjective 'sure' takes 'in' plus prepositional case.
- 'Yes, Elena Pavlovna, I will supervise everything!' Lyova loyally backs up Barinov's scheme so they can leave.
