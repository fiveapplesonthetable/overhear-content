## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'So, dear guest, what will you say about the wine?' Nagiyev eagerly awaits praise for his five-thousand-dollar bottle.
- 'This is a young Spanish wine, if I am not mistaken.' The sommelier's palate immediately detects the Spanish table wine.
- 'Last year's harvest, it costs six hundred rubles a bottle.' the guest pairs 'last' with 'bottle' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
- 'Bravo to you, Aleksey, goodbye and farewell!' Believing the bottle is genuine, Nagiyev thinks the expert is incompetent.
- 'We will definitely call you back later, all the best!' Prefixed future verb 'will call back' is a polite brush-off.
- 'Nothing but deceivers all around, real swindlers surround us!' In everyday Russian, 'all around' flows straight into 'surround' without extra linking words.
- 'Dmitry Vladimirovich, and what happened over there right now?' Kostya holds his breath at the bar counter.
- 'I wanted to pay this sommelier a huge salary!' Notice how Russian puts 'wanted' and 'huge' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'And he cannot tell elite wine from cheap wine!' Here Nagiyev anchors the thought on 'wine' and finishes on 'cheap, inexpensive', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'Kostya, here, taste it yourself from this glass!' Nagiyev hands the glass to Kostya for validation.
- 'A very tart taste, you can taste clover and honey!' Kostya plays along brilliantly with poetic tasting notes.
- 'Exactly right, you can immediately tell real expensive wine!' Max and Kostya are miraculously saved by Nagiyev's vanity.
