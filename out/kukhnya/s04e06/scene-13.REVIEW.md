## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good evening, are you waiting for the head chef?' Lyova awkwardly checks a random table in the dining room.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, I cannot run to every table!' Multidirectional infinitive 'to run around' takes the dative phrase.
- 'How will I find out whom exactly you are waiting for?' Animate accusative 'whom' introduces the object question.
- 'Photograph the guests on your phone and send it to me.' Two singular perfective imperatives solve the identification problem.
- 'Lyova, eat a carrot, it is good for the brain!' Short neuter adjective 'useful' governs 'for' plus the genitive singular.
- 'And here is your friend, greet him quickly!' Barinov spots Volodya in the photo Lyova sends him.
- 'Hello, Viktor Petrovich, your guests are already in the hall.' Lyova welcomes Barinov at the back door of Arcobaleno.
- 'Excellent, right now I will quickly cook them a hot dish.' Substantivized neuter adjective 'hot' means a main course.
- 'And then I will immediately run back to Lena.' Perfective future 'will run' captures his frantic dash next door.
- 'Just make sure that Elena Pavlovna does not notice anything!' Lyova warns him that the ruse is risky.
- 'And here I am back in the dining room again!' Out of breath, Barinov slides back into his chair opposite Elena.
- 'I hope they will not bring our dessert anytime soon.' Meanwhile, Nagiyev is arriving at Claude Monet for his wine.
