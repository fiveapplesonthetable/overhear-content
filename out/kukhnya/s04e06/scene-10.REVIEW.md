## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Denis, so you are working for Louis today?' Preposition 'for' with the accusative means substituting for someone.
- 'Then give me two French pastries, please.' Numeral 'two' requires the genitive singular form on substantivized neuter nouns.
- 'What trouble, Louis labeled everything in a foreign language!' Adverb with 'not in Russian' explains why Denis cannot read the notes.
- 'And how am I to understand which dessert is where?' Dative pronoun with an infinitive forms a deliberative question.
- 'Denis, I also need a cherry tart with cream!' Instrumental plural 'with cream' describes the dessert topping.
- 'Great, now some complicated tart on top of that!' Indefinite determiner expresses frustration with the unfamiliar menu.
- 'Listen, let me quickly help you figure it out.' Senya spots an opportunity for free sweets.
- 'Senya, tell me, which of these pastries should I hand out?' Preposition 'out of' governs the genitive plural.
- 'Right now we will look closely and taste everything.' Two perfective future verbs sound helpful while Senya grabs a spoon.
- 'No, this is definitely not that pastry, give me another.' Senya finishes one cake and immediately reaches for the next.
- 'What are you doing, you ate everything!' Denis realizes Senya has no idea which French pastry is which.
- 'Go away from here, Senya, I will figure it out myself!' Imperative of motion sends the prankster back to his meat station.
