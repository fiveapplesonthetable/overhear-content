## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Fedya, where is the cod in Martian salt?' Even on Mars a hundred years from now, Fedya is still frying cod in the kitchen.
- 'Lyova, will the chicken be ready soon?' The feminine short-form adjective 'ready' agrees with 'chicken'.
- 'Yes, chef, just a couple more seconds.' The noun for 'pair/couple' governs the genitive plural of 'seconds'.
- 'Chef, Nagiyev is calling you right now.' Lyova points to the futuristic communicator.
- 'Soon the Emperor of the Galaxy will fly in to us!' The verb 'will arrive by air/space' fits an intergalactic VIP.
- 'Dima, you have to warn me in advance!' The adverb 'in advance' pairs with the imperfective infinitive 'to warn'.
- 'Vitya, that is completely impossible.' Hologram Nagiyev shakes his head at the complaint.
- 'The lifespan of these creatures is seven hours.' Numbers from five upward require the genitive plural ('seven hours').
- 'Born, ate a meal, and died right away.' Three rapid masculine past-tense verbs summarize the alien emperor's seven-hour life.
- 'Do not worry, Dima, we will do everything.' The reflexive command 'do not worry' reassures the owner.
- 'We will host the guest in first-class style!' The idiom 'by the highest grade' means 'first-class' or 'royally'.
- 'Right, otherwise it is the end for all of us.' The conjunction 'otherwise' warns of galactic consequences if dinner is late.
