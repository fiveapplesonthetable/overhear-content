## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Bro, you just bit me!' Tied-up Senya manages to chomp Fedya's shoulder.
- 'We should have shot you right away.' Again, 'need' plus neuter past 'was' forms the regretful phrase 'should have'.
- 'Here are brains in red wine.' In his own story, German thrives during the apocalypse by cooking haute cuisine for zombies.
- 'And a dish with pine nuts.' In Russian, pine nuts are called 'cedar little nuts' and take the instrumental plural after 'with'.
- 'Also pâté made from goose brains.' The preposition 'out of/from' plus the genitive plural describes what a dish is made of.
- 'And a fresh brain smoothie.' A relational adjective built from 'brain' modifies the drink.
- 'Senya, hand me the big knife.' German smiles chillingly as he asks his zombie sous-chef for a knife.
- 'Yeah, that is quite a cheerful story you have.' Back in reality, Max reacts with dry irony to German's gruesome tale.
- 'Our team turns out rather strange in it.' The reflexive verb means 'turns out' or 'comes out looking like'.
- 'Not a funny movie at all.' Fedya shudders at how everyone in German's story turned on one another.
- 'Let's just go back to boiling coconuts instead.' Even boiling coconuts sounds better than German's zombie kitchen.
- 'Get to work, do not get distracted.' German walks off pleased with the impression he made.
