## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'All right then, you hero of ours.' Putting the possessive 'our' after the noun adds teasing irony.
- 'Why did you run away yesterday then?' Fedya reminds Senya how he actually ran away from teenagers on the street the day before.
- 'Well, I simply do not hit children.' Senya excuses his retreat using the first-person singular of 'to beat/hit'.
- 'What kind of movies do you watch yourself?' Now Senya turns the tables and asks about Fedya's viewing habits.
- 'I only watch romantic movies.' Fedya claims his favorite films are purely about romance.
- 'Hi there, beautiful girls!' In his daydream, Fedya arrives dressed as a handsome pizza courier.
- 'Did someone order a pizza to a restaurant?' The accusative 'into the restaurant' marks the destination of the delivery.
- 'This is the first time I have seen that.' Russian uses the present tense 'I see' with 'first time' where English uses 'I have seen'.
- 'We have a sanitary cleaning day today.' Russian restaurants and shops close periodically for a 'sanitary day' of deep cleaning.
- 'We really felt like having something hot.' The diminutive adjective in the genitive singular acts as a noun meaning 'something nice and hot'.
- 'It is somehow very hot in here at your place.' The impersonal adverb 'hot' describes the room temperature.
- 'Come on in to us quickly.' The informal command invites the courier inside.
