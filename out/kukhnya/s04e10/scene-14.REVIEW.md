## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What are you doing, that is my nephew!' Timur protects his relative using the masculine noun for 'nephew'.
- 'If he bites you, you will become a zombie!' A real future condition in Russian uses the perfective future in both clauses.
- 'Where we come from, biting an elder is a disgrace.' The adjective 'older/elder' acts as an animate masculine noun here.
- 'Then we are not driving anywhere with you.' Double negation pairs the directional word 'to nowhere' with 'will not drive'.
- 'Oh, my poor brains!' Stumbling out and clutching his head, Barinov groans in pain.
- 'Come on, get into the car already.' Timur hustles his nephew into the van and drives off.
- 'Oh horror, what have I done!' After hitting the groaning chef, Louis uses the prefix 'na-' on 'did' to mean 'what a mess I have made'.
- 'I simply had no choice.' Past lack of choice uses 'there was not' plus the genitive singular of 'choice'.
- 'Our chef has become a zombie too!' Louis panics thinking he just struck down a zombie.
- 'What zombie, you idiots?' Rising from the ground, Barinov roars at his terrified staff.
- 'I have a wild hangover this morning!' Barinov explains why he was groaning and clutching his head.
- 'I was drinking Bloody Marys all night!' The red cocktail explains the red stains around his mouth.
- 'And you are yelling so loud that my brains hurt!' That explains why he was shouting 'brains!' when he walked out.
