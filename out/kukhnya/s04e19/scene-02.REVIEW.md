## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dmitry Vladimirovich, may I ask one question?' Mentioning the question to Nagiyev, Senya leans on "mozhno" ("it is possible, one may"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'Are we allowed to participate too?' Senya asks evenly how Nagiyev expects that to work in practice.
- 'Everyone is allowed, except our Vitya.' Nagiyev counters warmly during the talk and leans on "mozhno" ("may, can") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Of course you can, my dear friends.' Nagiyev evenly offers this confirmation in front of Senya using "konechno" ("of course") — spoken with a soft "shn" sound in everyday Moscow Russian to mean "of course, certainly".
- 'Thank you very much, Dima.' Senya directly shares this word of thanks as they meet using "spasibo" ("thank you") — the everyday word for "thank you", which grew out of the old blessing "spasi Bog" ("God save you").
- 'What if I win and become the head chef.' Senya pushes back evenly with Nagiyev and uses "vyigrayu" ("will win", from "vyigrat") to make the point stick.
- 'I am just rehearsing with you in advance.' Pointing out how advance he is during the talk, Senya uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'I sit in a red armchair.' Nagiyev speaks candidly on the spot, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'I sit with my back to all the contestants.' Bringing up the participants around Senya, Nagiyev chooses "uchastnikam" ("participants", from the root "uchastnik") so Senya hears the exact meaning.
- 'And I choose only by taste.' Focusing on the taste with Fedya, Nagiyev lands on "vkusu" ("taste, style", built on "vkus") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'We determine the very best dish.' Nagiyev reacts promptly with Fedya, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'Awesome, that is really original!' Touching on the cool in this beat, Fedya reaches for the Russian word "originalno" ("original") right at the heart of the line.
