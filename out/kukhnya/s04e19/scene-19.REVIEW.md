## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dmitry Vladimirovich, our chef is worthy of this.' The short masculine adjective meaning worthy requires the genitive case on the pronoun.
- 'Viktor Petrovich is worthy of the second round.' Lyova repeats the construction with the genitive singular of the second round.
- 'Excellent, we have to make a show out of this!' The television producer is thrilled by the unexpected dramatic twist.
- 'You will give up your spot right in front of the cameras.' The preposition meaning in front of governs the instrumental plural of the noun meaning cameras.
- 'Welcome to the second round, Viktor!' Forced to smile for the cameras, Nagiyev welcomes Barinov with the standard greeting.
- 'Lyova, come here for one minute.' Viktor Petrovich calls his sous-chef aside after the cameras stop.
- 'I was wrong to laugh at your participation.' Barinov admits that he should not have mocked Lyova for entering the show.
- 'Thank you very much, Lyova, forgive me.' He sincerely thanks his deputy and asks forgiveness with a singular imperative.
- 'We cannot take him, I know him!' Over by the stage, Nagiyev warns the producer that Barinov will get drunk and ruin the show.
- 'Excellent, give him a separate camera!' The producer loves the idea of a rebellious chef and orders a dedicated camera crew for him.
