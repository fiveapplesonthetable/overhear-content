## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Katya, look at my hands.' Once at his hands, Denis can finally tell Katya what he means.
- 'Tell me, is everything all right with them?' Asking whether tell him is everything all right with them puts Katya right on the defensive. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Good grief, you have two of them.' Katya tells Denis that good grief Denis has two of them without dressing it up. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Lie back down and go to sleep.' By telling Denis to lie back down and go to sleep, Katya keeps her side of the story grounded.
- 'I just had a strange dream.' Mentioning that he just had a strange dream moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'You will tell me everything in the morning, good night.' Saying that Denis will tell her everything in the morning good night is how Katya sets the tone with Denis.
- 'Kostya, it is six o'clock already, time to get up.' Because it is six o'clock already time to get up, Katya treats the matter as settled.
- 'We need to take Styopa to my parents' Needing to take Styopa to her parents is how Katya lays out the situation for Denis. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'Nastya, maybe he can get there by himself?' Wondering if maybe he can get there by himself shows Kostya caught off guard. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'I will explain everything to him in detail.' Claiming that he will explain everything to him in detail sounds confident, even if Denis has doubts.
- 'It is not complicated at all.' Explaining that it is not complicated at all lays the cards on the table for Denis.
- 'All right, do not be angry, I am getting up already.' Kostya explains that all right do not be angry he is getting up already, keeping his voice calm and direct.
