- 'Denis, I do not want to upset you.' Senya sets up a joke using a negated verb of wishing and an imperfective infinitive.

- 'You will just break your arm soon, and that's all.' In Russian, one word covers both hand and arm, used here in the accusative singular.
- 'Thanks, Ainura, you really comforted me.' Denis replies sarcastically using a feminine past perfective verb meaning comforted.
- 'Enough, why did you jump on the guy?' The animate accusative singular of guy follows the directional preposition after a verb of attacking.

- 'All right, pal, do not worry, give me five!' The colloquial idiom for a high five uses a singular imperative of giving and the numeral five.
- 'All right, everyone disperse to your stations quickly!' A plural past reflexive verb functions as a sharp command for the crowd to scatter.
