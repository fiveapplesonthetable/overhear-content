## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hi, Vika, do you need help again?' Standing firm right here, Denis puts the real issue right on the table for Katya.
- 'I need to move the couch in the office.' Katya frames one loaded word in earnest: "kabinete" means "office" (from "kabinet"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Oh, I dreamed about that dog again!' Denis offers the reply with ease: the "-sya" ending on "prisnilas" ("to dog") turns the action inward or makes it happen between people naturally.
- 'Listen, maybe you should go see a doctor?' Katya inquires promptly where this conversation is heading next.
- 'No, everything is fine with me.' Denis highlights one telling expression at once: "poryadke" means "order" (from "poryadok"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Let me make you some hot tea.' Katya drops this line with candor: starting with "davay" is the classic everyday Russian way to say "come on, let's do this" between people on close terms.
- 'No, thank you, let's just go back to sleep.' Denis chimes in dryly toward Katya, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'The ship requests permission to come ashore.' Denis highlights one practical term right here: "razresheniya" means "permission" (from "razresheniye"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Nothing is going to happen between us tonight.' Nastya states that thought with ease: the phrase "u nas" literally means "by us" or "at our place", the standard Russian way to talk about what a group or couple shares.
- 'First you will agree to a second child.' Nastya cuts in pointedly with a nod, making her position completely clear.
- 'Only then will I allow you everything.' Nastya spotlights this vivid touch in reply: "razreshu" means "I will allow" (from "razreshit"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Nastya, come on, why are you starting like that?' Denis shapes that remark briskly now: putting the question word "zachem" right at the front of the line lets Nastya hear the point of the question on the very first beat.
