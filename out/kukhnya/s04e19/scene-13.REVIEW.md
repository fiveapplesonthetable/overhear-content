## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why did you cook meat in the contest?' Wondering why Elena cooks meat in the contest is how Barinov puts Elena on the spot.
- 'You are our fish specialist, after all!' Saying that Elena is their fish specialist after all is how Barinov takes charge of the moment. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'You gave me that advice yourself!' Senya declares that Barinov gave him that advice themselves as the tension in the room peaks. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I lost today only because of you!' Because he lost today only because of Barinov, Senya does not bother softening the blow for Barinov.
- 'I should have just cooked fish.' Mentioning that he should have just cooked fish moves the talk in a new direction. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'You are getting in my way, and you will not advance either!' Once Elena is getting in his way and Elena will not advance either, Barinov makes sure Elena hears every word. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Hello, Elena Pavlovna, glad to see you.' Over the situation and the room, Senya answers Barinov without a second thought.
- 'Did you come for our contest too?' Asking whether Barinov comes for their contest cuts straight through the tension between Senya and Barinov. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Yes, I decided to give it a try too.' Elena mentions that she decided to give it a try so Barinov sees what is at stake.
- 'What do you think, will I succeed?' Here wondering what Barinov thinks will she succeed shows Elena looking for the real truth.
- 'Elena Pavlovna, chance decides everything in here.' Pointing out that chance decides everything in here gives Barinov a sobering reality check.
- 'I wish you great luck on the show!' Wishing Barinov great luck on the show is Senya's blunt reaction in front of Barinov. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
