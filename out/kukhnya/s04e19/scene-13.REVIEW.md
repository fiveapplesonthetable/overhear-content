- 'Why did you cook meat in the contest?' Fedya demands to know why fish specialist Senya chose to cook meat for the judges.

- 'You gave me that advice yourself!' Senya blames Fedya for telling him that a true head chef must cook meat.
- 'I lost today only because of you!' Senya pairs 'today' with 'lost' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
