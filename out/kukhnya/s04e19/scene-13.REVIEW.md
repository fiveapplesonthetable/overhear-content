## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why did you cook meat in the contest?' Fedya demands to know why fish specialist Senya chose to cook meat for the judges.
- 'You are our fish specialist, after all!' The dative preposition with the dative singular of fish indicates a professional specialty.
- 'You gave me that advice yourself!' Senya blames Fedya for telling him that a true head chef must cook meat.
- 'I lost today only because of you!' The compound preposition meaning because of with the genitive pronoun expresses blaming someone for a loss.
- 'I should have just cooked fish.' An impersonal modal word with the neuter past of to be expresses what should have been done.
- 'You are getting in my way, and you will not advance either!' Sure enough, Fedya tries to cook fish and fails to reach the second round as well.
- 'Hello, Elena Pavlovna, glad to see you.' Leaving the contest area, Senya spots former Arcobaleno chef Elena Sokolova.
- 'Did you come for our contest too?' He asks politely in the plural past tense with an accusative prepositional phrase.
- 'Yes, I decided to give it a try too.' Elena replies with a feminine past perfective verb followed by an infinitive.
- 'What do you think, will I succeed?' An impersonal third-person singular future reflexive verb with a genitive pronoun means to succeed.
- 'Elena Pavlovna, chance decides everything in here.' Bitter about his own loss, Senya claims that blind luck determines who advances.
- 'I wish you great luck on the show!' The noun for luck stands in the genitive singular after the verb of wishing.
