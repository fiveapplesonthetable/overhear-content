- 'But after all, that is the point of your contest.' Elena points out that blind judging is the whole purpose of the competition.

- 'Definitely come back to us next year.' A polite plural imperative invites her to return for the next season.
- 'Thank you very much to you and all the viewers.' The dative plural of viewers follows the word of thanks.
- 'I wish you great luck in everything!' Nagiyev sends her off warmly with a genitive noun of luck and prepositional pronoun.
- 'Goodbye, all the very best to you.' Elena leaves the stage with a gracious traditional Russian farewell.
