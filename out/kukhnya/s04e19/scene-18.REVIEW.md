## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Viktor, that was very tasty and talented.' Nagiyev responds quietly with a nod, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'If it were up to me, you would win.' Turning to the willpower at once, Vika puts the emphasis on "volya" ("willpower") in the middle of the talk.
- 'But there is one small catch here.' Describing how short things are to Vika, Nagiyev brings in "malenkiy" ("short, little") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Unfortunately, we have already selected all the contestants.' Nagiyev counters briskly during the talk, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'There are no more spots for the second round.' Stressing how second things are right here, Nagiyev centers the remark on "vtoroy" ("second") without any extra fuss.
- 'Sometimes life can be very unfair, friends.' Discussing the life in the exchange, Nagiyev centers the thought on "nespravedliva" ("unfair, unjust", from "nespravedlivyy") without wasting a word.
- 'But life is life, and show business is show business.' Nagiyev follows up evenly without a pause, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'We will meet with you in a week, bye!' Focusing on a week with Barinov, Nagiyev uses "vstretimsya" ("we'll meet", from "vstretitsya") to make the point stick.
- 'Cut! Dima, what are you doing right now?' Jumping in evenly in this beat, Barinov puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Nagiyev hears the focus immediately.
- 'What rules? We definitely have to take him!' Barinov presses wryly how Nagiyev expects that to work in practice.
- 'No, we cannot take him into the show at all!' Bringing up the show before Barinov, Nagiyev reaches for the Russian word "voobshche" ("at all") right at the heart of the line.
- 'But that dish was simply brilliant!' Talking about the dish beside Nagiyev, Barinov uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
