- 'Viktor, that was very tasty and talented.' Nagiyev speaks up sharply with a smile, grounding the moment in plain reality.

- 'But there is one small catch here.' Nagiyev opens up bluntly without any doubt, watching Barinov's face to see how the news lands.
- 'Unfortunately, we have already selected all the contestants.' Nagiyev jumps in briskly without a pause, so Vika knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'There are no more spots for the second round.' Nagiyev colors this direct expression for Barinov: "vtoroy" means "second", and it carries the main weight of what Nagiyev is saying.
- 'Sometimes life can be very unfair, friends.' Nagiyev chimes in honestly to be clear, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'But life is life, and show business is show business.' Nagiyev answers evenly without any doubt, before Barinov can change the subject.
- 'We will meet with you in a week, bye!' Nagiyev points to one colorful touch right here: "vstretimsya" means "we'll meet" (from "vstretitsya"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Cut! Dima, what are you doing right now?' Barinov delivers the remark briskly now: the "-sya" ending on "seychas" ("now") turns the action inward or makes it happen between people naturally.

- 'No, we cannot take him into the show at all!' Nagiyev reaches for one key expression toward Barinov: "voobshche" means "at all", and it carries the main weight of what Nagiyev is saying.
- 'But that dish was simply brilliant!' Barinov drops the reaction with warmth: the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") works like a shrug in the sentence, making a big claim sound effortless.
