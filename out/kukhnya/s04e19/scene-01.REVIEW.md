- 'The grand prize is one million rubles.' The masculine numeral phrase meaning one million governs the genitive plural of rubles.
- 'And the head chef spot at the best restaurant.' A superlative genitive singular phrase modifies the noun for restaurant.
- 'The owner of this restaurant is me.' The formal reflexive verb meaning to be or serve as requires the instrumental singular on the noun for owner.
- 'My name is Dmitry Nagiyev.' Nagiyev introduces himself using the standard third-person plural naming construction.
- 'I wish everyone bon appetit.' The traditional mealtime wish stands in the genitive singular after a verb of wishing.
- 'Why are we keeping so quiet?' Snapping out of his daydream, Nagiyev asks the silent staff why nobody is applauding.
- 'Our ship has been left without a captain.' The preposition meaning without governs the animate genitive singular of the noun for captain.
- 'A great thought came to me in the night.' A feminine past verb agrees with the feminine nominative singular noun meaning thought.
- 'We will organize a popular television show.' Two neuter singular adjectives modify the indeclinable loanword meaning show.
- 'And we will find a new head chef too.' The animate accusative singular of head chef takes a genitive-like ending.
