## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The cooks will meet in a fair fight.' Once the cooks will meet in a fair fight, Nagiyev can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'Watch the main culinary show of the country.' Saying that Barinov should watch the main culinary show of the country is how Nagiyev sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'The grand prize is one million rubles.' Nagiyev tells Barinov that the grand prize is one million rubles without dressing it up. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'And the head chef spot at the best restaurant.' With the conversation and prize in play, Nagiyev keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'The owner of this restaurant is me.' Mentioning that the owner of this restaurant is him moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'My name is Dmitry Nagiyev.' Nagiyev reminds Barinov that his name is Dmitry Nagiyev before the moment passes.
- 'I wish everyone bon appetit.' Explaining that he wishes everyone bon appetit lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
- 'Why are we keeping so quiet?' Wondering why they keeping so quiet are cuts straight through the tension between Nagiyev and Barinov. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Our ship has been left without a captain.' Nagiyev mentions that their ship has been left so Barinov sees what is at stake.
- 'A great thought came to me in the night.' Claiming that a great thought came to him in the night sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts.
- 'We will organize a popular television show.' Pointing out that they will organize a popular television show gives Barinov a sobering reality check.
- 'And we will find a new head chef too.' Saying that they will find a new head is how Barinov sets the tone with Nagiyev.
