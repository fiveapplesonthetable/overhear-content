## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Viktoriya Sergeyevna, what is going on?' German confronts Vika in front of all the cooks.
- 'We have a banquet in two days!' Preposition 'cherez' with time expressions means 'in / after' two days.
- 'And I find out about it right now!' Present-tense 'uznayu' means 'I am finding out'.
- 'We might simply fail to finish in time.' Adverb 'elementarno' means 'quite simply' or 'at the most basic level'.
- 'I will not have time to order the alcohol!' Kostya chimes in with his bar problem.
- 'And how much meat and fish should we order?' Here German anchors the thought on 'how much' and finishes on 'to order', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'That is simply unprofessional on your part!' German delivers the ultimate insult for a restaurant director.
- 'I am a professional at my job!' Stung by the criticism, Vika snaps back defensively.
- 'All right, stop, wait and calm down.' Seeing everyone shouting at once, Vika raises both hands.
- 'There will not be a banquet for a hundred people.' Here Vika anchors the thought on 'banquet' and finishes on 'will be', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'But for thirty people, there will be.' Having trapped herself in front of the staff, Vika has to accept Sasha's thirty-person wedding!
- 'So there really will be a banquet for thirty?' Relieved that thirty guests is easy to prepare for, German calms down immediately.
