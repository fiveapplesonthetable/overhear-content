## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Wait, did you drag me out because of Sasha?' Hearing Sasha's name on the phone, Denis realizes why Max needed an alibi.
- 'Max, are you even a normal person?' Denis is shocked that Max is risking his marriage to Vika.
- 'I lied to Vika because of you!' In everyday Russian, 'Vika' flows straight into 'lied' without extra linking words.
- 'Stop and let me out of the car.' Denis pairs 'stop' with 'cars' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
- 'I do not want to take part in your little affairs.' Notice how Russian puts 'little' and 'want' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'Listen, what little affairs are you talking about?' Max insists there is nothing romantic going on.
- 'Sasha asked for help, I could not refuse her.' In everyday Russian, 'asked' flows straight into 'to refuse, to turn down' without extra linking words.
- 'Max, hi, forgive me for being so late.' Outside Claude Monet, Sasha runs up to meet them by her car.
- 'I do not know what to do with him.' Her huge fiancé Misha is yelling drunkenly on the pavement.
- 'Come on, get up, do you hear me!' Max and Denis try to lift Misha onto his feet.
- 'Hey, who are these guys?' Drunk Misha swings wildly at Max and Denis.
- 'Boys, easy, he is completely drunk!' Short-form adjective 'pyan' ('drunk') warns them Misha doesn't know what he is doing.
