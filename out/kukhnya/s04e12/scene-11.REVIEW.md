## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'By the way, this is burglary with breaking and entering!' As they sneak into Claude Monet, Lyova quotes another article from the criminal code.
- 'Lyova, do not get in the way, I am sick of it!' Barinov hushes his anxious sous-chef.
- 'Behind this door is fish worth millions!' Notice how Russian puts 'door' and 'millions' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'So come on and help me search.' They slip inside the walk-in cooler and start gutting the fish.
- 'Well, did you find anything in there?' After twenty minutes of messy work, both their hands are empty.
- 'No, I have not found anything at all yet.' Then the kitchen lights suddenly flick on outside!
- 'Well, boys, did you find many diamonds?' Stepping out, Barinov sees Senya and Fedya surrounded by gutted fish at their prep table!
- 'What diamonds do you mean, Viktor Petrovich?' Senya and Fedya try to play innocent with fish scales all over their aprons.
- 'Do not try to make an idiot out of me!' In everyday Russian, 'need to, must' flows straight into 'idiot' without extra linking words.
- 'Actually, it is three o'clock in the morning right now.' Chef Barinov pairs 'actually, in fact' with 'night' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
- 'You decided to clean fifty kilograms of fish?' Barinov mocks the idea that Senya and Fedya came in at 3 a.m. out of love for kitchen prep.
- 'Chef, we found one stone this morning.' Senya pulls the morning diamond from his pocket.
- 'And right now there is nothing in this fish!' Both teams realize the second delivery had zero stones in it.
- 'Maybe we should call Timur and have him bring more?' Blinded by diamond fever, all four cooks decide to wake Timur up at 3 a.m.!
