## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, look, what is this?' Lyova holds out a clear, sparkling stone on a napkin.
- 'Stay calm, Lyova, it seems this is it.' Barinov inspects the stone against the light with wide eyes.
- 'Do you remember where our fish is from?' The question word 'otkuda' means 'from where'.
- 'Our fish is from Yakutia.' Russians say 'by me' or 'with us' where English says 'I have' or 'we have', without needing a separate word for 'have'.
- 'And what do we have in Yakutia?' Yakutia is famous across Russia for its diamond mines.
- 'That's right, they mine real diamonds there!' Barinov connects the Yakutian fish directly to Yakutian diamonds.
- 'Chef, could this really be contraband?' Lyova whispers the word for smuggling in alarm.
- 'Maybe we should call the police?' In spoken Russian, the words stay in their normal statement order and Lyova's rising voice at the end turns the line into a question.
- 'Hello, Timur, this is Viktor Petrovich!' Instead of calling the police, Barinov dials the fish supplier immediately.
- 'Do you remember I took a fish this morning?' In spoken Russian, the words stay in their normal statement order and Chef Barinov's rising voice at the end turns the line into a question.
- 'Do you still have any of this fish left?' Barinov tries to sound casual on the phone.
- 'Bring absolutely all the fish over here!' Notice how Russian puts 'bring' and 'fish' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'Yes, I liked the fish very much!' Barinov claims the stale fish was delicious.
