## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, Timur, did I wake you up?' Transitive perfective 'razbudil' means 'woke someone else up'.
- 'No, everything is fine, what happened?' Sleepy Timur answers his phone in bed.
- 'Do you remember you brought me fish today?' Barinov asks carefully about the afternoon crates.
- 'Yes, I remember, and what is wrong with it?' Timur worries the chef is going to complain about freshness again.
- 'Could this fish be bad too?' That single word 'too' catches Barinov's ear.
- 'Wait, in what sense 'too'?' The phrase 'v kakom smysle?' is the natural Russian way to ask 'what do you mean by that?'.
- 'Well, you did not like the morning fish.' Timur reminds him how he complained at the loading dock.
- 'So I brought you fresh Norwegian fish.' Aha — the afternoon crates were from Norway, not Yakutia!
- 'I get it illegally through Kazakhstan.' Timur admits his real smuggling operation is just sancion-busting Norwegian salmon.
- 'And can you bring the morning batch?' Barinov desperately wants the stale Yakutian batch back.
- 'That morning Yakutian one, I really need it!' He stresses every adjective so Timur brings the right crates.
- 'Fine, I will bring it, just do not get worked up.' Baffled why a top chef wants stale Yakutian fish, Timur agrees to deliver it at eight.
- 'Yes, Sasha, we are pulling up, where are you?' Outside in Vika's car, Max picks up Denis and takes another call from Sasha.
