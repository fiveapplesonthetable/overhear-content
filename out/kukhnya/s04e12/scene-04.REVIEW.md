## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Sasha, didn't we settle all the questions?' Checking whether not they settle all the questions is how Vika puts Kostya on the spot.
- 'I accidentally peeked into the order logbook.' With the banquet and plan in play, Sasha keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'And discovered that Thursday is free!' Insisting that discovered that Thursday is free shows Sasha is not backing down. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Oh, I just got completely run off my feet.' Even though she just got completely run off her feet, Vika keeps a steady face in front of Kostya.
- 'And simply forgot to write it down.' Admitting that simply forgot to write it down shows Vika being honest for a change. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'On Thursday there is a banquet for a hundred people.' Saying that on Thursday there is a banquet for a hundred people is how Vika sets the tone with Kostya.
- 'There, you see, we really are booked.' Once there Kostya sees they really are booked, Vika can finally tell Kostya what she means.
- 'What banquet for a hundred people?' With the moment and banquet hanging in the air, Kostya puts Sasha on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Why do I know nothing about this?' Wondering why he knows nothing about this is how Kostya puts Sasha on the spot. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'My bar is half-empty, after all!' Insisting that his bar is half empty after all shows Kostya is not backing down.
- 'I need to place an order urgently.' Saying that he needs to place an order urgently is how Kostya sets the tone with Sasha. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Wait, we will do everything a little later.' Saying that they will do everything a little later is how Vika sets the tone with Kostya.
- 'Later they will not make it in time to deliver!' Kostya insists that later they will not make it in time to deliver, leaving Sasha no room to argue.
- 'Kostya, there will not be any fine!' Saying that there will not be any fine is how Vika takes charge of the moment. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
