## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Sasha, didn't we settle all the questions?' Particle 'razve' expresses surprise: 'haven't we already settled this?'.
- 'I accidentally peeked into the order logbook.' The prefix 'za-' on 'looked' means 'peeked inside' or 'glanced into'.
- 'And discovered that Thursday is free!' Neuter adverbial 'svobodno' means 'it is free/open' on Thursday.
- 'Oh, I just got completely run off my feet.' Colloquial reflexive 'zamotalas' means 'got swamped / worn out running around'.
- 'And simply forgot to write it down.' Vika grabs a pen to cover her tracks right in front of Sasha.
- 'On Thursday there is a banquet for a hundred people.' As she writes it into the official logbook, she speaks the words aloud.
- 'There, you see, we really are booked.' Short-form plural 'zanyaty' means 'busy' or 'booked'.
- 'What banquet for a hundred people?' Walking up to the podium, Kostya reads the fresh entry in horror.
- 'Why do I know nothing about this?' Preposition 'ob' ('about') takes the prepositional case 'etom'.
- 'My bar is half-empty, after all!' Compound adjective 'polupustoy' joins 'half-' and 'empty'.
- 'I need to place an order urgently.' With a hundred guests coming in two days, the bar needs dozens of cases of alcohol.
- 'Wait, we will do everything a little later.' Vika whispers for him to wait until Sasha leaves.
- 'Later they will not make it in time to deliver!' The verb 'uspet' means 'to manage to do something in time'.
- 'Kostya, there will not be any fine!' Impersonal negated future 'ne budet' takes the genitive singular 'nikakogo shtrafa'.
