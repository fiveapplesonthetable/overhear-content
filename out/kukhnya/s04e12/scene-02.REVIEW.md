- 'They brought it from Yakutia just this morning.' Timur weighs in dryly without holding back, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'She just got a little tired on the road.' Addressing the road during the talk, Timur puts the weight on "doroge" ("road", from "doroga") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'She flew for six hours on a plane.' Turning to the airplane before Barinov, Timur centers the thought on "samolyote" ("airplane, aircraft", from "samolyot") without wasting a word.
- 'She traveled for two days by dog sled!' Timur chimes in warmly with a nod, before Barinov can change the subject.

- 'Fine, hold this, give the fish to Lyova.' Barinov warmly sends this word of agreement while facing Timur using "ladno" ("alright") — built from the old Russian root for harmony and order, used every day for "okay, fine".
