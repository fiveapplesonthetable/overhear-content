## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'At eight o'clock Timur will bring the fish.' Stepping out into the courtyard for some air, Barinov briefs the cooks.
- 'So we all need to chip in money.' Colloquial reflexive 'skinutsya' means 'to pool money / chip in together'.
- 'Wait, I think that is our Oguzok over there!' Hearing a crash by the street, Barinov spots Max struggling with a giant man.
- 'Hey, enough fighting, calm down right now!' All four cooks charge in to grab Misha's arms and legs.
- 'What a huge brute this guy is!' In colloquial Russian, 'zdorovy' often means 'huge/burly' rather than just 'healthy'.
- 'Sasha, get in the front, hold him tight!' Together the six men cram Misha into the car.
- 'Oguzok, what on earth was that?' Barinov stares as Max climbs in beside Sasha.
- 'Sasha asked him to help with her fiancé.' Denis explains the situation to the bewildered cooks.
- 'Wait, does Vika know about this?' Fedya immediately spots the danger for Max.
- 'Why did you call us in so early?' Hours later at eight a.m., Timur delivers the Yakutian crates and the cooks gut every fish.
- 'We wasted so much money for nothing today!' Not a single stone is inside — their pooled money is gone!
- 'A little later I will go to the pawnshop.' Barinov proposes selling the two stones they found yesterday.
- 'I will sell the stones, and we will split the money equally.' The adverb 'porovnu' means 'equally / in equal shares'.
- 'Tell me, does everyone agree with this?' Plural short-form adjective 'soglasny' seals the pact.
