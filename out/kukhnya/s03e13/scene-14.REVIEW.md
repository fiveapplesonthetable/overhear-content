## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, Dmitry Vladimirovich, it is me.' With the work and office in play, Vika keeps this short reply brisk and direct. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'I know very well that it is you.' Admitting that he knows very well that it is Kostya shows Max being honest for a change. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'We had an emergency in the bar here.' Mentioning that they had an emergency in the bar here moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'How do you already know about this?' Wondering how Kostya already know about this is how Vika puts Kostya on the spot. Notice how she sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'In my kingdom I know everything.' Because in his kingdom he knows everything, Max treats the matter as settled.
- 'Put the bartender on, I will solve the problem right now.' Here telling Kostya to put the bartender on he will solve the problem right now sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'Hello, yes, I understood you.' Kostya mentions that he understood Max so Max sees what is at stake. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Alright, I will calculate the total amount.' Claiming that Alright he will calculate the total amount sounds confident, even if Max has doubts.
- 'Alright, goodbye, Dmitry Vladimirovich.' Over the point and the office, Kostya answers Max without a second thought.
- 'In short, Max and Katya are not guilty.' Kostya explains that in short Max and Katya are not guilty, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'Well, they are guilty, but only of two bottles.' Realizing that they are guilty but only of two bottles changes how Kostya handles the situation.
- 'Turn on the video recording yourselves and watch it all.' Turning on the video recording yourselves and watch it all is how Kostya lays out the situation for Max.
