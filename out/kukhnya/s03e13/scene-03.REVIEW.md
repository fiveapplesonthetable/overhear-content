## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Kostya, what happened here?' Max presses straight out why things suddenly took this turn between them.
- 'Did you have a bad bartender show?' Max underscores one colorful expression for Kostya: "neudachnoye" means "unsuccessfully" (from "neudachno"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'I will find out who was juggling here at night.' Kostya stands firm bluntly to be clear, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Ainura, could you clean up faster?' Kostya inquires firmly while looking straight at Max here.
- 'Listen, Kostya, do not bother me.' Ainura colors this direct term in reply: "slushay" means "listen" (from "slushat"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Let me breathe in the expensive cognac a bit.' Ainura reacts earnestly with a smile, before Max can change the subject.
- 'They smashed three bottles last night.' Kostya weighs in calmly toward Ainura, shifting the whole mood of the room in a single beat.
- 'Maybe only two bottles?' Max features one telling touch on cue: "butylki" means "bottle" (from "butylka"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'No, not two, three.' Kostya jumps in wryly before Ainura, saying out loud what both of them already know.
- 'They forgot to lock up, and someone broke in at night.' Kostya chimes in dryly in their talk, making his position completely clear.
- 'Who could have done that?' Max frames one clear expression in earnest: "sdelat" means "to do", and it carries the main weight of what Max is saying.
- 'Well of course, not me.' Kostya follows up openly without a pause, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'And what are you doing here?' Ainura wonders frankly to see how Kostya will answer on the spot.
