## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'And what was Vika doing here, I did not understand?' Kristina questions plainly whether Vika really thought this through.
- 'She came to see me about work.' Mentioning the work around Kristina, Nagiyev puts the weight on "prikhodila" ("to come", from "prikhodit") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'I am calm, I am completely calm.' Describing how calm he is with Kristina, Nagiyev links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'I hear the sound of the surf, nothing disturbs me.' Nagiyev speaks up steadily in their talk, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Calmness is slowly spreading through me.' Touching on the calmness in front of Kristina, Nagiyev uses "spokoystviye" ("calmness, composure") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Nothing is working except anger!' Turning to the anger in this beat, Nagiyev pairs the negative word with "ne" ("not"), since Russian naturally doubles its negatives for emphasis.
- 'And why are you getting so angry?' Kristina probes briskly whether Nagiyev really thought this through.
- 'Because I do not know how to control my emotions!' Pointing to his emotions right here, Nagiyev brings in "kontrolirovat" ("to keep an eye on") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'I am also very glad to see you, Dima.' Kristina replies dryly without any doubt, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'First of all, your door was unlocked.' Kristina steps in promptly beside Nagiyev, before Nagiyev can change the subject.
- 'And second of all, I found your documents.' Mentioning the documents during the talk, Kristina puts the weight on "dokumenty" ("the documents", from "dokument") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'Here are your important papers, take them.' Vika answers candidly facing Kristina, letting the remark speak for itself.
