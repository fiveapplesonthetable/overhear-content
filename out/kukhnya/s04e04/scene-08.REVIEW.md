## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hi there.' Between the restaurant and the room, Vika gives Denis a quick, grounded response.
- 'Why did you come over here?' Here asking why Denis comes over here shows Max looking for the real truth. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I figured you would be stuck here with Denis for a long time.' Vika mentions that she figured Denis would be stuck here with Denis for a long time so Denis sees what is at stake.
- 'I am already done with Denis. Shall we go home?' Being already done with Denis is how Max puts Denis on the spot. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Sorry. I just wanted to apologize for yelling at you.' Noting that he just wanted to apologize for yelling at Max keeps Denis's side of the story simple and clear. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Could you not have come in at least three minutes later?' Here checking whether Denis not have come in at least three minutes later shows Max looking for the real truth.
- 'Why three minutes later?' With the work and restaurant hanging in the air, Denis puts Max on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Den, do you hear us? Close the door.' When Max asks if someone do Denis hears them, Denis has to answer fast. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Vika, don't be angry. He is my brother. We need to help him.' Once do not be angry, Max can finally tell Denis what he means.
- 'Especially since he has already left.' Adding that especially since he has already left fills in the missing piece for Denis.
- 'I haven't left yet.' Denis explains that he has not left yet, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'I married you, not the two of you.' With the restaurant and plan in play, Vika keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
