## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'On your mark. Get set. Go!' Around the restaurant and doctor, Vika makes her point loud and clear. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Listen, you are calling at a really bad time right now.' Pointing out that Denis is calling at a really bad time right now gives Denis a sobering reality check.
- 'Hello, Max. I think they are going to fire me today.' Thinking they are going to fire him today is how Denis lays out the situation for Max.
- 'What happened?' With the restaurant and work hanging in the air, Max puts Denis on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'German asked me to peel two sacks of potatoes before evening.' Noting that German asked him to peel two sacks of potatoes before evening keeps Denis's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Maybe you can come over and help?' Questioning whether maybe Max can come over and help is how Denis tests Max's story. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Well then, my hunter?' Over the kitchen and the phone, Vika presses Denis for a straight answer. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Listen, Den just called, he is in trouble at work.' Saying that someone just called he is in trouble at work is how Max sets the tone with Denis.
- 'I have to help him, forgive me.' Here having to help him forgive him sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'Did you forget what the doctor said?' Asking whether Denis forgets what the doctor said cuts straight through the tension between Vika and Denis. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Darling, we have two whole days ahead of us.' Saying that they have two whole days ahead of them is how Max sets the tone with Denis.
- 'I will still have plenty of time.' Since he will still have plenty of time, Max does not waste words on excuses.
