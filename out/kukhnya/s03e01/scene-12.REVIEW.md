## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I am not particularly fond of you.' Barinov responds steadily across the room, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'And I know that it is mutual.' Barinov answers naturally around Nastya and centers the remark on "vzaimno" ("likewise, mutually") without any extra fuss.
- 'If until the end of the week nobody orders...' Discussing the ending before Nastya, Barinov pairs the negative word with "ne" ("not"), since Russian naturally doubles its negatives for emphasis.
- 'Do we have a deal?' Barinov casually speaks this word of agreement for Nastya using "dogovorilis" ("deal") — literally "we have talked it through to an agreement", used just like "deal!" or "settled!".
- 'Yes, Chef. I will do everything, Chef.' Nastya speaks frankly during the talk and lands on "sdelayu" ("I'll do", built on "sdelat") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, I think this is unfair.' Nastya proceeds decisively before Barinov and lands on "nechestno" ("unfair, not fair") as the key everyday word here.
- 'I will remove the milk-fed lamb from the menu.' Barinov replies earnestly on the spot, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Tell me, what is this molecular cuisine?' Noting how molecular things are to Nastya, the guest brings in "molekulyarnaya" ("molecular", from "molekulyarnyy") as the key Russian word here.
- 'You know, I would not advise you to get it.' Nastya counters dryly without a pause, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Pure chemicals, and very expensive.' Nastya fires back promptly hearing the guest, testing the guest's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'I would not advise you to order this.' Nastya responds pointedly before the guest and centers the thought on "sovetovala" ("to advise, recommend", from "sovetovat") without wasting a word.
- 'The cook is new and inexperienced.' Making clear how inexperienced things are on the spot, Nastya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
