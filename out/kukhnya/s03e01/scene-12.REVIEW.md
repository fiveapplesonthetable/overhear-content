## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I am not particularly fond of you.' Barinov tells the guest that he is not particularly fond of the guest without dressing it up. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'And I know that it is mutual.' Adding that he knows that it is mutual fills in the missing piece for the guest.
- 'If until the end of the week nobody orders...' Adding that if until the end of the week nobody orders fills in the missing piece for the guest. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'Do we have a deal?' By checking whether they have a deal, Barinov presses the guest for a straight answer. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Yes, Chef. I will do everything, Chef.' Since she will do everything, Nastya does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, I think this is unfair.' Realizing that she thinks this is unfair changes how Nastya handles the situation. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'I will remove the milk-fed lamb from the menu.' Saying that he will remove the milk fed lamb from the menu is how Barinov sets the tone with the guest.
- 'Tell me, what is this molecular cuisine?' When the guest asks if tell him what is this molecular cuisine, Barinov has to answer fast.
- 'You know, I would not advise you to get it.' Explaining that she would not advise Barinov to get it lays the cards on the table for Barinov. Even in the middle of the drama, she keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Pure chemicals, and very expensive.' With the truth and menu in play, Nastya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'I would not advise you to order this.' Claiming that she would not advise Barinov to order this sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts.
- 'The cook is new and inexperienced.' Pointing out that the cook is new and inexperienced gives Barinov a sobering reality check.
