## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What did your parents say?' Wondering what Max's parents say cuts straight through the tension between Kostya and Max.
- 'Oh, nothing much.' Between the shift and the news, Nastya gives Kostya a quick, grounded response.
- 'I haven't told them yet.' Admitting that she has not told them yet shows Nastya being honest for a change.
- 'When will you tell them?' By wondering when Max tells them will, Kostya presses Max for a straight answer.
- 'Chef, will you look for a molecular cook?' Asking whether Kostya looks for a molecular cook cuts straight through the tension between Max and Kostya. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'You will go out with me anyway.' Mentioning that Kostya will go out with him anyway moves the talk in a new direction. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I will tell them tomorrow.' Nastya reminds Kostya that she will tell them tomorrow before the moment passes.
- 'No, Dad, I cannot.' Because dad she cannot, Nastya treats the matter as settled.
- 'I already took days off.' Explaining that she already took days off lays the cards on the table for Kostya.
- 'I am not going on a horseback ride.' Here being not going on a horseback ride sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'Alright, I have to go. Bye.' Claiming that Alright she has to go sounds confident, even if Kostya has doubts. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Nastya, what horseback ride?' Around the call and phone, Kostya wants to know where Max really stands. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
