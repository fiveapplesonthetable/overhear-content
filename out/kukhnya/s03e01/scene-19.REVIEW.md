## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'How did the family reunion go?' Wondering how the family reunion go is how Lyova puts Barinov on the spot.
- 'I know everything, I know.' By knowing everything he knows, Barinov keeps his side of the story grounded.
- 'I think it is normal, it is right.' Because he thinks it is normal it is right, Lyova treats the matter as settled. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Both the daughter is nearby and the dad is under supervision.' Lyova tells Barinov that both the daughter is nearby and the dad is under supervision without dressing it up.
- 'I want everyone to understand.' Since he wants everyone to understand, Barinov does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Here there is only the chef.' Mentioning that here there is only moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Nobody must know about this, clear?' Wondering if nobody must know about this clear shows Barinov caught off guard.
- 'Yes, good evening.' With the family and conversation in play, Vika keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Come aboard. Well, how do you like it?' When Lyova asks if well how do Barinov likes it, Barinov has to answer fast. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'It seemed to me that this was a business meeting.' Vika mentions that it seemed to her that this was a business meeting so Barinov sees what is at stake.
- 'And this looks like a date.' Claiming that this looks like a date sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'That is exactly what it is.' Pointing out that that is exactly what it is gives Barinov a sobering reality check.
