## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, come here right now!' Vika demands the young cook's immediate attention.
- 'Hi, Vika, what happened?' Max greets her, unaware of the impending storm.
- 'Listen to me very attentively once.' Vika tells him to listen closely because she will only say this once.
- 'Do not touch my waitresses ever again!' Vika strictly forbids him to bother her staff in the hall.
- 'Or I will take very strict measures.' Vika threatens severe consequences if he disobeys.
- 'Vika, why are you so angry?' Max is genuinely confused by the intense hostility.
- 'Poor Nastya has been crying because of you all day.' Vika explains the damage his actions have caused.
- 'But absolutely nothing happened between us!' Max desperately tries to clear his name.
- 'I saw your strange conversations by the bar.' Vika dismisses his excuses, having witnessed the drama firsthand.
- 'I warned you for the last time.' Vika firmly states that this is his final warning.
- 'Alright, I understood you, thanks for the advice.' Max wisely backs down and accepts the scolding.
- 'Now I need to quickly salt the soup.' Max awkwardly uses his work as an excuse to escape.
- 'And not forget to add fresh cream.' Max mutters about his recipe to diffuse the tension.
