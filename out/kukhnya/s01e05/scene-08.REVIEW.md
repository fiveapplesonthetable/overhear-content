## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Who? Mom?' Max is surprised when he hears his mother is on the kitchen phone.
- 'And how does she know this number?' Here 'where from' starts the question about the restaurant phone number.
- 'Hello, Mom?' Max answers the phone cautiously in front of the other cooks.
- 'Yes, it is Mom, your mom.' Chef Viktor jokes on the other end of the line, pretending to be Max's mother.
- 'You will cook trout with classic julienne.' Viktor tells Max which trout dish to prepare for the cooking bet.
- 'First, clean the fresh fish.' The phrase for 'for a start' introduces the first step of the recipe.
- 'Then carefully remove the backbone.' Viktor gives a clear kitchen command for preparing the trout.
- 'And put the fillet in the fridge.' He tells Max to chill the fish fillet right away.
- 'Did you understand everything, Mommy?' Viktor teasingly checks that Max caught every step.
- 'I will call you back later, I am busy.' Max keeps up the act so the cooks around him think he is talking to his mother.
- 'Kisses to you, bye.' Russians often end family phone calls with the word for 'I kiss you'.
- 'That was Mom calling from Voronezh.' Max calmly tells the kitchen crew that his mother called from his hometown.
