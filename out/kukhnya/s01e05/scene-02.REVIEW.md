## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Nastya, is everything serious between you and Max?' Russian says 'at you with Max everything serious', with no 'is' at all. 'you' is the plural one, because Sasha means Nastya and Max together.
- 'Yes, we even kissed yesterday.' 'kissed' starts with a little 'po', which makes it one kiss that happened and is done. Earlier you heard the plain form, which sounded more like kissing over and over.
- 'Really? And how did that happen?' Sasha's first word for 'really' is 'truth'. The last word ends in 'o' because it refers to 'it', and 'it' takes that ending in the past.
- 'It was incredibly romantic!' 'incredibly' is built from 'not' plus 'believable', so it's literally 'unbelievably'. 'Romantic' you can guess, since Russian borrows plenty of words like it.
- 'Did you really fall in love with him?' The first word is a surprised 'no way', and you'll hear it a lot. Russian falls 'into' someone, and the ending 'las' tells you a woman is being asked.
- 'He is such a kind and attentive guy.' 'such' is handy: put it before any description to say 'such a'. 'Kind' and 'attentive' both end in 'iy' or 'yy', which is how a describing word for a man usually ends.
- 'You have only known each other one week.' The small word 'vedy' in the middle means 'after all', and it nudges Nastya to be realistic. 'week' is a woman-type word, so 'one' gets an 'u' on the end.
- 'True love knows no time.' It sounds like a line from a song, and that's the point. 'knows' is the one in 'I don't know', so the phrase is 'love doesn't know time'.
- 'Imagine what beautiful children we will have!' The first word is 'you imagine', used like 'just imagine' in English. Russian doesn't say 'we will have': it says 'at us will be', which is how every 'have' in the future is built.
- 'You're already thinking about children?!' The small word for 'already' does a lot of work here, as it's what makes Sasha so shocked. 'About children' needs a different ending on the word for children.
- 'Of course, we will create a wonderful family.' 'of course' is spelled with a 'ch' but said with 'sh', so it sounds like 'konyeshno'. 'We will create' ends in 'im', the ending for 'we will'.
- 'And does Max know about your plans?' It's Sasha's best question, and the one Nastya has avoided. 'Your' is in the plural form here to match 'plans', so it ends in 'ikh'.
- 'Soon we will definitely discuss everything.' 'We will discuss' ends in 'im' again, like 'create' did. 'Definitely' is a long word, and a final 'o' is what turns many describing words into this kind of 'how' word.
