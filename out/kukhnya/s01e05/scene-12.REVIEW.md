## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Alright, I agree to this plan.' The short adjective expresses agreement and takes the preposition with an accusative demonstrative phrase.
- 'Kostya has already been looking at you for a long time.' The adverb combination emphasizes an ongoing duration of action leading up to the present.
- 'Of course, he likes you very much.' In Russian, the object of affection is the grammatical subject, while the admirer takes the dative pronoun.
- 'Max will definitely regret his behavior.' The future tense verb governs the prepositional case with the reflexive possessive pronoun.
