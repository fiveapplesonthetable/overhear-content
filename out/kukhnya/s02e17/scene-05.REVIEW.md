- 'Did you tell her yesterday?' Stepping in frankly to Fedya, Senya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Fedya purely through rising pitch.
- 'I told her...' Fedya counters frankly without any doubt, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Sun. Transformer.' Underscoring the transformer in this beat, Senya chooses "transformator" ("transformer") so Fedya gets the point right away.
- 'Wait, what did he even say?' Answering frankly at once, Fedya puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Senya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Oh, what a guy.' Senya speaks honestly in their talk, making his position completely clear.
- 'Something about love, probably.' Addressing the love in this beat, Fedya brings in "lyubov" ("love") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'I do not get it, girls, are you on state welfare or what?' Following up boldly beside Fedya, Senya puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Fedya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Get to work fast, you stumps!' Fedya follows up plainly without holding back, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'What is wrong with him today?' Holding firm readily around Fedya, Senya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.

- 'Let us just accept that he is simply nuts.' Senya responds quietly toward Fedya, so Fedya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Go knock on your own head.' Addressing the head in this beat, Fedya uses "golove" ("the head", from "golova") to make the point stick.
