## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, you two certainly deserve each other.' Barinov jumps in pointedly with a sigh, before Vika can change the subject.
- 'Just listen to him talk.' Vika follows up firmly with Barinov and reaches for the Russian word "poslushayte" ("listen", built from "poslushat") right at the center of the line.
- 'And what about yourself?' Following up boldly around Vika, Max keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Vika purely through rising pitch.
- 'What are you allowing yourself to do, Vitya, who is this?' Vika checks plainly what Max is actually getting at here.
- 'What nonsense are you all spouting in here?' Vika fires back quietly at once and puts the weight on "nesete" ("bring", from "nesti") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'Why on earth do you need Louis?' Weighing in frankly on the spot, Max puts the question word "zachem" right at the start so Barinov hears the focus immediately.
- 'I am going to explain everything right now.' Barinov weighs in frankly beside Max, before Max can change the subject.
- 'Why does all of this remind me of something?' Vika proceeds sharply around Max and uses "napominayet" ("reminds of", from "napominat") to make the point stick.
- 'I am going to explain it to all of you right now!' Max replies quietly with a shrug, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'You are not even trying to change.' Barinov replies briskly without holding back, making his position completely clear.
- 'Every reckless action in our lives is a mine.' Pointing out how rash things are in the exchange, Vika leans on "neobdumannyy" ("rash, hasty") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Looking back, we see nothing but ruins.' Returning to the ruin before Vika, the "-sya" ending on "oglyadyvayas" ("to look back") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'You know what?' Barinov presses earnestly where this conversation is heading next.
