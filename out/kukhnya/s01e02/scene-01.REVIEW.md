## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'On the one hand, I work in a cool restaurant.' That opener sets the pattern for the whole scene: Russians use it just like we do, and the "on the other hand" that follows undercuts it.
- 'And on the other hand, you eat ordinary sausages.' Here is the other hand, and it's a joke: cool restaurant, cheap sausages. Kostya says it with a grin.
- 'Our chef cooks the most delicious food.' Max sounds proud. Saying "our chef" rather than "the chef" makes him sound like part of the family already.
- 'But total chaos always reigns in the kitchen.' Kostya goes straight for the deflating comment. The Russian word for "reigns" is built from the word for tsar, so chaos is literally on the throne.
- 'Guests think this is the most peaceful place.' These are the guests' illusions: out in the dining room, everything looks calm. Max says it with a little wink.
- 'They will never know the truth about us.' Kostya lets the secret out: the staff know, the guests never will. Russian happily doubles the no here, "never" and "not" together, and that's correct.
- 'Every customer considers themselves an important person.' A small dig at vanity. Max isn't being cruel, just honest about how restaurants flatter people.
- 'And we simply pour them wine.' That is Kostya's job in one line. The tiny word for "simply" is the shrug in the sentence.
- 'Work in the kitchen is very hard and nerve-wracking.' No joke this time; Max says plainly how hard the work is. It's a handy sentence for any job you find tough, so swap in your own work for "kitchen".
- 'On the other hand, it's never boring here!' Kostya's little word "zato" means "but on the plus side", and Russians reach for it all the time to turn a complaint around.
- 'Our morning shift is starting soon.' The mood shifts here. The banter is over and Max is looking at the clock.
- 'Then prepare for a new workday!' A friendly pep talk. Where English says "get ready for", Russian says something closer to "get ready to a new workday".
- 'I will prove to the chef that I know how to cook.' This is what the scene has been heading towards: Max's promise. It sets up his first real morning in the kitchen.
