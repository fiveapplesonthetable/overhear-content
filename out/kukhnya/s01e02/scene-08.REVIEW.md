## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef flatly refuses to cook this dish.' Victoria enters the kitchen with dramatic news about the strike.
- 'But why? What happened in the kitchen?' Senya asks with sudden worry about their volatile boss.
- 'He lost his favorite knife.' Victoria explains the cause of Barinov's refusal to work.
- 'Soon you will have a new chef!' Victoria warns the kitchen crew that the owner is running out of patience.
- 'He will be fired if he does not make the fricassee.' Fedya states the immediate consequence of the refusal.
- 'Guys, let us cook this dish ourselves!' Senya boldly proposes a rescue plan to save the restaurant.
- 'Are you out of your mind? He will kill us!' Fedya fears Viktor's furious reaction if he finds out.
- 'Yes, you are right, better not risk it.' Senya temporarily backtracks after considering the wrath.
- 'But if we do not cook, Chef will be fired.' Fedya realizes inaction is equally disastrous.
- 'Exactly, you are right, we must cook right away!' Senya swings back to action with fresh conviction.
- 'Darn, I am confused. This is a dangerous idea.' Fedya admits his deep ambivalence about the secret operation.
- 'But we have a duty to save our chef.' Senya appeals to their collective kitchen solidarity.
- 'All right, turn on the stove and get the meat!' Fedya commits to the risky culinary endeavor.
