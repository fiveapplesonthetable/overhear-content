## Part 1 — Intro

> Outside the back entrance of Claude Monet, bartender Kostya runs after the computer technician. Realizing the expensive restaurant cognac has disappeared right after the technician worked behind the bar, Kostya demands its return. When the smug technician tries to intimidate him, Kostya warns that he is a trained boxer who won't back down. Let's listen to their confrontation in Russian first.
