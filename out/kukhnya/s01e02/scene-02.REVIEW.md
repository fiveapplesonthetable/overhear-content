## Part 1 — Intro

> Max arrives two hours late to his morning shift at Claude Monet, sending the kitchen into a flurry. Head chef Viktor Barinov celebrates winning a betting pool on Max's tardiness and collects money from the cooks. As punishment, Barinov refuses to let Max cook, assigning him to unload heavy deliveries all day. Let's listen to their confrontation in Russian first.
