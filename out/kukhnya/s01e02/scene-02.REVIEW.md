## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Lavrov, you are two hours late!' Barinov doesn't say "Max". He uses the surname, the way a stern boss puts you in your place. The two hours are the whole problem.
- 'He is late, meaning I won the bet!' He isn't even angry yet: the chef had money on this.
- 'Everyone who bet, pay up quickly!' Now he turns to the rest of the kitchen to collect. The word he uses means "chip in", and it's what you'd shout at a group who all owe you money.
- 'Chef, forgive me, I got stuck in a traffic jam.' Max tries the oldest excuse there is. "Forgive me" is the polite, formal version, the kind you say to a boss and not to a friend.
- 'You lifted my mood this morning.' Sarcasm, and not very hidden. A bad morning for Max is good news for Barinov, and he says it with a straight face.
- 'Therefore I reward you with an important task.' Notice how grand he makes it sound. "Reward" is a prize word, and the prize is a chore. That gap is the joke.
- 'You will unload groceries the whole day!' And there is the reward: unloading deliveries from morning to night. After "important task" it lands like a punchline.
- 'Chef, but I thought I would be cooking.' Max dares to say what he hoped for. "I thought" is a handy way to point out, politely, that your expectations were different.
- 'You credited yourself with two non-existent abilities!' Barinov turns Max's words into a joke at his expense. Literally it's close to "you wrote them down for yourself", as if Max had padded his CV.
- 'The ability to think and the ability to cook!' He says "ability" twice, once for each skill, so the insult lands twice, like a drum beat.
- 'I studied very well at the culinary college.' Max defends himself with his one card, school. It's a weak answer and he knows it.
- 'And here is a restaurant, not your college!' Barinov shuts that down fast. In Russian the same small word can mean "and" or "but", and here it means "but".
- 'Now go to work immediately, oguzok!' "Oguzok" is Barinov's nickname for Max. It's actually a cut of meat from the rear of the animal, so the chef is calling him scraps.
