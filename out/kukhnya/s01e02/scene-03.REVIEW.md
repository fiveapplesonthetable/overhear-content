## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Nastya, you will drive me crazy one of these days!' He's already in a mood. The word for "one of these days" means "someday", and said like this it's a warning.
- 'One must not run into the kitchen so unexpectedly.' The house rule: you don't burst into his kitchen. There is no "you" in the Russian, just "it is not allowed", which is how rules are stated.
- 'Chef, I have a very urgent question.' Nastya doesn't flinch. In Russian she says "at me is a very urgent question" rather than "I have": the question sits with her.
- 'What is the correct term for a female chef?' Nastya asks it innocently. Russian has no obvious feminine for "head chef", which is exactly what sets him off.
- 'A female head chef is a freak of nature.' Barinov's answer, and it's a harsh one. "Freak of nature" is a real Russian expression for something that shouldn't exist.
- 'Why do you speak so unfairly?' She uses the formal "you" even while disagreeing, which is how a waitress objects to her boss.
- 'Because a real kitchen is a man's business!' His reasoning is that a kitchen is a man's business. He sounds very sure, and he is about to be proved wrong.
- 'Then a freak of nature is sitting in our hall.' Now Nastya takes his own words and hands them back. The little word for "then" is where she starts to smile.
- 'Where exactly is this woman sitting?' He bites at once. The word for "exactly" sharpens the question: where, precisely?
- 'She is sitting in our main dining hall.' Nastya stays calm and plain, which makes the news worse for him.
- 'Where did this strange guest come from?' The chef has switched from sneering to suspicion. He calls her strange, but he is no longer laughing.
- 'This is the head chef from the neighboring restaurant!' There is the punchline: the woman in the dining room is a head chef from next door, the very thing he just insulted.
- 'What do the rivals want in my restaurant?' Barinov's first thought is spying. "My restaurant" says it all: this place is his.
