## Part 1 — Intro

> Summoned from his back office by cleaner Ainura, Chef Viktor Barinov walks into the dining room to meet Elena Sokolova. Elena, the head chef of the competing restaurant Arcobaleno, delivers a surprisingly positive verdict on the fricassee, though she makes sure to criticize the agonizingly long wait. Let's listen to their encounter in Russian first.
