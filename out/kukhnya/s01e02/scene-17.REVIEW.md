## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Viktoriya Sergeyevna, should I bring you a cup of coffee?' Respectful patronymic address with an infinitive offer.
- 'Lord, how sick of everything I am!' Emotional invocation with an emphatic particle and accusative reflexive idiom.
- 'Have new problems arisen in the restaurant again?' Past tense question with locative phrase and plural subject.
- 'Of course, expensive cognac went missing in the bar.' Parenthetical adverb followed by past verb and subject.
- 'Bartender Kostya is looking for that bottle now.' Present tense transitive clause governing an accusative direct object.
- 'Well why does everything always go wrong?' Interrogative particle and adverb introducing a negative motion idiom.
- 'Our work is always very stressful.' Possessive subject with an intensifying adverb and predicate adjective.
- 'The chef shouts, cooks argue, mess everywhere!' Series of short coordinated clauses describing kitchen chaos.
- 'You need to rest a bit and calm down.' Impersonal dative construction with coordinated perfective infinitives.
- 'Can a day really not pass normally?' Rhetorical modal question with an adverb of manner.
- 'I am sure everything will work out soon.' Short form predicate adjective governing a future reflexive verb.
- 'I want at least one calm day!' Present transitive verb governing a restrictive phrase with accusative object.
- 'We all want this very much, believe me.' Plural subject with degree adverb and imperative parenthetical.
