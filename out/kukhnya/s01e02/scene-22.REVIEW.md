## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why did you put a dirty box here?' Barinov scolds the kitchen staff for leaving clutter around.
- 'Remove it from here very quickly!' The head chef demands immediate cleanliness in the kitchen.
- 'Lyova, have you already found my knife?' Barinov checks in on the urgent search for his tool.
- 'We have not found it yet, Chef.' Lyova nervously reports that the blade has not turned up.
- 'Did you definitely look well everywhere?' Barinov presses his sous-chef to verify every spot.
- 'Yes, we checked everything in the kitchen.' Lyova confirms they searched all normal work areas.
- 'Then continue to look further!' The chef refuses to accept that the knife is gone.
- 'Someone could accidentally leave the knife inside.' Barinov suspects someone got careless while prepping.
- 'For example, someone forgot the knife in the fish.' The chef suggests an absurd place where it might be.
- 'In the fish? That is impossible, Chef!' Lyova reacts with complete disbelief at the wild suggestion.
- 'I do not know. Look everywhere!' Barinov insists that no possibility can be ruled out.
- 'Alright, Chef, we will find everything.' Lyova obediently promises to continue the search.
- 'Without the knife we will not be able to work!' Barinov emphasizes how crucial the blade is to the restaurant.
