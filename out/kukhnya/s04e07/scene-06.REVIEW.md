## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well how is it, Denis, did you accept all the groceries?' Senya checks whether the newcomer finished unloading.
- 'Listen, I made a huge mistake out there today.' Denis confesses his blunder to the veteran chef.
- 'I carried part of the groceries to our storeroom.' Feminine noun 'part' governs the genitive plural 'of groceries'.
- 'And when I returned, the dog had eaten all the pigeons!' Animate plural accusative marks the eaten birds.
- 'Everyone knows that this dog is very voracious.' Descriptive feminine adjective means greedy or gluttonous.
- 'Maybe you will call our Timur right now?' The verb for calling by phone governs the dative case on Timur's name.
- 'Let him quickly bring us more pigeons!' Third-person imperative particle combines with the future verb of bringing.
- 'Hello, Timur, we have run out of pigeons here.' Reflexive plural past verb 'ended' means running out of stock.
- 'What pigeons? I do not have any more!' Timur is already driving through busy Moscow traffic.
- 'If you need them so badly, go catch some on the street!' Timur makes a joke that Denis takes completely seriously.
- 'Senya, please cover for me for one hour!' Preposition 'for' with the accusative marks the duration of his absence.
- 'Hello, Fedya, our client has gone out on the hunt!' Senya gleefully alerts Fedya that the rookie is chasing city pigeons.
