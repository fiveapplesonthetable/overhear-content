## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Our restaurant is truly very rich in bright talents!' Highlighting his restaurant right here, Nagiyev chooses "deystvitelno" ("really, indeed") so Denis gets the point right away.
- 'Our young cook and musician plays wonderfully.' Bringing up the musician beside Denis, Nagiyev reaches for the Russian word "muzykant" ("musician") right at the heart of the line.
- 'He can play at your evening banquet too!' Nagiyev follows up honestly toward Denis, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'Lenochka, darling, wait for me today by four o'clock.' Pointing to the clock in front of Nagiyev, Denis brings in "chetyryom" ("o'clock") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'And I will come to you at six o'clock sharp!' Denis weighs in candidly hearing Nagiyev, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'There should always be enough of a real man for everyone!' Denis responds promptly facing the guest, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Look who is here, why do you not drop by the theater?' Turning to the theater to Nagiyev, the guest puts the weight on "zakhodish" ("to come in", from "zakhodit") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'There is no time at all, lots of work at the restaurant.' Checking on the restaurant in the exchange, Nagiyev adds "sovsem" ("completely, at all") so the statement leaves zero room for doubt.
- 'So maybe you need some help with something?' The guest probes deadpan before Nagiyev can change the subject.
- 'And maybe I really do need a little help!' Nagiyev pushes back vividly in front of the guest and centers the remark on "deystvitelno" ("really, indeed") without any extra fuss.
- 'Tell me, did Barinov himself definitely cook this dish?' Bringing up the dish in this beat, the guest keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Nagiyev purely through rising pitch.
- 'Of course, right now they will serve you his onion soup!' Nagiyev cuts in at once with a nod, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
