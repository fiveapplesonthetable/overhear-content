## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Our restaurant is truly very rich in bright talents!' Short masculine adjective 'rich' governs the instrumental plural.
- 'Our young cook and musician plays wonderfully.' Denis plays the grand piano effortlessly between kitchen duties.
- 'He can play at your evening banquet too!' Prepositional case marks the event where he can perform.
- 'Lenochka, darling, wait for me today by four o'clock.' Preposition 'toward/by' governs the dative of the numeral 'four'.
- 'And I will come to you at six o'clock sharp!' Denis schedules three separate dates in a single evening.
- 'There should always be enough of a real man for everyone!' Impersonal verb 'to suffice' takes the genitive singular.
- 'Look who is here, why do you not drop by the theater?' Gavril Ilyich recognizes an old actor acquaintance.
- 'There is no time at all, lots of work at the restaurant.' Genitive singular 'of time' follows the negative predicate.
- 'So maybe you need some help with something?' Instrumental pronoun 'with what' accompanies the infinitive 'to help'.
- 'And maybe I really do need a little help!' Nagiyev realizes this actor was the backup impersonator he hired earlier.
- 'Tell me, did Barinov himself definitely cook this dish?' Gavril Ilyich prepares to taste the famous onion soup.
- 'Of course, right now they will serve you his onion soup!' Just as the soup arrives, German marches out to expose the ruse.
