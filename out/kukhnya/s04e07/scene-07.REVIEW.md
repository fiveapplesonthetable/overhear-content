## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hi everyone, my dear honest company!' Barinov greets his former brigade with a warm traditional phrase.
- 'I see you are all still holding a grudge against me?' Reflexive verb 'to feel offended' takes 'on' plus accusative.
- 'Forgive me for my bad behavior.' Plural perfective imperative asks the whole team for forgiveness.
- 'After all, I did not say all those things out of malice.' Idiom 'not out of evil' means without bad intentions.
- 'And life has already punished me for that.' Feminine past verb agrees with the feminine noun 'life'.
- 'Yes, I know that I am no angel.' Literally 'not a gift', a classic Russian idiom for a difficult person.
- 'But you are my closest people.' Superlative plural adjective 'closest' expresses deep affection.
- 'I am too old to look for new friends.' Purpose conjunction 'in order to' introduces the infinitive clause.
- 'And besides, I do not need new friends.' Plural short adjective 'needed' agrees with 'new friends'.
- 'I also wanted to pay back my old debts to you.' Prefixed infinitive 'to distribute/pay off' wins the cooks over immediately.
- 'Chef, that is what you should have started with!' Senya grins as the tension in the kitchen melts away.
- 'We are very glad to see you home, Chef!' The brigade warmly welcomes their old master back.
