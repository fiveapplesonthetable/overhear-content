## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hi everyone, my dear honest company!' Over the point and the apology, Barinov lets his real feelings show.
- 'I see you are all still holding a grudge against me?' Here seeing Fedya is all still holding a grudge against him shows Barinov looking for the real truth. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Forgive me for my bad behavior.' With the truth and apology in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'After all, I did not say all those things out of malice.' Once after all he did not say all those things out of malice, Barinov can finally tell Fedya what he means.
- 'And life has already punished me for that.' Adding that life has already punished him fills in the missing piece for Fedya. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Yes, I know that I am no angel.' Noting that he knows that he is no angel keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'But you are my closest people.' Since Fedya is his closest people, Barinov does not waste words on excuses.
- 'I am too old to look for new friends.' Pointing out that he is too old to look for new friends gives Fedya a sobering reality check.
- 'And besides, I do not need new friends.' Barinov reminds Fedya that besides he does not need new friends before the moment passes.
- 'I also wanted to pay back my old debts to you.' Because he also wanted to pay back his old debts to Fedya, Barinov treats the matter as settled.
