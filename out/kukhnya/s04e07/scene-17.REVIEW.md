## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'And here is our signature onion soup!' Presentational particles introduce the steaming bowl.
- 'Simply fantastic, what an amazing and tender taste!' Gavril Ilyich savors the balanced sweet-and-savory broth.
- 'Allow me to interrupt your lovely conversation!' German marches into the dining room to blow the whistle.
- 'Respected guest, they are brazenly deceiving you here!' Impersonal third-person plural verb accuses the hosts.
- 'And how did you like this onion soup?' Diminutive masculine noun 'little soup' carries a sarcastic edge.
- 'The soup turned out very tasty, and what is the matter?' Gavril Ilyich looks at German in surprise.
- 'But after all it is completely impossible to eat this soup!' In everyday Russian, 'soup' flows straight into 'there is' without extra linking words.
- 'Right now it is as sour as pure vinegar!' German is certain that the bottle of vinegar ruined the pot.
- 'Allow me, taste it yourself from my bowl.' The guest offers his spoon so German can judge for himself.
- 'Well why did you go silent, it is a magnificent soup after all!' Barinov grins triumphantly as German tastes the broth.
- 'Forgive me, I do not understand anything at all right now.' Thanks to Senya's sugar, the caramelized onion soup tastes sublime.
- 'Oh no, this simply cannot be!' German pairs 'simply, just' with 'maybe' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
