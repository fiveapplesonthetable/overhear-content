## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'And here is our signature onion soup!' Emphasizing how signature things are in this beat, Barinov links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Simply fantastic, what an amazing and tender taste!' The guest reacts firmly watching Barinov, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Allow me to interrupt your lovely conversation!' Bringing up the conversation during the talk, German brings in "razreshite" ("allow", from "razreshit") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Respected guest, they are brazenly deceiving you here!' German counters pointedly on the spot, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'And how did you like this onion soup?' German challenges calmly whether the guest really thought this through.
- 'The soup turned out very tasty, and what is the matter?' Pointing to the soup before German, the guest leans on "poluchilsya" ("to turn out", a form of "poluchitsya") to give the remark its edge.
- 'But after all it is completely impossible to eat this soup!' Turning to the soup in the exchange, German reaches for the Russian word "sovershenno" ("quite, completely") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Right now it is as sour as pure vinegar!' German answers evenly around the guest, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Allow me, taste it yourself from my bowl.' Pointing to the plate beside German, the guest leans on "poprobuyte" ("try, taste", a form of "poprobovat") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Well why did you go silent, it is a magnificent soup after all!' Mentioning a magnificent soup beside the guest, Barinov keeps the guest's attention locked on what matters.
- 'Forgive me, I do not understand anything at all right now.' German reacts wryly hearing Barinov, so Barinov knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Oh no, this simply cannot be!' Pushing back plainly in the exchange, German uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
