## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, what are you doing, you miserable traitor?' Fedya is shocked to see his best friend pouring vinegar into the pot.
- 'Fedya, calm down, I have already thought everything through well!' Perfective past verb 'thought through' reassures his partner.
- 'Vinegar acid can easily be fixed with ordinary sugar.' Instrumental singular 'with ordinary sugar' marks the neutralizing agent.
- 'As soon as German leaves right now, I will fix everything!' Senya plans to appease German first and save the soup second.
- 'Denis, where did you get a live pigeon in your hands?' Denis bursts through the back door clutching a flapping city pigeon.
- 'All right, folks, where is my Dijon squab?' Barinov comes over looking for the main course.
- 'Quiet, Denis, everything is fine, hide that bird!' Seeing the live bird, Barinov hushes the panicked rookie.
- 'I already cooked a partridge for the guest instead of squab.' Preposition 'instead of' governs the genitive singular 'of pigeon'.
- 'Chef, we need to do something about German!' Fedya warns that German is watching every move in the kitchen.
- 'Thank you, pals, I am in your debt now.' Noun 'debtor' expresses warm gratitude for their loyalty.
- 'Quickly pour the soup into the bowls and bake it!' French onion soup is baked under a crust right before serving.
- 'Right now we will quickly serve the hot onion soup!' Now Senya just needs a distraction to add the sugar.
