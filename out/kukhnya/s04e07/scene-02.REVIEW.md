## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dmitry Vladimirovich, an important guest has come to see us.' Even though an important guest has come to see them, Vika keeps a steady face in front of the guest.
- 'Good morning, but our restaurant is still closed.' Admitting that their restaurant is still closed shows Nagiyev being honest for a change.
- 'Allow me to introduce myself to you first, gentlemen.' Once Nagiyev should allow him to introduce himself to Nagiyev first gentlemen, the guest can finally tell Nagiyev what he means.
- 'I have come on behalf of the famous film director.' Here having come on behalf of the famous film director sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'For the sake of filming I am ready to clear any dates!' Insisting that for the sake of filming he is ready to clear any dates shows Nagiyev is not backing down. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Thank you, but I am here on another matter.' Saying that he is here on another matter is how the guest sets the tone with Nagiyev.
- 'We want to hold a banquet in your restaurant.' Here wanting to hold a banquet in Nagiyev's restaurant sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'We had lunch at your place recently, we liked everything.' Mentioning that they had lunch at Nagiyev's place recently they liked everything moves the talk in a new direction. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Everyone was delighted with Viktor Barinov!' Saying that everyone was delighted with Viktor Barinov is how the guest takes charge of the moment.
- 'We eagerly look forward to meeting him again.' Around the point and shift, the guest sets a calm, everyday tone with Nagiyev.
- 'I will drop by this evening to taste the banquet menu.' Explaining that he will drop by this evening to taste the banquet menu lays the cards on the table for Nagiyev.
- 'You need not doubt it, Barinov will definitely be here!' Insisting that the guest needs not doubt it Barinov will definitely be here shows Nagiyev is not backing down.
