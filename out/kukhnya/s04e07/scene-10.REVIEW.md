## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Enjoy your meal, respected Gavriil Ilyich!' Discussing the appetite around the guest, Nagiyev leans on "uvazhayemyy" ("respected, esteemed") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'I thank you, I will taste it with pleasure right now.' The guest chimes in boldly with a sigh, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Tell me, did Barinov definitely cook this dish?' The guest checks evenly what Nagiyev is actually getting at here.
- 'You know, we would rather celebrate in another place.' Pointing to the place in the exchange, the guest uses "otprazdnuyem" ("to celebrate", from "otprazdnovat") to make the point stick.
- 'I beg you, this is just a small misunderstanding!' Checking on a small misunderstanding around the guest, Nagiyev uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'Right now we will fix everything very quickly!' Nagiyev counters frankly with a shrug, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'They will serve you a dish personally from Viktor Barinov.' Touching on a dish in front of Barinov, Nagiyev uses "blyudo" ("dish") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'I will personally oversee this strictly myself!' Nagiyev chimes in dryly on the spot and lands on "proslezhu" ("to see to it", built on "prosledit") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Well then, Mister Know-It-All, chop the tomatoes and egg!' Barinov cuts in candidly around Nagiyev, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Judging by everything, I am the one who won our bet!' Pointing to the judging at once, Barinov chooses "sudya" ("judging") so Nagiyev gets the point right away.
- 'So put on an apron quickly and get to work!' Emphasizing an apron to Nagiyev, Barinov leans on "fartuk" ("apron") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Vitya, go to the kitchen quickly to save the banquet!' Nagiyev pushes back frankly facing Barinov, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
