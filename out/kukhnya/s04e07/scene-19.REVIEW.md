## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What an amazing atmosphere you have in here!' Gavril Ilyich reacts with dry irony as feathers flutter over the table.
- 'Live pigeons are flying in the hall, just like in Paris!' Multidirectional verb 'are flying around' matches the chaotic bird.
- 'Gavriil Ilyich, wait, I will explain everything right now!' Nagiyev makes one last desperate attempt to salvage the deal.
- 'No, thank you, gentlemen, all the very best to you!' Genitive farewell wish 'all the best' signals his final departure.
- 'All the best, goodbye, respected Gavriil Ilyich.' Defeated, Nagiyev watches the Filmmakers' Union banquet walk out the door.
- 'Oh no, this simply cannot be!' Even Chef Barinov is stunned to see a live street pigeon emerge from a serving platter.
- 'Where did that live pigeon under the cover come from?' Preposition 'under' governs the instrumental singular 'cover'.
- 'Sasha, please, just shut your mouth and leave!' Nagiyev tells the hapless actor impersonator to get out before he loses his temper.
- 'Go away from here quickly, do not make me angry right now!' Negated imperative warns the waiter and actor to clear the room.
- 'Denis, was it you who hid a street pigeon in there?' Barinov immediately realizes what happened to Denis's captured bird.
- 'Such an important banquet fell through because of a silly bird!' Reflexive past verb 'broke off/fell through' describes a ruined deal.
- 'All right, Dima, then it was just not meant to be today.' Idiom 'not fate' accepts the absurd twist of luck.
