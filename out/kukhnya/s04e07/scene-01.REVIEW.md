## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What a wonderful idea to put a beautiful piano in the restaurant!' Indeclinable neuter loanword 'piano' stays unchanged in the accusative.
- 'Do you think I have nowhere else to put my money?' Negative predicate adverb 'nowhere' takes a dative pronoun and infinitive.
- 'Yesterday I hosted a big party for customs officers.' Past-tense masculine verb 'led' means hosting an event as master of ceremonies.
- 'And they paid all the performers with strange things.' Reflexive verb 'paid off' governs 'with' plus the instrumental plural.
- 'I ended up with this piano for my work.' Reflexive past verb 'fell to one's lot' takes a dative recipient.
- 'I will think about that on Monday.' Preposition 'in/on' with the accusative marks the day of the week.
- 'Wait, Vika, until what Monday on earth?' Preposition 'until' governs the genitive singular.
- 'After all, I warned you about this in advance.' Imperfective past feminine verb reminds her boss of an earlier notice.
- 'Max and I are going to my mom's place.' The idiom 'we with Max' pairs with the verb of going by vehicle.
- 'My mom is having a big anniversary this weekend.' Prepositional plural 'on the weekend' marks Saturday and Sunday.
- 'All right, I was still lucky with this piano.' Impersonal dative verb 'it was lucky to me' expresses good fortune.
- 'They gave another performer a whole truckload of chocolate!' Feminine accusative 'whole truck' governs the genitive 'of chocolate'.
