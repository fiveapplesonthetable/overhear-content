## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, I am not crying because of the onion at all.' Denis answers plainly watching Senya, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'I just remembered my beloved grandmother right now.' Focusing on the grandmother before Senya, Denis reaches for the Russian word "lyubimuyu" ("favourite, beloved", built from "lyubimyy") right at the center of the line.
- 'The chef is clearly in a very bad mood today.' Touching on the today at once, Senya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'We had better not make him nervous at all today.' Senya counters steadily around Timur, testing Timur's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Gentlemen cooks, I beg your pardon!' Addressing the forgiveness during the talk, Timur reaches for the Russian word "proshcheniya" ("forgiveness", built from "proshcheniye") right at the center of the line.
- 'You know that I bring fresh groceries.' Timur reacts honestly without a pause, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'But my crates do not have hands or legs!' Timur chimes in right away without any doubt, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'They will not carry themselves to the storeroom!' Mentioning the warehouse at once, Timur centers the remark on "sklad" ("warehouse") without any extra fuss.
- 'I have been waiting ten minutes already, have a conscience!' Mentioning a conscience with Senya, Timur lands on "sovest" ("conscience") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Go quickly, Denis, accept the fresh groceries.' Senya responds pointedly in their talk, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Why do I have to be the one to go to the storeroom?' Emphasizing the warehouse around Senya, Denis leans on "dolzhen" ("has to, must") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Of course you, after all that is the duty of all newcomers!' Senya proceeds wryly with a shrug, steering the talk right where he wants it.
