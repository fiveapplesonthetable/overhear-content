## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, I am not crying because of the onion at all.' Compound preposition 'because of' takes the genitive singular 'of onion'.
- 'I just remembered my beloved grandmother right now.' Perfective past verb 'remembered' takes the feminine accusative.
- 'The chef is clearly in a very bad mood today.' The idiom 'not in spirit' or 'in bad spirit' describes a foul mood.
- 'We had better not make him nervous at all today.' Comparative adverb 'better' pairs with a negated imperfective infinitive.
- 'Gentlemen cooks, I beg your pardon!' Timur interrupts the kitchen banter with ironic formality.
- 'You know that I bring fresh groceries.' Imperfective verb of motion by vehicle expresses regular deliveries.
- 'But my crates do not have hands or legs!' Negated existential 'no' governs the genitive plural 'of hands' and 'of legs'.
- 'They will not carry themselves to the storeroom!' Emphatic reflexive phrase 'themselves' underscores the joke.
- 'I have been waiting ten minutes already, have a conscience!' Present tense 'I wait' expresses an action continuing from the past.
- 'Go quickly, Denis, accept the fresh groceries.' Double singular imperatives send the junior cook outside.
- 'Why do I have to be the one to go to the storeroom?' Emphatic particle highlights the subject pronoun 'I'.
- 'Of course you, after all that is the duty of all newcomers!' Substantivized adjective in the genitive plural means 'of newcomers'.
