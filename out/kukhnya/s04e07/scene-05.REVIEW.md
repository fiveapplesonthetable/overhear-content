## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Viktor Petrovich, may I have you for a second?' Nagiyev starts with polite formality in front of Elena.
- 'Elena Pavlovna, there is a strange person next to you.' Barinov pretends not to recognize his former boss.
- 'Vitya, stop sulking, you are a grown man!' Singular imperative 'drop it' urges him to stop holding a grudge.
- 'After all, I came to you on business.' Dative phrase 'on business' explains that the visit is serious.
- 'Today important guests will come to my restaurant.' Plural future 'will come' introduces the Filmmakers' Union visit.
- 'They want to see you as the head chef of the restaurant.' Instrumental case marks the role of head chef.
- 'I will give you ten percent of the order!' Numeral 'ten' governs the genitive plural 'of percents'.
- 'Good, I agree, but I do not need the money.' Masculine short adjective 'agreed' surprises Nagiyev.
- 'As payment I will give you three kicks!' Compound preposition 'in the capacity of' governs the genitive 'of payment'.
- 'What, you want to give me three kicks?' Numeral 'three' takes the genitive singular of the masculine noun for a kick.
- 'Vitya, why are you playing the fool again?' The classic Russian idiom 'rolling the fool' means clowning around.
- 'Fine, I agree, just help me out tonight!' Desperate for the Mikhalkov banquet, Nagiyev accepts the humiliating deal.
