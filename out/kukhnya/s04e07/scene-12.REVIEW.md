## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Gentlemen cooks, our task is very difficult!' Feminine adjective 'difficult' agrees with the subject noun 'task'.
- 'The guest still has the unpleasant taste of the previous dish.' Possessive phrase 'at the guest' combines with 'remained'.
- 'We must show him that we know how to cook!' Modal predicate 'must' takes a perfective infinitive and dative pronoun.
- 'Fedya, quickly make a tender poached egg for the salad!' Barinov assigns stations with brisk familiarity.
- 'It will be ready in exactly two minutes, Chef!' Preposition 'in/after' governs the accusative time phrase.
- 'Senya, and the fluffy mashed potatoes are on you!' Indeclinable neuter French loanword denotes puree or mashed potatoes.
- 'Our mashed potatoes will be the tenderest in the world!' Instrumental superlative adjective follows the future of 'to be'.
- 'Louis, my pal, will you delight us today?' Perfective future verb 'will delight' takes the accusative pronoun 'us'.
- 'Louis said that his dessert will be magnificent!' Instrumental adjective 'magnificent' marks the future predicate.
- 'How good it is when everyone is busy with their own craft!' Short passive participle 'occupied/busy' governs the instrumental case.
- 'It is for this very craft that we were all created.' Plural short passive participle 'created' agrees with 'we all'.
- 'Working with you is a pure pleasure, Viktor Petrovich!' While the brigade celebrates, German plots sabotage from the shadows.
