- 'Grandpa, we will buy the best medicine!' Max follows up right away facing the guest, before the guest can change the subject.
- 'I have grown old. There is your diagnosis.' Talking about the diagnosis in front of Max, the guest brings in "diagnoz" ("diagnosis") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'And do not get upset.' Pressing on plainly before Max, the "-sya" ending on "rasstraivaysya" ("be upset, worry") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'I have lived a happy life.' The guest speaks at once with a shrug, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
