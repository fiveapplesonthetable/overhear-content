## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'It looks great! You can see right away the hand of a master.' Because it looks great, Senya does not bother softening the blow for Denis.
- 'Come on!' With the luck and work at stake, Denis reacts without holding back. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Well, good luck! You are going to room three eleven.' Once Denis is going to room three eleven, Senya makes sure Denis hears every word. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Eleonora Andreyevna, your quail.' With the truth and luck in play, Denis keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Why did it take so long?' By wondering why it take so long, Eleonora presses Denis for a straight answer. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'You will have to make up for it!' Saying that Denis will have to make up for it is how Eleonora takes charge of the moment. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'To be honest, I do not think it will work out for us.' Claiming that to be honest he does not think it will work out for them sounds confident, even if Eleonora has doubts.
- 'What if he figures everything out?' With the shift and secret hanging in the air, Denis puts Eleonora on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'He will not figure it out!' Insisting that he will not figure it out shows Eleonora is not backing down. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'As long as Vitya considers you my enemy.' Around the plan and secret, Eleonora sets a calm, everyday tone with Denis.
- 'You will be like his own son to him.' Noting that Denis will be like his own son to him keeps Eleonora's side of the story simple and clear. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I hope you are right.' Noting that he hopes Eleonora is right keeps Denis's side of the story simple and clear. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
