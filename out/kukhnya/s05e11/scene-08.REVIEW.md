## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, are you ready? Listen, I got terribly hungry.' Here asking whether Barinov ready shows Elena looking for the real truth. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Yes, I am coming, I am coming.' Barinov explains that he is coming he is coming, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'Maybe he feels shy when you look at him?' Asking whether maybe he feels shy when Barinov looks at him puts Barinov right on the defensive.
- 'Hello, Viktor Petrovich, Lyova is not giving me orders.' Because Lyova is not giving him orders, Denis treats the matter as settled.
- 'I am the one who told him that.' Adding that he is the one who told him fills in the missing piece for Denis. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'For now you look around and study the menu.' Barinov mentions that for now Denis looks around and study the menu so Denis sees what is at stake.
- 'I understood the task. Goodbye!' Saying that he understood the task is how Denis takes charge of the moment. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Goodbye.' With the plan and menu in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Did you hire a new cook?' Here asking whether Barinov hires a new cook shows Elena looking for the real truth.
- 'Yes. She nearly tore that poor guy to pieces!' Over the moment and the menu, Barinov lets his real feelings show. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Surely Eleonora planted him.' Over the plan and the menu, Elena answers Barinov without a second thought.
- 'What does Eleonora have to do with it?' Wondering what Eleonora have to do with it is how Barinov puts Denis on the spot.
