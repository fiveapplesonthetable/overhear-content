## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Quiet, sit down. Why did you tell her?' Cutting in dryly in front of Vika, Max puts the question word "zachem" right at the start so Vika hears the focus immediately.
- 'I did not tell them anything!' Vika jumps in earnestly in their talk, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Grandma, who told you about everything? Call a doctor quickly!' Emphasizing the old lady in this beat, Max puts the emphasis on "babushka" ("old lady") in the middle of the talk.
- 'She had an attack, but it is nothing terrible.' Insisting on how terrible things are beside Max, Vika reaches for the Russian word "strashnogo" ("terrible, serious", built from "strashnyy") right at the center of the line.
- 'For now we need to avoid physical strain and buy these medicines.' Vika steps in honestly without a pause, testing Max's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'All the best, thank you!' Max openly delivers this word of thanks in front of Vika using "spasibo" ("thank you") — the everyday word for "thank you", which grew out of the old blessing "spasi Bog" ("God save you").
- 'Listen, now I am even afraid to be late for work.' Touching on the job in this beat, Lyova chooses "rabotu" ("job", from the root "rabota") so Max hears the exact meaning.
- 'Of course the ancients were right, but one should not forget about life either.' Max continues sharply without any doubt, before Lyova can change the subject.
- 'You should have seen his face!' Underscoring the face with Max, Lyova chooses "litso" ("face") so Max gets the point right away.
- 'Buy flowers for your wife!' Checking on the spouse at once, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 3 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'We are not married. So what, not give flowers now? Give me all of them!' Lyova tests curiously how Vika expects that to work in practice.
- 'Here you go!' Lyova counters evenly around Vika and lands on "proshu" ("beg, ask", built on "prosit") to keep the statement grounded.
