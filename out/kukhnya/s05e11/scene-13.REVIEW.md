## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Maxim Ilyich, hold on! What is that clinking in your bag?' With the conversation and hotel hanging in the air, Vika puts the guest on the spot.
- 'Oh, I bought lemonade.' Mentioning that he bought lemonade moves the talk in a new direction. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Will you treat me? Give me the bag, please.' By checking whether the guest treats, Vika presses the guest for a straight answer. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Do not. If I do not have a drink, I will weep from grief.' Because if he does not have a drink he will weep from grief, the guest treats the matter as settled.
- 'My Lyubanya asked me not to tell anyone.' Explaining that his asked him not to tell anyone lays the cards on the table for Vika.
- 'She has two months left to live. She is dying.' The guest mentions that she has two months left to live so Vika sees what is at stake.
- 'My checkup is a pretext to come here and see you.' Claiming that his checkup is a pretext to come here and see Vika sounds confident, even if Vika has doubts.
- 'Are you serious right now?' Checking whether the guest serious right now cuts straight through the tension between Vika and the guest. Even in the middle of the drama, she keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'People do not joke about such things!' Once people do not joke about such things, the guest makes sure Vika hears every word. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Only do not tell Maxim. He loves his grandma very much.' Realizing that only do not tell Maxim changes how the guest handles the situation.
- 'That is why I feel like having a drink.' Adding that that is why he feels like having a drink fills in the missing piece for Vika.
- 'Stay strong, Maxim Ilyich.' Admitting that the guest should stay strong Maxim Ilyich shows Vika being honest for a change.
