## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'They say a dog is man's best friend.' Turning to a dog before Barinov, Max reaches for the Russian word "sobaka" ("dog") right at the heart of the line.
- 'A dog, not some cat.' Pointing to a dog right here, Barinov lands on "sobaka" ("dog") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Hold on a second, a friend does not attack a person.' Max opens up at once facing Barinov, before Barinov can change the subject.
- 'Have you ever heard of that?' Barinov reacts frankly around Max and leans on "kogda-nibud" ("someday, one day") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'They have their own functions.' Pointing to the function in the exchange, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 4 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Well, and what other functions are those?' Barinov challenges sharply to see how Max will answer on the spot.
- 'Listen, are you arguing just for the sake of arguing?' Max cuts in sharply to Barinov and uses "slushay" ("listen", from "slushat") to make the point stick.
- 'And you are not arguing just for the sake of it.' Jumping in earnestly on the spot, Barinov uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'The Americans do not need that anyway.' Max replies pointedly with a smile, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Of course, way more.' Barinov casually speaks this confirmation in front of Max using "konechno" ("of course") — spoken with a soft "shn" sound in everyday Moscow Russian to mean "of course, certainly".
- 'And do you know how much Galkin has?' Stepping in warmly in the exchange, Max keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Barinov purely through rising pitch.
- 'That is him over there.' Barinov counters at once hearing Max, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'What did you just say?' Reacting dryly at once, Max keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Barinov purely through rising pitch.
