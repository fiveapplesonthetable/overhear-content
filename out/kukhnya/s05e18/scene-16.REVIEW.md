## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Lyova, wait, I lied to you.' Over the morning and the story, Gulya answers Lyova without a second thought.
- 'I just got very scared back then.' Pointing out that she just got very scared back then gives Lyova a sobering reality check.
- 'In fact, I do not have a husband.' Gulya explains that in fact she does not have a husband, keeping her voice calm and direct. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'And I do not have children either.' Realizing that she does not have children either changes how Gulya handles the situation.
- 'Really? So you are not married at all?' Asking whether so Gulya is not married at all puts Gulya right on the defensive. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'No, I am single and very glad.' Being single and very glad moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'What a delicious treat we dropped here!' Over the kitchen and the fear, Lyova lets his real feelings show.
- 'Vitya, you do not need to explain anything to me.' Adding that Gulya does not need to explain anything fills in the missing piece for Gulya. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'I do not want to find out what happened here.' Once she does not want to find out what happened here, Tanya can finally tell Gulya what she means.
- 'Make sure everything is ready by Vika's arrival.' Telling Gulya to make sure everything is ready by Vika arrival moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'Tanya, we will definitely get everything done here.' Saying that they will definitely get everything done here is how Lyova sets the tone with Gulya.
- 'I firmly promise you that.' Between the moment and the fear, Lyova gives Gulya a quick, grounded response. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
