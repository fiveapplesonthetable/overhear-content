## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Do you have a friend who plays the guitar?' Bringing up the guitar in front of Denis, Nikita uses "u tebya" (literally "by you") where English says "you have" or "your".
- 'And does it have to be specifically on the guitar?' Nikita wonders point-blank whether Denis really thought this through.
- 'Yes, I specifically need a guitarist today.' Addressing a guitarist in front of Denis, Nikita puts the emphasis on "gitarist" ("guitarist") in the middle of the talk.
- 'I arranged it, nobody will be in the dining room.' Focusing on the hall on the spot, the "-sya" ending on "dogovorilsya" ("made a deal") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'You sit in the corner and play softly.' Nikita opens up calmly beside Denis, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Of course, I will gladly play.' Denis casually directs this confirmation without hesitation using "konechno" ("of course") — spoken with a soft "shn" sound in everyday Moscow Russian to mean "of course, certainly".
- 'I would like to get the money up front right now.' Focusing on the money in the exchange, Denis brings in "poluchit" ("to get, to receive") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'I have to pay rent for the apartment today.' Denis cuts in steadily around Nikita, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'No problem, let's ride over to the hotel.' Underscoring the problems on the spot, Nikita centers the thought on "problem" ("problems", from "problema") without wasting a word.
- 'Oguzok, let's work very fast.' Weighing in readily during the talk, Barinov starts with "davay" ("come on, let's") to invite Nikita right into the action.
- 'I hang the shelves, and you assemble the crib.' Barinov pushes back plainly without any doubt, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Just be more careful with the tools.' Focusing on the tool at once, Barinov leans on "instrumentom" ("tool, instrument", a form of "instrument") to give the remark its edge.
