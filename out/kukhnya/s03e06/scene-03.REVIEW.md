## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What are you going to manage there?' Nagiyev quizzes curiously how Kristina expects that to work in practice.
- 'These are all my people.' Turning evenly beside Kristina, Nagiyev links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'You will not manage them, is that clear?' Answering boldly during the talk, Nagiyev keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Kristina purely through rising pitch.
- 'No, I will manage them.' Kristina proceeds sharply with a sigh, steering the talk right where she wants it.
- 'Take the champagne and go away from here.' Discussing the champagne in front of Kristina, Nagiyev lands on "shampanskoye" ("champagne") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Well why, leave it here.' Kristina follows up plainly around Nagiyev and leans on "ostav" ("keep, leave", a form of "ostavit") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Quickly take this champagne away from here.' Nagiyev steps in dryly with a shrug, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'And I told you, leave it, otherwise I will fire you.' Kristina speaks decisively beside Nagiyev and puts the emphasis on "inache" ("otherwise") in the middle of the talk.
- 'You two had a hard breakup.' Kostya reacts frankly hearing Kristina, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'That is why it gives you no peace.' Kostya proceeds right away with a shrug, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Thank you, friend, you explained everything clearly to me.' Touching on the thank right here, Nagiyev uses "spasibo" ("thank you") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'That way you will gradually forget her.' Kostya steps in calmly in the exchange and uses "postepenno" ("gradually") to carry the main weight of the thought.
