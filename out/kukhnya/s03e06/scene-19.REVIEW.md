## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dima, I beg you very much.' Explaining that she begs Nagiyev very much lays the cards on the table for Nagiyev.
- 'Please, just calm down right now.' Between the moment and the plan, Vika gives Nagiyev a quick, grounded response.
- 'I will give him a rough time!' Declaring that he will give him a rough time brings Nagiyev's temper right to the surface. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'He will fly out of here like a cork.' Saying that he will fly out of here like a cork is how Nagiyev sets the tone with Vika.
- 'Please, do not do anything.' Once do not do anything, Vika can finally tell Nagiyev what she means. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'After all, Maxim is not at fault for anything.' Adding that after all Maxim is not at fault for anything fills in the missing piece for Nagiyev.
- 'And who is at fault for this then?' Here asking who at fault for this then is shows Nagiyev looking for the real truth.
- 'He and I broke up long ago.' With the work and plan in play, Vika keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Believe me, all of that is already in the past.' Adding that Nagiyev should believe her all of that is already in the past fills in the missing piece for Nagiyev. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'I used to have many women.' Nagiyev reminds Vika that he used to have many women before the moment passes.
- 'But I have never experienced such feelings before.' Claiming that he has never experienced such feelings sounds confident, even if Vika has doubts. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'I simply want very much to be with you.' Explaining that he simply wants very much to be with Vika lays the cards on the table for Vika. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
