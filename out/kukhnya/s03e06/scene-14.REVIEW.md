- 'Dima, this is not at all what you thought.' Even though this is not at all what Max thought, Vika keeps a steady face in front of Max. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Yes, this is not at all what you thought.' Admitting that this is not at all what Nagiyev thought shows Max being honest for a change.
- 'I keep forgetting that you are just friends.' Nagiyev mentions that he keeps forgetting that Max is just friends so Max sees what is at stake. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.

- 'And I will ask you to stay here.' Since he will ask Max to stay here, Nagiyev does not waste words on excuses. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Maxim, you are already a grown boy.' Mentioning that Maxim Max is already a grown boy moves the talk in a new direction.

- 'I have serious plans for Victoria Sergeyevna.' Realizing that he has serious plans for Victoria Sergeyevna changes how Nagiyev handles the situation. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
