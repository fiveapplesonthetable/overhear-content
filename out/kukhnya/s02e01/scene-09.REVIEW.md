## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, Kostya, can you hear me?' Max greets his friend on the phone.
- 'Yes, Max, what happened to you?' Kostya hears the panic in Max's voice.
- 'I have a big problem right now.' Max admits he is in trouble.
- 'Did you already talk with Vika?' Kostya knows Max was trying to apologize to Victoria.
- 'I wanted to talk with her seriously.' Max explains what he tried to do.
- 'But she just turned around and left!' Both past-tense verbs describe Victoria's quick exit.
- 'Well then come back to the kitchen.' Kostya advises him to return to his shift.
- 'That is the thing, I cannot!' This conversational phrase highlights the main problem.
- 'Why can you not come?' Kostya does not understand why Max is still outside.
- 'Because I lost a small key.' Max admits he cannot unlock himself.
- 'What key did you lose?' Kostya is confused by Max's explanation.
- 'Kostya, come out to the street to me urgently!' Max begs his friend to hurry outside.
- 'Fine, wait there, I will come right now.' Kostya agrees to come and help him out.
