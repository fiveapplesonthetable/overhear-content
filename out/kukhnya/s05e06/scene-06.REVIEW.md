## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good morning, I brought you dinner.' Barinov responds evenly facing Katya, so Katya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'I do not want to eat.' Reacting evenly on the spot, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 4 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Just try it: water and aspirin.' Barinov fires back steadily with a nod, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Very delicious.' Katya answers sharply hearing Barinov, before Barinov can change the subject.
- 'I was thinking here, you and I rarely see each other.' Barinov follows up directly to Katya and puts the weight on "vidimsya" ("see each other", from "videtsya") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'Let us go out somewhere.' Answering plainly before Katya, Barinov starts with "davay" ("come on, let's") to invite Katya right into the action.
- 'Sure, where to?' Katya inquires point-blank to see how Barinov will answer on the spot.
- 'To the zoo or to the movies?' Emphasizing the zoo in the exchange, Barinov centers the remark on "zoopark" ("zoo") without any extra fuss.
- 'Dad, do you hear yourself, what zoo?' Reacting promptly to Barinov, Katya puts the question word "kakoy" right at the start so Barinov hears the focus immediately.
- 'Well, we will go somewhere else.' Barinov reacts steadily with a shrug, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'I will think of something.' Barinov pushes back calmly in front of Katya and centers the thought on "pridumayu" ("will come up with", from "pridumat") without wasting a word.
- 'Yeah, and what can you surprise me with?' Opening up smoothly at once, Katya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Barinov purely through rising pitch.
