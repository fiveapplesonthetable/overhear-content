## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You are looking at it as if you are going to marry it.' Answering briskly at once, the "-sya" ending on "zhenitsya" ("to marry") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'The fish is expensive, it must be in perfect condition.' Lyova speaks openly toward Timur, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Listen, do you want the same one at a discount?' Touching on a discount in front of Lyova, Timur chooses "skidkoy" ("discount", from the root "skidka") so Lyova hears the exact meaning.
- 'It is a special offer: stronger smell, bigger discount.' Turning to the rally at once, Timur links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Thank you, I had better take the fresh one.' Lyova simply sends this word of thanks at the start of the beat using "spasibo" ("thank you") — the everyday word for "thank you", which grew out of the old blessing "spasi Bog" ("God save you").
- 'Did stellate sturgeon appear on our menu?' Discussing the stellate sturgeon right here, Senya centers the remark on "sevryuga" ("stellate sturgeon") without any extra fuss.
- 'Yes, we decided to try it out.' Pushing back evenly at once, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 3 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Will you cut me a little piece?' Senya probes straight out about what Lyova just brought up.
- 'I have never eaten stellate sturgeon.' Mentioning the stellate sturgeon in this beat, Senya uses "sevryugu" ("stellate sturgeon", from "sevryuga") to make the point stick.
- 'And do you know how much it costs?' Answering readily before Senya, Lyova puts the question word "skolko" right at the start so Senya hears the focus immediately.
- 'I know.' Senya speaks up bluntly during the talk, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'That is why I am asking.' Senya answers openly with Lyova and leans on "poetomu" ("that's why") to give the whole sentence its punch.
