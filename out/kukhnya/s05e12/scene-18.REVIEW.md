## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We called him, we thought he was with Vika!' Max responds bluntly around Senya, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Well, to be honest...' Senya jumps in boldly in front of Max and lands on "chestno" ("honestly") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Well, tell me, why did a ring appear instead of a pendant?' Mentioning a pendant during the talk, Barinov puts the question word "pochemu" right at the start so Senya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Chef, we thought that you wanted to make a proposal.' Senya fires back evenly without any doubt, watching Barinov's face to see how the news lands.
- 'We lost your gift and bought a ring.' Pointing to the gift in this beat, Senya brings in "podarok" ("gift, voucher") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'You have just ruined my whole life!' Barinov jumps in firmly with a shrug, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Chef, or maybe you ruin everything yourself?' Max prompts plainly if there is more going on behind the scenes.
- 'Say thank you that they gave you a chance.' Talking about the thank during the talk, Max reaches for the Russian word "spasibo" ("thank you") right at the heart of the line.
- 'To do the right thing for once in your life!' Talking about the life to Barinov, Max chooses "zhizni" ("life", from the root "zhizn") so Barinov hears the exact meaning.
- 'Once in my life?' Barinov tests evenly what Max is actually getting at here.
- 'Exactly so, chef.' Jumping in readily before Barinov, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 3 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Where is Elena right now?' Answering briskly to Max, Barinov links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
