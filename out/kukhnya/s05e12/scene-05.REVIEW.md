## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Forgive me, I did not notice you. You broke my nose!' Eleonora speaks wryly around Elena, so Elena knows she is not just making small talk.
- 'It hurts me too, I did not notice you.' Elena follows up promptly around Eleonora, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'You should watch where you are going!' Eleonora opens up briskly at once and leans on "smotret" ("to watch") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'What happened? How interesting this turned out!' Speaking briskly right here, the "-sya" ending on "poluchilos" ("worked out") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'The ex-wife nearly killed the future one.' Barinov reacts frankly with a nod, making his position completely clear.
- 'Future? I am not in that status yet.' Pointing out how future she is at once, Elena lands on "budushchuyu" ("future", built on "budushchiy") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Careful! Lena, are you alright?' Discussing the order around Elena, Barinov links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'I am fine. I bumped into her myself. Until tonight!' Elena continues honestly in this moment, without trying to dress up how she really feels.
- 'Everything is fine, girls.' Mentioning the girl at once, Barinov reaches for the Russian word "devochki" ("girl's", built from "devochka") right at the center of the line.
- 'You always ruin everything!' Leaning in directly on the spot, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 4 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Today is Lena's birthday.' Barinov pushes back wryly with a smile, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'It is morning, and you are not celebrating yet?' Highlighting the morning in the exchange, Eleonora puts the weight on "otmechayesh" ("celebrate", from "otmechat") as the exchange unfolds.
