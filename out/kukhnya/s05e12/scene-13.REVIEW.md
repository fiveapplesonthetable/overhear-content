## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, tell me, did a tall man stop by your shop this morning?' Discussing a tall man before Kostya, Senya chooses "zdravstvuyte" ("hello") so Kostya gets the point right away.
- 'In a black coat and with a moustache?' Mentioning a black coat on the spot, Senya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Kostya purely through rising pitch.
- 'Yes, it seems there was someone like that.' Kostya counters promptly with a shrug, testing Senya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'And what did he buy?' Senya reacts quietly at once and puts the weight on "pokupal" ("bought, were buying", from "pokupat") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'An engagement ring.' Bringing up an engagement ring to Senya, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 2 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Do you have the exact same one?' Senya presses sharply how Max expects that to work in practice.
- 'Who is that guy with Vika?' Bringing up the guy during the talk, Max links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Probably a masseur. Vika forgot the business card on the table.' Talking about the massage therapist right here, Kostya brings in "massazhist" ("massage therapist") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'He does not look like a masseur at all!' Max opens up boldly with a nod, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Max, are you a fool? It says: massage!' Touching on the massage to Max, Kostya centers the remark on "massazh" ("a massage") without any extra fuss.
- 'This masseur said that he would do everything for Vika very carefully!' Discussing the massage therapist with Kostya, Max brings in "massazhist" ("massage therapist") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'I heard it myself! Right, Nastya, dial his number!' Max responds right away on the spot, watching Kostya's face to see how the news lands.
