## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, tell me, did a tall man stop by your shop this morning?' Senya chooses this direct touch with Kostya: "zdravstvuyte" means "hello", and it carries the main weight of what Senya is saying.
- 'In a black coat and with a moustache?' Senya offers the comment calmly now: Russian keeps the exact word order of a normal statement here, turning it into a question for Max purely through rising pitch at the end.
- 'Yes, it seems there was someone like that.' Kostya stands firm promptly without holding back, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'And what did he buy?' Senya stresses one telling term on cue: "pokupal" means "bought, were buying" (from "pokupat"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'An engagement ring.' Kostya gives this warning toward Max: spoken Russian trims this down to just 2 words, dropping the extra helper words that English has to spell out.
- 'Do you have the exact same one?' Senya presses sharply what Max is actually getting at here.
- 'Who is that guy with Vika?' Max states the remark right away: notice how Russian needs no word for "am", "is", or "are" in the present — placing the words side by side says it all.
- 'Probably a masseur. Vika forgot the business card on the table.' Kostya fires back pointedly without holding back, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'He does not look like a masseur at all!' Max chimes in boldly to be clear, watching Kostya's face to see how the news lands.
- 'Max, are you a fool? It says: massage!' Kostya stresses one sharp element on cue: "massazh" means "a massage", and it carries the main weight of what Kostya is saying.
- 'This masseur said that he would do everything for Vika very carefully!' Max holds firm bluntly around Kostya, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'I heard it myself! Right, Nastya, dial his number!' Max continues right away without holding back, before Senya can change the subject.
