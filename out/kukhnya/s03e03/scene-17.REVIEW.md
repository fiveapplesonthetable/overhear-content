## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Pregnant guy, is that you again?' When the girl asks if pregnant guy is that Max again, Max has to answer fast. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.

- 'Listen, I braked way before that.' Between the phone and the plan, the girl gives Max a quick, grounded response.
- 'I hit the asphalt!' Hitting the asphalt is Max's blunt reaction in front of the girl. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Such tricks won't work with me, got it?' Questioning whether such tricks will not work with her got it is how the girl tests Max's story. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Do you want to blackmail me? You will fly right out of Moscow!' Here asking whether Max wants to blackmail shows the girl looking for the real truth.
- 'Vika! Vika!' With the point and room at stake, Max reacts without holding back. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'You? Nobody will believe you. You have no proof.' Asking whether nobody will believe Max puts Max right on the defensive. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'I am afraid you are not right.' Being afraid the girl is not right moves the conversation forward without extra drama.

- 'And here is the proof!' Saying that here is the proof is how Max takes charge of the moment. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
