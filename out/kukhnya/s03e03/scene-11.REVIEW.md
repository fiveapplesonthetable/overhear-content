## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, this is madness.' Vika mentions that this is madness so Max sees what is at stake. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Rodion Sergeyevich, we have an offer for you.' Claiming that they have an offer for Rodion sounds confident, even if Rodion has doubts. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'I have financial difficulties right now, other offers do not interest me.' Having financial difficulties right now other offers do not interest him moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'You need to get hit by a car. For money.' Max explains that Rodion needs to get hit by a car, keeping his voice calm and direct. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'According to the rate among my colleagues, that costs five thousand.' Realizing that according to the rate among his colleagues that costs five thousand changes how Rodion handles the situation. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'But for you I agree to three.' Noting that for Max he agrees to three keeps Rodion's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Max, what if she injures Rodion Sergeyevich?' Over the shift and the car, Vika presses Max for a straight answer. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'No, this is foolish and dangerous. I forbid you.' Admitting that this is foolish and dangerous shows Vika being honest for a change. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Fine, whatever you say.' Saying that fine whatever Rodion says is how Max sets the tone with Rodion.
- 'If I understood Viktoria Sergeyevna correctly, the operation is canceled?' Asking whether if he understood Viktoria Sergeyevna correctly the operation is canceled puts Max right on the defensive. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'No way. Listen to a woman and do the opposite.' Listening to a woman and do the opposite moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'Senya, good morning!' With the moment and car at stake, Max reacts without holding back.
