## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'She thinks that she is conducting an orchestra.' Max cuts in warmly without holding back, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Right now she is having a seasonal flare-up.' Emphasizing a seasonal flare with Barinov, Max centers the remark on "obostreniye" ("flare-up, relapse") without any extra fuss.
- 'She could land right back in the clinic.' Touching on the clinic at once, Max uses "kliniku" ("clinic", from "klinika") to make the point stick.
- 'Just do not tell anyone about this, the Chef will get upset.' Max responds at once with a nod, before Barinov can change the subject.
- 'So what do you do for a living?' Barinov replies briskly during the talk and puts the weight on "zanimayetes" ("are busy with", from "zanimatsya") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'I work with people all the time.' Katya chimes in boldly at once and chooses "postoyanno" ("constantly") so Barinov gets the point right away.
- 'Wait, I will guess it myself right now.' Barinov replies honestly watching Katya, so Katya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Are you a teacher, a doctor, or a flight attendant?' Bringing up a teacher during the talk, Barinov reaches for the Russian word "uchitelnitsa" ("teacher") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Hello, I am very busy right now.' Noting how busy she is in this beat, Katya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Vitya, after dinner will we go to your place?' Katya presses openly why things suddenly took this turn between them.
- 'Why would we go to my place?' Barinov responds dryly in the exchange and lands on "yekhat" ("to drive, to go") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Or to a hotel, will there be a continuation of the party?' Discussing a continuation in the exchange, Katya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Barinov purely through rising pitch.
