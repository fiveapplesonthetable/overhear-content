## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'If I offended you, I ask your forgiveness.' Even though if she offended the guest she asks the guest's forgiveness, Katya keeps a steady face in front of the guest. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'No, now I must be by your side.' Admitting that he must be by the guest's side shows Lyova being honest for a change.
- 'Together you and I will overcome everything.' Saying that together the guest and he will overcome everything is how Lyova sets the tone with the guest.
- 'Wait, what exactly will we overcome?' Katya asks whether what exactly will they overcome, refusing to let the point slide. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Katenka, Maxim told me everything.' Adding that someone Maxim told him everything fills in the missing piece for the guest.
- 'And about your psychiatric condition too.' Between the work and the secret, Lyova gives the guest a quick, grounded response.
- 'That you were treated in France, and it is secret.' Since that the guest was treated in France and it is secret, Lyova does not waste words on excuses. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I understand, wait for me for one minute.' Saying that she understands wait for her for one minute is how Katya sets the tone with the guest.
- 'Oh, how quickly time flies today!' Declaring that how quickly time flies today brings the guest's temper right to the surface.
- 'It is already time for me and Victor to leave.' Because it is already time for him and Victor to leave, the guest treats the matter as settled. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'We do not eat after six at all.' Explaining that they do not eat after six at all lays the cards on the table for Katya.
- 'We do not want to be fat.' The guest mentions that they do not want to be fat so Katya sees what is at stake.
