## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, do not worry, I will send her away.' Even though do not worry he will send her away, Senya keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'Her time is paid for until the end of the day!' Barinov declares that her time is paid for until the end of the day as the tension in the room peaks. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Let her work it off and peel potatoes.' Letting her work it off and peel potatoes moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'Does anyone else in the kitchen know about this?' Asking whether anyone else in the kitchen know about this cuts straight through the tension between Barinov and Nagiyev. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'No, Chef, only we know about it.' Around the conversation and order, Senya sets a calm, everyday tone with Barinov.
- 'Maybe this girl here will do? She is too skinny.' Senya asks whether maybe this girl here will do, refusing to let the point slide.
- 'On the other hand, her apartment is clean.' Since on the other hand her apartment is clean, Senya does not waste words on excuses. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'You can tell right away that she is very tidy.' Mentioning that Barinov can tell right away that she is very tidy moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Here is the deal, not a word to anyone at all!' Barinov declares that here is the deal not a word to anyone at all as the tension in the room peaks.
- 'Get out of here quickly and go work!' Declaring that Nagiyev should get out of here quickly and go work brings Barinov's temper right to the surface. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'It is time for us to reset our relationship.' Explaining that it is time for them to reset their relationship lays the cards on the table for Barinov. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Wait one second, here, take this.' Between the work and the table, Nagiyev gives Barinov a quick, grounded response.
