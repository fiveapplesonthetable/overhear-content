## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, well then, can we congratulate you?' Here checking whether they congratulate Barinov shows Senya looking for the real truth.
- 'The two of you are simply total idiots!' Because the two of Fedya is simply total idiots, Barinov does not bother softening the blow for Fedya. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Chef, I do not understand what you are talking about.' Saying that he does not understand what Barinov is talking is how Fedya sets the tone with Barinov. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'I do not even know a word like that.' Once he does not even know a word like, Fedya can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'But we only wanted to do what was best!' Declaring that they only wanted to do what was best brings Senya's temper right to the surface. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Things were not going very well for you.' Senya tells Barinov that things were not going very well for Barinov without dressing it up. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Shut your mouth, first I am going to fire you!' Here shutting Fedya's mouth first he is going to fire Fedya lets Barinov's real feelings break through.
- 'Then I will tie you up and hang you by your feet!' Barinov declares that he will tie Fedya up and hang Fedya by Fedya's feet as the tension in the room peaks. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'All day long I will be tearing something off you!' Because all day long he will be tearing something off Fedya, Barinov does not bother softening the blow for Fedya. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Hello, yes, Dima, are you in the restaurant already?' By asking whether Fedya in the restaurant already, Barinov presses Fedya for a straight answer.
