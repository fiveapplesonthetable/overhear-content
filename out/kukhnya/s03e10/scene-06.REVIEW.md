## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Do not be upset, so it did not work out today.' Max continues warmly toward Katya, so Katya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Maybe not everything is lost yet?' Max answers quietly beside Katya and lands on "poteryano" ("is lost", built on "poteryat") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Hi, Max, I wanted to tell you something.' Katya responds evenly hearing Max, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'Maybe we could go have some coffee tonight?' Katya presses firmly where this conversation is heading next.
- 'Hi, what? Have coffee tonight?' Max briskly voices this casual greeting in front of Katya using "privet" ("hi") — the everyday informal hello used between friends and close coworkers.
- 'Yes, then it is settled between us.' Following up evenly at once, the "-sya" ending on "dogovorilis" ("agreed, deal") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'I do not understand anything at all now.' Lyova steps in sharply facing Katya, making his position completely clear.
- 'I did everything according to your recipe.' Bringing up the recipe beside Katya, Lyova lands on "retseptu" ("recipe, prescription", built on "retsept") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'You did nothing at all, and she is going with you.' Reacting openly beside Max, Lyova pairs the negative word with "ne" ("not"), since Russian naturally doubles its negatives for emphasis.
- 'Honestly, I do not know why myself.' Max continues warmly around Lyova, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'Tell me, why did it turn out like this?' Lyova replies openly during the talk and lands on "skazhi" ("say", built on "skazat") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Because I am completely worthless.' Highlighting how worthless he is on the spot, Lyova adds "sovsem" ("completely, at all") so the statement leaves zero room for doubt.
