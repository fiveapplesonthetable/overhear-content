## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'God, what have you done right now?' Wondering if someone what have Kostya done right now shows Max caught off guard. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'I never thought that everything would end right like this.' Pointing out that he never thought that everything would end right like this gives Kostya a sobering reality check. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'Our life turned out to be too short.' Max explains that their life turned out to be too short, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'I did not have time to understand simple truths.' Realizing that he did not have time to understand simple truths changes how Max handles the situation.
- 'If you love a person, tell them about it.' Noting that if Kostya loves a person tell them about it keeps Max's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Always thank those who teach you something.' Between the conversation and the plan, Max gives Kostya a quick, grounded response.
- 'Love your friends and value friendship.' Max mentions that Kostya should love Kostya's friends and value friendship so Kostya sees what is at stake.
- 'What a pity that I understood all this too late.' Saying that what a pity that he understood all this too late is how Max sets the tone with Kostya. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'But after all, better late than never.' Turning from the work to the shift, Max keeps the exchange moving.
- 'There must be some good option.' Adding that there must be some good option fills in the missing piece for Kostya.
- 'Tell us, Kuzma or Dmitry after all?' With the kitchen and situation hanging in the air, Nastya puts Kostya on the spot. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Alright, let us ask Max right now.' Since Alright let them ask Max right now, Kostya does not waste words on excuses.
