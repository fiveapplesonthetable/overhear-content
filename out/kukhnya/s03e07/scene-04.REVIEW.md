## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'In what sense, and why is that so?' Focusing on the sense on the spot, Nagiyev centers the thought on "smysle" ("sense", from "smysl") without wasting a word.
- 'Because she will stay with me.' Holding firm dryly at once, the "-sya" ending on "ostanetsya" ("will remain") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'Vika, I do not want you to leave with him.' Max weighs in frankly during the talk, so Vika knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'After all, you and I must be together.' Max continues frankly at once and centers the thought on "dolzhny" ("must, should", from "dolzhen") without wasting a word.
- 'You know, I will tell you this right now.' Nagiyev opens up lightly without a pause, before Max can change the subject.
- 'Every person must know his place.' Nagiyev cuts in warmly with Max, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'For example, I am the owner here.' Pointing to the owner in the exchange, Nagiyev brings in "khozyain" ("owner") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'And you simply clear dirty plates.' Discussing the plate before Vika, Nagiyev uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'Dima, please, do not speak like that.' Vika opens up pointedly with a nod, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'You are still very young and foolish.' Nagiyev reacts boldly at once and reaches for the Russian word "yeshchyo" ("more, still") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Alright, go on and get to work quickly.' Nagiyev directly offers this word of agreement beside Max using "ladno" ("alright") — built from the old Russian root for harmony and order, used every day for "okay, fine".
- 'No, I am not going anywhere from here!' Max follows up warmly without any doubt, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
