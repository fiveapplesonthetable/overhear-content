## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, good day, dear Dmitry Vladimirovich.' Over the moment and the phone, Max answers Barinov without a second thought.
- 'Come quickly, our chef is feeling bad here.' Noting that Barinov should come quickly their is feeling bad here keeps Max's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'It seems he had another stroke.' Max explains that it seems he had another stroke, keeping his voice calm and direct. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Vika, wait for me in the car, I will be right back.' Realizing that for him in the car he will be right back changes how Nagiyev handles the situation.
- 'A cockroach again, whose dish is it?' Questioning whether a cockroach again whose dish is it is how Barinov tests Max's story.
- 'It is your dish, respected Chef.' Even though it is Barinov's dish respected Chef, Max keeps a steady face in front of Barinov. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Vitya, they told me that you are feeling bad.' Admitting that they told him that Barinov is feeling bad shows Nagiyev being honest for a change. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Ah yes, my salary could use a little raise.' Saying that his salary could use a little raise is how Barinov sets the tone with Max.
- 'From a joke like that there are big problems, is that clear?' Wondering if from a joke like that there are big problems is that clear shows Nagiyev caught off guard. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'I will definitely find out who tricked me like this.' Adding that he will definitely find out who tricked him like this fills in the missing piece for Barinov.
- 'I will punish those guys very strictly.' Saying that he will punish those guys very strictly is how Nagiyev sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'I give my word, I definitely will not forgive this!' Nagiyev declares that he gives his word he definitely will not forgive this as the tension in the room peaks. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
