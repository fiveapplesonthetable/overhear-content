## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'He simply knows all the laws, he studies at the university.' Turning to the university on the spot, Kostya uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'Well I see, you cannot drive on a red light.' Max opens up frankly beside Kostya, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'Yet in that outfit you can drive around Moscow.' Emphasizing the state in front of Kostya, Max reaches for the Russian word "razyezzhat" ("drive around") right at the heart of the line.
- 'That is it, Nastya, I urgently need to go right now.' Reacting calmly on the spot, Kostya leans on "nado" ("it is necessary, one needs to"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'Of course, you are simply walking away from the conversation, right?' Nastya inquires sharply how Kostya expects that to work in practice.
- 'Nastya, name our son whatever you want, just not Kuzma.' Mentioning his son on the spot, Kostya reaches for the Russian word "khochesh" ("do you want", built from "khotet") right at the center of the line.
- 'And I will name him Kuzma anyway!' Nastya fires back sharply with Kostya and reaches for the Russian word "nazovu" ("will name", built from "nazvat") right at the center of the line.
- 'If it is Kuzma, I renounce Kuzma.' Kostya answers firmly during the talk, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Wait, are you rejecting our son?' Touching on her son on the spot, Nastya centers the thought on "otkazyvayeshsya" ("refuse, reject", from "otkazyvatsya") without wasting a word.
- 'Go away, I do not want to see you anymore!' Nastya replies pointedly toward Kostya, watching Kostya's face to see how the news lands.
- 'Nastya, listen, not from our son!' Kostya fires back quietly with a shrug, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'I am only rejecting the name Kuzma.' Turning to the name around Nastya, Kostya brings in "otkazyvayus" ("refuse", from "otkazyvatsya") as the key Russian word here.
