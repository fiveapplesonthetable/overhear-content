## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Kostya, everything is fine, I already drove it away.' Claiming that everything is fine he already drove it away sounds confident, even if Kostya has doubts.
- 'Max, wait, I will explain everything to you right now.' Pointing out that he will explain everything to Max right now gives Max a sobering reality check. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'I was simply calming Victoria Sergeyevna down.' Kostya explains that he was simply calming Victoria Sergeyevna down, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'Kostya, do not be mad, let the name be Dmitry.' Realizing that do not be mad let the name be Dmitry changes how Vika handles the situation.
- 'Dear Nastya, just do not worry.' Noting that just do not worry keeps Kostya's side of the story simple and clear. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Stop, no, it cannot be like this either!' Telling Kostya to stop no it cannot be like this either shows Max is done holding back. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Let us ask Max instead.' Asking Max instead together moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'Max, Dmitry is better, please tell us.' Saying that Dmitry is better please tell them is how Kostya sets the tone with Max.
- 'Not a fact, Dmitry infuriates me altogether.' With the conversation and car in play, Max keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Let us listen to normal music instead.' Adding that they should listen to normal music instead fills in the missing piece for Kostya.
- 'Alright, let us do it this way, neither for you nor for me.' Max tells Kostya that Alright let them do it this way neither for Kostya nor for him without dressing it up. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Vika, stop, please hear me out!' Saying that Kostya should stop please hear him out is how Max takes charge of the moment. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
