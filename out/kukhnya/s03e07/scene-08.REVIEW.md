## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, a guest out there found a cockroach in his dish.' Addressing a cockroach with Barinov, Max puts the weight on "tarakana" ("cockroach", from "tarakan") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'A cockroach again, whose dish is it?' Barinov inquires sharply about what Max just brought up.
- 'It is your dish again, Chef.' Addressing the dish on the spot, Max brings in "blyudo" ("dish") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Quieter, he was just yelling at a cat.' Turning to a cat with Max, Nagiyev uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'Quickly catch up with my car, mine!' Nagiyev opens up warmly around Max, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Here is a thousand rubles for you right away.' Talking about a thousand before Max, Nagiyev chooses "tysyacha" ("thousand") so Max gets the point right away.
- 'If you catch up, I will give you five thousand.' Nagiyev replies briskly facing Max, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Well that is it, let us go quickly!' Nagiyev continues pointedly on the spot, testing Max's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'In short, my son will be named Dmitry.' Turning to his son beside Nagiyev, Max lands on "koroche" ("long story short") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Give birth to a son yourself and name him Dmitry.' Addressing a son on the spot, Barinov puts the weight on "nazovyosh" ("to call, to name", from "nazvat") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'Chef, what are we going to do with the guest?' Max probes firmly to see how Barinov will answer on the spot.
- 'Right now I will go out to him myself.' Barinov reacts dryly at once and brings in "vyydu" ("I'll get out", from "vyyti") as the key Russian word here.
