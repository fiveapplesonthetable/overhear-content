## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, a guest out there found a cockroach in his dish.' Claiming that a guest out there found a cockroach in his dish sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts.
- 'A cockroach again, whose dish is it?' Asking whether a cockroach again whose dish is it puts Lyova right on the defensive. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'It is your dish again, Chef.' Lyova explains that it is Barinov's dish again, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'Quieter, he was just yelling at a cat.' Realizing that quieter he was just yelling at a cat changes how Nagiyev handles the situation.
- 'Quickly catch up with my car, mine!' With the truth and work at stake, Nagiyev reacts without holding back. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Here is a thousand rubles for you right away.' Even though here is a thousand rubles for Barinov right away, Nagiyev keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'If you catch up, I will give you five thousand.' Admitting that if Barinov catches up he will give Barinov five thousand shows Nagiyev being honest for a change. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Well that is it, let us go quickly!' Declaring that that is it let them go quickly brings Nagiyev's temper right to the surface. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'In short, my son will be named Dmitry.' Once in short his son will be named Dmitry, Lyova can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'Give birth to a son yourself and name him Dmitry.' Here giving birth to a son themselves and name him Dmitry sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'Chef, what are we going to do with the guest?' Wondering what they going to do with the guest are cuts straight through the tension between Lyova and Barinov. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Right now I will go out to him myself.' Since right now he will go out to him himself, Barinov does not waste words on excuses.
