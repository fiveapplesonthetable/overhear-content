## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Not everything, perhaps, but she knows how to cook.' Ilya tells Max that not everything perhaps but she knows how to cook without dressing it up.
- 'We do not need the menu, thank you, Ilya.' Since they do not need the menu thank Ilya Ilya, Max does not waste words on excuses. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'I designed tonight's dinner menu myself.' Over the moment and the dinner, Ilya answers Max without a second thought.
- 'Well, we can share.' Max reminds Ilya that they can share before the moment passes.
- 'I am curious, where is the restroom in this place?' When Ilya asks if he is curious where is the restroom in this place, Max has to answer fast. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Just so I know, just in case.' Explaining that just so he knows just in case lays the cards on the table for Ilya.
- 'Right over there, please.' Around the conversation and dinner, Ilya sets a calm, everyday tone with Max.
- 'He is just a sociable boy.' Being just a sociable boy moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'Are you a fool or what?' Checking whether Max a fool is how Ilya puts Max on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'You do not believe me.' Max explains that Ilya does not believe him, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'Now they even have a line at the restroom.' Realizing that they even have a line at the restroom changes how Ilya handles the situation. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Maybe something funny will finally happen in this place.' Noting that maybe something funny will finally happen in this place keeps Max's side of the story simple and clear. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Keep talking and I will flatten you right here, got it?' Wondering if keep talking and he will flatten Max right here got it shows Ilya caught off guard.
