## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What do you mean, how is it?' Asking what Elena means how is it is how Nagiyev puts Elena on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Well, how is it?' Here asking how it is shows Elena looking for the real truth.
- 'I just made that pate myself.' Mentioning that he just made that pate himself moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Taste it and tell me what you think.' Telling Nagiyev to taste it and tell her what Nagiyev thinks is how Elena lays out the situation for Nagiyev. Notice how she sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'No, you taste it and tell me.' Because Elena tastes it and tell him, Nagiyev treats the matter as settled.
- 'If you like it, then it is a masterpiece.' Explaining that if Nagiyev likes it then it is a masterpiece lays the cards on the table for Nagiyev. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I prepared something else.' Around the dinner and table, Nagiyev sets a calm, everyday tone with Elena.
- 'Hey there. Hello.' With the dinner and room in play, Elena keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Chatting with an invisible friend?' Over the conversation and the table, Nagiyev presses Elena for a straight answer. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'And why is this romance happening at my favorite table, huh?' Here asking why this romance happening at her favorite table huh is shows Elena looking for the real truth. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Dima, forgive me, I probably should have called you first.' Realizing that he probably should have called Elena first changes how Nagiyev handles the situation.
- 'I invited Elena here for dinner tonight.' Around the room and restaurant, Elena sets a calm, everyday tone with Nagiyev.
- 'I figured you would not mind.' Even though he figured Elena would not mind, Nagiyev keeps a steady face in front of Elena. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
