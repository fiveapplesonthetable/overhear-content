## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Ladies and gentlemen, we are starting the final round!' Declaring that ladies and gentlemen they are starting the final round brings Nagiyev's temper right to the surface.
- 'Without any doubts, I give my vote...' Even though without any doubts he gives his vote, German keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'I give my vote, of course, to Oksana.' Even though he gives his vote of course to Oksana, German keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'And I give my vote to Viktor.' Pointing out that he gives his vote to Viktor gives German a sobering reality check.
- 'Dmitry Vladimirovich, now your word is decisive.' Once Barinov's word is decisive, German can finally tell Barinov what he means. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Both dishes turned out simply delicious.' Adding that both dishes turned out simply delicious fills in the missing piece for Barinov.
- 'Vitya, I have known you for a very long time.' Nagiyev mentions that he has known Barinov for a very long time so Barinov sees what is at stake.
- 'But I truly got to know you only today.' Since he truly got to know Barinov only today, Nagiyev does not waste words on excuses. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'I did not think that the choice would be so difficult.' Mentioning that he did not think that the choice would be so difficult moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'I am making the choice not with my mind and not with my stomach.' Adding that he is making the choice not with his mind and not with his stomach fills in the missing piece for Barinov.
- 'I am making this difficult choice with my heart.' Being making this difficult choice with his heart is how Nagiyev lays out the situation for Barinov.
- 'The winner of our show today becomes Oksana!' Because the winner of their show today becomes Oksana, Nagiyev does not bother softening the blow for Barinov. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
