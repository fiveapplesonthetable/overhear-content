- 'Kostya, I got scared myself yesterday too.' An emphatic masculine pronoun accompanies a reflexive past perfective verb of getting frightened.
- 'Tell me, how did all of this happen yesterday?' A singular imperative of telling introduces an indirect question with a neuter past verb.
- 'Do you remember I told you about my dreams?' Denis reminds Kostya of their earlier talk using the accusative plural of dreams.
- 'Well then, that woman was Vika.' The instrumental singular of that woman pairs with the feminine past tense of to be.
- 'Wait, you mean Vika in your dream?' Kostya double-checks that Denis is still talking about a dream using the prepositional singular.
- 'Well, tell me about that in more detail.' Curious, the bartender uses a comparative adverb meaning in greater detail.
- 'She called me into the office to help.' Just as Katya approaches unseen, Denis begins describing the dream as if it were real.
- 'In there she kisses me on the couch.' Using the vivid present tense, Denis describes Dream-Vika kissing him on the office sofa.
