## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dima, and where is our cook Barinov?' Looking at his watch before filming, the producer asks where their star contestant is.
- 'I do not know, he is probably sleeping at home.' Hungover Nagiyev assumes Barinov is out of commission after last night.
- 'And why are you wearing a hat today?' The preposition meaning in with the prepositional singular of hat expresses what someone has on.
- 'Good grief, Dima, what is that on you?' When Nagiyev pulls off the hat, the producer stares at the huge lump on his forehead.
- 'What is this? It is my big brain.' Nagiyev deadpans that the bump is simply his massive brain.
- 'It is too smart, so it bulges out.' He keeps a straight face while the makeup artist hurries over with powder.
- 'Excuse me for being late, I am here already!' Right on cue, Viktor Petrovich strolls briskly into the studio.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, and what happened to you?' Seeing a small bandage on Barinov's cheek, the producer asks what happened.
- 'Nothing serious, I accidentally slipped on a bald head.' Barinov winks at Nagiyev as he jokes about slipping on a bald spot.
- 'Do not worry, I will film today.' He reassures the producer using the compound future of the reflexive verb to be filmed.
- 'Dima, guess which finger I am showing?' Holding his hand inside his pocket, Barinov teases his rival with a singular imperative.
- 'All right, everyone take your places quickly!' Fuming, Nagiyev orders all contestants to their stations so the round can begin.
