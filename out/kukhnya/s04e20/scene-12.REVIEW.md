## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, dear friends, this is the finale of the show!' Nagiyev welcomes the audience to the championship round of the competition.
- 'Our third judge did not come today.' He explains that restaurant critic Kochetkov is in the hospital after his kitchen mishap.
- 'German Land will judge the final round!' The inanimate accusative phrase final round precedes the compound future verb and subject name.
- 'The former head chef of our restaurant Claude Monet.' Nagiyev grins at Viktor Petrovich as his vengeful rival takes a seat on the jury.
- 'Stop, cut, let's take a break for a minute!' As soon as the director calls a makeup break, Barinov marches straight over to German.
- 'What on earth are you doing here right now?' Barinov confronts German bluntly in the second-person singular.
- 'I am very glad to see you again.' German smiles with icy politeness using a short masculine adjective and infinitive.
- 'What, have you gotten tired of being free already?' An impersonal dative past verb asks if German is tired of staying out of prison.
- 'That guy's bruises healed a long time ago.' German shrugs off the beating he gave Markus in the office last week.
- 'And did you forget about our video recording?' Barinov reminds him of the incriminating video from Riga.
- 'Do you really not know at all yet?' German chuckles and asks a mocking question in the polite plural.
- 'I bought that recording from your cooks.' He reveals that Senya and Fedya sold him the tablet with the video for cash.
