## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Katya, I beg you, hear me out.' Adding that he begs Katya hears him out fills in the missing piece for Katya.
- 'This is a recording from our Vika's office.' Even though this is a recording from their Vika office, Denis keeps a steady face in front of Katya. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Look, nothing happened between us.' Over the truth and the doubt, Denis answers Katya without a second thought. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'I am not going to watch anything at all!' By being not going to watch anything at all, Katya raises the stakes in the room at once.
- 'I will not leave until you watch it.' Once he will not leave until Katya watches it, Denis can finally tell Katya what he means.
- 'Denis, are you completely sure about that?' Asking whether Denis completely sure cuts straight through the tension between Katya and Denis. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Yes, just look at this screen.' Between the room and the office, Denis gives Katya a quick, grounded response.
- 'You see, I just walked over to the window.' Since he just walked over to the window, Denis does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Do not come near me, I will jump!' Denis declares that do not come near him he will jump as the tension in the room peaks. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Denis, had you lost your mind back then?' Asking whether had Denis lost Denis's mind back then puts Denis right on the defensive.
- 'I told you, I had a bad dream!' Insisting that he told Katya he had a bad dream shows Denis is not backing down. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'All right, let me pass quietly right now.' Explaining that all right let her pass quietly right now lays the cards on the table for Denis.
