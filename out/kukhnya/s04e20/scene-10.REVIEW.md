## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Katya, I beg you, hear me out.' Here Denis anchors the thought on 'ask for' and finishes on 'to listen to, hear out', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'This is a recording from our Vika's office.' In everyday Russian, 'the recording' flows straight into 'Vika' without extra linking words.
- 'Look, nothing happened between us.' Putting the short word for 'not' right in front of the action word is all Russian needs to make a sentence negative, with no extra helper words like 'do' or 'did'.
- 'I am not going to watch anything at all!' Notice how Russian puts 'at all, even' and 'to look, watch' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
- 'I will not leave until you watch it.' Here Denis anchors the thought on 'will leave' and finishes on 'you will see', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'Denis, are you completely sure about that?' In spoken Russian, the words stay in their normal statement order and Katya's rising voice at the end turns the line into a question.
- 'Yes, just look at this screen.' Denis stands his ground and presses play on the tablet screen.
- 'You see, I just walked over to the window.' On the recording, Katya watches Denis back away from Vika toward the open window.
- 'Do not come near me, I will jump!' The video plays Denis's panicked shout right before he tumbles out the window alone.
- 'Denis, had you lost your mind back then?' Watching the absurd leap on screen, Katya cannot help asking if he went crazy.
- 'I told you, I had a bad dream!' Denis reminds her that he jumped because he thought he was still inside his nightmare.
- 'All right, let me pass quietly right now.' Realizing she accused Denis and Vika falsely, an embarrassed Katya slips past him.
