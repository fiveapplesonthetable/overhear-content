## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why did you sell German that recording?' Wondering why Nagiyev sells German that recording cuts straight through the tension between Barinov and Nagiyev.
- 'Chef, we thought he had already resigned.' Even though they thought he had already resigned, Senya keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'How could we know that he would come back?' Here asking how they know that he would come back could shows Senya looking for the real truth. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'And could you two not make a copy?' By checking whether Nagiyev two not make a copy, Barinov presses Nagiyev for a straight answer.
- 'He gave us good money for not keeping a copy.' Once he gave them good money for not keeping a copy, Senya can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'Put the cabbage back in place and listen!' Insisting that Nagiyev should put the cabbage back in place and listen shows Barinov is not backing down. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Go on to the stage faster, the crowd is waiting!' Here going on to the stage faster the crowd is waiting lets Nagiyev's real feelings break through.
- 'Dima, I am not going to film anymore.' Because he is not going to film anymore, Barinov treats the matter as settled.
- 'That's it, this movie is over for me.' Mentioning that that is it this movie is over for him moves the talk in a new direction. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Good job, go on and get out of here.' With the shift and contract in play, Nagiyev keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Only a huge fine is waiting for you out there.' Because only a huge fine is waiting for Barinov out there, Nagiyev treats the matter as settled. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'You signed a contract before filming, after all!' Over the work and the contract, Nagiyev lets his real feelings show. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
