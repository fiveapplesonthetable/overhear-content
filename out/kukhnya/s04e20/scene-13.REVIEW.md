## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why did you sell German that recording?' Barinov storms into the kitchen and demands an answer using a plural past perfective verb.
- 'Chef, we thought he had already resigned.' Senya explains that since German quit last week, they thought the video was no longer needed.
- 'How could we know that he would come back?' The interrogative adverb literally meaning from where is used idiomatically for how could we know.
- 'And could you two not make a copy?' Barinov asks in exasperation why they did not save a backup file first.
- 'He gave us good money for not keeping a copy.' Senya confesses that German paid extra to watch them delete the file.
- 'Put the cabbage back in place and listen!' As Senya shields himself with a head of cabbage, Nagiyev walks in to summon Barinov.
- 'Go on to the stage faster, the crowd is waiting!' The collective singular noun meaning the people or public takes a singular verb.
- 'Dima, I am not going to film anymore.' Knowing German will never let him win, Barinov takes off his apron.
- 'That's it, this movie is over for me.' A neuter past reflexive verb agrees with the indeclinable neuter noun meaning cinema.
- 'Good job, go on and get out of here.' Nagiyev pretends to applaud Barinov's departure before dropping the bombshell.
- 'Only a huge fine is waiting for you out there.' He reminds Barinov of the penalty clause for quitting before the finale.
- 'You signed a contract before filming, after all!' The preposition meaning before governs the instrumental plural of the noun meaning filming.
