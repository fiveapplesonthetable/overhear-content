- 'There is nothing between him and Vika.' Both pronouns and nouns after the preposition meaning between stand in the instrumental case.
- 'I told you that right from the start.' Barinov reminds his daughter that he defended his young cook earlier.
- 'Denis really is a good guy.' He praises Denis using a masculine nominative singular adjective and noun.
- 'Just spare me your male solidarity.' The preposition meaning without governs the genitive singular of the feminine phrase male solidarity.
- 'All right, sweetheart, what do you think yourself?' Barinov softens his tone and addresses Katya affectionately as little daughter.
- 'What do you think, should I forgive him?' The impersonal verb of worth with a dative pronoun and infinitive asks if something is worth doing.
- 'You know, I am a poor adviser for you right now.' A masculine predicate noun expresses that he is in no position to give relationship advice.
- 'My own life is a complete mess.' An emphatic genitive pronoun highlights that Barinov's own affairs are in chaos.
