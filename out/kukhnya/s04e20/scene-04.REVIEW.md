## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Katya, let me explain everything to you.' Denis pleads with Katya using the imperative particle and a first-person future verb.
- 'Denis, just leave me alone right now!' Katya pushes him away with a singular perfective imperative and genitive pronoun.
- 'Katya, I only dreamed all of that!' He insists that the entire episode in Vika's office happened only in his sleep.
- 'I was just telling Kostya my dream.' The dative singular of Kostya marks the listener, while dream is the accusative direct object.
- 'Let's go to him, he will confirm it for you.' An inclusive plural imperative of motion leads into a third-person singular perfective future verb.
- 'Yes, Kostya, was that really just a dream?' Overhearing them, Ainura steps in with heavy sarcasm because nobody believed her gossip earlier.
- 'I never lie in my life!' Ainura swears that she is telling the absolute truth using a negated present verb.
- 'I saw everything with my own eyes.' The instrumental plural of own eyes expresses seeing something firsthand.
- 'The two of them were there by the window.' Having seen Denis tumble out of Vika's window yesterday, Ainura jumped to the wildest conclusion.
- 'Because of that Denis fell out of the window!' The prefixed perfective past verb meaning fell out takes the genitive preposition.
- 'Katya, wait, that really did not happen!' An impersonal negative past construction requires the genitive pronoun form meaning that.
- 'Do not touch me, get out of my way!' Furious, Katya warns him off with a negated imperfective imperative.
