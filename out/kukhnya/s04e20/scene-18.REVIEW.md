## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, Dima, step into my office.' Barinov calls Nagiyev on the phone and invites him into the chef's office.
- 'Well then, kiddo, have you changed your mind already?' Walking in, Nagiyev assumes Barinov wants to apologize and beg for mercy.
- 'No, I know you are sleeping with Oksana.' Chef Barinov pairs 'know' with 'oksana' directly, since spoken Russian skips English filler words like 'the', 'a', and 'is'.
- 'With what Oksana? I do not understand.' Putting the short word for 'not' right in front of the action word is all Russian needs to make a sentence negative, with no extra helper words like 'do' or 'did'.
- 'With the young finalist of your culinary show.' Barinov specifies the exact contestant who is competing against him in the finale.
- 'Here is a video where you two are in the office.' He points to the recording from Vika's hidden security camera.
- 'So what? Everyone will say that I am a stud!' At first, Nagiyev brags that leaking the video will only boost his macho image.
- 'Only nothing worked out for you in there.' Barinov reminds him of the second half of the clip, where Oksana had to console an embarrassed Nagiyev.
- 'Do not worry, Dima, it happens to anyone.' He repeats Oksana's comforting words with a wry smile.
- 'All right, speak plainly, what do you want?' Mortified, Nagiyev asks what Barinov demands in exchange for the flash drive.
- 'Look, I am putting the flash drive into the microwave.' Instead of making a demand, Barinov tosses the flash drive into the microwave and turns it on.
- 'I do not want a dirty victory, Dima.' Notice how Russian puts 'want' and 'victory, triumph' together in a short, direct phrase where word endings show how the ideas connect.
