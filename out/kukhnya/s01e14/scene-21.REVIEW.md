## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The guests are loudly singing La Marseillaise.' Dmitry points out the French national anthem echoing through the room.
- 'It seems our French guest is tired.' Vika politely softens the awkward situation.
- 'Let us all forgive Jacques.' The word for 'let us' invites the whole table to excuse their guest.
- 'He apparently had too much today.' Dmitry jokes that Jacques indulged plenty already.
- 'Even without our main dish.' He notes that the main course has not even been served yet.
- 'He can no longer judge the food.' Vika realizes Jacques cannot act as the culinary judge.
- 'That is why he is just singing right now.' The word at the start means 'therefore' or 'that is why'.
- 'Well then, dear friends.' Dmitry gathers everyone's attention with a warm address.
- 'Then I will be the main taster.' He volunteers to judge the French dish himself.
- 'I will taste this dish myself.' Adding the word for 'myself' emphasizes his personal role.
- 'I hope you will really like the dish.' Vika nervously hopes the meal passes the test.
- 'Let us see what our chef cooked.' Dmitry prepares to take the first bite.
