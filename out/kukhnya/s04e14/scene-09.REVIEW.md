## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Katya, this is just my half-eaten breakfast.' Denis chimes in bluntly without any doubt, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'Guys, why is she walking after me all day?' Focusing on the boys around Senya, Denis uses "rebyata" ("the boys, the lads") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Tell me, what does she need from me?' Holding firm calmly during the talk, Denis puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Senya hears the focus immediately.
- 'You see, she is a little strange here with us.' Senya continues bluntly with Denis, watching Denis's face to see how the news lands.
- 'She simply fell in love and wants you!' Fedya speaks up pointedly to Senya and brings in "vlyubilas" ("fell in love", from "vlyubitsya") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Previously she ran after all the guys here.' Touching on the boyfriend beside Fedya, Senya leans on "parnyami" ("boyfriend, guy", a form of "paren") to give the remark its edge.
- 'And until she gets her way, she will not back off!' Fedya follows up candidly without any doubt, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Whoa, but she is the chef's own daughter!' Stressing how dear she is at once, Denis reaches for the Russian word "rodnaya" ("dear, darling", built from "rodnoy") right at the center of the line.
- 'So what, on the other hand she is a very pretty girl.' Making clear how pretty she is right here, Senya pivots with "zato" ("on the bright side, to make up for it") to flip a drawback into a plus.
- 'Look, Katya is looking closely at you again!' Fedya cuts in briskly on the spot, testing Senya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'We told you after all, she is like a barracuda!' Emphasizing a barracuda on the spot, Senya reaches for the Russian word "barrakuda" ("barracuda") right at the heart of the line.
- 'She will not let go until she achieves her goal!' Leaning in dryly right here, the "-sya" ending on "dobyotsya" ("to achieve") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
