## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, what are you drinking over there right now?' Fedya sharpens one vivid touch on cue: "seychas" means "now", and it carries the main weight of what Fedya is saying.
- 'I am drinking warm chamomile here for my throat.' Senya jumps in openly to be clear, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Guys, someone stole the blue cheese!' Katya stands firm at once beside Fedya, so Fedya knows she is not just making small talk.
- 'Just this morning I saw it in the refrigerator.' Katya highlights this key piece toward Senya: "kholodilnike" means "fridge" (from "kholodilnik"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'What scoundrels, they have completely lost their conscience!' Senya sends that remark without fuss: adding "sovsem" ("completely, at all") pushes the statement all the way, leaving zero room for doubt.
- 'Do you know what a shortage we have?' Katya checks earnestly what Senya is actually getting at here.
- 'Give me some chamomile, I strained my throat.' Katya sharpens one loaded piece for Fedya: "romashki" means "chamomile", and it carries the main weight of what Katya is saying.
- 'Sorry, Katya, there is almost none left in the cup.' Senya directs this point without fuss: saying "izvini" ("excuse me, sorry") is the informal apology you use with someone close when you want to smooth things over fast.
- 'You had better pay attention to our newcomer.' Senya fires back dryly watching Fedya, so Fedya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Before his arrival there was no such shortage.' Senya picks one clear detail for Katya: "nedostachi" means "shortage", and it carries the main weight of what Senya is saying.
- 'As soon as he appeared, groceries immediately started disappearing!' Senya aims that thought with ease: the "-sya" ending on "poyavilsya" ("appeared") turns the action inward or makes it happen between people naturally.
- 'Thank you, Senya, I will keep an eye on him.' Katya proceeds calmly on the spot, saying out loud what both of them already know.
