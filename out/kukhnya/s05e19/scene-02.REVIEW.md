## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, have you seen my hat today?' Opening up firmly to Max, Katya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Max purely through rising pitch.
- 'If you want to find the hat, follow the clues.' Katya continues openly in their talk, steering the talk right where she wants it.
- 'The first clue will perk you up early in the morning.' Discussing the first clue in this beat, Katya centers the remark on "podskazka" ("clue") without any extra fuss.
- 'Okay, we need diapers, baby powder, and a car seat.' Highlighting the diapers right here, Max uses "podguzniki" ("diapers", from "podguznik") to make the point stick.
- 'Vika, is it not too early to buy a seat?' Max checks candidly where this conversation is heading next.
- 'Alright, do not worry, I will buy everything today.' Max replies sharply in the exchange and centers the thought on "volnuysya" ("worry", from "volnovatsya") without wasting a word.
- 'Kostya, how much money goes into supporting a child?' Addressing the upkeep in this beat, Max puts the question word "skolko" right at the start so Kostya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Honestly speaking, absolutely everything goes to the child.' Kostya replies earnestly around Max, so Max knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'And how do I support a daughter on a cook's salary?' Bringing up the salary beside Kostya, Max centers the thought on "zarplatu" ("salary", from "zarplata") without wasting a word.
- 'Well, that depends on what exactly you cook.' Kostya weighs in openly with Max and brings in "smotrya" ("depending on", from "smotret") as the key Russian word here.
- 'I urgently need to earn more money.' Max proceeds evenly beside Kostya, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Then think about how to get a raise.' Addressing a raise beside Max, Kostya leans on "pribavku" ("pay raise", a form of "pribavka") to give the remark its edge.
