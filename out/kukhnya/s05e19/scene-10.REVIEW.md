## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, little fish, are you full now?' Asking whether little fish are Barinov full now puts Barinov right on the defensive.
- 'Will you stop growling at me now?' Asking whether Barinov stops growling at her now is how Katya puts Barinov on the spot. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'The next clue is with the grandfather of your children.' Explaining that the next clue is with the grandfather of Barinov's children lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
- 'I wonder where people get that kind of money.' By wondering where people get that kind of money, Barinov presses Katya for a straight answer.
- 'In Moscow, kiddo, you have to know how to hustle.' Claiming that in Moscow kiddo Katya has to know how to hustle sounds confident, even if Katya has doubts. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'But that, of course, is not given to everyone.' Pointing out that that of course is not given to everyone gives Katya a sobering reality check. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'They will return the missing item this evening by the botanical garden.' Katya explains that they will return the missing item this evening by the botanical garden, keeping her voice calm and direct.
- 'I wonder who arranged all of this for me.' Wondering who arranged all of this cuts straight through the tension between Katya and Barinov. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Friends, now I am in charge of our kitchen.' Once he is in charge of their kitchen, Max can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'If you have fresh ideas, suggest them.' Even though if Barinov has fresh ideas suggest them, Max keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'We must save a good amount of money today.' Admitting that they must save a good amount of money today shows Max being honest for a change.
- 'Good luck to you today as head chef!' With the conversation and fish at stake, Katya reacts without holding back. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
