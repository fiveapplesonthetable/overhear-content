## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Strong contractions.' Mentioning the contractions before Kostya, the doctor centers the thought on "skhvatki" ("contractions", from "skhvatka") without wasting a word.
- 'I am with you.' Cutting in dryly during the talk, Kostya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'My butterfly.' Kostya answers quietly with the doctor, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'Take more air in.' Underscoring the air during the talk, the doctor leans on "vozdukh" ("air") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Come on, everything is fine.' Holding firm directly in this beat, Kostya starts with "davay" ("come on, let's") to invite the doctor right into the action.
- 'We are almost there already.' The doctor follows up warmly beside Kostya, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Come on, let's breathe.' The doctor fires back vividly in front of Vika and brings in "dyshim" ("to breathe", from "dyshat") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Do you have a conscience?' Focusing on a conscience at once, Vika keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for the doctor purely through rising pitch.
- 'The baby is only five months along.' Vika fires back warmly across the room, testing the doctor's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'What is with you, mommy, huh?' Bringing up the mommy right here, the doctor lands on "mamochka" ("mommy") as the key everyday word here.
- 'We have become completely absent-minded.' Cutting in frankly before the doctor, Vika adds "sovsem" ("completely, at all") so the statement leaves zero room for doubt.
- 'Oh my God.' Vika counters candidly without a pause, watching the doctor's face to see how the news lands.
