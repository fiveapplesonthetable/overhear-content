- 'My butterfly.' Kostya follows up quietly in this moment, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.

- 'We have become completely absent-minded.' Vika frames this answer without fuss: adding "sovsem" ("completely, at all") pushes the statement all the way, leaving zero room for doubt.
- 'Oh my God.' Vika proceeds candidly without any doubt, so Kostya knows she is not just making small talk.
