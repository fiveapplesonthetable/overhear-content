- 'Were they here yesterday?' Vika brings out the contrast between 'they' and 'to be' in a few short words.

- 'No, not very much yet.' Notice how Russian expresses 'no' and 'very' with crisp inflection rather than helper words.
- 'Kostya and I started to suspect.' Nastya anchors the line on 'we', while 'to suspect' completes the thought.
- 'Please make coffee for the tables.' Vika brings out the contrast between 'make' and 'please' in a few short words.
