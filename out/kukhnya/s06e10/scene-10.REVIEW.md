## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I do not understand at all what is happening with her.' Because he does not understand at all what is happening, Denis treats the matter as settled.
- 'In my opinion, everything is clear, you spoiled her with attention.' Noting that in his opinion everything is clear Barinov spoiled her with attention keeps Nikita's side of the story simple and clear. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'And what am I supposed to do about this now?' Around the party and kitchen, Denis wants to know where Barinov really stands. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'You can try to make her a little jealous.' Admitting that Barinov can try to make her a little jealous shows Nikita being honest for a change.
- 'For example, come to the birthday party with another girl.' With the kitchen and party in play, Nikita keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Chef, are you going to the birthday party today?' Asking whether Barinov going to the birthday party today is how Denis puts Barinov on the spot. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Ask one more time and I will chip in for your funeral wreath!' Once Denis should ask one more time and he will chip in for Denis's funeral wreath, Barinov makes sure Denis hears every word.
- 'Viktor, I know what to do with Eleonora.' Since he knows what to do with Eleonora, Nikita does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Agreed, only we will carry her in the trunk of your car.' Since agreed only they will carry her in the trunk of Denis's car, Barinov does not waste words on excuses.
- 'I am serious, we need to urgently bring Nagiyev here.' Once he is serious they need to urgently bring Nagiyev here, Nikita can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'They need to talk, her mental state will improve.' Noting that they need to talk her mental state will improve keeps Nikita's side of the story simple and clear. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Hello, Dima, this is the ex-husband of your ex-wife!' Saying that this is the ex husband of Denis's ex wife is how Barinov takes charge of the moment. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
