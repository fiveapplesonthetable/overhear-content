## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Attention, everyone is chipping in three thousand for the birthday.' Realizing that attention everyone is chipping in three thousand for the birthday changes how Senya handles the situation.
- 'And why so much, who is this gift for?' Wondering if why so much who is this gift shows Denis caught off guard. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'For me of course, because I am having a crisis right now.' Even though for him of course because he is having a crisis right now, Senya keeps a steady face in front of Denis.
- 'Katya, tell me, where did you disappear to again?' Denis asks whether tell him where did Katya disappear to again, refusing to let the point slide. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'We were going to drink coffee, and you are silent.' Saying that they were going to drink coffee and Katya is silent is how Denis sets the tone with Katya. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'You should better go to the cafe with Sveta.' Once Denis should better go to the cafe with Sveta, Katya can finally tell Denis what she means.
- 'At the same time you will drink tasty coffee and kiss again.' Adding that at the same time Denis will drink tasty coffee and kiss again fills in the missing piece for Denis.
- 'Katya, wait, after all that means nothing.' Saying that after all that means nothing is how Denis sets the tone with Katya. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'It happened by accident, just in a friendly way.' With the gift and conversation in play, Denis keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Then keep going to your friend.' Noting that Denis should keep going to Denis's friend keeps Katya's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Katya, there is nothing between Sveta and me.' Pointing out that there is nothing between Sveta and him gives Katya a sobering reality check. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'If you do not believe me, ask her.' Because if Katya does not believe him ask, Denis treats the matter as settled.
- 'For me that will be an excellent gift!' Because for him that will be an excellent gift, Senya does not bother softening the blow for Denis. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
