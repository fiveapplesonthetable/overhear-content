## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Good morning, thank you for the tasty breakfast.' Over the doctor and the shift, Lyova answers the doctor without a second thought. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'How wonderfully you thought of living all together.' Noting that how wonderfully the doctor thought of living all together keeps Eleonora's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Why are you waking me up so early in the morning?' Here wondering why the doctor waking him up so early in the morning is shows Lyova looking for the real truth. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Lyova, get up, I need to rewash your bed.' Pointing out that the doctor should get up she needs to rewash the doctor's bed gives the doctor a sobering reality check. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Eleonora Andreevna, you need a change of scenery.' Saying that Eleonora needs a change of scenery is how the doctor sets the tone with Eleonora.
- 'There is one good hospital, or rather a quiet sanatorium.' Once there is one good hospital or rather a quiet sanatorium, the doctor can finally tell Eleonora what he means.
- 'It is located in the forest right outside the city.' Adding that it is located in the forest right outside the city fills in the missing piece for Eleonora.
- 'There is fresh air, sound sleep, and horses there.' The doctor tells Eleonora that there is fresh air sound sleep and horses there without dressing it up.
- 'Tell me, do they have a large swimming pool there?' Asking whether tell her do they have a large swimming pool there puts the doctor right on the defensive. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Of course, there is a simply wonderful warm pool there.' Mentioning that there is a simply wonderful warm pool there moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'Do you think this will really help me right now?' Here checking whether the doctor thinks this will really help her right now shows Eleonora looking for the real truth.
- 'Without a doubt, you need complete peace and rest.' Because without a doubt Eleonora needs complete peace and rest, the doctor treats the matter as settled. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
