## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Excuse me, may I have literally two words with you?' Questioning whether may he has literally two words with the inspector is how Lyova tests the inspector's story. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Yes, of course, I am listening to you very carefully.' Being listening to Lyova very carefully is how the inspector lays out the situation for Lyova.
- 'Comrade commander, please release those two women over there.' Between the apartment and the truth, Lyova gives the inspector a quick, grounded response.
- 'Tell me, is the second woman also your girlfriend?' Asking whether tell him is the second woman also Lyova's girlfriend puts Lyova right on the defensive. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'No, not at all, that is her aunt.' Saying that not at all that is her aunt is how Lyova sets the tone with the inspector.
- 'Then that will cost five thousand each.' Once that will cost five thousand each, the inspector can finally tell Lyova what he means. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'And do you not feel sorry for all the other people at all?' By asking whether Lyova not feel sorry for all the other people at all, the inspector presses Lyova for a straight answer. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Let them go back home to unemployment?' The inspector asks whether let them go back home to unemployment, refusing to let the point slide.
- 'Why not sorry, I feel very sorry for them.' Since why not sorry he feels very sorry for them, Lyova does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Then hand over the money and take everyone at once!' Handing over the money and take everyone at once is the inspector's blunt reaction in front of Lyova. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Citizens, say a big thank you to this hospitable resident of the capital!' With the truth and apartment at stake, the inspector reacts without holding back.
- 'Everyone is free, walk out calmly back into the courtyard!' The inspector insists that everyone is free walk out calmly back into the courtyard, leaving Lyova no room to argue. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
