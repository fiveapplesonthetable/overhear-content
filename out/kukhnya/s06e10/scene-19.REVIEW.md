## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well what, do you want to fire me now?' Barinov answers frankly beside Eleonora and centers the thought on "khochesh" ("do you want", from "khotet") without wasting a word.
- 'On the contrary, I want to promote you big time today.' Eleonora replies decisively during the talk, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'You were head chef of the restaurant, and now you will be the owner.' Barinov jumps in wryly in their talk, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
- 'Wait, sit down, I did not understand you at all.' Barinov pushes back naturally around Eleonora and uses "podozhdi" ("wait", from "podozhdat") to make the point stick.
- 'What is unclear here, marry me, Vitya!' Underscoring how unclear things are to Barinov, Eleonora uses "neponyatnogo" ("unclear", from "neponyatnyy") to make the point stick.
- 'I understood everything, you still love me.' Eleonora opens up honestly in this moment, watching Barinov's face to see how the news lands.
- 'You even specially called Nagiyev here for my sake.' Eleonora opens up directly on the spot and reaches for the Russian word "spetsialno" ("on purpose") right at the heart of the line.
- 'Vitya, that is the exact kind of husband I need!' Checking on the husband in front of Barinov, Eleonora links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Elya, wait, you do not mean this seriously right now, do you?' Barinov asks skeptically why things suddenly took this turn between them.
- 'Admit to yourself that you love me, and everything will work out.' Eleonora counters readily with Barinov and puts the emphasis on "poluchitsya" ("will come out") in the middle of the talk.
- 'Elya, stop, I have loved another woman for a long time!' Mentioning the woman at once, Barinov leans on "zhenshchinu" ("a woman", a form of "zhenshchina") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Forgive me, I did not want to hurt you.' Barinov reacts lightly around Eleonora, so Eleonora knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'You did not want to, well now I want to!' Eleonora reacts lightly hearing Barinov, leaving no doubt about where she stands.
