## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Dad, what about that one over there?' With the plan and table hanging in the air, Kostya puts the guest on the spot.
- 'No, thin women are often mean.' Pointing out that thin women are often mean gives Kostya a sobering reality check.
- 'Good, so we need a very kind one.' Between the plan and the table, Kostya gives the guest a quick, grounded response.
- 'That woman over there, in my opinion, is very kind.' Realizing that that woman over there in his opinion is very kind changes how Kostya handles the situation.
- 'Listen, is she not a bit too old for me?' Asking whether she not a bit too old for him cuts straight through the tension between the guest and Kostya. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Well, in principle, this is a great option.' Even though in principle this is a great option, Kostya keeps a steady face in front of the guest. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Meet him, please, he liked you very much.' Because the guest should meet him please he liked the guest very much, Kostya treats the matter as settled.
- 'My name is Valentina, very pleased to meet you.' Saying that his name is Valentina very pleased to meet Kostya is how the guest sets the tone with Kostya.
- 'I am helping you for the last time.' Being helping the guest for the last time is how Kostya lays out the situation for the guest. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Alright, I have to go, and you two relax.' Adding that Alright he has to go and the guest two relax fills in the missing piece for the guest.
- 'Konstantin, tell me, what do you do for a living?' Wondering if Konstantin tell him what do Kostya does for a living shows the guest caught off guard. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'It seems I am doing all kinds of nonsense today.' Since it seems he is doing all kinds of nonsense today, Kostya does not waste words on excuses.
