## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Look how beautifully everything is bubbling here!' Describing how beautifully things are to Sveta, the guest leans on "krasivo" ("beautifully", a form of "krasivyy") to give the remark its edge.
- 'I wish you a bright and happy life.' The guest counters sharply with a shrug, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'And love is always bubbling between us!' Highlighting the love in this beat, Sveta lands on "lyubov" ("love") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Tell them, is that not true, my darling Denis?' Checking on the honestly at once, Sveta keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Denis purely through rising pitch.
- 'That is very good, we are glad to hear it.' The guest follows up frankly without any doubt, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'The most important thing in life is to find love.' Noting how important things are during the talk, the guest chooses "glavnoye" ("important", from the root "glavnyy") so Sveta hears the exact meaning.
- 'To find such a love for your whole life.' Underscoring a love in the exchange, the guest lands on "lyubov" ("love") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Tell me, do you agree with me, Denis?' The guest tests skeptically how Denis expects that to work in practice.
- 'Yes, I completely agree with you.' Denis pushes back quietly around the guest and centers the thought on "soglasen" ("agreed", from "soglasnyy") without wasting a word.
- 'It seems to me that I have found such a love.' Touching on a love in front of the guest, the "-sya" ending on "kazhetsya" ("it seems, I think") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'Forgive me, Sveta, but it is not you.' Denis reacts warmly with Sveta, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Then goodbye forever and do not call anymore!' Sveta responds candidly to Denis and puts the emphasis on "navsegda" ("forever") in the middle of the talk.
