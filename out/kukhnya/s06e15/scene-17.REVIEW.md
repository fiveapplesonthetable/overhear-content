## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Look how beautifully everything is bubbling here!' Saying that how beautifully everything is bubbling here is how the guest takes charge of the moment. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'I wish you a bright and happy life.' Wishing Denis a bright and happy life is how the guest lays out the situation for Denis. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'And love is always bubbling between us!' Insisting that Denis should love is always bubbling between them shows Sveta is not backing down.
- 'Tell them, is that not true, my darling Denis?' Sveta asks whether tell them is that not true her darling Denis, refusing to let the point slide. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'That is very good, we are glad to hear it.' Noting that that is very good they are glad to hear it keeps the guest's side of the story simple and clear. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'The most important thing in life is to find love.' Even though the most important thing in life is to find love, the guest keeps a steady face in front of Denis.
- 'To find such a love for your whole life.' With the table and work in play, the guest keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Tell me, do you agree with me, Denis?' With the dessert and table hanging in the air, the guest puts Denis on the spot. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Yes, I completely agree with you.' With the dessert and table in play, Denis keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'It seems to me that I have found such a love.' Over the dessert and the truth, Denis answers the guest without a second thought.
- 'Forgive me, Sveta, but it is not you.' Saying that it is not the guest is how Denis sets the tone with the guest. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Then goodbye forever and do not call anymore!' Once goodbye forever and do not call anymore, Sveta makes sure Denis hears every word.
