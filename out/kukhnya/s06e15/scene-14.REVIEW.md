## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Excuse me, but the dishes are identical.' Mentioning the dish in their talk, Elena keeps Barinov's attention locked on what matters.
- 'Fine, then you will write the resignation letter yourself.' Barinov jumps in pointedly with Elena, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'Chef, I respect you very much, of course.' Elena weighs in readily at once and lands on "konechno" ("of course") as the key everyday word here.
- 'But you peeked, and Lyova did not.' Elena chimes in boldly with a nod, making her position completely clear.
- 'Therefore Lyova won in this bet.' Elena weighs in at once on the spot, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'I saw myself how Senya was cutting.' Elena continues promptly at once and centers the thought on "videla" ("saw", from "videt") without wasting a word.
- 'I did not see anything through there!' Replying plainly around Elena, Barinov pairs the negative word with "ne" ("not"), since Russian naturally doubles its negatives for emphasis.
- 'It is unpleasant when your student cooks better.' Elena pushes back right away beside Barinov, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'By the way, you did not tell Nikita either.' Senya fires back calmly right here and lands on "kstati" ("by the way") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Sveta, we really need to talk seriously.' Holding firm naturally in front of Elena, Senya leans on "nuzhno" ("it is needed, one has to"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'Let us talk later, meet my parents.' Senya fires back earnestly in their talk, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'Mom, Dad, and this is my Denis.' Highlighting the mum in the exchange, Senya brings in "mama" ("mum, mother") to make the meaning unmistakable.
