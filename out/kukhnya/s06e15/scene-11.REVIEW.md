## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Viktor Petrovich, how could you do that?' Lyova voices this warning before Barinov: putting the question word "kak" right at the front of the line lets Barinov hear the point of the question on the very first beat.
- 'You are simply afraid to work without me!' Lyova weighs in bluntly toward Senya, saying out loud what both of them already know.
- 'Lyova, do you have a new suit today?' Barinov grounds one colorful touch at once: "kostyum" means "suit", and it carries the main weight of what Barinov is saying.
- 'Leave me alone, Lyova, I am cooking an important dish.' Barinov opens up bluntly beside Senya, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'You cooked one dish and you are already a chef?' Lyova checks plainly how Barinov expects that to work in practice.
- 'I will easily cook any dish of yours!' Lyova uses this clear detail in reply: "prigotovlyu" means "will prepare" (from "prigotovit"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'I will cook it with my eyes closed no worse!' Lyova cuts in dryly with a smile, making his position completely clear.
- 'Right with your eyes closed and no worse?' Barinov questions briskly whether Senya really thought this through.
- 'Fine, deal, right now we both cook blindfolded!' Barinov chooses one pointed piece at once: "dogovorilis" means "agreed, deal" (from "dogovoritsya"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'If you cook worse, you will work for free!' Barinov stands firm candidly in their talk, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'I bet a thousand rubles on the chef!' Senya pushes back dryly as they talk, making his position completely clear.
- 'Give us the blindfolds, I am ready to start the bet.' Lyova anchors one key choice at once: "povyazki" means "blindfolds" (from "povyazka"), giving the line its exact edge.
