## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Nikita, do you have a minute right now?' Asking whether Barinov has a minute right now cuts straight through the tension between Katya and Barinov. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Of course, for you I even have two.' Pointing out that for Barinov he even has two gives Barinov a sobering reality check.
- 'Listen, here is the thing with us.' Katya explains that here is the thing with them, keeping her voice calm and direct. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'It seems to me that we need to talk.' Realizing that it seems to her that they need to talk changes how Katya handles the situation. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'Easy, darling, let me look at your eye right now.' Noting that easy darling let him look at Barinov's eye right now keeps Denis's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Forgive me, I did not do it on purpose.' Even though he did not do it on purpose, Denis keeps a steady face in front of Barinov. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Nikita, go quickly and call an ambulance here!' Going quickly and call an ambulance here shows Denis is done holding back. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Although I will take her to the doctor myself.' Saying that although he will take her to the doctor himself is how Denis sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'Can you imagine, I raised him myself.' Once can Denis imagines he raised him himself, Barinov can finally tell Denis what he means.
- 'I taught him everything here myself.' Adding that he taught him everything here himself fills in the missing piece for Denis.
- 'I treated him like my own son.' With the room and kitchen in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'And besides that, he handles all the purchasing!' Barinov declares that besides that he handles all the purchasing as the tension in the room peaks. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
