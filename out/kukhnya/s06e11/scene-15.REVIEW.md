## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'She herself asked her brother to play along with her this morning.' Senya opens up decisively across the room, watching Denis's face to see how the news lands.
- 'It looks like she really did fall seriously in love with you.' Talking about the honestly with Denis, Senya centers the remark on "pravda" ("honestly") without any extra fuss.
- 'Since she is staging such foolishness for your sake.' Senya steps in earnestly with Denis, so Denis knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Nikita, tell me quickly, where is your father right now?' Senya wonders quietly how Denis expects that to work in practice.
- 'It seems to me he went to his restaurant recently.' Underscoring the restaurant in this beat, Denis leans on "restoran" ("restaurant") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'Denis, hi, does your face not hurt anymore?' Bringing up the face at once, Sveta uses "u tebya" (literally "by you") where English says "you have" or "your".
- 'Everything is fine, and is your ex not bothering you?' Denis probes frankly about what Sveta just brought up.
- 'He sure is bothering me, he calls me constantly.' Sveta responds directly right here and reaches for the Russian word "nadoyedayet" ("bothers, annoys", built from "nadoyedat") right at the center of the line.
- 'I am afraid to go home, what if he waits in ambush.' Sveta steps in calmly in the exchange and brings in "karaulit" ("to wait in ambush") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'Of course he will, after all a brother always worries!' Denis cuts in briskly across the room, before Sveta can change the subject.
- 'Alright, I will go, what if another brother shows up.' Mentioning the brother around Sveta, Denis reaches for the Russian word "poyavitsya" ("shows up, appears") right at the heart of the line.
- 'And thinks that I am hitting on you again!' Denis speaks pointedly with Sveta, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
