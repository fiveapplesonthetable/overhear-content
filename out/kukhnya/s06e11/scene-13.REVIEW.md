## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Your father yelled at Katya and forbade her to date you.' Nastya anchors the line on 'your', while 'to date' completes the thought.
- 'Katya asked me to stay quiet, but I cannot.' Nastya brings out the contrast between 'Katya' and 'asked for' in a few short words.
- 'Nikita, do not dare raise your voice at me!' The negative particle sits right before the verb meaning 'to dare' to negate the statement.
- 'Listen to me carefully, and you will have everything.' Chef Barinov brings out the contrast between 'listen' and 'me' in a few short words.
- 'You will have big money and high status.' Russians say 'by me' or 'with us' where English says 'I have' or 'we have', without needing a separate word for 'have'.
- 'Listen, dad, why don't you take all your money.' Notice how Russian expresses 'listen' and 'money' with crisp inflection rather than helper words.
- 'And leave so that I never see you again!' Here the Russian phrasing highlights 'and' before landing on 'saw'.
- 'Because I love Katya and will be with her!' The meaning hinges on 'that is why' working together with 'her' in spoken Russian.
- 'Lena, I just want to play a little prank on my brother.' Spoken Russian keeps this brisk, leaning on 'Lena' and finishing on 'to play'.
- 'Do not worry, everything will be fine with this money.' Chef Barinov brings out the contrast between 'not' and 'worry' in a few short words.
- 'Just do not lose someone else's envelope with big money.' In Russian, the idea of 'only' flows naturally into 'money' without extra filler words.
- 'I will hide the envelope and return it soon.' Chef Barinov brings out the contrast between 'I' and 'will hide' in a few short words.
