## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'So, we will see each other this evening.' Saying that they will see each other this evening is how Barinov sets the tone with the guest.
- 'Everything is fine with us, go on out.' Realizing that everything is fine with them go on out changes how the guest handles the situation.
- 'Wait, I will explain everything to you right now.' Adding that he will explain everything to Barinov right now fills in the missing piece for Barinov. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'You are going to explain all of this to Katya right now!' Because the guest is going to explain all of this to Katya right now, Barinov does not bother softening the blow for the guest. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Listen to me, I am a Michelin inspector.' Admitting that to him he is a Michelin inspector shows the guest being honest for a change.
- 'Word of honor, here is my identification card.' Saying that word of honor here is his identification card is how the guest sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'Understand, I like your daughter very much.' Saying that Barinov should understand he likes Barinov's daughter very much is how the guest sets the tone with Barinov. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'And this was simply a random mistake.' Adding that this was simply a random mistake fills in the missing piece for Barinov.
- 'As a man, you should understand me.' The guest tells Barinov that as a man Barinov should understand him without dressing it up. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'I have a proposal for you.' Having a proposal for Barinov is how the guest lays out the situation for Barinov. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'You will not tell Katya about this incident.' Mentioning that Barinov will not tell Katya about this incident moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'And I will write a good report about the restaurant.' Saying that he will write a good report about the restaurant is how the guest sets the tone with Barinov.
