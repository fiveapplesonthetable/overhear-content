## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I wanted to sincerely apologize to you.' Opening up candidly at once, the "-sya" ending on "izvinitsya" ("to apologise") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'For the television and for the words about mom.' Kostya speaks up pointedly facing Nastya, so Nastya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'Well then, shall we forgive him today?' Nastya counters smoothly right here and uses "segodnya" ("today") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'We will forgive him and pour him hot tea.' Touching on the tea at once, the guest uses "goryachego" ("hot", from "goryachiy") to make the point stick.
- 'Let us go buy a new television right now instead.' Kostya jumps in plainly toward the guest, making his position completely clear.
- 'I already borrowed money from friends.' Underscoring the friends in the exchange, Kostya brings in "druzey" ("friends", from "drug") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Nastya, and where are my jeans?' Mentioning his jeans on the spot, Kostya puts the question word "gde" right at the start so Nastya hears the focus immediately.
- 'I put the clothes away on the top shelf.' Nastya replies briskly with a nod, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'After all, this is the wallet he lost yesterday!' Bringing up the wallet around Nastya, Kostya uses "bumazhnik" ("wallet") to carry the main weight of the thought.
- 'Forgive me, I hid it myself.' Nastya responds dryly beside Kostya and puts the weight on "spryatala" ("to hide", from "spryatat") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'I thought that you would become friends faster that way.' Nastya counters firmly across the room, to clear the air between them once and for all.
- 'I want him to leave right now!' Kostya speaks naturally right here and lands on "uyekhal" ("left, went away", built on "uyekhat") to keep the statement grounded.
