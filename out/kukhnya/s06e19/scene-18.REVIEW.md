## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Gulya will kill me for this.' Pointing out that Gulya will kill her for this gives Lyova a sobering reality check. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'But I cannot keep silent.' Claiming that she cannot keep silent sounds confident, even if Lyova has doubts.
- 'She has nobody in Bishkek.' By having nobody in Bishkek, Ainura keeps her side of the story grounded. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'She loves only you alone.' Noting that she loves only Lyova alone keeps Ainura's side of the story simple and clear. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Then why did she lie to me?' Wondering why she lie to him cuts straight through the tension between Lyova and Ainura.
- 'Her migration card expired yesterday.' With the wedding and truth in play, Ainura keeps this short reply brisk and direct. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Now she has to fly away urgently.' Saying that she has to fly away urgently is how Ainura sets the tone with Lyova. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'She will come to Moscow in five years.' Once she will come to Moscow in five years, Ainura can finally tell Lyova what she means.
- 'You have your work and mother here.' Adding that Lyova has Lyova's work and mother here fills in the missing piece for Lyova. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'She decided not to stand in your way.' Ainura tells Lyova that she decided not to stand in Lyova's way without dressing it up. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'In two hours she will fly away.' Since in two hours she will fly away, Ainura does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Lyova, run after her quickly!' By running after her quickly, Ainura raises the stakes in the room at once. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
