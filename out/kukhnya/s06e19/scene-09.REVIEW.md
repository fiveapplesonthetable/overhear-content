## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'You sit quietly in here for now.' Pointing out that Katya sits quietly in here for now gives Katya a sobering reality check. Notice how she sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Nastya, tell me, what should I do?' Wondering if tell her what should she does shows Katya caught off guard.
- 'Wait, have we arrived already?' By checking whether they arrived already, Nastya presses Katya for a straight answer. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Denis offered for me to go away with him.' With the wedding and shift in play, Katya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Where to, your wedding is tomorrow!' Once where to Katya's wedding is tomorrow, Nastya makes sure Katya hears every word.
- 'Thank you very much for reminding me.' Around the wedding and conversation, Katya sets a calm, everyday tone with Nastya.
- 'Is it normal that I am having doubts?' By asking whether it normal that she is having doubts, Katya presses Nastya for a straight answer. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Nikita is a very good person, after all.' Once Nikita is a very good person after all, Nastya can finally tell Katya what she means.
- 'Yes, he is truly very good.' Adding that he is truly very good fills in the missing piece for Nastya.
- 'But I cheated on him with Denis.' Over the car and the wedding, Katya answers Nastya without a second thought.
- 'On that day he proposed to me.' Around the car and wedding, Katya sets a calm, everyday tone with Nastya.
- 'Everything is fine, let us go now.' Mentioning that everything is fine let them go now moves the talk in a new direction.
