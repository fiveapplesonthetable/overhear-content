## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Denis, how did you get in here?' Here asking how Denis gets in here shows Katya looking for the real truth.
- 'I wanted to talk with you.' Explaining that he wanted to talk with Katya lays the cards on the table for Katya. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Before the wedding you must not see me.' Realizing that before the wedding Denis must not see changes how Katya handles the situation. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'I am allowed, I am not the groom.' Pointing out that he is allowed he is not the groom gives Katya a sobering reality check.
- 'I came to ask your forgiveness.' Even though he came to ask Katya's forgiveness, Denis keeps a steady face in front of Katya. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'I am not angry with you.' Explaining that she is not angry with Denis lays the cards on the table for Denis. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'All of that is already in the past.' Saying that all of that is already in the past is how Katya sets the tone with Denis.
- 'Let us leave here together today.' Realizing that they should leave here together today changes how Denis handles the situation. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'We will start our life over.' Adding that they will start their life fills in the missing piece for Katya.
- 'And where will we go?' By wondering where they go will, Katya presses Denis for a straight answer.
- 'Anywhere at all, choose for yourself.' Over the wedding and the room, Denis answers Katya without a second thought.
- 'Believe me, right now it is not too late.' Noting that Katya should believe him right now it is not too late keeps Denis's side of the story simple and clear.
