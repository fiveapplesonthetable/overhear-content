- 'I steal from the rich and give to the poor.' Admitting that he steals from the rich and give to the poor shows Senya being honest for a change.

- 'Alright, and where is the lamb fillet?' Wondering if Alright and where is the lamb fillet shows Lyova caught off guard.
- 'Senya, have you not seen it?' Here checking whether Fedya not seen it shows Lyova looking for the real truth. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.

- 'And why are you so nervous?' Asking why Fedya so nervous is is how Lyova puts Fedya on the spot. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.

- 'Why do you suspect me?' By wondering why Fedya suspect him, Senya presses Fedya for a straight answer.
