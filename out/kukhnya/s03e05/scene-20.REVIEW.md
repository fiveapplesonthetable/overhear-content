## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Nagiyev wants to give Vika this car as a gift?' Pointing to the car in the exchange, Max keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Nagiyev purely through rising pitch.
- 'And what if he gave me one like that?' Nagiyev checks evenly if there is more going on behind the scenes.
- 'Well let him just try.' Max follows up decisively to Nagiyev and reaches for the Russian word "poprobuyet" ("to try, to taste", built from "poprobovat") right at the center of the line.
- 'What, do you want to steal it right now?' Turning directly in the exchange, Nagiyev puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Max hears the focus immediately.
- 'I will simply move it quietly somewhere.' Max chimes in quietly in their talk, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Let him run around and look for it now.' Max chimes in warmly beside Barinov and reaches for the Russian word "pobegayet" ("runs around", built from "pobegat") right at the center of the line.
- 'Hello, Katya, I accept your terms.' Barinov weighs in warmly watching Max, making his position completely clear.
- 'Please, come back to us quickly.' Barinov plainly drops this courtesy word without hesitation using "pozhaluysta" ("please") — the all-purpose Russian politeness word that covers both "please" and "you're welcome".
- 'Every person has another side.' Underscoring the side in the exchange, Max chooses "obratnaya" ("reverse, other", from the root "obratnyy") so Barinov hears the exact meaning.
- 'A side that is hidden from others' Insisting on how hidden things are around Barinov, Max links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'That side can surprise someone greatly.' Max speaks pointedly across the room, so Barinov knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'And it can make someone else very happy.' Max opens up evenly in the exchange and lands on "obradovat" ("make happy") as the key everyday word here.
- 'At the right moment he finds the strength to yield.' Talking about the right moment in front of Barinov, Max puts the emphasis on "nuzhnyy" ("necessary, needed") in the middle of the talk.
