## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Katya, what was that just now?' Barinov presses curiously if there is more going on behind the scenes.
- 'Do you have nothing better to do?' Replying boldly at once, Barinov puts the question word "chto" right at the start so Katya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Why are you stepping ahead of your father?' Jumping in calmly in the exchange, Barinov puts the question word "kuda" right at the start so Katya hears the focus immediately.
- 'You yourself refused right away.' Katya follows up calmly with a nod, steering the talk right where she wants it.
- 'You could have said thank you too.' Discussing the thank in this beat, Katya puts the emphasis on "spasibo" ("thank you") in the middle of the talk.
- 'I saved you from tomorrow.' Katya weighs in bluntly without holding back, so Kostya knows she is not just making small talk.
- 'Let us be together, I am ready to change.' Kostya jumps in quietly without holding back, before Katya can change the subject.
- 'And she kicked me out right away.' Kostya proceeds briskly in front of Barinov and reaches for the Russian word "vygnala" ("kicked out", built from "vygnat") right at the center of the line.
- 'Come on, is it the first time?' Describing how first things are beside Kostya, Barinov links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'And do you know why she kicked me out?' Kostya presses point-blank while looking straight at Barinov here.
- 'Because she was waiting for Nagiyev.' Kostya proceeds directly before Barinov and chooses "potomu" ("because") so Barinov gets the point right away.
- 'Which Nagiyev?' Holding firm naturally to Kostya, Barinov keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Kostya purely through rising pitch.
