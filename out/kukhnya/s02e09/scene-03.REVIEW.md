## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We are just friends.' Noting that they are just friends keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Nothing at all.' Over the moment and the secret, Max answers Barinov without a second thought.
- 'Well, friendship is a wonderful thing.' Admitting that friendship is a wonderful thing shows Barinov being honest for a change. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Listen, the Chef is being way too kind today.' Saying that the Chef is being way too kind today is how Max sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'That does not bode well.' Once that does not bode well, Barinov can finally tell Max what he means.
- 'Maybe Spartak won?' Wondering if maybe Spartak won shows Max caught off guard. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'No, it looks like they lost.' Barinov tells Max that it looks like they lost without dressing it up.
- 'The man simply got wasted first thing in the morning, that is all.' Since the man simply got wasted first thing in the morning that is all, Max does not waste words on excuses.
- 'No, he seemed completely sober.' With the conversation and kitchen in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Something else is going on here.' Max reminds Barinov that something else is going on here before the moment passes.
- 'How do you even know about that?' By wondering how Max even know, Barinov presses Max for a straight answer.
- 'Well, I have an acquaintance who told me all about it.' Having an acquaintance who told him all about it is how Max lays out the situation for Barinov. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Anyway, look here.' Around the conversation and truth, Barinov sets a calm, everyday tone with Max.
