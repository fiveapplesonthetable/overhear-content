## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Under the table, as a bribe.' Around the point and conversation, Max sets a calm, everyday tone with Sasha.
- 'I got it, yes.' Even though she got it yes, Sasha keeps a steady face in front of Max.
- 'If there are any problems, call me.' Admitting that if there are any problems call him shows Max being honest for a change.
- 'Everything adds up.' Saying that everything adds up is how Sasha sets the tone with Max.
- 'And you do not know?' Questioning whether Sasha does not know is how Max tests Sasha's story. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'I will take a look right now.' Adding that she will take a look right now fills in the missing piece for Max.
- 'What, are you afraid of me?' Here asking what Sasha afraid of him is shows Max looking for the real truth.
- 'Well then, what is the problem?' By wondering what the problem is, Sasha presses Max for a straight answer. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'I am not afraid.' Saying that he is not afraid is how Max sets the tone with Sasha.
- 'Were you not looking for me?' Checking whether Max not looking is how Sasha puts Max on the spot.
- 'And I was just looking for you too.' Because he was just looking for Sasha, Max treats the matter as settled. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'I cooked such an amazing dorado.' Over the plan and the kitchen, Sasha answers Max without a second thought.
- 'Come on, I will show you.' Telling Sasha to come on he will show Sasha moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
