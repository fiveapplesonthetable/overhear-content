## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'At least give me back my phone!' With the conversation and phone at stake, Max reacts without holding back. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'As long as you keep hitting on me.' Even though as long as Kristina keeps hitting, Nastya keeps a steady face in front of Kristina.
- 'Maksim, I always get what I want.' Admitting that she always gets what she wants shows Kristina being honest for a change.
- 'Especially in my own restaurant.' Between the moment and the room, Max gives Kristina a quick, grounded response.
- 'But you do not own the people.' Once Kristina does not own the people, Nastya can finally tell Kristina what she means.
- 'You can even fire me if you want.' Adding that Max can even fire her if Max wants fills in the missing piece for Max.
- 'Why fire you?' Around the room and shift, Max wants to know where Kristina really stands. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Well, or fire your friend Kostya.' Explaining that Kristina should fire Kristina's friend Kostya lays the cards on the table for Kristina.
- 'And here is why.' Over the conversation and the room, Kristina answers Max without a second thought.
- 'I am waiting for you after work.' Saying that he is waiting for Kristina after work is how Max sets the tone with Kristina. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Here is the thing.' Because here is the thing, Nastya treats the matter as settled.
- 'Could you possibly give me yours?' By asking whether Max possibly give, Kristina presses Max for a straight answer. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Not a chance.' With the conversation and phone in play, Max keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
