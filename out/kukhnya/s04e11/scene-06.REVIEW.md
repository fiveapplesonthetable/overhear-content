## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Irochka, may I have you for a second?' Nagiyev asks whether irochka may he has Nastya for a second, refusing to let the point slide. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Why do you not correct me?' Asking why Nastya not correct him is how Nagiyev puts Nastya on the spot.
- 'You are Nastya, after all!' Insisting that Nastya is Nastya after all shows Nagiyev is not backing down. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'I have already gotten tired of correcting you.' Saying that she has already gotten tired of correcting Nagiyev is how Nastya sets the tone with Nagiyev. Notice how she sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'But I am pleased that you remembered.' Once she is pleased that Nagiyev remembered, Nastya can finally tell Nagiyev what she means.
- 'Do not get smart, I read it on your badge.' Since do not get smart he reads it on Nastya's badge, Nagiyev does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Call our sommelier over to us.' Even though Nastya should call their sommelier over to them, Nagiyev keeps a steady face in front of Nastya.
- 'It is time for us to drink some good wine.' Saying that it is time for them to drink some good wine is how Nagiyev sets the tone with Nastya.
- 'Our sommelier is not here today.' Because their sommelier is not here today, Nastya treats the matter as settled.
- 'What do you mean not here, where is he?' Asking what Nastya means not here where is he is how Nagiyev puts Nastya on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'He did not come in to work again.' Nastya mentions that he did not come in to work again so Nagiyev sees what is at stake.
- 'Then bring us the best bottle yourself.' Bringing them the best bottle themselves is how Nagiyev lays out the situation for Nastya.
- 'An excellent choice, I will bring it right away!' Nastya insists that an excellent choice she will bring it right away, leaving Nagiyev no room to argue. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
