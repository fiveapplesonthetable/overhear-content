## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well done, simply a great job!' Hearing the crash of glass, Nagiyev applauds sarcastically.
- 'You broke it yourself, you will pay for it yourself.' Repeating 'yourself' links the mistake directly to the bill.
- 'Got it, I will pay for everything.' In spoken Russian, the past tense 'understood' is the brisk way to say 'got it'.
- 'And a fine on top of that too!' Nagiyev adds a penalty on top of the bottle's price.
- 'Kostya, have you completely lost your mind?' Literally 'stepped down from your mind', this is the most common idiom for going crazy.
- 'Let's just tear up our money then!' Nastya despairs over the tight family budget.
- 'We have so much money, after all!' Ironic 'after all' highlights how tight money actually is with a toddler at home.
- 'Quiet, Nastya, just do not shout.' Kostya glances nervously around the dining room.
- 'Hello, we are stuck in the elevator here.' Meanwhile, Vika speaks through the elevator crack to a neighbor on the stairs.
- 'Press the button and call the technician.' The neighbor gives the obvious advice first.
- 'We already pressed it, nobody answers.' Imperfective past 'we were pressing' shows repeated attempts.
- 'Please call our friend for us.' Since they have no cell signal inside the shaft, Vika gives the neighbor Denis's number.
- 'Give me the number, just make it quick.' The neighbor agrees to dial the number on his way out.
