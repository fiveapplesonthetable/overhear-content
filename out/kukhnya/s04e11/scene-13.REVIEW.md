## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'It seems I have found us a new sommelier.' Nastya weighs in vividly at once and leans on "kazhetsya" ("it seems, I think", a form of "kazatsya") to give the remark its edge.
- 'He does not drink and does not smoke.' Nastya replies boldly with Nagiyev and lands on "kurit" ("to smoke") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Handsome and a very good family man.' Nastya replies pointedly facing Nagiyev, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'It is our bartender Kostya.' Focusing on her bartender during the talk, Nastya lands on "barmen" ("bartender") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Are you pitching your own husband to me?' Looking over calmly at once, Nagiyev keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Nastya purely through rising pitch.
- 'He smashed three bottles today!' Nagiyev continues lightly in this moment, so Nastya knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'But at his own expense, after all!' Discussing the counting right here, Nastya leans on "schyot" ("counting, account") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'You are a kind and generous person, after all.' Highlighting how good Nagiyev is with Nagiyev, Nastya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'And the best TV host in the whole country!' Nastya answers frankly during the talk, before Nagiyev can change the subject.
- 'Fine, be ready by evening.' Nagiyev briskly directs this word of agreement right on cue using "ladno" ("alright") — built from the old Russian root for harmony and order, used every day for "okay, fine".
- 'I will set up a wine tasting for you.' Nagiyev jumps in plainly with a smile, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Nastya, I do not know anything about wines!' Kostya pushes back openly on the spot, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'The main thing is to say 'excellent choice' Making clear how important things are during the talk, Nastya puts the emphasis on "prekrasnyy" ("beautiful") in the middle of the talk.
- 'Your wife and child are sure that you will succeed.' Nastya jumps in pointedly hearing Kostya, refusing to let the moment slip past.
