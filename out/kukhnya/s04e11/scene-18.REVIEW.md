## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, let's begin our exam.' Nagiyev pours the first glass while hiding the bottle label.
- 'Why are you gulping wine like water?' Kostya takes a huge bartender gulp instead of a delicate sommelier sip.
- 'Name the country and the year for me.' Nagiyev demands the exact provenance of the wine.
- 'This is France, the year two thousand eight.' Listening to Denis's French tune and eight loud chords, Kostya answers confidently.
- 'Well, suppose so, and what region?' Then Nagiyev asks a question they forgot to rehearse: the region!
- 'Wait, I will tell you right now.' Kostya stalls while Denis switches to Paul McCartney's song 'Bordeaux'.
- 'Hey, musician, stop playing over there!' Annoyed by the loud piano, Nagiyev orders Denis to stop playing.
- 'Oh no, he is not bothering me at all.' Desperate for the musical clue, Kostya tries to keep Denis playing.
- 'He is bothering me, silence in the room!' Here Nagiyev anchors the thought on 'bothers' and finishes on 'hall', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'France, two thousand eight, Bordeaux region.' Remembering the song title just in time, Kostya nails the first bottle.
- 'Fine, then try the second bottle.' Without Denis's piano, Kostya has to taste the second bottle on his own.
- 'Italy, two thousand seven, Tuscany!' Spotting the label reflected in Nagiyev's shiny sunglasses, Kostya reads every detail aloud!
- 'Could you really be a genuine sommelier?' Stunned and impressed, Nagiyev hires Kostya on the spot.
