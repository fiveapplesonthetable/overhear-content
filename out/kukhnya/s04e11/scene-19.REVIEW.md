## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, you deceived me again!' Returning for his forgotten phone, Lyova catches Barinov drilling the hole back open.
- 'Live however you want now.' Heartbroken, Lyova shaves off his moustache in protest.
- 'I do not care about you anymore.' With the preposition 'na', this idiom means 'I don't care about you'.
- 'Careful, chef, step back from the wall!' Spotting a fuse burning inside the hole, Lyova pulls Barinov back just before German's firecracker blows a crater in the wall.
- 'If I say yes, will you forgive me?' Nursing a bruised ego after the elevator ordeal, Max talks softly with Vika.
- 'I did solve the problem after all.' Max points out that the mechanic did let them out of the elevator.
- 'Forgive me, this was all because of me.' Vika apologizes warmly using 'because of me' in the genitive.
- 'We still need to change the wheels.' Stepping into the courtyard, they see Vika's car sitting on bricks with all four wheels stolen!
- 'And there is no story sadder in the world.' Max quotes the famous Russian translation of Romeo and Juliet's closing line.
- 'Than the story of the stolen wheels.' He adapts Shakespeare's line to mourn Vika's missing tires.
- 'Good afternoon, how can I help you?' Next day at Claude Monet, Vika greets a guest at the podium and freezes.
- 'Hi, I decided to celebrate my wedding here!' It is Max's ex-girlfriend Sasha, beaming as she announces her upcoming wedding!
- 'That is great, isn't it?' Sasha's cheerful question sets up the next episode's drama.
