## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What is this terrible smell in here?' By asking what this terrible smell in here is, German presses Max for a straight answer. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'We need to air out the room urgently.' Needing to air out the room urgently moves the conversation forward without extra drama. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'What smells so strange in here?' With the work and apartment hanging in the air, German puts Max on the spot.
- 'Senya, are you deflecting suspicion from yourself?' Here asking whether Max deflecting suspicion from themselves shows German looking for the real truth. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Oh, it seems the elevator has stopped.' Max tells German that it seems the elevator has stopped without dressing it up.
- 'Vika, do not panic, we are stuck.' Since do not panic they are stuck, Max does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Well, do something at least!' Because do something at least, Vika does not bother softening the blow for German. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'And what can I do in here?' Here wondering what he does in here can shows Max looking for the real truth. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'I do not know, you are the man here.' Because she does not know German is the man here, Vika treats the matter as settled.
- 'Fine, I will think of something right now.' Explaining that fine he will think of something right now lays the cards on the table for German.
- 'Hey, is anyone out there?' Asking whether anyone out there is how Max puts German on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Help us, please!' Helping them please shows Max is done holding back.
