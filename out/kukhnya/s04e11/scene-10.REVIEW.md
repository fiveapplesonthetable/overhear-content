## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, what are you doing here?' Lyova finds Barinov crouching by a hole in the wall between the two restaurants.
- 'A fine aroma, isn't it?' Barinov grins as he fans stinky cheese toward the neighboring dining room.
- 'But the restaurant dining room is on the other side!' Lyova is horrified that guests in Claude Monet will smell it.
- 'What are you doing all this for?' The question word 'zachem' asks about purpose or goal.
- 'Relax, Lyova, everything is great.' Here Chef Barinov anchors the thought on 'relax' and finishes on 'perfectly', keeping the spoken rhythm brisk and natural.
- 'It is a real French restaurant, after all.' Adjectives from country names are written with a lowercase letter in Russian.
- 'I am creating the atmosphere of France for them.' Barinov jokes that pungent cheese is authentic French ambiance.
- 'Choose an ironic tone in conversation.' Lyova recalls his audiobook's advice to use irony to show an opponent his plan is childish.
- 'Chef, you are behaving like a child.' Lyova tries to shame Barinov into stopping.
- 'Well, and what will you do next?' The adverb 'further/next' asks where this escalation leads.
- 'I do not know myself yet.' Barinov shrugs, out of ideas for his next prank.
- 'Maybe you will release cockroaches in there too?' In spoken Russian, the words stay in their normal statement order and Lyova's rising voice at the end turns the line into a question.
- 'Lyova, you got so much smarter with that moustache!' Instead of feeling ashamed, Barinov thinks releasing cockroaches is a stroke of genius!
- 'That is simply a brilliant idea!' Lyova's psychology book fails for the second time in a row.
