## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Nikita, let's do it tomorrow.' Explaining that they should do it tomorrow lays the cards on the table for Katya. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Mom, you look gorgeous.' Admitting that Mom Eleonora looks gorgeous shows Katya being honest for a change.
- 'Alright, I am joking, I am joking.' Saying that Alright she is joking she is joking is how Eleonora sets the tone with Katya.
- 'I told you that I had nothing to do with it.' Once she told Katya that she had nothing to do with it, Eleonora can finally tell Katya what she means.
- 'Forgive me, I was wrong.' Adding that she was wrong fills in the missing piece for Eleonora.
- 'And where is my betrothed?' Asking where her betrothed is is how Eleonora puts Katya on the spot. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'We might be late to the registry office like this.' Since they might be late to the registry office like this, Eleonora does not waste words on excuses.
- 'I already thought that you ran away from me.' Mentioning that she already thought that Katya ran away moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'I wish, just kidding.' Noting that he wishes just kidding keeps Nagiyev's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Where would I run from such beauty of yours?' Wondering where he runs from such beauty would is how Nagiyev puts Eleonora on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Let's go, or we will be late to the registry office.' Proposing that they go or they will be late to the registry office is how Eleonora lays out the situation for Katya.
- 'I am running, darling.' Nagiyev explains that he is running, keeping his voice calm and direct.
