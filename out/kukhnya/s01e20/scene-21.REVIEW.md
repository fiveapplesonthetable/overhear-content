## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'People often sleep.' A metaphor for not paying attention.
- 'They do not want to think.' It is easier to avoid hard thoughts.
- 'Everyone loves to dream.' Dreaming is a safe place.
- 'We see dreams.' We live in a world of our own imagination.
- 'Such is our life.' This is how many people exist.
- 'Nobody sees the truth.' The reality is hidden from us.
- 'We.' A powerful single word to summarize humanity.
- 'Many close their eyes.' We ignore the obvious signs around us.
- 'They are afraid.' Fear keeps us from acting.
- 'Just wait for a miracle.' Waiting for something to happen automatically.
- 'Nobody knows anything.' Ignorance is bliss in a complicated world.
- 'And everyone is silent.' We do not speak up when we should.
