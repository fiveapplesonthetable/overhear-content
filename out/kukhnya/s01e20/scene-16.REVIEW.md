## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Excuse me. Is this normal?' Russian uses "normalno", 'normal', all the time for 'okay' or 'fine'.
- 'Well, it happens.' The word "byvaet", 'it happens', is what you say in Russian to shrug something off.
- 'Here. Hang in there, old man.' Russian hands things over with "na", a little word like 'here, take it', and "derzhis" literally means 'hold on'.
- 'You need it more.' The word "nuzhnee" means 'more needed', so "tebe nuzhnee" is literally 'to you it's more needed'.
