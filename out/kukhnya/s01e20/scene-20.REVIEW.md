## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hi. Good morning.' Russian clips zdravstvuyte down to zdraste in quick speech, and dobroe utro means 'good morning'.
- 'Max, I can't reach him at home.' The verb dozvonitsya means 'to get through' on the phone: do-, 'all the way', plus zvonit, 'to ring'.
- 'I don't know.' Russian drops ya, 'I', here: just ne znayu, 'don't know'.
- 'I guess his keys are hanging here, so he's home.' Russian says klyuchi visyat, 'the keys hang', and znachit, literally 'it means', for 'so'.
- 'Come in.' The polite prokhodite, from prokhodit, 'to pass through', is how you'd say 'come in' to someone you call vy.
- 'Thank you.' The word spasibo comes from spasi bog, 'God save'.
