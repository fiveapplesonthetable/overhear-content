## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We very often exist in illusions.' We build a perfect fake world.
- 'These are our fantasies.' We made them ourselves.
- 'We forget who we are.' We lose our true identity.
- 'Who are we really?' A deep philosophical question.
- 'Everyone plays roles.' Like actors on a stage.
- 'These are foreign masks.' We pretend to be someone else.
- 'But the moment of awakening comes.' Eventually, reality hits.
- 'Life tears these masks off us.' The truth is revealed abruptly.
- 'They were endless.' We have so many layers of deception.
- 'Light lays bare the reality.' There is nowhere left to hide.
- 'We see the truth.' The painful truth is finally visible.
- 'We were not prepared for it.' It always comes as a shock.
