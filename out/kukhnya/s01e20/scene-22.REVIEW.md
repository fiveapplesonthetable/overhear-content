## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We very often just exist in illusions.' Russian sushchestvuem means 'we exist', a heavier word than zhivyom, 'we live'.
- 'We forget who we really are.' Russian says na samom dele, literally 'in the very deed', for 'really'.
- 'We play roles.' Russian igrat means 'to play' a game, a part or music, just as in English.
- 'The roles of people we don't know.' The word neizvestnykh means 'unknown': ne plus izvestnyy, 'known'.
- 'But a moment of awakening comes.' Russian probuzhdenie, 'awakening', comes from probudit, 'to wake someone up'.
- 'It tears the endless masks off us.' Russian beskonechnye is bez, 'without', plus konets, 'end'.
- 'And lays reality bare.' The verb ogolyaet comes from golyy, 'naked'.
- 'We weren't ready for it at all.' Russian says gotovy k, literally 'ready towards', and sovsem ne means 'not at all'.
