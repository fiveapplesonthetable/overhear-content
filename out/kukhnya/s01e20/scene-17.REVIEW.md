## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Bye.' The word poka is the casual goodbye; literally it means 'for now'.
- 'Oh, you're leaving already?' Russian uses ukhodish, 'you're going away', for leaving a place, and uzhe means 'already'.
- 'Yeah. It's kind of dull here.' The word nevesyolo is ne, 'not', plus vesyolo, 'cheerful', and aga is a casual 'yeah'.
- 'Wait. I...' The word podozhdi is how you'd say 'wait' to a friend; to a stranger you'd say podozhdite.
- 'What?' In Russian chto means 'what', and in speech it sounds like 'shto'.
