## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Kostya, we have to talk.' Nastya insists on having the delayed conversation.
- 'Can it wait?' Kostya immediately tries to postpone it again.
- 'No, it is important.' Nastya refuses to delay any further.
- 'Nastya, I have missed you so much.' Kostya tries to distract her with affection.
- 'Please, listen to me.' Nastya begs him to focus on her words.
- 'I am listening to you.' Kostya reluctantly agrees to pay attention.
- 'Our relationship is moving fast.' Nastya states her observation about their pace.
- 'I think this is great.' Kostya offers a positive, superficial response.
- 'But we need to discuss our future.' Nastya introduces the heavy topic she has been building up to.
- 'We have a lot of time.' Kostya tries to brush off the urgency.
- 'No, the time is now.' Nastya firmly rejects his attempt to stall.
- 'Okay, I am ready to talk.' Kostya finally surrenders and agrees to the serious talk.
