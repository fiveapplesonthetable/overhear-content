## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, why did rare truffles end up in the dumpster?' "Okazalis", ended up, is from "okazatsya", 'to turn out to be'. "Redkie", rare, is the plural to match "tryufeli".
- 'Chef, forgive me, please!' "Izvinite", forgive me, is the "vy" form, the one you use with a boss. With a friend Max would say "izvini".
- 'I have never seen truffles before.' "Nikogda ne videl", 'never not saw', is the usual double negative. After that "ne", "tryufeli" turns into "tryufeley".
- 'How could you throw away such a treasure?' "Kak ty mog", how could you, uses "mog", the past of "moch". "Vybrosit", to throw out, is "brosit", throw, with vy- for 'out'.
- 'In Voronezh, truffles are only candies!' "V Voronezhe", in Voronezh, adds -e to the city because it's where. "Konfety" are sweets in general, and chocolate truffles are one kind.
- 'Candies? This is the most expensive mushroom!' "Samyy dorogoy", the most expensive, is "samyy" plus the adjective. Barinov throws "konfety" back at Max as a question.
- 'I thought that they had spoiled.' "Podumal", I thought, is the finished partner of "dumat", so it means one thought, now over. "Isportilis", went off, is the past of "isportitsya", the verb for food going bad.
- 'Good thing the homeless man returned them to us.' "Bezdomnyy", homeless, is "bez", without, plus "dom", home. "Khorosho, chto", 'good that', means 'luckily'.
- 'Alright, I am very kind today.' "Ladno", alright, is the reluctant 'fine, then'. "Dobryy", kind, is the "dobryy" in "dobryy den", good day.
- 'We successfully passed the difficult inspection.' "Proshli", passed, is the plural past of "proyti", 'to go through'. "Slozhnuyu", difficult, ends in -uyu to match "proverku".
- 'But you will pay the bill for his dinner.' Barinov puts "ty" at the very end, so the stress lands on 'you'. "Oplatit schyot za" is 'pay the bill for'.
- 'Twenty-two thousand roubles?!' "Dve", two, is the feminine form because "tysyacha", thousand, is feminine. After "dve", "tysyacha" becomes "tysyachi".
- 'This is how you will remember this lesson!' "Vot tak", 'like this', is what you say while showing how. "Zapomnish", you'll remember, is za- plus "pomnit", remember, so 'fix it in your memory'.
