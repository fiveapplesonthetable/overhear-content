## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, guys!' Max walks into the locker room before the shift.
- 'How are your things?' Asking casually how everything is going.
- 'Senya, are you nearly done?' Fedya waits impatiently with shaving cream in hand.
- 'I need the foam!' Asking for the shaving supplies.
- 'I will just shave my right arm, and that's it.' Senya acts out the ridiculous shaving ritual.
- 'And what are you doing?' Max stares in utter confusion.
- 'Shaving your arms?' Asking why they are grooming their forearms.
- 'Today is the fifteenth, a big sanitary inspection!' Inventing a terrifying regulatory excuse.
- 'Hair must not fall into the food!' Citing a supposedly catastrophic health risk.
- 'Come on, this is complete nonsense!' Max questions the bizarre story initially.
- 'This is a strict rule of the sanitary inspection.' Fedya maintains a completely straight face.
- 'If they find even one hair — you'll be fired immediately!' Escalating the stakes to panic the rookie.
- 'Why didn't you warn me earlier?' Panicking as he falls into the trap.
- 'I don't even have a razor with me.' Realizing he has no shaving tools.
- 'All right, runt, take my razor.' Generously offering his personal razor to seal the prank.
- 'For arms I don't mind the razor at all.' Pretending it is a small favor among colleagues.
- 'Thank you, Fedya, I will quickly shave everything.' Expressing sincere gratitude for the malicious setup.
