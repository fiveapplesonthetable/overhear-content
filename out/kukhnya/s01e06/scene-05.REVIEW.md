## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Hello, guys! How are your things?' "Parni", guys, is more than one "paren", young man. "Kak dela", 'how are things', is the question for a friend, and Max says "vashi", your, because he's talking to two people.
- 'Senya, are you nearly done? I need the foam!' "Ty skoro", 'you soon?', has no verb at all, and it's exactly how you'd hurry someone. "Mne nuzhna pena" is literally 'to me is needed foam'.
- 'I will just shave my right arm, and that's it.' "Ruka" is both 'arm' and 'hand'; Russian doesn't split them. "I vsyo", 'and all', means 'and that's it'.
- 'And what are you doing? Shaving your arms?' "Breete", you shave, comes from "brit", which changes its stem: "ya breyu", "vy breete". Max's second question is just two words and a rising voice.
- 'Today is the fifteenth, a big sanitary inspection!' Russians give a date with "chislo", number: "pyatnadtsatoe chislo", 'the fifteenth number'. "Proverka", check, is what Russians call any inspection.
- 'Hair must not fall into the food!' "Volosy", hair, is plural in Russian, like 'hairs', so "dolzhny", must, is plural too. "V edu", into the food, takes -u because it's movement into.
- 'Come on, this is complete nonsense!' "Da ladno vam" is 'come off it, you two'. "Gluposti", nonsense, is literally 'stupidities', more than one "glupost", from "glupyy", stupid.
- 'This is a strict rule of the sanitary inspection.' "Inspektsiya" becomes "inspektsii" for 'of the inspection'. Fedya sounds like he's reading from an official notice.
- 'If they find even one hair — you'll be fired immediately!' "Naydut" and "uvolyat", they'll find and they'll fire, come with no 'they': nobody in particular, just 'the authorities'. One hair is "volos", without the -y.
- 'Why didn't you warn me earlier?' "Predupredili", you warned, starts with pred-, 'before', so a warning is telling someone ahead of time. The -li ending is the past for "vy".
- 'I don't even have a razor with me.' "U menya net" is 'I don't have', and after "net" the razor changes: "britva" becomes "britvy". "S soboy", with me, is literally 'with oneself'.
- 'All right, runt, take my razor.' "Vozmi", take, is the command from "vzyat". "Moyu britvu", my razor, both end in -u, the ending for the thing taken.
- 'For arms I don't mind the razor at all.' "Mne ne zhalko", 'to me not a pity', is how you say 'I don't begrudge it'. "Sovsem ne" is 'not at all'.
- 'Thank you, Fedya, I will quickly shave everything.' "Pobreyu", I'll shave, is the po- partner of "brit", the one for a job you finish. Max thanks the man who has just tricked him.
