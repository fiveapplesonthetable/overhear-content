## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Wait, did you doubt me for even a second?' Checking whether Max doubts him for even a second is how Barinov puts Max on the spot.
- 'No, of course I did not doubt you.' Once he did not doubt Barinov, Max can finally tell Barinov what he means. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Good boy, and now put on your gloves.' Adding that good boy and now put on Max's gloves fills in the missing piece for Max.
- 'I do not understand at all, why put on gloves?' Over the menu and the kitchen, Max presses Barinov for a straight answer.
- 'Kitchen, attention! We will operate by the new rules!' Barinov insists that they will operate by the new rules, leaving Max no room to argue. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Chef, you said that you decide everything here!' Saying that Barinov said that Barinov decides everything here is how Max takes charge of the moment. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'This is my new firm decision.' Saying that this is his new firm decision is how Barinov sets the tone with Max.
- 'Sorry to interrupt, I am here about the new menu.' Because to interrupt she is here about the new menu, Vika treats the matter as settled.
- 'We need to add okroshka, salad, and chebureki to it.' Needing to add okroshka salad and chebureki to it is how Vika lays out the situation for Barinov. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'Well, team, it is time for us to experiment!' Once team it is time for them to experiment, Barinov makes sure Max hears every word.
- 'Listen, what on earth happened to him all of a sudden?' Around the menu and work, Max wants to know where Barinov really stands. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'It seems they simply bribed our Chef.' Pointing out that it seems they simply bribed their Chef gives Barinov a sobering reality check.
