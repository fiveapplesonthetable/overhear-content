## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Changes can begin at absolutely any moment.' Turning to the changes during the talk, Max brings in "peremeny" ("changes", from "peremena") as the key Russian word here.
- 'They always catch you completely off guard.' Stepping in calmly to Barinov, Max adds "sovsem" ("completely, at all") so the statement leaves zero room for doubt.
- 'I do not understand, what is this barrier?' Barinov challenges evenly what Vika is actually getting at here.
- 'Good morning, Viktor Petrovich, you are late today.' Bringing up the morning at once, Vika leans on "opazdyvayete" ("are late", a form of "opazdyvat") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Trains are late! Maybe you will explain everything to me?' Answering candidly right here, Barinov keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Vika purely through rising pitch.
- 'We have become part of a large restaurant chain.' Vika fires back firmly facing Barinov, without trying to dress up how she really feels.
- 'The general rules of this chain are being introduced here.' Making clear how shared things are to Barinov, Vika lands on "obshchiye" ("shared, common", built on "obshchiy") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Here is your new electronic pass, take it.' Vika answers evenly with a shrug, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'What, do we work at a factory now?' Barinov quizzes frankly before Vika can change the subject.
- 'Why did you even put this here?' Barinov speaks vividly in this beat and reaches for the Russian word "voobshche" ("at all") right at the heart of the line.
- 'To strictly record when an employee arrives.' Focusing on an employee in front of Barinov, Vika lands on "sotrudnik" ("employee") as the key everyday word here.
- 'Cooks are not allowed to leave their workstation.' Focusing on the cooks toward Barinov, Vika makes it clear to Barinov that she means every word.
- 'Can we not even go out for a smoke now?' Barinov weighs in naturally in the exchange and uses "pokurit" ("to have a smoke") to carry the main weight of the thought.
