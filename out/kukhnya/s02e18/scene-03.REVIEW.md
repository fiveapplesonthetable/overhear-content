## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Alright, vegetables to the storage room, meat into the fridge.' Max chimes in dryly with Barinov, before Barinov can change the subject.
- 'What, do you think they cannot figure that out themselves?' Barinov reacts warmly in the exchange and chooses "dogadayutsya" ("to guess, to realise", from the root "dogadatsya") so Lyova hears the exact meaning.
- 'I will go get busy with something useful, maybe cook something.' Emphasizing the case in the exchange, Lyova brings in "prigotovlyu" ("will prepare", from "prigotovit") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Yeah, go right ahead.' Max reacts sharply without a pause, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'And here are the snails.' Highlighting the snail during the talk, Barinov uses "ulitki" ("snail, escargot", from "ulitka") to make the point stick.
- 'No, they are simply sleeping.' Cutting in openly with Barinov, Lyova uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
- 'The finest ones in the city.' Max speaks up candidly hearing Lyova, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Why do we need so many spoiled snails?' Touching on the snail in front of Lyova, Barinov leans on "propavshikh" ("missing, lost", a form of "propavshiy") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Well, take a look for yourself.' Following up plainly before Barinov, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 4 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Just look at the state of this one.' Max replies dryly in their talk, testing Lyova's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'It is obviously dead and spoiled.' Emphasizing how missing things are to Max, Barinov puts the weight on "propavshaya" ("missing, lost", from "propavshiy") as the exchange unfolds.
- 'Why did you say they are not the freshest?' Showing how fresh things are in front of Barinov, Lyova keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Barinov purely through rising pitch.
- 'Man, you really are something, Oguzok.' Barinov replies briskly without a pause, letting the remark speak for itself.
