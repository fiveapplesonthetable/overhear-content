## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Nastya. Little Nastya.' With the shift and call in play, Nastya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Good evening once again.' With the shift and money in play, Senya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Where is my daughter?' Asking where her daughter is cuts straight through the tension between Nastya and Senya.
- 'We want to receive a lot of money for her.' Wanting to receive a lot of money is how Senya lays out the situation for Nastya.
- 'This very day.' Between the moment and the call, Nastya gives Senya a quick, grounded response.
- 'How much is a lot?' Senya asks whether how much is a lot, refusing to let the point slide.
- 'How much do we need?' Asking whether how much do they need puts Senya right on the defensive. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'It is me.' Adding that it is him fills in the missing piece for Nastya. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'And right now.' Over the conversation and the call, Nastya answers Senya without a second thought.
- 'Your daughter is going to suffer.' Since Nastya's daughter is going to suffer, Senya does not waste words on excuses. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'My compliments to you.' Around the conversation and call, Nastya sets a calm, everyday tone with Senya.
- 'Well, otherwise everything is going according to plan.' Senya reminds Nastya that otherwise everything is going according to plan before the moment passes.
- 'You will collect the money from my dad.' Because Senya will collect the money from her dad, Nastya treats the matter as settled.
