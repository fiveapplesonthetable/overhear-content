## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, wait, where are you hurrying off to like that?' Katya weighs in directly with Senya and uses "podozhdi" ("wait", from "podozhdat") to make the point stick.
- 'I need to quietly take some fresh fish home.' Touching on the fish in front of Katya, Senya leans on "nado" ("it is necessary, one needs to"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'Can you imagine, the chef cooked such a terrible sauce in there!' Senya chimes in candidly in this moment, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Anyway, Denis decided to propose to me.' Pointing to the offer in front of Denis, Katya puts the emphasis on "predlozheniye" ("offer, proposal") in the middle of the talk.
- 'No way, Sveta, are you speaking seriously right now?' Stepping in candidly right here, Katya leans on "seryozno" ("seriously"), which carries the whole predicate in Russian without needing "it is".
- 'Yes, yesterday he even bought me a beautiful ring.' Denis counters openly with a sigh, testing Katya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'My goodness, how quickly he decided on that.' Katya replies frankly before Denis and chooses "reshilsya" ("make up one’s mind", from the root "reshitsya") so Denis hears the exact meaning.
- 'Only it is a big secret, do not tell anyone, alright?' Pressing on naturally in front of Katya, Denis keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Katya purely through rising pitch.
- 'Well then, we wish you great love.' Katya answers earnestly with Denis, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Katya, wait, did you get upset over this?' Denis replies smoothly on the spot and leans on "rasstroilas" ("got upset", a form of "rasstroitsya") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Why no, not at all, I am even happy for you.' Confirming how glad she is on the spot, Katya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'After all, a real wedding is always very cool!' Turning to a real wedding with a nod, Katya makes it clear to Denis that she means every word.
