## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Listen, I found out in secret that you and Sveta are getting married.' Touching on the secret with a sigh, Nikita speaks plainly so Nastya gets the message at once.
- 'But that is far from the best news yet.' Nikita cuts in at once without a pause, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Do you know how Katya reacted to this news?' Highlighting the news beside Nastya, Nikita lands on "otreagirovala" ("reacted", built on "otreagirovat") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'She said a wedding is cool, as if she were envious.' Touching on a wedding in front of Nastya, Nikita reaches for the Russian word "zavidovala" ("to envy", built from "zavidovat") right at the center of the line.
- 'Guys, why are you not eating the navy-style pasta?' Nastya tests straight out to see how Nikita will answer on the spot.
- 'No, Kostya and I will not have lunch today.' Nikita proceeds boldly right here and puts the emphasis on "segodnya" ("today") in the middle of the talk.
- 'Because tonight is our wedding anniversary.' Turning to the anniversary to Nikita, Nastya reaches for "u nas" ("by us, at our place") to talk about what they share.
- 'If we eat now, there will be no room for our romantic dinner.' Nastya opens up right away with a sigh, leaving no doubt about where she stands.
- 'And I will take all the leftover pasta home for myself.' Mentioning the leftovers with Nastya, Nikita chooses "ostatki" ("leftovers", from the root "ostatok") so Nastya hears the exact meaning.
- 'Well how was the soup, tell me, did you like everything today?' Dwelling on the soup before Nastya, Barinov puts the question word "kak" right at the start so Nastya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Yes, thank you very much, the soup was very tasty.' Nastya reacts dryly during the talk, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Then tell me the results of all the fixed matches!' Addressing the results in this beat, Barinov brings in "dogovornykh" ("fixed", from "dogovornoy") as the key Russian word here.
