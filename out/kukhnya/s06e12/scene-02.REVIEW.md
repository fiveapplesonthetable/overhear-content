## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Anna Petrovna, tell me, what happened at your place?' Lyova hands over this point with candor: using "u vas" (literally "by you") is how Russian politely asks or talks about what someone has on their side.
- 'I merely slipped by accident in the bathroom.' The guest pushes back lightly in their talk, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Nothing terrible, you did not need to run so fast.' The guest centers one direct word in earnest: "strashnogo" means "terrible, serious" (from "strashnyy"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'The doctor says that I have a small fracture.' The guest offers the reply on cue: Russian phrases this around "u menya" (literally "by me") rather than "I have", a pattern you will hear in almost every scene.
- 'They ordered me to lie in bed for a whole month and not get up.' The guest reacts wryly with a sigh, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'Well and how will you be alone for a whole month?' Lyova emphasizes one telling element with Gulya: "mesyats" means "month", and it carries the main weight of what Lyova is saying.
- 'The doctor says rightly, she needs good care.' Gulya jumps in lightly across the room, before the guest can change the subject.
- 'Mom, let us forget all old grievances right now.' Lyova responds promptly with a smile, before Gulya can change the subject.
- 'Alright, son, let us forget our old grievances.' The guest sharpens one key term at once: "staryye" means "old" (from "staryy"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Then Gulya and I are moving in with you together.' Lyova spotlights this vivid detail on cue: "pereyezzhayem" means "we are moving" (from "pereyezzhat"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Certainly not, I am saying this for the last time!' The guest replies at once across the room, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Choose right now: either her or me!' The guest centers one pointed word on cue: "vybiray" means "to choose, select" (from "vybirat"), giving the line its exact edge.
