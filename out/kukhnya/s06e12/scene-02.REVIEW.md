## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Anna Petrovna, tell me, what happened at your place?' Replying promptly in front of the guest, the "-sya" ending on "sluchilos" ("happened") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'I merely slipped by accident in the bathroom.' The guest opens up lightly without a pause, testing Lyova's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'Nothing terrible, you did not need to run so fast.' The guest continues frankly around Lyova and brings in "strashnogo" ("terrible, serious", from "strashnyy") as the key Russian word here.
- 'The doctor says that I have a small fracture.' The guest cuts in sharply across the room, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'They ordered me to lie in bed for a whole month and not get up.' The guest responds wryly around Lyova, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Well and how will you be alone for a whole month?' Underscoring a whole month to Gulya, Lyova puts the emphasis on "mesyats" ("month") in the middle of the talk.
- 'The doctor says rightly, she needs good care.' Talking about the doctor in this beat, Gulya puts the emphasis on "khoroshiy" ("good") in the middle of the talk.
- 'Mom, let us forget all old grievances right now.' Lyova reacts promptly with a smile, without trying to dress up how he really feels.
- 'Alright, son, let us forget our old grievances.' Highlighting the son before Lyova, the guest lands on "staryye" ("old", built on "staryy") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Then Gulya and I are moving in with you together.' Lyova pushes back bluntly without holding back, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'Certainly not, I am saying this for the last time!' The guest steps in at once in this moment, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Choose right now: either her or me!' The guest responds directly in the exchange and puts the weight on "vybiray" ("to choose, select", from "vybirat") as the exchange unfolds.
