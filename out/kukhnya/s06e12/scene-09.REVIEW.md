## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, come into my office for a minute.' Barinov answers steadily facing Senya, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Here, I invented a new sauce, taste it quickly.' Touching on a new sauce on the spot, Barinov lands on "pridumal" ("came up with", built on "pridumat") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'Thank you very much for trusting me specifically.' Senya answers plainly without any doubt, refusing to let the moment slip past.
- 'Careful, the sauce is hot, do not jump to conclusions right away.' Barinov follows up pointedly with Senya, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'Wait a little, because the aftertaste is very important here.' Talking about the aftertaste to Senya, Barinov puts the emphasis on "poslevkusiye" ("aftertaste") in the middle of the talk.
- 'Well how do you like my new sauce, Senya?' Answering frankly around Senya, Barinov puts the question word "kak" right at the start so Senya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Honestly speaking, this new sauce of yours is not tasty at all.' Senya replies at once around Barinov, so Barinov knows he is not just making small talk.
- 'There is too much sage here, it is simply impossible to eat.' Discussing the sage on the spot, Senya brings in "shalfeya" ("sage", from "shalfey") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Such nonsense you sometimes cook in the kitchen, chef!' Senya proceeds decisively without holding back, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'Interesting, you never said that to me before.' Barinov replies earnestly with Senya, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'I do not even know what suddenly came over me.' Senya steps in readily during the talk and centers the thought on "nashlo" ("to find", from "nayti") without wasting a word.
- 'Probably it just built up inside, alright, I will go work.' Weighing in frankly on the spot, Senya uses the little word "prosto" ("simply, just") to make the point sound effortless.
