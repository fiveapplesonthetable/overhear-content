## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Anything else?' With the plan and date hanging in the air, Nastya puts the guest on the spot. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Yes, His Highness has shown an interest in you.' Saying that his Highness has shown an interest in Kostya is how the guest sets the tone with Kostya.
- 'And he would like to get to know you more closely.' Once he would like to get to know Kostya more closely, the guest can finally tell Kostya what he means.
- 'I cannot return his feelings.' Mentioning that she cannot return his feelings moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'He is not accustomed to refusal.' The guest reminds Kostya that he is not accustomed to refusal before the moment passes.
- 'And the sheikh invited me on a date.' With the point and date in play, Nastya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Nastya, what are you making up?' Asking what the guest making up is is how Kostya puts the guest on the spot. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'When I leave you, we will see what tune you sing.' Nastya reminds the guest that when she leaves the guest they will see what tune the guest sings before the moment passes.
- 'And how will you see what tune I sing if you leave?' By wondering how the guest sees what tune he sings if the guest leaves will, Kostya presses the guest for a straight answer. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Do you not feel sorry to lose me at all?' Asking whether the guest not feel sorry to lose her at all cuts straight through the tension between Nastya and the guest. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Go work, daydreamer.' Explaining that the guest should go work daydreamer lays the cards on the table for the guest.
- 'Fine then, you will regret it yourself later.' Claiming that fine then the guest will regret it themselves later sounds confident, even if the guest has doubts.
