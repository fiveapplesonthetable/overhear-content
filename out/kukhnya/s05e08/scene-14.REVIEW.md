## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'If Katya is in the harem, maybe Nastya is there too?' Questioning whether if Katya is in the harem maybe Nastya is there is how Kostya tests Max's story. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Call her one more time.' Calling her one more time is how Max lays out the situation for Kostya.
- 'Maybe you will answer? Kostya is worried.' Asking whether maybe Kostya will answer puts Kostya right on the defensive.
- 'That is alright, it is good for him.' Adding that that is alright it is good for him fills in the missing piece for Kostya.
- 'So what is the surprise that you scared me over?' By asking what the surprise that Kostya scared is, Nastya presses Kostya for a straight answer. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'We talked it over here and thought about it.' Since they talked it over here and thought about it, Max does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Your family is big now.' Mentioning that Kostya's family is big now moves the talk in a new direction. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'And we bought you an SUV!' Max declares that they bought Kostya as the tension in the room peaks. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Such a huge car?' With the hotel and shift hanging in the air, Nastya puts Kostya on the spot.
- 'Get behind the wheel, daughter!' By telling Kostya to get behind the wheel daughter, Max raises the stakes in the room at once. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'She is still not picking up the phone!' Once she is still not picking up the phone, Kostya makes sure Max hears every word. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Let us go look for her in the rooms.' Saying that they should go look for her in the rooms is how Max sets the tone with Kostya.
