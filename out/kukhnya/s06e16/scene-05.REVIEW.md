- 'Guys, we are on a strict diet before the show.' Underscoring how tough things are in the exchange, the guest centers the thought on "zhyostkaya" ("tough, hard", from "zhyostkiy") without wasting a word.
- 'The designer watches us all day long.' Emphasizing the designer in the exchange, the guest brings in "modelyer" ("fashion designer") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'He is afraid we will not fit into the new collection.' The guest cuts in evenly during the talk, making his position completely clear.
- 'I will turn green soon from this salad!' Mentioning the salad with Senya, the guest lands on "pozeleneyu" ("will turn green", built on "pozelenet") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'If you want, can we feed you in your room?' Turning frankly in this beat, Senya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Fedya purely through rising pitch.
- 'We will bring delicious meat completely unnoticed.' Fedya steps in steadily facing Senya, testing Senya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'And I will pay you directly.' The guest weighs in naturally before Fedya and centers the remark on "napryamuyu" ("directly") without any extra fuss.

- 'Wait, we do not need your money.' Bringing up the money facing Senya, Fedya makes it clear to Senya that he means every word.
- 'Alright, guys, then we have a deal.' Emphasizing the boys at once, the guest reaches for the Russian word "dogovorilis" ("agreed, deal", built from "dogovoritsya") right at the center of the line.
