## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'What is going on with all of you here?' By wondering what going on with all of Elena here is, Barinov presses Elena for a straight answer. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Some strange fashion designer checked into the hotel.' With the point and dress in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Wow, I know him very well!' Elena declares that wow she knows him very well as the tension in the room peaks.
- 'Well let us buy something from him.' Admitting that they should buy something from him shows Barinov being honest for a change. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'You will walk to work at the restaurant looking beautiful.' Claiming that Elena will walk to work at the restaurant looking beautiful sounds confident, even if Elena has doubts.
- 'One does not go to work in a dress like that.' Pointing out that one does not go to work in a dress like gives Barinov a sobering reality check.
- 'I will marry you in it!' Declaring that she will marry Barinov in it brings Elena's temper right to the surface. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Hello, I will be glad to see you in the restaurant.' Realizing that he will be glad to see Elena in the restaurant changes how Barinov handles the situation. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Our restaurant is a contender for a high award.' Noting that their restaurant is a contender for a high award keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'And I am the head chef of this restaurant.' Being the head of this restaurant is how Barinov lays out the situation for Elena.
- 'My future wife is a big admirer of yours.' Admitting that his future wife is a big admirer shows Barinov being honest for a change. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'I wanted to buy one of your dresses.' Saying that he wanted to buy one of Elena's dresses is how Barinov sets the tone with Elena.
