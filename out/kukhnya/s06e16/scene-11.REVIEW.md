## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'How can you look me in the eyes?' Pushing back dryly to Kostya, Nastya puts the question word "kak" right at the start so Kostya hears the focus immediately.
- 'Tell me, how could you cheat on me?' Nastya asks calmly what Kostya is actually getting at here.
- 'Wait, what are you talking about right now?' Kostya fires back directly in the exchange and leans on "podozhdi" ("wait", a form of "podozhdat") to give the remark its edge.
- 'I just heard everything myself!' Nastya jumps in plainly before Kostya and uses "slyshala" ("to hear", from "slyshat") to make the point stick.
- 'That model went to a room with the bartender!' Nastya answers lightly with a smile, testing Kostya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'That was not me, Nastya!' Kostya reacts calmly facing Nastya, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'I swear to you, that was Louis yesterday.' Reacting calmly before Nastya, the "-sya" ending on "klyanus" ("I swear") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'What do you mean Louis, why are you lying?' Nastya challenges quietly why things suddenly took this turn between them.
- 'Last night I needed to go to the restroom.' Highlighting the toilet during the talk, Kostya puts the emphasis on "tualet" ("toilet") in the middle of the talk.
- 'I asked him to watch the bar for a bit.' Bringing up the bar in front of Nastya, Kostya brings in "prismotret" ("to look") to make the meaning unmistakable.
- 'And he watched over that model instead!' Kostya opens up at once around Nastya, before Nastya can change the subject.
- 'This is the honest truth, believe me, Nastya!' Making clear how clean things are beside Nastya, Kostya reaches for the Russian word "chistaya" ("clean", built from "chistyy") right at the center of the line.
