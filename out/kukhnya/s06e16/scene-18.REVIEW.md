## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Please put away the pistol, do not shoot!' Kostya insists that Louis should put away the pistol do not shoot, leaving Louis no room to argue. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'But I am still scared right now.' Once he is still scared right now, Kostya can finally tell Louis what he means.
- 'Dad, stop, please do not touch him!' Nastya insists that dad stop please do not touch him, leaving Kostya no room to argue. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Nastya, Kostya, what are you doing here?' With the hotel and plan hanging in the air, Louis puts Kostya on the spot. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Louis, so it turns out you are the one with her?' Questioning whether it turns out Kostya is the one is how Nastya tests Kostya's story.
- 'After today I am not sure of anything.' Pointing out that after today he is not sure of anything gives Louis a sobering reality check.
- 'I met Polina, and everything turned upside down.' Louis explains that he met Polina and everything turned upside down, keeping his voice calm and direct.
- 'There you see, Nastya, I was telling the truth!' Once there Louis sees Nastya he was telling the truth, Kostya makes sure Louis hears every word.
- 'Forgive me, darling, I shouted for nothing.' Around the kitchen and hotel, Nastya sets a calm, everyday tone with Kostya.
- 'Alright, young people, sort things out here yourselves now.' Between the hotel and the plan, Kostya gives Louis a quick, grounded response.
- 'And we will probably head home.' Admitting that they will probably head home shows Kostya being honest for a change.
- 'Let us go quickly to watch the end of the fashion show.' By proposing that they go quickly to watch the end of the fashion show, Nastya keeps her side of the story grounded.
