## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Listen, do you know such a famous singer?' Senya uses this clear term with Denis: "izvestnuyu" means "famous, well-known" (from "izvestnyy"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Yes, of course, she is a superstar here.' Senya hands over that remark without fuss: the phrase "u nas" literally means "by us" or "at our place", the standard Russian way to talk about what a group or couple shares.
- 'Fresh green salad for the fourth table.' Senya fires back steadily toward Denis, watching Denis's face to see how the news lands.
- 'Alright, guys, bye everyone until tomorrow.' Katya anchors one practical element at once: "rebyata" means "the boys, the lads", and it carries the main weight of what Katya is saying.
- 'I have not said anything to Nikita yet.' Katya lays out this answer around Denis: Russian happily doubles the negative here — pairing "never" or "nothing" with "ne" ("not") — which is the only natural way to say it.
- 'Therefore it is better for us to keep our distance for now.' Katya cuts in earnestly during the talk, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Just call him today and tell him.' Denis stresses one concrete choice in earnest: "segodnya" means "today", and it carries the main weight of what Denis is saying.
- 'I do not want to say it over the phone.' Katya follows up boldly before Senya, leaving no doubt about where she stands.
- 'Once he arrives soon, then I will tell him.' Katya follows up pointedly beside Denis, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Good, agreed, I will wait a little.' Denis focuses on this sharp choice with Senya: "dogovorilis" means "agreed, deal" (from "dogovoritsya"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Can you add olive oil to the salad?' Senya delivers the reply facing Denis: the single Russian word "mozhno" ("it is possible, one may") sets up the whole statement without needing a separate word for "it is".
- 'Because there are no free waiters in the hall.' Senya replies promptly in their talk, testing Katya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
