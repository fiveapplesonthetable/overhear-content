## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Have you completely lost your mind?' With the point and wine hanging in the air, Kostya puts Barinov on the spot.
- 'The guests are laughing at you.' Admitting that the guests are laughing at Barinov shows Kostya being honest for a change. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Ladies and gentlemen, there is no need to laugh.' Saying that ladies and gentlemen there is no need to laugh is how Barinov sets the tone with Kostya. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'I come from an ancient Scottish clan.' Noting that he comes from an ancient Scottish clan keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'In our clan it is customary to wear kilts.' Adding that in their clan it is customary to wear kilts fills in the missing piece for Kostya. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Everything is fine. I told them I am a Highlander.' Noting that everything is fine keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'You are not a Highlander. You are an old man disgracing both yourself and the hotel.' Since Barinov is not a Highlander, Kostya does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Viktor Petrovich, are you aware that we have strict rules?' By checking whether Barinov aware that they have strict rules, Mikhail presses Barinov for a straight answer. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'well, non-traditional room service is prohibited?' Mikhail asks whether traditional room service is prohibited, refusing to let the point slide. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'What is that lady doing here?' Wondering what that lady doing here is is how Barinov puts Kostya on the spot.
- 'You can always win it back! I cannot.' Wondering if Barinov can always win it back shows Mikhail caught off guard. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Right now I am fulfilling another wish.' Barinov mentions that right now he is fulfilling another wish so Kostya sees what is at stake.
- 'Not yours.' With the conversation and hotel in play, Barinov keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
