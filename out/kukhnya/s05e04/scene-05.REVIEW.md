## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Why does he not want me?' Asking why he not want cuts straight through the tension between Katya and Nastya. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Because you turned him down. He is under stress.' Admitting that because Katya turned him down shows Nastya being honest for a change. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'When I yell at Senya, he cannot do anything for a week afterward.' Saying that when she yell at Senya he cannot do anything for a week afterward is how Nastya sets the tone with Katya.
- 'He is stressed, and I am the one suffering.' Once he is stressed and she is the one suffering, Katya can finally tell Nastya what she means. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'You need to help him relax.' Adding that Katya needs to help him relax fills in the missing piece for Katya. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'How about arranging a romantic evening?' With the truth and apartment hanging in the air, Nastya puts Katya on the spot.
- 'A bubble bath, candles, music.' With the apartment and room in play, Nastya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'He comes home, and you stand up like that,' Mentioning that he comes home and Katya stands up like moves the talk in a new direction. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'walk up to him, and take his face in your hands.' Saying that Katya should walk up to him and take his face in Katya's hands is how Nastya sets the tone with Katya.
- 'Anyway, Kostya is working the night shift on room service tonight.' Because anyway Kostya is working the night shift on room service tonight, Nastya treats the matter as settled.
- 'I will go stay with a friend.' Explaining that she will go stay with a friend lays the cards on the table for Katya.
- 'The apartment is at your disposal.' Nastya mentions that the apartment is at Katya's disposal so Katya sees what is at stake.
- 'You can do it everywhere.' Claiming that Katya can do it everywhere sounds confident, even if Katya has doubts. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'Except on my bed, of course.' Over the apartment and the room, Nastya answers Katya without a second thought.
