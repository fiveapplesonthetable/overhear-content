## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Oh, guys, how well these new caps suit you!' With the plan and restaurant at stake, Nagiyev reacts without holding back. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'You have such a strict, manly look now.' Claiming that the inspector has such a strict manly look now sounds confident, even if the inspector has doubts. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Vitya, I came into the dining room with a lady, cook something to eat.' Pointing out that he came into the dining room with a lady cook something to eat gives the inspector a sobering reality check.
- 'Good afternoon, Dmitry Vladimirovich! I reviewed your orders.' The inspector insists that he reviewed Max's orders, leaving Max no room to argue. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'You eat too much, I am reducing your discount.' Realizing that Max eats too much he is reducing Max's discount changes how the inspector handles the situation. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'What are you doing? I am the face of this restaurant!' Asking what the inspector doing is is how Nagiyev puts the inspector on the spot.
- 'Precisely, you are the face of this restaurant, not its stomach.' Even though precisely Max is the face of this restaurant not its stomach, the inspector keeps a steady face in front of Max. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Andrey, he humiliated me right in front of my cooks!' Insisting that Andrey he humiliated him right in front of his cooks shows Nagiyev is not backing down. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Dima, the cooks have not been yours for a long time, and Sergey is right.' Saying that the cooks have not been the inspector's for a long time and Sergey is right is how Max sets the tone with the inspector.
- 'You really do eat a lot lately.' Once the inspector really do eat a lot lately, Max can finally tell the inspector what he means. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Then let us do this: I will buy back this restaurant!' By doing this he will buy back this restaurant together, Nagiyev raises the stakes in the room at once. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'No problem, Dima, pay the money and take it.' With the shift and date in play, Max keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
