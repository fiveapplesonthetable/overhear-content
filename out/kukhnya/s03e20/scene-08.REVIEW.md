- 'The doctor advised me to ask you questions.' Over the apartment and the kitchen, Max answers Vika without a second thought.
- 'Well, that is needed to recover my memory faster.' Max explains that that is needed to recover his memory faster, keeping his voice calm and direct. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'Fine, and what exactly do you want to find out?' Wondering if fine and what exactly do Max wants to find out shows Vika caught off guard. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.

- 'In what sense strange? What are you talking about?' Over the apartment and the phone, Vika presses Max for a straight answer.
