- 'I am very tired.' Vika reacts openly with Barinov and chooses "ustala" ("are tired, got tired", from the root "ustat") so Barinov hears the exact meaning.
- 'I am going to head home.' Jumping in candidly in front of Vika, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 3 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Viktoriya Sergeyevna, you are the main star of tonight's evening.' Barinov weighs in sharply across the room, to clear the air between them once and for all.
