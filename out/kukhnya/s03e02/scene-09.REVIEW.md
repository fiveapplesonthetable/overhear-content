## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'The taste is good, really natural somehow!' Nagiyev fires back pointedly with a nod, before Senya can change the subject.
- 'Well done! A creative person, guys.' Focusing on the boys before Senya, Nagiyev chooses "kreativnyy" ("creative") so Senya gets the point right away.
- 'Clever girl! Let's go talk.' Checking on the clever girl during the talk, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 3 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'These rich people are so strange, huh? He eats it and rejoices.' Senya inquires deadpan about what Barinov just brought up.
- 'Have you completely lost all fear?' Emphasizing the fear on the spot, Barinov leans on "strakh" ("fear") to give the whole sentence its punch.
- 'And Katya cooked it. They started it first.' Senya steps in plainly in front of Barinov and centers the thought on "pervyye" ("first", from "pervyy") without wasting a word.
- 'And I will talk with you later.' Barinov pushes back quietly facing Senya, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'What will happen when Dima finds out what he ate?' Barinov chimes in firmly right here and lands on "uznayet" ("will find out", built on "uznat") to keep the statement grounded.
- 'God forbid any of you lets it slip!' Leaning in briskly around Senya, the "-sya" ending on "progovoritsya" ("lets it slip") marks an action that unfolds mutually or turns back on the speaker.
- 'Go work! I said, go work.' Barinov proceeds promptly with a sigh, testing Senya's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
- 'I am going, I am going. Why are you laughing? Nothing.' Senya fires back sharply around Barinov and uses "rzhyosh" ("are laughing", from "rzhat") to make the point stick.
- 'That is a head start for you while I am resting.' Senya follows up pointedly without any doubt, testing Barinov's reaction in the middle of the exchange.
