## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Whoa, vegetarian! Are you even aware that this thing is made of meat?' Checking whether Barinov even aware that this thing is made of meat cuts straight through the tension between Nastya and Barinov. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Kristina, I don't care whether you have the best lawyer or not.' Explaining that he does not care whether Barinov has the best lawyer or not lays the cards on the table for Barinov. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'If you talk like that, you will get nothing at all.' Even though if Barinov talks like that Barinov will get nothing at all, Nagiyev keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'Bon appetit.' Around the plan and dish, Barinov sets a calm, everyday tone with Nagiyev.
- 'It infuriated me!' With the shift and kitchen at stake, Nagiyev reacts without holding back. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'This is not that dish.' Once this is not that dish, Nagiyev can finally tell Barinov what he means.
- 'How is it not that one? It is the exact same thing.' Here wondering how it not that one is shows Barinov looking for the real truth. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'No, Vitya, not that dish.' Between the plan and the dish, Nagiyev gives Barinov a quick, grounded response.
- 'There the taste was so tart, pleasant, natural.' Since there the taste was so tart pleasant natural, Nagiyev does not waste words on excuses.

- 'One very important ingredient is missing.' Because one very important ingredient is missing, Barinov treats the matter as settled.
