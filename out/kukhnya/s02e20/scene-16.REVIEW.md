## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Get to work, lively now!' Barinov insists that Elena should get to work lively now, leaving Elena no room to argue.
- 'Lena, wait, Lena.' With the point and pride in play, Elena keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'I was talking about work.' Saying that he was talking about work is how Barinov sets the tone with Elena.
- 'That was not about the two of us.' Because that was not about the two of them, Elena treats the matter as settled.
- 'I only said that as an example.' Explaining that he only said that as an example lays the cards on the table for Elena. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'To admit that I was wrong.' Elena mentions that to admit that she was wrong so Barinov sees what is at stake.
- 'And right now I really need your help.' Claiming that right now he really needs Elena's help sounds confident, even if Elena has doubts.
- 'What happened to your famous male principles?' Over the shift and the pride, Elena presses Barinov for a straight answer. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'You never wanted to depend on women, remember?' Wondering if Elena never wanted to depend on women remember shows Barinov caught off guard. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'Lena, I do not want to argue about our relationship right now.' Realizing that someone she does not want to argue about their relationship right now changes how Elena handles the situation.
- 'I was not planning to argue about our relationship either.' Noting that he was not planning to argue about their relationship either keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'It never would have crossed my mind.' Even though it never would have crossed her mind, Elena keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'Who could have known?' Wondering who have known could is how Barinov puts Elena on the spot.
