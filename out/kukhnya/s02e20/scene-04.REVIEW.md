## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Well, here is some news for you.' Since here is some news for Nastya, Kostya does not waste words on excuses.
- 'But you said so yourself before.' Mentioning that Kostya said so themselves moves the talk in a new direction. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Well, what does it matter what I said before?' By asking what it matter what she said, Nastya presses Kostya for a straight answer.
- 'Do you not want to get married yourself?' Checking whether Kostya not want to get married themselves cuts straight through the tension between Nastya and Kostya.
- 'Nastya, of course I want to.' With the moment and trip in play, Kostya keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'What do you mean, let us just go?' Here asking what Kostya means let them just go shows Nastya looking for the real truth. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'There is something I do not understand here.' Claiming that there is something he does not understand here sounds confident, even if Nastya has doubts.
- 'Let us go to the registry office tomorrow and submit our application.' Proposing that they go to the registry office tomorrow and submit their application moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'What kind of proposal is that to you?' Questioning whether what kind of proposal is that to Nastya is how Kostya tests Nastya's story. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'I want it to be a romantic marriage proposal, do you understand?' Asking whether she wants it to be a romantic marriage proposal do Kostya understands puts Kostya right on the defensive.
- 'Guys, I beg you, please go talk to him.' Once he begs Nastya pleases go talk to him, Kostya can finally tell Nastya what he means. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'Because he does not even want to listen to me.' Even though because he does not even want to listen, Nastya keeps a steady face in front of Kostya.
- 'Of course we will talk to him.' Admitting that they will talk to him shows Kostya being honest for a change.
