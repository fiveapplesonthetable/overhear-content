## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I already love you so much as it is...' Since he already loves Kostya so much as it is, Barinov does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Kostyan, save me right now!' With the shift and work at stake, Vika reacts without holding back. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'I warned you not to come in here!' With the shift and truth at stake, Kostya reacts without holding back. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Nastya, wait, we will talk about it later.' Because they will talk about it later, Barinov treats the matter as settled.
- 'Chef, Chef, Chef, chasing him will not help.' Explaining that chasing him will not help lays the cards on the table for Barinov. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'I know him very well.' Mentioning that he knows him very well moves the talk in a new direction.
- 'It is better if you simply hear him out.' Claiming that it is better if Kostya simply hear him out sounds confident, even if Kostya has doubts.
- 'Chef, please, just talk to him for a minute.' With the kitchen and situation in play, Vika keeps this short reply brisk and direct.
- 'Alright, let us step outside for some air.' Kostya explains that Alright let them step outside for some air, keeping his voice calm and direct. Starting with "davay" is the everyday Russian way to say "come on, let us do this".
- 'Chef, I never wanted to betray you.' Realizing that he never wanted to betray Kostya changes how Barinov handles the situation.
- 'I simply could not have acted any other way.' Noting that she simply could not have acted any other way keeps Vika's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'You made your own choice yourself.' Even though Barinov made Barinov's own choice themselves, Kostya keeps a steady face in front of Barinov. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'And only after that come all the money issues.' Over the kitchen and the situation, Barinov answers Kostya without a second thought.
