## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We have serious problems out there.' Since they have serious problems out there, Barinov does not waste words on excuses. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Go to hell!' Telling Barinov to go to hell is Nagiyev's blunt reaction in front of Barinov. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Dima, what happened out there?' Over the wedding and the truth, Barinov presses Nagiyev for a straight answer.
- 'I am the one asking you that question.' Here being the one asking Barinov that question sets up the next turn in their exchange. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'What just happened?' Around the banquet and kitchen, Barinov wants to know where Nagiyev really stands. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'What do you mean we do not have it?' Wondering what Barinov means they do not have it cuts straight through the tension between Nagiyev and Barinov.
- 'I forgot to write it down.' Claiming that he forgot to write it down sounds confident, even if Nagiyev has doubts.
- 'Oh, so you forgot to write it down!' Nagiyev insists that Barinov forgot to write it down, leaving Barinov no room to argue. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Please do not worry, alright?' By checking whether not worry alright, Barinov presses Nagiyev for a straight answer.
- 'We will serve everything right away.' Realizing that they will serve everything right away changes how Nagiyev handles the situation.
- 'We will sort it out, so to speak.' Noting that they will sort it out so to speak keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'What the hell are you doing?' Asking whether what the hell are Barinov doing puts Barinov right on the defensive.
- 'And you, and you, and you too.' Between the truth and the menu, Barinov gives Nagiyev a quick, grounded response. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
