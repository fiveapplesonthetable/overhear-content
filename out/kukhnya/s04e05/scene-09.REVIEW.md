## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'We need to fire one extra cook.' Max stands firm calmly without a pause, making his position completely clear.
- 'Listen, can I have you for one minute?' Max centers one key choice for Fedya: "minutku" means "a minute" (from "minutka"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'We have a staff reduction in the restaurant right now.' Max directs the comment with candor: the phrase "u nas" literally means "by us" or "at our place", the standard Russian way to talk about what a group or couple shares.
- 'Forgive me, there is nothing personal here.' Max responds candidly on the spot, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'I beg you, just do not fire me today!' Notice how Fedya tells Max in reply — the Russian word "uvolnyayte" ("fire", built from "uvolnyat") does the heavy lifting.
- 'Please, I have a big family at home.' Fedya brings this point in earnest: Russian phrases this around "u menya" (literally "by me") rather than "I have", a pattern you will hear in almost every scene.
- 'I have small children and a bank loan.' Fedya opens up bluntly watching Max, bringing things back to earth after the buildup.
- 'Please, I am begging you very much!' Fedya leans on one familiar choice with Max: "proshu" means "beg, ask" (from "prosit"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'Well why are you kneeling, get up quickly now!' Max brings this answer with warmth: starting with "davay" is the classic everyday Russian way to say "come on, let's do this" between people on close terms.
- 'Everything is fine, I will not fire you.' Max opens up promptly hearing Fedya, steering the talk right where he wants it.
- 'On the contrary, you are a really great cook, got it?' Max centers this direct expression for Fedya: "naoborot" means "contrary", and it carries the main weight of what Max is saying.
- 'This downsizing will definitely not affect you.' Max stresses this vivid word at once: "sokrashcheniye" means "right" (from "sokrashcheniy"), giving the line its exact edge.
- 'So come on, go work calmly.' Max answers quietly around Fedya, shifting the whole mood of the room in a single beat.
