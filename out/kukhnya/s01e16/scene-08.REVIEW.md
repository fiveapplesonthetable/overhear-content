## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Max, did you hear important news today?' Kostya asks his friend if he has caught wind of the big event happening today.
- 'What other news could worry me?' Max seems completely uninterested, stating that nothing else matters to him right now.
- 'A huge inspection is going on in the neighboring restaurant right now.' Sharing the gossip, the bartender explains that a massive audit is taking place next door.
- 'Right now I do not care at all about outside inspections.' Brushing off the drama, the cook insists he has zero interest in what other places are dealing with.
- 'The police are checking all kitchen workers over there.' Adding more detail, the friend notes that authorities are actively looking at everyone's paperwork.
- 'Answer honestly, whose pâté is better right now?' Ignoring the news entirely, the young chef demands a straight answer about his food comparison.
- 'Do you promise not to be offended with me?' Kostya asks for reassurance that his honest opinion will not cause hurt feelings.
- 'I want to hear only the pure truth.' Promising to handle it, the cook says he only wants the absolute truth.
- 'And you definitely will not tell the chef?' The friend double-checks the safety of his secret, asking if the boss will find out what he said.
- 'No, I simply want to know the result.' Swearing to keep quiet, the cook insists he just needs to know the final verdict.
- 'The chef makes this pâté much better.' Delivering the tough news, the bartender confirms that the boss still holds the crown.
- 'I will definitely find his secret ingredient!' Max vows to keep digging, determined to uncover the missing component.
- 'I wish you great luck in that!' Wishing his buddy well, the bartender hopes he succeeds in his quest.
