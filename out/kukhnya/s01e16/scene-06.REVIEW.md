## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Chef, why are you smiling so cheerfully?' Senya wonders aloud why his boss is looking so visibly happy today.
- 'Look into the courtyard at the neighboring restaurant.' Pointing outside, the chef tells his colleague to observe the place next door.
- 'A strict inspection just arrived over there!' Noticing the commotion, the cook points out that a serious checkup is happening across the way.
- 'Yes, that is a special service arriving.' Confirming the observation, the boss mentions that a specific government agency has shown up.
- 'Did you call this inspection yourself?' Senya puts two and two together, asking if his boss is the one who made the phone call.
- 'Of course, they must respect the rules of order.' Barinov casually admits to it, suggesting that people need to learn how to follow the rules.
- 'Now their cooks will be running around a lot.' The cook pictures the chaos, imagining how frantic the kitchen staff over there must be.
- 'Let them quickly run around the building!' Laughing it off, the chef seems to enjoy the thought of his rivals scrambling in a panic.
- 'Elena Pavlovna will be extremely furious.' Thinking of the neighboring boss, the cook predicts she will be absolutely livid.
- 'She should not have parked her car here.' Brushing off the concern, the chef simply says she brought it on herself by parking poorly.
- 'This is indeed very cruel little revenge.' Senya notes that this payback is a bit harsh, though he seems amused by it.
- 'This is a fair lesson for insolent neighbors.' Standing by his actions, the boss insists it is a fitting punishment for their arrogant behavior.
- 'Then let us calmly watch out the window!' Relaxing into the moment, the cook suggests they just sit back and enjoy the show.
