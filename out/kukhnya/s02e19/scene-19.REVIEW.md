## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Oh, just look at that.' Around the plan and restaurant, Barinov sets a calm, everyday tone with Nagiyev.
- 'Come on, do not be so upset about it.' Coming on do not be so upset about it is how Vika lays out the situation for Barinov.
- 'You will have plenty of other experienced women in your life.' Claiming that Barinov will have plenty of other experienced women in Barinov's life sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'Not at all, you are true professionals.' Pointing out that not at all Nagiyev is true professionals gives Nagiyev a sobering reality check.
- 'Enjoy your meal.' Here telling Barinov to enjoy Barinov's meal sets up the next turn in their exchange.
- 'Forgive us for taking so long.' Since Barinov should forgive them for taking so long, Nagiyev does not waste words on excuses.
- 'It is all because of Viktoriya Sergeyevna.' Noting that it is all because of Viktoriya Sergeyevna keeps Barinov's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'I understand completely.' Explaining that she understands completely lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
- 'Wait, how come the waitresses did not show up, huh?' Wondering if how come the waitresses did not show up huh shows Nagiyev caught off guard. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'How am I supposed to know?' With the moment and restaurant hanging in the air, Barinov puts Nagiyev on the spot.
- 'Look, the redhead is at her post.' Once the redhead is at her post, Vika can finally tell Barinov what she means.
- 'The bartender is working a bit slower, right?' Nagiyev asks whether the bartender is working a bit slower right, refusing to let the point slide. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'The cooks look like real champions.' Barinov tells Nagiyev that the cooks look like real champions without dressing it up.
