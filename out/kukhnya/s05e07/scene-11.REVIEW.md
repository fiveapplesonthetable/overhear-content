- 'Just leave her alone already!' Senya proceeds openly watching Gulya, grounding the moment in plain reality.
- 'There is nothing and never was anything between Katya and me.' Senya counters frankly with a sigh, leaving no doubt about where he stands.
- 'And never will be!' Reacting calmly beside Senya, spoken Russian compresses the whole thought into just 3 words, skipping the extra helper words English needs.
- 'Of course there never will be.' Senya casually shares this confirmation without hesitation using "konechno" ("of course") — spoken with a soft "shn" sound in everyday Moscow Russian to mean "of course, certainly".

- 'Why do I have to say this for you?' Gulya continues firmly beside Senya and brings in "dolzhna" ("should, must", from "dolzhen") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Or maybe you still want her after all?' Looking over quietly beside Senya, Gulya keeps normal statement word order here, turning the line into a question for Senya purely through rising pitch.
- 'Why do you keep repeating that!' Senya reacts firmly beside Gulya, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'Crazy fool!' Pointing to the fool beside Senya, Katya centers the thought on "nenormalnaya" ("crazy, abnormal", from "nenormalnyy") without wasting a word.
