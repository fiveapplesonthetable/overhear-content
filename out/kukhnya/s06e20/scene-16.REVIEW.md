## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'This dish is simply utterly divine!' Declaring that this dish is simply utterly divine brings the inspector's temper right to the surface.
- 'Consider the star in your pocket.' Around the conversation and dish, the inspector sets a calm, everyday tone with Barinov. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'What is happening, call the police quickly!' Saying that what is happening call the police quickly is how Denis takes charge of the moment. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'I would not do that in your place.' Claiming that he would not do that in Barinov's place sounds confident, even if Barinov has doubts.
- 'Everyone stand against the wall quickly!' With the point and dish at stake, Denis reacts without holding back. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'And what should we do with this chef?' Asking what they do with this should is how Denis puts Barinov on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Let the disabled man sit quietly in his chair.' Letting the disabled man sit quietly in his chair is how Denis lays out the situation for Barinov.
- 'Look how many people you brought trouble to.' Noting that how many people Barinov brought trouble keeps Denis's side of the story simple and clear. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Hey, do not touch my daughter!' Insisting that do not touch his daughter shows Barinov is not backing down. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Right now we are taking these two.' Admitting that right now they are taking these two shows Denis being honest for a change.
- 'We are leaving the restaurant quickly.' Saying that they are leaving the restaurant quickly is how Denis sets the tone with Barinov.
- 'You sit quietly and do not make noise.' Once Barinov sits quietly and do not make noise, Denis can finally tell Barinov what he means. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
