## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Today I lost my father all over again.' Bringing up his father with Nastya, Kostya puts the emphasis on "segodnya" ("today") in the middle of the talk.
- 'Kostik, forgive me, please, darling.' Nastya replies warmly with Kostya, keeping the focus on what actually matters here.
- 'I am to blame for everything myself.' Nastya jumps in plainly before Kostya and brings in "vinovata" ("at fault, to blame", from "vinovatyy") as the key Russian word here.
- 'I should not have looked for him at all.' Leaning in briskly around Kostya, Nastya drops in "voobshche" ("in general, altogether") to sum up the whole situation at once.
- 'Kostik, you nearly ate the cotton swab.' Nastya replies lightly in this moment, steering the talk right where she wants it.
- 'It was I who did a paternity test.' Mentioning a paternity around Kostya, Nastya chooses "ottsovstvo" ("paternity") so Kostya gets the point right away.
- 'I took saliva from both of you.' Nastya chimes in at once in their talk, without trying to dress up how she really feels.
- 'Well, and what did that test show?' Bringing up the test in this moment, Kostya makes it clear to Nastya that he means every word.
- 'Kostya, he is your real father!' Confirming how real he is in the exchange, Nastya chooses "nastoyashchiy" ("real, genuine") so Kostya gets the point right away.
- 'Then I am running after him right away!' Kostya responds openly with Nastya and brings in "pobezhal" ("ran off", from "pobezhat") as the key Russian word here.
- 'Thank you for not telling the police officers.' Denis promptly voices this word of thanks toward Kostya using "spasibo" ("thank you") — the everyday word for "thank you", which grew out of the old blessing "spasi Bog" ("God save you").
- 'Forgive me, that was a very bad idea.' Underscoring the idea at once, Kostya lands on "plokhaya" ("bad", built on "plokhoy") to keep the statement grounded.
