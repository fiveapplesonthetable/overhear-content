## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Misha, well, this is somehow wrong.' Because this is somehow wrong, Barinov treats the matter as settled. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
- 'You deserved this star, after all!' Between the police and the point, Mikhail delivers a sharp reaction. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'How many inspections you and I have been through!' Mikhail declares that how many inspections Barinov and he has been as the tension in the room peaks.
- 'We cannot lose all of this right now.' Admitting that they cannot lose all of this right now shows Mikhail being honest for a change. There is no "you" needed in the Russian here: one short word states the rule for everyone in the room.
- 'Wait, he will suffocate like that!' Insisting that he will suffocate like shows Nikita is not backing down. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'I thought you would just beat him up.' Nikita mentions that he thought Barinov would just beat him up so Barinov sees what is at stake.
- 'And you are planning to kill him!' Declaring that Barinov is planning to kill him brings Nikita's temper right to the surface. Even in the middle of the drama, he keeps the polite plural "you" that workplace Russian expects.
- 'We did not agree on that.' Noting that they did not agree keeps Nikita's side of the story simple and clear. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'We did not make any agreement with you at all.' Even though they did not make any agreement with Barinov at all, Mikhail keeps a steady face in front of Barinov.
- 'You came here yourself, after all.' Admitting that Barinov came here themselves after all shows Mikhail being honest for a change. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Stop, you cannot do that at all!' Once Barinov should stop Barinov cannot do that at all, Nikita makes sure Barinov hears every word. Russian packs the whole idea of what is allowed or needed into one impersonal word at the front.
- 'I will report this to the police right now!' Insisting that he will report this to the police right now shows Nikita is not backing down.
