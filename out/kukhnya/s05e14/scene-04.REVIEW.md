## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Today for lunch there will be your favorite pilaf.' Addressing the lunch in this beat, Fedya centers the remark on "lyubimyy" ("favourite") without any extra fuss.
- 'Your pilaf always turns out simply wonderful.' Checking on the pilaf before Denis, Fedya uses "u tebya" (literally "by you") where English says "you have" or "your".
- 'Look, what kind of blue paint is this?' Denis questions curiously about what Fedya just brought up.
- 'An acquaintance from the bank brought me this yesterday.' Mentioning the bank in front of Denis, Fedya puts the emphasis on "znakomyy" ("familiar") in the middle of the talk.
- 'He works at the bank transporting money.' Underscoring the transport during the talk, Fedya centers the thought on "perevozke" ("transport, carriage", from "perevozka") without wasting a word.
- 'They put such paint in bags in case of theft.' Fedya pushes back boldly toward Denis, letting the remark speak for itself.
- 'And how am I supposed to work now? The shower broke!' Pointing to the shower before Katya, Denis leans on "slomalsya" ("broke down", a form of "slomatsya") to give the remark its edge.
- 'Take the key, you can take a shower in my hotel room.' Highlighting the key beside Denis, Katya centers the remark on "klyuch" ("key") without any extra fuss.
- 'Katya, did you move into our hotel?' Fedya challenges promptly while looking straight at Katya here.
- 'Yes, there is a small renovation at my home right now.' Showing how small things are around Fedya, Katya chooses "nebolshoy" ("small, modest") so Fedya gets the point right away.
- 'The plus is that my mom is the owner of the hotel.' Checking on the landlady in front of Fedya, Katya links the Russian words directly in the present with no word for "am", "is", or "are" needed.
- 'Cool, you are lucky to have a mom like that!' Fedya pushes back briskly with a smile, shifting the mood of the conversation in a single beat.
