## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Senya, bring me a fresh little lemon slice.' Bringing him a fresh little lemon slice is how Barinov lays out the situation for Mikhail.
- 'I still have plenty of time right now.' Claiming that he still has plenty of time right now sounds confident, even if Mikhail has doubts.
- 'Alright, Viktor Petrovich, I will bring it right now.' Explaining that Alright Viktor Petrovich he will bring it right now lays the cards on the table for Barinov.
- 'I propose a toast to the easiest victory!' Over the conversation and the chance, Barinov lets his real feelings show. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Wait, what kind of trick is this here?' Asking whether what kind of trick is this here puts Mikhail right on the defensive.
- 'Vitya, do not touch my glasses with your hands, remember?' Asking whether not touch his glasses with Barinov's hands remember is how Mikhail puts Barinov on the spot. In Russian, he does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
- 'Otherwise you immediately lost our bet!' Mikhail insists that otherwise Barinov immediately lost their bet, leaving Barinov no room to argue. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'Then how am I supposed to get the shot glass?' When Barinov asks if how am he supposed to get the shot glass, Mikhail has to answer fast.
- 'In no way, Vitya, you cannot get it at all!' Mikhail declares that in no way Vitya Barinov cannot get it at all as the tension in the room peaks. Russian stacks "never" or "nothing" together with "not", doubling the refusal for extra weight.
- 'You thought this up on purpose in advance!' Because Mikhail thought this up on purpose in advance, Barinov does not bother softening the blow for Mikhail. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'A deal is a deal, hand the ticket over to me.' Adding that a deal is a deal hand the ticket over to him fills in the missing piece for Barinov.
- 'Fine, take it, but I will remember this!' Once fine take it but he will remember this, Barinov makes sure Mikhail hears every word. Russian drops "is" in the present, setting the two halves of the thought side by side.
