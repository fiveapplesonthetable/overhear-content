## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'I do not know what is going on between you and Katya.' Since she does not know what is going on between Katya and Katya, Eleonora does not waste words on excuses. In Russian you say "by me" or "with us" rather than "I have", so the situation sits right with the person.
- 'But you must break up with her immediately.' Claiming that Katya must break up with her immediately sounds confident, even if Katya has doubts.
- 'Wait, listen to me first...' Over the breakup and the conversation, Max answers Eleonora without a second thought. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'Be quiet, I have not finished speaking yet!' Once be quiet she has not finished speaking yet, Eleonora makes sure Katya hears every word. Russian puts the key word right up front so her emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'I know your type of guy very well.' Knowing Katya's type of guy very well moves the conversation forward without extra drama.
- 'You will simply ruin my beloved daughter's life.' Noting that Katya will simply ruin her beloved daughter life keeps Eleonora's side of the story simple and clear. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'I am offering you twenty thousand for the breakup.' By being offering Katya twenty thousand for the breakup, Eleonora keeps her side of the story grounded. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'I hope you understand that Katya must not know.' Because she hopes Katya understands that Katya must not know, Eleonora treats the matter as settled.
- 'So you will not even hear me out?' Wondering if Eleonora will not even hear him out shows Max caught off guard. Notice how he sticks to the formal "you", keeping a professional line between them.
- 'I have already spent too much time on you!' Eleonora insists that she has already spent too much time on Katya, leaving Katya no room to argue. Using the informal "you" shows how direct and unfiltered their kitchen relationship is.
- 'Katya, listen, here is the thing with us...' Adding that here is the thing with them fills in the missing piece for Eleonora.
- 'Max, how much longer am I going to wait for my order?' Over the truth and the breakup, Katya presses Eleonora for a straight answer. In Russian, she does not need helper words like "do" or "did"—rising pitch alone turns it into a question.
