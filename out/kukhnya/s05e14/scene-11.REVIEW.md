## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Yes, the hostess vacancy is still open with us.' Vika mentions that the hostess vacancy is still open with them so Kostya sees what is at stake. Russian expresses having something as "by me is", keeping the phrase short and grounded.
- 'Come to us this evening for an interview.' Since Kostya should come to them this evening for an interview, Vika does not waste words on excuses.
- 'Yes, all the best to you, goodbye.' Around the room and money, Vika sets a calm, everyday tone with Kostya.
- 'In my neighborhood there is a pretty good maternity hospital.' Vika explains that in her neighborhood there is a pretty good maternity hospital, keeping her voice calm and direct.
- 'But at a modern center the staff is much better.' Realizing that at a modern center the staff is much better changes how Kostya handles the situation.
- 'Ten thousand dollars is, of course, rather expensive.' Noting that ten thousand dollars is of course rather expensive keeps Vika's side of the story simple and clear.
- 'Give birth calmly in a regular city hospital.' Giving birth calmly in a regular city hospital is how Kostya lays out the situation for Max.
- 'I was born in a regular hospital myself.' Admitting that he was born in a regular hospital himself shows Kostya being honest for a change.
- 'It really shows what kind of person you turned into!' Saying that it really shows what kind of person Kostya turned is how Vika takes charge of the moment.
- 'Max, Vika urgently needs money for the birth!' Kostya declares that Vika urgently needs money for the birth as the tension in the room peaks. Russian puts the key word right up front so his emotion lands on the first beat.
- 'Really? And why did you not tell me earlier?' Questioning whether and why did Kostya not tell him earlier is how Max tests Kostya's story. Speaking with the close, informal "you" strips away any polite distance between them.
- 'She says that she does not want anything from you.' Saying that she says that she does not want anything from Max is how Kostya sets the tone with Max.
