# The Naked Director — taster character sheet

This first catalogue lesson is drawn from the official Season 1 trailer. It does not certify Episode 1 dialogue. Character design is based on the visible role, with simple invented cartoon features rather than an actor portrait.

## Megumi / 恵美

Role key `megumi`, label `恵美`, female. An adult young woman who asks the filmmaker to let her appear in his films. In the request close-up she has long straight black hair with a side part, a navy coat and a high cream collar. Draw a fully clothed, calm, earnest cartoon woman with dot eyes, rosy cheeks and simple rounded shapes. Do not reproduce an actor’s likeness.

## Place

The trailer cuts across several shots while the request is heard. Its speaking close-up is at a film workplace with a metal staircase and an unbranded cream vehicle carrying a green stripe behind her. A simple studio background may suggest those shapes. Do not turn the montage into a fabricated continuous restaurant scene, add an invented male reply, or depict unrelated revealing costume shots.
