# The Naked Director — character & place sheet

Character designs are simple, clean, fully clothed flat-vector cartoon figures following `art/style/STYLE.md` (rounded geometric shapes, no outlines, tiny dark dot eyes, rosy cheek blush, dominant colour palette with a spotlight circle, zero text or letters). Never reproduce an actor's likeness, and keep every costume and pose 100% everyday and family-safe.

## Main & Recurring Characters

### Toru Muranishi / 村西とおる (`muranishi`, `村西`, male)
An earnest, energetic 1980s Japanese encyclopedia salesman in his early thirties with short neat black hair parted on the side, expressive dark eyebrows, dot eyes, and rosy cheeks. In Sapporo office and sales scenes he wears a crisp white dress shirt, a dark navy or burgundy striped tie, and a slightly rumpled charcoal suit jacket carrying a leather briefcase; in the Episode 1 studio prologue he wears a clean beige trench coat or suit jacket while holding a compact video camera on his shoulder.

### Ono / 小野 (`ono`, `小野`, male)
The cool, confident top encyclopedia salesman at Bianca International, early thirties, with stylishly swept-back wavy dark hair, relaxed half-smiling expression, and a sharp tan or olive three-piece 1980s suit with an open collar or patterned tie.

### Toshi Arai / 荒井トシ (`toshi`, `トシ`, male)
A streetwise young Susukino hustler in his mid-twenties with tousled medium-length black hair, lively grin, and casual 1980s Sapporo streetwear: a warm rust-orange or maroon bomber jacket over a dark turtleneck and jeans.

### Kaoru Kuroki / 黒木香 (`kuroki`, `黒木`, female)

## Recurring Places (1980s Sapporo & Tokyo)

- **Tokyo Interview Studio (Scene 01)**: Bright studio lights on tripods, a vintage shoulder-mounted camera, warm amber backdrop, zero readable text.
- **Sapporo Coffeehouse Arcade (Scene 03)**: Wood-panelled 1980s Japanese kissaten with flat glass-top cocktail arcade tables, coffee cups, warmorange pendant lamps.
- **Bianca International Office (Scenes 04, 05, 17, 19)**: Rows of grey steel desks, rotary phones, boxed sets of bound encyclopedias (unlabelled spines), wall charts with simple bars (no letters/numbers), and a heavy metal office safe.
- **Muranishi Family Home (Scenes 06, 07, 17, 22)**: Modest 1980s Sapporo wooden dining room and entryway (`genkan`), low table with rice bowls and teapot, sliding screen doors.
- **Sapporo Cinema & Residential Streets (Scenes 08, 10, 11, 15, 16)**: Velvet cinema seats bathed in projector light; snowy-eaved Sapporo residential doorways and tidy living rooms.
- **Nakai Mansion (Scenes 12, 13, 14)**: Grand stone entrance gate with pine trees; spacious tatami room with sliding shoji screens, low lacquered table, whistling kettle, and encyclopedia volumes.
- **Post-war Fukushima Home (Scene 24)**: Warm sepia-toned rural wooden cottage interior with sliding paper doors and futon bedding.
- **Susukino Night District & Snack Bar (Scenes 25, 26, 28, 29)**: Warmly lit wooden bar counter with bottles and glasses; glowing evening street lanterns (pure colour shapes, zero kanji or neon lettering) and cassette tapes on a shop counter.
