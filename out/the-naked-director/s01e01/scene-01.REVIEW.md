## Walk-through narration (English only, no target-language words quoted)

- 'Because I wanted to be my true self.' The opening phrase means 'my true self', although no separate word says 'my'. The word for 'because' comes at the end, introducing the reason for her request.
- 'I want you to let me appear.' Japanese puts the wish after the action. The word for 'appear' is changed to mean 'let someone appear', so she is asking for permission to perform.
- 'In adult videos.' The two borrowed English words are written in Japanese sounds. The final small word connects this phrase to appearing in something, completing the request.
